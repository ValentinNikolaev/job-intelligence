**Your Role**

- You have a strong interest in financial markets, particularly Finance, Trading, Foreign Exchange
- Ideally you bring a solid understanding of sales processes, including LinkedIn engagement and basic automation tools
- You feel comfortable with proactive outreach, including cold calling and direct client interaction
- Demonstrated strong curiosity combined with a proactive, hands-on, and solution-oriented mindset
