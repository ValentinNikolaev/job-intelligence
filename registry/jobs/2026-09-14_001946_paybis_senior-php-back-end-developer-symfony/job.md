# Senior PHP Back-end Developer (Symfony)

Posted: 2026-09-14

### About Paybis

Paybis is a global fintech company operating in the crypto and payments space. We build scalable financial products used by customers worldwide and continue expanding our backend engineering capabilities to support rapid product growth.

We are currently strengthening our backend engineering team to accelerate development of our native mobile application and core backend services. This role is critical for improving delivery speed, scalability, and long-term architecture quality across our platform.

We are looking for a strong Senior PHP Back-end Developer who combines deep PHP/Symfony expertise with architectural thinking, ownership mindset, and passion for building reliable high-load backend systems.

### Role Overview

As a Senior PHP Backend Developer (IC-4), you’ll work on scalable backend services powering our fintech and crypto products. This is a hands-on engineering role with high ownership and autonomy — you’ll contribute to API development, microservices architecture, legacy refactoring, performance optimization, and engineering quality improvements across the platform.

You’ll collaborate daily with backend engineers, DevOps, QA, mobile developers, and product teams. The ideal candidate is proactive, technically mature, comfortable with complex systems, and capable of driving backend improvements independently.

### Key Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services using PHP 8 and Symfony 6/7
- Build and support secure RESTful APIs and third-party service integrations
- Contribute to microservices architecture development and service decomposition
- Improve backend scalability, reliability, and performance
- Refactor legacy code and systematically reduce technical debt
- Participate in architecture discussions and propose engineering improvements
- Conduct code reviews and raise engineering quality standards across the team
- Troubleshoot production issues and support incident resolution
- Collaborate with QA, DevOps, Product, and Mobile teams
- Support CI/CD processes and production releases
- Mentor junior and middle engineers as you settle in

### What We’re Looking For

***Must have:***

- 5+ years of commercial PHP development
- 2+ years hands-on experience with Symfony 6/7 in production
- Strong understanding of OOP, SOLID principles, and design patterns — applied in practice, not just in theory
- Experience building and maintaining REST APIs in service-oriented or microservices architecture
- Strong knowledge of MySQL/MariaDB: query optimization, Doctrine ORM, migrations
- Experience with RabbitMQ or Kafka in production environments
- Caching experience: Redis, Valkey, or Memcached
- Git proficiency: branching strategy, PR process, active code review participation
- Comfortable working in Docker-based environments
- Strong production debugging and troubleshooting skills
- Awareness of security fundamentals: OWASP Top 10, IDOR, SQL injection prevention
- English B2/C1 — daily written communication: PRs, tickets, async discussions

***Soft skills we genuinely care about:***

- **Ownership Mindset** — proactive improvements and end-to-end task ownership are an expectation, not an option
- **Engineering-First Thinking** — instinctively considers scalability, maintainability, and code quality
- **Autonomous Execution** — self-organizes, accurately estimates, and delivers without micromanagement
- **Adaptability** — switches between tasks and changing requirements without loss of quality
- **Cross-functional Collaboration** — works effectively with QA, DevOps, Product, and Mobile teams daily
- **Pragmatic Problem-Solving** — makes the right trade-off between speed and quality under pressure
