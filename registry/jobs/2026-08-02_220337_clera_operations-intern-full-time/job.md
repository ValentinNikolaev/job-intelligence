# Operations Intern → Full-Time

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

This is not a classic ops internship. We don't want someone who *manages* operations — we want someone who **automates them away**. You'll report directly to a founder and own real parts of the business from day one: no slide decks, no shadow work, no coffee runs. If something is broken, you fix it. If something is manual, you kill it.

This is a **3–6 month paid internship** with a clear path to convert into a full-time Operations role for the right person. We're a pre-seed healthtech startup building a personalized preventive health platform — biomarker testing, clinician-reviewed insights, and concierge support for members across Europe.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the customer experience end-to-end: onboarding, blood draw scheduling, results delivery, and retention

-

Coordinate multi-stakeholder partnerships across Europe — lab partners, phlebotomy clinics, and supplement manufacturers

-

Build and maintain internal ops tooling and automation workflows using **n8n, Make, Zapier, Notion, Linear, and Airtable**

-

Write Python scripts to eliminate repetitive manual work

-

Design and scale operational playbooks so we can grow to 10× the customers without 10× the headcount

-

Identify process gaps and drive fixes independently, with speed and low overhead
