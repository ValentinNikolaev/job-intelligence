### **About the role**

Hostaway is seeking a highly motivated **Senior Backend Engineer** to join the Core team. This team is responsible for the integrations with our key partners (Online Travel Agencies). These integrations are critical to our customers’ daily workflows, powering seamless connectivity between their properties and major booking platforms. If you’ve worked on similar tools that handle huge volumes and interact with a variety of complex APIs, you’ll be a great fit!

### **What you'll do**

-

Design, build, and operate backend services in **PHP and Go.**

-

Drive the **extraction of services out of our monolith**, defining clean boundaries, ownership, and migration paths.

-

Collaborate closely with the **Product Manager **to shape the technical direction and ensure our backend services deliver maximum value.

-

Design and ship **APIs built for agentic interfaces** — making our guest-facing domain consumable by AI agents and automated workflows.

-

Improve **page speed and end-to-end performance**, working closely with frontend peers since conversion depends on it.

-

Contribute to high-level architectural decisions and help us move quickly without accumulating regret.

-

**Leverage AI to accelerate code delivery** — we provide a Claude Team Premium subscription and expect engineers to use it well.

-

6+ years of experience as a Backend Engineer, with a strong foundation in software engineering,

-

Strong-level proficiency in **PHP** and/or **Golang**, with a strong willingness to dive into both.

-

Proven experience designing and operating **high-traffic, customer-facing systems.**

-

Experience **decomposing monoliths** or extracting services, with a pragmatic view on when to do it (and when not to).

-

Solid experience **designing APIs** — REST, GraphQL, or similar — with an eye on clarity, versioning, and consumability (bonus if you've built APIs for AI agents or LLM-driven clients).

-

Familiarity with transitioning event-driven service-oriented architectures by applying Domain Driven Design (DDD) principles

-

Comfortable **using AI coding tools** (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, etc.) as part of your daily workflow, and able to get real leverage out of them.

-

Experience working in a Cloud environment (AWS is a plus).

-

Experience with message queues (e.g., AWS SQS) is a plus.
