## Strict Hiring Manager Review

Three strengths stand out. The Go gateway demonstrates reusable contracts across vendor boundaries. Telephony shows architecture and backend delivery through security review. CRURATED adds personally owned production event infrastructure and external integration work.

Three weaknesses matter. First, the original emphasis does not prove how engineering choices affected an enterprise account's commercial results. Present a real operating consequence and explain how its evaluation would transfer, without inventing revenue. Second, generic reliability statements invite doubts about personal contribution. Rewrite them around the owned system and verified consequence. Third, an imported leadership title can overstate the historical job record. Keep Software Developer headings where supported and describe the confirmed backend-lead contribution in the bullet itself.

## Red Flags

The largest recruiter risk is the distance between proven production ownership and direct margin or account-growth accountability. Ask what evidence the hiring team expects at the Staff level. Next, distinguish actual employment titles from target seniority. Leadership responsibilities support the narrative but do not justify changing past titles.

Simple App ended in August 2026 according to the latest direct correction. Its CV heading uses the configured year precision, without implying current employment. CRURATED is explicitly concurrent part-time consulting. The older Sixt/PDFfiller overlap remains visible and unexplained. Availability, salary and current authorization wording require confirmation before entering the official form. September telephony delivery must not be used to extend the employment period.
