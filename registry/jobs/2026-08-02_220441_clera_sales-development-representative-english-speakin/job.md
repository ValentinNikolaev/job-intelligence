# Sales Development Representative (English-Speaking)

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

We're a fast-growing, seed-stage European AI SaaS startup building AI-powered phone assistant technology for SMBs. Our platform automates inbound call handling — from lead qualification and appointment booking to customer service and order processing — and we're expanding aggressively into English-speaking markets.

As our **Sales Development Representative (English-Speaking)**, you'll own the entire English-language top-of-funnel: qualifying inbound leads, running multi-channel outbound outreach, and handing off warm demos to Account Executives. This is a high-ownership, metrics-driven role with a clear and fast path to AE for top performers.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the English-speaking top-of-funnel from first contact through to qualified demo handoff.

-

Qualify inbound leads generated by marketing on a daily basis.

-

Execute multi-channel outreach across phone, email, LinkedIn, and emerging channels.

-

Build and iterate on outreach sequences — form hypotheses, A/B test, measure results, and improve continuously.

-

Shape and improve SDR processes in a fast-paced, high-growth startup environment.

-

Support new customer onboarding and ensure smooth transitions to Account Executives.

-

Operate with end-to-end ownership and receive direct feedback to accelerate your development.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

Native or C2-level English proficiency.

-

1+ years of experience in outbound B2B SaaS sales, top-of-funnel activities, and delivering qualified opportunities.

-

Willingness to temporarily relocate to Vienna, Austria for approximately 1–2 months to immerse in company culture (relocation support provided).

-

Demonstrated ability to qualify leads and hand off demos to Account Executives.

-

Experience running multi-channel outbound outreach (phone, email, LinkedIn) and optimising outreach sequences.

-

Track record of documenting and testing sales hypotheses to improve sequence performance.

-

Experience supporting new customer onboarding and initial adoption.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Familiarity with B2B SaaS sales processes and fast-paced startup environments.

-

Experience with CRM platforms (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce) and sales enablement tooling.

-

Naturally competitive, target-driven, and motivated by uncapped commission potential.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

**Base salary:** $50,000–$75,000 per year (plus uncapped commission)

-

Clear, fast-track promotion path to Account Executive for top performers

-

Direct mentorship and rapid feedback loops in a small, high-ownership team

*Please note: Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.*

### Location

This is an **on-site role based in London, England, UK**. Candidates must also be willing to temporarily relocate to Vienna, Austria (approximately 1–2 months) at the start of employment to onboard and absorb company culture.
