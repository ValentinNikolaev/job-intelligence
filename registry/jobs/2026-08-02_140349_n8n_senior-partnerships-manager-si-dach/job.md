🎯 **Your main goal will be to build and scale n8n’s Service Partner ecosystem across the DACH region, creating a strong network of consulting firms and systems integrators that drives partner-sourced pipeline, revenue growth, and customer success.**

You’ll own the development of high-impact, long-term relationships with regional partners, helping them build successful service offerings around n8n and supporting our growth across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

**Here’s how you’ll make that happen:**

**🔍 Acquire and qualify high-potential DACH partners**

-

Identify, recruit, and onboard consulting firms, boutique systems integrators, and digital agencies across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

-

Build a strong understanding of the regional partner landscape and prioritize firms aligned with our go-to-market strategy.

-

Assess partner-market fit, technical capabilities, customer reach, and commercial potential.

-

Recruit partners motivated to build and scale dedicated automation and AI service offerings around n8n.

-

Develop relationships with senior stakeholders across partner organizations, from founders and practice leads to sales and delivery teams.

**🧠 Enable, support, and scale partnerships**

-

Design and deliver onboarding, enablement, and certification programs that help partners become commercially and technically successful.

-

Support partners in building internal n8n expertise, service packages, and repeatable implementation capabilities.

-

Create reusable resources, including playbooks, toolkits, templates, and best practices, to scale partner operations.

-

Drive partner education across technical, sales, and go-to-market tracks.

-

Help partners move from initial onboarding to active pipeline generation and customer delivery.

**📈 Drive pipeline and co-selling success**

-

Build, track, and grow partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline across the DACH region.

-

Collaborate closely with Sales, Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, and Marketing to identify and activate opportunities.

-

Develop joint account plans and co-selling motions for strategic customers and prospects.

-

Co-own joint business plans with priority partners and ensure they deliver measurable commercial outcomes.

-

Maintain clear visibility into partner performance, pipeline progression, and revenue contribution.

**📣 Activate regional go-to-market initiatives**

-

Plan and execute joint campaigns, webinars, events, and regional initiatives with partners.

-

Build strong relationships with local sales and field teams to ensure partners are embedded within regional go-to-market activity.

-

Co-create German-language content and marketing initiatives that increase awareness, engagement, and demand.

-

Represent n8n at relevant partner, industry, and technology events across the DACH region.

-

Keep the partner ecosystem engaged through regular communication, updates, and community initiatives.

**🌀 Champion partner feedback and continuous improvement**

-

Act as the voice of DACH partners internally, sharing insights that shape future programs, incentives, and product priorities.

-

Identify regional challenges and opportunities, and translate them into scalable improvements.

-

Use partner and performance data to improve the partner experience and increase operational efficiency.

-

Contribute to the evolution of n8n’s global partner program based on regional learnings.

-

Help define what excellent SI partnerships look like as n8n continues to scale.

🌍 **DACH market knowledge:** You have a strong understanding of the German, Austrian, and Swiss technology and consulting ecosystem, with an established network or proven experience developing relationships in the region.

-

-

📈 **Commercial ownership:** You have owned partner-sourced pipeline or revenue targets and know how to move partnerships from relationship-building to measurable commercial results.

-

⚡ **High-growth mindset:** You have thrived in fast-paced technology environments and know how to prioritize, execute, and make decisions in ambiguous situations.

🌐 **Regional network:** You already have relationships with influential consulting firms, boutique SIs, digital agencies, or technology partners across the DACH region.
