# Mid Market Account Executive (m/w/d) - Germany

Posted: 2026-08-05T14:40:30Z

## About Multiverse StackFuel

Multiverse x StackFuel is the leading provider of data and AI upskilling programmes in Germany.

In 2025, StackFuel was acquired by Multiverse and we are now building our GTM engine for the enterprise and mid market, to replicate the UK trajectory in Germany. As a Mid Market Account Executive, you get the best of both worlds: StackFuel’s local expertise and product-market fit combined with Multiverse’s GTM leadership, sales playbooks and resources.

Together with our customers, we are redefining how people learn and work, enabling organisations to successfully navigate AI adoption.

Our mission is to open up new career paths for people around the world through practical, future-proof training in data and AI, while helping organisations build the capabilities they need for the digital age.

In this role as an Mid Market Account Executive, you will play a direct and visible part in advancing this mission with our mid market customers in Germany.

## The role

As an Mid Market Account Executive, you are uniquely positioned to accelerate AI adoption and enablement by delivering a value proposition that sits at the core of German mid market’ transformation strategies:

-

Own a portfolio of strategic mid market accounts in Germany.

-

Be responsible for winning new customers and growing existing ones.

-

Manage the full sales cycle from prospecting to closing.

You’ll work closely with Customer Success, Marketing and Product to bring the best of Multiverse StackFuel to our customers and drive measurable business outcomes for them.

## What you’ll do

-

Ramp up quickly on the Multiverse GTM playbook, supported by market‑leading sales training and a strong development culture.

-

Apply and master the MEDDICC methodology to qualify, manage and scale your pipeline and opportunities.

-

Own new business activities in our most strategic mid market accounts – including building and nurturing relationships with decision-makers at C‑level and C‑2.

-

Deeply understand your prospects’ challenges around digital transformation, capacity, capabilities and diversity – and position Multiverse StackFuel solutions in a way that clearly links to their strategic priorities and business outcomes.

-

Systematically build champions to win new logos, convert upsell and cross-sell opportunities, and develop accounts into strategic key accounts.

-

Build, grow and manage your own portfolio of strategic existing customers with a long-term, value-based approach.
