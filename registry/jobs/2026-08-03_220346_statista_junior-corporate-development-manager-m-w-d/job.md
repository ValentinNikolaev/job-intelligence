# Junior Corporate Development Manager (m/w/d)

Posted: 2026-08-03T20:40:29Z

At **Statista**, we’re all about facts and data, for we are the world's leading business data platform. By providing reliable and easy-to-use data as well as various data analytics products and services, we empower people worldwide to make fact-based decisions.

Founded in Hamburg in 2007, we have quickly grown into a global company with offices in major cities such as London, New York, Berlin and Tokyo. And we still have a lot of plans. Our constant growth does not only prove our success, but also keeps creating new development and career opportunities for our employees.

We value and celebrate our diverse culture. You are welcome here for who you are, no matter where you come from, what you look like, or whether you prefer bar graphs to pie charts. Your story matters – keep writing it as part of our team.

Are you ready to join us?

As a **Junior Manager Corporate Development **you will be working on strategic and contributing to the strategic cadence of the company. We are looking for a strong strategic mind with a hands-on attitude who is eager to take ownership, work closely with senior leadership, and actively shape Statista’s next growth chapters.

***We are currently not actively hiring for this role, but we keep this posting open and will reach out as soon as a suitable position becomes available.***

**YOUR JOB**

**What you do**

-

Act as a central point of contact for all strategically relevant initiatives and topics

-

Facilitate cooperation between different teams to ensure the success of the project

-

Specifying strategic objectives for the coming year and breaking them down into individual areas

-

Carrying out and preparing well-founded market and competition analyses to identify new growth areas

-

Connect the dots between ongoing initiatives, C-level priorities, and market developments, ensuring alignment and timely information flow

-

Preparation of decision papers for the management based on complex information using qualitative and quantitative methods

-

Preparation and realization of workshops with senior management and key stakeholders

**YOUR SKILLS**

**Who you are**

-

Approx. 1-3 years of experience in strategy, start-up, consulting-related role or corporate development (internships or working student roles count)

-

Bachelor’s or master’s degree in business administration, economics, engineering, or a related field

-

Strong analytical skills with the ability to analyze data independently and derive clear conclusions and recommendations from it

-

Ideally basic knowledge of data analytics, as well as the ability to question analyses and optimize them together with the specialist department

-

Understanding of and interest in market and competitive trends

-

Excellent perceptive skills with exceptional organizational and communication skills

-

Ability to work in a team and a positive attitude towards change and continuous improvement processes

-

High level of initiative and commitment to the quality of work results as well as a structured and energetic way of working

-

Excellent English and German language skills
