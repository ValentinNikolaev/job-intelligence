# Sales Intern

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

We are an early-stage **AI marketing technology (martech) startup** based in Munich, Germany, helping brands win visibility in AI-driven search platforms. Our platform enables companies across a wide range of industries — including pharma, medtech, finance, law, and media — to optimize their content for AI search engines and generative AI platforms.

We're looking for an enthusiastic **Sales Intern** to join our small, fast-moving team. You'll work side-by-side with experienced sales professionals, gaining hands-on exposure to B2B sales processes, outreach tools, and pipeline management — all while making a real contribution to the team's goals from day one.

### What You'll Do

-

Identify and qualify inbound and outbound leads to support pipeline growth.

-

Conduct guided outreach to prospective customers via email and phone.

-

Maintain and update CRM records to ensure accurate pipeline management.

-

Prepare sales collateral, presentations, and tailored proposals for prospects.

-

Coordinate meetings and product demos between prospects and account teams.

-

Track sales metrics and generate reports to inform team decisions.

-

Collaborate with marketing and product teams to align messaging and materials.

-

Support social media and content outreach efforts across platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Currently enrolled as a university student (undergraduate or graduate) with internship eligibility in Germany.

-

Authorized to work in Germany — **visa sponsorship is not available**.

-

Proficiency in **German** (conversational to professional level, spoken and written).

-

Strong proficiency in English (verbal and written).

-

Genuine interest in sales, marketing, content creation, or AI/search technology.

-

Familiarity with social media platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, etc.) and basic analytics tools.

-

Excellent communication, teamwork, and presentation skills.

-

Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and a results-oriented mindset.

-

Eagerness to learn quickly and thrive in a fast-paced startup environment.

**Nice to Have:**

-

0–1 years of prior sales, marketing, or customer-facing experience.

-

Familiarity with CRM tools such as Salesforce or HubSpot.

-

Basic video editing or content creation skills.

-

Interest in AI, generative search, or SEO/AEO.

-

Additional languages beyond German and English.

Exposure to a fast-growing sector at the intersection of AI and digital marketing.
