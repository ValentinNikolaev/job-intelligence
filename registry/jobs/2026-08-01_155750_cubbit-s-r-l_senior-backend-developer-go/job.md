Work location:

full remote or hybrid (possibility to work in our offices in Bologna, Italy)

Experience:

high seniority (5+ years of experience in the same role)

Languages requested:

English full proficiency

Compensation:

Base salary €45,000, up to €65,000, depending on your experience, technical expertise, and overall fit for the role. In addition, you'll be eligible to participate in our Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP).
