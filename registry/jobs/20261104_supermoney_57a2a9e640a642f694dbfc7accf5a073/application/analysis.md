## Red Flags

1. **Working model.** The live posting states three office days per week near Termini. The candidate is based in the Rome area, but the preparation profile records a remote preference, not an agreement to this schedule. Ask whether the cadence and commute are acceptable before applying.
2. **Compensation.** The published €45,000–50,000 gross range may or may not meet the candidate's current expectation. The profile still marks that expectation unconfirmed. Avoid entering or promising a number without the candidate's choice.
3. **Framework and language screening.** Confirm PHP/Symfony years, PHP 8+ experience and Italian proficiency before entering the mandatory form answers. No confirmed Italian level is in the candidate profile.
4. **Source drift.** The saved MongoDB `job.md` is a short job alert while the public direct posting has a full specification. If the public text changes, refresh the application context before final submission. Do not treat the stored short excerpt as proof of detailed requirements.
