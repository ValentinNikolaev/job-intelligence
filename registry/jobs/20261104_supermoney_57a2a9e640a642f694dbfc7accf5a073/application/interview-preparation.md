## Technical Interview

**High — PHP/Symfony.** Review PHP object-oriented design, dependency injection, service boundaries, validation, testing and Symfony components. The [posting](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4484679789/) names PHP 8.x and Symfony 5/6/7. State the versions and components actually used, not an inferred match. Rehearse one CRURATED PHP production example without assigning undocumented libraries to it.

**High — APIs and integration.** Practice contract versioning, idempotency, authentication, error handling and retries. Ground actual experience in the Simple App gateway and CRURATED integration. The posting also mentions CRM, ERP and payment gateways; distinguish those examples from unverified direct experience.

**High — MySQL and messaging.** Review transactions, indexes, query plans, schema migration, queue guarantees and duplicate processing. MySQL and RabbitMQ are supported skills. A useful hypothetical exercise is connecting customer inputs to partner offers while keeping asynchronous changes consistent.

**Medium — architecture.** Sketch boundaries for offers, partners, eligibility and customer requests. The posting asks for DDD, but formal DDD experience is not established by the candidate sources. Present this as a design exercise, not a claim about his history or Supermoney's codebase.

**Medium — deployment.** Explain airSlate CI/CD and supported AWS/Kubernetes work. Clarify actual hands-on depth with Docker, GitLab or GCP if asked; the current source base does not establish them. Review rollout, monitoring and rollback for a PHP service.

**Medium — coding.** Practice a small PHP service with explicit validation, domain rules, repository boundary and tests. Offer filtering by customer constraints is a speculative exercise, not a predicted interview task.

**Low — regulated subsidiaries.** The company ownership page identifies insurance and credit entities, but this job does not place the engineer in those systems.

## Preparation Plan

**Before recruiter screening:** decide on the three office days and salary range, confirm availability and language descriptions, and rehearse the overlap and end dates. Explain why a PHP-focused role appeals after recent Go work.

**Before technical interview:** refresh PHP/Symfony versions used, prepare a CRURATED production story, rehearse API-contract and queue design, and identify what the candidate personally decided. Use source-backed MySQL experience; do not add a metric without its basis.

**Before final conversation:** decide what scope, ownership and team collaboration would make the role attractive. Test whether the real work matches the broad posting.
