## Company Description

✨ About Voyage Privé

Born in France in 2006, Voyage Privé has grown from an ambitious startup into becoming Europe’s leading travel tech platform. Operating across 9 markets with tens of millions of users, we're not just another e-commerce success story — we're a tech powerhouse revolutionizing online travel.

What makes us unique? A mission-driven culture where performance meets impact. Our innovative campus brings together tech talent, professional athletes, students, and artists, creating an ecosystem where digital innovation drives both business growth and positive change.

We have reached this scale by staying fully independent, fully profitable, and achieving double-digit growth.

We're now at an inflection point, upgrading our entire technical foundation with cloud architecture, AI, and real-time systems to become a global reference for luxury travel — known by travelers for its excellent offer and customer experience, and by our providers as a high-performance business development partner.

## Job Description

** Your Mission**

As a Software Engineer, you are a product-driven builder embedded in on of our four domains alongside Product Managers, Designers, Data and Business stakeholders.

You design, build and operate software systems that power high-traffic, business-critical travel experiences.

You don’t just ship features — you own systems end to end and contribute directly to measurable business and customer impact.

** What You’ll Do**

**Build high-impact solutions**

-

Develop, test and deploy reliable, scalable software in a high-traffic environment, embedding AI-first capabilities

-

Break down complex problems into pragmatic solutions that deliver value fast

-

Continuously improve performance, maintainability and system reliability

**Own systems end to end**

-

Take responsibility for design, implementation, deployment and production operations

-

Participate in on-call rotations and incident resolution

-

Proactively identify technical debt and improvement opportunities

-

Ensure systems are observable, resilient and ready to scale

**Design robust and scalable architectures**

-

Contribute to architecture decisions with a strong understanding of trade-offs (performance, cost, scalability, security)

-

Design APIs and services that integrate smoothly within a complex travel ecosystem

-

Apply security-by-design principles to protect customers and business operations

**Work with a strong product & data mindset**

-

Collaborate closely with Product Managers to align technical decisions with business goals

-

Use data and feedback to validate impact and guide improvements

-

Enable experimentation, iteration and continuous learning

**Contribute to engineering excellence**

-

Uphold high standards in code quality, testing and documentation

-

Actively participate in code reviews and knowledge sharing

-

Help evolve engineering practices, tooling and team maturity
