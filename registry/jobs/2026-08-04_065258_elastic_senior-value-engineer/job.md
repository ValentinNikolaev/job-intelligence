# **What is The Role**

Are you looking to make a real impact and play a meaningful role in the growth of our company?

Elastic is seeking a Value Engineer based in Germany to support our customers in the region.

Now is an exciting time to join Elastic; you'll be joining at a pivotal stage where your expertise will be instrumental in building the foundations for our future success with our strategic clients. Your role is crucial in ensuring our clients unlock the full business impacts of Elastic, driving value and fostering long-term partnerships.

We’re seeking individuals who thrive in dynamic environments, have a passion for customer excellence, delivering exceptional outcomes, and are ready to collaborate with a team of high-performing sales professionals. If you have experience working as a Value Engineer, Management Consultant, or a related customer facing role, we would love to hear from you!

# **What You Will Be Doing**

- Collaborate closely with account teams to identify and execute value delivery strategies for key accounts.

- Engage with stakeholders to identify, understand, and quantify their unique business challenges. Translate Elastic’s value proposition and technical capabilities into clear economic benefits aligned with their corporate goals.

- Lead business value reviews / business cases: Effectively communicate the realized value and potential economic benefits of new opportunities.

- Conduct thorough analysis of customer workflows and processes, identifying opportunities for optimization and increased efficiency.

- Create and deliver boardroom-quality, executive documents.

- Work collaboratively with product and engineering teams to tailor solutions to meet the specific needs of Elastic’s customers.

- Provide guidance on best practices and innovative approaches to maximize the value of Elastic solutions.

- Act as a subject matter expert on our solutions with our customers, remaining informed about industry trends and advancements.

- Conduct training and workshops with Elastic teammates to increase the quality of their discovery and their understanding of business value & customer excellence.

- Help build the future of the value engineering practice at Elastic: contribute to the development of best practices and tools for an enhanced value based selling adoption.

# **What You Bring**

- 3+ years of experience in value engineering, management consulting, solution architecture, or a related customer-facing role within the software or consulting industries.

- Advanced knowledge and experience in value-based-selling methodologies.

- Demonstrated experience in building and briefing business cases to executives.

- Interest in progressing on the technical side of selling Elastic (systems, process, people from a CTO perspective).

- Expertise in identifying and prioritizing use cases, implementing improvement measures and becoming a change agent for Elastic customers by establishing a value delivery model.

- Ability to project manage across multiple workstreams, including defining scope, expectations, timelines and delivery.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; ability to act decisively with ambiguous guidance and circumstances.

- Based in Germany, with business-fluent German and English.

- Ability to travel to meet clients is required (approximately 2x/month).

# **Bonus Points**

- Experience in cloud infrastructure software (Data+AI, Observability, Security).
