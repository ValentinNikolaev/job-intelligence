# Working Student - Data Migration (all genders) - Fixed-term 2 months

Posted: 2026-08-02T02:30:52Z

Fashion Cloud is a Hamburg-based B2B software company connecting brands and retailers across the fashion industry. We build the digital infrastructure that helps our partners share data, content, and processes more efficiently - with offices in Hamburg and Amsterdam and a close-knit, international team of around 50 people.

As part of an ongoing system migration, we're moving files and data from our legacy infrastructure to a new system, and we're looking for two working students to help us run this process smoothly, reliably, and with a close eye on data quality.

**What you´ll bring **

-

Some hands-on coding experience - the specific language isn't critical; we care more about your ability to read, understand, and reason about code and data

-

Strong critical thinking skills and a methodical, detail-oriented approach to spotting and troubleshooting issues

-

Comfort working with or curiosity about AI agents/tools - prior experience here is a real plus, and the more you have, the better

-

Reliable, independent way of working - you'll be trusted to keep an eye on things without constant supervision

-

Good communication skills in English; German is a plus but not required

-

**Important**: You are currently enrolled as a student (Werkstudent status)

**Your tasks**

-

Run and actively monitor our migration agent throughout the migration period

-

Review migrated files and datasets for completeness, accuracy, and consistency

-

Spot anomalies or errors, document them clearly, and work with the project team to resolve them

-

Help refine and improve the monitoring process as you learn more about the system and typical error patterns

-

Communicate regularly with the project team on progress, blockers, and open questions

-

Support ad-hoc data checks or reporting as needed during the migration window

**What’s in it for you?**

-

€15/hour, paid working student position

20 hours/week, with scheduling that works around your lecture times and exams

Limited to 2 months

Real responsibility on a live migration project - not just observing, but actively owning part of the process

-

Direct exposure to how a B2B SaaS company plans and executes a system migration, including the tooling and workflows involved

-

A friendly, supportive team in our Hamburg office who's happy to answer questions and help you ramp up

-

The chance to build practical experience with data quality checks, monitoring workflows, and AI-assisted tooling - useful skills well beyond this one project
