# Senior/Lead Software Data Engineer (Roads Team)

Posted: 2026-08-02T14:40:41Z

Mapbox is the leading real-time location platform for a new generation of location-aware businesses. Mapbox is the only platform that equips organizations with the full set of tools to power the navigation of people, packages, and vehicles everywhere. More than 4 million registered developers have chosen Mapbox because of the platform’s flexibility, security and privacy compliance. Organizations use Mapbox applications, data, SDKs and APIs to create customized and immersive experiences that delight their customers.

## **What We Do**

Data forms the foundation of everything we do at Mapbox. It is integral to every product we offer, be it maps, navigation, or search, and it directly benefits our customers. The Mapbox Roads team plays a crucial role in constructing comprehensive global road networks for both visual and navigational purposes.

As AI becomes embedded in modern engineering workflows, we value engineers who can thoughtfully integrate AI into design, development, and decision-making. Our interview process will include discussion of how you use AI in your engineering workflow.

## **What You'll Do**

Joining us as a Software Development Engineer, you'll play a key role in one of the aspects of developing software/tech for best-in-class road features, navigation, and high-definition maps. You'll bring your experience and skills to our exciting project within a competent, cross-functional, passionate, and self-organized team.

In this role, you will:

-

Take ownership, improve, scale, and iterate on existing data processing pipelines.

-

Design and implement new data processing pipelines.

-

Collect and monitor performance metrics.

-

Play a key role in discussing and implementing security best practices.

-

8+ years of experience as a Data Engineer.

Experience with AWS services (S3, EC2, IAM, EMR, Glue, Athena, Kinesis) or any other cloud platform.

Creative, resourceful, and innovative problem solver.

Good communication skills in English (B2 level minimum). Mapbox has offices across the globe. You should communicate about complex ideas concisely to an audience worldwide.
