## Strict Hiring Manager Review

The CV should earn confidence on Go ownership, API boundaries, event compatibility, production reliability, and cross-functional delivery. It cannot claim that consumer support or fintech-related integration equals gaming experience. The candidate should address the domain gap through questions about authoritative events, tenancy, reconciliation, and failure handling rather than overstating prior work.

EventBridge and queues must not be presented as Kafka, and REST must not be presented as gRPC. Confirm work authorization and logistics before progressing.

## Interview Focus

The interview preparation directs the candidate to explain Go ownership under variable demand, versioned event contracts, production integration boundaries, and reliable failure handling. The Simple.life example supports a discussion of a support platform operating at least 20,000 tickets in an ordinary month and up to three times that volume in the U.S. peak season. The CRURATED example supports production event-version publication and integration ownership. The airSlate example supports database-load reduction and monitoring-led production fixes. Each example is deliberately framed around the candidate’s contribution, affected system, and documented outcome. The candidate should ask Block Labs which player events are authoritative, where ordering and idempotency are enforced, how tenancy is isolated, and what correctness guarantees apply to balances and player-facing decisions.
