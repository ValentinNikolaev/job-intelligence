# Partnership Manager

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

We're a seed-stage deeptech startup operating at the intersection of physics-informed AI, robotics, and advanced materials science — accelerating the discovery of next-generation materials for the energy sector and beyond. We're looking for a **Partnership Manager** to own the post-signing relationship with our strategic commercial partners and ensure that signed deals translate into real, delivered outcomes and lasting trust.

This is an on-site role based in **Berlin, Germany**. **Visa sponsorship is not available** — candidates must already have the right to work in Germany.

### What You'll Do

-

Own commercial partner success end-to-end and be fully accountable for delivery against agreed outcomes.

-

Serve as the trusted primary counterpart for a portfolio of strategic commercial partners.

-

Clarify and define what success looks like for partners beyond contract language.

-

Advocate for partners internally and represent the company externally with equal clarity and credibility.

-

Drive delivery through influence, not authority — coordinating across AI, chemistry, automation, and lab teams.

-

Translate partner goals into clear scopes, milestones, and priorities.

-

Resolve ambiguity, manage trade-offs, and maintain project momentum across complex technical workstreams.

-

Communicate early and clearly when risks or changes arise to protect trust and keep expectations aligned with reality.

-

Identify opportunities to grow delivered value into deeper, expanded collaboration.

-

Partner closely with Business Development to shape and convert follow-on engagements.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

5+ years of experience in partnerships, consulting, customer success, or account management within complex technical environments.

-

Proven track record of owning end-to-end delivery across multiple stakeholders in technical projects.

-

Experience coordinating cross-functional teams spanning domains such as AI, chemistry, automation, and/or lab environments.

-

Strong stakeholder management skills — able to represent partner interests internally and company interests externally.

-

Ability to translate partner goals into scopes, milestones, and priorities with clear, precise communication.

-

Proactive risk management mindset — surfaces issues early and communicates changes transparently to protect momentum and trust.

-

Technical literacy in deep tech, science, or engineering — comfortable working alongside scientists and engineers.

-

Calm under pressure with a high sense of ownership and accountability.

-

Fluent in English (written and spoken); additional languages are a plus.

-

Based in or able to relocate to Berlin, Germany (on-site role; no visa sponsorship available).

**Nice to have:**

-

Background in materials science, chemistry, cleantech, or adjacent deep-science industries.

-

Experience in early-stage or scaling startup environments.

-

Additional European languages.
