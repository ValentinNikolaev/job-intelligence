**Two venture capital funds. One financial engine room that keeps them running. Ready to help us build it out?**

As our Working Student Finance, you take ownership of the numbers behind our funds and of the systems that turn them into decisions.

You work hand in hand with our team to define what information we need, then build the models and automations that deliver it.

In doing so, you help us invest in outstanding founder and emerging fund manager teams and power the next wave of Deep Tech innovation.

Here, you'll dig into real questions and the calculations behind them, not follow someone else's template: which numbers matter, how to structure them, and how to automate the rest.

## Aufgaben

- **Turn numbers into decisions:** From ad-hoc analyses to quarterly investor reporting and liquidity planning, you keep the calculations accurate and translate their insights into dashboards that drive real decisions.

- **Think in systems:** You see how our data, models, and processes connect, and design every upgrade with the whole picture in mind.

- **Own our financial engine room:** You consolidate, validate, and reconcile the data flowing through it. Google Sheets is our reliable V1 and you help shape what V2 looks like.

- **Build, don't just maintain:** You take our financial data flows and calculation logic to the next level, choosing the right tech together with us and implementing it yourself as our needs grow.

## Qualifikation

- Currently enrolled in a degree program in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, Business Informatics, Data Analytics, or a related field. Ideally at the start of your master's.

- Solid financial know-how, ideally with first practical exposure to Finance Operations or Controlling (internship, working student role, or similar).

- Comfort with complex models; experience with Google Apps Script, other automation tools, or AI-supported workflows is a strong plus.

- Strong hands-on skills in Google Sheets, or Excel skills that transfer quickly, including structured data handling and building clear, user-friendly dashboards.

- An analytical, conceptual mind with a genuine drive to dive deep into the finance side of venture capital.

- Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and a high sense of ownership for data accuracy.

- Fluent written and spoken English (ideally C2 level); German is a plus.

- A rare look behind the scenes of how a venture capital fund actually runs, from raw numbers to the decisions they drive.

- Quick, direct feedback from your team, so your projects move as fast as you do.

- A young, entrepreneurial team where you're a full, integral member from day one.

- A mix of office and home office: this is a hybrid role with regular on-site presence at our Aying headquarters, e.g. 1-2 days per week, as far as your studies allow.
