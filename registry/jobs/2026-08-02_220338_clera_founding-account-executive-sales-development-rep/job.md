# Founding Account Executive / Sales Development Representative

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to join a fast-growing, **Y Combinator–backed conversational AI startup** as their **founding Sales Development Representative** on the Growth team in Berlin. You'll own pipeline generation for a voice AI platform that automates customer call operations for B2C companies — a product with strong, proven market demand.

You won't just be executing a playbook — you'll be helping to write it. This role is built for someone hungry to move fast, do meaningful work, and grow into full-cycle deal ownership within as little as six months.

### What You'll Do

-

Generate qualified pipeline to help 10x revenue over the next year

-

Identify the right prospects and engage them across multiple channels — email, LinkedIn, and phone

-

Build and manage relationships with customers from first touch through handoff

-

Manage pipeline and maintain CRM tooling with discipline and accuracy

-

Shape the GTM motion, build a repeatable sales process, and refine outreach strategies

-

Contribute to hiring and building out the sales team and sales playbook

-

Develop into owning and closing deals end-to-end after demonstrating strong performance

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

2+ years in SDR, BDR, or sales roles delivering qualified pipeline in a startup or fast-moving environment — **this is a dealbreaker requirement**

-

Proven track record of consistently exceeding targets or KPIs in sales roles

-

Hands-on experience with multi-channel outbound outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone) and CRM pipeline management — **dealbreaker**

-

Strong business sense to understand the problems and needs of different companies

-

Experience identifying the right prospects, engaging them, and building lasting customer relationships

-

Willingness to do unglamorous, high-impact work with a scrappy, ownership mindset

-

Openness to growing into full-cycle deal ownership as you demonstrate performance

-

**Fluent German (C2 / near-native)** and strong English (B2–C1) — **dealbreaker**

-

Based in Berlin and willing to work on-site five days a week — **dealbreaker**

**Nice to Have:**

-

Prior experience shaping GTM motions or building repeatable sales processes from scratch

-

Exposure to AI, voice technology, or SaaS products with enterprise or B2C use cases

**Not a fit if you:** lack direct sales experience, prefer remote or minimally collaborative environments, avoid direct customer interaction, or are looking for a purely strategic role.
