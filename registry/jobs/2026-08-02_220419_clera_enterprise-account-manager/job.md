# Enterprise Account Manager

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

We're a seed-stage enterprise AI platform startup founded in Berlin, helping organizations securely adopt and integrate generative AI across their entire operations. Our model-agnostic platform covers AI chat, automated workflows, and custom AI agents — deployed compliantly in Europe — and is trusted by thousands of enterprises across a wide range of industries.

As an **Enterprise Account Manager**, you'll own a book of enterprise accounts and be the driving force behind their AI transformation journeys. You'll work at the intersection of customer success, commercial growth, and product influence — and you'll use AI agents and automation to scale your own work. This is a high-ownership, high-impact role in a small, ambitious team operating fully on-site in Berlin.

### What You'll Do

-

Own a portfolio of enterprise accounts and be directly accountable for retention, adoption, and expansion.

-

Build structured account plans including stakeholder maps, health assessments, growth opportunities, and risk mitigation strategies.

-

Run strategic customer conversations — QBRs, executive business reviews, and proactive check-ins — grounded in data and measurable outcomes.

-

Identify and drive expansion opportunities across users, use cases, and deployment scope.

-

Navigate complex enterprise stakeholder landscapes: find champions, manage blockers, and build multi-threaded relationships from end users to C-level.

-

Build AI agents and automated workflows to manage your own book of business, systematically eliminating manual effort.

-

Work cross-functionally with Solutions Engineering, Product, and Sales to translate customer needs into product improvements and action plans.

-

Serve as the internal voice of the customer while aligning with the company's product direction and roadmap.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

5+ years in a customer-facing enterprise role — Account Management, Customer Success, or consultative post-sales.

-

Proven track record driving adoption, renewals, and expansion in complex enterprise organizations.

-

Hands-on experience building AI agents and automating workflows; comfortable using AI to replace manual work in your own day-to-day.

-

Technical credibility to hold substantive conversations about model selection, data privacy, and integration architectures with technical stakeholders.

-

Strong commercial acumen: you understand the levers of a SaaS business and know how to position and close expansion conversations.

-

Fluency in **German (C1+) and English** — both are required.

-

Ability to work **fully on-site in Berlin** (relocation support available).

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Background at an AI-native company, technical consulting firm, or early-stage startup.

-

Founder or early-employee experience.

-

Portfolio of hands-on AI agent or workflow deployments in enterprise contexts.

-

Experience at firms such as BCG, SAP, Microsoft, PwC, EY, or similar tier.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

Relocation support for candidates moving to Berlin

-

High ownership in a small, fast-moving team shaping how enterprises adopt AI

-
