# Software Engineer (m/w/d)

Who are we?

The innovation mission of NVISO is to leverage technology as a pivotal enabler for pioneering cyber security services that have a positive impact on our customers, brand exposure, and bottom line. The Labs team collaborates with all business units by offering an innovation framework, ambassadorship and in-house expertise to transform the best ideas into impactful outcomes. Building a well-oiled machine where people like to work and inspiring all birds to become internal innovators are at the heart of our mission.

## Tasks

As a Software Engineer in the NVISO Labs team, your responsibilities will include:

- Designing and developing scalable back-end software and services using (mostly) Python.

- Leveraging Azure cloud services such as Functions, App Services, storage, and identity capabilities to build reliable solutions.

- Designing APIs and integrating back-end components with internal and customer-facing platforms where needed.

- Applying sound software engineering practices to deliver maintainable, secure, and production-ready back-end solutions.

- Troubleshooting performance, reliability, and scalability issues across services and cloud-based workloads.

- Identifying bottlenecks and continuously improving the efficiency, resilience, and observability of back-end systems.

## Requirements

- **You hold citizenship in one of the 32 NATO member states.**

- At least **5 years** of experience as a software engineer

- A master's degree in computer science or a related technical field

- High proficiency in developing professional Python and/or C# code

- Passionate about best practices regarding reusability, maintainability, security, and testing

- Thorough understanding of computer systems going beyond application development (including operating systems, networking, infrastructure, etc.)

- Experience with Azure-native development, API design, and cloud-based architectures is a plus

- Excellent English communication skills, both verbal and written

- Ability to work independently and keep track of your priorities

- Strong interpersonal and verbal/written communication skills that enable you to work effectively in a collaborative team environment across the entire company

- Positive, team and mission-oriented attitude

- Ability to prepare and present your work to your colleagues

- Team player who works well under occasional pressure
