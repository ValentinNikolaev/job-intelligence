- **Drive Motion Direction:** Set, define, and pitch the overarching visual and motion direction for key projects—from initial discovery through to final delivery.

- **Advanced Motion & Animation:** Craft high-end motion graphics, custom animated typography, smooth transitions, and visual effects, elevating static assets into dynamic brand experiences.

- **Master Pacing & Retention:** Apply an expert understanding of timing, narrative rhythm, and platform best practices to maximize audience retention and engagement.

- **End-to-End Creative Polish:** Direct the full post-production vision, integrating sound design, music, color grading, and finishing touches to deliver broadcast-quality output.

- **Team Guidance & Quality Control:** Guide and review the work of other motion designers, ensuring strict adherence to project visions, client brand identities, and high craft standards.

- **Pioneer Workflows & AI Tools:** Stay ahead of industry trends, emerging technologies, and AI tools, integrating innovative techniques to elevate team workflows and creative output.

**WHAT YOU BRING:**

- **Visual Judgment & Creative Leadership:** Strong aesthetic vision and confidence in shaping concepts, setting creative direction, and guiding other motion designers on the team.

- **Art Direction Mindset:** Proven experience as a Motion Designer with an Art Director’s eye for branding, design quality, and overarching visual storytelling.

- **Excellence in Craft:** Solid, high-level motion design skills with a fine-tuned sense of timing, framing, pacing, and narrative rhythm.

- **Styleframes & Visual Systems:** Hands-on confidence in creating high-end styleframes, scalable visual systems, and narrative-driven motion concepts.
