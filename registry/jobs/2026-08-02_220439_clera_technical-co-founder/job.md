# Technical Co-Founder

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

A Berlin-based pre-seed AI SaaS startup is looking for a **Technical Co-Founder** to join as the third member of the founding team. The company is building an AI-powered platform that automates the end-to-end lifecycle of public procurement participation — from tender discovery and requirements analysis through to compliant concept and proposal generation — targeting the DACH region. This role carries full technical ownership and a direct impact on product direction, architecture, and go-to-market traction.

The platform is built on LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and NLP, and must be fully GDPR-compliant, hosted in EU cloud environments. Incorporation is in preparation and a public funding application is currently in progress.

### What You'll Do

-

Design and own the overall system architecture, with a focus on GDPR-compliant EU cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS Bedrock, Vertex AI — Frankfurt region).

-

Build the core AI engine that generates complete, compelling proposal concepts from structured company data.

-

Develop and maintain a reference database for past projects, certificates, and CVs, and integrate them dynamically into generated concepts.

-

Implement entity resolution, reliability scoring, and confidence quantification across data pipelines.

-

Work deeply in AI/ML (LLMs, RAG, NLP) and/or broadly across full-stack product engineering — depending on your strengths.

-

Set technical priorities collaboratively within a small founding team.

-

Contribute to business strategy during the pre-seed phase alongside the other co-founders.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

5+ years of experience in product/engineering roles delivering AI-powered software with LLM, NLP, and/or RAG capabilities.

-

Hands-on experience with GDPR-compliant architecture in EU cloud environments (data handling for NLP/LLM workloads).

-

Prior experience as a technical founder or technical lead who has built an AI-first product from zero with end-to-end ownership.

**Required:**

-

Strong, production-grade full-stack development skills for AI-enabled products.

-

Practical experience integrating LLMs, RAG pipelines, and NLP into real products.

-

Genuine founder mentality — you want to help build a company, not just work through tickets.

-

Willingness to join as a technical co-founder with equity as the primary compensation during the pre-seed phase.

-

German language proficiency (the product operates in the DACH regulatory context: VgV, HOAI, etc.).

**Strongly preferred:**

-

Experience with enterprise data pipelines and secure data handling in EU cloud environments (DSGVO/GDPR context).

-

Familiarity with German public procurement law or the architectural/planning office domain.

-

Pragmatic, ship-first working style — you iterate quickly and avoid over-engineering.
