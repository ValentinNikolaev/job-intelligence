# Interview Preparation — Mineo

## Recruiter / HR Screening

Open with a backend proposition: Go and PHP services, integration design and production ownership. Explain that the CV deliberately shows the experience the candidate can defend. The first conversation should resolve how much of the position is frontend work and whether the company expects immediate independent React/TypeScript delivery. The candidate evidence does not establish that capability or a full-stack tenure claim. Ask how the role is evaluated and whether there is a backend-focused route. Do not describe a prospective learning plan as previous production experience.

Rome fits the advertised CET working window. Confirm the actual employment or contracting entity, working hours, compensation and equipment arrangements. The posting requests a fast start, but current availability needs a fresh answer. Professional upper-intermediate English is supported; prepare a technical explanation aloud and let the conversation establish whether it meets the employer’s fluent-English expectation. Explain the part-time CRURATED overlap directly. Preserve Simple.life’s completed role and do not let project completion after the employment period become an invented extension.

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview

- How do you work with a product designer when backend constraints affect a feature? Use a real interface or product-collaboration example and distinguish your backend decisions from the designer’s work.
- Tell us about simplifying a system so others could extend it. The vendor-neutral helpdesk gateway supplies contracts and adapter boundaries; do not invent adoption beyond its demonstrated result.
- Describe a production delivery involving security stakeholders. Use the telephony review and rollout with backend, frontend and SRE colleagues.
- How do you balance delivery and maintainability? Use consolidation of duplicated analytics-to-support flows and the removed operating restrictions.
- Tell us about a cross-functional plan. Use airSlate roadmaps and release responsibility, explaining what you coordinated and what others decided.
- Describe owning an external integration. Use CRURATED’s production account and collection integration, including the verification boundary.
- What would you do if a role expected frontend skills you had not used professionally? Answer honestly about the gap and ask what support and ramp-up would be available.

Prepare concise STAR outlines with the real situation, personal contribution and documented result. Unknown commercial impact can remain unknown. A strong story does not need a fabricated percentage, user count or dramatic conflict.

## Technical Interview

High Priority: Go service design, HTTP/API boundaries, concurrency, context cancellation, error propagation and testing. Practise a small handler and worker flow with a deterministic fake dependency. Explain the observable behaviour under cancellation and failure before choosing abstractions. Prepare to justify where state belongs, how contracts evolve and how repeated requests are handled. Use supported API and gateway experience without claiming every protocol named in the advertisement.

High Priority: distributed-service reliability, tenant isolation, queue processing and database design. Rehearse event delivery, duplicates, backpressure and safe retries. Use CRURATED and the helpdesk gateway as experience anchors. Ask whether peer-to-peer communication means an established protocol or a broader product feature; do not assume WebRTC expertise. Be ready to trace a request through storage, an asynchronous consumer and an external integration, distinguishing a failed request from an uncertain external side effect.

High Priority: performance analysis and security. Describe the airSlate database bottleneck qualitatively and how workload redistribution improved stability. For telephony, explain the reviewed privacy boundaries and lawful-calling behaviour without exposing internal identifiers. Discuss how access checks, logs and vendor calls fit together. Do not reuse the withdrawn airSlate migration as infrastructure evidence.

High Priority as a fit screen: frontend fundamentals and the exact SPA stack. The candidate must identify what is actually known before an assessment. If the employer requires production React, Redux, TypeScript and HTML/CSS immediately, preparation cannot manufacture that experience. A learning exercise can show current reasoning but should be labelled as an exercise. Medium Priority: UI/API contract collaboration and cross-platform compatibility, explored from the backend side. Low Priority: recruitment-algorithm theory unless the team confirms that the role owns those algorithms rather than application engineering around them.

## CV Deep-Dive Questions

For the gateway, prepare the event model, adapter separation, tenant boundaries, deployment and what the B2B demonstration showed. Explain which parts you personally designed and which depended on colleagues. For telephony, outline the review sequence and the difference between architecture ownership and formal security approval. For GDPR work, distinguish exports from deletion and request cancellation. For the evaluation pipeline, explain the conversation, action and subscription-state records and what evaluation they enabled. Do not claim that the pipeline established a quantified quality improvement unless separately supported.

For CRURATED, explain why the engagement is labelled concurrent part-time consulting, what full technical ownership meant and which integrations ran in production. For airSlate, describe release-cycle improvements and planning responsibility without presenting all team results as individual coding. Hyprr’s prototype-to-beta progression can support delivery judgment, while detailed product adoption remains unknown. Date-only older roles are chronology entries, not an invitation to invent extra stories.

## Company-Specific Preparation

The [selected advertisement](https://himalayas.app/companies/mineo/jobs/senior-fullstack-developer-go-golang-remote) describes a recruitment platform using AI. The candidate can discuss dependable integration boundaries and evaluated automation as transferable engineering abilities. Do not claim prior use of Mineo, recruitment-domain experience or personal familiarity with its founders. The linked external site timed out during research, so confirm the product and legal employer in conversation. Keep questions focused on the actual team and workload rather than importing assumptions from the company-profile summary.

## Preparation Plan

Must prepare: backend introduction, exact frontend evidence boundary, current availability and a question about the work split. Before a technical stage, review Go concurrency, API design, database bottlenecks and queue-delivery semantics, and rehearse a small failure-oriented exercise. Ask for the assessment scope before spending substantial time learning a guessed frontend framework. Before a final or culture discussion, prepare one cross-functional planning example and clarify how backend and frontend engineers share decisions. Keep a private list of unsupported technologies so confidence in adjacent areas does not turn into an inaccurate answer.

## Questions to Ask

- What share of the first months is frontend implementation?
- Which frontend skills must be productive from day one?
- Can a senior backend engineer own a defined platform area?
- What does peer-to-peer communication mean in this product?
- Who owns matching algorithms versus the application around them?
- How do product and engineering decide API and UI boundaries?
- What is the legal hiring arrangement for someone based in Italy?
- How are the technical assessment and first deliverables structured?
