## UI/UX Designer (f/m/d)

We are looking for a UI/UX Designer to work on our hit game ‘Idle Bank Tycoon’.
You will be part of our IBT Team, contributing to a game with over 60 million downloads. In this role, you will design screens, flows, and prototypes, conduct playtests, and refine user-centric designs. Working in an agile, fast-paced environment, you’ll help identify and develop future features by gaining a deep understanding of our player needs and motivations. By leveraging data, player insights, and F2P expertise, you’ll ensure the game remains intuitive, visually polished, and highly engaging - always keeping the player experience at the heart of every decision.

**Responsibilities**

-

Work closely with product and cross-functional teams to enhance player experiences

-

Create concepts, prototypes, user flows, and mockups for existing and new features

-

Design and maintain UI Assets, icons, interactive elements, and UI/UX documentation

-

Use player feedback, data, and usability testing to refine designs

-

Proactively propose and evaluate design improvements with product impact in mind.

-

Be an active contributor in improving design team processes

-

Conduct surveys and research, translating player insights into design actions

-

Organize UI layouts and content hierarchies for usability, consistency, and navigation

**Required skills**

-

Prior experience in the gaming industry

-

Experience in interface design and user flows, showcased by your portfolio

-

Good understanding of design and perception principles

-

Strong visual design skills, capable of creating low and high fidelity mockups

-

Proficiency in Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, or similar design tools

-

Skilled in creating wireframes, user flows, prototypes, and conducting user tests

-

Excellent communication and experience working in a fast-paced environment

-

Actively plays mobile games and keeps up with industry trends

-

Player-centric mindset

**Desirable skills**

-

Basic knowledge of game design, human psychology, and behavioral science.

-

Experience in organizing and conducting usability tests and player research

-

Experience in creating and implementing UI assets and animations in Unity.
