# Engineering Manager - Automation Platform (x/f/m)

Posted: 2026-07-28T11:56:10Z

We are looking for an **Engineering Manager** to join Doctolib's **Automation Platform** (AUTOP) team, which empowers all Doctolib engineers to build, test, and deploy software with confidence and speed.

As part of the Platform organization, Autop’s mission is to **build and maintain the automation infrastructure and developer tools** that enable our engineering teams to deliver high-quality software efficiently. Every feature shipped at Doctolib — from appointment booking to teleconsultation to AI-powered clinical tools — flows through AUTOP's automation infrastructure. We're the platform behind the platform. When product teams ship faster, safer, and with more confidence, it's because AUTOP built the rails they run on.

AUTOP (Automation Platform) is responsible for critical developer-facing systems including: CI/CD automation, testing infrastructure (unit, integration, E2E, contract, visual regression), local and ephemeral development environments, release management, dependency management, and developer productivity tooling.

###

### **What you will do**

As the Engineering Manager for AUTOP, your responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

#### People leadership

- Build and lead a high-performing team of 6 engineers who are passionate about developer productivity;

- Create a culture where engineers own outcomes, not tasks — where platform adoption and developer satisfaction are the north stars;

- Recruit, mentor, and retain top platform engineering talent in a competitive market;

- Conduct impactful 1:1s, performance reviews, and career development conversations that help engineers reach their full potential.

#### Technical Strategy & Execution

- Define and drive the technical vision for your team's domain (CI/CD, testing infrastructure, developer environments, or release automation);

- Make high-impact technical decisions that balance short-term delivery with long-term sustainability and scale;

- Set the bar for automation, quality, reliability, and security — your systems must be highly reliable because 800 engineers+ depending on them daily;

- Champion a "Platform as a Product" mindset: treat internal developers as customers, measure adoption and satisfaction, iterate based on feedback;

- Partner strategically with product teams, Infrastructure Platform, and Applicative Platform to solve cross-cutting challenges;

- Drive measurable improvements in developer productivity metrics: build times, deployment frequency, test reliability, time-to-production… supporting Doctolib teams to monitor their metrics (DORA, performance, costs, etc).

#### Impact & Influence

- Be a visible technical leader across the organization — your team's work enables everyone else's success;

- Influence engineering culture: establish best practices, contribute to technical strategy beyond your team;

- Navigate ambiguity and lead through influence, not authority — platform adoption requires trust and advocacy, not mandates.

**About our tech environment**

- Our automation platform supports a hybrid architecture: a large Ruby on Rails monolith and an expanding ecosystem of modern microservices (Java, Kotlin, TypeScript)

- We maintain sophisticated CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, testing frameworks across multiple languages, and infrastructure-as-code solutions

- Our systems handle thousands of CI builds daily, manage comprehensive test suites across multiple languages, and manage deployments across multiple environments for 200+ development teams

- Our stack includes: Ruby, Java, Kotlin, Go, TypeScript, Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, Terraform, and various testing frameworks

- We intentionally leverage AI to improve developer productivity and reliability, in a sustainable manner .
