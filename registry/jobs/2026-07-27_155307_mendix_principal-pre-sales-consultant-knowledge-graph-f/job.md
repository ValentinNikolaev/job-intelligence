**Position Overview**

We are seeking a Principal Pre-Sales Consultant to help enterprise customers navigate AI transformation, connecting business challenges to Siemens' end-to-end digital, data, and AI capabilities. You'll operate at the intersection of deep technical mastery and strategic storytelling, shaping how the world's most ambitious companies unlock the power of connected data. This role is at the forefront of transforming global leading companies into agentic enterprises by defining, scaling, and reinforcing their semantic layer through our Knowledge Graph platform.

**Key Responsibilities**

-

Lead technical discovery sessions with enterprise customers, assessing their Data & AI infrastructure, AI/ML maturity, and uncover strategic opportunities to position Siemens’ Knowledge Graph platform as the foundation for enterprise-grade Agentic and Generative AI

-

Own the technical relationship throughout the sales cycle, identifying high-value business cases for graph, architect scalable graph-based solutions, and provide hands-on guidance during customer evaluations.

-

Plan and orchestrate the delivery of strategic Proof-of-Value activities with clients

-

Collaborate with the product teams, you will also feed real-world customer insights back into the product roadmap, ensuring RapidMiner's graph capabilities stay ahead of market demand

-

Demonstrate how Graph Studio & Graph Lakehouse are the foundation of an agentic enterprise and enhance LLM accuracy, reduce hallucinations, and enable agents

-

Lead the creation of comprehensive business impact demonstrations

-

Advise C-suite and technical stakeholders on Data-and-AI strategy and roadmap

**Required:**

-

Minimum of 5 years experience in Pre-Sales, sales engineering, solution architecture, or technical consulting

-

Deep understanding of knowledge graphs, semantic web technologies, graph databases, or data fabrics

-

Strong communication skills, explaining complex problems in simple terms

-

Ability to influence executives and guide enterprise-scale AI adoption

-

Full professional proficiency in German and English (written and spoken)

**Preferred:**
