# Full Stack Engineer

Posted: 2026-07-19T00:07:29Z

### Description

We are seeking a **Senior Full Stack Engineer **to join our Development team. In this role, you will design, develop, and maintain scalable web applications, working across both frontend and backend technologies. You will collaborate closely with Product, QA, UX/UI, and other engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions while contributing to technical design, architecture, and best practices.

### Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain scalable full-stack applications using .NET and React.
- Build high-quality, reusable, and maintainable code for both backend and frontend components.
- Develop and integrate RESTful APIs and backend services.
- Collaborate with Product, UX/UI, QA, and other development teams to deliver end-to-end solutions.
- Participate in system design, architecture discussions, and technical decision-making.
- Optimize application performance, scalability, and security.
- Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve technical issues across the full stack.
- Work with Docker, deployment pipelines, and monitoring tools to support reliable application delivery and operations.
- Conduct code reviews and mentor junior developers.
- Follow Agile development methodologies and software development best practices

### Requirements

- 5+ years of experience in full-stack software development.
- Strong experience with .NET / C#.
- Strong experience with React and modern JavaScript/TypeScript.
- Experience building and consuming REST APIs.
- Experience with SQL databases.
- Experience with Docker, deployment configurations, and monitoring tools.
- Experience with Git and CI/CD workflows.
- Strong understanding of object-oriented design principles and software architecture.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively in a cross-functional team.
- Good written and spoken English.
