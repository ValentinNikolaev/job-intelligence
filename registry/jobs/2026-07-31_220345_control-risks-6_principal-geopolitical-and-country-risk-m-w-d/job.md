# Principal, Geopolitical and Country Risk (m/w/d)

Posted: 2026-07-31T20:40:05Z

As Principal you will lead and grow Control Risks’ Europe & CIS Geopolitical & Country Risk (GCR) practice, providing strategic direction, client leadership, and operational excellence as part of a global team of over 100 analysts and consultants. The role combines practice leadership with hands-on delivery of complex advisory engagements for senior decision-makers at multinational companies and other major clients. Ideally you will be based in Berlin but we will also consider applicants based in London.

Key responsibilities include:

**Leadership of the GCR Europe & CIS Practice**

- Lead the strategy, growth, and performance of the Europe & CIS GCR practice, ensuring revenue, profitability, and delivery targets are achieved.
- Oversee complex, high-value consulting engagements, including cross-service line and multi-regional projects involving senior client stakeholders.

- Support analytical leadership on political, regulatory, and security developments across the region.
- Drive innovation through the development and rollout of new products, services, and solutions.

**People Leadership**

- Lead, develop, and mentor the Europe & CIS team through effective performance management, coaching, and role-modelling collaborative leadership and wellbeing.

**Business Development**

- Meet personal and team sales origination targets through client engagement, thought leadership, conference participation, and development of integrated solutions.
- Build and maintain senior client relationships, strengthen market positioning, and identify new opportunities across existing and prospective clients.

**Business Management**

- Manage the practice efficiently, maintaining financial discipline, target margins, and robust operational oversight.

**Brand & Market Presence**

- Represent Control Risks externally through media, conferences, thought leadership, and social media engagement.

**Risk Management**

- Protect the firm's reputation by ensuring appropriate risk assessment, governance, and escalation of potential issues.

**Requirements**

- Master’s degree or equivalent (in relevant subject matter).
- Minimum of 10 years of full-time professional experience in winning and delivering political risk intelligence and advisory consulting on highly regulated, policy-sensitive sectors, such as energy, infrastructure, defence, telecoms, healthcare/life sciences, financial services and/or digital technologies.
- Access to a large network of reliable, credible sources in European policy communities, think tanks, ministries, security agencies and from the business community.
- Excellent communication, listening and negotiation skills in English and preferably at least one other European language, ideally German.
- Experience in public speaking, including larger audiences (100+)
- Advanced analytical and report writing skills in English (and preferably one other European language).
- Excellent knowledge and demonstrated experience in applying methodologies for political risk analysis (i.e. threat assessments, risk assessments, scenario development and planning); ability to develop bespoke methodologies.
- Excellent knowledge of risk management frameworks and understanding of how private businesses manage both strategic and operational risks in practice.
- Demonstrable experience selling consulting services to international clients.
- Strong project management skills & demonstrable ability to multi-task and manage multiple commitments under time pressure.
- Demonstrable commercial acumen when planning and pricing projects.
- Willingness to travel for work.
