# Account Executive – Central Europe (DACH)

- Strong work ethic: self-motivated with an ability to learn quickly and hold yourself accountable to hitting quota

- Clear and concise communication skills, as well as effectively listen with both clients and internal business partners

- Fluent written and oral in German and English

**Experience nice to have:**
