# Solution Engineer | DACH

SoSafe has the ambition to become the leading human risk management provider in Europe. Our award-winning awareness platform triggers behavioural change by providing effective and engaging training and simulations on cybersecurity and data protection. Cybercrime is costing the world >$10 trillion annually and growing by 15% p.a. - we invite you to be part of the solution!

**Location**
This role is open to candidates based in Germany, Ireland, and the UK.
**Ireland & UK:** Fully remote.
**Germany**: Choose the way you work best—either fully remote or hybrid from one of our offices in Berlin, Munich, or Cologne.

As a Senior Solutions Engineer, you'll own the technical pre-sales process for our Enterprise customers, partnering closely with Account Executives to help customers understand how SoSafe can solve their security challenges.
You'll lead technical discovery, deliver tailored product demonstrations, validate technical requirements, and guide customers through security and procurement processes. Working cross-functionally with Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Product Marketing, you'll play a key role in winning complex enterprise opportunities and ensuring customers have confidence in our solution.
