# Lead PHP/Laravel Engineer — Product-Focused

Posted: 2026-09-07

### About the Role

Our client is a leading European company providing solutions for product, material, and logistics cycles, with a strong focus on recycling and sustainability.

We are looking for a Lead Engineer to help shape and build an enterprise multi-tenant SaaS platform. You’ll work closely with Product Managers, Product Owners, and Product Designers, contributing not only to technical implementation but also to product decisions.

You’ll help translate complex business and customer needs into scalable technical solutions, challenge assumptions, and balance user value, business impact, and technical complexity.

### What You’ll Do

- Design and develop web applications and services using PHP, Laravel, SQL, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.
- Work closely with Product and Design to turn business and customer needs into effective solutions.
- Contribute to product and technical decisions, bringing engineering perspective and alternative solutions.
- Challenge requirements constructively and help evaluate trade-offs.
- Take ownership of technical solutions from idea to implementation.
- Break down complex problems and drive solutions independently.
- Evolve the multi-tenant SaaS platform, architecture, APIs, and engineering practices.
- Develop and maintain Laravel-based logistics applications and Magento-based web shop solutions.
- Mentor engineers and contribute to technical discussions and initiatives.

### What We’re Looking For

- Comfortable challenging requirements and discussing technical trade-offs.

You won’t just receive tickets. You’ll work directly with Product and Design and have a real voice in how the product evolves.

Your technical opinion matters. We value engineers who can challenge assumptions, explain trade-offs, and use data and evidence to make better decisions.
