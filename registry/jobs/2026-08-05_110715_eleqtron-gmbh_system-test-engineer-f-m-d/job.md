# System Test Engineer (f/m/d)

Posted: 2026-08-05T08:30:16Z

**eleQtron** develops and operates full-stack quantum computers based on trapped-ion technology. Quantum computing is a new computing paradigm rapidly approaching commercial viability. Our unique approach combines laser-cooled trapped ions with microwave control and tailored magnetic fields. Our next-generation computing machines under development will be able to find solutions to problems that are out of reach today, thus enabling new innovative products and services for a brighter future.

**We are looking for an RF Test Engineer (m/f/d) for our Siegen location to test, characterise, and validate RF and microwave systems that enable the operation of our next-generation trapped-ion quantum computing platforms.**

**Responsibilities:**

As an RF Test Engineer in the Electronic Engineering Team, you will play a key role in testing, validating, and integrating the RF systems which support our next-generation quantum computing platforms. In this role, you will be part of an international team of physicists and engineers to develop, characterise, debug, and optimise high-frequency hardware systems in advanced laboratory environments.

-

Execute and develop validation & characterisation protocols for RF systems

-

Take ownership of RF testing and validation activities, supporting the prototyping, integration, debugging, and validation of RF components and subsystems

-

Contribute technical expertise and validation insights toward the development of next-generation quantum computing platforms

-

Contribute to improving lab infrastructure, RF testing methodologies, and automation workflows

-

Support the operation team during validation & characterization of our quantum computing platform with hands-on expertise of RF systems

**Required Skills:**

-

Bachelor’s or master's degree in Electrical or Electronic Engineering or a related technical field

-

Hands-on experience with RF measurement equipment (VNA, Spectrum Analyser, Oscilloscope, Signal generator)

-

Strong understanding of RF testing, validation, characterisation, and debugging methodologies for electronic systems

-

Excellent organizational skills

-

Structured, analytical working style with strong ownership and execution focus

-

Fluent English

**We value: **

-

Experience with Python or a similar high-level scripting language for data analysis

-

Experience with RF Design
