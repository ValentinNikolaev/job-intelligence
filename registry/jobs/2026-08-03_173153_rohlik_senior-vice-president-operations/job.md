# Senior Vice President Operations

Posted: 2026-08-03T15:01:03Z

**Rohlik** is one of Europe’s leading online grocery businesses, operating in the Czech Republic, Hungary, Austria, Germany and Romania through [Rohlik.cz](http://Rohlik.cz), [Kifli.hu](http://Kifli.hu), [Gurkerl.at](http://Gurkerl.at), [Knuspr.de](http://Knuspr.de) and [Sezamo.ro](http://Sezamo.ro).

By owning our end-to-end operations and developing our technology in-house, we are able to deliver a customer experience built around freshness, speed, reliability and a broad assortment of both local and international products.

As we continue to scale, we are looking for a **Senior Vice President Operations** to lead the next stage of our operational development across Europe.

This is one of the most critical executive roles in the company, combining full ownership of the performance, scalability and continuous improvement of Rohlik’s end-to-end operations across all markets with a highly visible, hands-on mandate that requires moving confidently between group strategy and detailed operational performance.

## **Why this role is exciting**

-

You will lead the operational agenda of one of Europe’s most ambitious and complex e-grocery businesses.

-

You will have a direct impact on growth, profitability and customer experience across multiple countries.

-

You will shape how our operations scale, from fulfillment and supply chain to last-mile delivery and customer operations.

-

You will lead both operational performance and long-term transformation, including automation, technology and new-market readiness.

-

You will influence the daily experience of millions of customers and thousands of employees across Europe.

## **What you will own and deliver**

You will take full ownership of Rohlik’s group-wide operations strategy and execution, ensuring that our operating model delivers consistent performance today while remaining ready for future growth.

Your scope will include:

-

Leading end-to-end operations across all markets

-

Aligning and leading local COOs to deliver consistently high standards

-

Driving performance across fulfillment centres, supply chain, warehousing, last-mile delivery and customer operations

-

Owning group-wide operational P&L performance and driving year-on-year improvements in productivity, efficiency and cost

-

Establishing clear group-wide standards across safety, quality, reliability, customer satisfaction and operational performance

-

Ensuring consistent execution and aligned KPIs across all markets

-

Building scalable operations that support growth without compromising customer experience

-

Ensuring new markets, fulfillment centres and operational capabilities launch on time and are ready to scale

-

Partnering with the CEO Retail and the Executive Leadership Team on company strategy, growth and expansion

-

Providing operational input into financial planning, budgeting and investment decisions

-

Leading major transformation, automation and continuous-improvement initiatives

-

Strengthening business continuity, risk management and regulatory compliance

-

Building a culture of accountability, innovation, ownership and customer obsession

-

Developing and inspiring senior operational leaders across countries and functions

-

Strengthening leadership capability and employee engagement across operational teams

Success in this role will be visible through stronger operational P&L performance, consistent execution across markets, measurable improvements in productivity and cost, high levels of safety and customer satisfaction, and an operating model that can scale with Rohlik’s continued growth.
