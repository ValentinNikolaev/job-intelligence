# **People operations & payroll - Working Student (Berlin)**

**Berlin, Germany | Working student | Fixed-term**

Join our Northern Europe People Enablement team and gain hands-on experience in a regional HR function at SumUp. As a Working Student, you'll support People Operations in Germany, helping manage contracts, HR administration, benefits, HR systems and payroll processes. It's an opportunity to build practical HR experience while contributing to a team that's shaping how People Operations are delivered across five countries.

🎥 Get a feel for our Berlin office:[ Berlin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9DG64C3moeQ)

## **What you'll do**

- Handle employee queries via our People team service desk and keep HRIS data accurate in Workday

- Manage employment contract administration, including contract creation, amendments, and signature tracking

- Manage benefits administration end to end — sign-ups, Workday updates, payroll communication, and invoices

- Organise, digitise, and file incoming physical post and sensitive employee documents

- Support the payroll team with data accuracy tasks and basic Excel-based reporting where capacity allows

## **You'll be great for this role if…**

- Fluency in both German and English (written and verbal) — essential for day-to-day communication and document handling

- Strong attention to detail and a high level of accuracy when managing confidential information

- Comfortable working with Excel, including pivot tables and VLOOKUP

- Ability to manage multiple recurring tasks independently, with strong time management and organisational skills

- Currently enrolled in university for at least three semesters, with a proactive mindset and a genuine interest in People Operations or HR

You'll work alongside experienced People professionals, gain exposure to regional HR operations across multiple countries, and build valuable experience in the fast-growing fintech industry
