## Vacancy Summary

Nova Digital is hiring a Lead Backend PHP Developer for its R&D department, whose
mission is fast validation of business hypotheses through PoC and MVP work, followed
by production hardening. The company describes itself as the technological backbone of
the NOVA ecosystem, handling 50+ million requests daily for 10+ million users. The role
wants PHP 7/8 with Laravel or Symfony, PostgreSQL and RabbitMQ, REST/gRPC/WebSockets,
DDD and microservice architecture, Docker/Kubernetes, GCP plus on-premise
infrastructure, AI-orientation (LLM API awareness), and confident Ukrainian with
English at B1.

## Company Research

The posting itself is the primary source: it states Nova Digital's scale directly
("50+ млн запитів щодня," "10+ млн активних користувачів") and describes the R&D
team's mission in its own words. No separate company website research was performed
for this pass; company context is drawn entirely from the posting, which the candidate
should supplement with direct questions during the recruiter screen about the specific
PoC/MVP projects currently active.

## Initial Resume Audit

The previous CV led with CRURATED although the Simple.life work ended later. It also
used five CRURATED bullets while repeating the same tenfold DataLake outcome twice;
airSlate had only two and PDFfiller one. The updated CV restores reverse chronology
and gives Simple.life five distinct recent examples: a multi-tenant gateway that
enabled a B2B demo, production LLM support-agent work and its evaluation pipeline,
an outbound-calling backend that passed security sign-off, consolidation of three

at CRURATED remains clearly labelled as a part-time subcontract. The candidate has
confirmed PHP in the Simple.life technology mix but no specific PHP achievement in
that role; the bullets do not relabel Go work as PHP. The later source conflicts on
title and exact end month, so the CV retains the earlier confirmed Software
Developer title and gives only the end year 2026.

## Strict Hiring Manager Review

A hiring manager can now see a genuinely recent backend product story and current
PHP depth together. The new Simple.life gateway bullet answers the PoC/MVP request
with a documented first B2B demo; the LLM agent bullet provides direct AI integration
evidence rather than vague cross-team proximity. CRURATED shows an event system in

reliability. Hyprr adds a CTO-level core-stack decision and separate backend work on

gRPC and WebSockets. AWS/Kubernetes and REST are relevant but do not prove those
specific technologies. The letter uses two stories, CRURATED and Hyprr, rather than
replaying all roles.

## Red Flags

The concurrent, part-time CRURATED engagement is disclosed explicitly. The newer
Simple.life block says Senior Backend Engineer and extends into September 2026;
earlier direct career clarification says Software Developer ending in July. The
candidate later authorized treating August–September work as past experience but
did not settle the exact final month or formal title. The CV preserves the confirmed
title and an end year. The Ukrainian-language letter matches the posting, but a
written letter alone is not proof of a specific spoken-language proficiency level.

## Cross-Document Consistency Check

The revised CV shows real LLM-agent backend work from the 2026 candidate-supplied
block; older interview guidance that says the candidate has no hands-on LLM work
must be changed. The CRURATED tenfold and 99.9% figures have candidate-reviewed

not NOVA's request volume. The updated letter does not imply the candidate has
handled NOVA's platform scale. No application document should present the newer
Simple.life estimates as measured outcomes.
