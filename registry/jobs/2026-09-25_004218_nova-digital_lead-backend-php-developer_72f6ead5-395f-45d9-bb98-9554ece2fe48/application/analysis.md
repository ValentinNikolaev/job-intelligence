## Vacancy Summary

Nova Digital is hiring a Lead Backend PHP Developer for its R&D department, whose
mission is fast validation of business hypotheses through PoC and MVP work, followed
by production hardening. The company describes itself as the technological backbone of
the NOVA ecosystem, handling 50+ million requests daily for 10+ million users. The role
wants PHP 7/8 with Laravel or Symfony, PostgreSQL and RabbitMQ, REST/gRPC/WebSockets,
DDD and microservice architecture, Docker/Kubernetes, GCP plus on-premise
infrastructure, AI-orientation (LLM API awareness), and confident Ukrainian with
English at B1.

## Company Research

The posting itself is the primary source: it states Nova Digital's scale directly
("50+ млн запитів щодня," "10+ млн активних користувачів") and describes the R&D
team's mission in its own words. No separate company website research was performed
for this pass; company context is drawn entirely from the posting, which the candidate
should supplement with direct questions during the recruiter screen about the specific
PoC/MVP projects currently active.

The CV leads with the CRURATED PHP subcontract engagement (Aug 2024 to Jan 2026),

## Strict Hiring Manager Review

A hiring manager would see a candidate who has both recent PHP production ownership
and direct technical-lead experience defining a roadmap with a CTO -- exactly the
combination this posting asks for in one sentence. The CRURATED throughput and
reliability figures are concrete and specific rather than generic claims. The clearest
soft spots are GCP/on-premise infrastructure (verified experience is AWS/Kubernetes)
and the AI-orientation requirement, where the verified evidence is cross-team
collaboration rather than hands-on LLM API development; both are surfaced honestly in
interview preparation rather than glossed over or inflated with the unverified
OpenAI-specific percentage from the candidate's raw LinkedIn export.
