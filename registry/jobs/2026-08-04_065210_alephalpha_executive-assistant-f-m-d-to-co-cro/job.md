# Executive Assistant (f/m/d) to Co-CRO

Posted: 2026-08-04T05:40:27Z

We are looking for a motivated, highly-organized and detail-oriented (Senior) Executive Assistant to support one of our two Co-CROs. The role can be full or part-time in our Heidelberg HQ, perfect for an experienced EA looking for a change or returning to the workforce.

You will work as part of the CRO Office within a collaborative support team, ensuring the Co-CRO’s day-to-day operations run smoothly. While you will have guidance from the broader team, we value someone who is eager to take ownership of their tasks and who brings a proactive and flexible "can-do" attitude to every challenge.

# Your responsibilities:

-

**Calendar Coordination:** Manage a busy calendar, scheduling meetings, and ensuring the Co-CRO is well-prepared for their daily appointments.

-

**Inbox Management:** Support with organization and prioritization of incoming e-mails.

-

**Travel Arrangements:** Organize domestic and international travel, including booking flights, hotels, and local transport, and preparing clear itineraries.

-

**Expense Handling: **Organize and submit monthly expense reports accurately and on time.

-

**Meeting Support:** Help collect and prepare meeting materials and presentations. Track action items and remind the team of upcoming deadlines.

-

**Project Management:** Step in on behalf of the CRO and run contained internal-facing projects across various topic areas such as P&C, striving to define sustainable processes where possible.

-

**Team Support**: Provide organizational expertise to the broader Research organization for team meetings, special events, offsites, and the like.

# Your profile:

-

Significant prior Experience as an Executive Assistant in 1-2 fast-paced environments, can include project management or similar tasks.

-

Language Skills: Professional working proficiency in both German and English

-

Reliability & Detail: You are someone who catches the "small things" and takes pride in delivering high-quality and accurate work.

-

Proactive Mindset: You don't wait to be told what to do; you look for ways to be helpful and enjoy solving problems independently.

-

Discretion: You understand the importance of confidentiality and handle sensitive information with the utmost integrity.

-

Tech Savvy: Comfortable using the Google Suite, Slack and Jira and quick to learn new digital tools.

-

Quick-Thinking & Adaptable: You stay calm when plans change and pivot between different tasks quickly and easily.
