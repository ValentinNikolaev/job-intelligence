# Interview preparation — VMWay Backend Developer PHP

## Recruiter / HR Screening
Explain your actual interest in hands-on backend work. The posting specifies Rome, one or two client-site days weekly and €25,000–35,000 gross under CCNL Commercio. Confirm the site and whether the arrangement works for you. Follow the compensation benchmark workflow before stating an expectation.

Explain the concurrent CRURATED subcontract and your actual reasons for changing roles. Confirm notice and start date. Ask the team language. English is supported at upper-intermediate level, while Italian proficiency is not established. Describe your current work authorisation without inferring citizenship.

Likely questions: Why this role now? How much recent work was hands-on PHP? Can you attend the client site? What contract, compensation and start date do you seek? What languages do you use?

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview
Prepare these prompts using concrete records. For each, separate the situation, your own action, the team's contribution and the observed result. If a metric or decision is not supported, use a qualitative outcome or say it is unknown.

1. Tell us about a technical decision with security implications. Use the Simple App outbound calling platform, its privacy controls and formal security review. Explain your architecture and backend ownership, the review process and the cross-functional team's production delivery.
2. Describe a time you made systems easier to integrate. Consider the vendor-neutral helpdesk gateway and its adapters. The supported outcome is a first B2B demonstration.
3. Tell us about improving service reliability under load. Use airSlate's database bottleneck removal and workload redistribution. Avoid inventing the database schema or benchmark.
4. How have you worked with product and engineering colleagues to deliver? Use airSlate planning and delivery from draft to production, with your actual level of authority and the team's contribution.
5. Describe a change that protected existing consumers. Discuss parallel event versions at CRURATED. Prepare the actual compatibility and rollout details only if you can recall and substantiate them.
6. Tell us about a platform you owned in production. Use the Simple App support platform, including operational responsibilities and how you handled peak load. Do not attribute every platform automation to yourself.
7. How have you supported engineers or managed trade-offs? Draw on the documented backend leadership at Hyprr or PDFfiller, and describe a specific real situation rather than manufacturing an anecdote.

## Technical Interview
**High priority — PHP and Symfony.** Review dependency injection, service boundaries, testing and maintenance. Describe the airSlate Laravel/Symfony logger and its interservice standard. Doctrine is required but its use is unverified. Review entity lifecycle, migrations and transactions without presenting study as production experience.

**High priority — SQL and relational data.** Review joins, indexes, plans and transactions. Explain airSlate bottleneck removal and stability without inventing a query, schema or percentage. Ask about the client’s data volumes and schema ownership.

**High priority — REST and service integration.** Review contracts, status codes, idempotency, authentication and compatibility. Discuss the gateway’s OpenAPI contracts, ingestion and adapters. SOAP use is unverified.

**Medium priority — system design and delivery.** Discuss event versioning and routing at CRURATED, including the supported ownership of production components. Prepare to reason about duplicate events, backpressure, CI/CD and safe releases without inventing implementation details.

**Medium priority — infrastructure and operations.** Review AWS, monitoring, queues and practical production diagnosis using actual examples. Do not repeat the withdrawn airSlate ECS-to-Kubernetes migration. Git and SVN are listed in the vacancy, but GitHub Actions alone does not prove the exact repository responsibilities. Clarify your real workflow before making that claim.

**Lower priority until clarified — frontend and environment.** HTML/CSS, JavaScript with jQuery and LAMP/LEMP are explicit requirements, although their share of the work is unknown. Ask how deeply they are used. Refresh fundamentals as needed and make a clear distinction between learning and prior production work.

## Preparation Plan
**Must prepare:** settle practical availability, office attendance and language answers; revisit the PHP/Symfony logger, the CRURATED production scope and the airSlate database example. Confirm what you can substantiate about Doctrine and SQL. Research compensation under the repository's benchmark workflow before providing an expectation.

**Before the technical interview:** practise a PHP exercise, SQL query and REST design discussion. Review Doctrine concepts honestly and prepare one incident or reliability story with actual decisions, limits and outcome. Rehearse the overlapping roles and avoid unsupported metrics.

**Before a final discussion:** explain the work and feedback you value and ask about delivery ownership and assignment changes.

2. Which Symfony and PHP versions are in use, and how central is Doctrine to daily work?
3. What database technology is used, and who owns schema and query decisions?
4. How much of the role involves JavaScript and jQuery versus backend development?
5. How are releases, production support and code reviews organised between VMWay and the client?
6. Where is the client site, and how are the one or two weekly office days scheduled?
7. Which language does the team use for meetings and written technical discussions?
8. What are the contract terms, the compensation structure and the process if the client assignment changes?
