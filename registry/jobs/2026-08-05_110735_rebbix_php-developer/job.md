### About the Role

Our client is a leading European company providing solutions for product, material, and logistics cycles, with a strong focus on recycling and sustainability. The company helps businesses manage recyclable materials responsibly, improve sustainability performance, and reduce the use of primary resources.

We are looking for a PHP Developer to join the product team and contribute to the development of an enterprise SaaS platform. In this role, you will primarily work on building new functionality, taking ownership of technical implementation, and continuously improving the product. Your main focus will be on backend development with Laravel while contributing to Magento-based web shop solutions where needed.

### Responsibilities

- Independently develop complex web applications and services using PHP, SQL, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS

- Contribute to the further development and maintenance of existing systems
- Develop and maintain backend applications
- Contribute to frontend development where required
- Develop and enhance Laravel-based logistics applications and Magento-based web shop solutions

### Requirements

- Product mindset — able to translate business needs into technical decisions
- Structured, analytical, independent way of working

**Nice to have**

- Experience working on enterprise or SaaS products
- Magento 2.x experience
- JavaScript/React frontend experience — a plus for this role, not required if you’re backend-focused
