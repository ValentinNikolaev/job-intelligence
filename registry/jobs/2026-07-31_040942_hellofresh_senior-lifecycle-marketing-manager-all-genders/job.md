# Senior Lifecycle Marketing Manager (all genders)

Posted: 2026-07-30T23:56:01Z

## **The role**

For our global Customer Engagement team in Berlin, we are looking for a Lifecycle Marketing Manager to own the lifecycle strategy for one of our core topics: AOV, merchandising, partnerships & retail media, or unpausing.

Customer Engagement sits within the HelloFresh Group’s Global Customer Lifecycle organization. Our mission is simple: keep active customers active, and make sure they get the most out of their subscription — across our 6 brands, 18 countries and 30 markets.

As a Lifecycle Marketing Manager, you’ll be a critical link between strategy and execution, partnering closely with CRM, Product Marketing, Physical Product, Tech and local marketing teams to design, brief and scale programs that keep our customers engaged with their subscription.

Above all, we are looking for people who will **make HelloFresh** **better.** We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you.

## **What you’ll do**

- Own the lifecycle strategy deployment for your topic area and translate it into programs and campaigns that keep customers engaged with their subscription

- Partner with Product Marketing, Physical Product and Tech to spot opportunities that improve the customer experience and support your topic’s goals

- Brief campaigns end-to-end to CRM Operations and Creative teams, ensuring messaging, targeting and execution meet our lifecycle and brand standards

- Work with local market teams across our 30 markets to adapt and roll out global strategy, balancing consistency with local relevance

- Use data to test, measure and continuously optimize campaign performance

- Track and report on the health of your topic area, flagging risks and opportunities to the wider Customer Engagement team

- Support cross-topic initiatives across the Customer Engagement team as needed

## **What you’ll bring**

- Strong commercial acumen — experience operating in a highly commercial function (not necessarily CRM), with the ability to bridge lifecycle marketing thinking with commercial opportunities, and to create opportunity rather than just streamline execution

- A successful experience (3+ years) in CRM, lifecycle marketing, product marketing or a similar role, ideally in a subscription, e-commerce or FMCG environment

- Strong project management skills, with experience briefing and managing cross-functional stakeholders (CRM Operations, Creative, Tech, local teams)

- An analytical mindset — comfortable using data to inform decisions and optimize campaigns

- Ability to manage multiple priorities independently, across global and local stakeholders

- Fluent in English; *notions of German, French, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian or Danish is a plus*

- Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business or a related field

If you're excited about driving measurable reactivation uplift across the world, we'd love to hear from you!
