PROJECT**

The project is a strategic self-service advertising solution designed to make streaming inventory more accessible, measurable, and performance-driven for advertisers of all sizes. It includes a real-time bidding and optimization platform capable of handling high-load, low-latency auction mechanics. The system integrates forecasting.

**RESPONSIBILITIES**

- Design and implement core Back-End services for real-time bidding, optimization, and ad delivery workflows
- Build custom bidding agents for real-time bid decisioning
- Develop high-load, low-latency components supporting real-time auction and delivery mechanics
- Implement proprietary pacing, bid-shading, and optimization logic to improve campaign efficiency
- Design and integrate forecasting capabilities for impressions, reach, outcomes estimation, and campaign planning
- Work with OpenRTB, JSON, protobuf, and other ad-tech protocols and data formats
- Integrate with data and metrics pipelines to support real-time optimization and reporting
- Implement observability, monitoring, and troubleshooting capabilities (LGTM/ELK stack)
- Collaborate with product, engineering, data, and ad operations stakeholders to deliver scalable performance advertising capabilities

- Experience with low-latency data stores and caching technologies such as Aerospike, Redis, ElastiCache or similar

- Experience building production-grade services with strong reliability, scalability and observability requirements

- At least an Upper-Intermediate level of English
