# Founder's Associate to the CTO

Posted: 2026-08-02T15:00:39Z

**Who We Are**

We build the intelligence that lets robots sense, reason, and act in the real world—moving beyond the lab and into everyday industrial settings like warehouses and factories. Our technology closes the automation gaps that traditional systems can't solve. We are on a mission to redefine how physical work gets done, and we're looking for curious, bold thinkers to help shape the future of robotics with us.

**Overview**

As Sereact scales, the visibility of our technological vision and the efficiency of our leadership team become key strategic advantages. We are looking for a Founders Associate to our CTO, Marc Tuscher, to act as his right hand. Your mission is twofold: positioning Marc as a leading voice in the "Embodied AI" space and mastering the operational daily business of the CTO office. This is a true "zero-to-one" mandate: you will work at the intersection of technology, branding, and operations, directly impacting the company’s growth and public perception.

**
Organization & Executive Support**

**
Special Projects & Strategic Initiatives**

Taking full ownership of mission-critical ad-hoc projects, ranging from deep-dives into emerging tech trends to the preparation of Board-level materials.

Evaluating and facilitating strategic technical partnerships to ensure our ecosystem remains at the forefront of the industry.
