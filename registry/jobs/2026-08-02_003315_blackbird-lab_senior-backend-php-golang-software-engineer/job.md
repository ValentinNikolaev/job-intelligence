Position and client

Join a team building a large-scale digital platform used by millions of users worldwide. The product helps consumers discover relevant offers, save money, and make informed purchasing decisions, while enabling retailers and brands to better engage customers across digital and physical channels.

As a Senior Backend Software Engineer, you will become part of a cross-functional engineering team responsible for designing, building, and maintaining backend systems that power high-traffic mobile applications. The focus is delivering scalable, secure, and reliable distributed services.

Responsibilities span system design, backend development, testing, and technical leadership, with a strong focus on supporting and evolving distributed backend systems for Flipp and Shopfully mobile applications.

Key responsibilities:

- Design and evolve scalable, secure, fault-tolerant distributed systems within an event-driven microservices architecture.
- Own and advance backend service architecture with a platform mindset, creating reusable services, APIs, and shared components for the internal developer community.
- Translate product requirements into clear technical solutions in collaboration with Product, Design, Quality, and Data.
- Own the end-to-end software development lifecycle from technical design, estimation, implementation, testing, and deployment to production support.

- Act as a senior technical leader by guiding architectural decisions, mentoring through code reviews, and serving as a reference point during production incidents.
- Integrate AI-assisted development tools into daily workflow and help the team adopt effective engineering productivity practices.
