# Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer (PHP/Laravel, Vue.js)

Posted: 2026-09-18

We’re looking for a Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer to work on the project of our Client who empowers organizations to conduct globally connected research and modernize research administration. Their industry-leading cloud-based research platform powers a suite of applications that maximizes researchers’ success, improves collaboration, and simplifies work across the entire research life cycle including commercialization and tracking of research outcomes. Its global community includes academic institutions, pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals and health systems, government agencies, independent research institutions, and non-profit organizations. The platform facilitates organized and efficient lab-to-market work on inventions. Built on user experience at the Ohio State University, Duke University, and Washington University in St. Louis, and others, it is designed to ensure data transparency and integrity, simplify access to information, and improve collaboration across the chain — from invention creation to commercialization. The platform manages intellectual property assets resulting from funded research. The program supports the entire life cycle of Intellectual Property management, providing tools and resources to stakeholders that allow them to spend less time on administration and more time on commercialization.
