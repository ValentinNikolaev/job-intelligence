**What you’ll own**

As a Lead Developer, you’ll set the technical direction for the team and take end-to-end ownership of how we build and scale our tax platform. Your focus will be on:

- defining the architecture and technical strategy for tax calculation and reporting logic
- leading the integration of third-party tax providers and owning those technical partnerships
- driving system resilience, uptime, and scalability across services
- making high-impact architectural decisions and establishing engineering standards and best practices
- leading, mentoring, and growing the engineering team, and helping shape hiring as the team scales

In your first months, you’ll deliver core tax functionality, establish a solid technical foundation, and define the engineering practices that will support future product and team growth.

We rely on automated testing over manual QA — unit, integration, functional, and e2e tests are a core part of how we ship and scale safely, and you’ll champion this culture across the team.
