## **Brief info about Vinted **

Our mission is to make second-hand the first choice, and we're looking for people who want to help us get there. Every day, we work together to help our members buy and sell pre-loved clothing and lifestyle items, giving each piece a second life – or even a third.
The Vinted Group is made up of three business units that support this mission:

**[Vinted Marketplace](https://careers.vinted.com/marketplace)** is Europe’s leading platform for second-hand fashion and a go-to destination for all kinds of pre-loved items, with a growing range of categories. Our platform connects millions of members across 20+ markets, helping great items find a new life.

[**Vinted Go**](https://careers.vinted.com/vintedgo) enhances the shipping experience with a vast network of over 500,000 pick-up and drop-off points, partnering with more than 60 carriers across Europe, with added services like item verification for peace of mind on high-value pieces.

[**Vinted Pay**](https://careers.vinted.com/vintedpay) is the newest part of the Vinted Group, dedicated to bringing secure, reliable payments to buyers and sellers across Europe. Seamlessly integrated into the Vinted app, it helps keep every transaction safe, efficient, and easy for our members.

Founded in 2008 in Lithuania, Vinted began as a way for friends to find new homes for clothes they no longer needed. In 2019, we became Lithuania's first unicorn! Today, our headquarters remain in Vilnius, and we've grown with offices across Europe, supported by a team of over 2,000 people.

## **Information about the position **

As our new Staff Engineer for AI in Engineering Experience, you will be a key technical leader shaping how hundreds of Vinted employees build software with AI in engineering, design, and product. You will own the technical strategy for our assisted and agentic AI infrastructure: tools, platforms, and golden paths that turn AI into a force multiplier across the software development lifecycle.

Think you’re up to the challenge? Read on.

## **In this position, you’ll **

- Own the technical vision and strategy for Vinted’s agentic development ecosystem with AI: assisted and agentic coding (Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex), agentic PR reviews, and MCP infrastructure that connects AI tooling to Vinted’s systems.

- Drive the expansion of AI-enabled engineering workflows so engineers can lean on AI for debugging with logs, traces, configs, and system context.

- Lead the application of AI assistance beyond coding, including incident investigation and resolution, on-call runbook guidance and automated handovers, and easier navigation of services, ownership, and system relationships.

- Define what AI-ready means at Vinted for repositories, services, and workflows, and drive adoption through shared skills, scorecards, and golden paths that product teams can follow with minimal friction.

- Guide the technical roadmap of our AI tooling team, ensuring deliverables compound into a coherent platform.

- Lead high-stakes experiments on emerging AI capabilities (automated refactoring, test generation, agentic PR reviews) and turn them into company-wide standards.

- Evaluate external tooling vs. in-house solutions for AI-enabled engineering capabilities, guide build-versus-buy decisions, and navigate the fast-moving pricing and contract landscape with technical judgment.

- Define and operationalize clear success metrics for AI-enabled engineering, including adoption, quality, and impact on developer productivity.

- Represent Vinted’s AI engineering direction within the Staff/Principal Engineer layer, and contribute to the broader AI strategy.
