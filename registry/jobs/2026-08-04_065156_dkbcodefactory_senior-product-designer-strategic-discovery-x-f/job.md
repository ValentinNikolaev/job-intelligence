We are looking for a **Senior Product Designer** to help us shape products at their earliest and roughest stage. We are building a discovery team for the phase before the roadmap is fixed, where ideas are still vague and the right direction has not been chosen yet. You take rough ideas and messy customer problems and turn them into one tangible product direction a team can build. You lead discovery, build strong prototypes, and help teams decide what to build, test, change, or stop.

This is **not** a delivery role. You move across topics as the work changes and stay hands-on throughout.

**} } ; **

#### **{ ****your_responsibilities****: [**

- **Design with high craft.** You turn concepts into high-fidelity prototypes, product flows, and interfaces that feel like a real product. You care about the details and the polish.

- **Turn ideas into bold concepts.** You think in bold ways and shape strong ideas from vague input. You are a sharp product thinker who knows what is worth building, and you design for the whole product, not separate features.

- **Lead early discovery.** You frame unclear problems and set hypotheses. You guide teams from first questions to a clear product direction.

- **Facilitate alignment.** You run workshops, design sprints, and co-creation with Product, Research, Engineering, and Business. You bring the right people in at the right time.

#### ] } ;

**{ following_qualifications_would_be_in_line_with_our_desired_profile_for_this_position: [**
