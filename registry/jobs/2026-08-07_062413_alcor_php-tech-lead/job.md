**Information about the project: **
Building Alcor OS is strategically positioned to be one of the top priorities for Alcor. Alcor OS is our all-in-one platform for managing every contractor we place: contracts, approvals, payroll, time off, and reporting, all in one place, replacing the Excel files and long email threads that global workforce management usually runs on. Unlike traditional CRM systems, Alcor OS is designed to be a technically complex solution, setting a new standard in global workforce management.

We are hiring an experienced engineer to lead the technical strategy of Alcor OS. In this role, you will be responsible for defining the tech vision, and product architecture, crafting and executing a technology roadmap, and overseeing the development team. As a key member of our dedicated Alcor OS team, you will contribute to the strategic direction of our company and play a pivotal role in building a best-in-class contractor management platform. Join us in reshaping the future!

**Requirements: **

We need someone who can go deep on PHP and also step back and own the whole system. Years of Laravel experience matter less to us than how you think.

**Baseline:**

- 4+ years as a Tech Lead, Principal Engineer, or Architect — owning architecture decisions, not just delivering tickets as a senior engineer;
- 8+ years writing PHP professionally, with real depth in Laravel — you should be able to explain what’s happening under the framework, not just use it;
- Comfortable with (or genuinely excited to quickly get comfortable with) Domain-Driven Design, CQRS, and Event Sourcing — our platform’s write side lives in event streams and aggregates, not Eloquent models with mutable rows. You don’t need prior production experience with this exact stack, but “I’ve read about it” isn’t enough either;
- Comfortable working across cloud platforms (AWS or equivalent) — compute, storage, and networking trade-offs, not just spinning up services;
- Practical experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar);
- Strong relational data modeling in MySQL, and — because much of our state is event-sourced — comfort modeling aggregates and domain events, not just normalized tables. For a product like Alcor OS, this modeling is the product;
- A real testing discipline (TDD/BDD, PHPUnit or equivalent) end to end — from unit tests around domain logic up to browser-level E2E (Playwright or equivalent) around real user flows. With an architecture like ours, tests are what let you change things with confidence instead of hoping;
- GitHub-based CI/CD workflows.

**What we weigh more than any of the above:**

- **System design **— can you take a fuzzy business problem (e.g. “handle payroll across 40 clients with different rules”) and turn it into an architecture with real trade-offs, not a diagram from a course;
- **Data architecture **— how you’d model something like contracts, approvals, payroll, and time tracking, where a wrong schema quietly breaks the platform months later;
- **Helicopter view **— can you step back from a single feature and see how it touches the rest of the system, other teams, and where it’ll hurt a year from now;
- **Judgment on AI-generated code — this isn’t “have you used Copilot.” Our engineering process is built around AI agents that plan, implement, test, and review code alongside us. We need someone who can direct that process, catch when it’s confidently wrong, and take full ownership of anything that ships — regardless of who or what drafted it first.**
