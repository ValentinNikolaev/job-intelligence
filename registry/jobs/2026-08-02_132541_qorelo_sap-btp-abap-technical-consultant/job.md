# SAP BTP & ABAP Technical Consultant

Posted: 2026-08-02T11:40:53Z

## About Qorelo

Qorelo uses AI to automate some of the most mission-critical work in the enterprise: the management of SAP systems. SAP runs the core of the world's largest companies, including finance, supply chain, and operations, yet keeping it modern is slow, manual, and consultant-heavy. We're changing that.

Our AI agents help teams integrate, migrate, and modernize SAP faster and with fewer errors, compressing work that used to take months. We've raised $3.5M from HPI Ventures, the fund tied to the institute of SAP co-founder Hasso Plattner, along with other strong backers. We already work with Fortune 500 and DAX 40 companies, including Mercedes-Benz.

## The role

This is a rare profile, and we know it. We're looking for someone who has actually shipped in real landscapes, writes ABAP they'd defend in a code review, and at the same time has real depth in AI agents: how they're built, how they're orchestrated, and how SAP's post-Sapphire AI stack (Joule, Joule Studio, AI Foundation, and the new Business AI Platform) actually behaves in production rather than in keynote demos.

You'll be the person in the room who can whiteboard an agent architecture with our engineers in the morning and walk a DAX 40 CIO through an S/4HANA modernization roadmap in the afternoon, without losing depth in either conversation. Two backgrounds in one: hands-on SAP technologist and AI strategist.

You will not be writing slideware. Everything you design, you'll help build and land in real customer systems.
