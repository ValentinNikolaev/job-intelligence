**The Role**

Black Semiconductor is looking for a motivated **AMHS Technician (f/m/d)** to join our on-site team in Aachen, Germany. In this role, you will be responsible for maintaining and ensuring the reliable operation of Automated Material Handling System (AMHS) equipment in a semiconductor manufacturing environment. This includes preventive and corrective maintenance, troubleshooting system issues, supporting installations and upgrades, and ensuring minimal production downtime while adhering to strict safety and cleanroom standards

This role is critical in supporting the reliability, efficiency, and performance of our semiconductor production operations

**Your Responsibilities**

-

Perform preventive and corrective maintenance on Automated Material Handling System (AMHS) equipment, including Overhead Hoist Transport (OHT) vehicles, Under-Track Storage (UTS) systems, conveyors, and substrate and film frame sorting systems

-

Diagnose and resolve mechanical, electrical, and control system issues affecting material transport and wafer handling operations to ensure reliable system performance and minimal downtime

-

Monitor system performance, respond promptly to alarms, and restore equipment functionality in a timely manner to minimize production disruptions and maintain operational efficiency

-

Support installation, commissioning, qualification, and upgrade activities for AMHS and sorter equipment in collaboration with vendors and engineering teams to ensure successful deployment and optimalperformance

-

Maintain accurate maintenance records, spare parts inventory, and technical documentation while ensuring compliance with fab safety requirements and operational standards

**Your Qualifications**

-

Completed “Ausbildung” (vocational school) in Mechatronics, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field

-

Ideally first experience in automation maintenance within a manufacturing or semiconductor environment; **recent graduates are welcome**

-

Ability to follow detailed procedures and work instructions accurately, with disciplined documentation practices.

-

Ability to work in confined spaces (less than 4 feet clearance) and at heights of up to 4 meters on AMHS highway levels for equipment recovery activities

-

Understandings of semiconductor cleanroom protocols and EHS standards

-

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills in English and German

**Tools, Technologies & Systems (as plus)**

-

Hands-on experience in maintaining AMHS (OHT) systems in a semiconductor Fab environment

-

Good knowledge of Microsoft Office tools, RFID systems, and graphical user interface (GUI) dashboards for troubleshooting, reporting, and system monitoring

-

Proven experience performing electrical troubleshooting using electrical measurement and diagnostic instruments, as well as carrying out maintenance and repair activities with a wide range of mechanical hand tools

-

Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams to resolve technical issues and coordinate effective communication with internal experts and external vendors

-

Proactive in identifying improvement opportunities and implementing continuous improvement initiatives to enhance equipment reliability, operational efficiency, and system performance
