# Backend Team Lead (PHP)

Posted: 2026-09-02

We are looking for an experienced **Backend Team Lead** with a strong PHP background to take ownership of a high-load SMS AdTech project.

This is a hands-on leadership role combining backend development, technical ownership, team management, and direct communication with the client and project stakeholders.

You will lead a small engineering team of 2 Backend Developers and 1 DevOps Engineer, while remaining actively involved in architecture, code quality, performance optimization, and production stability.

**Technical Stack**

- PHP, Laravel
- Node.js, NestJS
- SQL, ClickHouse, OpenSearch
- AWS: SQS, S3
- Vue.js and Blade templates

**Key Responsibilities**

- Lead and mentor a team of 2 developers and 1 DevOps Engineer.
- Remain hands-on in backend development and technical problem-solving.
- Define and maintain high standards of code quality and conduct architectural and code reviews.
- Design and develop scalable backend services and microservices.
- Optimize high-load database queries, ClickHouse workloads, and OpenSearch indices.
- Troubleshoot production issues, performance bottlenecks, and system stability problems.
- Make technical and architectural decisions related to the evolution of the platform.
- Participate in regular calls with stakeholders and translate business needs into technical solutions.
- Support and improve existing monolithic services while gradually evolving the system architecture.

**Requirements**

- **8+ years of software development experience, with strong hands-on expertise in PHP and Laravel.**
- **3+ years in a Team Lead, Tech Lead, or similar technical leadership role**.
- Proven experience developing and supporting **high-load / high-traffic backend systems**.
- Strong hands-on experience with Node.js, ideally NestJS.
- Practical experience with ClickHouse and OpenSearch in production environments.
- Strong knowledge of SQL databases, query optimization, and performance tuning.
- Strong understanding of backend architecture, scalability, distributed systems, and microservices.
- Experience troubleshooting complex production issues and performance bottlenecks.
- Ability to combine technical leadership with hands-on development.
- **Upper-Intermediate English proficiency**, comfortable communicating directly with an international client and project stakeholders.

**Nice to Have**

- AWS services such as SQS and S3.
- Vue.js or Blade.
- Previous experience with AdTech, messaging platforms, or other high-traffic products.
