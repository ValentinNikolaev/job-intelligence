# Backend Developer

Brownie Suite is seeking a backend developer / full-stack developer to improve its proprietary framework and CMS and develop websites, e-commerce projects, and web applications for the company and its clients. The team is consolidating its framework while managing client accounts and says it can consider junior candidates.

Required skills: LAMP stack, PHP, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, familiarity with frameworks and MVC / TE structures, and version-control systems. The company also asks for team spirit and English knowledge. Desired qualities include a creative, solid, flexible, and adaptable approach to developing internal projects and evolving proprietary technology.
