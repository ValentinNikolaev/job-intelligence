Prepare a concise explanation of Rome location, English working level, notice period, salary expectations, and interest in an on-site Milan role. Explain that CRURATED was a concurrent part-time consulting engagement, not overlapping full-time employment.

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview
Prepare STAR examples: leading the PDFfiller team through peak demand; investigating airSlate bottlenecks; owning Simple.life support automation; moving Hyprr from prototype to beta. Emphasize collaboration, pragmatic ownership, and adaptability without claiming client-CMS experience.

## Technical Interview
High Priority: PHP, Laravel/Symfony, MySQL query design and indexing, REST APIs, MVC, testing, Git workflows, performance diagnosis. Medium: Linux/web-server operations, queues, e-commerce integrations, JavaScript collaboration. Low: jQuery and the proprietary framework, unless confirmed as central.

## CV Deep-Dive Questions
Expect questions on database load reduction at airSlate, production incidents, message retries, 50-million-email scale, and leading engineers. Be precise about personal contribution versus team results.

## Company-Specific Preparation
Read Brownie Suite's commerce positioning and careers post. Ask which framework components are actively being consolidated, whether the role is backend-only or full stack, and which client domains drive the roadmap.

Must prepare: two PHP/MySQL performance stories and the consulting-timeline explanation. Before technical: refresh Laravel/Symfony patterns, SQL indexing, HTTP/API security, and Git practices. Before final culture stage: connect ownership and teamwork examples to agency/client delivery.
