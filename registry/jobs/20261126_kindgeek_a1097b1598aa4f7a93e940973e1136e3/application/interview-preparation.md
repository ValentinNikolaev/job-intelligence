# Interview preparation — Kindgeek

## Recruiter / HR Screening

Summarise your PHP leadership and recent Go and PHP work. Explain CRURATED as a concurrent part-time subcontract. If accurate, describe your interest in understanding existing systems and transferring knowledge.

The direct form says fully remote without location restrictions. Confirm contracting arrangements from Italy, extension prospects, your notice period and earliest start date. The initial engagement is three to four months. Prepare to discuss technical work in English.

The form requires salary. Follow the compensation benchmark workflow before providing a number. No benchmark has been calculated here. Clarify currency and gross/net basis.

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview

Use real details and be clear about what you personally did. Prepare the situation, decision, action and result for each story; leave an outcome unknown when the source does not establish one.

1. **How do you take over unfamiliar code?** Use a real system you have worked on to discuss reading entry points, tests and production signals. Distinguish past actions from proposals.
2. **Tell us about a difficult technical decision.** CRURATED event versioning offers a discussion of consistent event contracts and parallel publication. Explain the specific alternatives only if you remember them.
3. **How do you collaborate with product on scope?** AirSlate planning and epic decomposition provide a basis. Describe your actual participation rather than claiming sole product ownership.
4. **Describe a production reliability improvement.** Use the airSlate database load reduction, stating what you changed and how stability was assessed. Avoid an unsupported percentage.
5. **How have you helped another engineer understand a system?** AirSlate knowledge sharing and onboarding are supported. Choose an actual instance and describe the recipient and result honestly.
6. **Tell us about evaluating two approaches.** The Simple.life ticket-categorisation A/B test and migration provide a real example. Do not use unconfirmed accuracy or cost estimates as measured results.
7. **How do you handle conflicting priorities?** Draw on delivery planning at airSlate or another remembered situation, and explain who made the final decision.

## Technical Interview

**High priority — PHP and Symfony.** Revisit dependency injection, service configuration, request lifecycle, validation, exception handling, migrations and testing. Explain the airSlate logger, its interface, standard and substantiated rollout. Verify the required two commercial Symfony projects and their versions.

**High priority — architecture and knowledge transition.** Practise mapping an unfamiliar codebase from endpoints and jobs through storage and external dependencies. Identify data owners, failure modes and operational signals, then propose a small documented change with tests. Ask which documentation exists and what the current owners can explain. Present this as an approach, not a claim about the client's system.

**High priority — SQL and data integrity.** Review transactions, isolation, indexing, query plans and safe schema changes. Use the airSlate database stability work to explain a real investigation. MySQL and PostgreSQL are evidenced. If asked about MariaDB, distinguish confirmed familiarity from transferable SQL knowledge.

**High priority — distributed systems and integrations.** Explain CRURATED event schemas, version coexistence, routing and backpressure where supported. Discuss duplicate delivery, ordering, idempotency and observability as design considerations, without claiming a particular guarantee unless verified. For the Crutrade integration, describe the supported authentication, account linking and collection flows.

**Medium priority — testing and code review.** Prepare examples of direct testing and team review work. Explain how to add tests around an inherited boundary before changing it, including what you would do if a dependency is unavailable. Avoid asserting a test coverage figure that is not confirmed.

**Medium priority — frontend and Python.** React, NextJS, FastAPI and Django knowledge is not established in the candidate records. Assess your actual exposure before the call. Ask whether the role involves architecture discussions, reviews or direct implementation in each stack. Acknowledge limits plainly and explain how you would work with a specialist.

**Medium priority — infrastructure and operations.** Review CI/CD, monitoring, logs and Kubernetes at the depth supported by your work. Explain a release or incident from your own experience and what responsibility continued after deployment. Avoid treating a routine fix as a measured organisational outcome.

**Low priority until clarified — banking domain specifics.** Refresh customer-data privacy and secure integration fundamentals, including your actual PCI DSS-related code work at Sixt. Ask the team which regulatory and security obligations apply. Do not imply experience with a particular banking regulation solely from the employer's marketing.

## Company-Specific Preparation

Kindgeek offers fintech product engineering and embedded teams. The partner and its architecture are not identified. Prepare to discuss making inherited services understandable to a successor.

## Preparation Plan

**Must prepare:** verify two commercial Symfony examples, role dates, your actual framework exposure and availability. Prepare the logger and CRURATED integration stories with clear personal boundaries.

**Before a technical interview:** practise a short architecture walkthrough, SQL investigation and a test around an inherited integration. Review event versioning and likely failure modes, then rehearse explaining a trade-off without invented metrics.

**Before a final or culture conversation:** prepare an actual knowledge-sharing example, clarify the short engagement and confirm working arrangements. Revisit your questions once the client and responsibilities are explained.
