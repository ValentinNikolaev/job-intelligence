## Aufgaben

As Head of Business Transformation, you will be responsible for coordinating and driving strategic transformation initiatives across our group of companies. You will work closely with our CEO and leaders from Finance, IT, HR, Sales and Operations to ensure that strategic initiatives are successfully implemented and sustainably embedded within the organization.

Your responsibilities will include:

- Leading and further developing company-wide transformation programs

- Supporting the CEO in strategic decision-making through in-depth analyses and business recommendations

- Designing and continuously improving management reporting, KPI and performance management systems

- Supporting the implementation and further development of Microsoft Business Central from a business perspective

- Developing a group-wide financial and reporting architecture together with Finance and IT

- Analyzing business processes and identifying opportunities for optimization and digitalization

- Managing cross-functional projects and ensuring their successful implementation

- Establishing governance structures, management frameworks and steering mechanisms to support sustainable business growth

- Preparing business cases, financial analyses, investment evaluations and scenario models

- Supporting M&A projects and post-merger integration activities

- Developing executive dashboards, performance indicators and decision-support tools for senior management

- Driving organizational change initiatives and supporting change management across the group
