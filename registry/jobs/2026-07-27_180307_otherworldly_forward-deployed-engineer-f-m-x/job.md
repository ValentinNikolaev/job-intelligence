# Forward Deployed Engineer (f/m/x)

Posted: 2026-07-27T15:30:48Z

We're looking for a **Forward Deployed Engineer** to sit between our platform and the people who use it, quickly turning what our team and our clients actually need into working software.

**Who we are 👪**

We believe live music is one of the best things humans do, and that far more artists deserve to make a living from it than currently can. That's the opportunity we're building for.

The industry still runs on cold emails and who-knows-who, and it's about to be rebuilt. AI now lets small teams achieve incredible things, and we intend to be the team that rebuilds this one.

***Otherworldly*** is an AI-native talent management and booking company. We run an actual roster, meaning real tours, real riders, real settlements, and we're turning everything we've learned into agentic infrastructure. That means a database of 250,000+ bookers worldwide and algorithmic pitching systems that get the right artist in front of the right promoter at scale.

It's working. We closed our seed round with roster and revenue traction already built. Between us, we've managed No.1 artists and Grammy nominees, led strategy for household-name brands, and founded YC-backed companies. Most music-tech is built by people who've never booked a show, but we book shows every week.

## Tasks

Most companies keep their engineers away from customers.

We do the opposite.

Our team runs real tours, pitches real promoters, and manages real artists every day, and every one of those workflows is a system waiting to be built. Your job is to live at that boundary. That means watching the work happen, finding the friction, and shipping the feature that removes it.

● Embed with the booking and artist-care teams. Sit with them, watch how they actually work, and turn the messiest, most manual parts of their day into software

● Build and ship end-to-end. You'll take a problem from "the team keeps doing this by hand" to a deployed, working feature, often in days, not sprints

● Own the last mile of the agentic platform. The integrations, workflows, and interfaces that decide whether our AI systems actually provide value

● Close the loop between roster and product. You're the person who hears an artist's or booker's real problem and comes back with a solution, then feeds what you learn back into the core platform

● Be the technical face to our users when it counts. You're comfortable in the room with a manager, a promoter, or an artist, not just behind a screen
