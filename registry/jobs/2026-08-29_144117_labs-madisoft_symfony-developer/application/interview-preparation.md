# Interview Preparation

## Recruiter / HR Screening

Explain interest in a fully remote Italian role and the appeal of a product used in the school sector. Confirm availability for full-time permanent employment or a VAT-contractor arrangement as appropriate.

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview

Prepare examples of collaborative delivery with Product, Support Ops and engineering teams. Describe how you use monitoring and clear ownership during production issues without assigning team results solely to yourself.

## Technical Interview

Review Symfony and PHP design choices, OOP and SOLID principles, RabbitMQ integration patterns, retries and idempotency, database performance, API design, CI/CD and the tradeoffs in moving from a monolith to microservices.

## CV Deep-Dive Questions

Be ready to explain the airSlate Laravel/Symfony work, ECS-to-Kubernetes migration and logging package; the CRURATED event schema and delivery guarantees; and the Simple.life message-delivery pipeline.

## Company-Specific Preparation

Connect the candidate's clean, observable backend delivery to Nuvola's large daily user base and the posting's emphasis on quality, pair programming, code review and continuous learning.

## Preparation Plan

Prepare one concise example each for a performance investigation, a failed asynchronous delivery and an architecture decision. Review the basics of DDD and BDD/TDD so you can discuss them candidly as practices you want to deepen.

## Questions to Ask

Ask how the team structures Symfony bounded contexts, how testing and review work in practice, what RabbitMQ responsibilities the role owns, what the monolith-to-microservices roadmap looks like, and how contract and growth expectations are set.
