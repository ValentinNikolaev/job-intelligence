Join our Broker / Investment team and work across investment products and customer journeys!

In the first months, your focus will be a live, high-impact investment product post launch: understanding what has been built, improving the experience, and helping shape what comes next.

During that time, you will also work on other Broker / Investment topics, such as existing investment flows, savings plans, portfolio journeys, and new feature ideas.

We are looking for a Senior Product Designer who can turn complex investment, business, legal, and technical requirements into clear, useful customer experiences. You should be comfortable working with ambiguity, but also able to bring structure, make decisions visible, and move topics forward.

You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Business, Legal / Compliance, your Design Manager, and your design peers. This role is a lot about collaboration without being passive: we need someone who can spar, ask the right questions, challenge unclear requirements, and decide where the biggest impact can be achieved.

#### **{ ****your_responsibilities****: [**

- Work on Broker / Investment products and journeys, with an initial focus on improving and further iterating on a live, high-impact investment product post launch.

- Pick up existing work quickly, understand what has been built, and identify what needs to improve, be clarified, tested, designed, or escalated.
