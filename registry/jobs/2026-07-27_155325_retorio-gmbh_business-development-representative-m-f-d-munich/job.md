About the role (Munich-based, German speaking): You are the tip of the spear for our enterprise motion in DACH. Paired one to one with a Senior Account Executive, you own the top of the funnel. - Generating qualified pipeline through proactive, multi-channel outbound: cold calls, personalized email sequences, and LinkedIn, into your target accounts

- Running crisp first conversations that qualify need and book meetings your paired Account Executive accepts

- Keeping the CRM (HubSpot) clean and disciplined, so pipeline, activities, and handoffs stay reliable

- Testing and improving messaging, sequences, and target lists together with marketing and your AE

- Using Retorio's own platform to sharpen your outbound and discovery skills, and to know the product you sell from the inside

## Requirements

- 2 to 4 years of B2B SaaS outbound experience as an SDR or BDR, ideally into enterprise

- A track record of consistently hitting or beating pipeline and meeting targets, with real numbers you can speak to

- Strong on cold outbound: you can open a cold call, steer the conversation, and handle objections without a script

- Business-fluent German (C1+) and English, the role is Munich-based and sells into the DACH market

- Coachable, self-driven, and resilient: you seek feedback, act on it, and stay in the game through rejection

- A clear ambition to grow into an Account Executive (see the path below)

- Comfortable with modern outbound tooling: CRM (HubSpot), sales engagement, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and data enrichment

- Bonus: experience selling into Pharma, Telco, FMCG, Insurance, Automotive, or Retail, or into sales enablement, coaching, or L&D-adjacent buyers
