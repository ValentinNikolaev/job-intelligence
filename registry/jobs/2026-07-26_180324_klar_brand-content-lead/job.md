# Brand & Content Lead

Posted: 2026-07-26T15:30:46Z

**In Short:** We are looking for someone that loves content and eCommerce to own our entire brand surface — content, community, product marketing, and website — and turn Klar into *the* go-to resource for actionable eCom knowledge in Europe.

**About Klar**

**🚀 The Mission**

Klar is the *Data Operating System* for growing eCommerce companies, meaning **no more spreadsheets**. We integrate with all of a brand's data sources to provide a holistic understanding of their business, offering detailed insights on how to accelerate growth and increase profitability.

**🛍️ The Reach**

We are already the "single source of truth" for over 1,000 leading eCom brands. Market leaders like Sunday Natural, Loop Earplugs, Holy, Maniko, and Armed Angels use Klar to make better decisions every single day.

**🤝 The Backstory**

We deeply empathize with our customers because we *were* our customers. The founders (who met in South Africa working for Zando) spent the last 10 years in e-commerce. We know the pain of running these businesses, and we built Klar specifically to solve it.

**✨ The Principle**

Our guiding principle is ***Do Work You Are Proud Of***. We emphasize long-term quality over short-term results. We evaluate our tools and decisions wisely because we want to be proud of what we build together.

## Tasks

**About The Position**

Klar has two big assets outside of our software: the content we produce and the eCom Unity, Germany's largest eCommerce community with 2,500+ active operators. Both are running today — but honestly, neither is running at the level it could. That's where you come in.

You will own our brand end-to-end and build it into a well-oiled machine. Concretely, that means four areas:

**1. The Content Engine**

We are in the early stages of building a scalable, consistent content operation, and you will play a major role in deciding what it becomes. You will own the overall direction, identify which formats and channels we should double down on, and turn them into a content engine that ships reliably and at a high bar.

Here is what we are currently thinking:

- A weekly newsletter that shares the biggest knowledge bombs from our community, workshops, and own research — no fluff, operational insights only.

- A podcast and/or YouTube channel where we go deep with the best operators in eCom on how they actually grow their businesses. Not “here is our founding story” — straight to the operational meat.

- Personal branding on LinkedIn for our founders and team members. You will build the system, develop the angles, and yes — write in other people’s voices. If ghostwriting feels beneath you, this is not your role.

You will already have a Content & Video Producer on your team, who is also happy to jump in front of the camera and take ownership beyond production. Together, you will orchestrate the content process from idea to distribution. We work AI-first - a well-set-up Claude gets you 90% of a draft; your judgment, taste, and editing get it to 100%.

**2. Community & Events**

The eCom Unity runs 20+ IRL events per year like our pre-OMR meetup with 150 operators and a bunch of online workshops. Two people on the team run this today, and it's largely on autopilot — your job is to guide them, connect the community to the content engine (every event is content, every discussion is a newsletter), and push the eCom Unity into its next phase as a genuine media asset.

**3. Product Marketing**

How we talk about Klar — our positioning, our messaging, and our anti-hype stance in a market full of hype — is something you will own and shape. You will be the strategic counterpart to Max, our founder, who is deeply opinionated on this. Your role is to challenge his perspective, sharpen our thinking, and translate a complex product into clear, compelling messaging that makes its value immediately obvious.

**4. Website & Sales Collateral**

You own our website (Webflow) and the collateral our sales team needs. You should be able to execute most of this yourself — with Figma, Webflow, and AI tools like Claude Code — and pull in engineering or freelancers when needed.

**What success looks like after 12 months:** a content machine that ships consistently and at high quality, a growing audience (newsletter, community, social) that considers Klar one of the go-to resources for eCom knowledge in Europe — and a measurable content contribution to our pipeline.

## Requirements

**About You**

- **You are among the best at what you do** - we are looking for extraordinarily talented people who are looking to take their career and knowledge to the next level. You likely have 5–10 years of experience across content, brand, or product marketing.

- **You are smart & naturally curious** - existing in-depth knowledge of eCommerce would be ideal, of course, but we understand that this might be a stretch. Thus, you need to be excited to dig into new industries and gain knowledge of the brands, people and operations in it.

- **You are a great writer and editor** - most content starts as words. You write excellent copy in English (German is a plus), and you have the taste to take an AI-generated draft from good to great. You know the difference.

- **You are a builder, not just a strategist** - you don't need a team of ten to ship. You're comfortable in Webflow, Figma, and AI tools, and you'd rather publish this week than perfect a strategy deck.

- **You lead through others** - you'll guide our community team and work with our video editor. You don't need formal authority to get people moving.

- **You optimize for learning** - your main motivation at this point of your career is to learn as much as possible and broaden your tool set. You want to work with people of the same mindset and learn from each other.

**Location**: Munich preferred, remote possible.
