# Lead PHP Developer (EU)

Posted: 2026-08-11

The company is a leading Digital-out-of-Home provider in Germany. It operates a growing network of digital screens at high-frequency retail locations and develops its own technology platform for campaign management, screen operations, monitoring and advertising delivery.

The internal platform supports business-critical processes across campaign management, inventory handling, screen operations, monitoring, advertising delivery and operational workflows. The technology landscape includes long-lived PHP-based applications with significant business logic and operational relevance.

### Key responsibilities

### Technical responsibilities

- Develop new features for existing PHP-based business applications
- Maintain and improve legacy PHP codebases
- Refactor existing components without disrupting production workflows
- Support stabilization and modernization of business-critical applications
- Build and improve APIs and system integrations
- Work with MySQL / relational databases
- Support performance, stability, maintainability and code quality improvements
- Understand and document complex business logic in existing systems
- Work directly in Jira, Git and established development processes
- Collaborate closely with internal developers and technical leadership in Germany
- Document relevant technical changes, architectural decisions and implementation details

### Team lead responsibilities

- Act as the first technical lead for the nearshore development setup
- Support the selection, interviewing and onboarding of further nearshore developers
- Help evaluate technical skills, seniority, communication style and team fit of future candidates
- Serve as the main technical contact for nearshore developers
- Structure and coordinate development work within the nearshore team
- Translate technical requirements and business priorities into clear development tasks
- Support sprint planning, task refinement, estimation and delivery tracking
- Establish practical development standards, coding guidelines and review practices
- Conduct or support code reviews and ensure consistent quality standards
- Identify risks, blockers, dependencies and technical debt early and communicate them clearly
- Help build a reliable, pragmatic and productive nearshore development culture
