# Intermediate Back-End Developer

Posted: 2026-08-13

### About Us

At eduki, we believe that supporting teachers leads to the best possible lessons, the best possible schools, and the best possible education. That’s why we built a platform where teachers can find and share high quality, ready-to-use teaching materials. We offer both printable and digital resources for all school types, subjects, and grade levels. Whether it’s for a last-minute lesson or long-term inspiration, we make it easier for educators to feel confident and prepared.

eduki is used by more than 3 million teachers internationally who download more than 100.000 teaching resources a day. That’s where your impact begins. Join us!

### Your Impact

As an Intermediate Back-End Developer (all genders) at eduki, you provide additional power to our development teams and help us to reach our goal of empowering teachers to deliver the best school education. Here are the key tasks and responsibilities that you will be taking on in this particular role:

- Microservices Development: You develop and maintain Microservices using PHP, implementing well-defined features and functionalities within existing patterns.
- Integration and Configuration: You configure and integrate Microservices into our existing infrastructure, with support from senior engineers on more complex integrations.
- Teamwork: You participate in architecture discussions, contributing ideas and asking questions, as part of a diverse, international team.

** **

### Your Skills and Expertise

We are searching for an experienced PHP Microservice Developer (all genders) who would like to work on a purpose-driven product and in an internationally operating team. Furthermore, you should check the following boxes:

- You have experience working with Microservices. Maintaining existing ones, not necessarily building new ones from scratch.
- You have working knowledge of PHP 8+, Docker, and SQL; familiarity with Kubernetes is a bonus.
- You’re reliable and can work with a good degree of independence, while comfortable asking for guidance when needed.
- You feel comfortable communicating in English and working in an international team
