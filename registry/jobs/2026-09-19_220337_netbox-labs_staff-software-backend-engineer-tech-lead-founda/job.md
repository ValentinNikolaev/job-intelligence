# Staff Software Backend Engineer, Tech Lead - Foundations

Posted: 2026-09-19T12:42:02Z

NetBox Labs seeks a **Staff Software Backend Engineer** to technically lead our Foundations team. Foundations is the platform team that builds shared platform primitives that other engineering teams depend on including core services for authentication, access control, customer secrets management, and customer telemetry. This role will own the technical direction for Foundations. You will partner with the Foundations Engineering Manager, who owns people and process, while you own architecture and technical execution.

### **Responsibilities:**

-

Own the architecture of the Platform Authorization Service (PAS), the system that controls access across the platform.

-

Set the technical direction for identity, single sign-on (SSO), access control, secrets management services and more.

-

Lead delivery of Foundations core services, including login, authorization, and tenancy.

-

Act as the primary technical decision maker for Foundations.

-

Lead and mentor the Foundations engineering team through daily technical work.

-

Partner with the Foundations Engineering Manager and Product Manager on team execution.

-

Drive core components through to GA (general availability) launch.

-

Define API contracts for platform services that others consume, including Delivery Platform and Application teams.

-

Work with the Foundations Product Manager to align technical plans with product priorities.

-

Set engineering standards for code review, testing, and documentation across the team.

### **Requirements:**

-

7+ years of software development experience, including technical ownership of a platform or infrastructure area.

-

3+ years of experience in a B2B software startup or high-growth organization.

-

Direct experience with authentication and authorization systems (for example, OAuth, SSO, or role-based access control).

-

Experience leading engineering work without formal manager authority.

-

Backend programming experience in Python and Go.

-

Experience designing APIs consumed by other engineering teams.

-

Strong Linux and distributed systems fundamentals.

-

A track record of making technical decisions under ambiguity.

### **Nice to Have:**

-

Experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration.

-

Experience with network management or infrastructure software (for example, NetBox).

-

Open-source contributions or project involvement.

-

Comfort with AI-enhanced tooling (for example, Copilot, ChatGPT, Cursor).

Join NetBox Labs. Help build the identity and access foundation the rest of the platform relies on.

### About NetBox Labs:

NetBox Labs helps companies build and manage complex networks. We help customers accelerate network automation by delivering open, composable products and supporting the network automation community.

Our products include NetBox Enterprise, a fully supported self-managed NetBox with advanced features, and NetBox Cloud, a secure, scalable, and reliable SaaS edition of NetBox.
