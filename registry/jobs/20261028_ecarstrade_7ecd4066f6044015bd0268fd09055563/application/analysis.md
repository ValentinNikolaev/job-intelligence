Requirements include six or more years of PHP, two or more years leading a team, Symfony or Laravel, Docker, queues, caching, CI/CD, performance, reliability, and cross-functional work. English and Russian proficiency are mandatory. The candidate has PHP and Technical Lead experience, including consulting, API performance, and event systems; LinkedIn records Russian as Native or Bilingual. The stored score of 76 used only an email excerpt and missed the Docker screen. Work arrangements conflict between channels.

## Strict Hiring Manager Review

Three strengths stand out: airSlate Technical Lead evidence links coordination to delivery, the recent CRURATED engagement proves PHP system ownership in production, and airSlate API and database work supports performance and reliability discussion. Three weaknesses matter to the hiring decision. First, the most recent primary role at Simple.life is predominantly Go; the new CV keeps it because it is real and recent, while placing the PHP consulting engagement and PHP skills where a reviewer can find them. Second, `Team Lead` can mean line management, technical direction, or both; the available source supports planning, mentoring, and delivery work but not every possible management duty. The candidate should explain actual scope in interview, not assume it matches eCarsTrade's 30% management component. Third, Docker and caching are named by the employer but not established in the selected evidence. The CV does not insert them. The resulting gaps may matter in a technical screen.
