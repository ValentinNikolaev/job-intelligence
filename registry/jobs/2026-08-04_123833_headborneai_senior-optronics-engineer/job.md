# Senior Optronics Engineer

Safe, mission-based AI solutions for first responders and defense.

### **About the Role**

HEADBORNE seeks a Senior Optical Engineer to join our Imaging team. You will develop novel visible-to-infrared optical systems from concept through production—contributing to architecture decisions, defining performance requirements under multidisciplinary constraints, and designing and tolerancing systems for ground and airborne applications. You’ll lead lab testing, data analysis, and support the transition from R&D to production, including root-cause evaluation of prototype and manufacturing issues. Working closely with hardware and systems engineers, you’ll deliver high-performance optical systems that operate reliably in real-world conditions. We’re looking for a brilliant, motivated engineer to join TEAM HEADBORNE—delivering mission-based, safe AI solutions that matter.

### **Responsibilities**

-

Design, simulate, and optimize visible-to-infrared optical systems for AR helmets, drones, and robotics.

-

Define optical performance requirements and conduct system-level architecture trades.

-

Lead STOP (Structural, Thermal, Optical Performance) analyses for ruggedized, field-ready optics.

-

Develop and execute test plans to validate prototypes and production hardware.

- Analyze performance data and investigate root causes of optical system issues.

-

Collaborate with hardware, mechanical, and embedded teams to integrate optical designs end-to-end.

- Support the transition from R&D to production with documentation, tolerancing, and validation.

-

Contribute to codebase quality, model reproducibility, and CI/CD workflows for ML pipelines.

- Explore new optical technologies that enhance mission capabilities across air and land.
