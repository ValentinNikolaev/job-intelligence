**About our technology**

The semiconductor industry’s growing demand for more powerful chips with higher bandwidth and lower power finds its solution in our technology. We connect chips to high-throughput, low-delay computing networks. The key lies in harnessing the physical properties of graphene to combine electronic computing with photonic communication, allowing countless chips to interact almost as if they were one. Our graphene photonic innovation increases computing power and efficiency to a new order of magnitude

**In short,** we connect chips to create powerful and energy-efficient networks, overcoming connectivity limitations in the semiconductor industry. We deliver the graphene solution

**The Role**

At Black Semiconductor, we are building the foundation of next-generation chip manufacturing with **Fab One**, our first production facility. To support this, we are looking for an **Industriemeister Electrical Engineering / Master Electrician (f/m/d)** who is not only experienced in hands-on electrical work, but also eager to take ownership of complex facility infrastructure in a high-tech industrial environment.

In this role, you will play a key part in the founding team establishing the electrical and building infrastructure of Fab One. You will be directly involved in the setup, commissioning, and ongoing development of electrical distribution systems and building services.

Beyond traditional electrical engineering tasks, you will progressively develop expertise in building automation and facility control systems (FMCS), with external training provided to support your growth in this area.

If you enjoy understanding complex technical systems, working in a structured and analytical way, and contributing to the build-up of a highly advanced industrial environment, this role offers a strong long-term growth path.

**To join our team, you should be excited to**

-

Take on a key first-hire role in the setup and commissioning of facility infrastructure for Fab One, directly contributing to the development of building automation (FMCS) systems. This includes supporting commissioning activities, system integration, troubleshooting, and gradually building expertise in control logic and HMI systems.

-

Oversee electrical works performed by external contractors, ensuring safety, quality, and timely execution on site.

-

Diagnose faults in complex installations and control circuits, and review/approve electrical documentation, including schematics, wiring diagrams, and as-built drawings.

-

Manage electrical compliance, inspections, and testing in coordination with relevant authorities, ensuring accurate technical documentation and certification.

-

Oversee procurement and quality assurance of electrical materials and contribute to technical design reviews for both new installations and retrofit projects.

-

Work closely with facility managers, project engineers, and external contractors to deliver reliable, compliant installations on time.

**Your Qualifications**

-

Successfully completed Meister qualification in Electrical Engineering (Industriemeister Electrical Engineering or Master Electrician, certified by a German Chamber of Commerce or equivalent)

-

Strong hands-on background in electrical engineering with deep experience in industrial or commercial environments

-

Extensive practical experience in installation, maintenance, commissioning, or troubleshooting of electrical systems

-

Solid knowledge of relevant German standards and regulations

-

Ability to read and interpret electrical schematics, single-line diagrams, and wiring plans

-

Structured, analytical, and solution-oriented approach to technical problem-solving and fault diagnosis

-

Initial exposure to building automation (FMCS/BMS), industrial controls, or similar systems is a strong advantage and will be further developed in this role.

-

Excellent communication skills in German (both written and spoken); professional working proficiency in English is considered a plus.
