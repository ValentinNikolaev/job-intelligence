# Call for Offers: Technical Waste Management Expert (Consultant)

Posted: 2026-08-04T09:30:31Z

**Project Background:**

Margins 2 Markets (M2M) is building key components of an effective waste management system in three ecologically valuable coastal regions: Raja Ampat/Sorong (Indonesia), Tamil Nadu (India), and Zanzibar (Tanzania). These regions face similar challenges: remote and growing coastal communities, insufficient waste management infrastructure, limited access to recycling markets, and increasing plastic pollution from tourism and population growth, driving marine litter and threatening biodiversity, fisheries, and local livelihoods.

The project establishes regional collection, pre-processing, and recycling infrastructure, including Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) and pre-processing sites, and connects these to national and international recycling markets. It combines this physical infrastructure with digital traceability systems, incentive schemes for waste collectors, training, occupational health and safety measures, and transparent payment systems. The overarching goal is to prevent approximately 40,000 tonnes of plastic waste from entering coastal and marine ecosystems and to develop a replicable model for other coastal regions.

Key infrastructure includes:

- A regional MRF in Ramanathapuram, Tamil Nadu, cleaning, sorting, and baling fishing nets and marine plastics, alongside collection activities across 14 coastal districts.

- Pre-processing facilities near Kendwa and Paje (Zanzibar) plus a central MRF between Zanzibar City and the landfill.

- Local collection and pre-processing sites across the Raja Ampat islands, linked to a central facility in Sorong.

Technical concept review and optimization

- Review and advise on facility layouts, process flows, and equipment specifications for MRFs and pre-processing sites.

- Recommend improvements to sorting, baling, and material-densification processes to increase material value and reduce transport costs.

- Advise on capacity planning relative to expected waste volumes and collection growth.

Implementation support

- Support technical due diligence during facility set-up

- Review technical documentation, tenders, or equipment specifications from local contractors/suppliers.

- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation measures

Operational ramp-up & Optimization

- Support commissioning and start-up of facilities, including workflow design and staff operating procedures.

- Advise on occupational health and safety (OHS) standards for waste sorting and processing operations.

- Troubleshoot operational bottlenecks as facilities scale up throughput.

Cross-country knowledge transfer

- Share good practice and lessons learned across the three country operations (Indonesia, India, Tanzania) to support a replicable, scalable model.

- Provide input into internal guidance documents / SOPs as needed.

Ad hoc technical operational and economical advisory

- Respond to technical operational and economical queries from the project team and local partners on a rolling basis over the project duration.
