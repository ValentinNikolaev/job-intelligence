# Working Student Accounting (m/f/d)

Posted: 2026-08-01T11:09:06Z

**JOIN OUR TEAM**

As a **Working Student Accounting**, you will gain hands-on experience in our core financial operations. You will get to know our team and our way of working, actively supporting us with a variety of tasks across our daily accounting and tax processes. Whether checking business transactions, assisting with month-end closures, or maintaining accurate records, you will bring your passion for numbers to a dynamic business environment. This position is **permanent **and based in **Munich**.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

- Collaborate on our monthly closures and our diverse tax declarations
- Independently review and allocate business transactions (e.g. incoming invoices) and travel expenses
- Create sales invoices for the B2B business and maintain customer master data
- Take responsibility for proper documentation and (electronic) filing
- Support with projects and special topics

**YOU COME WITH**

- Current enrolment in a Bachelor's or Master's programme in Accounting, Finance, Taxation, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field and ideally, initial experience in accounting, finance, or taxation
- Experience with standard MS Office applications, along with an interest and ability to work with various IT tools
- A joy for working with numbers and tackling new challenges, combined with an exact, structured approach to work, discretion, and a high level of commitment
- An independent working style paired with a dedicated, team-oriented mindset
- Fluency in English; good German skills are a plus
