## Sell transformative AI solutions to some of the most innovative enterprises in EMEA.

Are you passionate about the transformative power of artificial intelligence (AI)? Join Coveo and help bring cutting-edge AI solutions to leading enterprise organizations across the EMEA market.

At Coveo, we build AI-powered solutions that deliver hyper-relevant and personalized digital experiences. Our platform combines enterprise AI and generative AI to help businesses drive profitability while creating meaningful customer and employee experiences.

**As our Senior Enterprise Account Executive, you will:**

- Identify, engage, and develop relationships with enterprise clients across the EMEA region.

- Build strategic account plans focused on delivering measurable business outcomes for customers.

- Collaborate with alliances managers and partners to generate and expand business opportunities.

- Lead complex enterprise sales cycles from initial engagement through negotiation and closing.

- Develop compelling business cases and proposals that clearly articulate the value of Coveo’s platform.

- Consistently achieve or exceed quarterly and annual sales targets while maintaining accurate pipeline reporting.

****Here is what will qualify you for the role:****

- 10+ years of experience managing complex enterprise software sales cycles, engaging stakeholders from business champions to C-level executives.

- Proven ability to consistently exceed quota in a hunter-style sales environment.

- Experience selling enterprise technology solutions to organizations in the Commerce or Service industries.

- Strong consultative and challenger sales approach, with the ability to communicate complex technology value.

**What will make you stand out:**

- Experience selling artificial intelligence or generative AI solutions.

- MEDDPICC certification or experience applying the methodology.

- Demonstrated success building strong partner relationships to accelerate enterprise sales.

- Curiosity, creativity, and a collaborative mindset that helps drive innovative solutions for customers.
