## About Zeit AI

Zeit AI builds ZeitMind, the agent framework for business intelligence. Founded by Marvin and Leopold (both ex-Palantir), YC S24, backed by Oxford Seed Fund and Sequoia Scout. ## What this role is

**As a Business Development Representative, you'll help drive our growth by building our outbound sales engine and creating new customer opportunities.**

Cold calling is how we won our first customers, and it's still our strongest channel.
You'll be the first point of contact for prospects: identifying pain points, generating leads, and converting interest into real opportunities. Within weeks, you'll run your own discovery calls and define use cases directly with C-level prospects.

**This role is a fast track into sales.**

Outbound experience? You'll move to (Junior) AE in 3-6 months.
New to sales? Same path - your ramp speed sets the pace.

**We invest in you** with daily coaching, clear targets, uncapped upside. You won't be handed a list and left alone we grow together.

## What you will do

-

Outbound Execution: Run high-velocity outbound with cold calling as your primary channel, backed by email and LinkedIn cadences where they add value

Customer Relationships: Engage with prospects daily and build genuine relationships from the first call
