## Technical Interview

- **High — PHP modernization:** version migration, deprecations, static analysis, dependency upgrades, compatibility layers, and safe rollout.
- **High — Laravel:** service container, middleware, queues, Eloquent trade-offs, transactions, validation, testing seams, caching, and upgrade planning.
- **High — Large-codebase refactoring:** characterization tests, boundaries, modularization, risk sequencing, and exit criteria.
- **High — MySQL/performance:** query plans, indexes, locking, transactions, N+1 issues, slow-query analysis, and load testing.
- **High — Observability/troubleshooting:** structured logs, correlation IDs, metrics, traces, hypotheses, mitigation, and prevention.
- **Medium — AI-assisted review:** tests, dependencies, security, hallucinated APIs, and human accountability.
- **Medium — AWS/deployment:** verified ECS-to-Kubernetes work, CI/CD, rollbacks, and health checks.
- **Medium — Docker/Redis:** review fundamentals, while stating hands-on exposure precisely.
- **Low — MongoDB:** know document-model trade-offs without claiming production experience.

Expect a PHP refactoring, Laravel design, or SQL optimization exercise. Preserve behavior, narrate trade-offs, and add tests before broad changes.
