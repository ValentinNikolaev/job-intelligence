**Your Mission**

You will lead Data Collection — the newest production line within Statista’s Data Production division, alongside Modeled Data, Survey Data, and Researched Data. It is our most scalable and most technical production line: it collects third-party data from publicly available sources and exclusive partnerships, and ingests, structures, and harmonizes it at scale for one of the world’s largest business data platforms.

Your mission: Collect customer-valuable third-party data we can sell at scale — free data that complements and contextualizes our portfolio, and exclusive partner data as a value driver.

**Your Role**

As Head of Data Collection, you will build and lead a production line structured along the data collection workflow — identify & unlock sources, explore sources, extract & ingest data — with three specialized sub-teams:

-

**Source Identification & Unlocking** — secures the sources that feed the collection line: unlocking access through partnerships and contractual agreements, and running a structured discovery process to identify and qualify the best source for a given data need. Owns the commercial and contractual side of getting to “unlocked” data.

-

**Source Exploration** — brings the domain expertise to understand an unlocked source: what data it offers, its structure and quality, and which fields are worth retrieving. Translates this into production-ready extraction specifications — the bridge between a raw source and scaled extraction.

-

**Source Extraction & Ingestion** — builds and operates the scalable, standardized setup that extracts data via partner dumps, scraping, or APIs, and ingests it into our systems — structuring and harmonizing it in the process, steered by KPIs and focused on efficiency and technical excellence. This team wil disciplinarily report into the Head of Data Engineering, but you steer them operationally.
