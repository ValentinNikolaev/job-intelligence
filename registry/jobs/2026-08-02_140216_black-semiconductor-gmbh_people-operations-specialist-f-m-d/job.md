# People Operations Specialist (f/m/d)

Posted: 2026-08-02T12:01:37Z

We are seeking a People Operations Specialist (f/m/d) to play a central role in building and scaling our People & Culture function as we grow. This role is instrumental in managing payroll, contracts, mobility, compliance, and reporting, while also acting as a trusted partner to our leadership team. You will be responsible for ensuring operational excellence across core HR processes, building reliable and scalable People Operations systems, supporting employees and managers in a fast-moving, international environment, and reducing dependency on manual workflows as the company scales.

The successful candidate will bring a blend of operational expertise, strategic partnership, process-building capabilities, and a people-centered approach to help us scale smoothly and sustainably.

**To join our team, you should be excited to:**

-

Scale People Operations and Global Mobility to support rapid company growth.

-

Take ownership of payroll for NL and DE, while supporting cases involving international payroll.

-

Manage contract management, employee lifecycle processes, compliance, and employee administration within our HRIS.

-

Build reliable, scalable systems and reduce dependency on manual workflows for daily People Operations.

-

Stabilize and document core People Operations and Global Mobility processes, including developing Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

-

Deliver reporting and analytics that inform decision-making, translate People data into meaningful insights, and shape company policies.

-

Partner with external experts (legal, insurance, accounting, relocation/visa specialists, payroll providers) to ensure smooth operations as the company scales.

-

Build strong collaboration with other People & Culture areas, including Talent Acquisition, People Experience, and People Development.

-

Provide business support to the leadership team, acting as a sparring partner on People Operations and business partnering topics.

-

Contribute to writing and rolling out policies that support compliance, clarity, and culture.

**Your Qualifications**

-

At least 6-8 years of hands-on experience in People Operations within an international company, ideally in a scaling technology, engineering, or deep-tech environment.

-

Practical experience applying German labour law in an HR environment, with the ability to make informed day-to-day decisions.

-

Strong knowledge of HRIS systems (Personio required), relocation & visa platforms, payroll and benefits administration systems, and reporting analytics.

-

Proven experience managing immigration, visas, relocation, and international mobility programs.

-

Experience building processes, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and HR infrastructure.

-

Skilled in decision-making, collaboration, and stakeholder management, with proven ability to balance operational needs with employee experience.

-

Strong hands-on mindset with high attention to detail and the ability to execute in a fast-paced environment.

-

Knowledge in mobility topics, including relocation, visa processes, immigration, and cross-border support.

-

High emotional intelligence (EQ), with the ability to build trust and act with professionalism and integrity.

-

Fluency in German (minimum C1) and English.

-

Advanced Microsoft 365 skills.

-

Previous experience in a fast-growing startup environment, ideally in deep tech.

-

Previous exposure to Dutch labour law is an advantage.
