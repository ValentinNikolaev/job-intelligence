## Who we are

YGO is building the AI infrastructure layer for travel.

The way people plan and book trips is changing fundamentally. Fragmented booking journeys are being replaced by personalized, AI-driven experiences. Most travel companies aren't built for this shift. Their systems are complex, disconnected, and were never designed for modern AI.

YGO bridges that gap.

Our modular platform spans AI Search, Recommendations, Packaging, Content Automation, Customer Service, and AI Assistant Integration, enabling travel companies to modernize their customer experience without rebuilding their core systems.

Leading European travel brands including **Condor, DER Touristik, Kurzurlaub** already rely on YGO. We're backed by **Caesar Ventures** and the founders of **FlixBus, HomeToGo, and GetAway Group**, and we've just closed our **Series A**.

Our ambition is simple:

**When someone in travel thinks about AI infrastructure, they should think about YGO first.**

Your mission is to make sure that's exactly what happens.

## The role

You'll be our **first marketing hire** and build the function from the ground up.

Sales is owned by our Head of Sales. Your job is different: make sure enterprise buyers already know, understand, and trust YGO before sales ever reaches out.

You'll turn a technically complex product into stories, proof points, and content that decision-makers actually care about. You'll define how we position ourselves, what we say, where we show up, and how the market perceives us.

This isn't about chasing clicks or generating MQLs.

It's about building reputation.

You think in narratives, not campaigns. In trust, not clicks. In long-term positioning, not short-term metrics.
