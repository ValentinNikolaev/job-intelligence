# Team Lead Backend (Golang/C++)

Posted: 2026-07-31

**Ixilix** is looking for a **Team Lead (stack: Golang/C++)** to join our team and work on a high-load backend service. The project is based on a microservices architecture, with C++ as the primary language and additional services written in Go, Python and Node.js (knowledge of Go is preferred) with the gradual transition to almost all-Golang.
As a Team Lead, you will split your time 50/50 between hands-on development and team management. You will lead a team of four developers while closely collaborating with other teams (DevOps, Front End team, QA Engineers, and Tech Leads from other projects).

**Responsibilities:**

- Writing high-performance, reliable backend code.
- Managing and mentoring a team.
- Maintaining and improving existing codebase.
- Designing and developing new features and modernizing older parts of the system.
- Collaborating with other teams (DevOps, QA, Front End and other Tech Leads).
- Making architectural decisions with significant freedom in choosing technologies, in consultation with the Engineering Manager, Solution Architect and CTO.

** Requirements:**
• 2+ year of experience in a Team Lead or similar leadership role.
• 5+ years of total development experience.
• 2+ years of experience with Go.
• 2+ years of experience with C++ (preferably).
• Experience with high-load systems and performance optimization.
• Strong understanding of concurrency, memory management, and distributed systems.
• Familiarity with RabbitMQ, Redis, MySQL, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Git.
• Proactivity and problem-solving mindset.
• Good communication skills for cross-team collaboration.
• English B1+
• Ukrainian C1+

**Tech Stack:**

- Backend: Go, C++.
- Testing: Python.
- Messaging & Storage: RabbitMQ, Redis, MySQL, ClickHouse.
- Infrastructure: Kubernetes (EKS), RDS.
- Version Control & CI/CD: Git, Jenkins, Gitlab CI.
- Additional Services: Some internal tools in Python, a small amount of Node.js (optional knowledge).
- Development Environment: Mac.
