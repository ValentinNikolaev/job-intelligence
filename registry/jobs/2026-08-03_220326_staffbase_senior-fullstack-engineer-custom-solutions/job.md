## **The team**

You’ll join a newly formed team dedicated to building custom solutions that extend the Staffbase platform for our enterprise customers. We turn specific customer needs into reliable, scalable extensions — widgets, plugins, and integrations — that make our product work exactly the way each customer needs it to.

Because the team is new, there’s real room to shape how we work: to set engineering standards, bring structure to our processes, and grow together. That’s where you come in.

**What you’ll be doing **

- Own the development of custom solutions end-to-end — from technical design through implementation, testing, and release

- Partner with stakeholders (customers, consultants, and internal teams) to define and detail use cases before development, then validate solutions and gather feedback afterward

- Build across the stack: customer-facing UI in React and TypeScript, backend services, and integrations with Staffbase and third-party APIs

- Mentor mid-level engineers and help bring structure, engineering standards, and good practices to a newly formed team

- Work in a cross-functional, agile setup and produce high-quality, well-tested code

- Give and receive valuable feedback in our code review process, and continuously learn and share knowledge with your peers

- Work iteratively — pushing code frequently, keeping a healthy CI/CD pipeline, and automating where it helps
