**Our people are our greatest strength.** We are a global team of colleagues who collaborate to move forward together and exceed our customers’ expectations.

As a Customer Service Representative, you will play an essential role at the heart of our operations, helping to deliver an outstanding experience for our customers.

## Tasks

**What will you do?**

Deliver an exceptional customer service experience by consistently providing high-quality support and exceeding customer expectations.

You don’t need to come from a chemicals or manufacturing background — we’re open to candidates from other sectors such as logistics, FMCG, or similar industries.

- Communicating with customers and colleagues across different countries.

- Processing and managing customer orders.

- Providing updates on order status, shipments, and deliveries.

- Working closely with Supply Chain, Logistics, Production, and Sales teams.

- Supporting quotation requests and customer enquiries.

- Maintaining accurate customer records and documentation.
