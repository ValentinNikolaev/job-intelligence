### **
What Will Your Role Be
**

We're looking for a Senior Web Analyst to own and evolve our digital measurement ecosystem.
You'll help us understand how millions of users interact with our digital products and turn that understanding into better product and business decisions.You'll work at the intersection of Product, Marketing and Engineering, ensuring that our tracking is accurate, scalable and future-proof. From defining measurement strategies to implementing tracking solutions and uncovering actionable insights, you'll play a key role in improving our digital products and customer experience.

This is not a generic Data Analytics or Data Science role. We're looking for someone with a strong background in Web Analytics, Digital Analytics, Marketing Analytics or Product Analytics who enjoys solving real business problems through reliable data.

**

-
