tracking slot games and managing large-scale structured data for players and industry
stakeholders. The role is a Senior PHP Developer expected to own backend services end
to end -- design, implementation, release, monitoring, and long-term stability -- using
PHP 8+, Laravel and/or Symfony, PostgreSQL/MySQL, REST APIs, Docker, CI/CD, and the
Filament (v5) admin-panel builder, with English C1-C2 required.
