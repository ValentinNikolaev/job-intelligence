## 💡 Your Mission: Be the technical backbone of our French launch

France is our next big market - and as our **Founding Technical Account Manager (France)**, you are the first dedicated customer-facing technical hire for it. You become the single clear technical owner for our first French customers across their entire lifecycle: implementation, technical setup, rollout support, ongoing troubleshooting, and continuous improvement.

This is a true founding role. You'll operate as a one-person Swiss army knife across Technical Support and Solution Engineering - solving hands-on today, and building the playbooks, documentation, and processes that a whole French technical team will run on tomorrow. Your work makes voize's first French customers wildly successful - and referenceable.

## 🚀 Your Daily Business - No two days are alike

-

**Own technical planning and setup end-to-end** for new French customers and rollouts - from the first technical scoping call through go-live and steady-state usage

-

**Own inbound customer and technical support for the French market:** every French support request has a clear owner - you triage, troubleshoot, resolve, or escalate within agreed response expectations

-

**Diagnose ambiguous issues hands-on** across our app, backend, customer-side IT (networks, devices, MDM), and system integrations - and keep customers clearly informed along the way, in French

-

**Build the French playbook from scratch:** setup and support documentation, escalation paths, response standards, and recurring-issue tracking that future hires in the market will ramp on

-

**Be the technical voice of the French customer:** categorize escalations and recurring blockers and feed them back into Product, Support, and Solution Engineering

-

**Work hand in hand with our EU teams:** clear escalation paths into Support, Solution Engineering, and Product - with the goal that standard French requests no longer depend on ad hoc help from the existing team

-

**Turn learnings into leverage:** identify the most common technical blockers in the French market - from facility IT patterns to local care software ecosystems - and translate them into better processes, documentation, and product feedback

## 🤝 Your Skillset - What you bring to the table

-

**Several years of experience** in technical support, solutions engineering, technical account management, or implementation - ideally in B2B SaaS, health-tech, or another operationally complex environment

-

**Hands-on technical troubleshooting skills:** you're comfortable working with logs, APIs, integrations, networking fundamentals, and device/MDM setups - and you can isolate a fault across systems that don't belong to you

-

**Founding-role autonomy:** you thrive with high ownership and low structure - you build processes and documentation where none exist yet, without waiting to be asked

-

**Excellent communication across audiences:** you can walk a non-technical facility administrator through a fix with patience and warmth, then hand engineering a precise, reproducible bug report

-

**Structured under pressure:** you manage many customers and open cases without losing overview - and you use data and dashboards to spot patterns, track recurring issues, and prove things are improving

-

**Full professional proficiency in French (spoken & written)** - you'll work with French facilities and their IT partners every day - plus a very good command of English for internal collaboration. German is a plus.

Nice to have (but not required)

-

Experience in healthcare, senior care, or another regulated industry - knowledge of the French care sector and its software ecosystem is a big plus
