Job Description

We are seeking a Software Consultant for Cross-Platform Apps with .NET MAUI to join our consulting team. You will work with clients to translate requirements into technical concepts and contribute to the development of multi-layer B2B software solutions for iOS, Android and Windows using .NET MAUI, C# and related technologies.

## Tasks

What you will do

- Provide technical and functional consulting for our clients around our software solutions

- Capture, analyze, and translate client requirements into technical concepts, implementing them in own responsibility or in small teams

- Develop our multi-layer B2B software solutions for iOS, Android and Windows using .NET, C# and MAUI

- Occasionally conduct client trainings online and, if needed, on-site

- Collaborate closely between clients and internal teams (development, sales, marketing)

- Actively contribute to the ongoing development of our products and services

## Requirements

Who you are

- 3+ years of professional experience as a developer, technical consultant, or technical point of contact

- Ideally process knowledge in at least one of logistics, maintenance, or production

- Experience with .NET based UI frameworks (MAUI, Xamarin, WPF) and proficient with Entity Framework and MS SQL Server

- Ideally strong skills in [ASP.NET](http://ASP.NET) Core and Blazor

- Some SAP experience is advantageous

- Strong customer orientation and service mindset

- Confident demeanor, strong communication skills, and enjoyment of direct client contact

- Fluent German (C1) and good English
