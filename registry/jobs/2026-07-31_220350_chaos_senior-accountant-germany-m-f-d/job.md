Chaos is a leading global software company that provides world-class visualization and design solutions, empowering creative minds to bring ideas to life.

For over twenty years, Chaos has developed innovative technologies serving multiple industries, including architecture and design, media and entertainment, and product e-commerce. Chaos’ solutions help architects, designers, VFX artists/animators, and other creative professionals share ideas, optimize workflows, and create immersive experiences.

**Role Overview**

The **Senior Accountant** role owns the end-to-end accounting for Chaos GmbH and acts as Global Process Owner (GPO) for intercompany and transfer pricing. This role works closely with colleagues across our global business, bringing both technical rigour and a process-improvement mindset to enable our business to move fast and operate at scale.

**Main Responsibilities**

-

Own the full accounting cycle for Chaos GmbH, including general ledger, AP/AR, fixed assets, and monthly/quarterly/annual close in line with HGB and group reporting requirements.

-

Serve as primary contact for external auditors, tax advisors, and statutory filings.

-

Manage local treasury and payment cycles in coordination with Chaos’s group finance team.

-

Define and maintain global standards for transfer pricing working with teams across Chaos’s international entities to harmonise workflows, close process gaps, and drive automation in NetSuite and connected finance tools.

-

Actively manage the intercompany and transfer pricing positions across the group, working directly with external advisors in relation to the technical tax aspects of transfer pricing to ensure in line with legislation.

-

Support the Accounting Director with process improvement across finance. Suggest opportunities and ways of improving overall efficiencies and improved ways of working to enable a culture of optimisation.

**Required Experience**

-

Track record in financial accounting within an international corporate environment ideally a technology, software, or SaaS business.

-

Experience with German statutory requirements and local GAAP is essential.

-

Experience with large international group consolidations and international accounting standards is essential.

-

Experience of intercompany and transfer pricing is essential.

**Required Skills**

-

Advanced command of German GAAP (HGB).

-

Detailed knowledge of IFRS and group consolidation.

-

Hands-on experience with NetSuite or a comparable ERP, and comfort with finance tooling in a software product environment.

-

Fluent in German and English (written and spoken).

-

Able to operate confidently across Chaos’s multicultural, multi-timezone teams.

-

A process-improvement mindset, with the ability to step back from operational tasks, spot inefficiencies, and build scalable solutions that work across the group.

**Required Qualifications and Certifications**

-

Degree in Finance, Accounting, or a comparable field.

-

A professional qualification (e.g. ACCA, or equivalent) is advantageous but not required if offset by strong practical experience.
