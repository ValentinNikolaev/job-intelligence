# Head of Engineering

Posted: 2026-08-12T14:19:46Z

*This role is part of our ****[Seobility](https://www.seobility.net/en/) ****team, one of our growing brands at saas.group.*

Seobility joined saas.group in 2022. The Seobility all-in-one SEO suite empowers businesses and marketers around the world to boost their website visibility with professional SEO tools. Starting in 2013 with a commitment to usability and actionable advice to make professional-grade SEO accessible, Seobility today has over 700,000 users and earned international awards along the way!

**Role Overview**

As the **Head of Engineering** at Seobility, you lead the technical heart of a mature, high-traffic SEO SaaS product. We've just completed a large relaunch of the platform last year — and are digging into the real work now: evolving what an SEO tool does as AI rewrites how people search and what they expect from software. In a rapidly changing market shaped by AI-driven search, you lead our organizational and technical evolution. Reporting directly to the CEO, you will bridge the gap between engineering challenges and business impact. You guide the team through a transformation toward greater autonomy and lead them towards outcome-focussed execution and AI-native engineering. We're a small, senior team — ambitious and driven, but we genuinely look out for each other. You'll find real scope to own and room to grow.
**Your Impact (First 3-6 Months)**

- **Build Trusting Partnerships:** Establish deep working relationships with the engineering team and tech leads to foster our culture of psychological safety and high performance.

- **Enable Distributed Ownership:** Support the team in fostering ownership for outcomes. Guide the transition from functional (FE/BE) to end-to-end ownership.

- **Optimize and Leverage:** Partner with the team to identify friction, bottlenecks and AI leverage potential. Implement a value-driven delivery flow with improved predictability and velocity.

- **Strategic Roadmap:** Start executing on a roadmap that balances the strategic transformation of our engineering (e.g. tech debt, AI-native engineering, upskilling) with our product innovation goals.

**Responsibilities**

- **AI-Native Engineering: **Spearhead the adoption of AI-native engineering practice to substantially increase engineering efficiency.

- **Organizational Transformation:** Guide the evolution from functional roles to broader roles and outcome-orientation. You bridge gaps between Product and Engineering and make the two grow closer, reduce dependencies and empower the team to deliver end-to-end value.

- **Technical Vision:** Own and co-create the technical vision & roadmap with your Tech Leads, balancing "tech debt" reduction with feature delivery.

- **Platform Health: **Make sure our data-heavy platform is fast, reliable, and secure as it scales — with data privacy and GDPR handled as a matter of course.

- **People Development:** Mentor and empower an international engineering team to embrace full ownership and foster confidence in autonomous decision-making.

- **Strategic Collaboration: **Act as a high-level sparring partner to the CEO and Product leadership, and partner with the saas.group Central Platform team on DevOps and infrastructure topics. Navigate trade-offs between technical sustainability and delivery speed.

**What you bring to the table**

- **SaaS Leadership:** Proven track record of leading engineering teams in a high-scale production environment, with a focus on efficient team structures and operational excellence.

- **Technical Credibility:** You possess high technical credibility across modern stacks (ideally PHP/Laravel, Vue.js, and cloud infrastructure) and bring a particular strength in architecture and system-design.

- **AI First:** A forward-thinking approach to AI as a core lever for both product features and engineering productivity.

- **Product-First Mindset: **With your strong product-sense, you are a key partner for user value creation and prioritize engineering efforts based on customer value and business impact rather than technical elegance for its own sake.

- **Change Management:** You are skilled at building alignment during transitions, leading teams through change and resistance to a new rhythm and velocity.

- **Enabler Mentality: **You measure your success by what your team (incl. you) ships, not only by what you personally build.

- **Domain understanding:** Domain understanding of SEO-related web technologies (websites, crawling, indexing, content analysis) is a strong plus.

- **Communication: **Fluent English is a must; German is a strong advantage given the brand’s roots.

**What’s in It for You**

- **Ultimate Flexibility:** Fully remote role with optional Nuremberg office; preferred time zone ±2 hours CET.

- **High Trust & Autonomy: **We hire experts and get out of their way. You’ll have the freedom to solve problems your way with minimal bureaucracy.

- **The saas.group Network: **Direct access to a global community of SaaS founders, engineering leaders, and experts to exchange knowledge and scale your own career.

- **Impact:** A seat at the leadership table of a brand with thousands of users where your decisions move the needle every day.
