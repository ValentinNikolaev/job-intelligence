# Senior IT Service Engineer

**The Team:**

As an IT Service Engineer at Celonis, you will be a vital part of our international IT Service Team, responsible for ensuring seamless IT operations across the company. You will play a critical role in troubleshooting, resolving, or escalating IT-related issues for Celonis employees globally, with a special focus on supporting our Munich office. This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced IT professional with hands-on expertise in both macOS and Windows environments who thrives in a fast-paced, dynamic, and international workplace.

**The work you’ll do:**

- Act as the first point of contact for onsite IT requests at the Munich office, resolving issues directly
or escalating them as needed — complementing our central First Level Support team.

- Provide hands-on support across both macOS and Windows platforms, troubleshooting hardware,
software, and network issues.

- Deliver onsite IT support to Celonis employees at the Munich office and collaborate with the global IT Service Team via phone, email, and remote assistance.

- Install, configure, and maintain computing equipment and peripherals — desktop and laptop systems (macOS and Windows), network printers, and mobile devices.

- Support and manage mobile devices through MDM platforms such as Intune and Kandji.

- Own the IT onboarding experience for new employees in the Munich office.

- Perform basic repairs and replacements of computing equipment when needed.

- Ensure clear, empathetic communication with users to understand, document, and resolve their issues.

- Own the setup, operation, and maintenance of media and AV technology across the Munich office meeting-room systems and audio/video equipment.

- Configure and maintain video conferencing systems and ensure a reliable, seamless meeting experience.

- Provide AV and technical support for town halls, all-hands, and internal events.

- Continuously improve the media/AV standard across rooms and proactively identify upgrades as the office scales.

- Handle procurement and provisioning of hardware, including laptops and phones.

- Manage the hardware lifecycle from provisioning through to decommissioning.

- Develop and maintain documentation and user guides for common issues to improve IT service efficiency.

- Contribute to the continuous improvement of local and global IT processes.

**Required Skills:**

- Strong communication skills in English with the ability to work effectively in a global, multicultural team.

- Detail-oriented, with excellent problem-solving abilities and a proactive mindset.

- Capable of managing multiple tasks and projects in a fast-paced environment

- A team player who is motivated to provide excellent customer service and continuous improvement in IT processes.

- ITIL V4 Foundation certification
