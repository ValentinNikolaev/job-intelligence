# Back End Engineer (Go)

Posted: 2026-07-28T20:35:37Z

[Oddball](https://himalayas.app/companies/oddball) believes that the best products are built when companies understand and value the things they are working on. We value learning and growth and the ability to make a big impact at a small company. We believe that we can make big changes happen and improve the daily lives of millions of people by bringing quality software to the federal space.

We're looking for a **Backend Engineer** to join our CMS team, building and maintaining the APIs and backend services that power Healthcare.gov tools used by millions of Americans enrolling in and exploring health coverage — including third party enrollment platforms.

### What you'll be doing:

- Develop, enhance, and maintain backend services and APIs in **Go** supporting tools for plan comparison, provider and drug coverage information, and local enrollment help

- Design and build **REST APIs** that power consumer-facing and internal healthcare marketplace features across a diverse set of consumers

- Maintain consistent, well-documented API interfaces with a focus on reliability and backward compatibility

- Write clean, well-tested code with a focus on performance, maintainability, and security

- Support ongoing operations and maintenance of existing digital products alongside new feature development

- Collaborate with frontend engineers, product managers, and stakeholders to deliver scalable backend solutions

- Contribute to code reviews and help uphold engineering standards across the team

### What you’ll bring:

- Hands-on experience writing backend services in **Go**

- Experience designing and building **REST APIs** in production environments

- Proficiency with **PostgreSQL** or similar relational databases and **SQL**

- Experience with **Linux** shell commands, scripting, and process control

- Familiarity with **Node.js** and/or **Python** is a plus

- Familiarity with **AWS**, **ECS** and federal security and compliance requirements is a plus

- Familiarity with containerized applications like **Docker **is a plus

- Thrives in a remote, collaborative **Agile** environment and genuinely enjoys working closely with a cross-functional team

- Communicates clearly and openly, whether in a code review or a cross-team planning session

- Performs other related duties as assigned
