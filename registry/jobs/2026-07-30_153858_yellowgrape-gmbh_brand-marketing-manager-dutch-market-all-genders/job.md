# Brand Marketing Manager - Dutch Market (all genders) // Medior

Posted: 2026-07-30T13:01:02Z

As a Brand Marketing Manager (all genders), you are responsible for developing, managing, and executing creative, cross-media brand marketing strategies and campaigns for a variety of brands in the Netherlands. You help our clients take their brands to the next level by combining strategic thinking with a sharp eye for trends, inspiring and engaging diverse target audiences.

You are a source of ideas, a strategic advisor, and a hands-on executor all in one. You excel at developing and delivering 360° marketing campaigns across all relevant channels, always with the goal of making brands more visible, relevant, and successful. You also know how to present your concepts, strategies, and results in a compelling and persuasive way.

**Please note that this job requires native-level Dutch and is based in Berlin or Amsterdam, with regular travel between both offices.**

## Tasks

- Developing brand concepts, brand strategies, and positioning for our clients.

- Planning and managing integrated 360° campaigns across social media, influencer marketing, newsletters, content, and paid media.

- Creating and executing content and social media strategies that align with the brand and its target audience.

- Coordinating and managing influencer campaigns and partnerships.

- Developing and expanding newsletter and CRM marketing concepts.

- Analyzing and optimizing campaigns based on KPIs.

- Preparing and presenting concepts, campaign strategies, and performance reports during client presentations.

- Collaborating closely with our Performance Marketing and Data teams.

## Requirements

- At least 2 years of experience in brand marketing.

- A strong understanding of digital brands, content trends, and storytelling.

- Knowledge of and experience in social media marketing, influencer management, email marketing, and content marketing.

- A creative mindset with an excellent feel for target audiences, trends, and storytelling.

- An independent, structured, and solution-oriented way of working.

- Strong presentation and communication skills, both online and offline.

- Dutch native speaker, fluent in English. German is an advantage.
