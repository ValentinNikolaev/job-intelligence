## Role Overview

The **Principal Product Manager (PPM)** is responsible for driving the strategy and execution of the assigned business stream within operations, leading multiple innovation projects, and ensuring the delivery of impactful improvements. This role oversees a team of **Product Owners and Process Designers**, aligning business objectives with other streams, technology, and operations teams to maximize efficiency and scalability.

The **PPM **collaborates closely with Engineering, Operations, and Data teams to define roadmaps, manage dependencies, and implement high-value initiatives. The role requires a balance of strategic thinking, hands-on execution, and strong leadership to drive measurable improvements in key operational KPIs.
