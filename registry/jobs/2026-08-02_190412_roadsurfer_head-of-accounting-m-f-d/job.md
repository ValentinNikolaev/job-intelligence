As Head of Accounting & Tax, you own the entire accounting function of the Roadsurfer Group and lead your team through a period of real change. You report directly to the CFO and are part of a finance organisation that brings together Accounting, Tax, Controlling, Corporate Finance, and Finance Operations.

What makes this role special: you are not stepping into a finished structure – you actively shape it. You translate the demands of a fast-growing company into clean, audit-proof processes, and you play a key role in introducing SAP as our central ERP system.

**The Role - Your Passion**

-

You lead the accounting function of the roadsurfer Group and you are responsible for reliable, timely, and audit-proof financial reporting under German GAAP (HGB).

-

You ensure tax compliance across the roadsurfer universe.

-

You lead, develop and structure your accounting team, establishing a culture of clear ownership and reliable closing timelines.

-

You help build an accounting function that is scalable, data-driven, and ready for an AI-native finance setup – combining strong accounting expertise with smart automation, clear process ownership, and high-quality financial data.

-

You play a central role in the company-wide SAP implementation – from designing the accounting processes through testing to go-live and post-launch stabilisation.

-

You are the key contact for auditors, tax advisors, and authorities, and you steer the audit process.

-

You work closely with Controlling, Corporate Finance, and Finance Operations, and drive automation and digitalisation in accounting.

**What you need to ride the wave with us**

-

Several years of relevant accounting experience, including responsibility for financial statements under German GAAP (HGB) and proven leadership of an accounting team.

-

A strong accounting qualification, such as a degree in finance/accounting or a comparable certification, e.g. certified accountant / Bilanzbuchhalter.

-

Experience with SAP and finance transformation projects; ideally, you have supported an SAP implementation or migration from an accounting perspective.

-

A structured, hands-on leadership style with the ability to build scalable processes in a dynamic environment.

-

Curiosity for automation, AI-supported finance workflows, and data-driven process improvements.

-

An eye for detail, a positive mindset combined with a solution focus.

-

Fluent in English and good German skills.
