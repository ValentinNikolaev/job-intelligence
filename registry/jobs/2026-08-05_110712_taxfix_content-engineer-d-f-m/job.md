# Content Engineer (d/f/m)

Posted: 2026-08-05T08:40:34Z

## Our story:

Every year millions of people are either filing their taxes in fear or giving up on their tax refund altogether. We're working on fixing that. Our intuitive app enables anyone, regardless of education or background, to file their taxes with newfound confidence.

Spread across Germany, Spain and the UK, the team at Taxfix Group with its brands Taxfix and Steuerbot, is a compassionate group of solution-finders. We speak our minds openly, and with over 400 professionals, including tax experts, developers, and IT security experts, we're rich in ideas and voices. The group has facilitated more than 3.5 billion euros in tax refunds for its customers since its founding in 2016.

## **The opportunity:**

We’re looking for a Content Engineer to join our Organic Acquisition team. This is not a traditional content role - you won’t be writing blog posts or landing pages. We have an excellent agency partner handling content production across DE, UK, and ES.

Instead, you’ll be the person who builds the systems, automations, and workflows that make our content operations scale across three markets. You’ll design and run CRO and ASO experiments with proper rigor, automate structured content pipelines, and create cross-market templates that eliminate repetitive manual work.

Your playground: a proven multi-market organic acquisition engine with real room to systematise, automate, and experiment - using AI-powered tools and no-code automation platforms to move at a pace that compounds.

**Your responsibilities and decisions:**

**CRO, ASO & Personalisation Experimentation**

-

Design, execute, and analyse conversion rate optimisation experiments across web and app store listings

-

Build and own a structured experimentation framework: hypothesis → prioritisation → execution → measurement → learning

-

Design and implement multi-armed bandit testing to continuously optimise content variants across segments and markets

-

Drive content personalisation for key user segments (e.g., tax classes, employment types, filing interests) to improve relevance and conversion

-

Work with our ASO agency and tooling ([Peec.ai](http://Peec.ai)) to improve experiment quality and velocity; own the experimentation process end-to-end

-

Document experiment results and build a searchable knowledge base of learnings that compounds over time

**Content Automation & Structured Data**

-

Build automated workflows using no-code/low-code tools (n8n, Make) and AI tools (Claude, Langdock, Antigravity)

-

Automate structured content generation beyond FAQs - including HowTo, Article, TaxCalculator, Breadcrumb, and Q&A schema, as well as emerging formats optimised for AI answer engines (AEO) and LLM consumption.

-

Define and maintain scalable content templates for diverse content types: comparison pages, how-to guides, tax topic explainers, calculators, and editorial formats

-

Create translation automation pipelines that work across DE, EN, and ES content

-

Design and roll out cross-market content templates that can be localized efficiently without manual duplication

**Content Operations & Tooling**

-

Identify manual, repetitive processes in our content workflow and systematically automate them

-

Work daily with our content and SEO stack: **Uniform** (CMS), **Ahrefs**, [**Peec.ai**](http://Peec.ai) (ASO), **Google Search Console**, and **Google Analytics**

-

Leverage our AI tooling stack - **Langdock**, **Antigravity**, **Claude (Code)** - to accelerate content operations and experimentation

-

Build and maintain dashboards to track content performance and experiment results

-

Manage the interface between our content agency and internal systems to ensure seamless handoffs

-

Ensure content quality and consistency across all three markets through automated checks and validation

**Your profile:**

-

2–4 years of experience in content operations, growth marketing, or a similar role at a digital product company

-

Hands-on proficiency with automation platforms: n8n, Make, or similar - you can build workflows independently without engineering support

-

Strong working knowledge of AI tools and how to use them productively: Claude, AI agents, MCPs, custom skills

-

Experience designing and running structured experiments (A/B tests, multivariate tests, multi-armed bandits) with proper statistical rigor

-

Understanding of ASO and/or CRO fundamentals - you know what moves the needle in app stores and on landing pages

-

Understanding of structured data formats (JSON-LD, [schema.org](http://schema.org) markup) and how search engines and LLMs consume content

-

Experience with or strong interest in content personalisation across user segments

-

Familiarity with CMS platforms (ideally headless/composable like Uniform) and SEO tooling (Ahrefs, Search Console)

-

High ownership mindset - you drive problems to resolution without waiting to be directed

-

Full professional proficiency in English; German and/or Spanish are a bonus but not required

*Nice-to-haves:*

Basic scripting skills (Python, JavaScript) - not required, but it opens doors

-

Experience with AEO tooling ([Peec.ai](http://Peec.ai) or similar)

-

Experience in a multi-market content setup

-

Background in fintech or tax-related products
