# **Overview:**

We are looking for a Director People & Culture (m/f/d) to support our employees from recruiting to development, and to strategically and operationally establish and expand our HR function.

# **Your Responsibilities:**

**Onboarding & Offboarding**

-

Design and conduct structured onboarding programs to integrate new employees effectively.

-

Manage the offboarding process, including exit interviews, feedback evaluations, and process improvements.

**Employee Development & Culture**

-

Develop and implement training and development programs to foster sustainable growth among team members.

-

Actively shape a positive corporate culture by promoting team-building activities, a strong feedback culture, and high employee satisfaction.

**Support & Advice**

-

Act as the central point of contact for managers and employees on all HR-related and labor-law issues.

-

Develop HR guidelines and processes (e.g., vacation policies, working time models, compensation and bonus structures).

**Strategic Expansion of the HR Function**

-

Gradually build an HR team, including recruiting, onboarding, and developing new colleagues.

-

Collaborate closely with management and other departments to devise a future-oriented people strategy.

# **Qualifications:**

**Education & Experience**

-

Completed degree (e.g., in Business Administration, Psychology, HR Management) or comparable qualification.

-

Several years of professional experience in HR, ideally in a fast-paced environment (startup, tech company, etc.).

**Professional Competencies**

-

Solid knowledge of labor and social security law. Experience in recruiting and employee development, ideally with a focus on employer branding and cultural initiatives.

**Soft Skills**

-

Hands-on mentality and a high degree of initiative.Strong communication skills, empathy, and enjoyment in working with people.

-

Structured, solution-oriented work style and the ability to build and advance processes independently.

-

Excellent German and English skills (spoken and written).

**Perspective**

-

Motivation to shape and scale the HR function strategically.

-

Enthusiasm for working in a tech-driven environment with flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths.
