# Account Executive - DACH (Mid Market)

Posted: 2026-07-29T14:55:25Z

## **Who we are:**

At OpenUp, we believe mental well-being should be as accessible and normal as going to the gym. Our mission is simple but ambitious: to make mental well-being available to everyone.

We partner with companies to support their employees and families through proactive, practical mental health services. Our platform brings together 1:1 check-ins, courses, content, group sessions, and mindfulness practices - all supported by a dedicated network of experts. We take a holistic view, connecting mental and physical well-being through lifestyle coaching focused on sleep, nutrition, and exercise.

Today, we support over 1,500 companies and 500,000+ employees globally, including Rituals, Deloitte, Decathlon, KPMG, and McDonald’s. Recognised as a Top 3 LinkedIn Startup in 2023, 2024 and 2025 - and we've got big plans to keep growing our client-base, which is where this role comes into play.

## **Your opportunity:**

We’re growing our presence in the DACH market and are looking for entrepreneurial, purpose-driven Account Executives to help build this market with us.

In this role, you own the full sales cycle end-to-end - from identifying opportunities and opening conversations, through to closing deals and building long-term relationships. This is not a “plug-and-play” sales role; it’s an opportunity to shape how we grow in DACH.

You’ll work closely with our Business Development, Customer Success, and Marketing teams as part of a collaborative, international commercial team.

**You will:**

- Drive new business by identifying and engaging potential clients across the DACH region

- Build and expand a strong network of B2B partnerships

- Grow existing accounts by spotting expansion opportunities (including international rollouts)

- Act as a trusted first point of contact for your customers

## **
What you'll bring to our team:**

You genuinely enjoy working with people; you’re energised by conversations, building relationships, and connecting with stakeholders at different levels.

You bring a combination of drive, curiosity, and resilience. You take ownership, learn from setbacks, and continuously look for ways to improve. You’re comfortable operating in a fast-paced environment and enjoy being part of a team that builds together.

Mental well-being resonates with you as something that matters and something you want to contribute to.

**You have:**

- 3+ years of experience in sales (ideally B2B SaaS or high-growth environments)

- Strong relationship-building skills - you’re a natural, confident and engaging networker

- Experience - or interest - in building a network within HR or People teams

- Proven success in closing deals with medium- to large-enterprises

- A structured, action-oriented way of working and a relationship-focussed sales approach

- Enthusiasm for working in a highly dynamic, AI-forward international environment

- Familiarity with tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive

- Fluent language skills in both German and English
