# Backend Engineer (PHP / modern PHP framework)

PappaChat builds an AI assistant for customer conversations across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, email and telephone. The Backend Engineer will design and maintain panel APIs and integrations for messaging, telephony, payments and calendars; operate queues for knowledge-base ingestion, sends, reminders and monitoring with retries and idempotency; maintain a multi-tenant data model and performance-sensitive queries; test billing, quotas, permissions and message delivery; diagnose platform events; and participate in reviews, deployments and production troubleshooting.

Requirements: at least three years with modern PHP and a serious production application maintained after launch; relational-database knowledge including execution plans, indexes and transactions; real experience with queues, failed jobs, retries and scheduled work; third-party API integration; and careful permissions and tenant data isolation. Fluent Italian or English is required. Helpful experience includes messaging webhooks, payment subscriptions and billing webhooks, semantic/vector search, WebSockets, OAuth 2.1 and incident response. The role is in Trieste or remote in Italy, with full-time employment or contractor engagement to be agreed.
