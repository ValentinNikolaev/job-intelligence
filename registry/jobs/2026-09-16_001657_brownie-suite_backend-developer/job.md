CARREERS

Join the future of commerce.

We are looking for new talents to join our team. Curious minds, full of great ideas and strong convictions, but above all people who have the ambition to change the rules of the game, building a new model of doing business.

OPEN POSITIONS

Brownie is a fast-growing technology company, providing digital solutions to the new generations of merchants, to sell both online and offline. Our goal is to build the stores of the future.

We could never make it without our excellent team, a group of curious people who share the natural desire to work with new and exciting products and technologies.

front-end

frontend developer

We are looking for a junior front-end developer to join the team.
The candidate must have good knowledge of HTML, CSS and Javascript, and, desirable but not necessary, familiarity with PHP, in order to freely develop templates and design websites.

skill and qualifications

Proficient knowledge on how to create a html layout from scratch, not falling into an existential crisis without the Bootstrap grid.
Proficient understanding of both jQuery and JavaScript frameworks, although the candidate will always use only the first one.
Critical thinker, the ideal candidate does not work like a machine without thinking, but also wants to add its plus.
Problem-solving skills.
Team player with good time-management skills whilst enjoying doing things well.

The required skills are therefore the following:

Proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery.
Good knowledge of Adobe suite software.
Understanding of versioning and .git

also required

Creative approach to work and the desire to do / learn: we often face problems or manage projects for which we have to develop ad hoc solutions.
Elasticity and flexibility: the projects on which we find ourselves working are the most various, from simple institutional sites to complex multi-brand e-commerce to web-applications and browser games.
Good knowledge of social media adding a creative approach.
Keep abreast of latest web design trends.

back-end

backend developer

Improve our framework and always develop new web applications. We are looking for a full stack developer to join our team. At nss factory we have developed a proprietary framework and a related CMS, with which we create websites, e-commerce and web applications for us and our clients. Thanks to our system we have achieved excellent results in our sector (fashion) with constantly growing and profitable revenues. Our goal is to consolidate our framework and simultaneously manage our clients' accounts, and for this reason we are looking for new members, even junior figures, to add to the team.

skills and qualifications

Good knowledge of the LAMP stack. Good knowledge of PHP. Familiarity with frameworks and MVC / TE structures. Good knowledge of MySQL. Good knowledge of HTML, CSS, Javascript, jQuery. Good knowledge of versioning systems. Team spirit. English knowledge. Adobe Creative Suite. CSS Pre-processors

The required skills are therefore the following:

Knowledge of the LAMP stack, PHP, MySQL, HTML, CSS Javascript and jQuery. Skills and knowledge of frameworks and MVC / TE structures and versioning systems

also required

In addition to mere technical knowledge, we require a creative and solid approach to work, developing all our internal projects and working on our proprietary technology, which is constantly evolving. For this reason, flexibility and adaptability are strongly required.
