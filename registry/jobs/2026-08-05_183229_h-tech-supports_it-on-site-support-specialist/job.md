# IT On-Site Support Specialist

Posted: 2026-08-05T14:30:28Z

We are seeking a proactive and skilled IT On-Site Support Specialist to deliver exceptional hands-on technical support to our teams. In this role, you will diagnose and resolve end-user incidents, deploy and maintain equipment, manage AV and meeting room tech, and leverage cutting-edge AI and DEX automation tools to ensure seamless daily operations and superior employee service.

## Tasks

**User Support & Ticket Management:** Provide hands-on support for on-site incidents, requests, and enquiries. Accurately record and update tickets in the ITSM tool to ensure full operational traceability.

**AI & Automated Workflow Integration:** Partner with internal digital agents by executing, validating, and feedback-looping AI-guided actions and DEX (Digital Employee Experience) tools.

**Hardware & Workstation Lifecycle:** Prepare, configure, deploy, replace, and commission desktop workstations, laptops, peripherals, printers, and docking stations.

**AV & Meeting Room Operations:** Support and maintain videoconferencing setups, collaborative tools, and room AV equipment. Conduct routine preventative health checks to ensure meeting readiness.

**Relocations & Logistics:** Oversee physical workstation moves, hardware migrations, inventory tracking, and logistics operations.

**Cross-Team Collaboration:** Serve as a bridge between end-users, the global Service Desk, and specialized technical teams to escalate and resolve complex issues.

**Continuous Improvement:** Proactively spot and propose process, infrastructure, and user experience enhancements.

## Requirements

- **Experience:** 3 to 5 years of proven experience in IT on-site, desktop, or field support within a corporate environment.

- **End-User Operations:** Strong proficiency in end-user OS (Windows/macOS), mobile devices, remote connectivity (VPN, Wi-Fi), inventory management, and endpoint management tools.

- **Microsoft Ecosystem:** In-depth knowledge of Microsoft 365 applications (Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, and business apps) and modern Workplace environments.

- **AV & Collaboration:** Practical experience configuring and troubleshooting corporate videoconferencing and AV systems.

- **Problem-Solving & Soft Skills:** Strong analytical capabilities focused on first-time resolution, coupled with exceptional communication skills and a service-first mindset.

- **Tech Adaptability:** Ability to work autonomously while using standardized operating procedures and AI-driven automation systems.

**Scope of Technology Support**

- **Workstations & Mobility:** Laptops, desktops, peripherals, printers, docking stations, and mobile devices.

- **Productivity & Connectivity:** M365 suite, Wi-Fi networks, VPNs, and remote access systems.

- **Infrastructure & AV:** Conference room hardware, display systems, and interactive tools.

- **Operations & Service Delivery:** ITSM platforms, endpoint management systems, automation solutions, DEX tools, and digital support agents.
