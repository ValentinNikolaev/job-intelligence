**Welcome to Planet. We believe in using space to help life on Earth.**

Planet designs, builds, and operates the largest constellation of imaging satellites in history. This constellation delivers an unprecedented dataset of empirical information via a revolutionary cloud-based platform to authoritative figures in commercial, environmental, and humanitarian sectors. We are both a space company and data company all rolled into one.

Customers and users across the globe use Planet's data to develop new technologies, drive revenue, power research, and solve our world’s toughest obstacles.

As we control every component of hardware design, manufacturing, data processing, and software engineering, our office is a truly inspiring mix of experts from a variety of domains.

We have a people-centric approach toward culture and community and we strive to iterate in a way that puts our team members first and prepares our company for growth. Join Planet and be a part of our mission to change the way people see the world.

Planet is a global company with employees working remotely world wide and joining us from offices in San Francisco, Washington DC, Germany, Austria, Slovenia, and The Netherlands.

**About the Role: **

You will join a collaborative team of Software Engineers responsible for delivering high quality software that supports satellite-related solutions (web based GUIs and public facing APIs). You will be involved in all phases of the Software Development Life Cycle and engage with the latest software technologies. You will also be part of a cross-functional team working in an Agile, highly collaborative, cross-functional environment in which we strive to build quality into every step of the delivery process.

This role emphasizes teamwork, process improvement, and shared ownership. The ideal candidate is innovative, results-driven, proactive, and an effective communicator with a mix of curiosity and passion for satellite technologies.

This is a part-time, in-office position based in our Berlin office approximately 3 days per week.

**Impact You’ll Own:**

- Participate in all phases of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), from requirements analysis to deployment, including planning, feature implementation, test automation.

- Contribute to maintenance and improvement of existing tools and test automation frameworks, microservice applications and test data management.

- Support internal workflows and prepare documentation.

**What You Bring:**

- Enrolled in a Bachelor’s/Master’s degree.

- Basic knowledge of Python or Javascript/Typescript.

- Tech savvy: eager to learn new technologies and skills.

- Ability to work in a dynamic team environment.

- Creative thinker with the ability to approach problems from multiple angles.

- Professional working proficiency in English, the language of the company.

**What Makes You Stand Out:**

- Development background using Python, Javascript, Typescript, Git.

- Web Application development experience.

- Understanding of DevOps and CI/CD concepts.

- Familiarity with testing practices, API testing and Python (pytest)..

- Excellent organizational skills and detail-oriented.
