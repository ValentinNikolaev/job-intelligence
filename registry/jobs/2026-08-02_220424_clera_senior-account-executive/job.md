# Senior Account Executive

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

We're an early-stage AI SaaS company building the intelligence layer for wholesale distributors — helping their sales teams move from reactive to proactive, data-driven selling. Our platform integrates directly with existing ERP systems to surface opportunities, automate routine tasks, and drive measurable revenue growth for wholesalers.

We're looking for a **Senior Account Executive** to own revenue and relationships with wholesale distribution businesses. This is a high-ownership, high-impact role at the centre of our go-to-market motion. You'll work closely with SDRs, our Growth Lead, and the founding team to close meaningful deals and help shape how we sell.

**Visa sponsorship is available for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Own your pipeline end-to-end — from first discovery call to contract signature.

-

Close new business across all deal sizes, from fast-moving SMBs to larger, more complex accounts.

-

Run discovery sessions, product demos, negotiations, and pricing discussions with owners and executives in wholesale distribution.

-

Act as a trusted advisor by deeply understanding customers' operational workflows, data, and decision-making processes.

-

Translate customer pain into compelling business cases and ROI narratives that clearly demonstrate value.

-

Manage multi-stakeholder buying processes, handle objections, and drive deals to close with structure and urgency.

-

Build and maintain a healthy, predictable pipeline in close collaboration with SDRs; ensure CRM hygiene and forecasting accuracy.

-

Provide feedback to improve lead quality, messaging, and outbound strategies; partner with Marketing on campaigns.

-

Represent the voice of the customer internally to inform product and GTM strategy.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

**Native German speaker** (non-negotiable) with full professional fluency in English.

-

**3+ years of B2B SaaS sales experience** in a closing AE or hybrid Growth role.

**Required:**

-

Proven track record of consistently exceeding quota and building pipeline through structured outbound and experimentation.

-

Experience selling into mid-market or enterprise customers with non-transactional, multi-stakeholder sales cycles.

-

Prior experience selling to wholesale distributors or similar B2B industries is a strong advantage.

-

Strong business acumen — able to articulate ROI clearly to C-level leaders and business owners.

-

Comfortable with CRM systems, modern outreach tooling, and AI-assisted workflows for research, prep, and follow-up.

-

Entrepreneurial mindset: you take ownership, thrive in ambiguity, and drive initiatives end-to-end.

-

Collaborative, cross-functional approach — you work well with Marketing, Growth, and founding team members.

### Location

This role is based **on-site in Berlin, Germany**. Visa sponsorship is available.
