# Founders Associate (Sales-Focused)

Posted: 2026-08-03T20:40:10Z

### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to join a fast-growing **AI-powered content and brand visibility platform** as one of the first commercial hires — working directly alongside the CEO across sales, operations, and company building. The company operates in the emerging space of **Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)**, helping brands appear in AI-generated responses on platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The business already serves 40+ clients across 14+ industries and is backed by leading startup programmes including Google Cloud for Startups and Microsoft Founders Hub.

This role is ideal for someone early in their career who is hungry to learn, comfortable operating with ambiguity, and excited by the intersection of AI technology and go-to-market execution.

### What You'll Do

-

Partner directly with the CEO on day-to-day sales activity — from prospecting and outreach to closing deals and managing relationships

-

Own and grow pipeline, contributing directly to revenue targets and business development initiatives

-

Support the design and execution of scalable sales processes and commercial operations

-

Assist with company-building activities including hiring, partnerships, and strategic projects as the team scales

-

Work cross-functionally across sales, marketing, product, and operations — acting as a force multiplier for the founding team

-

Represent the company externally with prospects, partners, and clients
