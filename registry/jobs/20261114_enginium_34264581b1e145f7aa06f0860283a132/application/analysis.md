## Red Flags

First, fluent Italian is not confirmed in the candidate records. The position explicitly requires it for team and client conversations. Ask the candidate for an honest level and ask the employer whether English suffices for some projects. Second, the records do not establish native OpenTelemetry instrumentation or correlation of logs, traces and metrics. Monitoring and metrics work are related but narrower. Third, the posting asks for PostgreSQL execution-plan/index experience and pgx/sqlc. The candidate CV lists PostgreSQL, yet no specific example was confirmed for those tools. Fourth, AWS experience is real but the complete ECS/Lambda/SQS/Step Functions/IAM/VPC operational set is not all directly evidenced. Fifth, the gateway's per-tenant store and queues do not by themselves prove a formal cross-tenant leak-prevention design. Finally, the posted salary band may be lower than some senior markets, but no candidate-specific expectation should be inferred. These are targeted questions, not automatic disqualifiers.
