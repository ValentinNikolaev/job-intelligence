# Financial Accountant (gn)

Posted: 2026-07-27T12:00:48Z

## Your mission

The Bikeleasing Group continues on a strong growth path and makes sustainable mobility simple and attractive for companies and their employees. With our leasing model for company bikes and our multi‑benefit service, we create real added value – straightforward, flexible, and future‑oriented. Our team is growing, which is why we are looking for you as a **Financial Accountant (gn)**. You will become part of a dynamic environment that values not only professional expertise but also shared success. Our goal is clear: to further build and internationalise our teams. This means that, as part of our large accounting team, you will not only take on operational tasks, but continuously improve, expand, and optimise existing structures. With your support and input, we will continue to drive the Bikeleasing Group forward.

- You support us in the timely and accurate preparation of local quarterly and annual financial statements in accordance with German GAAP (HGB) and IFRS

- You monitor and perform monthly closings – your focus is on topics such as intercompany reconciliations, inventory valuation, provisions, and accounting of software and fixed assets

- You contribute to the documentation, implementation, and optimisation of new processes in Accounting and their interfaces

- You support analyses and take responsibility for your own small legal entity

- You are in close communication with external partners and colleagues from the group companies

## Your profile

- You are a qualified balance sheet accountant (gn) or have completed commercial training with relevant professional experience, or you hold a degree in economics or business administration

- You have sound knowledge of accounting in accordance with HGB, as well as relevant experience with SAP in financial accounting and financial statement preparation. Knowledge of IFRS accounting is a plus

- You have a strong understanding of single‑entity and consolidated financial statement processes

- Explaining complex issues in a way that is tailored to your audience is one of your strengths

- You bring strong team spirit and communication skills, combined with a high level of ownership and initiative

## Why us?

We live team spirit: for us, YOU matter. Every day, our steadily growing, dynamic team achieves great things, and we want you by our side. Here is what you can look forward to:

- We provide you with an excellent technical set‑up so you can really get started – both in our office and in your home office

- Instead of a strict office presence, you benefit from maximum flexibility thanks to a modern flexitime model and the option to work remotely

- We believe in real sparring: open communication, honest feedback, and collaboration at eye level

- Via Probonio you receive a monthly voucher worth €50 of your choice – plus €60 per year for your birthday

- In addition to attractive conditions for your company pension scheme, you also receive capital‑forming benefits

- And of course, you can lease up to two bicycles or pedelecs through us as your employer

We see diversity as an asset to our team. We therefore welcome all applications and especially want to encourage people with disabilities or other challenges to apply.
