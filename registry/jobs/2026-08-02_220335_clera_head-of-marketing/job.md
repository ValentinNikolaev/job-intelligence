# Head of Marketing

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

A fast-growing, seed-stage conversational AI SaaS company is looking for a **Head of Marketing** to own and scale its entire marketing engine — from strategy and budget through to hands-on execution. This is a foundational role at a company that automates customer call operations for B2C businesses, handling sales, support, and operational workflows via intelligent AI voice agents.

You'll translate a highly technical product into compelling positioning, build a repeatable AI-first growth motion, and generate measurable pipeline. As the company expands into the UK and US markets, you'll be a key strategic voice while remaining operationally involved from day one. This role is based on-site in **Berlin, Germany**. Visa sponsorship is not available.

### What You'll Do

-

Own marketing end-to-end — full strategic and operational responsibility including budget ownership and reporting on pipeline impact.

-

Define positioning, messaging, and content that make a technical product memorable and conversion-focused.

-

Set strategy, priorities, and KPIs across all channels; report regularly on performance and business impact.

-

Build and run a growth playbook that prioritises AI and automation to scale inbound across organic and paid channels.

-

Plan and execute go-to-market launches for new segments and geographies, including UK and US expansion.

-

Continuously test new channels using structured experiments and data-driven decision-making.

-

Operate hands-on initially, then hire and scale a high-performing marketing team as the business grows.

-

Manage vendor and agency relationships and oversee the annual marketing budget.

-

Collaborate closely with product, sales, design, and executive leadership to align on messaging and campaigns.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

5+ years of marketing leadership experience, ideally including an early-stage or high-growth environment where you owned the function.

-

Proven track record of translating technical products into clear, compelling positioning and storytelling.

-

Strong hands-on growth experience across organic and paid channels with measurable results.

-

Experience developing and executing comprehensive multi-channel strategies (digital, content, social, email).

-

Data-driven mindset — proficient with marketing analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics / GA4) and experienced in running structured experiments and tracking KPIs.

-

Proficiency with marketing automation and CRM platforms (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce).

-

Experience managing an annual marketing budget and overseeing vendor/agency relationships.

-

Deep familiarity with AI and automation tools applied to marketing workflows.

-

Fluent in both **English and German** (written and verbal).

-

Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Background in B2B SaaS or AI/voice technology marketing.

-

Experience building and leading teams while remaining operationally hands-on.

-

Prior exposure to UK or US market expansion.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

Opportunity to shape the marketing function from the ground up at a well-funded, high-trajectory startup

-

Backed by leading early-stage investors (Y Combinator and top-tier European VCs)

-

Collaborative, high-ownership culture with direct impact on company growth
