# Tax & Corporate Structuring Manager (m/f/d)

Posted: 2026-07-31T16:00:37Z

As a **Tax & Corporate Structuring Manager (m/f/d)**, you will be responsible for designing and implementing tax-efficient corporate structures that support MIRAI Power's strategic growth across Europe. Acting as a trusted advisor on tax and corporate matters, you will play a key role in transactions, reorganizations, governance initiatives, and ensuring compliance with applicable tax regulations.

## Your Contribution to the Energy Transition

- Develop and implement tax-efficient corporate structures aligned with the company’s strategic, operational and commercial objectives.

- Advise on domestic and international tax matters, assessing the tax implications of investments, financing arrangements, new business initiatives and corporate transactions.

- Lead the tax aspects of mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures, divestitures and internal reorganizations, while supporting legal entity governance and structuring initiatives.

- Ensure compliance with applicable tax legislation, transfer pricing requirements and reporting obligations, while proactively managing tax risks, audits and interactions with tax authorities.

- Collaborate closely with Legal, Finance, Treasury and external advisors, monitor regulatory developments and provide recommendations to senior management on tax planning and structuring matters.

## Your Profile

- 5+ years of professional experience in tax advisory, accounting, corporate tax, audit or the energy sector.

- Strong expertise in tax, corporate structuring, compliance and audit-related matters.

- Ability to interpret financial statements and provide recommendations to senior stakeholders.

- Strong leadership skills combined with a hands-on mindset and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.

- Fluent in German and English.
