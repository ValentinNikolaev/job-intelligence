# Platform Hardware Engineer – Data Center Accelerator Platforms

Posted: 2026-08-04T13:35:34Z

**The Role:**

We are looking for a **Platform Hardware Engineer** to help develop next-generation accelerator platforms based on Openchip silicon for AI, Machine Learning (ML) and High-Performance Computing (HPC) applications.

In this multidisciplinary role, you will contribute to the development of complex hardware platforms from concept through production, working across board design, manufacturing, bring-up, validation and system integration. You will collaborate closely with silicon, firmware, software, power, mechanical, thermal and manufacturing teams, as well as external suppliers and technology partners, to deliver reliable, high-performance products.

This is an excellent opportunity for an engineer who enjoys combining hands-on hardware development with cross-functional collaboration to solve challenging technical problems.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Support the development of accelerator boards and platform hardware throughout the full product lifecycle, from concept to production.

- Contribute to hardware architecture, schematic reviews, design integration and technical trade-off analysis.

- Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to ensure successful hardware implementation and system integration.

- Review schematics, PCB layouts, manufacturing documentation and engineering changes.

- Support component selection, qualification, lifecycle management and supply chain readiness.

- Drive hardware bring-up, debugging, root-cause analysis and board validation activities.

- Develop and execute functional, electrical and system-level validation plans.

- Investigate and resolve hardware, firmware, manufacturing and system integration issues.

- Support Design for Manufacturing (DFM), Design for Assembly (DFA), Design for Test (DFT) and broader Design for Excellence (DFX) activities.

- Work with manufacturing partners, suppliers and technology vendors throughout product development.

- Support EMC, safety and regulatory compliance activities.

- Contribute to technical planning, project execution, risk assessment and engineering

**Qualifications & Skills:**

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering or a related discipline.

- Experience in board-level hardware development, hardware validation, system integration or a related engineering role.

- Ability to read and understand schematics, PCB layouts, manufacturing documentation and component datasheets.

- Good understanding of digital hardware design, PCB development and hardware bring-up methodologies.

- Hands-on experience with laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, power supplies, electronic loads and digital multimeters.

- Familiarity with embedded systems and firmware-hardware interactions.

- Strong analytical, troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.

- Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary teams.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

- Experience developing server, networking, accelerator or data center hardware platforms.

- Knowledge of high-speed interfaces such as PCIe, DDR, Ethernet, SPI, I²C/SMBus and UART.

- Experience with Siemens Xpedition or similar PCB design tools.

- Understanding of Signal Integrity (SI), Power Integrity (PI) and high-speed PCB design principles.

- Experience with silicon bring-up, board validation or system integration.

- Knowledge of EMC/EMI design practices and regulatory compliance.

- Experience working with PCB manufacturers, assembly partners and component suppliers.

- Basic scripting or automation skills using Python, Shell or similar languages.

- Familiarity with product lifecycle management and engineering change processes.

- Experience in AI, HPC or accelerator hardware environments.
