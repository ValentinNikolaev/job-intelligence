## **What you will do:**

-

**Own our ATS (Ashby):** Get hands-on admin experience with Ashby, where you’ll manage user access, maintain templates, and help the team experiment with AI features and automations.

-

**Champion Data Quality:** Keep our recruitment engine running smoothly by maintaining dashboards, auditing candidate profiles, and finding smart, proactive solutions to keep our database spotless.

-

**Supercharge our Tech & Partnerships:** Manage our agency contracts, ensure partners have the right system access, and support integrations with new tools that make our recruiters' lives easier.

-

**Set our Recruiters up for Success:** Partner closely with recruiters to get new roles live, ensuring perfect interview pipelines, excellent candidate experience, and compliant job postings.

-

**Build our Knowledge Base:** Take full ownership of our internal Confluence space, creating and updating high-impact guides and resources that the entire team will rely on.
