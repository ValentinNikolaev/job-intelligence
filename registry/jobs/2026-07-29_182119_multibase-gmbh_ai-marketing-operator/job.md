# AI Marketing Operator

Posted: 2026-07-29T16:30:31Z

*Remote (European time zones) · Full-time · Start: Q4 2026 · Quarterly in-person team events*

*Apply with: CV + LinkedIn profile*

**We are not looking for a marketing person who understands AI. We are looking for an AI person with a marketing background.**

**About MultiBase**

MultiBase is a Business Intelligence and Analytics consultancy with 35 years of experience guiding mid-sized companies in the DACH region to turn data into profit. MultiBase's 1,500+ projects. 400+ clients. Serving the DACH region since 1991. Our audience: CFOs and Heads of Controlling.

We are at an inflection point: shifting from traditional BI consulting to an AI-accelerated, IP-driven business built on our proprietary DECIDE framework. AI is not a tool here. It is an operational layer. Our founder creates the content and sets the strategy. Our AI agents write the copy. What is missing is the person who makes the whole machine run.

## Tasks

**The Role**

You are the production and distribution engine of our marketing. Content direction and strategy come from the founder. Your job is to make that machine run at scale - and improve it as you go.

You report to the management. You will not be managed day-to-day, but you have a clear escalation path when a decision needs to be made. You interpret a brief correctly the first time, deliver without back-and-forth, and raise problems before anyone asks.

**Concretely:**

- Own and run the marketing digital workforce: build, deploy, monitor, and improve AI agents and automated workflows across marketing and business development

- Own the content calendar and the full publishing operation: YouTube uploads (titles, descriptions, tags, chapters, thumbnail briefings), LinkedIn scheduling, newsletter dispatch via our CRM. Zero missed publish dates

- Build automations that remove manual steps from our workflows (agentic platforms, CRM automations, API integrations), so the human time required in marketing goes down, not up

- Coordinate video production timelines with our editor and manage asset quality against the brief

- Brief and steer an external paid-ads specialist: audience and ICP input, tracking setup (pixel, UTM), performance review against lead quality, not vanity metrics

- Build and maintain an automated weekly performance report (YouTube, LinkedIn, newsletter, CRM) that management can read in 5 minutes

- Produce and format supporting marketing assets: presentations, papers, website updates

## Requirements

**Who You Are**

- An executor, not a talker. Your value is that things ship, reliably, without anyone chasing you

- Systematic by nature: you build checklists, SOPs, and processes because chaos offends you

- AI-native: you work with AI agents as colleagues, not as occasional tools. You brief them, challenge their output, and push back when they are wrong

- Self-correcting: when something breaks, you fix it fast and adjust the process so it does not break again

- You care about outcomes (qualified leads, published assets, hours saved), not titles

**Must-Have**

- **AI-native, proven:** daily, hands-on proficiency with at least one major agentic platform (n8n, Make, Claude Code / Cowork, OpenClaw, Hermes, or any comparable orchestration and automation platform). You have built and run agents and automated workflows, not just written prompts. This is the single most important requirement

- 2+ years in a B2B marketing environment (agency or in-house), in a small team where you owned outcomes end-to-end

- Content operations experience: you have run a content calendar and multi-channel publishing workflow (video, social, email) for a real brand

- Marketing automation: you build workflows yourself, without engineering support

- Paid ads literacy: enough to brief a specialist, review campaign performance, and catch obvious errors in targeting and tracking. You do not need to be the specialist

- Business-level English required. German at any level is a meaningful advantage for this role

- Self-directed: you do not need daily management

**Nice to Have (Not Required)**

- German: a strong plus for reviewing and quality-checking DACH content

- GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM experience

- Short-form video production (Reels, Shorts), thumbnail creation, basic editing

- Experience using LLM APIs (Claude, GPT) for marketing automation

- B2B content marketing experience for mid-market DACH companies

**This Role Is Not For You If...**

- Your primary identity is "I'm a creative" or "I'm a strategist". Strategy and creative direction are owned by the founder

- You use AI "sometimes" and cannot describe specific workflows you have built

- You need a detailed brief and daily check-ins for every task

- You cannot name concrete results from your previous marketing work

- You have only worked in large marketing departments with clear lane separation

- You accept AI output without questioning whether it is right

**What Success Looks Like**

**After 3 months:**

- You own the content calendar completely: zero missed publish dates

- Management spends zero time on content logistics: they create, the machine handles the rest

- At least two automations are live that removed manual steps from the current workflow

- At least one paid campaign is running with clean tracking and a clear performance report

- The automated weekly marketing report exists and takes 5 minutes to read

**After 6 months:**

- A consistent flow of warm, qualified leads comes from content and paid channels, without management having to run marketing operations

- The marketing digital workforce is operational, documented, and measurably reducing manual work every month
