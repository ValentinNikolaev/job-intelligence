## **About the role**

We're looking for a motivated **Working Student (Werkstudent)** to join our IT team and gain hands-on experience supporting a fast-paced, AI-driven technology company.

This is an excellent opportunity for a university student who wants to develop practical IT skills while continuing their studies. You'll work alongside experienced IT professionals, learning how technology operations support a growing global business.

Based in our office, you'll gain exposure to modern workplace technologies, IT service management, hardware operations, and AI-powered tools that are used every day across DeepL.

## **What you'll do**

As part of the IT team, you'll support a variety of day-to-day operational activities, including:

-

Prepare, configure, cleanse, and manage IT hardware for employees.

-

Maintain accurate IT asset records and update internal systems to reflect hardware changes.

-

Learn and follow IT operational processes in a live production environment.

-

Support employees by assisting with IT requests and incidents through Jira Service Management and the Atlassian suite.

-

Gain hands-on experience with corporate technologies including Google Workspace, Zoom, and Claude AI.

-

Assist with onboarding and offboarding activities, ensuring equipment is prepared and tracked correctly.

-

Provide general administrative and ad hoc support to the IT team as required.

-

Continuously develop your technical knowledge while contributing to the smooth running of our internal IT operations.

## **What we're looking for**

We're looking for someone who is curious, organised, and excited to learn.

**You'll ideally have:**

-

Current enrolment in a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.

-

A genuine interest in IT support, workplace technology, or IT operations.

-

Basic technical knowledge of computer hardware, operating systems, or troubleshooting.

-

Familiarity with tools such as Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, or similar productivity platforms is beneficial.

-

Excellent communication skills and the confidence to work with colleagues across the business.

-

A proactive mindset, strong attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple tasks.

-

Enthusiasm for learning new technologies and working in an AI-first environment.

-

Good level of English written and verbal

## **What you'll gain**

This role is designed to help you build practical experience while studying. You'll have the opportunity to:

-

Learn from experienced IT professionals through day-to-day mentorship.

-

Gain real-world experience supporting technology in a global SaaS business.

-

Develop skills with enterprise tools including Google Workspace, Zoom, Jira, Atlassian, and AI-powered technologies such as Claude.

-

Build an understanding of IT service management and corporate technology operations.

-

Work in a collaborative, supportive, and innovative team environment.
