# Application analysis — MAKEUP PHP Tech Lead

## Vacancy Summary

The role is a hands-on PHP Tech Lead position combining small-team leadership, architecture, predictable delivery, quality, reliability, and safe legacy modernization.

## Company Research

MAKEUP is described in the posting as an e-commerce retailer for cosmetics and perfumery, operating in 40 countries with a catalogue of more than 200,000 products and more than 340 million customers. The PHP Tech Lead role combines a small-team management remit with hands-on architecture, code review, critical implementation, predictable delivery, quality, testing, performance, reliability, legacy modernization, and cross-functional work. The company-specific connection used in the package is the engineering challenge of maintaining a large e-commerce product while balancing product delivery with technical debt and safe legacy change. Source: [MAKEUP PHP Tech Lead posting](https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/makeup/vacancies/375291/) (accessed 2026-10-02).

## Requirements and fit

The strongest fit is PHP/backend depth, supported Laravel and Symfony skills, production PHP ownership at CRURATED, technical leadership, e-commerce capacity experience, AWS, CI/CD, monitoring, and event-driven delivery. The CRURATED work provides the most recent PHP evidence: a production DataLake/event-analytics platform, modular stream setup, reliability work, parallel event-version publication, and a production integration. The record supports more than 15 years across PHP and Go, a five-engineer backend team at PDFfiller, and Hyprr technical direction with the CTO.

Material gaps stay explicit. The evidence does not establish two or more years of current people management for roughly three to eight people. It also does not directly establish Docker, automated-test practice, caching, transaction design, DDD, monolith decomposition, application security, or the exact current Laravel scope. These are interview questions, not keywords added to the CV. English is recorded as Upper-intermediate, while the posting does not state a language requirement.

## Final Quality Gate

**Strengths:** credible PHP framework foundation; recent production PHP integration and event-platform ownership; relevant e-commerce peak-capacity experience; and direct technical-lead history. The package remains readable because it puts the most concrete operational evidence before older roles.

**Risks:** people-management duration and current team scope may fail a strict screen; the posting’s testing, Docker, caching, transactions, and framework-depth questions need direct answers; and the concurrent CRURATED role needs a clear explanation. The Simple.life title/date conflict was resolved conservatively, but its Go focus means it should not be treated as current PHP proof.

**Mitigation:** lead screening with the CRURATED PHP ownership and describe leadership facts precisely. Ask MAKEUP how it defines the management scope. Prepare specific, truthful examples of planning, technical decisions, and review practice. If a required tool or practice is not evidenced, state it as a learning or clarification point.
