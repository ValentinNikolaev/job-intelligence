## CV Deep-Dive Questions

**How do you balance product changes with technical debt?** Describe how you would make the trade-off explicit: identify operational risk, impact on planned delivery, reversibility, and monitoring. Use your documented experience with roadmap and release planning at airSlate only as responsibility scope; do not claim an unrecorded outcome.

**How do you handle an incident?** Start with concrete evidence, containment, communication, and a follow-up change that removes the identified cause. CRURATED provides a reliability story, but the record does not support a particular incident narrative, so say what you would do rather than inventing an incident.

**What is your Laravel experience?** State Laravel as a supported skill and identify CRURATED as recent PHP-oriented work. Confirm the team’s Laravel version, testing practice, deployment model, and boundaries before claiming specific framework techniques.

**Have you led people?** State the documented five-engineer PDFfiller team and 10-developer Hyprr scope. The record does not establish the exact two-year, three-to-eight-person people-management scope requested in the posting. Say that directly, then ask how MAKEUP defines that responsibility in practice, including 1:1s, hiring, development plans, delivery accountability, and the current team structure. Do not claim an exact duration not in the record.
