# Application analysis: ITExpert PHP Tech Lead

## Vacancy Summary

ITExpert advertises a PHP Tech Lead for an unnamed international e-commerce project. The stated requirements are modern PHP with Laravel, Symfony or another mature framework, SQL, REST APIs, queues, caching, transactions, production systems and ClickHouse. The role combines planning, decomposition, prioritisation, delegation and unblocking for a small PHP team with hands-on architecture, code review and critical implementation. Remote work, a flexible start between 9 and 10, 24 calendar days of leave and Ukrainian public holidays are listed.

The candidate has source-backed PHP, Laravel, Symfony and backend leadership experience, including planning and team delivery. ClickHouse is explicitly mandatory and not established in the reviewed candidate sources. This is the largest technical uncertainty. The advertisement does not say whether the employer will accept a candidate who must learn it, so ask rather than assume either acceptance or automatic rejection.

## Company Research

**Fact:** [The Djinni posting](https://djinni.co/jobs/858063-php-tech-lead/) identifies ITExpert and describes the end client only as a large international e-commerce business, a market leader in Ukraine expanding into the EU and US. The public application form requests LinkedIn or a CV and its message field is optional in the saved HTML.

**Inference:** A lead who can discuss production trade-offs, team delivery and technical decisions is likely to be useful. This follows from the responsibilities, not from knowledge of the client's internal organisation.

**Unknown:** The client's identity, physical team location, compensation policy, contract, precise team size, technologies beyond the posting and interview stages. Confirm that the remote contract accommodates a candidate based in Italy. No company-specific product narrative can be assigned to an unnamed client.

## Strict Hiring Manager Review

The strongest evidence for this role is a combination rather than a single perfectly matching story. The candidate's production data lake work at CRURATED includes technical ownership and observed throughput and stream setup improvements. At airSlate, the candidate worked on backend bottlenecks and delivery, with separate evidence of lead responsibilities. At Hyprr and PDFfiller, the candidate led backend work or engineers. Simple.life demonstrates recent architecture and operational work, although those projects are Go-oriented.

A strict reviewer will ask about production ClickHouse, recent hands-on PHP, the size and duties of teams led, code review practice and specific trade-offs. The provided records do not establish ClickHouse. Ask the candidate an open question about actual experience and ask the recruiter how essential it is on day one. Do not transform an interview preparation exercise into a claim of production experience. Caching and transaction depth also need concrete candidate examples before claiming particular ownership.

## Red Flags

1. **ClickHouse:** mandatory in the posting and not verified. Resolve the factual experience question and discuss employer flexibility early.
2. **Recent PHP scope:** the most recent Simple.life examples are Go. The parallel CRURATED assignment is PHP-oriented, but a particular PHP implementation must be tied to its source rather than inferred from the role title.
3. **Lead scope:** demonstrate actual decomposition, review and coordination from the airSlate record and other roles. Do not conflate technical ownership with people management.
4. **Location and contract:** confirm the remote contract for Italy and the team's physical market. The Ukrainian holidays and schedule describe the offer but do not establish tax or payment terms.
5. **Dates and overlap:** retain the corrected September end date and label the CRURATED engagement as concurrent.

## Final Quality Gate

Scores out of five: relevance 4, evidence grounding 4, clarity 4, requirement coverage 3, application readiness 3. These are editorial judgments, not hiring predictions. The mandatory ClickHouse requirement remains unverified, and the remote contract needs confirmation. The application message is optional. Benchmark compensation after the team location and pay policy are established.

## Recommendation
