# Majors Account Executive

Posted: 2026-08-02T12:16:14Z

**About Rubrik**

Rubrik is on a mission to secure the world’s data. We are the pioneer of Zero Trust Data Security™, helping organizations achieve business resilience against cyberattacks, malicious insiders, and operational disruptions. Rubrik’s Centralized Data Management and Protection platform keeps enterprise data safe, discoverable, and always available.

As a **Major Account Executive for Germany**, you will drive new and existing business across specific verticals within the region, focusing on top-tier, large Enterprise accounts. You will achieve this through strategic partner planning, direct pipeline generation, and executing a sophisticated cyber-resilience sales strategy.

### **Where You Can Make An Impact**

-

**Territory Strategy:** Define and execute comprehensive sales plans within your assigned German territory to consistently meet and exceed quota through rigorous prospecting, qualifying, managing, and closing of complex sales opportunities.

-

**Pipeline Management:** Develop and manage a robust sales pipeline, effectively moving high-value, strategic transactions through the enterprise sales cycle.

-

**New Logo Acquisition & Expansion:** Identify, target, and close net-new opportunities while strategically expanding Rubrik’s footprint within existing mid-to-large enterprise accounts.

-

**Channel Ecosystem Collaboration:** Co-sell and strategize with key local partners, distributors, and VARs across Germany to accelerate market penetration and build a scalable growth engine.

-

**Market Intelligence:** Provide leadership with actionable feedback regarding the German market landscape, emerging regulatory trends (e.g., NIS2, DORA), and new business or channel partnership opportunities.

### **About You**

-

**Experience:** Ideally 10+ years of successful technology sales experience, specifically selling IT Infrastructure, SaaS, Cyber Security, or Enterprise Storage & Backup solutions.

-

**Proven Track Record:** A consistent history of landing net-new enterprise logos and driving significant expansion within existing Tier 1/Major German enterprise accounts.

-

**Pipeline Builder:** Hands-on and proactive with pipeline generation activities; collaborative with inside sales and partner teams, but entirely self-reliant when it comes to hunting.

-

**Partner Network:** Strong, pre-existing relationships within the German partner ecosystem, including major technology partners, top-tier resellers, and regional/global System Integrators (SIs).

-

**Technical & Value Acumen:** High-level understanding of complex enterprise customer infrastructures and the ability to articulate business value up to the C-suite (CISO, CIO, CFO).

-

**Methodology:** Trained in the **MEDDPICC** sales methodology (highly advantageous).

-

**Language Skills:** Fluent/Native **German** and professional proficiency in **English** are required for this role.

-

**Core Attributes:** Naturally driven, hungry for success, collaborative, and highly coachable with a strong sense of integrity. You are fiercely competitive yet value a team-first culture
