# PHP Developer Senior

Web To Emotions seeks a senior PHP developer to collaborate with its development team on robust solutions using PHP and related frameworks. The role turns creative concepts into secure, performance-optimized, user-oriented web applications; contributes to database design and API integration; and monitors and resolves code errors.

Minimum requirements include demonstrated PHP web-development experience with and without frameworks such as Laravel, Symfony, or CodeIgniter; HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and jQuery; relational databases such as MySQL or PostgreSQL and SQL; clean, structured, documented code; full-stack capability; teamwork and communication; security best practices; web-server management and production deployment. Active participation in the PHP developer community, including open-source contributions and knowledge sharing, is requested. React, Vue.js, Angular, and WordPress are nice to have.

The company offers professional development, a competitive experience-based contract, benefits, flexible hours, and the possibility of remote work.
