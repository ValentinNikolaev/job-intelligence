Professional-level requirements add real tech-lead or architecture experience, cloud-based/serverless/microservice applications, container-based development, CI/CD pipelines, distributed systems, and testing strategy for distributed platforms.

Reasonable inferences: SparkFabrik will value client-facing communication, pragmatic consulting behavior, autonomy in distributed teams, quality discipline, and willingness to work with Drupal and frontend-adjacent teams. The vacancy is not a pure backend-only role; it has full-stack adjacency through Angular and Vue and a strong enterprise consulting context.

Candidate fit is good for a Senior or Professional-leaning PHP/backend profile. Strongest evidence: 15+ years backend experience, PHP, Laravel/Symfony, REST APIs, Git, Unix-oriented cloud delivery, CI/CD, Kubernetes, AWS, performance optimization, team leadership, and long-running production systems. Main gaps: no clear Drupal 7+ evidence, limited current frontend framework evidence, and limited Italian proficiency.
