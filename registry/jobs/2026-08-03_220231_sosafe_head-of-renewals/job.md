# Head of Renewals (m/f/d)

SoSafe has the ambition to become the leading human risk management provider in Europe. Our award-winning awareness platform triggers behavioural change by providing effective and engaging training and simulations on cybersecurity and data protection. Cybercrime is costing the world >$10 trillion annually and growing by 15% p.a. — we invite you to be part of the solution!

As our **Head of Renewals (m/f/d),** you will be the architect of our renewals engine. You won't just manage a growing team of commercially-minded Renewal Managers - you will be the driving force behind protecting and expanding SoSafe's recurring revenue base. Your team owns the late-stage commercial conversation: pricing, contract terms, and hard negotiation. Your counterpart in Customer Success Management owns adoption, value communication, and problem triage - your job is to make sure that handoff is seamless, so that by the time a renewal reaches negotiation, the commercial case is already strong. Your mission is to build a predictable, commercially rigorous renewal motion that defends Gross Retention, unlocks Net Revenue Retention, and turns contract renewal into a strategic moment rather than an administrative one.

This role is based in Germany, ideally in Cologne or Berlin. We're open to other German locations for the right candidate, with travel required to connect with the team and key accounts.

**How you will contribute to our success:**

-

**Lead & Enable:** You will lead, mentor, and empower a growing team of Renewal Managers, fostering commercial rigor, negotiation craft, and the operational discipline to run a book of business like a business.

-

**Retention Optimization:** You have a sharp eye for risk, prioritizing accounts and interventions that move the needle on GRR and NRR while phasing out low-impact activities to keep the team focused on what truly protects the business. You will ensure that pricing frameworks, discount governance, and contract terms are perfectly aligned with our growth targets.

-

**Commercial Negotiation:** Together with your team, you will drive late-stage, high-stakes renewal negotiations - holding firm, defensible commercial lines while finding creative paths to close.

-

**Partnership with CSM:** You will build a tight operating rhythm with the Customer Success Management team, ensuring clean handoffs on account health, adoption signals, and open issues, so your team walks into every negotiation with full context and no surprises.

-

**Executive Stakeholder Management:** You will support your team in engaging with and influencing executive stakeholders with budget ownership and decision making authority.

-

**Cross-functional Collaboration:** You will work closely with Customer Success, Sales, and RevOps teams to ensure seamless account handoffs, accurate forecasting, and coordinated commercial strategy.

-

**Scale-up Mindset:** You will actively develop our renewals playbooks and best practices - you aren't afraid to "roll up your sleeves" to improve processes operationally.

-

**Lead by Example:** You will personally manage selected high-value or at-risk renewals to lead by example and protect our most strategic accounts.

**What success looks like in the first 6-12 months:**

-

Successful takeover and further development of the newly formed Renewals team.

-

Optimization of the renewals framework to increase forecast accuracy and reduce late-stage surprises.

-

Ensuring high Gross Retention and improving deal velocity through improved renewal enablement and effective risk mitigation.

-

Active contribution to pricing and discount governance, including clear guidance on price increase clauses and contract segmentation.

-

Achieving team targets regarding Gross Retention (GRR) and expansion at the time of renewal

**What makes you a great fit:**

-

**Renewals & SaaS Experience:** At least 5 years of experience in Renewals, Account Management, or a commercial Customer Success capacity within the SaaS environment, ideally with a focus on complex security solutions.

-

**Leadership Experience:** You have at least 2 years of management experience and are passionate about coaching a team to peak commercial performance.

-

**Operational Impact:** You cut through complexity to decisive action, ruthlessly prioritise the interventions that actually move GRR and NRR, and have the courage to kill low-impact activity. Every story you tell ends in a measurable business outcome, not just a better process.

-

**Grit:** You've turned around a losing deal, a slipping forecast, or a difficult renewal before, and you know the difference between persisting and knowing when to cut losses.

-

**Commercial Acumen:** You are comfortable holding firm, defensible commercial lines under pressure - contract negotiation, pricing methodology, and discount governance are second nature to you.

-

**Growth Mindset:** You own your setbacks, actively seek out feedback, and change your approach based on it rather than repeating the same play.

-

**Clear Communicator:** Concise, structured, and precise - whether it's a Slack update, a customer negotiation, or a business review with your Head of Department.

-

**Technological Edge:** You use AI and modern tooling to change how you and your team work, not just to work faster, and you're excited to build for a scale-up that thinks AI-first.

-

**Linguistic Fluency:** You communicate with full professional proficiency in both German (native or bilingual level) and English.

-

**Bonus Points:** Experience negotiating enterprise renewals in a cybersecurity or compliance-driven market.
