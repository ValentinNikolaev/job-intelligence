# Administrative Assistant

Posted: 2026-08-04T15:00:41Z

Here at The Exploration Company, we are developing, producing, and operating Nyx, a modular and reusable space orbital vehicle that can eventually be refuelled in orbit and that can carry cargo - and potentially humans in the longer run.

We are looking for administrative support to assist our engineering team with day-to-day operational and coordination tasks. The role is intended to offload administrative work from engineers, allowing them to focus on core technical responsibilities.

**Key Responsibilities**

-

Follow up on purchases and orders with subcontractors and vendors

-

Manage receipts and invoices using our online expense and accounting tools

-

Support travel planning and logistics for team members when required

-

Assist with planning and coordinating occasional team and site events

-

Handle other ad hoc administrative tasks as needed

**Requirements**

-

Previous experience in an administrative, operations, or support role

-

Strong organizational skills and attention to detail

-

Comfortable working with online tools for invoicing, expenses, and coordination

-

Clear written and verbal communication skills. English is the working language in the company. German is a big plus.

-

Ability to work independently and manage recurring workload

**Why you should join us!**

What makes us special here at The Exploration Company and why we think you will enjoy working here is:

-

We’re Agile - we make decisions fast whilst keeping our goals and systems in mind

-

We’re Open and Collaborative - we are transparent about risks and obstacles, so that we can cooperate to overcome them

-

We have a lot of Fun - we refuel our energy knowing we are democratising space. It’s a wonderful and rare opportunity, are YOU up for the challenge?
