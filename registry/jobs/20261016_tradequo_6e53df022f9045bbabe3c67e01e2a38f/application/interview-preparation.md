**Must prepare:** a candid stack-fit explanation, supported introduction, truthful location/language/start information and the gateway/security examples.

**Before technical:** rehearse callback failure scenarios, one Go coding exercise, database consistency and the exact scope of each numeric CV result. Separate hypothetical payment design from delivered experience.
