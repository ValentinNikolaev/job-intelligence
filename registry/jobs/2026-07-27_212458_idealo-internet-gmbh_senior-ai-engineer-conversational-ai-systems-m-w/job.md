# (Senior) AI Engineer - Conversational AI Systems (m/w/d)

Posted: 2026-07-27T20:30:41Z

We are building a conversational AI experience at idealo – a product-aware assistant that helps users explore, compare, and decide on purchases through natural dialogue. The system integrates capabilities like RAG-based access to product data, agent orchestration, and explainable result evaluation.

As an **AI Engineer**, you will combine **conceptual thinking** with hands-on engineering to design, implement, and evolve this AI-native experience. You will stay **up-to-date** in this dynamic field, continuously testing the latest AI developments and integrating them into production systems when they meet a real business need. You will work closely with our **Fullstack Developers** and AI Engineers, collaborating across the stack to deliver robust, scalable AI workflows that connect seamlessly with idealo’s product ecosystem.

### About your new role

-

Build a sustainable, modular agent-style application that integrates existing components and conversational UIs.

-

Develop and iterate frontend conversational formats (React, streaming/SSE) and connect them to backend services and prompt systems.

-

Design, implement, and evolve agent architectures using common patterns and frameworks (e.g. Strands Agents, orchestrator/sub-agent designs on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore).

-

Work with product search and retrieval pipelines to ensure relevant and up-to-date retrieval of product information.

-

Develop and evaluate prompt flows – optimizing language quality, task alignment, and output stability.

-

Define and monitor AI-specific non-functional requirements (e.g. hallucination rate, response quality, content safety, explainability).

-

Stay on top of new AI capabilities, assess them critically, and integrate them into production systems when they add clear business value.

-

Collaborate closely with product teams, Fullstack Developers, and MLEs to translate user and business needs into a flexible AI roadmap.

### **Skills & Requirements**

-

Proven experience building agent-style applications with real-world impact (LLM orchestration, tools, memory, autonomy).

-

Hands-on knowledge of retrieval / RAG concepts and implementation (must).

-

Track record in prompt engineering, prompt evaluation, and optimization strategies.

-

Strong skills in both frontend development (conversational UIs) and backend orchestration (Python).

-

Experience with retrieval architectures, context assembly, and vector stores.

-

Ability to think conceptually and strategically about AI systems, while executing on technical implementation.

-

Deep curiosity and initiative: you enjoy fast prototyping, experimentation, and iterative design.

-

Bonus: Experience in AI system scaling, performance tuning, and quality assurance.
