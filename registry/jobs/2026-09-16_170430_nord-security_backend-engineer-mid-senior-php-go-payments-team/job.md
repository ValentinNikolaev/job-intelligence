Join our team as

Backend Engineer · Mid - Senior · Go · Payments Team

Poland

Remote

Engineering

The world’s most advanced VPN, and a whole lot more.

If you’re a curious problem-solver who carves their own path, join the team behind Threat Protection Pro, the NordLynx protocol, and the fastest VPN on the planet—tools that put privacy, security, and control back in people’s hands.

Your impact?
Helping millions take back control of their online security, privacy, and data.

Main Responsibilities

Design, build, and maintain APIs, services, and systems in our Payments’ infrastructure;

Write high-quality code that is tested, maintainable, documented, and performs well under load;

Work with different payment methods from all around the world;

Integrate new service providers, each with its own code and functionality;

Improve engineering standards, tooling, and processes;

Collaborate with your team members, QA, DevOps, and security teams on day-to-day tasks;

Mentor colleagues and provide technical guidance when needed;

Perform R&D operations and find solutions to new and challenging problems we encounter;

Demonstrate ownership of tasks from start to finish;

Constantly learn and grow professionally.

Core Requirements

Proven experience working with Golang;

Experience working with PHP;

Proven experience building microservices and knowledge of microservices patterns;

Understanding of OOP principles and good code design;

That you know a thing or two about API design;

Good (My)SQL knowledge;

Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, RabbitMQ, and Redis is a plus;

That working with Git is nothing new to you.

Tools You Will Use

Golang;

PHP;

MySQL;

Redis

Valkey

KeyDB;

RabbitMQ;

Docker;

Kubernetes;

ArgoCD;

Debezium;

OpenSearch/ElasticSearch;

Grafana.

What we offer

🎓 Sharpen your edge

Growth is built into how we work. Extensive online and in-person training programs, access to platforms like Coursera and Skillshare, a mentorship program, and internal career moves when you’re ready for something new — your development has no ceiling here.

🌿 Take the time you need

Rest isn’t a reward — it’s part of the deal. Enjoy extra vacation days that grow with your time here, plus additional days off for sick leave, special occasions, and parenting.

🏥 Get premium healthcare

Your health shouldn’t be something you worry about. We provide premium private health insurance so you have peace of mind and fast access to care whenever you need it. Available in Lithuania and Poland.

🪷 Protect your peace

A peaceful mind isn’t a luxury — it’s the foundation. We provide free subscriptions to top-rated Calm, Headspace, and Mindletic apps, offer resilience and mindfulness trainings, and run dedicated mental health events.

Feeling good shapes how you work, think, and create. That’s why we offer in-house gyms, Multisport and Urban Sport cards, and online workouts. Need something tailored? Our Physical Well-Being experts are here with group mobility sessions, fitness guidance, and one-on-one consultations.

🌴 Pack your bags for workation

Picture this: the entire company flies out abroad. The days are packed with workshops, activities, concerts, and real time together. It’s legendary. If you’ve heard stories, they’re probably true.

✈️ Work from anywhere

New surroundings spark new thinking. When you need a reset, switch location and work from wherever keeps you sharp, creative, and in the zone.

🎁 Celebrate your milestones

The moments that make life special shouldn’t pass quietly. Birthdays, work anniversaries, weddings, new family members — each is marked with a special gift.

👶🏻 Parent with ease

When you’re a parent, the juggle is real. To make it smoother, we organize summer camps for the little ones and offer flexibility that lets you show up both for your family and your team.

🎉 Join the party

It never gets boring here. Get ready for team buildings that bring people closer, year-round company events that are equal parts educational and energizing, and the highlights everyone waits for — our iconic summer and winter celebrations.

⭐ Unlock Cyber City

Whether Vilnius-based or visiting from remote, our Vilnius HQ is fully yours to enjoy. Need focus? Silent room. Need energy? Open gyms. Need a break? Game room, music room, coffee bar. It’s a modern workspace built with your comfort in mind.
