### **About the role**

We're looking for an impact-focused **Senior Backend Engineer** to join **Bookineers**, the team that owns [Hostaway](https://himalayas.app/companies/hostaway)'s guest-facing services: the **guest portal** and the **direct sales site builder**. These products are how our customers' guests discover, book, and interact with their stays — they're public-facing, high-traffic, and directly tied to our customers' revenue.

You'll work on services that need to be fast, reliable, and scalable. A big part of our current focus is **extracting existing services out of the monolith** to give us the autonomy, performance, and resilience these guest-facing flows require. In parallel, we're starting to **expose our domain through APIs designed for agentic interfaces** — opening up new ways for guests and partners to interact with [Hostaway](https://himalayas.app/companies/hostaway). You'll have a real impact on the architecture, the roadmap, and how the team operates.

### **What you'll do**

-

Design, build, and operate backend services in **PHP and Go** that power the guest portal and [direct sales site builder](https://www.hostaway.com/features/direct-booking/).

-

Drive the **extraction of services out of our monolith**, defining clean boundaries, ownership, and migration paths.

-

Collaborate closely with **Product and Design** to shape and ship features that move the needle for direct bookings.

-

Design and ship **APIs built for agentic interfaces** — making our guest-facing domain consumable by AI agents and automated workflows.

-

Improve **page speed and end-to-end performance**, working closely with frontend peers since conversion depends on it.

-

Contribute to high-level architectural decisions and help us move quickly without accumulating regret.

-

**Leverage AI to accelerate code delivery** — we provide a Claude Team Premium subscription and expect engineers to use it well.
