# Senior Account Executive, Commercial Sales - DACH

Posted: 2026-08-05T11:55:23Z

**The Team + The Role**

Pendo is hiring a Senior Account Executive for our Commercial Sales team in Munich, Germany to drive new business across our the DACH region to target prospect accounts. This role is built for high-performing Senior Commercial with a proven track record of closing self generated deals across and running sales motions with autonomy and excellence.

You’ll lead complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles, selling cutting-edge Data & Analytics solutions powered by AI—backed by exciting product innovation and momentum at Pendo—while accelerating growth in one of our most strategic segments.

***This role is a hybrid position working 3 days per week from our office in Munich. ***

**What this looks like day-to-day**

- Win new enterprise logos across Germany and the DACH region

- Lead complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles from first engagement to close

- Consistently deliver and hit quotas

- Build trusted relationships with C-level and senior decision-makers

- Develop and execute clear, strategic account plans

- Position Pendo’s AI-powered Data & Analytics platform as a critical driver of digital transformation

- Collaborate cross-functionally to win and expand strategic accounts

- Maintain accurate forecasting and disciplined pipeline management
