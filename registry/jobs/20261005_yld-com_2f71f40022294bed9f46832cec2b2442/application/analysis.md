## Strict Hiring Manager Review

Strengths: recent Go platform and gateway ownership; measured production scale and reliability; backend leadership across product and security work. Three weaknesses warrant direct preparation. First, no named TDD practice or complete testing strategy appears: this matters because YLD emphasizes tests across the lifecycle; discuss a real test-first example if one exists, without adding TDD to the CV now. Second, client-facing consultancy delivery is less explicit than product-team delivery: the Client Partner may probe how technical decisions are explained commercially; use a documented design and cross-functional launch example, then describe the candidate’s actual stakeholder role. Third, employment chronology is complex: Simple.life’s end month remains unresolved and CRURATED overlaps; the factual rewrite uses “November 2023 - 2026” and calls CRURATED concurrent consulting.
