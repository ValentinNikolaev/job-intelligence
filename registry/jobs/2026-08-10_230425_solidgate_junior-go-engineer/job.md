### **About the Role**

You’ll join the **Billing team **as a** Go Engineer**, which is building Billing v2 — a big update to how our subscription system works. There’s no old legacy code to deal with, so you’ll be learning and building on a fresh, modern codebase from the start.

Billing is one of the key parts of our product — it’s responsible for issuing bills, retrying failed payments, managing subscriptions, and helping the company earn revenue.

Your team: 1 Product Manager, 3 Senior Engineers, 1 Automation QA, 1 Domain Analyst.

### **What you’ll own**

- Building automated email reminders that recover failed payments and keep subscriptions active
- Contributing to a new Billing Dashboard where merchants can see their revenue, subscriptions, and payment stats
- Taking part in splitting a payment-retry feature into its own separate service, with a clean API between it and the main system
- Working on the Coupons service — building its new API and moving existing users over to it

**You’re a great fit if you have**
