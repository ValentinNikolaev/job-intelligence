# Senior Fullstack Engineer

Posted: 2026-08-10T06:28:24Z

Come and push your journey to the next level!

At [Immense Group](https://himalayas.app/companies/immense-group), we are guided by our core values of passion, boldness, and integrity. We strive to create an environment where our people thrive, driven by a love for innovation and a commitment to delivering exceptional player experiences. If you’re passionate about iGaming and tech development bold in your approach, and act with integrity, we’d love for you to join our team as a Senior Fullstack Engineer.

### A taste of what you’ll do

The Senior Fullstack Engineer is a technically accomplished and influential contributor within Immense Studio, bringing deep expertise across both backend and frontend disciplines to bear on the platform that underpins our game portfolio. Beyond delivery, this role helps shape how the team builds — contributing to architectural direction, setting quality standards through code review and mentorship, and taking ownership of complex, high-impact features end-to-end. The ideal candidate has a proven track record in fullstack platform development, thrives with autonomy, and is motivated by leaving things meaningfully better than they found them.

### Engineering and architecture

- Design and deliver robust backend services, frontend UI components, data models, messaging systems, and API specifications across the studio's game platform.

- Take a leading role in architectural discussions and technology research, contributing considered, well-reasoned perspectives that help shape the platform's long-term direction.

- Translate complex functional requirements into precise, elegant technical designs that balance immediate delivery needs with sustainable long-term maintainability.

- Write high-quality, well-documented code with performance, reliability, and testability as non-negotiable standards.

### Quality and technical leadership

- Work within and actively champion a test-driven development culture, taking personal accountability for the correctness and resilience of your output.

- Lead thorough peer code reviews that raise engineering standards across the team, providing specific, actionable, and constructive feedback.

- Act as a technical reference point for less experienced engineers, sharing knowledge, supporting problem-solving, and contributing to a culture of continuous learning.

- Proactively identify technical debt, architectural risks, and opportunities to improve platform quality — and drive the work to address them.

### Delivery and collaboration

- Manage individual project priorities, deadlines, and deliverables with a high degree of autonomy, taking ownership of outcomes rather than just tasks.

- Work closely with QA analysts, product stakeholders, and fellow engineers to deliver features that fully meet functional, technical, and commercial requirements.

- Contribute to the identification and implementation of process and automation improvements across the development lifecycle.

- Maintain accurate, up-to-date tracking of work, issues, and progress using project management tooling (JIRA or equivalent).

### Who and what we are after?

- 4+ years of professional experience as a Fullstack Engineer or in an equivalent senior role, with a demonstrable track record of platform-level delivery.

- Solid backend development experience in PHP, Python, or a comparable server-side language, with the ability to design and own services from the ground up.

- Experience working with distributed systems and microservice architectures at scale.

- Ability to work independently with a high degree of autonomy, taking initiative and driving outcomes without close direction.

- Strong written and verbal communication skills in English.

- Exceptional attention to detail and a genuine, deep commitment to software quality.

- Familiarity with DevOps practices and containerisation technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) in cloud-based environments.

- Strong proficiency in JavaScript and TypeScript, with hands-on experience across modern frontend frameworks (Angular, Vue, React, or equivalent) and contemporary build tooling.

### Nice to have

- Experience in C# and .NET as an additional backend capability.

- Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.

- Experience within an iGaming or online gaming platform environment.

- Background in mentoring or technically leading junior engineers within an agile team setting.
