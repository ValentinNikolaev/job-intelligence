## **The Opportunity**

You will build and own customer-facing features end-to-end — and alongside that, work with the team on the data platform they stand on: ingestion pipelines, the construction ontology and the project graph.

Each customer project consists of 300 - 3,000 files: PDFs, construction drawings, scans, 3D models. Performant ingestion and a well-structured graph are the foundation for everything we are building from accuracy to ROI and user experience.

## **What You'll Do**

***Build and own end-to-end features***

-

-

From concept, to db schema to UI - own the full development lifecycle of customer-facing features

***Manage our web application & APIs***

-

Maintain and improve our web application's security and performance; build out existing and new features

***Work on the construction ontology & project graph***

-

Help model and evolve canonical entities of our customers

-

-

-

Extend our multi-modal ingestion of unstructured documents: PDFs, scans, drawings, 3D models. Orchestrated and cost-efficient at scale

***Help build the abstraction layers that turn use cases into a platform***

-

Contribute to the workflow harness, decision-trace standard and eval tooling so a new use case ships in ~1 month

-

Build reusable human review & approval surfaces where trust in AI output is earned
