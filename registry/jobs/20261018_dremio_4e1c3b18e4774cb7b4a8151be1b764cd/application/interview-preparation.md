## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview

Prepare the following questions as brief STAR outlines, separating personal decisions from team activity and leaving unknown outcomes unfilled:

1. When did you turn an ambiguous integration goal into an executable design? Use the helpdesk gateway and describe the domain model, adapters and demo milestone.
2. How did you make a consequential security decision? Use telephony ownership, formal review and the distinction between agent access and administration.
3. How have you protected other teams from a breaking change? Use production event-version publication and explain actual compatibility decisions.
4. Describe technical disagreement or a trade-off. Select a real gateway or roadmap decision; do not fabricate a conflict merely to complete the story.
5. How did you balance delivery and long-term maintainability? Use pipeline consolidation with preserved interfaces and no downtime.
6. How have you influenced a team without formal authority? Prepare an actual collaboration from CRURATED or Simple.life, identifying who decided what.
7. What evidence changed your diagnosis? Use the airSlate database or API performance work and explain the observations personally available.
8. How have you helped another engineer improve? Draw on actual onboarding or knowledge-sharing duties; do not invent a mentoring outcome.

For each outline, rehearse the problem, the boundary of responsibility, the decision, validation and result. Avoid turning a platform result into a personal achievement. If the interviewer asks about a failure, choose a real event and describe the corrective action without blaming a vendor or colleague.
