- Manage and control all reconditioning operations with a strong focus on safety, quality, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, profitability, productivity, and cost management

- Drive performance improvement within underperforming facilities by working closely with local management and the Business Improvement team

- Develop and execute the operational strategy for the reconditioning network in line with the overall business objectives and growth plans

- Follow up on major capital investment projects to ensure delivery on time, within budget, and according to defined business objectives

- Ensure the implementation, sustainability, and continuous development of a Lean culture across production and support functions, including Earnings Improvement Projects

- Continuously improve efficiency, productivity, quality, and operational excellence through standardization, best-practice sharing, and process optimization

- Ensure accurate financial reporting, budget control, forecasting, and timely resolution of questions from Corporate Headquarters
