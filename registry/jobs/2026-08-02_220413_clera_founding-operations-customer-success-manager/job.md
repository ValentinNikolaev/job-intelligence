# Founding Operations & Customer Success Manager

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to join a fast-growing AI-focused B2B SaaS company as their **first dedicated Operations & Customer Success hire**. You'll work directly alongside the founding team to onboard, support, and grow a portfolio of 40+ B2B clients across 14+ industries. The company builds an AI Visibility Content Engine that helps brands appear in AI-generated answers and overviews across leading platforms. You'll be foundational to how clients experience the product and how the team scales.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the end-to-end onboarding experience for new B2B clients, ensuring a smooth and value-driven start

-

Serve as the primary point of contact for a growing portfolio of clients, building strong relationships and driving retention

-

Identify client needs and translate them into actionable product feedback for the founding team

-

Develop and refine scalable processes for client communication, reporting, and success tracking

-

Collaborate closely with the founders on operations, tooling, and internal workflows as the company scales

-

Support upsell and expansion conversations by demonstrating product value and understanding client goals

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

0–2 years of experience in customer success, operations, or a startup environment with a focus on onboarding and supporting B2B SaaS clients

-

Full professional fluency in both **German and English**

-

A proactive, ownership-driven mindset — comfortable in an early-stage environment where processes are still being built

-

Strong communication and organisational skills, with an ability to manage multiple client relationships simultaneously

**Nice to Have:**

-

Familiarity with AI-focused or content-engine SaaS products (e.g. AEO, GEO, or SEO tooling)

-

Experience translating client feedback into concrete product improvement requests

-

Background at a consulting firm, growth-stage startup, or tech company in a client-facing role
