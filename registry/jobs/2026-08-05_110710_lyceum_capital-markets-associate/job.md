# Capital Markets Associate

Posted: 2026-08-05T08:40:44Z

## **Your mission**

Building Europe's sovereign GPU cloud takes serious capital - and you'll be the one raising it. Working closely with our CFO, you'll own the full financing stack: equity, debt, and strategic partnerships. From modelling GPU project finance to structuring term sheets and running data rooms, you'll turn our growth plans into the capital that funds them. This is a rare chance to shape the capital structure of a company building critical AI infrastructure from the ground up.

**What You'll Do**

-

Source and manage relationships with equity investors (VC, growth, sovereign wealth), debt providers (banks, infra funds, asset-backed lenders), and strategic partners

-

Build and maintain detailed financial models for fundraising: DCFs, LBO-style returns analyses, project finance models for GPU infrastructure

-

Structure and negotiate term sheets, credit facilities, and co-investment arrangements

-

Own the data room, investor materials, and due diligence process for all capital raises

-

Monitor capital markets for relevant financing opportunities, benchmark Lyceum's capital structure against peers, and advise on optimal timing and instruments

-

Support grant and subsidy applications (e.g. IPCEI, EU Innovation Fund) where relevant

## **Your profile**

**What We're Looking For**

-

2-4 years in investment banking (coverage or capital markets), infrastructure/project finance, private credit, or growth equity

-

Strong financial modelling and structuring skills across equity and debt instruments

-

Track record of investor or lender relationship management

-

Understanding of infrastructure economics, asset-backed financing, or technology capital markets

-

Fluent in English; German is a plus

-

High conviction about the opportunity in European AI infrastructure
