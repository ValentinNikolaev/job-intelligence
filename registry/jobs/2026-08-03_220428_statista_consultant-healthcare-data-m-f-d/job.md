**About Statista Healthcare**
Statista Healthcare is a vertical business unit of Statista, focused on making healthcare quality transparent, measurable, and comparable across countries, care settings, and specialties. We work with leading healthcare stakeholders worldwide to support data-driven quality measurement and transformation by combining in-depth analytical expertise with scalable technology and communication power.

Our offerings span international rankings, ratings, benchmarking studies, and customized consulting projects. Our global healthcare rankings and hospital ratings are published in collaboration with our trusted media partner Newsweek, enabling high visibility and credibility across markets.

We help healthcare organizations define relevant key performance indicators, validate and harmonize data structures, and translate insights into actionable strategies. At the heart of our approach is a data driven understanding of healthcare quality, grounded in robust methodologies, global comparability, and stakeholder trust.

In our consulting projects, we support healthcare providers and systems in developing tailored KPI frameworks, standardizing and validating data processes, and deriving actionable insights that enable measurable improvements in patient care and system-level performance. We combine structured methodologies with hands-on delivery to unlock the full value of healthcare quality data.

**Your Role**
As Data Science Consultant Statista Healthcare, you will join our international team of data professionals, supporting the analytical backbone of Statista’s healthcare activities.

Your work will focus on the collection, processing, and analysis of healthcare data that power our Rankings, Ratings, and Benchmarking projects, as well as the development of new, data-driven products.

In Benchmarking and custom quality development projects with healthcare providers and other stakeholders you will be part of an interdisciplinary project team that works directly with our clients.

You will work hands-on with large and diverse datasets, transform raw data into clean, structured information, and contribute to the automation of our analytical workflows.

In close collaboration with internal analysts, engineers, and project managers, you will ensure that our healthcare data infrastructure remains reliable, scalable, and ready for future integration into the Statista Healthcare Data Platform.

Your work will directly support Statista’s mission to make healthcare quality measurable, comparable, and actionable - globally.
