# Business Operations Manager

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

We are a growing biotechnology company based in Regensburg, Germany, focused on enhancing the bioavailability and functionality of plant-based ingredients for use in nutraceuticals, food, pharmaceuticals, and personal care. With a lean team of around a dozen people, we develop and produce proprietary ingredient technologies that help brands create more effective, natural products.

We are looking for a hands-on **Business Operations Manager** to keep our day-to-day operations running smoothly. You will sit at the intersection of production coordination, procurement, logistics, and administration — working closely with our internal team, production partners across Europe, and global suppliers. This is a central, high-ownership role that directly supports the company's operational reliability and continuous improvement.

### What You'll Do

-

Ensure productions run on schedule and that materials arrive on time.

-

Coordinate production planning and monitor associated orders.

-

Oversee logistics and maintain visibility on transports and delivery timelines from factory to customer.

-

Manage procurement of production materials and laboratory equipment.

-

Serve as the primary interface to European production partners and global suppliers.

-

Handle administrative tasks and provide support in financial areas such as budgeting and basic accounting.

-

Work directly with our ~12-person internal team, management, and external logistics providers.

-

Continuously improve procurement and process workflows and help build operational structure from the ground up.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Completed vocational training or degree in operations, logistics, procurement, supply chain, business administration, or equivalent.

-

Relevant hands-on experience in operations, logistics, procurement, supply chain, or administration.

-

Demonstrated ability to coordinate production scheduling and manage procurement of production materials and lab equipment.

-

Experience managing logistics from factory to customer, including oversight of transportation timelines.

-

Fluent German **and** English — minimum C1 level in both spoken and written communication (this is a dealbreaker requirement).

-

Confident, proficient use of MS Office, especially Excel/spreadsheets.

-

Strong organizational skills with the ability to prioritize across multiple concurrent workstreams.

-

Solution-oriented and pragmatic mindset with a strong bias for action and ownership.

-

Enthusiasm for building processes and structures in an early-stage environment.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience with ERP or process management systems.

-

Background supporting budgeting and accounting processes.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

-

Full-time, permanent position with flexible working hours.

-

Hybrid work arrangement with home office options.

-

30 days of vacation.
