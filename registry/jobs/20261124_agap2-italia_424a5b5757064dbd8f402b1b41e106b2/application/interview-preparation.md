# Interview preparation — agap2 Italia

## Recruiter / HR Screening

Describe your background in PHP and Go backend development, production reliability and technical leadership. Distinguish the concurrent part-time CRURATED engagement from your Simple.life role. The full posting says the first two weeks are on site in Naples and the role is remote thereafter. Confirm whether you can meet that condition, and ask how travel, lodging and the onboarding schedule are handled. The advertised range is €30,000–40,000 under CCNL Commercio e Terziario, with exceptional cases subject to approval. Establish the actual contract and team arrangements before discussing a specific expectation through the project's compensation workflow.

The vacancy is written in Italian, but it does not give a language level. Ask which language the team uses for meetings, written work and customer communication. Do not imply a proficiency level that the candidate record does not establish. The Easy Apply route inspected contains contact information, a resume and an optional Top Choice step. No mandatory cover letter was indicated, and reaching its review screen did not submit an application.

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview

Prepare a real example of working with product and engineering colleagues at airSlate. Describe how you divided responsibility, made a technical decision and followed the work through delivery. The candidate-confirmed source establishes backend-lead scope, not sole implementation of every team result. For another example, use the Simple.life telephony launch. Explain your architecture and backend responsibility, the involvement of frontend and SRE, and the security review. Do not frame a cross-functional team as direct reports without evidence.

Mentoring is in the vacancy. Select a specific instance of helping a colleague, giving review feedback or onboarding an engineer that you can describe accurately. If you cannot recall the effect, say what you did rather than inventing a measurable result. Expect questions on how you balance hands-on work and technical direction, and how you handle disagreement over a design or implementation.

## Technical Interview

Review the PHP and Laravel versions and features you have actually used, and prepare one example of maintaining or evolving a production application. Discuss dependency boundaries, error handling, testability and a migration path for an existing monolith. The vacancy asks for design patterns and scalable, maintainable architecture, but naming patterns is less useful than explaining a real design choice and its consequences.

Be candid about JavaScript and Vue 3. The reviewed candidate sources do not establish production Vue 3 delivery. If asked, distinguish what you have used from what you would need to learn. Ask whether the position requires independent frontend implementation or collaboration on backend integrations. Similarly, your experience with LLM integrations does not establish that you have built MCP integrations. Explain the actual support automation work and its boundaries instead.

For relational databases, revisit a real airSlate performance problem: how the team identified load, what was changed and what the supported outcome was. Prepare to discuss indexes, query plans, transactions and concurrency conceptually, while identifying which techniques you personally used. Do not supply invented database metrics or claim that a particular SQL statement caused an improvement without evidence.

## CV Deep-Dive Questions

The Simple.life helpdesk gateway gives you a concrete integration story. Explain the vendor-neutral model, the adapters and how the service supported the first business-to-business demonstration. Identify your contribution and the limits of the released work. For telephony, describe how masking and server-side handling of customer phone numbers reduced exposure, and how security review preceded production. Avoid claiming a legal certification beyond the documented reviews.

At CRURATED, prepare the analytics pipeline and event-version decisions. The candidate confirmed technical ownership and production use. Explain the need for consumers to handle changes while maintaining compatibility, if that reflects your actual decisions. The integration example can cover account linking and purchase verification. At airSlate, explain the Laravel/Symfony logger package and why a consistent interservice standard mattered, without inventing adoption figures.

## Company-Specific Preparation

The company lists IT among its activities. The posting describes complex web applications and a technical reference role, but does not name the client or application. Ask who owns the product, the size and composition of the team, and whether the work is for one client or changes between assignments. The description mentions LLM and MCP features; ask what is already in production and what problem the team expects those features to solve. Do not assume a particular architecture or customer from the agency's public site.

## Preparation Plan

Before the conversation, confirm the Naples onboarding condition and list any practical questions about the schedule. Review PHP and Laravel work from the source-backed career record. Prepare concise stories for database reliability, cross-team delivery and secure telephony, each with the problem, your contribution and a supported outcome. Write down your actual frontend exposure and the boundaries of your Vue knowledge. Rehearse a direct answer on Italian and English working languages based on your own ability. Finally, prepare questions about the role's frontend share, client and compensation structure.

## Questions to Ask

- What share of the work is PHP and Laravel backend, and what share requires hands-on Vue 3 development?
- Is the initial two-week onboarding fully on site, and are travel and accommodation supported?
- Which language does the team use in meetings and in written technical work?
- Who owns the application and how much autonomy does this team have over architecture?
- What database and concurrency issues are currently most difficult for the team?
- What are the planned AI and MCP use cases, and what would success look like in the first three months?
