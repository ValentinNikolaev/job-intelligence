## Red Flags

The Italian-language posting does not state a language threshold, so do not invent one or assume the team works in English. The role's frontend expectations, client identity and actual pay flexibility are also unknown. An advertised range should not be converted into the candidate's desired salary without the required benchmark workflow.

## Recommendation

This is a conditional fit based on backend and leadership experience. Clarify the expected depth of Vue 3 work, the daily team language, the Naples onboarding arrangements and the project context before committing to an application. The office requirement may be acceptable, but it should not be silently assumed. If the team needs independent frontend delivery from day one, the unverified experience is a material concern. If the scope is chiefly backend with collaboration on integrations, the supported PHP and technical leadership background is more directly relevant.
