# Application analysis — Sezzle Senior Software Engineer (Turkey)

Sezzle seeks a senior engineer for a backend-focused role using Go and React. The stated work includes distributed architecture, shipping features, partnering with Product and Support, mentoring and collecting metrics. It asks for at least seven years of full-stack experience, Go REST APIs, distributed cloud experience and practical use of Claude or equivalent AI tools. The employer lists a gross monthly range of USD 5,000–9,500. That band is not a personalised salary recommendation.

The employer's own form identifies the location as Türkiye, Remote and requires a Turkish work-authorisation answer. The aggregator's EMEA label does not establish that someone working from Italy is eligible. This should be clarified before investing in the application process. The form also requires full-stack years, undergraduate GPA and monthly gross salary expectation. The letter field is optional.

A direct eligibility confirmation is more valuable than assuming EMEA includes Italy.

## Strict Hiring Manager Review

The strongest evidence is the Go helpdesk gateway with vendor-neutral events and SQS, the production telephony backend, and the Simple.life LLM support work. Together they demonstrate relevant service ownership and a support-facing environment. CRURATED adds confirmed technical ownership of production analytics and parallel event publication. AirSlate supports team delivery, database reliability and release experience.

A hiring manager may still ask for a specific example of making a distributed-system trade-off, the failure and recovery path, and the candidate's exact role in architecture decisions. Do not turn technical ownership into sole authorship. The React requirement could be material, particularly if frontend delivery is expected regularly. A technical interview should establish the actual split before assuming that the backend focus removes it.

## Red Flags

1. **Eligibility:** the employer asks about Turkish work authorisation. The candidate's answer and the employer's acceptance of work from Rome are unknown. Ask whether this requisition permits the proposed working arrangement and confirm authorisation before answering.
2. **Full-stack depth:** seven years is explicit, but available evidence principally establishes backend work. Confirm actual frontend contribution and duration, including any React work, rather than entering total career length.
3. **Form data:** undergraduate GPA and monthly USD expectation are required. Obtain a real GPA and defensible conversion only if applicable. Research the team's physical market and pay policy under the compensation workflow before calculating an expectation. Contract type may change the comparison.
4. **AI tools:** product integration is supported, but the requested personal use of Claude or equivalent needs confirmation. Describe only actual tools and use cases.
