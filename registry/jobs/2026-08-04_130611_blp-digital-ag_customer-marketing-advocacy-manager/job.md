# About The Role

Our customers are our strongest asset - and their stories are still largely untold. As our Customer Marketing & Advocacy Manager, you will own the customer voice at BLP end-to-end: building the advocacy program that turns 550+ enterprise customers into referenceable champions, published case studies, review-site presence, and speakers who carry our story into the market. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with full ownership of the advocacy motion. You will work at the intersection of marketing, sales, and customer success - and directly with CFOs, COOs, and operations leaders at some of Europe's leading industrial enterprises

# What You'll Do

-

**Build and run the customer reference program**. Identify, recruit, and nurture referenceable customers; manage the reference pipeline for sales; match the right customer voice to the right deal stage.

-

**Produce customer proof.** Own case studies, success stories, and customer quotes end-to-end - from securing internal and customer-side approvals through interviews to published assets for web, sales decks, and campaigns.

-

**Own review-site presence.** Drive systematic review generation and management on platforms like G2 and Gartner Peer Insights, and turn ratings into market-facing proof.

-

**Recruit customer speakers.** Source and prepare customer champions for webinars, events, analyst conversations, and press opportunities.

-

**Partner with Customer Success and Sales. **Establish a structured interface with account teams - joint identification of advocacy candidates, coordinated asks, and a shared view of which customers are asked for what, and when.

-

**Measure and report.** Track reference coverage by industry and use case, advocacy asset usage in the sales cycle, and the pipeline influence of customer proof.

-

**Feed the wider marketing engine. **Supply customer evidence to campaigns, website, PR, and executive presentations, ensuring every claim we make is backed by a real customer outcome.
