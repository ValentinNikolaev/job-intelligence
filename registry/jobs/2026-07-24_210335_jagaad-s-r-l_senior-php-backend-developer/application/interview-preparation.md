Prepare a concise account of 15+ years in backend engineering, recent Go platform work, and continued PHP/Laravel/Symfony relevance. Confirm Rome location, remote-work setup, English level, notice period, compensation expectations, and whether the engagement model works. Explain the Simple.life/CRURATED date overlap factually; do not improvise dates.

## Culture Fit / Behavioral Interview

Likely questions: working in a distributed team; handling a difficult delivery deadline; collaborating with product/support; improving an unclear process; receiving feedback; balancing quality and sprint commitments; resolving an incident; mentoring or sharing knowledge; making a technical trade-off; and adapting to a client’s changing requirements.

STAR material: Simple.life message-delivery reliability and Zendesk-to-Intercom migration; airSlate ECS-to-Kubernetes migration and API troubleshooting; Hyprr prototype-to-beta delivery; PDFfiller peak-traffic leadership. Use only documented outcomes.

## Technical Interview

**High Priority:** PHP, Laravel/Symfony experience, REST API design, API integration failure handling, MySQL/PostgreSQL query performance, production debugging, and code-quality practices you can substantiate.

**Medium Priority:** microservices, RabbitMQ, AWS, Kubernetes, CI/CD, observability, retries/fallbacks, and deployment safety.

**Low Priority:** front-end/mobile implementation ownership, a specific Jagaad cloud provider, or testing framework details not stated in the vacancy.

Expect to defend API performance investigations at airSlate, the design of resilient messaging at Simple.life, and operational decisions for a 50-million-email/month service.

## CV Deep-Dive Questions

Be ready to explain: why PHP remains a strong fit alongside recent Go work; your exact contribution to the ECS-to-Kubernetes migration; how you approached API/query bottlenecks; the responsibilities of a five-person team lead; and the scope of the Hyprr technology roadmap. Distinguish direct implementation from leadership or collaboration.

4. What does code review and quality assurance look like?
5. Which database, cloud, and delivery tools are used most often?
6. How does the remote team coordinate across time zones?
7. What would a successful first 90 days look like?
8. Is the role assigned to one long-running product or several client engagements?
