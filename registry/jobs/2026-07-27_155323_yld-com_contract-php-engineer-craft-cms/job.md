**Location:** Brazil (Remote)

## About the role

As a Senior Software Engineer at YLD, you'll join a client team building and evolving digital products for a high-traffic, experience-led organisation. We're looking for someone curious, driven, and enthusiastic — skilled at problem-solving and experienced in delivering clean, maintainable PHP on modern CMS platforms.

You'll work closely with the client's in-house engineering team on a mix of ongoing Craft CMS feature work and a larger migration from a legacy CMS, with UI/UX rework in scope. The team is collaborative and values direct partnerships over black-box agency arrangements — which is exactly the kind of engagement YLD runs.

## Your day-to-day responsibilities will look like this

- Building and maintaining features on a production Craft CMS platform, with consistent commits and a short feedback loop;

- Contributing to the migration of a legacy CMS onto Craft, including UI/UX rework, targeting mid-2026 launch;

- Working closely with client engineers and a product designer, collaborating on engineering initiatives and keeping each other sharp;

- Mentoring teammates and sharing knowledge across the team to accelerate growth;

- Participating in regular show and tells, promoting your work to the wider team;

- Maintaining production quality and monitoring in line with SLAs;

- Using good judgement about where emerging tools (including AI) genuinely improve the work.

## Preferred Skills

- Strong PHP development skills, with production experience on Craft CMS;

- Solid understanding of the Craft CMS templating system, plugin ecosystem, and content modelling;

- Experience migrating content from legacy CMS platforms: familiarity with Epic Server or similar is a plus;

- Frontend competence: working with designers to implement UI/UX rework, including responsive design and accessibility;

- Database design and query optimisation (relational databases; SQL Server experience useful);

- API development (REST), with an understanding of how CMS platforms expose and consume data;

- CI/CD pipelines and version control (Git) in a team environment;

- Comfortable with cloud-hosted environments and standard deployment tooling;

- Monitoring and performance awareness: you keep an eye on things in production;

- You stay calm when debugging live issues and communicate clearly about what's happening.
