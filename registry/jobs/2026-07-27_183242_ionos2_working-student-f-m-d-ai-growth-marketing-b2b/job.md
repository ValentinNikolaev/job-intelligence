As a Working Student in AI Growth Marketing, you will support our B2B Domains marketing team in driving and scaling innovative, AI-powered initiatives across content, CRM, and performance marketing in a dynamic test-and-learn environment.

#### Tasks

- Support the B2B Domains marketing team in planning, executing, and optimizing AI-driven growth marketing initiatives across content, CRM, and performance marketing.

- Create and adapt marketing content (landing pages, emails, social posts, ads) using generative AI tools — from first draft to publication-ready copy.

- Help build and maintain CRM and email automation workflows, including segmentation, A/B testing, and campaign setup.

- Assist in managing performance marketing campaigns (e.g. paid search, paid social) and monitor budgets, results, and learnings.

- Support reporting and attribution: maintain dashboards, analyze campaign data, and prepare clear summaries for the team.

- Contribute your own ideas — we run a test-and-learn culture and value fresh perspectives.

**What You Will Learn**

- How an AI-first B2B marketing organization works in practice — tools, processes, and skills.

- Hands-on experience across the full funnel: content, CRM, performance marketing, and analytics.

- How to design, run, and evaluate experiments with solid measurement and ROI thinking.

#### Qualifications

- Enrolled student (Bachelor's or Master's) in marketing, business, communications, data/analytics, or a related field for the duration of employment.

- Genuine interest in AI and digital marketing, with initial practical experience using generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, image/creative tools).

- First experience in online marketing, CRM, content creation, or performance marketing (through internships, personal projects, or previous working student roles).

- Analytical mindset and comfort working with data, including Excel/Sheets skills.

- Hands-on, curious, reliable way of working, and open to technology.

- Experience with agentic AI coding tools such as Claude Code is a plus.

- Initial exposure to web or digital analytics tools is a plus.

- Knowledge of domains, hosting, cloud, or digital infrastructure is a plus.

- Fluency in English (spoken and written); German skills are welcome.
