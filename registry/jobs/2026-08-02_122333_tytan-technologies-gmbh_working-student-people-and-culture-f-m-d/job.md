# Working Student - People and Culture (f/m/d)

Posted: 2026-08-02T02:51:11Z

At **TYTAN**, we aim to set a global standard in autonomous air defense with AI-driven, cost-efficient systems that counter unmanned aerial threats at scale. We protect people. We protect critical infrastructure. We protect Europe’s sovereignty.

As our Working Student in People & Culture (m/f/d), you will help create an exceptional employee experience. In this role, you'll support the day-to-day operations that keep our people programs running smoothly, from benefits administration and to culture initiatives and team events.

This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience in HR while contributing to projects that have a direct impact on our employees and company culture.

### **Your Roadmap to Impact:**

-

**Workplace Culture:** Support initiatives that strengthen TYTAN's culture and create opportunities for connection, collaboration, and belonging across the team.

-

**Benefits Support:** Assist with the administration and communication of employee benefits and perks, respond to benefit-related questions, and support the rollout of new programs as we grow.

-

**Internal Events:** Coordinate and support company events, team offsite/onsite celebrations, and social activities from planning and logistics to execution, creating memorable experiences for our teams.

-

**Employee Engagement:** Support engagement initiatives of coordinating surveys, collecting feedback, and helping turn insights into meaningful actions that improve the employee experience.

-

**Recognition & Wellbeing:** Help manage employee recognition programs, celebrate milestones, and support wellbeing initiatives that encourage a healthy, inclusive, and high-performing workplace.

-

**Internal Communication:** Assist in the coordination of People & Culture communications, including event invitations, culture updates, benefits announcements, and other employee-facing content to keep everyone informed and connected.

-

**People Operations Support:** Assist with day-to-day People Operations activities, maintain accurate employee records, support onboarding processes, and contribute to generally improving our people operation processes.

-

You are currently enrolled as a student (Bachelor's or Master's), ideally in HR, psychology, business administration, or a related field.

-

You are genuinely interested in workplace culture, employee experience, or people operations.

-

You are organized, dependable, and have a keen eye for details.

-

You have a creative streak and enjoy coming up with ideas that make the workplace more engaging.

-

You communicate well and enjoy interacting with people.

-

You are fluent in German and English, both spoken and written.

-

Prior work experience or internship in people operations, or a people-facing role is highly welcome.
