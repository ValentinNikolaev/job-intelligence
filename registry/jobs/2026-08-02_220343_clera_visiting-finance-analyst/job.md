# Visiting Finance Analyst

Posted: 2026-08-02T20:40:54Z

### About the Role

A fast-growing, AI-powered EdTech startup based in Berlin is looking for a **Visiting Finance Analyst** to join the Finance team on a **full-time, on-site basis**. This is a rare opportunity for an early-career finance professional or student to work directly alongside the Director of Finance & People and company founders — with real ownership, genuine exposure to leadership, and a front-row seat to building a modern finance function from the ground up.

If you're curious, proactive, and excited about the intersection of finance and AI-driven automation, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Act as a right hand to the Director of Finance, preparing decision briefs, supporting cross-functional initiatives, and keeping projects on track.

-

Support monthly, quarterly, and annual financial close processes and coordinate with external auditors.

-

Contribute to accurate, reliable financial reporting across multiple jurisdictions.

-

Assist with operational controlling, including budget planning, financial forecasting, and liquidity tracking.

-

Build monthly reports, conduct ad hoc deep dives, and translate data into clear, decision-ready analysis.

-

Identify and implement opportunities to automate manual workflows using AI tools, low-code platforms, and smart integrations.

-

Help transition the finance function from static reporting to dynamic, AI-enhanced financial intelligence.

-

Coordinate with tax advisors, auditors, and legal providers, and collaborate cross-functionally with teams such as Growth and Engineering.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Currently studying, on a gap year, or recently graduated in business, finance, economics, or a related field.

-

Strong analytical mindset with solid Excel skills — able to turn messy data into clear, actionable answers.

-

Keen interest in AI tools and automation.

-

Foundational understanding of accounting principles.

-

Strong written and spoken **German and English** (both required).

-

Proactive, self-driven, and pragmatic — comfortable taking ownership and working closely with leadership.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Prior internship or coursework in finance, accounting, controlling, or consulting.

-

Familiarity with BI or low-code platforms.

-

Knowledge of German accounting practices (HGB) or IFRS.
