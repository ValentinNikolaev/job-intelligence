# Candidate correction of automation volume — 2026-10-02

The candidate corrected his earlier statement of 20,000 tickets per ordinary
month for the Simple.life Go support platform. His reply in the achievement
update was:

> 20 тысяч я писал автоматизации, но была ошибка, я думал если умножить процент автоматизации на количество писем то будет норм

He then clarified the relationship between the report and his service:

> Фактически все тикеты прохоядт через свервис, под автоматизхацией подразумевается количество успешных автоматизаций. Т.е. количество тикетов можно принять за общее количество тикетов через сервис

The 20,000 figure was an attempted calculation of automation volume, not a
measured count of tickets handled by the Go service. It must not be used as a
verified service-throughput or automated-closure claim. The earlier evidence
entry combines this figure with a peak-season multiplier; that combined entry
is retracted until those claims can be sourced and reviewed separately. The
January–June comparison report measures *closed* conversations across the whole
support operation. The candidate now confirms that all tickets pass through his
Go service, so its reported all-handler closure count can describe the
service's closed-ticket volume. The report's automated count is the number of
successfully automated closures, not an estimate obtained by multiplying a
percentage by an email count. This newer clarification supersedes the earlier
statement in `user-confirmed-support-volume-scope-2026-10-02.md` that the two
populations had to remain separate.
