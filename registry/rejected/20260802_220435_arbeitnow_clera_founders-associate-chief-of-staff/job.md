### About the Role

A fast-growing **AI-powered energy intelligence startup** in the DACH market is looking for a **Founders' Associate / Chief of Staff** to work directly alongside the founding team. This is a high-ownership, hybrid GTM and operations role — ideal for someone who thrives at the intersection of outbound sales, strategic projects, and operational execution in an early-stage environment.

The company provides an AI-driven platform for holistic energy cost optimisation for industrial enterprises, helping them make smarter procurement decisions and manage energy consumption more efficiently. The business is well-funded at the pre-seed stage and is actively scaling its commercial and engineering teams across the DACH region.

**No visa sponsorship is available for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Build and maintain a strong enterprise sales pipeline across DACH in close partnership with the founders.

-

Run outbound execution including cold calls, emails, and LinkedIn outreach with tailored, persona-specific messaging.

-

Refine ICP, targeting strategy, and value propositions using real market and prospect feedback.

-

Produce high-quality sales materials — decks, one-pagers, follow-ups, and business cases — to improve conversion rates.

-

Join discovery calls to surface customer insights and translate them into sharper positioning and product messaging.

-

Act as an extension of the founders on strategic and operational topics, including investor updates and reporting.

-

Own and lead high-priority special projects end-to-end: market research, partnership development, and internal initiatives.

-

Support marketing content and event preparation (newsletters, social posts, event messaging).

-

Rapidly develop deep knowledge of energy markets and the product to drive improvements across sales and product roadmap.

-

Operate autonomously in ambiguous, fast-changing environments and continuously iterate on team processes.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

3+ years of experience in enterprise sales or outbound demand generation, building and delivering top-of-funnel pipeline in the **DACH region**.

-

Demonstrated experience leading cross-functional initiatives and acting as an operational right hand to founders in a startup environment, owning projects end-to-end.

**Required skills and experience:**

-

Experience preparing investor updates and collaborating with founding teams on investor relations and strategic communications.

-

Proven track record building and managing enterprise sales pipelines and outbound prospecting across DACH.

-

Ability to create and deliver compelling sales enablement materials; proficiency with **Notion, Google Workspace, HubSpot, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator**.

-

Knowledge of energy markets, or a demonstrated ability to rapidly learn and translate insights into sales and product improvements.

-

Experience conducting or participating in discovery calls and extracting insights to refine positioning and messaging.

-

Comfort driving cross-functional initiatives (market research, partnerships, internal projects) with limited structure.

-

Strong startup mindset — gritty, resourceful, and ownership-driven.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience in energy sector partnerships or procurement contexts.

-

Familiarity with MEDDICC or similar enterprise sales methodologies.

-

Background at or adjacent to industrial corporates (e.g. manufacturing, energy, engineering sectors).

### Location

This is a **primarily on-site role** based in **Nuremberg or Munich, Bavaria, Germany**. Candidates should be able to work on-site approximately 4 days per week. Remote-only candidates will not be considered.

Fluency in **German** is strongly preferred given the DACH-focused commercial scope.
