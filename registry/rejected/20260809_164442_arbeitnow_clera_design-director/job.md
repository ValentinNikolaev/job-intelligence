### About the Role

We're an early-stage ambient AI startup building a continuous intelligence system that seamlessly integrates into daily life — helping people offload routine tasks and reclaim their focus for what matters most. We're looking for a **Design Director** to shape how our company is seen and experienced across every touchpoint.

This is a **hands-on creative and strategic role** with real influence over brand direction. You'll translate brand strategy into compelling visual systems, campaigns, and narratives that shape company perception and drive business goals — with strong potential for leadership growth as the team scales.

*This position is fully remote with flexibility to travel globally as needed. Visa sponsorship is not available.*

### What You'll Do

-

Develop and continuously evolve the company's visual identity across brand, marketing, product, communications, and content channels.

-

Translate brand strategy into visuals, campaigns, narratives, and customer experiences that resonate deeply.

-

Create high-quality visual assets including launch materials, presentations, campaign creative, social content, web pages, and brand collateral.

-

Build design concepts that make complex technology feel clear, accessible, and culturally relevant.

-

Shape visual storytelling for key company moments and establish scalable design principles that ensure consistent branding.

-

Partner closely with Marketing, Product, and Leadership to align design execution with broader business goals.

-

Bring strong taste and strategic judgment to influence how the company is perceived in market.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

8+ years of design experience specializing in brand identity, visual systems, campaigns, and creative direction.

-

Demonstrated ability to translate brand strategy into visual systems, campaign narratives, and end-to-end customer experiences.

-

Proficiency creating polished, production-ready assets across launch materials, presentations, social, web, and brand content.

-

Experience developing and evolving visual identity systems across multiple channels simultaneously.

-

Ability to establish design principles and visual storytelling frameworks that scale across an organization.

-

Strong cross-functional collaboration skills — comfortable working alongside Marketing, Product, Brand, and executive Leadership.

-

Deep understanding of how brand strategy shapes market perception and product experience.

-

Portfolio demonstrating the combination of strategic thinking *and* hands-on design execution.

-

Proven comfort thriving in fast-paced startup environments with high design standards.

**Nice to Have**

-

Prior experience with luxury, DTC (direct-to-consumer), wellness, or technology brands.

-

Experience making complex or emerging technology feel approachable and culturally resonant through design.
