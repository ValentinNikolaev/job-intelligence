### About the Role

We're an early-stage ambient AI startup building a continuous system of context, memory, and intelligence designed to integrate seamlessly into daily life — helping people offload routine tasks and stay more focused on what matters. We're looking for a **Community Operations Lead** to build and own our community presence from the ground up.

This is a zero-to-one role. You'll be a key, visible figure within the community itself, working directly with company leadership to define and shape the role as the work surfaces. If you thrive without a pre-built playbook and love turning engaged early users into true product evangelists, this is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Build and run community presence across the platforms where early users are active (Discord, X, and others).

-

Own moderation, engagement, and day-to-day community operations — including resolving conflicts and driving meaningful participation.

-

Coordinate community events and touchpoints, both digital and in-person.

-

Partner with product and research teams to leverage the community for UX research and user studies.

-

Partner with Go-to-Market to execute coordinated campaigns with the most dedicated users and convert power users into product evangelists.

-

Define operational scope and build processes for community programs in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment.

-

Work directly with senior leadership to shape the direction of this function as it evolves.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

2+ years of experience in community operations or management roles, building or growing online communities for **B2C or prosumer products**.

-

Demonstrated experience building community operations and engagement strategies **from scratch** in early-stage environments — without established playbooks.

-

Hands-on experience moderating communities across platforms such as **Discord and X**, including resolving user conflicts and sustaining active engagement.

-

Experience partnering cross-functionally with product, research, or GTM teams to surface community insights or run coordinated user campaigns.

-

Experience coordinating community events and digital touchpoints.

-

Operationally sharp — comfortable defining scope and building processes in ambiguous, rapidly changing environments.

-

Independently capable of making judgment calls and representing the brand publicly with confidence.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience coordinating in-person (IRL) community events.

-

Multilingual capabilities for engaging with international community members.

-

Background in consumer technology, hardware, or AI product communities.
