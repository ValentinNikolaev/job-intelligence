At **GALVANY**, our goal is to make climate-neutral living a reality for everyone. We focus on execution – developing concrete, smart solutions and making heat pumps, battery storage, and smart metering easy to access, reliable, and affordable. GALVANY-Tech drives the energy transition through software. We're building an AI-based Operating System for sales, planning, installation and operation of heat pumps and integrated energy systems – from single-family homes to multi-unit buildings, and across our Energy Community as a Virtual Power Plant.

We are a profitable green-tech startup and believe that sustainable impact and long-term growth are only possible when grounded in a healthy business model. Driven by customer value, clarity, and responsibility, we stand for high-quality heating solutions and an environment where people take responsibility and drive lasting impact.

**The Role**

As an Engineering Lead at GALVANY, you will shape the technical direction of critical product domains within our AI-based Operating System. Whether you're driving our ERP, automation, and AI agent systems or our CRM and customer-facing apps – you'll own architecture decisions, elevate your team, and bridge the gap between engineering and the wider business. You combine deep technical expertise with the ability to align stakeholders and mentor engineers.

**Responsibilities:**

-

Own architectural decisions for your domain and drive long-term technical vision.

-

Lead a team of engineers: hire, develop, and retain talent through coaching, code reviews, and continuous feedback.

-

Mentor engineers through code reviews, pairing, and clear technical guidance.

-

Stay hands-on: contribute directly to the codebase alongside architectural and leadership responsibilities.

-

Collaborate closely with Product, Design, and Operations to align technical decisions with business goals.

-

Balance innovation with pragmatism – make smart build-vs-buy decisions at system level.

-

Integrate LLMs and AI agents into product workflows where they create real value.

-

Drive operational excellence: observability, reliability, and incident response.

**Tech Stack:** Backend with GoLang and Laravel; ML with Python, PyTorch, and LLMs; Frontend with React, Tailwind, and Shadcn; Mobile with Flutter; General tools including Azure, GitHub, Linear, Notion, Kafka, Benthos (data pipeline), n8n / Kestra (workflow engine), and low-code process automation platforms.
