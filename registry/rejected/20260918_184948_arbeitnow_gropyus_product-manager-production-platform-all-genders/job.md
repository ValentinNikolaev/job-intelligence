**About The Company**

GROPYUS is a technology-based construction company focused on building multi-story residential buildings. Thanks to its prefabricated building system with various design options, industrial offsite construction, and fully digitalized processes, the company manufactures aspirational, sustainable, and affordable homes using timber construction methods. GROPYUS is using scalable construction and manufacturing solutions to tap into a future market, boost Europe's strength in innovation, while also playing a substantial role in improving sustainability.

## **About the Role**

As a Product Manager for our Production Platform you will drive digital solutions that connect manufacturing equipment, production systems, and operational data into a scalable and reliable production ecosystem.

You will work with software engineers and manufacturing / automation experts to define products and services that support production control, validation and simulation services in the design and series operation phases of manufacturing as well as solutions for maintaining proper operational transparency. By translating factory needs into product solutions, you will help improve how manufacturing capabilities are integrated, monitored, and continuously optimized across our production environment.

**ON-SITE COLLABORATION **Regular on-site work at our Richen production plant is expected. You will work closely with software developers, automation engineers, industrial engineers, and production stakeholders to develop a deep understanding of the manufacturing environment, discover opportunities, validate solutions, and support successful product adoption.

## **What You'll Be Doing**

- Drive end-to-end product development, from discovery through delivery and iteration with a focus on process creation and improvement, scoping manufacturing system integration, operational data availability, and simulation of manufacturing processes / production capabilities

- Translate business needs into clear product roadmaps, and actionable requirements

- Ensure alignment of product goals with business objectives, operational efficiency, and customer needs

- Collaborate closely with engineering, design, and stakeholders to build and launch high-impact solutions

- Use data and user feedback to inform decisions, validate solutions, and continuously improve products

- Drive transparency and alignment through clear documentation and stakeholder communication

- Focus on documenting processes or support their documentation

- Identify opportunities to optimize product performance

- Develop a deep understanding of production processes, machine capabilities, operational workflows, and constraints

- Collaborate with production teams to align product decisions with factory realities

- Support efforts in product standardization, complexity reduction, and cost optimization
