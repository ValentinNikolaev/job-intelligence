Are you a talented engineer who loves building clean, functional code and solving technical challenges in **PHP**?

Join our team as a Mid-Level Backend Engineer, where you will help evolve our global platform serving more than 20 million users. We value strong engineering fundamentals, a growth mindset, and the ability to deliver reliable features in a fast-paced environment. While we use **Laravel**, we highly value the core engineering skills of **Symfony** developers who are eager to adapt their expertise to our ecosystem.

**Your main responsibilities are:**

- **Develop and maintain** robust backend services capable of handling high-volume global traffic.

- **Implement functional APIs** and optimize query performance to ensure a seamless user experience.

- **Contribute to our modular architecture**, helping us build resilient, service-oriented systems.

- **Write clean, maintainable code** by applying SOLID principles, OOP best practices, and design patterns.

- **Master the art of debugging**, identifying and resolving issues across our stack with precision.

- **Integrate asynchronous processes** using queues and basic event-driven patterns to improve system efficiency.

- **Collaborate closely** with Senior Engineers and Product teams to turn requirements into high-quality technical solutions.

**Does this sound like you:**

- **3-5 years** of professional backend development experience with a solid command of **PHP**.

- **Proven experience** building applications in **Laravel** or a strong background in **Symfony** with a desire to transition.

- **Strong understanding of OOP**, interfaces, and code modularity; you know why “clean code” matters.

- **Solid knowledge of SQL**, including database indexing, migrations, and basic query optimization.

- **AI Proficiency:** Ability to effectively use AI tools during technical tasks; we value engineers who know how to prompt, validate, and iterate with AI to build better software faster.

- **Familiarity with modern infrastructure**, including Docker and basic CI/CD pipeline troubleshooting.

- **Experience with testing**, specifically writing unit and feature tests to ensure code reliability.

- **Fluent English** with the ability to communicate technical progress and seek support effectively within a remote team.

**Bonus:**

- Familiarity with caching mechanisms (Redis/Memcached) and Cloud services (AWS).

- Experience with SaaS or Traveltech solutions.

- Experience with eSIM or GSMA technologies.

- A curiosity for distributed systems and horizontal scaling.
