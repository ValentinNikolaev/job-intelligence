### About the Role

You will be the primary technical partner for the most strategic accounts at a fast-growing, open-source AI engineering platform — think of it as the observability layer for the LLM era. Your mission is to make top customers impossible to churn: driving adoption, delivering outcomes, and creating a tight feedback loop back to product and engineering.

You understand AI architectures deeply enough to have opinions. You write documentation that engineers actually follow. You close loops rather than escalate them.

### About the Company

This is a seed-stage (Series A/B trajectory), engineering-heavy startup in the **open-source AI/ML developer tooling** space. The platform helps teams trace, evaluate, monitor, and debug LLM applications and AI agents across the full development lifecycle — from prototype to production. It is widely adopted across the enterprise and developer communities, and recently joined forces with a major analytics infrastructure company.

### What You'll Do

-

**Own 10–20 strategic accounts** — lead onboarding, success planning, and deep dives into customers' AI architectures; drive adoption and expansion across the full lifecycle.

-

**Guide production readiness** — advise on instrumentation strategy, evaluation setup, data modeling, security and privacy, and operational playbooks.

-

**Troubleshoot and unblock** — debug SDK/API integrations, support self-hosted deployments, and own incident response alongside engineering teams.

-

**Drive net revenue retention (NRR)** — identify expansion opportunities, manage churn risk proactively, and report on customer health metrics.

-

**Be the voice of the customer** — synthesize feedback from strategic accounts into actionable product insights and partner closely with engineering and product teams.

-

**Create scalable assets** — write integration guides, runbooks, and technical documentation that benefit the broader customer base and open-source community.

-

**Engage the community** — contribute to open-source community support, issue triage, and evangelism where relevant.
