## Requirements

**What we’re looking for**

We don’t believe in checklists - but if most of these apply to you, we’d love to chat:

✔ 5+ years of backend development experience.

✔ 2+ years of experience with a strongly typed language or TypeScript.

✔ Strong experience with RDBMS (raw queries or via ORM).

✔ Solid background in unit and integration testing.

✔ Experience with message brokers (SNS, RabbitMQ, Kafka, Google Pub/Sub, etc.).

**Bonus:** Experience with Node.js, GraphQL, GCP or AWS, gRPC and protobufs.

**Bonus:** Strong grasp of SOLID principles.
