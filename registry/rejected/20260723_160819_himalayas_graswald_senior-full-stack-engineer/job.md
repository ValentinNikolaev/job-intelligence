**The Role:**
The Senior Full Stack Software Engineer is a core member of a product team, responsible for designing, building, testing, and deploying high-quality software. Engineers work closely with their Team Lead (TL) and Product Manager (PM) to turn product requirements into reliable, scalable features that deliver real value to users.
Engineers at [Graswald](https://himalayas.app/companies/graswald) operate in a highly dynamic environment, where requirements and architectures evolve quickly. This role requires comfort with ambiguity, strong ownership, and the ability to make pragmatic technical decisions with incomplete information.
