## Tasks

- Manage the backlog for one of our two business lines: turn business needs and customer input into clear requirements with well-defined acceptance criteria.

- Set day-to-day priorities based on direction from your business line manager.

- Join meetings with our bank customers from day one, and run them yourself within your first few months: requirements sessions, demos, walkthroughs, and follow-ups.

- Present our product clearly and accurately to bank stakeholders, prospects, and colleagues.

- Work closely with engineering: answer the questions that help the team move forward, and weigh the trade-offs that come back.

- Get to know the product well enough to answer customer questions yourself.

- Keep the product documented: maintain the notes, specs, and materials the team and customers rely on.

- Monitor the market, regulation, and customer requests, and feed what you learn into the roadmap.

## Requirements

- Experience working with demanding customers, gained at a top consultancy, a large corporate, or a startup/scale-up serving enterprise clients.

- Around 2–4 years of professional experience. Formal product experience is welcome but not required. Consulting, business analysis, delivery, or client-facing technical work all count.

- You learn quickly, think in a structured way, and get to the core of unfamiliar problems fast.

- Comfortable presenting to customers and staying composed with senior stakeholders.

- Excellent written and spoken English.

- Comfortable with technical topics.

- Well-organised and proactive: you notice what needs doing and follow through.

- Curious about AI, and already using it in your own work.

- Nice to have: German, experience in banking or anti-financial-crime, familiarity with agile teams, and data literacy.
