## 💡 Your Mission: Delight customers, optimize processes!

As **Data Protection Manager (m/w/d)**, you own data protection at voize end-to-end: answering customer and DPO questions quickly, maintaining the GDPR foundation behind those answers, and shaping how a fast-growing AI company handles sensitive data responsibly.

##

## 🚀 Your Daily Business - No two days are alike

-

You own incoming data protection inquiries from customers and their data protection officers end-to-end. From broad questionnaires to very specific questions.

-

You maintain our internal GDPR foundation: retention periods, records of processing activities, technical and organizational measures - and you make sure they are actually implemented together with the teams that own the tools

-

You keep an up-to-date picture of how customer data flows through our product, working closely with our tech leads

-

You review vendor contracts and data processing agreements (DPA/AVV) and support the negotiations

-

You maintain the data protection documentation in our trust center and act as our main interface to our external DPO

## 🤝 Your Skillset - What you bring to the table

-

Hands-on data protection experience with a clear GDPR specialization

-

Experience in a modern SaaS environment, including products that use AI, and the confidence to assess and structure the compliant use of international sub-processors instead of ruling them out

-

A solid grasp of how data flows through a software product, and the curiosity to keep that picture current

-

Structured thinking: you build processes and policies, and you see how data protection connects to information security, quality and regulatory topics rather than treating it as a silo

-

A pragmatic, creative approach: you find the compliant path, explain it clearly and enjoy hands-on ownership in a fast-changing environment

-

Business-fluent English (German is a plus)
