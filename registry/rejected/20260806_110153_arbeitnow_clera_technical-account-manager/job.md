### About the Role

Join a fast-moving, early-stage marketing AI company that is automating the marketing services industry — starting with SEO and AI search optimization. As a **Technical Account Manager**, you'll be the trusted technical partner to a portfolio of fast-growing B2B SaaS startups, helping them unlock measurable growth outcomes from a cutting-edge marketing automation platform.

This is a high-impact, cross-functional role sitting at the intersection of customer success, product, and engineering — ideal for someone who thrives in a hands-on, startup environment and loves turning complex technical problems into clear client wins.

### What You'll Do

-

Serve as the primary technical point of contact for clients, driving platform adoption and delivering measurable growth results.

-

Translate client needs into precise technical requirements and coordinate with product and engineering teams to deliver solutions.

-

Onboard and coach customers on platform best practices to help them maximize marketing ROI.

-

Monitor key success metrics across your client portfolio and provide strategic, data-driven recommendations for continuous improvement.

-

Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and customer success teams to resolve issues and ensure clients realize full platform value.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Experience at a fast-growth startup with a demonstrable track record of delivering measurable wins for customers.

-

Hands-on experience with marketing automation tools, data workflows, and integrations.

-

Proven ability to onboard and coach customers toward successful platform adoption and ROI optimization.

-

Ability to translate client needs into technical requirements and collaborate effectively with product and engineering teams.

-

Experience monitoring success metrics and translating insights into strategic recommendations.

-

Impeccable written and verbal communication skills — clear, concise, and persuasive.

-

High technical aptitude paired with strong interpersonal and emotional intelligence.

-

Comfort operating autonomously in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment; a self-starter who rolls up their sleeves.

-

US work authorization required — **visa sponsorship is not available**.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Industry experience in marketing services, SEO, or AI search optimization (AEO).

-

Strong CS fundamentals or equivalent problem-solving background.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

**Salary:** $90,000 – $122,000 per year, depending on experience.

-

Early-stage equity opportunity at a well-funded, high-growth startup.

### Location

**On-site in New York City (SoHo), NY** — this role requires full-time, in-office presence. No remote option is available.
