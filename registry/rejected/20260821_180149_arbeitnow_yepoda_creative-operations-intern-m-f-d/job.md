### **About Yepoda**

**Clean K-Beauty Innovation. No compromises.**

Yepoda is a clean K-Beauty brand that actually proves it works. We make Korean skincare without any of the compromises. Everything is clinically tested, 100% vegan, and formulated above EU standards. So you get the innovation and the results that K-Beauty is known for, plus a real commitment to sustainability. And we make it genuinely fun and easy to use — skincare you actually look forward to.

Founded in 2020 by Sander Joonyoung van Bladel and Veronika Strotmann, Yepoda is a **certified B Corp™** and 1% for the Planet member, based in the heart of Berlin. Our products are available through our own online shop and, since March 2025, at Sephora Europe — online and in over 650 stores. With a team of 140+ brilliant individuals from around the world, we are actively shaping Yepoda's story every day.

We are looking for an organized and detail-oriented **Creative Operations Intern** to support the Creative Operations Lead and help keep the creative team’s projects, systems, and information running smoothly.

This is a great opportunity for someone at the beginning of their career who is interested in the intersection of **project management, marketing, business operations, and creative work**. You do not need to be an expert in our tools or processes — we are looking for someone who is curious, structured, eager to learn, and excited to grow their Creative Operations skill set over the course of the internship.

### **Your Tasks**

-

Support the Creative Operations Lead with the day-to-day coordination and tracking of creative projects, deadlines, feedback, approvals, and deliverables.

-

Help maintain the creative team’s Notion ticketing system and keep project information accurate and up to date.

-

Review incoming tickets for missing information and support smooth handovers between stakeholders and the creative team.

-

Assist with weekly planning, project status updates, meeting notes, action items, and stakeholder follow-ups.

-

Collect and organize data for simple reporting on project volume, timelines, overdue work, and team capacity.

-

Support project coordination and administrative tasks across campaigns, launches, and ongoing creative requests.

-

Assist with the organization and migration of creative assets into a digital asset management system, including file auditing, naming, tagging, and quality checks.

-

Help maintain process documentation, trackers, templates, and other Creative Operations resources.

### **Your Profile**

-

You are at the beginning of your career, ideally with **up to 1–2 years of relevant internship or professional experience**.

-

You are studying or have recently graduated in a field such as **business, marketing, communications, project management, media, or a related area**.

-

You are organized, detail-oriented, and enjoy bringing structure to information and processes.

-

You have an interest in how creative teams work, while also enjoying the coordination, planning, and operational side of projects.

-

You communicate clearly, work collaboratively, and follow through reliably on tasks.

-

You are comfortable working with spreadsheets, trackers, databases, or other structured information.

-

You are curious, proactive, and keen to learn new tools and ways of working.

-

Previous experience with tools such as **Notion, Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, Miro, or digital asset management systems is a plus, but not required**.

-

Some exposure to marketing, creative production, or project coordination through university, internships, working student roles, or previous employment is beneficial.

-

You are fluent in English.
