### Mobile Software Engineer

### Reporting to: Head of Department

Work Arrangement: Remote | Standard Office Hours

### Company Overview:

We are forward-thinking fintech company specializing in innovative trading solutions and financial products. With a dynamic platform and a tech-savvy team that works seamlessly across time zones, we strive to provide cutting-edge services to traders worldwide.

### About the Role:

We are looking for a Mobile Software Engineer to build native applications of our technologies for the Apple App Store and Google Play. You will take our trading and fintech products to mobile, owning the native app experience from development through store release. You will report to the CPO.

### Key Responsibilities:

- Build and maintain native mobile apps for iOS (Swift) and Android (Kotlin)

- Port and adapt our platform technologies into performant native experiences

- Integrate with internal APIs, trading services, and card/wallet features

- Manage App Store and Google Play submissions, releases, and compliance

- Implement secure authentication and data handling on device

- Ensure app performance, stability, and responsiveness across devices

- Handle push notifications, deep links, and mobile-specific features

- Collaborate with backend, design, and product teams on requirements

- Maintain CI/CD pipelines for mobile builds and testing

- Monitor crash reports, reviews, and analytics; ship fixes and improvements

### Requirements:

- Proven experience building and shipping native iOS and/or Android apps

- Proficiency in Swift and/or Kotlin (cross-platform frameworks a plus)

- Experience publishing to the Apple App Store and Google Play

- Understanding of mobile security, API integration, and app performance

- Familiarity with fintech or trading apps preferred

- Strong attention to quality, detail, and user experience

### What We Offer:

- Fully remote role

- Schedule: Monday–Friday, 9:00 – 18:00

- Visa sponsorship and medical insurance from week one

- Career growth opportunities within a fast-scaling fintech environment

- Participation in international events, meet-ups and business trips to our global offices
