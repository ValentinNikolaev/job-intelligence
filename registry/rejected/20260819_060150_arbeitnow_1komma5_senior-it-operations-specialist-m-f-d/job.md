**Deine Position**

As a Senior IT Operations Specialist, you will be the assigned Tech counterpart for employees, and solve localised problems in our Hamburg HQ. This means that every 1KOMMA5 employee in Hamburg will know you by name, and have trust to include you in all technical discussions that improve their productivity.

The average day of a Senior IT Operations Specialist
The role is divided into 3 key value areas:

- **Tech Support** on level 1, 2 and 3 tickets created by holding employees. The goal here is to be familiar with stakeholders for faster, more efficient turnaround. Tickets are not escalated, but are triaged and discussed to improve technical understanding and develop. You will also monitor queues for the other markets for urgent out of hours requests, and action them when necessary. Level 1 and 2 tickets may be delegated to working students in the team, with guidance and accountability.
- **Tech Projects** - This refers to projects imposed by Employee Technology Platform product strategy such as rollout of Chrome OS and tool consolidations for the holding. This also refers to inbound projects imposed by holding teams (e.g. tailor onboarding experience, upgrading office infrastructure etc).
- **Tech Consulting** - Providing solutions using existing tools first, through enablement sessions. If no solutions exist, working with teams to technically own and benchmark new applications. There is a particular push to enable AI usage to boost productivity. This means any queries on how to use basic or advanced AI toolings would be directed to this role.
