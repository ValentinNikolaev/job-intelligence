### About the Role

A fast-growing, Series A AI search analytics SaaS company based in Berlin is looking for a **Senior Backend Developer** to architect and build the systems powering its AI-driven recommendation engine. You'll work at the intersection of scalable infrastructure and cutting-edge AI integrations, collaborating closely with product, data, and frontend teams to ship reliable, high-quality software. This is a fully on-site role (Berlin office, 5 days/week).

The company operates in the AI/ML space, helping marketing teams track, analyze, and optimize brand performance across AI search platforms. Backed by Series A funding and trusted by thousands of brands and agencies, the team is scaling quickly and looking for engineers who thrive in a high-ownership, fast-moving environment.

### What You'll Do

-

Architect and build backend systems that power real-time AI-driven recommendations.

-

Design, implement, and maintain scalable REST and GraphQL APIs, data pipelines, and database models.

-

Integrate with AI model APIs (OpenAI, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Llama, and others) to support live recommendation features.

-

Develop and maintain automated tests (unit and integration) and CI/CD pipelines to ensure quality and rapid delivery.

-

Enforce security best practices, authentication/authorization, and data privacy across backend services.

-

Implement end-to-end changes across backend services with clean, maintainable, well-styled code.

-

Translate product and data requirements into robust technical specifications and solutions.

-

Leverage AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Cursor) to enhance productivity while keeping committed code concise and maintainable.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Deep proficiency in **TypeScript**.

-

Hands-on experience with **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**, including Cloud Functions, Cloud Tasks, PostgreSQL, and Firestore.

-

Proven experience designing and building scalable REST and/or GraphQL APIs and data pipelines for AI-driven features.

-

Experience integrating with AI model APIs (OpenAI, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Llama, etc.) for real-time use cases.

-

Strong system design and architectural decision-making skills.

-

Experience implementing automated testing (unit/integration) and CI/CD pipelines.

-

Knowledge of security best practices, authentication/authorization, and data privacy in backend services.

-

Ability to match an existing codebase's style and implement changes end-to-end.

-

Excellent collaboration skills across product, data, and frontend teams.

-

A demonstrated track record of exceptional performance or notable achievements.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience with AI productivity tooling (e.g., Cursor or similar).

-

Background at companies working in AI/ML infrastructure, LLM tooling, or marketing analytics SaaS.
