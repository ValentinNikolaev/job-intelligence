### About the Role

We're a fast-growing, seed-stage AI startup automating inside sales processes — quoting, order handling, and product matching — for distributors and manufacturers. Our platform integrates deeply into enterprise ERP and CRM systems (including SAP and Oracle) and is trusted by a growing number of customers across industries like HVAC, electrical, and building materials.

As a **Forward Deployed Engineer**, you'll be the critical technical bridge between our AI product and its real-world deployment inside complex enterprise environments. You'll own the full implementation lifecycle — from sales handoff to go-live — working directly with customers every day while feeding insights back to the core engineering team. This is a high-ownership, high-impact role at the intersection of engineering, product, and customer success.

### What You'll Do

-

Own end-to-end technical implementation of customer deployments, from initial handoff through go-live.

-

Configure and fine-tune AI models for customer-specific use cases, including prompt engineering and model behavior adjustments.

-

Structure customer data and build system integrations with platforms like SAP and various REST/GraphQL APIs.

-

Interpret unstructured RFQs (PDFs, emails, Excel files) and translate them into automated quoting workflows.

-

Develop prototypes to demonstrate new features or solve emerging customer problems, then evolve them into scalable solutions.

-

Engage daily with technical users and senior business stakeholders to translate real pain points into engineering decisions.

-

Act as the voice of the customer internally, surfacing product gaps and patterns to the core engineering team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Native fluency in **German** (non-negotiable).

-

Strong full-stack engineering skills — comfortable with **Python, React, TypeScript, SQL**, and APIs across frontend, backend, and data pipelines.

-

Proven experience delivering customer-facing software deployments and gathering client requirements.

-

Hands-on experience with system integrations: ERP systems, REST/GraphQL APIs, webhooks, or B2B SaaS implementations.

-

Ability to wrangle messy, inconsistent real-world data and establish reliable structures quickly.

-

Practical experience with **LLMs, AI agents, and prompt engineering**, with ability to adapt model behavior to specific domains.

-

Excellent written and verbal communication skills — able to explain complex technical concepts to diverse audiences.

-

High agency and ownership mindset; proactively identifies and drives solutions without needing explicit direction.

-

Comfortable with ambiguity, imperfect data, and legacy systems; scrappy and resourceful problem-solver.

-

Willingness to work **on-site in Munich** with a high-intensity work ethic.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Familiarity with AI/ML concepts and reinforcement learning, particularly product-matching engines.

-

Background in distribution, manufacturing, or industrial sales technology.
