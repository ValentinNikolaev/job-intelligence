## **About this role**

We're looking for a Senior Product Marketing Manager – Cybersecurity (m/f/x) to join our Product team.

As our business grows and our platform evolves, this role will play a key part in shaping how we position our cybersecurity solutions in the market. You'll take ownership of product marketing strategy, driving messaging, sales enablement, content, competitive intelligence, and go-to-market execution for our MDR/XDR portfolio.

Sitting at the centre of Product, Commercial, and Security Operations teams, you'll translate complex cybersecurity capabilities into clear, compelling value for clients and partners. You'll work closely with our product and sales teams to ensure consistent positioning, support successful launches, and keep our market positioning sharp and competitive.

This is a senior individual contributor role designed for someone who thrives on autonomy, strategic thinking, and executing with speed in a fast-growing environment.
