### About the Role

This is the **first dedicated Revenue Operations hire** at a fast-scaling, Series A AI/SaaS company in the industrial procurement intelligence space. You'll partner directly with senior revenue leadership to design, build, and operate the systems that power the full revenue cycle — from outbound business development through enterprise pipeline, customer onboarding, and expansion. This is a high-ownership, high-impact role for someone who thrives in ambiguous, fast-moving environments and wants to shape how a company goes to market at a critical growth stage.

The company is headquartered in Munich, Germany, and is backed by leading European and global venture investors. The team is ~150 people and growing rapidly, with a strong culture of entrepreneurial thinking, cross-functional collaboration, and extreme ownership.

### What You'll Do

-

Partner with Revenue Operations leadership and the Managing Director of Revenue to build and scale revenue systems company-wide.

-

Lead strategic projects that reshape the sales motion and support enterprise and international expansion initiatives.

-

Own and maintain the operational backbone spanning business development, AE pipeline management, customer onboarding, and expansion.

-

Build reporting and analytics frameworks that surface meaningful patterns and translate data into strategic decisions.

-

Collaborate closely with marketing, business development, account executives, customer experience, and expansion teams to identify and remove friction across the revenue cycle.

-

Design and implement enablement programs, AI tooling integrations, and onboarding solutions that multiply team output.

-

Contribute to the design of compensation and equity structures in support of revenue team growth.

### What We're Looking For

**Dealbreakers (must-haves):**

-

**5+ years** of experience in Revenue Operations or an equivalent function building and delivering revenue processes and systems end-to-end.

-

Demonstrated experience operationalizing revenue processes across the **full revenue cycle**: outbound/BDR, AE pipeline management, customer onboarding, and expansion.

-

**Fluency in both German and English** (written and spoken) — non-negotiable for this role.

**Required:**

-

Strong systems thinker who can design structures *and* operationalize them independently, end-to-end.

-

Proven track record delivering measurable revenue improvements in a **high-growth or VC-backed startup** environment (e.g., built from zero, rapid scaling).

-

Ability and willingness to be **on-site in Munich 4+ days per week**.

-

Experience working directly with senior leadership (founders, managing directors, revenue leaders) to define and execute strategy.

-

Hands-on experience enabling or implementing **AI tooling and automation** within a revenue operations context.

-

Track record of extreme ownership: you identify issues, build fixes, and ship without waiting for permission.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Prior founder experience or a clear entrepreneurial orientation — e.g., you've started something, backed something, or have a strong desire to do so.

-

Background at high-calibre consulting, enterprise software, or process intelligence firms.
