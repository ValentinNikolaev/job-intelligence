**Reach for the stars with us. **Limehome is building something rare in hospitality: a tech-enabled operator that treats revenue not as a spreadsheet exercise, but as a full-contact commercial sport. As we scale our portfolio toward 12,000+ units, we’re looking for a Senior Business Development Manager to build and drive the commercial engine of a dedicated cluster of properties. This is a builder’s role: you’ll own a real P&L, spin up commercial projects from scratch — new B2B channels, GOPPAR optimization plays, OTA and SEM frameworks — and scale what works. The automated tech stack does the daily heavy lifting; you make the calls and build the playbook the rest of the portfolio will run on.

If you want to run a cluster like it’s your own — with the autonomy to test bold ideas and the data to prove they worked — read on.

**How you’ll be hosting with heart:**

**Build & Scale Commercial Levers**

-

Develop new commercial levers from zero to impact: GOPPAR optimization plays, personalized incentives, loyalty mechanics — test them on your cluster, measure hard, and scale what works across the portfolio

-

Build OTA product frameworks: crack where Preferred Partner, sponsored listings, visibility boosters and campaigns actually pay off — and turn it into a repeatable playbook

-

Run Ads experiments via sponsored listings: measure CAC against OTA commissions and steer spend to protect GOPPAR

**Drive Local B2B Growth**

-

Spin up local B2B as a demand-generation engine: identify, pitch and close the accounts that fill your cluster — corporates, hospitals, universities, contractors, event organizers

-

Build a non-OTA channel of direct stays you can activate whenever demand pacing needs a push; warm leads from the central B2B team are your head start, the pipeline and the results are yours

**Own a Commercial Cluster to Test & Validate**

-

Own commercial performance (RevPAR, GOPPAR) for a cluster of ~10 Limehome properties — your numbers, your calls and your playing field to solidify your ideas

-

Treat every lever — rate, channel, demand, cost — as something you can pull to move the bottom line

**Cross-Functional Influence & Project Development**

-

Develop, pilot and roll out new local growth initiatives end-to-end (e.g. personalized incentives, loyalty mechanics, local partnerships) – from idea to execution, without a ready-made playbook

-

Loop in central Channel Managers when your cluster needs a visibility push – OTA ads, content, campaigns

-

Quantify the commercial cost of ops, content or review-score issues and chase the right team to fix them — backed by numbers, not opinions

-

Flag the commercial impact of operational issues (e.g. a maintenance issue hurting your [Booking.com](http://Booking.com) score) so Ops can prioritize accordingly
