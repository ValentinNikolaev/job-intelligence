## Deine Position

We are looking for an **Analytics Engineer** who enjoys building trusted data foundations and enabling teams to make better decisions, measure outcomes, and demonstrate the impact of their work.

As an Analytics Engineer, you will

- **Build and maintain** scalable, production-grade data models and analytics foundations that power decision-making across our 7 international markets

- **Partner with** Product Managers and business stakeholders to define success metrics, evaluate product outcomes, and turn data into actionable insights

- **Enable self-service analytics** through trusted metrics, reusable semantic models, and scalable data products that help teams answer their own questions and demonstrate the impact of their work

- **Leverage AI and modern analytics tooling** to accelerate insight generation, improve analytics workflows, and enable stakeholders to interact with data more effectively

- **Continuously improve** our analytics platform through robust modeling, testing, documentation, and engineering best practices
