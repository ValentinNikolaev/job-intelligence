### About the Role

We're a fast-moving, pre-seed energy technology company based in Berlin Mitte, building a full-stack electricity retail platform that enables hardware businesses and energy providers to offer dynamic electricity plans to European households. As a **Member of Commercial Staff**, you'll be a versatile operator at the heart of our commercial team — working directly alongside founders to build the systems and processes that power our growth in a regulated, high-stakes industry.

This is a generalist role with real scope: you'll own problems end-to-end across finance, legal, recruiting, customer support, and partner management. If you thrive in ambiguity, learn fast, and take genuine pride in your work, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Tackle varied commercial challenges across finance, legal, recruiting, customer support, and partner management as the business scales.

-

Own problems from initial scoping through to execution — delivering high-quality outcomes you're proud of.

-

Adapt your focus and priorities as the business evolves, always working on what matters most.

-

Help design and improve operating practices and internal processes alongside your core responsibilities.

-

Contribute to a culture defined by effective communication, strong ownership, and craftsmanship — working in-person at our Berlin Mitte office.

### What We're Looking For

-

**2+ years** of proven performance in a high-performance environment — high-growth startup, consulting, investment banking, professional sports, or high-end hospitality.

-

A **versatile background**: banking, legal, operations, partnerships, or any domain where you can add commercial leverage.

-

A **rapid learner** who can go from novice to effective in days by talking to experts, reasoning from first principles, and executing with conviction.

-

Strong interpersonal style: kind and engaging, but capable of pushing for results when the situation demands it.

-

A **proactive owner** who notices broken processes and fixes them without waiting for permission.

-

**German fluency at C1+ level is required.**

*Note: Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.*

### Compensation & Benefits

-

**Salary:** €75,000 – €100,000 per year, depending on experience.

-

Opportunity to help shape the commercial foundation of a high-conviction energy technology company at an early stage.

-

Direct collaboration with founders and a small, high-calibre team.

### Location

This role is fully **on-site in Berlin Mitte, Germany**. We believe in the power of in-person collaboration, especially at this stage of company building.
