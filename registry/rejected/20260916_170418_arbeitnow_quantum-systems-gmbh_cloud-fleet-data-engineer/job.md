#### About the Role

Every flight creates valuable data. Your mission is to make it visible, accessible and actionable. You will build the product-specific cloud services and data workflows that connect our interceptors, launch boxes, engineering teams and field operations.

#### **What is your Day to Day Mission:**

• Build the central source of truth for device identity, software versions, configurations and fleet health.

• Develop secure backend services and APIs connecting interceptors, launch boxes and support systems.

• Create resilient communication workflows for devices operating with intermittent connectivity.

• Automate the upload and processing of flight logs, telemetry, video and sensor data.

• Build pipelines, dashboards and alerts that help engineers identify and solve problems quickly.

• Connect flight data with the software, hardware configuration and mission context that produced it.

#### **What you bring to the team:**

You do not need to check every box. We are looking for strong fundamentals, hands-on ability and real ownership.

• Experience in cloud, backend, data-platform, IoT or distributed-systems engineering.

• Strong programming skills in Python, Go, Java, TypeScript or a comparable language.

• Experience with APIs, databases, object storage, message queues or data pipelines.

• Familiarity with telemetry, observability, monitoring or connected-device systems.

• The drive to turn incomplete information into reliable services and fast operational answers.
