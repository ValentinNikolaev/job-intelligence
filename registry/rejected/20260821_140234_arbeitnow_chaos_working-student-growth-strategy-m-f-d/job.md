**Role Overview**

The **Working Student** supports the GTM Strategy & Transformation function, working directly with the Senior Director on high-impact strategic and operational priorities. The role contributes to market research, competitive analysis, process improvement, and growth initiatives that directly support the revenue organisation's go-to-market execution. This is a hands-on, project-based role suited to a student with strong analytical foundations, an interest in commercial strategy, and the initiative to take ownership of outcomes rather than just tasks.

Support the end-to-end delivery of high-priority GTM initiatives: tracking milestones, managing stakeholder inputs, and ensuring projects deliver measurable outcomes.

-

Identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies in go-to-market processes, proposing and helping implement frameworks that improve scalability across the organisation.

-

Translate data and analysis into structured, concise storylines and presentations for senior stakeholders.

-

Conduct market research and competitive benchmarking, synthesising findings into clear briefings and recommendations that support executive-level decision-making.

-

Work closely with the Senior Director, GTM Strategy & Transformation and cross-functional teams to support evolving strategic priorities as they arise.
