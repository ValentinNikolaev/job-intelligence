🎯 Your main goal will be to own the forward-looking financial plan for the top and mid funnel of n8n’s commercial engine, helping Marketing leadership make sharper investment decisions across both our PLG and SLG motions.

To do so, you’ll turn demand, funnel, and marketing investment decisions into an integrated financial view that leadership can act on:

### OWN MARKETING PLANNING & FORECASTING

-

Drive n8n’s annual planning and regular forecasting process for Marketing end to end, from top-down targets and cross-functional alignment through regional segmentation, budget ownership, and consolidation into the company plan.

-

Partner with Marketing budget owners across demand generation, brand, community and DevRel, product marketing, and events to set budgets, forecast spend, and sharpen investment trade-offs.

-

Own program spend with the same rigor as headcount, including agency and media contracts, event and sponsorship commitments, phasing, seasonality, and in-year reallocations.

### MODEL FUNNEL & CHANNEL ECONOMICS

-

Model demand creation, signup and MQL volume, activation, qualified opportunity, conversion, and velocity with Marketing Ops and RevOps across both PLG and SLG motions.

-

Translate funnel and channel levers into the demand assumptions behind n8n’s integrated top-line model and quantify the financial impact and trade-offs of changing them.

-

Assess CAC and payback, conversion, pipeline contribution, and expected versus realized ROI using attribution, incrementality testing, and other causal methods where appropriate.

### BUILD A RIGOROUS MARKETING INVESTMENT FRAMEWORK

-

Establish a repeatable process for material marketing bets covering hypothesis, baseline, expected return, confidence range, leading indicators, scale-or-stop triggers, and post-investment review.

-

Build and defend investment cases for brand, community, content, events, and other spend that does not attribute cleanly to closed revenue.

-

Use internal evidence, external benchmarks, and post-investment learnings to drive in-year reallocations toward the highest-impact opportunities.

### TURN DATA INTO DECISIONS

-

Partner with Marketing Ops, RevOps, Data, and Controlling to keep the financial view aligned with n8n’s attribution, measurement, and reporting frameworks.

-

Own the financial metric definitions behind Marketing reporting and ensure teams are operating from a consistent view of performance.

-

Turn financial reporting and variance analysis into tangible insights and recommendations for Marketing leadership, executives, and board-level audiences when needed.

## REQUIREMENTS

### **Must-haves**

-

**📊 FP&A experience:** You have 7+ years of experience in FP&A or strategic finance, ideally in a fast-paced technology or B2B SaaS environment.

-

**🗂️ Complex planning experience:** You have led complex, multi-entity, multi-stakeholder annual planning, budgeting, and forecasting processes end to end.

-

**🤝 Marketing business partnering:** You have deep demand-side finance partnering experience in either a self-serve/PLG funnel or an enterprise/SLG demand generation motion, plus a strong working understanding of the other.

-

**📈 Funnel and channel economics:** You are fluent in CAC and CAC payback by channel and motion, blended versus paid CAC, CLV/CAC, pipeline attribution, funnel conversion, and velocity.

-

**🧭 Judgment on unattributable spend:** You have built and defended investment cases for brand, community, content, or similar spend that will never attribute cleanly to a closed deal.

-

**💶 Program spend ownership:** You are comfortable owning a budget weighted toward program spend, including commitments, phasing, agency costs, vendor economics, and reallocations.

-

**🧮 Financial modeling:** You build sophisticated, integrated financial models that remain robust as assumptions, scenarios, and operating inputs change.

-

**🗣️ Executive communication:** You translate complex analysis into clear recommendations and are comfortable presenting to Marketing leadership, executives, and board audiences when needed.

-

**🤖 AI- and automation-first mindset:** You naturally use AI and automation platforms such as n8n to improve the quality, speed, and repeatability of your planning, reporting, and analysis.

### **Nice-to-haves**
