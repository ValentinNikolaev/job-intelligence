**To strengthen our Customer Care team, we are looking for a**

**Director Customer Care (m/f/d)**

(Hybrid in Cologne, 8 days/month on site / Full-time / Permanent)

As we continue to scale our service excellence, we are looking for an experienced and hands-on leader to shape the future of Customer Care at Trusted Shops. In this role, you will drive operational performance, customer satisfaction, and service innovation across our consumer-facing services. Working closely with cross-functional stakeholders, you will help create secure, scalable, and customer-centric processes while leveraging automation and AI to deliver outstanding service experiences.

**Your Responsibilities**

-

Lead, coach, and develop our Customer Care Team Leads, providing both strategic and disciplinary leadership.

-

Own and continuously improve key customer service KPIs such as AHT, SLA, and CSAT across all Customer Care operations.

-

Drive service excellence by evolving standards, processes, and workflows with a strong focus on customer centricity and efficiency.

-

Monitor and optimize operational performance across critical processes, including Buyer Protection, refunds, reviews, and related customer journeys.

-

Co-create and implement automation and AI initiatives that improve scalability, productivity, and service quality.

-

Partner closely with Operations, Product, Controlling, Sales, and Finance to deliver sustainable business and customer outcomes.

**Your Profile**

-

Degree in Business, Communication, or a comparable field, or equivalent professional experience.

-

5+ years of leadership experience within Customer Care, Service Operations, or a comparable customer-focused environment, including experience leading leaders.

-

Proven track record of improving service KPIs, operational processes, and omnichannel customer support organizations.

-

Hands-on experience with CRM and ticketing systems, ideally Zendesk or Salesforce, as well as AI-supported service automation initiatives

-

Proactive, solution-oriented leadership style with excellent communication, organizational, and stakeholder management skills, ideally combined with experience with a works council.

-

Strong analytical approach to performance management, process improvement, and operational excellence.

-

Business-fluent English and German communication skills
