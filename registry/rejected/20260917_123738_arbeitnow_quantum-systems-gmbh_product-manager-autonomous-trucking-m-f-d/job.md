Join Quantum-Systems and bring autonomous trucks into real-world operations. As Product Manager Autonomous Trucking, you own the end-to-end strategy, roadmap, and delivery for the MOSAIC Ground Autonomy Kit, Quantum-Systems' solution to converting heavy-duty and tactical vehicles into autonomous assets. You operate at the intersection of customer value, technical feasibility, and commercial viability and in an extremely tight partnership with Engineering: you own the customer needs, they own how we achieve it technically and by when we deliver. Together you balance product ambition against engineering reality, make trade-offs consciously, and keep the roadmap that the whole organization can execute against.

-

End-to-End Product Management: Own the vision, strategy, and roadmap from discovery, concept, and validation through to certification, commercial launches, and long-term value optimization

-

Customer & Market Translation: Work directly with customers, end-users, and sales representatives to analyze operational demands and constraints, translating them into product features and services

-

Engineering Collaboration: Partner with software, hardware, safety, and systems engineering teams in product refinement, planning, and prioritization

-

Iterative Delivery: Consolidate customer, user, and field operational insights to set product priorities and continuously convert requirements into shipped capabilities

-

Ecosystem Management: Manage strategic relationships across vehicle OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers, and service providers.

-

Go-to-Market Strategy: Collaborate with Product Marketing to design and execute compelling go-to-market plans, ensuring that every product release is a step forward toward market leadership
