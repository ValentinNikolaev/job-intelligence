### What you will do

-

Reporting to the Director of Engineering, you will lead and grow a team of engineers , owning technical direction plus hiring, growth, and performance for the people on your team who build [Sysdig](https://himalayas.app/companies/sysdig)'s runtime agent, the software customers install directly in their environments and Sysdog’s the primary data source.

-

You will review designs and code across the team, unblock engineers on the hardest problems, and stay hands-on with the most technically demanding, cross-cutting work yourself.

-

You will partner with product, detection and security research teams to guarantee new functionality into our runtime agent without regressing performance or stability in production.

### What you will bring with you

- You have experience leading a team of engineers: hiring, mentoring, and managing performance while staying technical enough to set direction and review the work yourself.

- You have extensive experience building and operating low-level, performance-critical systems software (C, C++) that runs continuously in production on customer infrastructure as well as working experience with containerized and orchestrated systems (kubernetes, containerd, familiarity with cgroup).

- You have deep working knowledge of OSs internals and instrumentation techniques such as eBPF or kernel modules. Proficiency on Linux internal is required.

- You have experience operating distributed systems at scale, including the reliability and performance trade-offs of software that has to run safely on every host or container it touches.

### What we look for

- You have experience on managing a distributed team, with experience on planning with a long term roadmap and features that span across different teams.

- Expertise in Go or Rust will be a plus

- Experience growing a team through a period of rapid product change.

- Good knowledge of Windows and/or Mac’s internal is a plus.
