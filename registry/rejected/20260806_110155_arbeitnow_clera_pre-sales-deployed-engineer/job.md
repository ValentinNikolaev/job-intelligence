### About the Role

Join a fast-growing **AI developer tools company** as a **Pre-Sales Deployed Engineer** based in London. You'll be the technical heartbeat of the sales process — partnering closely with an Account Executive to win enterprise deals by running hands-on technical pilots with customers' engineering teams. This is emphatically *not* a pure sales role or a pure engineering role: you need to be genuinely capable of both writing production-quality code **and** closing deals.

The company has proven product-market fit with enterprise customers and is now in scaling mode — making this an exciting moment to join and shape how the go-to-market motion evolves across Europe.

### What You'll Do

-

Partner with an Account Executive as the dedicated technical expert throughout the pre-sales cycle, from discovery through to deal close.

-

Design and run **4-week technical pilots** with prospective customers' developer teams, including kickoff calls, office hours, and live pair-programming sessions.

-

Present the product's vision, benefits, and differentiators to audiences ranging from individual developers to C-suite executives.

-

Deliver technical training, live demos, and enablement resources (documentation, guides, blogs, videos) that help developers understand and maximise the product's value.

-

Create and maintain technical collateral tailored to enterprise customers.

-

Relay critical customer feedback to Product and Engineering teams, directly influencing the roadmap.

-

Travel to customer sites or industry events as needed to support engagements.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

1+ years of solutions or pre-sales engineering experience for a **technical product** (developer tools strongly preferred).

-

Strong computer science fundamentals — CS degree or equivalent practical experience.

-

Strong programming skills in at least one OOP language; comfortable pair-programming with customers to solve real problems in real time.

-

Proven ability to deliver technical training, live demos, and customer-facing enablement content.

-

Familiarity with AI-powered developer tools (e.g. AI code completion, LLM-based coding assistants) and genuine excitement for emerging AI/LLM technology.

-

Fluency in both **German and English**.

-

Executive presence — able to lead product kickoffs and earn the trust of senior engineering and business stakeholders alike.

-

Evidence of, or clear motivation for, moving into a sales-oriented role.

-

Commitment to long-term employment; preference for early-stage startup experience over large tech company environments.

**Nice to Have**

-

Background or degree from a top CS programme.

-

Experience working in fast-paced, high-growth startup environments.

-

Willingness and ability to travel across Europe for customer engagements.
