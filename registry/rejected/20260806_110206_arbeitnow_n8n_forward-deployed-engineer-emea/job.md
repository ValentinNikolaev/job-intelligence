### REQUIREMENTS

Must-haves

🧑‍💻 Strong engineering background: You have solid full-stack or backend engineering experience and have built integrations, APIs, or automation pipelines in production environments.

🧩 Integration-building expertise: You can create custom nodes, extend existing integrations, and work confidently with APIs, authentication patterns, data models, and event-driven systems.

🔧 Product-minded technical execution: You can distinguish between a one-off customer workaround, a reusable node improvement, and a product feature worth escalating.

🤝 Customer-facing confidence: You’re comfortable working directly in customer environments and can build trust with engineers, operators, and non-technical stakeholders.

🗺️ Solution design skills: You can take a messy operational problem and turn it into a clean, practical technical design.

⚖️ Strong technical judgment: You know when to build, when to push back, and how to avoid gold-plating solutions that should stay simple.

✈️ Willingness to travel: You’re open to travelling roughly 50-60% of the time for on-site engagements, primarily across Europe.

⚡ Speed to productive: You can quickly understand new industries, tech stacks, and customer environments without needing everything to be neatly defined.

Nice-to-haves

🔁 Workflow automation experience: You have familiarity with n8n or similar workflow automation tools.

🏢 Enterprise deployment experience: You’ve worked with strategic enterprise accounts in complex technical environments.

🧱 Node or connector development: You’ve built custom nodes, connectors, plugins, SDKs, or reusable integration frameworks.

🚀 Forward deployed experience: You’ve worked in a forward deployed, solutions engineering, or similar role at a product-led technical organization.

📣 Product feedback experience: You’ve helped turn customer learnings into clear product feedback, roadmap input, or engineering specifications.
