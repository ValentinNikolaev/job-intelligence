### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to join a small, high-conviction team building purpose-built AI agents for the sea and air freight industry. As a **Founding Engineer**, you will sit at the intersection of customer discovery, product strategy, and hands-on engineering. You'll help transition the product from demo-stage to a scalable, production-grade platform — and play a direct role in shaping the company's technical and strategic direction from the ground up.

The company is an early-stage B2B SaaS startup in the freight and logistics technology space, developing an AI operating system that automates operational workflows — such as rate requests, bookings, and exception handling — for top-tier freight forwarders. The platform integrates with existing industry systems and continuously improves with every shipment processed.

**Work arrangement:** Hybrid — Berlin, Germany. Visa sponsorship is available.

### What You'll Do

-

Lead customer conversations, deeply understand user pain points, and translate insights into concrete product features and technical strategy.

-

Design, build, and ship full-stack applications across the entire product surface area.

-

Advance the product's AI capabilities — including LLM integrations, agent orchestration, and data pipelines.

-

Drive the transition from MVP and demo-mode to scalable, high-quality production systems.

-

Contribute to shaping product direction, engineering culture, and company strategy as an early team member.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

2+ years of software engineering experience.

-

Full-stack development proficiency: **Node.js, TypeScript, and React** on the frontend/backend; **Python** for backend and ML-adjacent work.

-

Hands-on experience with **LLM design patterns**, vector stores, and model fine-tuning.

-

Proven track record designing, building, and deploying scalable products from MVP through production.

-

Comfort leading customer conversations and distilling feedback into clear product requirements.

-

Experience working in a startup or early-stage environment — you thrive with autonomy and ambiguity.

**Nice to have:**

-

Familiarity with the freight forwarding or broader logistics technology space.

-

Experience integrating with enterprise systems (e.g. ERP, TMS, or carrier portals).

-

Background at a high-calibre technology, consulting, or logistics-tech company.
