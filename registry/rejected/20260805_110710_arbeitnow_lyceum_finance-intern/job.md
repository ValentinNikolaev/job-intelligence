### The Role

Building the financial backbone of a company defining Europe's AI infrastructure is one of the most exciting places to start a career in finance. We're looking for a sharp, driven, and analytical Finance Intern to join our founding team for a 3–6 month internship, working directly with our CFO on the financial systems, models, and insights that move the business forward.

This is a hands-on role at the core of a fast-growing infrastructure company. Not a coffee-fetching internship, but a real seat at the table from day one. Successful candidates rely less on years of experience than on ownership, structure, and the drive to build things from the ground up.

### What You'll Do

-

Support financial reporting, forecasting, and strategic planning

-

Maintain and improve our financial models, including cost structures, P&L, CapEx, and cash flow

-

Prepare investor materials and support our equity and debt fundraising processes

-

Build internal tools and processes for finance and operations

-

Contribute to pricing strategy and business model development

-

Work across teams to support decision-making with clear financial insights

-

Curiosity for how infrastructure, compute, and finance intersect

Highly reliable, hands-on, and comfortable in fast-paced environments

-

Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's in business, finance, economics, engineering, or a related field, or a recent graduate

### Bonus Points
