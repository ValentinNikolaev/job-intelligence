Construction is one of the largest industries in the world, yet it is **inefficient**, **wasteful**, and **incredibly complex**. Rising cost pressure and increasing complexity are pushing the industry to its limits. At **comstruct**, we are on a mission to fundamentally change how construction companies operate and we are looking for people who want to build that future with us.

comstruct develops autonomous AI agents that reimagine the entire material procurement process, from requisition to posted invoice. Our platform takes over repetitive and error-prone tasks across the material flow, enabling construction companies to focus on what truly matters: building. Backed by a €12.5M funding round with investors such as **Google Ventures** and **20VC**, we are now entering our next growth phase and are looking for exceptionally driven and ambitious individuals to help scale our product.

Trusted by some of the world's largest construction companies (including Vinci), we are just getting started. To take our product to the next level, we are looking for a **Head of Engineering** who wants to build both the platform and the team behind it, at the intersection of AI, software, and one of the most complex industries in the world.

As our engineering team grows past 10 people across three teams, we need a technical leader who can own hiring, coaching, and org design end-to-end — freeing our CTO to focus on building and discovery, while making sure every engineer has the structure, feedback, and growth path to do their best work.

🚀 **What we are looking for:**

- You have 6+ years of software engineering experience, including meaningful time in high-growth startups or scale-ups

- You have led engineering teams of 10+ people, ideally managing managers or leads, not just individual contributors

- You are technical enough to be a real sparring partner in architecture and system design conversations, not a distant people manager

- You have built or scaled a technical recruiting engine before and know how to raise the hiring bar

- You are AI-first and use modern AI-assisted engineering workflows yourself

- You are structured and calm under ambiguity, and can design an org from scratch rather than just running an existing one

- Full professional proficiency in German and English

**🚀 You have ownership to drive initiatives like:**

- Redesigning comstruct's engineering org structure and career ladder as we scale past 10 engineers

- Building a recruiting engine that consistently brings in A-players across all three engineering teams

- Establishing the coaching, feedback, and performance rhythms that let every team lead grow into a strong manager

- **Your own idea**: You have an idea how we can 10x the value of our platform or our team? Go for it!

**📚 How are we setting you up for success:**

- A very clear path to success. You can turn the things you build from a team to a broader Engineering leadership function

- A competitive salary and equity options, as we want you to be a fully committed part of our team

- Mentorship from both experienced colleagues and external mentors, supporting you in turning ideas into reality and scaling them effectively

- Direct access to our customer champions to iterate on and refine your product ideas

- Top notch hardware, daily dinners in the office, learning and development budget, Wellpass subscriptions and yearly company off-sites & team events (the last ones were in Mallorca, Portugal and Italy)
