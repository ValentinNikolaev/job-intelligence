### About the Role

Join a fast-growing, AI-native B2B go-to-market agency based in Berlin that helps over 200 B2B tech companies across the DACH region generate qualified pipeline and scale revenue. Operating at the intersection of performance marketing, signal-based outreach, and AI-powered automation, the team partners with SaaS and IT service providers to drive measurable growth outcomes.

As a **B2B Account Executive**, you will be a key driver of sales success — owning the full sales cycle from inbound lead management through to close, while building long-term customer relationships and contributing to a culture of continuous process improvement.

### What You'll Do

-

Lead end-to-end sales conversations with strong negotiation skills, from discovery through contract signature.

-

Manage and qualify inbound leads, preparing thoroughly for every customer meeting and presentation.

-

Conduct discovery calls, needs analysis, and sales presentations to C-level and technical stakeholders.

-

Prepare, negotiate, and manage commercial proposals and contracts.

-

Maintain CRM data diligently, ensuring accurate pipeline records, forecasting, and quota tracking.

-

Monitor market trends, participate actively in team meetings, and produce regular performance reports.

-

Continuously expand your product and sales expertise to serve as a trusted advisor to clients.

-

Build and nurture long-term customer relationships through strategic account management and a consultative approach.

-

Actively identify and contribute to improvements in sales processes and go-to-market strategy.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

3+ years of experience in strategic B2B sales with a demonstrated track record of closing and retaining enterprise or mid-market customers.

-

Proven success closing B2B SaaS or enterprise software deals.

-

Fluent German and advanced English proficiency (written and spoken).

-

Experience managing CRM systems (e.g., Pipedrive, Salesforce, HubSpot) and maintaining accurate pipeline data.

-

Experience preparing and negotiating commercial contracts and proposals.

-

Experience with lead qualification and pipeline management in high-velocity sales environments.

-

Ability to create sales reports, forecasts, and track metrics against quota targets.

-

Strong communication skills, organisational rigour, and proven closing strength.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience selling to German-speaking B2B tech companies or strong knowledge of the DACH market.

-

Familiarity with AI-powered or AI-native software products.

-

Experience contributing to go-to-market strategy or sales process optimisation.

### Location

This is an **on-site role based in Berlin, Germany**. Candidates must be available to work from the Berlin office. **Visa sponsorship is not available** for this position.
