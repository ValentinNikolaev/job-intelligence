### Senior Python Engineer

### Europe – Remote

$5,000 to $6,500 per month, based on experience

Our Data Ingest team scrapes the data that powers insights for the world's biggest companies, central banks and organisations, and we’re looking for a Senior Python Engineer to join them. You’ll shape how we capture, process and scale the data pipelines that underpin our insights and fuel our products.

Implementing new scrapers and maintaining existing ones

-

Improving the scraping platform in the areas of data quality, performance and observability

### Own & Investigate

-

Carrying out research and technical investigations

-

Taking on ownership of architectural or conceptual components of the platform (e.g. owning reliability or QA processes or AI scrapers)

### Collaborate & Refine

-

Gaining sufficient understanding of the codebase to make contributions aimed at reducing overall complexity and removing technical debt

-

Performing code reviews, participating in intra and cross-team technical initiatives and discussions

### About the team:

-

The team has run for seven years, with over 200 scrapers and one mission: reliably collecting the financial data that powers our products

-

You will join a technical team of c. 40 technologists with a key purpose of continuously and reliably collecting large amounts of financial data describing cross-border payments, then processing the data, checking the quality and serving it to our users so that they can make the best business decisions

-

The Data Ingest team is the oldest team at [FXC Intelligence](https://himalayas.app/companies/fxcintel), having existed in some form for over seven years now. We provide the platform for collecting unstructured data from open third-party websites and mobile apps, and make sure that the data collected corresponds to the company's data quality standards and SLAs. As the business grows and requirements change, so do we – the platform has survived several iterations and is currently in the process of being migrated to its fourth generation. The team also maintains over 200 scrapers for different providers of data, which power most of the company’s critical datasets
