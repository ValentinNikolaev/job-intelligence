**WHO WE ARE**

Proxima Fusion is Europe’s fastest-growing fusion company and the continent’s best-funded fusion player, as well as the first spin-out from the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (IPP). Backed by over €650M and powered by a growing team across Munich, Zurich, and Oxford, we are developing the hardware and infrastructure needed to deliver the world’s first commercial stellarator fusion power plant.

Our concept advances the most mature fusion technology out there, the Wendelstein 7-X stellarator, through two next-generation machines: Alpha and Stellaris. Our work combines stellarator optimization, advanced computation, machine learning, and high-temperature superconducting magnets to unlock higher-performance designs that were previously out of reach.

Turning these designs into a functioning fusion power plant requires excellence and ownership across every discipline, from physics and engineering to software, manufacturing, law, and business functions.

**YOUR IMPACT**
At Proxima Fusion, we're building the next generation of fusion power plants. That means sourcing some of the world's most advanced technical systems - from heating, cryogenics and vacuum supplier-designed systems to manufacturing machinery and diagnostics. As Procurement Manager for Integrated Systems, you'll lead sourcing for complex equipment where suppliers play a key role in the design, integration and long-term performance of the system. You'll work closely with Engineering teams to select the right partners, negotiate contracts and ensure systems are delivered, tested and commissioned successfully. This is a unique opportunity to shape the supply chain behind one of Europe's most ambitious engineering projects.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

-

Own procurement for integrated systems, manufacturing equipment and supplier-designed technical packages.

-

Develop sourcing strategies and identify the best suppliers for complex engineering systems such as cryogenic systems, fuel cycle, vacuum systems, plant electrical supply, cooling, instrumentation/control/data, plasma heating and diagnostics.

-

Lead RFIs, RFPs and supplier negotiations from market assessment through contract award.

-

Define commercial agreements covering performance, warranties, documentation, acceptance testing and lifecycle support.

-

Work closely with Engineering to manage supplier interfaces, technical risks and delivery milestones.

-

Manage interfaces between technical packages and suppliers, ensuring system-level performance requirements, integration responsibilities and acceptance criteria are clearly defined.

-

Support contract execution by managing supplier performance, change requests and commercial risks.

-

Improve procurement processes using digital tools and AI to simplify sourcing and decision-making.
