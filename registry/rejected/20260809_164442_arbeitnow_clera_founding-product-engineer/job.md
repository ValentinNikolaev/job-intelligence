### About the Role

This is a founding-level product engineering role at a fast-growing, European-headquartered startup building an AI-powered phone assistant for small and medium-sized businesses. The platform automates inbound and outbound calls, appointment scheduling, customer service, and other communication workflows — with a strong focus on the German-speaking market and a privacy-first, data-sovereign architecture built in Europe.

You'll join a lean, high-ownership engineering team at an early stage (recently closed a **Seed / Series A** round backed by prominent European and global investors) and have a direct impact on product direction, architecture, and customer outcomes. The company has grown from a handful of people to ~50 in under two years and is continuing to scale rapidly.

**Native-level German proficiency is a firm requirement for this role.** You must be able to work with CET time-zone overlap. An optional office in Vienna, Austria is available.

### What You'll Do

-

Own features end-to-end — from hypothesis and prototype through to production release — for a product used by thousands of companies across Europe.

-

Lead product discovery by designing and running rapid experiments to validate ideas before committing to large-scale builds.

-

Maintain close, recurring contact with customers, partners, and the Sales team to surface real pain points and translate them into product decisions.

-

Design and implement tracking and analytics to support data-driven iteration.

-

Build across the full stack, driving architecture decisions through to release.

-

Prioritise pragmatic solutions that balance speed of validation with long-term scalability.

-

Take radical ownership of initiatives and move them forward autonomously, without waiting for sign-offs.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

3+ years of product engineering experience delivering end-to-end software features.

-

Strong full-stack proficiency: **Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS, and React**.

-

**Native-level German fluency** (Deutsch auf Muttersprachniveau) — non-negotiable.

**Required:**

-

Experience integrating software with external systems such as CRMs, calendar apps, and databases.

-

Ability to gather customer feedback and translate pain points into product decisions in collaboration with customers, partners, and sales.

-

Comfort identifying, tracking, and analysing relevant metrics to drive data-driven decisions.

-

End-to-end development experience — from architecture choices to production deployment.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience integrating LLMs or building AI-powered product features (e.g. OpenAI or similar).

-

Familiarity with GDPR compliance and EU AI Act considerations in product development.

-

Experience working in a remote-first, asynchronous collaboration environment.

-

Background in early-stage or high-growth startup environments.
