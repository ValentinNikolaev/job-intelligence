# About the role

We're growing 13x YoY - and growing that fast only works if we know exactly which number we're growing toward, and why. That's what this role is for.

Working closely with the Head of Operations, you'll set ambitious financial targets for the whole company, then own the thinking that gets us there: knowing our P&L and balance sheet inside out, understanding exactly what it takes to hit the number, and turning that into a plan the whole team can rally around. You'll be the person everyone trusts to say clearly where we stand, what's needed to close the gap, and what has to happen next - across accounting for both our US and German entities, revenue, and how the company spends and pays its people.

# What you'll do

**Own the numbers**

Stay on top of our P&L and balance sheet, keeping them accurate and up to date. Own accounting for both our US and German entities in-house - running monthly reconciliations, closing the books, handling tax filings and compliance, and making sure our financials are clean and audit-ready in both jurisdictions.

**Help set the company's financial targets**

Partner with the Head of Operations to set financial targets for the whole company, and track performance against them.

**Own revenue operations**

Run revenue operations end to end - from invoicing and billing to reconciliation and reporting - so revenue data is always accurate and easy to trust.

**Own expenses and payroll**

Manage company expenses and own salary payouts, making sure everyone gets paid accurately and on time, and that spending is tracked and controlled.
