## This is what you move:

As Senior Digital Media Planner Luxury Automotive, you will assume strategic and operational responsibility for digital campaigns and budgets, and actively help our client grow as part of annual strategies and pitches.

- **Digital Campaign Planning:** You are responsible for implementing digital campaigns from planning through to completion. You take a holistic approach and coordinate closely with our channel experts in paid search, paid social, programmatic, and e-commerce.

- **Market & Media Monitoring**: At the same time, you monitor market and media trends and use these insights to develop recommendations for placements, formats, and targeting.

- **Stakeholder Management:** Overseeing the coordination with consultants, marketers, channel experts, and campaign management falls under your responsibility.

- **Offer & Partnership Evaluation**: Offers, partnerships, and special advertising formats will be requested and evaluated by you.

- **Conditions Management & Budget Responsibility: **Through close collaboration with the Investment Team, you ensure the best terms and conditions for our clients. At the same time, you are responsible for your clients’ entire digital budget through detailed annual forecasts.

- **Strategy & Pitch Development**: You further develop existing and new clients through innovative concepts and ideas as part of pitches and annual strategies.

## This is what you bring:

- **Education**: A degree or a completed vocational training in marketing, media, business, or a related field is what you bring.

- **Professional Experience:** You have at least 3 years of experience in planning or consulting on digital media campaigns.

- **Technical Expertise:** You are thoroughly familiar with common planning tools, relevant media terminology, and online KPIs.

- **Tools & Affinity For Numbers: **We value your ability to combine excellent Excel skills with strong analytical thinking and a commitment to high quality standards.

- **Working Style:** You are highly organized, resilient, and self-reliant, and you excel at clear communication and teamwork.

- **Language Skills:** Excellent written and spoken German skills round out your profile.

Furthermore you are:** #strategic #proactive #customer-focused**
