**Job Summary**

We are seeking a highly skilled Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) to sit at the core of enterprise transformation engagements, directly embedding with client teams to redesign workflows, establish operating models, and drive technology adoption. This is a highly hands-on, consultative role where you will conduct organizational diagnostics and rapidly build functional, AI-powered proofs of concept to demonstrate real-world value. The ideal candidate is a proactive diagnostician and builder who blends management consulting expertise with the technical capability to ship working prototypes autonomously, ultimately accelerating business adoption and delivering measurable ROI.

## **Responsibilities**

-

Map current-state workflows end-to-end to diagnose root causes of operational friction, including handoff failures, governance gaps, and tooling mismatches.

-

Redesign target-state operations for leaner workflows, clearer decision rights, and effective human-agent collaboration to accelerate throughput using **AI**.

-

Design comprehensive operating models, including organizational structure, roles, governance, cross-functional interaction models, and value realization.

-

Establish performance baselines and build measurement frameworks that track improvement continuously throughout the engagement.

-

Identify value gaps in stalled implementations, diagnosing where technology deployment has outpaced business adoption to restore the path to ROI.

-

Build working, **AI**-powered prototypes directly utilizing **LLM APIs**, platform-native features, and **low-code**/**no-code** environments to create functional solutions.

-

Utilize working demos and prototypes as the primary tool for stakeholder alignment directly within the client's actual processes.

-

Evaluate where **AI** practically enhances a workflow versus where it serves as a distraction, advising clients with realistic, grounded expectations.

-

Support the rollout of new tools and workflows, ensuring adoption is rooted in process design and change management.

-

Design enablement materials that ensure new technology implementations effectively connect to and support the intended operating model.

-

Support complex engagement scoping and conduct diagnostic assessments for value capture, implementation readiness, and adoption strategy.

-

Contribute reusable frameworks and tools that continuously improve practice qualification and scoping.
