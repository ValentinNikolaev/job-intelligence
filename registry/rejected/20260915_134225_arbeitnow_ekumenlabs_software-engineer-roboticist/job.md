We're looking for a Software/Robotics Engineer to join a project building a full robotics stack for an AGV (Automated Guided Vehicle), working directly on-site with our client in **Munich**. You'll work closely with the client's fleet manager, and depending on how the project evolves.

Contribute to the development of a full robotics stack, covering simulation, navigation, localization, robot behaviors, and internal tooling

Perform testing and tuning of complex navigation systems directly on the AGV, on-site

-

Work together with the client's fleet manager on the robot's perception stack

-

Diagnose and resolve hardware, networking, and embedded electronics issues on the robot in real time

-

Potential to manage a team and its resources as the project scales

-
