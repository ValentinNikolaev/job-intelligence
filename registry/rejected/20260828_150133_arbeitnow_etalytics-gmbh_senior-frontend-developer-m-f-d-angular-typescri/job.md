Do you want to build frontend software that makes industrial energy systems smarter and more sustainable?

At **etalytics**, we develop AI-powered energy management solutions that help industrial companies reduce energy consumption, CO₂ emissions, and operating costs. Our **etaONE® Energy Intelligence Platform** combines IoT, data analytics, and artificial intelligence to turn complex energy data into actionable insights.

As a **Senior Frontend Developer**, you will take ownership of complex frontend challenges and help evolve the technical quality of our platform. You will work hands-on with Angular and TypeScript, influence frontend architecture and development standards, and support the team through code reviews, knowledge sharing, and technical guidance.

#### Tasks

-

Develop and enhance scalable, user-friendly frontend features for our etaONE® platform using **Angular and TypeScript**.

-

Take ownership of complex features from technical concept through implementation, testing, and production.

-

Shape frontend architecture and reusable patterns with a focus on maintainability, performance, and clean system boundaries.

-

Work closely with **UX/UI, Product Management, and Backend** to turn product requirements into robust and intuitive solutions.

-

Improve code quality through automated testing, thoughtful refactoring, code reviews, and pragmatic technical standards.

-

Identify and resolve complex frontend issues using structured debugging, profiling, and root-cause analysis.

-

Support and mentor other frontend developers through code reviews, knowledge sharing, and technical guidance and contribute to modern development practices, including **AI-supported workflows**.

#### Requirements

-

You bring a **minimum of 4 years of professional frontend development experience** and have worked on complex web applications or **single-page applications (SPAs)**.

-

Strong hands-on expertise in **Angular and TypeScript** enables you to work confidently with modern Angular concepts such as Signals, dependency injection, change detection, and RxJS.

-

You have a solid understanding of **frontend architecture, maintainable code, automated testing, performance, and accessibility**.

-

You make pragmatic technical decisions and balance **software quality, user experience, business needs, and delivery speed**.

-

Experience with technologies such as **NgRx/SignalStore, NX, Angular Material, Jest, Storybook, Playwright, TanStack Query, or data visualization libraries** is an advantage.

-

You communicate confidently in **English** and collaborate effectively across teams.
