# Future Projects Thermal Engineer

Posted: 2026-08-18T20:40:05Z

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement
- Reason: vacancy is explicitly non-remote

Here at The Exploration Company, we are building innovative aerospace technologies that advance the future of space transportation.

We are seeking a talented Thermal Engineer to help us in our mission by contributing to the development of our future projects. As a Thermal Systems Engineer, you will oversee the thermal architecture, sizing and analysis of a variety of new projects. You will work closely with our mechanical, CAD, and system engineers to assess feasibility and preliminary sizing of the thermal systems of our future vehicles.

## Key Responsibilities

In your capacity as a Thermal Engineer, your role will be continuously evolving, but day-to-day your duties will include:

-

Designing conceptual and preliminary thermal control systems for new space systems, tailored to project maturity and goals

-

Providing technical solutions to optimise thermal management, in collaboration with other subsystems (propulsion, avionics, ECLSS…)

-

Perform preliminary thermal analyses to justify the design decisions

-

Effectively communicating and providing recommendations through analyses, reviews, documentation and presentation of technical data

-

Supporting spacecraft development from concept to operations, including feasibility studies, trade-off analyses, requirements review, system design, and documentation for major development milestones

-

Managing procurement, request for proposals, supplier evaluation and selection, testing and validation of thermal hardware

-

Providing operational analysis and support, including post-flight analyses

## What We Would Love to See from You

In the role of Future Project Thermal Engineer ideally, you will have the following:

-

**Education**: Master’s or PhD degree in Aerospace Engineering, Heat transfer and Fluid mechanics, or another related field.

-

**Technical skills: **Thermal analysis, Thermal management of typical spacecraft subsystems (structures, avionics, propulsion), and understanding of spacecraft thermal environments

-

**Experience: **Proven experience (3+ years) with (Active) Thermal Control System development and procurement, hands-on experience with Thermal Testing, including Thermal Cycling, Thermal Vacuum and Thermal Balance tests

-

**Programming Skills: **Proficiency with thermal software (Thermal Desktop, ESATAN), and in programming and data analysis (e.g., Python, MATLAB, C++).

-

**Communication: **Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to convey technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

-

**Feedback**: Willingness to give proactive and mindful feedback across all company levels.

-

**Mindset:** Passion for advancing sustainability in the space industry and highly motivated to work in a fast paced, autonomously driven, and demanding start-up environment.

-

**Languages: **Fluent in English.
