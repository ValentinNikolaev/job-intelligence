## **The role**

You will take each search from the initial brief to an accepted offer: learning the function well enough to question the brief, identifying the relevant market, sourcing directly, assessing candidates, and keeping decisions moving. You will change the approach when the evidence shows that a search is off track.

## **What you’ll do**

-

Full-cycle Product Owner and Product Manager

-

Direct sourcing and proactive product-talent pipelines ahead of confirmed demand

-

Assessing candidate relevance, securing timely decisions, and leading offer conversations through acceptance

-

Presenting hiring managers with clear evidence when the profile, search strategy, or interview process needs to change

## **What you need to have**

-

In-house ownership of product hiring at a technology company

-

Searches where you personally built the pipeline, qualified candidates, managed the stakeholder, and closed the hire

-

The ability to distinguish strong Product Owners from strong Product Managers and explain what changes across product contexts

-

Numerical ownership of hiring results and a clear account of which actions changed them

-

A record of recovering difficult product searches, challenging hiring decisions with evidence, improving recruitment processes, and closing difficult offers
