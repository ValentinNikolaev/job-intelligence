**Your mission**

Your mission is to design and maintain a scalable, asynchronous backend that powers OKAPI:Orbits' end-to-end solutions across mission design, Space Situational Awareness (SSA), Space Traffic Management (STM), Flight Dynamics (FD), and Space Traffic Coordination (STC), supporting satellite operators throughout their entire mission lifecycle.

You'll work primarily on external customer projects with a Python-based tech stack, while also contributing in parallel to our products built on Java Spring Boot. You design and implement robust microservices that process and distribute large volumes of orbital and telemetry data to deliver reliable APIs and automation processes. You already bring solid experience and take independent ownership of parts of the backend architecture across both environments.

You'll work on the systems that enable satellite operators and space agencies to analyze, coordinate, and protect their missions in an increasingly crowded space environment.

**Your tasks and responsibilities**

- Design, develop, and maintain asynchronous, microservice-based backend systems, primarily in Python for external customer projects and in parallel in Java Spring Boot for our products
- Implement and optimize data processing and distribution pipelines that handle large volumes of orbital, telemetry, and mission data
- Design and maintain message-based communication structures between microservices (e.g., via RabbitMQ or comparable message brokers)
- Collaborate closely with space engineers and scientists to translate mission-related requirements into scalable, robust software solutions
- Share responsibility for the backend architecture across both tech stacks, with a focus on performance, fault tolerance, and maintainability
- Plan and document implementation steps, from system architecture through deployment in a cloud-native environment
- Ensure code quality, automated testing, and adherence to security and development standards
- Advance CI/CD pipelines, observability, and automation to support stable, continuous releases
- Analyze and resolve complex issues in distributed systems with a focus on efficiency and reliability
- Work within small, interdisciplinary teams, contributing your backend expertise to cross-functional projects
