### About the Role

A fast-growing, Y Combinator–backed enterprise AI automation startup is looking for an **Enterprise AI Transformation Lead** to own and scale AI-driven change across the DACH region from Munich. This is a senior, high-ownership role working directly with the founding team to help multibillion-euro enterprises become AI-native operators.

You will serve as a trusted strategic partner to C-suite leaders, personally build and deploy high-impact AI agent solutions, and play a central role in growing the regional team as the company expands across Europe. This is a rare opportunity to sit at the intersection of elite consulting, cutting-edge AI, and hands-on company building.

### What You'll Do

-

Own regional outcomes across DACH, operating primarily from Munich.

-

Act as AI transformation partner to CEOs, CIOs, and Chief Transformation Officers at large enterprises.

-

Run each account as a structured consulting engagement — stakeholder mapping, diagnostics, roadmapping, rollout, and measurable results.

-

Identify the highest-value AI use cases for each customer and diagnose where AI creates the most leverage.

-

Build the first agents yourself to prove value, then drive adoption org-wide.

-

Host executive workshops, strategic briefings, and leadership-oriented QBRs.

-

Navigate complex enterprise politics to find champions, remove blockers, and expand impact.

-

Collaborate closely with the founding team on regional strategy, key accounts, and Munich team-building.

-

Represent the voice of the customer to product and engineering — prioritising what they need and pushing back when necessary.

-

Systematically automate your own workflows by building agents to eliminate manual work.

Proven experience leading C-level engagements and driving large-scale enterprise transformations with measurable outcomes.

-

Commercial acumen across renewals, expansions, and multi-stakeholder revenue motions.

-

Experience building and leading a regional team with ambitions to scale across Europe.

Comfortable with frequent travel and spending multiple days per week at customer sites.

**Mindset:** Extremely high agency, high energy, fast decision-making, concise communication. Low ego, high ownership — willing to do unglamorous work to drive results.
