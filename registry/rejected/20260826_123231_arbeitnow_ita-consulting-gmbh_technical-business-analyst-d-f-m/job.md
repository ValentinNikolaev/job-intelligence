## Your mission

As part of our Market Support team for LMS Next/ParcelParcel, you will work alongside a Product Owner and a Business Analyst to keep the platform’s day-to-day operations running smoothly across all live markets (NL, BE, DE, ES, and soon FR) – from technical troubleshooting to customer-facing questions. You’ll work in a fast-paced, pragmatic, hands-on environment, with direct escalation paths to Tech when needed.

## Your Key Responsibilities

- You own the daily inflow of operational and technical support requests for LMS Next/ParcelParcel, acting as the first point of contact and triage before issues are escalated to Product/Tech.

- You independently diagnose technical issues, such as payment integration failures, frontend display bugs or carrier-specific issues, and determine whether they can be resolved directly or need to be passed on to Tech.

- You are the point of contact for operational questions about LMS Next/ParcelParcel from internal stakeholders across Customer Service, Sales and Invoicing.

- You investigate and document the root causes of recurring bugs.

## What you need to succeed

- You have a good technical understanding of web applications, APIs and third-party integrations (e.g. payment providers, carrier APIs, frontend and backend), enabling you to identify where a technical issue originates – coding skills to fix it are not required.

- You are comfortable working with data to investigate issues independently, e.g. using Metabase, database queries or logs, rather than relying solely on someone else’s assessment.

- You have experience in Technical Support or as a Technical Business Analyst, ideally in e-commerce, logistics or a SaaS/platform environment.

- You are fluent in English and have a very good command of German and/or Dutch.

- You are self-organized and able to independently triage and prioritize an unpredictable daily inflow of requests.

- You are a strong communicator and can explain technical concepts clearly to internal stakeholders from non-technical teams.

- You are thorough and detail-oriented, with the patience required for careful bug diagnosis rather than focusing only on quick fixes.

- You stay calm under pressure and are comfortable working in an operational environment where issues often require an immediate response.
