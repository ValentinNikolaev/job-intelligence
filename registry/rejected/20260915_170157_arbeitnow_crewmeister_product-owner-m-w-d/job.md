**Your Responsibilities:**

After an intensive onboarding period, you will grow more in more into becoming a key driver of our product vision. Here’s what that means to us:

- You are curious and eager to understand our customers' needs and challenges and are relentless in pursuing the best possible solution for them.

- You collaborate with the development team in order to turn product ideas into product reality. Starting with understanding requirements, through providing constant support throughout implementation, you ensure our dev team has an organized understanding of the project and the customer needs.

- You take ownership of delivered product value: you help the team create user stories and acceptance criteria, you prioritise and participate in grooming the backlog items. You make sure the team is doing valuable work.

- You use data-driven decision making to ensure organization of the product implementation, acting as the bridge between the product and development team.

- You remain up to date about open bug tickets and the issues customers and stakeholders face.

- You work closely with our current Product Owner and internal stakeholders to understand and contribute to our product roadmap.

- You own the releases of our mobile app(s).
