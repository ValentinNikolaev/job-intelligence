### About the Role

Join a small, fast-moving engineering team at a seed-stage AI voice platform company building the infrastructure that powers thousands of automated customer conversations every day. This is a high-impact, end-to-end ownership role — you'll work directly alongside customers, shape core architecture decisions, and ship meaningful product experiences in an AI-first, communications-enabled environment. You'll be part of a driven, low-ego team working on-site in Berlin.

### What You'll Do

-

Work closely with customers to deeply understand their problems and design product experiences that solve real challenges.

-

Build scalable backend infrastructure capable of handling thousands of AI-driven phone calls per day.

-

Create intuitive, AI-first user experiences that set a new standard for human–machine interaction.

-

Own high-stakes projects end-to-end — from initial concept through production deployment and post-release support.

-

Contribute to core architectural decisions that shape the platform's future.

2+ years of professional software engineering experience.

-

Proven experience designing scalable backend architectures and making core product and architectural decisions.

-

-

Hands-on experience building products for AI-first or communications-enabled platforms.

-

Demonstrated ability to take a product from 0 to 1 and own it through production.

-

Familiarity with **TypeScript, React, Node.js,** and **Python**.
