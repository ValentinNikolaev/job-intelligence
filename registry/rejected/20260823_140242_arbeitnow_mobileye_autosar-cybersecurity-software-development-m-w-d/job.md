We are looking for skilled Security Engineers with hands-on experience in automotive software development to join Mobileye’s Automotive Software team. In this role, you will work on designing and implementing security solutions, ensuring the security and robustness of automotive systems.

This is a real engineering role for those who love problem-solving, working at a low level, and getting their hands dirty with embedded system development. If you enjoy conceptualizing and developing software rather than making PowerPoint slides, this is the right place for you!

**What will your job look like?**

- Develop security solutions according to customer and Mobileye requirements, from concept to implementation

- Implement and test/debug your application or system on a test bench, HIL, or other prototype environment

- Collaborate with internal experts and customers to explain and align security concepts, ensuring system-wide integration

**All you need is:**

- Bachelor’s degree (Bc) in a relevant field.

- At least 5+ years of practical hands-on engineering experience

- Strong knowledge of security design principles &best practices

- Hands-on experience in embedded system and application development, preferably in the automotive industry systems

- Understanding of automotive security concepts such as software updates, key provisioning, intrusion detection, secure boot, and HSMs

- Familiarity with automotive communication protocols, including CAN, SecOC, MACsec, IPsec, and TLS

- Strong fundamentals programming in C, embedded system concepts, and software build processes

- Strong problem-solving skills and experience with scripting languages/tools like Python, Bash, commandline tools and Regex

- Experience with software engineering tools such as JIRA, GIT, and CMAKE (advantageous)

- Knowledge of Autosar classic (basic knowledge required) and Autosar Adaptive (advantageous)

Mobileye changes the way we drive, from preventing accidents to semi and fully autonomous vehicles. If you are an excellent, bright, hands-on person with a passion to make a difference come to lead the revolution!
