**About**

You'll support our Localization Management team not only with day-to-day tasks like coordinating and tracking localization requests, but also help us bring AI-powered tools into how we run translation projects. You'll work closely with internal stakeholders, our translation team, and external partners.

**What you can expect**

- Support the Localization Management team in handling localization requests from stakeholders across the company, from marketplace content to internal communications.
- Create, assign, and close localization projects in memoQ, keeping every request on track from kickoff to delivery.
- Build and maintain our terminology databases so translations stay consistent across markets.
- Perform formal QA on translated content, even in languages you don't speak yourself, checking for issues like missing HTML tags, punctuation errors, or double spaces.
- Manage and align style guides and project instructions per language, keeping them up to date as our Localization team's processes evolve.
- Check texts formally before and after publication to make sure everything goes live polished.
- Help us test and refine AI-powered workflows that support localization project management.

**What sets you apart**

- You're studying translation, linguistics, or a related field.
- You can judge the formal quality of a translation, even without speaking the language yourself.
- Your eye for detail catches spelling and grammar mistakes that others might miss.
- You're comfortable with digital tools and common office applications, and you pick up new systems quickly.
- You're curious about AI-powered tools and enjoy exploring how they can support translation and localization work.
- You bring proactive thinking, team spirit, and self-motivated follow-through.
- You're flexible and open to evolving processes as our team's workflows grow.
