## **Mission**

Integral was founded in October 2024 and is already one of the fastest-growing startups in Germany. We're building the leading AI-native platform for accounting, payroll, and tax for European SMEs, and we're looking for the sharpest people to help us get there. The growth engine is running. Now we need someone to make it faster, smarter, and impossible to slow down.

As our GTM Engineer, you own the technical layer that powers every revenue-generating team at Integral: Sales, Marketing, and Partnerships. In other words, you are a force multiplier that builds the systems to unlock scalable value and momentum. You build the agentic stack, connect the data flows, and eliminate every bottleneck that stands between our team and closed deals.
