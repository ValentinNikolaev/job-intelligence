### About the Role

A fast-growing, Series B–funded EdTech startup is looking for an **Engineering Intern** to join their team. The company operates a widely-used AI-powered learning platform for students, with tens of millions of users across more than 30 countries. This is a hands-on internship where you'll work alongside a talented engineering team to help build and improve products that students rely on every day for homework help, exam prep, and AI-assisted learning.

### What You'll Do

-

Contribute to the development and improvement of a high-scale consumer mobile/web learning platform

-

Work on real product features used by millions of students globally

-

Collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, and designers in an agile environment

-

Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, and engineering best practices

-

Take ownership of tasks and deliver meaningful impact from day one

### What We're Looking For

-

Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field

-

Solid foundational programming skills and a genuine passion for building software

-

Eagerness to learn, take initiative, and work in a fast-paced, collaborative startup environment

-

Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively within a team

-

Authorized to work in **Germany or the United Kingdom** — no visa sponsorship is available

Experience with mobile development (iOS/Android), web technologies, or AI/ML tools is a plus, given the nature of the platform.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

Competitive internship compensation

-

Opportunity to work at a high-growth EdTech company with significant real-world impact

-

Mentorship from an experienced engineering team with backgrounds at leading tech and consumer companies

-

Exposure to a modern, high-scale product stack

### Location

This role is **on-site** in either **Berlin, Germany** or **London, United Kingdom**. Candidates must already be authorized to work in one of these locations — no visa sponsorship is provided.
