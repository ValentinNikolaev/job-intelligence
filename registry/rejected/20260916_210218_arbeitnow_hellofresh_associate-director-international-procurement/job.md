## **The role**

As an Associate Director, Procurement, you will report to the Procurement Director and own the strategy, performance, and people development for your category cluster. This is a 12-month fixed-term position. You will lead a team of Category Managers, set the direction for supplier relationships and commercial outcomes, and operate as the senior point of escalation across operational and strategic procurement challenges.

This is a role for a procurement professional who is equally comfortable in a negotiation room with a strategic supplier, a governance forum with senior cross-functional leaders, and a development conversation with a direct report. You will act as a connector between your team's day-to-day work and the broader supply chain strategy and as a builder of capability, process, and relationships that outlast the current planning cycle.

##

## **What you’ll do**

**Strategy & Category Leadership**

- Define and own the multi-year category strategy for your cluster sourcing model, supplier segmentation, risk posture, and cost ambition and present that strategy coherently to Director-level stakeholders.

- Translate market intelligence (commodity dynamics, seasonality, geopolitical risk, supply constraints) into actionable procurement decisions and communicate them with clarity and confidence.

- Lead cross-category standardization and process improvement initiatives, identifying leverage across the broader portfolio where applicable.

**Supplier Relationships & Partnerships**

- Build and steward the most strategically important supplier relationships in your cluster, including joint investment plans, multi-year contracts, and structured partnership governance.

- Lead or oversee tender processes end-to-end — from strategy and data-pack through to award, contract, and supplier briefing at pace and to a high standard of documentation.

- Act as the senior escalation point for supply disruptions, quality issues, and commercial disputes, resolving with a mix of supplier accountability and operational pragmatism.

- Develop new or alternative suppliers to strengthen resilience and competitiveness across your category network.

**Performance, Data & Commercial Delivery**

- Take full commercial accountability for your cluster's cost performance, savings targets, and COGS variance — ensuring outcomes are tracked, explained, and acted upon.

- Drive a data-first culture within your team: category reviews, investment cases, and supplier performance assessments should be grounded in robust analysis and structured decision-making.

- Contribute to seasonality planning, static pricing rounds, and volume S&OP processes with the rigour and cross-functional coordination those forums require.

- Oversee cost reporting, invoice validation and supplier database quality for your cluster.

**Team Leadership & Development**

- Lead, coach and develop a team of Category Managers, creating a high-performance environment that is psychologically safe, commercially ambitious, and genuinely invested in its people.

- Shift progressively from doing to enabling: your job is to build the capability of your team, not to be their best individual contributor.

- Own team capacity planning, workload prioritization, and performance development including calibration, promotion cases, and succession thinking.

- Foster a culture of continuous improvement, clear communication, and accountability without micromanagement.

**Stakeholder & Cross-functional Leadership**

- Build and maintain strong working relationships with Product, Supply Chain Operations, FSQA, Finance, and Legal representing Procurement credibly and influencing decisions in cross-functional forums.

- Represent GRO Strategic Procurement at Director-level planning and governance meetings as appropriate, contributing with the strategic perspective expected at that level.

- Manage upward effectively — keeping the Procurement Director informed, flagging risks early, and bringing well-framed recommendations rather than unresolved problems.

## **What you’ll bring**

**Experience**

- 7+/9+ years in strategic procurement, supply chain, or a closely related commercial discipline ideally in FMCG, or food ingredients, though we care more about the quality of thinking than the specific category.

- Demonstrated track record of building supplier relationships at a strategic level, including contract negotiation, partnership governance, and managing through disruption.

- Experience setting and executing category strategy across a meaningful portfolio ideally managing a cluster of subcategories or a team.

- Proven ability to lead and develop others, whether as a formal team lead or through mentoring, cross-functional project leadership, or stepping up in an interim capacity.

**Skills & Mindset**

- Strategically confident: you can connect day-to-day procurement decisions to a longer-term commercial and supply chain vision, and you communicate that narrative clearly to senior audiences.

- Data-driven: comfortable building investment cases, analyzing COGS variance, and driving evidence-based decision-making within your team.

- A natural relationship-builder inside the business and with suppliers who can hold a firm commercial position while maintaining the trust and goodwill the relationship depends on.

- Coach rather than doer: you instinctively look to develop the person next to you rather than solve the problem yourself.

- Operationally resilient: you stay focused and solution-oriented in a fast-paced, supply-volatile environment, and you bring that steadiness to your team.

- Proactive and accountable you take ownership, raise risks early, and bring solutions rather than problems.
