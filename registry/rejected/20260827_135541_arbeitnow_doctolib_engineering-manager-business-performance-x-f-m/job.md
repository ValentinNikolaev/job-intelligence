We are looking for an **Engineering Manager** to join the **Business Performance Team** in **Berlin**.

Your mission will be to enable seamless, secure connectivity between healthcare systems by building and scaling Doctolib's self-service integration platform; making system integrations effortless and accessible to healthcare providers across every market we operate in, from a single platform rather than country by country.. You will lead a hybrid team working on the technical backbone that powers Doctolib's international expansion, contributing directly to reducing integration time by 75% and unlocking 500+ certified integrations for 400K+ health professionals and 80M+ patients.

Working in the tech team at Doctolib means building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients.

**What you'll do**

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

- **Lead** a hybrid team of ~8 engineers (internal employees and contractors), growing internal talent while holding contractors accountable for delivery, and shaping how the two work together as the platform scales

- **Own** end-to-end delivery of integration projects on the platform, ensuring reliability, scalability, and building to national interoperability standards with full compliance to healthcare standards (HL7, FHIR R5/R6)

- **Drive** backend architecture decisions in a JVM-focused stack (Java, Kotlin), championing FHIR-native design and interoperability best practices

- **Build** integrations as a platform that scales across partners, countries and products

- **Partner** with Product, and cross-market engineering teams across France, Germany, and Italy to align on platform priorities and roadmap

- **Champion** AI adoption within the team, enabling MCP-native patterns and AI-assisted integration tooling as part of Doctolib's platform strategy

- **Mentor** and grow internal engineers, while maintaining high delivery standards and engagement with your full team.

**Who you are**

Before you read on: if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply.

**You'll be a great fit if you:**

- You have at least **6+ years** of software engineering experience and **3+ years** of engineering management experience, including leading **large or hybrid teams** (internal engineers + contractors), ideally 8+ people

- You have a strong background in **backend and system-to-system integration**: APIs, middleware, and healthcare data exchanges, with hands-on familiarity with **JVM ecosystems (Java, Kotlin)**

- You have experience with **healthcare IT integrations**, including standards such as HL7 and FHIR, and working within regulated, compliance-driven regulated environments

- You can differentiate your management approach between **developing internal engineers** and **driving delivery from external contractor profiles**, with proven success in both

**It would be fantastic if you:**

- Have exposure to AI tooling in engineering workflows: coding agents, LLM-assisted development, or MCP-native patterns

- Have delivered products across **multiple countries or international markets**
