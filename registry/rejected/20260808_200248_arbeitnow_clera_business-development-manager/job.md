### About the Role

A seed-stage deeptech startup operating at the intersection of AI, robotics, and advanced materials discovery — focused on accelerating climate-critical R&D — is hiring a **Business Development Manager** to translate scientific capability into commercial impact. You'll own customer relationships, drive sales strategy, and coordinate cross-functional delivery, working closely with senior commercial and technical leadership to operationalize growth and scale repeatable processes.

This is a high-ownership role for someone who thrives in fast-paced, science-driven environments and is genuinely excited about climate technology.

### What You'll Do

-

Bridge science and market needs to deliver scalable commercial impact in the climate tech sector.

-

Collaborate with the VP of Commercial and executive leadership to operationalize commercial efforts and scale BD workflows.

-

Act as the primary connection between customers and internal teams — developing projects from initial scope through to signed agreement.

-

Drive strategic sales efforts by researching and developing key commercial opportunities and managing pipeline movement.

-

Maintain a high-quality CRM with relevant reporting; build and refine BD templates and documentation for repeatable success.

-

Identify customer needs, qualify leads, and coordinate lead qualification processes.

-

Own post-deal activities, supporting onboarding and early-stage execution of partnerships.

-

Serve as the main point of contact to manage and strengthen ongoing client relationships.

-

Coordinate with technical and operations teams to ensure aligned, timely delivery on partner commitments.

-

Spot upsell and expansion opportunities within existing accounts and new contacts.

-

Represent the company at international exhibitions, trade fairs, and conferences — including techno-commercial presentations.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

1–7 years of experience in business development, sales, account management, or technical sales operations.

-

Demonstrated ability to own post-deal onboarding and early-stage execution of partnerships.

-

Willingness and availability to represent the company at international exhibitions, fairs, and conferences.

-

Based in Berlin, Germany, or willing to relocate.

-

Fluency in both English and German.

**Preferred:**

-

3–5 years of experience in international business development, account management, or technical sales, ideally in deep tech, advanced materials, or climate-related sectors.

-

Graduate-level education (MSc or PhD) in materials science, chemistry, physics, or chemical engineering.

-

Proven sales track record and commercial success in a B2B or technical sales environment.

-

Experience working across technical and commercial teams and coordinating complex projects.

-

Familiarity with CRM tools and basic sales tracking/reporting workflows.

-

Additional European languages beyond English and German are a plus.

-

Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and follow-through.

-

Comfortable navigating ambiguity in a fast-paced startup environment.

-

Genuine interest in climate technology and science-driven innovation.

### Location & Work Arrangement

This is a full-time, on-site role based in **Berlin, Germany**. Frequent international travel is expected as part of the role. **Visa sponsorship is not available** — candidates must have existing authorization to work in Germany.

Join a well-backed early-stage company (Series A/B equivalent funding) with serious scientific credibility and a compelling climate mission.

-

Work directly with senior leadership in a high-impact, high-visibility commercial role.

-

Help build the commercial function from the ground up — shaping processes, playbooks, and culture.

-

Be part of a multidisciplinary team that bridges cutting-edge research and real-world industry deployment.
