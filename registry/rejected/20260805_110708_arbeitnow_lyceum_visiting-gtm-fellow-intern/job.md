## **Your mission**

As a Visiting GTM Fellow, you’ll help Lyceum acquire and grow customers across outbound, inbound, partnerships, and product-led channels. You’ll run campaigns, talk to prospects, analyse pipeline data, and experiment with new acquisition strategies. This role is for someone who’s scrappy, data-driven, and excited about building the go-to-market engine for Europe’s most accessible GPU cloud.

**What You’ll Do**

-

Execute and iterate on outbound campaigns: prospecting, sequencing, personalisation, and follow-up

-

Support inbound lead qualification, respond to inbound interest, and manage early-stage pipeline

-

Research and map target accounts, ICPs, and market segments

-

Analyse GTM metrics (conversion rates, pipeline velocity, CAC) and surface insights to the team

-

Experiment with new channels: community, content, partnerships, events, and developer advocacy

-

Assist with marketing content: case studies, comparison pages, social posts, and sales collateral

Self-starter who thrives in fast-paced, unstructured environments

Prior internship or project experience in sales, marketing, or a startup is a plus
