At [Castor](https://himalayas.app/companies/castor), we’re modernizing the backbone of clinical research

Clinical trials are the gatekeepers of human health, yet the industry is still bogged down by fragmented, manual processes that can waste time and exclude patient populations. At [Castor](https://himalayas.app/companies/castor), we are building the operating system for the next era of clinical research.

We provide a modular cloud platform designed to run trials end-to-end. Our foundation is a robust Electronic Data Capture (EDC) system, supported by eConsent for recruitment and screening and eCOA to accurately capture patient reported outcomes. We don’t just stop at data collection; we use [Castor](https://himalayas.app/companies/castor) Catalyst (our high-precision AI automation) to strip away the manual effort that historically makes research slow and expensive.

### Your mission and impact

As a Senior Platform Engineer at [Castor](https://himalayas.app/companies/castor), you will be building the foundations that empower our product developers to ship secure, reliable services at scale. Your mission is to bridge the gap between code and production by building out a robust containerized environment on Azure and maintaining a reliable, high-performance messaging backbone that minimizes toil and maximizes efficiency. By championing automation and infrastructure-as-code, you’ll directly impact our ability to deliver life-changing health-tech solutions to patients and researchers worldwide.

### Who are you

- You are biased toward avoiding manual, repetitive work and strive to find the perfect balance between keeping the lights on and building elegant automated systems.

- You enjoy sharing knowledge, creating clear guidelines, and participating in training sessions to elevate the technical capabilities of the entire engineering organization.

- You value documentation and technical clarity, contributing to Architecture Decision Records to ensure our systems are understood today and scalable tomorrow.

- You are curious about the evolution of DevOps, particularly in how to effectively leverage AI tooling and agents to streamline workflows.

### What you'll be doing

- Designing and implementing solutions that allow product teams to run containerized services seamlessly.

- Developing internal best practices, guidelines, and documentation to help developers navigate cloud infrastructure.

- Writing and maintaining code for centrally managed services while improving security and reliability.

- Manage our centralized messaging infrastructure (NATS), ensuring its reliability, scalability and performance

- Providing support through a dedicated on call rotation.

- Contributing to technical documentation and Architecture Decision Records to keep our ecosystem transparent and stable.

### What you'll bring

- Proficiency in at least one general-purpose programming language (Python or Go preferred).

- Solid experience deploying and optimizing solutions on a major cloud provider (Azure or GCP).

- A strong grasp of Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) and the containerization ecosystem (like Kubernetes) and related tooling.

- An understanding of Linux, networking, web applications, and database fundamentals.

- Familiarity with monitoring server and application performance, with the troubleshooting skills to resolve issues in a distributed environment.

- You have a deep understanding of distributed systems and message brokers, and you are comfortable taking ownership of critical infrastructure components that keep our services communicating reliably.

Our preference is for this role to be based in CET+/-2.

We are currently unable to accept applications from people based in: Spain, France, Belgium, Switzerland and Germany.
