**About the Project **

We are looking for a Session Director to support live, supervised voice recording sessions for a multilingual conversational AI project in German (Germany). In this role, you'll facilitate recording sessions, provide real-time participant coaching, and monitor technical quality throughout, helping ensure recordings are natural, authentic, and meet project specifications.

**What You'll Do **

-

Manage session facilitation, real-time coaching, and recording quality monitoring throughout each session

-

Complete approximately 8 hours of calibration prior to production, observing English-language sessions to align on facilitation style, recording objectives, and workflow

-

Support recording sessions independently following calibration

-

Monitor recordings in real time, resolving technical or conversational issues as they arise to minimize re-records

**What We're Looking For **

-

Native-level fluency in German (Germany)

-

Experience facilitating recording sessions and coaching participants for natural, authentic delivery

-

Comfortable managing real-time technical quality monitoring (file format, recording specifications) during live sessions

-

Available to support sessions running approximately 4 hours each

**Commitment & Compensation **

-

Estimated time: Approximately 8 hours calibration, plus supervision across two recording sessions of ~4 hours each per participant

-

Compensation: $65.00 USD per hour
