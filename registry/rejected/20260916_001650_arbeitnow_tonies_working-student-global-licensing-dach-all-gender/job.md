**About tonies:**

tonies is the globally leading interactive audio platform for children with more than 10 million Tonieboxes and 125 million Tonies sold. The intuitive and award-winning audio system has changed the way young children play and learn independently with its child-safe, wireless, and screen-free approach. Tonieboxes have been activated in over 100 countries, the content portfolio includes around 1,400 Tonies in several languages. Since its inception, tonies has achieved significant milestones, including listing on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in November 2021 as well as a globally present team of over 550 dedicated professionals operating from various countries.

Joining our tonies team means being part of an inspiring mission to create innovative and meaningful experiences for children around the world. Together, we’re shaping the future of play, learning, and imagination.

**You as part of tonies:**

At Tonies Licensing, we're on a mission to bring the magic of audio to children everywhere. We are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Working Student to support our portfolio of global licensing accounts and help drive international licensing projects from inception to final product launch. This role is perfect for someone eager to gain hands-on experience in a commercially-minded environment, working alongside a team that manages relationships with major international licensors within the toy and entertainment industry across North America and EMEA.

**Your tasks and responsibilities will include:**

- Licensing Management Support: You support the Licensing team in maintaining and coordinating relationships with international licensing partners.
- Support on International Projects: You assist with international licensing projects across various stages – from initial discussions through contract execution to product development and market launch.
- Commercial Support: You help prepare and maintain licensing P&Ls and support the creation of reports and presentations for internal stakeholders.
- Cross-Functional Coordination: You support communication between internal departments (e.g., product development, marketing, legal, finance) and external licensing agencies or partners.
- Market & Industry Research: You research current trends and developments in the global licensing and entertainment industries and prepare findings for the team.

**What we are looking for:**

- Studies: Currently enrolled student, ideally in Business Administration, Media Management, Communications, or a related field.
- Interest in the Industry: Some exposure to or interest in the global toy and entertainment industry.
- Affinity for Numbers: Basic understanding of commercial concepts, comfortable working with Excel and reports.
- Prior Experience a Plus: Internships or working student experience in licensing, marketing, or business development are a plus but not required.
- Strong Communication Skills: Good communication and presentation skills in both German and English, confident when interacting with external partners.
- Personality: A proactive, structured, and eager-to-learn personality who enjoys working independently and thrives in an international, dynamic environment.
