### About the Role

Join the technical customer-success team at a fast-growing, Y Combinator-backed AI voice SaaS startup headquartered in Berlin. As a **Customer Engineer**, you'll sit at the intersection of product, engineering, and customers — ensuring successful adoption of the platform, resolving technical challenges, and translating real-world customer needs into meaningful product improvements. Your work will have a direct impact on customer satisfaction and the broader mission of automating customer call operations at scale.

### What You'll Do

-

Provide hands-on technical guidance to help customers deploy, configure, and optimize the platform for their specific use cases.

-

Troubleshoot PostgreSQL-backed data workflows and full-stack code (TypeScript, React, Node.js, Python) alongside customers.

-

Support customers in building effective prompting strategies and AI agent workflows to unlock maximum value from the product.

-

Build and maintain strong, trust-based customer relationships, acting as the primary technical liaison between customers and internal product/engineering teams.

-

Gather, synthesize, and communicate customer feedback to drive product improvements, documentation, and roadmap decisions.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

2+ years of Software Engineering experience with demonstrated, direct business impact through written code.

-

Solid experience with **TypeScript, React, Node.js, and Python**.

-

Familiarity with **PostgreSQL**, including troubleshooting data workflows.

-

Experience in a customer-facing capacity — technical support, solutions engineering, developer relations, or similar.

-

Ability to help customers with prompting techniques and AI agent workflows.

-

Excellent written and verbal communication skills; comfortable serving as a bridge between technical and non-technical stakeholders.

**Nice to Have**

-

Strong computer science fundamentals.

-

Prior experience in B2C SaaS, conversational AI, or voice automation products.

-

Genuine curiosity and enthusiasm for AI/LLM-driven products.

-

A pragmatic, ship-fast mindset and comfort operating in a small, high-velocity team.
