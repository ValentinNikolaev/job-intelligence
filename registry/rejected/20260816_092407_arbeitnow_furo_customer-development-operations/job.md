# Customer & Development Operations

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement
- Reason: vacancy is explicitly non-remote

You'll stay close to our customer base, spot which projects are moving toward deployment, and make sure Furo is there when they do: turning our planning platform into a gateway for the next stage of our product. You'll work directly with the founders and help build a new function from the ground up.

## You'll Have Fun If You Are

**Founder at Heart**

-

You get energy from building something that doesn't exist yet — no templates, no playbook

-

You thrive wearing multiple hats across sales, customer success, product, and business development

**Commercially Sharp**

-

You genuinely love talking to customers and are curious about their world

-

People want to say yes to you — whether you're building trust or closing the next step

**Data-Driven and Structured**

-

You're as comfortable in data (e.g., Supabase or Hubspot) as you are on a customer call

-

You don't just track things, you build systems: scalable processes, automated workflows, dashboards that tell you what to do next

-

You believe that the best commercial instinct is backed by data

## You Won't Have Fun If You

-

Are scared of hardware: You’ll build a solid understanding of the energy system on site and stay on top of our integration landscape to get our battery projects running

-

Can't spend half your day talking to customers: this role lives and dies on relationships

-

Feel uncomfortable digging into databases, user stats, or platform analytics

-

Expect top-down instructions: we trust you to figure it out

-

Prefer a typical 9–5: we move fast and take full ownership

-

Don't enjoy being close to customers and partners — we visit sites across Germany

## Qualifications

-

Fast-paced environment in your past: top-tier consulting, a hypergrowth startup, or a commercial role in energy or infrastructure

-

Experience in operations-focused roles and managing a large customer portfolio with structured processes

-

C1 German or higher — most of our customers are German-speaking

-

Bonus: Familiarity with energy project development, storage, or grid-connected assets
