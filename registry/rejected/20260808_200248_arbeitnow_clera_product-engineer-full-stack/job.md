### About the Role

A fast-growing, seed-stage enterprise AI platform company in Berlin is looking for a talented **Full-Stack Product Engineer** to join their small, high-impact engineering team. Founded in 2023 and backed by top-tier investors, the company builds a comprehensive, security-first AI platform that helps organisations securely adopt and integrate generative AI — from everyday chat and workflows to fully custom AI agents — all within a compliant, European-hosted environment.

This is a full-time, on-site role in Berlin. You'll work closely with the Chief Product Officer and cross-functional teammates to own features end-to-end and ship AI-powered capabilities that drive enterprise adoption at scale.

### What You'll Do

-

Contribute to the development of product features across the AI platform, from technical design through to user-facing implementation.

-

Build end-to-end solutions spanning frontend (**Next.js**), backend (**Node.js**), and database layers.

-

Collaborate with the design team and customers to gather insights and iterate on product features.

-

Implement user interfaces including full screens, reusable components, and complete user flows.

-

Maintain and optimise the codebase for performance and user experience.

-

Participate in rotating **Support Engineer** duties, periodically shifting focus to assist customers and the customer support team alongside the rest of the engineering team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

2+ years of professional Software Engineering experience.

-

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or an equivalent technical field.

-

Proficiency with **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Node.js**.

-

Hands-on experience with **PostgreSQL**, **Redis**, **Prisma**, **tRPC**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

-

Proven track record of delivering features end-to-end — from concept to production — in a full-stack environment.

-

Experience building AI-powered product features and integrating AI capabilities into enterprise applications.

-

Experience participating in customer support rotations or directly supporting customers during the product lifecycle.

-

Strong collaboration skills; comfortable working closely with a CPO and cross-functional teams to shape product direction.

-

Solid Computer Science fundamentals with a growth mindset for rapid learning and adaptation.

-

Willingness to work **on-site in Berlin, Germany** — fully remote arrangements are not available for this role.
