# Senior UX Researcher - Quantitative Focus (m|w|d)

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement

Not every good idea leads to a better product experience. Reliable user insights help teams prioritize product decisions, identify risks early, and measure whether product initiatives actually work. At idealo, you will help build our research practice with a clear focus on quantitative methods. Together with Product, UX, and Data, you will combine qualitative and quantitative evidence to create a stronger basis for product decisions.

### Your Responsibilities

-

You develop research strategies for complex product questions.

-

You select the right qualitative and quantitative research methods.

-

You design measurement frameworks and define meaningful success metrics.

-

You assess study designs, sample sizes, bias risks, and statistical results.

-

You connect product data with qualitative and quantitative research insights.

-

When evidence improves a product decision, the impact of your work is visible.

### Skills & Requirements

**Must-have**

-

Several years or more of UX Research experience with a quantitative focus.

-

You are confident using qualitative and quantitative research methods.

-

You apply significance tests, correlations, and regression analyses.

-

You understand complex study designs and data triangulation.

-

You conduct qualitative interviews confidently in German and English.

-

You take ownership and independently drive topics forward.

**Nice-to-have**

-

Experience with survey design, sampling, scaling, and questionnaire design.

-

Experience using AI tools to support research or analysis workflows.
