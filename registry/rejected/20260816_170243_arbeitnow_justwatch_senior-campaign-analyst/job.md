### Who we are

JustWatch delivers data-driven digital marketing for the global entertainment business. From the biggest blockbusters to award-winning shows, major sporting events, and best-selling console games. Not only are we entertainment obsessives ourselves, but we also own the world's largest streaming guide, which gives us unparalleled data and insights on audience behaviours and content tastes.

From a small startup, we've evolved into a 200+ strong team, based in Berlin, around the world, or working remotely. We've been all about building a profitable business while riding that wave of impressive growth.

### Who we need

Our Campaign team consists of more than 75+ specialists - and it keeps growing: As our new **Senior Campaign Analyst**, you are the go-to person for all kinds of data-related requests, reports, and their automation. Your expertise will ensure that our campaigns are fueled by accurate data, actionable insights, and efficient reporting processes.

Learn where your talents can be developed, deliver at your peak, and create value in your work.
Join us and experience a workplace that's the perfect fit for your talent.

**What you will be doing**

- Collaborate with our Business Intelligence department to maintain correct and clearly defined data and KPIs in campaign dashboards.

- Stay informed about exceptions made for different clients, reflecting them in reports and analyses.

- Accelerate the reporting process for the campaigns team.

- Design and provide dashboards, along with data for weekly and monthly reports.

- Ensure data sanity in our tools, Thoughtspot/Lightdash, and maintain data quality in external dashboards.

- Proactively detect the optimisation potential in our media campaigns.

- Conducting benchmarking and forecasting activities.

**What we need from you**

- Proven experience in marketing analytics or a related field, with a deep understanding of business models and digital marketing channels.

- Deep understanding of the channels we use (Meta, TikTok, DV360) and their relevant KPIs, including their definition.

- Strong analytical skills and the ability to derive actionable insights from complex datasets, KPIs, and benchmarks.

- Exceptional skills in Excel and Google Spreadsheets, with the ability to quickly set up reports with data from various sources efficiently.

- Excellent communication skills, with the capacity to convey technical information to non-technical stakeholders effectively, and simplify complex scenarios, bringing clarity and focus.

- Ability to remain composed and deliver error-free reports and dashboards even in high-pressure, stressful situations with tight deadlines, when faced with obstacles.

- A can-do attitude and positive mindset with a proactive approach to overcoming challenges and a passion for driving continuous improvement.

- Great conduct in English. German is a plus.

- Constant learning, delivering results, and creating value are the core characteristics of everyone at JustWatch, and we are looking for these values in every person we hire.
