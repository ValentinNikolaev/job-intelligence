### About the Role

We're a Munich-based, Seed/Series A AI-powered SaaS company operating in the Construction Technology (ConTech) and sales enablement space. Our platform helps construction industry sales teams identify, qualify, and win new business more efficiently — using AI to automate project research, CRM maintenance, lead tracking, and more.

We're looking for a **Customer Success Manager** to join our growing team on-site in Munich. In this role, you'll act as a strategic Value Engineer for our clients, driving measurable commercial outcomes from onboarding through long-term retention and expansion. You'll be the trusted partner our customers rely on to get real business value from our product — fast.

*Visa sponsorship is not available for this role. Fluency in both German and English is required.*

### What You'll Do

-

Own customer onboarding and kick-offs to deliver fast, measurable time-to-value for new clients.

-

Drive product adoption and translate customer needs into scalable best practices.

-

Manage commercial outcomes across your portfolio — including renewals, upsells, and churn mitigation.

-

Build and maintain trusted relationships with key decision-makers, keeping the focus on business outcomes.

-

Plan and run enablement sessions, trainings, and workshops to elevate customer operational excellence.

-

Lead regular business reviews using usage, pipeline, and performance data to surface risks and growth opportunities.

-

Advocate for customer needs internally and collaborate closely with the Product team to improve adoption and retention.

-

Identify and develop AI-driven opportunities to accelerate processes and deliver additional customer value.

-

Contribute to CS playbooks and scalable processes that strengthen retention and Net Revenue Retention (NRR).

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

1–3 years of Customer Success experience in a B2B SaaS company, or equivalent consultative client-facing experience.

-

Proven track record of driving retention, renewals, and commercial expansion within a customer portfolio.

-

Strong consultative mindset — able to translate strategy into concrete operational use cases for customers.

-

Sales and process affinity; comfortable working with modern sales organisations and revenue-focused metrics.

-

Data-driven approach: you use insights and performance data to demonstrate and quantify customer value.

-

Excellent communication and presentation skills in both **German and English** (fluent, written and spoken).

-

Willingness to travel for customer workshops and on-site onboarding sessions.

-

Strong relationship-building skills, diplomatic presence, and persistence in customer advocacy.

**Nice to Have**

-

Background in B2B advisory, strategic account management, or revenue engineering.

-

Experience working with or alongside AI/ML-driven products.

-

Familiarity with the construction industry or related verticals.

-

Prior experience at companies such as Clay, Apollo.io, Outreach, Qualified, or similar sales tech / GTM platforms.
