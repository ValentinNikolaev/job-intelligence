### About the Role

We're a Berlin-based B2B performance marketing and lead generation agency — over five years in operation, 30+ people strong, and growing fast. We help B2B tech and SaaS companies acquire more qualified leads, shorten sales cycles, and reduce customer acquisition costs through data-driven paid advertising and AI-powered outreach.

As **Founders Associate**, you'll work directly alongside the founding team, owning strategic projects end to end, automating operations, and helping shape the company's next stage of growth. This is a high-impact, high-ownership role for a hands-on generalist who wants to build — not just advise.

### What You'll Do

-

Own strategic and operational projects from start to finish, building and scaling processes using modern automation tools (n8n, Make.com, Zapier) and AI platforms.

-

Lead financial planning — budgeting, forecasting, and developing scalable frameworks to support sustainable growth.

-

Identify workflow bottlenecks and implement AI-driven automation to increase operational efficiency.

-

Build modern business intelligence structures using tools like **Snowflake** and **Looker Studio** to enable data-driven decision-making.

-

Act as a strategic sparring partner to the founders, contributing independent perspectives on key business decisions.

-

Drive new business ideas and go-to-market strategies from zero to one.

-

Translate complex challenges into structured analyses and clear, actionable implementation plans.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

1–3 years of relevant experience in a fast-paced environment — early-stage startup, strategy consulting, or venture capital.

-

Fluency in **both German and English** (written and spoken) — this is a hard requirement.

-

Proven experience developing and launching new business ideas or go-to-market strategies from scratch (0 → 1).

-

Hands-on experience with AI tools (e.g., Claude, OpenAI) and automation platforms (e.g., n8n, Make.com, Zapier).

-

Experience owning financial planning processes including budgeting and forecasting.

-

Familiarity with BI tooling such as Snowflake and/or Looker Studio.

-

Track record of structured analysis and delivering clear implementation plans for complex topics.

-

Experience advising founders or senior leadership on strategic and operational matters.

-

Completed Bachelor's or Master's degree.

**Nice to have:**

-

First experience managing employees or interns.

**Who thrives here:**

-

You have genuine passion for company-building and are deeply curious about how businesses grow.

-

You bias toward action, take full ownership, and hold yourself to high standards.

-

You're comfortable with ambiguity and energized — not intimidated — by a blank slate.

-

You embrace AI and automation as core parts of how modern operations should run.
