### About the Role

A fast-growing **Series A AI search analytics SaaS company** based in Berlin is looking for a **Senior Frontend Developer** to shape the user experience at the core of their product. You'll sit at the intersection of design, data, and AI — turning complex analytical insights into clean, intuitive, and high-performance web interfaces used by marketing teams around the world.

This is a fully on-site role in Berlin (5 days per week). Visa sponsorship is **not available**.

### What You'll Do

-

Take ownership of the frontend experience, driving UX quality across the product.

-

Collaborate closely with design, product, and data teams to translate requirements into polished interfaces.

-

Turn complex AI-generated insights into clear, beautiful, and accessible UI.

-

Own the development and ongoing optimization of web applications for speed and reliability.

-

Champion performance, accessibility, and responsiveness across all user-facing surfaces.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Strong CS fundamentals and a solid engineering foundation.

-

Proficiency with **TypeScript** and **React** to build scalable frontend applications.

-

Hands-on experience with **Tailwind CSS** and component libraries (e.g., shadcn/ui).

-

Proven experience with frontend **performance optimization and profiling**, delivering fast and responsive interfaces.

-

Strong knowledge of **web accessibility** and WCAG-compliant UI development.

-

A genuine **design sense** — you can review designs critically and iterate on them yourself.

-

Ability to collaborate effectively across design, product, and data functions.

-

Willingness to work **on-site in Berlin, Germany, 5 days per week** (dealbreaker).

**Nice to Have:**

-

Familiarity with AI tooling and integrating AI-generated insights into frontend applications (e.g., OpenAI, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Llama).
