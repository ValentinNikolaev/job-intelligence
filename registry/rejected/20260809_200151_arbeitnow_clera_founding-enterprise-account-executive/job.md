### About the Role

A fast-growing, early-stage B2B SaaS company building an **AI-native pricing optimization platform** is looking for a **Founding Enterprise Account Executive** to own and scale enterprise sales from the ground up. You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with the founding team to define GTM strategy, build repeatable playbooks, and land high-ACV enterprise customers — deals that will directly shape both product direction and commercial trajectory.

This is a rare opportunity to be the first enterprise sales hire at a company with a differentiated product in a high-value, underserved category. If you thrive in ambiguity, love building, and want your fingerprints on every aspect of a growing sales org, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the **full enterprise sales cycle** — from self-sourced prospecting through discovery, negotiation, and close.

-

Run consultative discovery with senior economic buyers including CFOs, CROs, CPOs, and Heads of Monetization.

-

Carry and accurately forecast an ambitious revenue target while maintaining week-to-week pipeline health.

-

Design, document, and iterate the enterprise playbook covering outreach, qualification, deal structure, commercial terms, and close mechanics.

-

Partner directly with founders on GTM strategy, ICP refinement, and positioning — feeding market insight back into product and sales.

-

Establish CRM discipline and operating rhythms for pipeline hygiene, prioritization, and clean forecasting.

-

Sell AI-natively by leveraging and improving AI agents and workflows to automate repeatable buyer-journey tasks, reserving human judgment for key deal moments.

-

Act as a trusted advisor to customers and deliver actionable feedback to product and engineering from live deals.

-

**In your first 90 days:** learn the product and ICP deeply, build pipeline through outbound and inbound, close initial deals end-to-end, and begin codifying a repeatable playbook.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

5–8+ years of enterprise B2B tech sales experience with a proven track record of closing high-ACV deals end-to-end.

-

Demonstrated success **self-sourcing pipeline** and consistently carrying and exceeding revenue targets.

-

Strong experience running multi-stakeholder enterprise deals and building consensus up to the C-suite.

-

Proficiency with CRM and sales-engagement tooling (e.g., Salesforce, Gong) and experience building automations for repeatable sales tasks.

-

Comfort working with AI-driven workflows and directing AI tools in a sales context.

-

Strategic, analytical approach to market assessment paired with creative, tactical execution.

-

High tolerance for ambiguity and ability to thrive in a fast-paced startup environment.

-

Professional fluency in English.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience with pricing, monetization, CPQ, or billing solutions.

-

Background at a high-growth B2B SaaS or AI startup in a founding or early sales role.
