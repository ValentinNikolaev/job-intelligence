Wemolo runs its commercial engine on Salesforce and RevOps owns it — along with the wider system landscape around it. Our data model is non-standard and our motion is anything but off-the-shelf, so we need an experienced admin who can run and improve the whole setup largely on their own, with the Head of RevOps as a soundboard. This is a builder role on a builder team, not a ticket queue, in an environment that leans heavily into AI. You own the platform end-to-end and set the standard for how change happens on it.

**What to Expect**

-

**Ownership:** Own Salesforce as our commercial system of record — its health, architecture and evolution across our data model, run and improve the surrounding system landscape (Weflow, Demodesk, Amplemarket and their integrations).

-

**Architecture:** Drive Salesforce architecture improvements so pipeline data becomes trustworthy.

-

**Build:** Build no-code / low-code configuration (Flows etc.) customizing and combining tools to solve recurring operational problems.

-

**Cross-functional impact:** Own changes that span Marketing, BizDev, Sales, CSM, Product and Finance and never deploy one without bringing the affected users along. A change is done when it's *adopted*, not when it ships.

-

**AI-first:** Put AI to work in daily admin — drafting validation rules, auditing duplicates, QA — in line with our heavy AI bet.

-

**Enablement:** Provide technical mentoring to our Junior GTM Engineer and guide the working students on Salesforce and systems topics.

**Inspire Us By**

**3–5+ years as a Salesforce Administrator**, ideally in a fast-moving commercial/RevOps environment

-

Proven ability to **run and improve a CRM and its surrounding tool landscape largely independently** — you don't need direction, you need a soundboard

Deep **no-code / low-code** craft: configuration, flows, third-party applications and combining tools/integrations to solve real operational problems

A **data-quality mindset**: You can trace why pipeline data is untrustworthy and fix it at the root

Structured, **cross-team problem-solving** and strong stakeholder communication; you write change announcements and documentation people actually act on

An **ownership mindset**: Builder, not service desk

Salesforce certifications and experience with other GTM tools
