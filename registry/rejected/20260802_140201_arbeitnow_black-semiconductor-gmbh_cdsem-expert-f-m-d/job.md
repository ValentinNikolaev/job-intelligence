**The Role**

At Black Semiconductor, we are currently searching for a CD-SEM Expert to join us and support the advanced semiconductor manufacturing and technology development. In this role, you will operate, optimize, and maintain CD-SEM metrology tools to deliver accurate nanoscale measurements on semiconductor wafers.

You will play a key role in enabling process control, yield improvement, and advanced node development by translating critical dimension data into actionable insights for process and integration teams. Working closely with lithography, etch, and metrology engineers, you will help ensure stable and high-performing development and production processes.

In addition, you will support metrology capability building through training activities, documentation, and backup support for broader metrology engineering tasks when required.

**To join our team, you should be excited to**

-

Operate CD-SEM tools to perform critical dimension, profile, and defect measurements on semiconductor wafers

-

Ensure accuracy, repeatability, and reproducibility of CD measurements for process control and yield improvement

-

Perform preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis to ensure tool stability and uptime

-

Collaborate with vendors for equipment repairs, maintenance, and spare parts replacement when needed

-

Work closely with process, lithography, and etch teams to correlate CD-SEM data with process performance

-

Generate reports and dashboards on key metrology metrics (e.g., CD bias, LWR, LER) to support process monitoring

-

Act as backup support for broader metrology engineering activities when required, support training of colleagues and maintain CD-SEM training documentation

-

Drive improvements in metrology methods and ensure all activities comply with EHS (Environment, Health, and Safety) guidelines

**Your Qualifications**
