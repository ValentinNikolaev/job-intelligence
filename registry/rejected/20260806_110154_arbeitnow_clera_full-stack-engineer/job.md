### About the Role

Join a lean, four-person engineering team at a fast-growing **Series A fintech startup** building an AI-powered investing platform targeted at the next generation of investors. With millions of downloads and a rapidly scaling user base, this is a high-ownership, hands-on role at a critical inflection point in the company's growth.

You'll own the technical foundation across iOS, Node.js backend services, and AWS infrastructure — reporting directly to the Head of Engineering from our **New York City office**. Visa sponsorship is available.

### What You'll Do

-

Lead deep investigations into complex production issues spanning iOS, backend services, and cloud infrastructure.

-

Perform root-cause analysis across API layers, async workflows, data models, third-party integrations, and infrastructure.

-

Improve backend service reliability, correctness, and performance in **Node.js and TypeScript**.

-

Optimize **MongoDB** queries, indexes, and data access patterns for performance at scale.

-

Strengthen AWS infrastructure stability across Elastic Beanstalk, EC2, deployments, and monitoring.

-

Improve observability using structured logging, metrics, and CloudWatch.

-

Reduce recurring bug classes by improving validation layers, error handling, and system boundaries.

-

Strengthen CI/CD and release processes to reduce production risk.

-

Build internal tools and scripts that improve debugging efficiency and engineering velocity.

-

Partner closely with product and engineering to drive platform-level improvements.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

3–7+ years of engineering experience, including time at a startup and exposure to systems at scale.

-

Strong production debugging skills — able to trace issues across iOS, backend, database, and AWS layers.

-

Deep experience building and improving backend services in **Node.js and TypeScript**.

-

Solid understanding of **MongoDB** performance, indexing, and data modeling.

-

Hands-on experience operating and optimizing systems in AWS environments.

-

High ownership mentality with clear, structured communication in fast-moving environments.

-

Based in or willing to relocate to **New York City**.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Comfort navigating and contributing to **Swift and SwiftUI** codebases.

-

Background in fintech or payments.

-

Strong CS fundamentals.
