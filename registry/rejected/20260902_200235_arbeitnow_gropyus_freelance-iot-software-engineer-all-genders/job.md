## About the role

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer freelancer to support GROPYUS on a 10-month project focused on our Smart Living and Smart Building technology stack.

- Improve zero-touch provisioning [Kotlin, Spring Boot, Linux, BLE / Bluetooth Mesh]

- Enhance monitoring capabilities [BLE / Bluetooth Mesh, Grafana, Prometheus, OTel Collector, Spring Boot]

- Build a Proof of Concept for the new BOS Architecture based on the defined functional requirements

The project is open to freelancers based anywhere in the EU, with an expected availability of 30–40 hours per week.
