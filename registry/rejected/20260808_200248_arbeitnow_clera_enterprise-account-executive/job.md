### About the Role

We are a fast-growing, Series A/B-stage B2B SaaS company operating at the intersection of artificial intelligence and industrial procurement. With a platform trusted by leading companies in the industrial sector across Europe, we're scaling our enterprise sales motion — and we're looking for an **Enterprise Account Executive** based in Munich to lead that charge in Germany.

You'll own the full sales cycle for large, complex industrial organizations — the German Mittelstand and beyond — engaging C-level stakeholders, building compelling business cases, and converting strategic opportunities into signed enterprise contracts. This is a high-impact, high-ownership role for someone who thrives in ambiguity and wants to help shape how enterprise sales is done at a scaling startup.

### What You'll Do

-

Acquire new enterprise customers and manage complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles from initial engagement through contract signature.

-

Build and execute targeted account and pipeline strategies for a defined set of enterprise accounts.

-

Act as a trusted advisor to senior stakeholders, developing robust business cases that quantify ROI and strategic impact (typical ACVs ~€80k+).

-

Lead executive demos, workshops, and negotiations; navigate procurement and legal processes to drive deals to close.

-

Refine messaging, positioning, and deal structuring to continuously improve enterprise win rates.

-

Collect and relay customer feedback and market insights to Product and Leadership teams to sharpen the enterprise offering.

-

Coordinate across Product, Solution Engineering, Marketing, Customer Value, and Leadership to ensure successful deal execution.

-

Contribute to building enterprise playbooks, processes, and best practices for the upmarket motion.

-

Leverage AI tools to research accounts, structure deals, prepare executive conversations, and manage complex pipelines efficiently.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

**3+ years** of closing experience in B2B SaaS or complex enterprise software sales.

-

Proven track record in **value-based selling** with business-case-driven deals — typical ACVs of €80k+ or comparable complexity.

-

Demonstrated experience managing **long sales cycles (6+ months)** with multiple senior stakeholders, including C-level executives.

-

Entrepreneurial ownership mentality — you own your territory, pipeline, and outcomes, and are comfortable shaping processes rather than just following them.

-

Strong working knowledge of enterprise sales methodologies (e.g., **MEDDIC, SPIN, SPICED**) applied pragmatically.

-

**Fluent German** with strong executive presence in both verbal and written communication.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Experience selling into **industrial, procurement, or supply-chain-heavy** organizations.

-

Background at a high-growth startup or similarly demanding, ambiguous environment.

-

Familiarity with AI-assisted selling tools and workflows.

**Please note:** Visa sponsorship is not available for this role. Candidates must have the existing right to work in Germany.

### Location

This is an **on-site role based in Munich, Bavaria, Germany**. Candidates should be located in or willing to relocate to Munich.

-
