## **About the role**

Your goal will be to build DACH into n8n's most predictable Enterprise sales region - developing a team of high-performing Enterprise AEs, instilling forecast and pipeline discipline, and creating a repeatable operating model that wins complex, multi-stakeholder deals

To make that happen, you'll be hands-on in coaching, deal inspection, and team development day-to-day, while staying close enough to strategic deals and customer executives to raise the bar on how we qualify, forecast, and win.

**What you’ll be doing**

**DEVELOPING A HIGH-PERFORMING, PREDICTABLE DACH TEAM**

-

Coach and develop a team of Enterprise AEs through structured 1:1s, field coaching, and deal reviews – raising both the floor and the ceiling on individual performance

-

Build a rigorous forecasting and pipeline management cadence that makes DACH's numbers predictable quarter over quarter, not dependent on late-quarter heroics

-

Create a repeatable operating model – clear playbooks, MEDDIC/MEDDPICC-based deal inspection, and coaching rhythms – DACH's execution shouldn’t rely on you personally driving every strategic deal

-

Hire, onboard, and scale the team over time, establishing career paths and succession plans as the region grows

**SETTING THE STRATEGY FOR THE DACH TERRITORY**

-

Prioritize and run the DACH Enterprise territory with focus – balancing new logo pursuits with expansion plays where community signals are strong

-

Set the strategy for the team's most complex enterprise cycles, from discovery to close, staying close enough to coach without taking the pen

-

Elevate sales methodology adoption (MEDDIC/MEDDPICC), ensuring meticulous qualification, mutual action plans, and disciplined inspection of technical + business wins

**BUILDING A STRATEGIC, MULTI-THREADED SELLING MOTION**

-

Build a strategic selling culture that shifts the team from “running opportunities” to multi-threaded, value-led enterprise execution

-

Orchestrate executive engagement across DACH accounts – aligning economic buyers, champions, and technical evaluators to keep momentum and unblock deals

-

Run high-quality deal reviews and QBRs that sharpen strategy, elevate coaching moments, and improve win rates, cycle time, and forecast accuracy

**PARTNERING ACROSS THE BUSINESS**

-

Collaborate cross-functionally (Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, Marketing, RevOps, Product) to refine messaging for technical buyers, strengthen the enterprise playbook, and remove friction in the sales process
