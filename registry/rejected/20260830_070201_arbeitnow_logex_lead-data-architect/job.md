**Our mission**

At LOGEX, we turn data into better healthcare.

As we continue to scale our healthcare intelligence platform across Europe, we're investing in a modern, unified data foundation that supports analytics, AI, and data-driven decision-making across our products and services.

You will define the future-state architecture that enables this vision. You will establish the standards, models, and principles that ensure our data is well-structured, interoperable, secure, and trusted across the organisation.

This is a highly visible role with broad influence across Data Engineering, Product, Platform, Governance, and AI teams. You will play a key role in shaping how LOGEX manages and leverages data as we continue to grow, including through acquisitions and expansion across Europe.

**Your impact and responsibilities**

**Define the target-state data architecture**

Own and evolve the enterprise data architecture, creating a clear roadmap from the current state to a scalable and future-proof data platform.

**Establish data standards and architecture principles**

Define the modelling, integration, interoperability, and storage standards that enable consistency across teams and products.

**Drive data modelling excellence**

Develop conceptual, logical, and physical data models while ensuring semantic consistency and trusted business definitions across the organisation.

**Enable governance by design**

Embed governance, lineage, security, privacy, and compliance requirements directly into the data architecture.

**Support analytics and AI at scale**

Ensure the architecture provides the foundations required for reliable analytics, data products, and AI-driven capabilities.

**Provide technical leadership**

Act as the senior architectural authority across distributed teams, helping engineers and data practitioners make sound design decisions while reducing fragmentation and technical debt.

**What you will do**

-

Define and maintain the target-state enterprise data architecture.

-

Create and drive the roadmap towards a unified data platform.

-

Develop standards for data modelling, integration, interoperability, and storage.

-

Design conceptual, logical, and physical data models.

-

Define canonical data structures and business definitions.

-

Support engineering teams with architectural guidance and design reviews.

-

Ensure governance, lineage, security, privacy, and compliance are built into platform design.

-

Partner with Data Governance, Product, Platform, and AI teams to align architecture with business goals.

-

Evaluate data technologies, tooling, and architectural trade-offs.

-

Improve consistency across teams while reducing complexity and technical debt.

-

Support the integration of acquired businesses into the broader LOGEX data landscape.
