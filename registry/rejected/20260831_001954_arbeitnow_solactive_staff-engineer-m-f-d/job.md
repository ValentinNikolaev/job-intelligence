**Job Overview**

Our Structure team builds the software foundation for consistent, scalable index development at Solactive. We provide APIs, libraries, configuration patterns, testing frameworks, and reusable components for index construction workflows, supporting a great UX for ideation, production, and maintenance of new indices with clients. In this senior role, you will shape the technical architecture of a business-critical service that accelerates index development and improves quality across the index lifecycle. If you thrive on leading complex initiatives and shaping engineering excellence, we'd love to hear from you.

**Your Tasks**

- Lead the technical direction and architectural evolution of the domain, delivering scalable and maintainable solutions for internal and external users of the index development service.

- Raise engineering standards through hands-on leadership, including code and design reviews, coaching, and a strong focus on simplicity, quality, and maintainability.

- Mentor engineers and support their growth in technical depth, ownership, and end-to-end delivery.
