***Location****: Hamburg, Berlin or Barcelona*

At Freenow, we are on a mission to empower smarter mobility decisions, helping people to move freely and cities to thrive.

We are looking for a Product Manager to join our Strategy & New Business team. You will own a high-potential vertical focused on B2B mobility, think enterprise integrations, automated booking workflows, and non-app ordering experiences that replace outdated manual processes.

You will take early-stage initiatives and turn them into scalable, revenue-generating products. This means working closely with Sales, Operations, and Engineering without having direct authority over any of them.

**YOUR DAILY ADVENTURES WILL INCLUDE:**

- Define and execute the product roadmap for your vertical, from pilot to scale

- Write clear, detailed specifications that engineering teams can build from independently

- Identify and prioritize features based on commercial impact and data

- Manage dependencies across internal teams and keep stakeholders aligned

- Track KPIs and use them to make better product decisions

## **TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE:**

- 4+ years of product management experience, ideally in B2B SaaS, or marketplace products

- Experience in complicated B2B product + integrations.

- Experience working with integrations, APIs, or complex backend workflows is a strong plus

- Comfortable with ambiguity — you can define structure where none exists

- Strong written communication and documentation skills

- Able to manage competing priorities and deliver in a resource-constrained environment

- Self-directed: you don't wait to be told what to do next

- Fluent in English; German is a significant advantage
