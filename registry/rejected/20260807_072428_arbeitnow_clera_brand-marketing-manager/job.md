### About the Role

A fast-growing, AI-powered Gen Z talent and gig marketplace is looking for a **Brand Marketing Manager** to own brand voice, content strategy, and creator programs across both student-facing and B2B partner channels. You'll be the connective tissue between a digitally native Gen Z audience and the brands that want to reach them — building emotional resonance with students while earning credibility with business partners.

This is a high-ownership role at a scrappy, mission-driven startup (11–50 employees) that sits at the intersection of career tech, influencer marketing, and the gig economy.

### What You'll Do

-

Define and steward the brand voice across both Gen Z student and B2B audiences; develop dual-audience content calendars and produce thought leadership that positions the company as a category leader.

-

Own social media strategy for **TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts** (Gen Z) and **LinkedIn** (B2B); partner with the social team to create engaging content and experiment with new formats.

-

Lead integrated cross-channel campaigns, experiential activations, and PR efforts designed to reach both students and brand partners simultaneously.

-

Build and scale influencer and creator partnerships end-to-end — from sourcing and contracting through to ROI measurement — while cultivating long-term creator relationships.

-

Monitor brand health metrics and analyze content performance across both audiences; iterate strategies based on data-driven insights.

### What We're Looking For

-

**Required:**

-

Proven experience developing and executing brand marketing strategies, including establishing and maintaining brand voice and overall content strategy.

-

Demonstrated ability to lead social media campaigns across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn.

-

End-to-end experience managing creator programs and influencer marketing campaigns (sourcing → execution → measurement).

-

Ability to craft messaging that resonates with **Gen Z student audiences** while simultaneously building credibility with **B2B brand partners**.

-

Comfortable and effective working in a hybrid environment.

-

Strong fundamentals in marketing, communications, or a related discipline.

-

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience at a marketplace, gig economy, or career/talent platform.

-

Background in experiential marketing or PR.

-

Familiarity with performance dashboards and content analytics tools.

**Years of experience:** 4–6 years in brand marketing, content strategy, or a closely related role.

**Visa sponsorship:** Not available.
