**The role**

We have big plans at Tomorrow! Our vision: take Banking with value out of its niche and bring it into the mainstream That's a hell of a lot of work – so let's get to it.

As Head of Accounting, you take full ownership of all accounting processes at Tomorrow end to end. You're the central point of contact for all accounting matters in the company and actively drive the automation of our accounting processes, particularly through the use of AI tools.

Your responsibilities include:

- Monthly bookkeeping: invoice management and preliminary account allocation in Spendesk (incoming invoice workflow, approvals), as well as the complete, independent posting of all business transactions in DATEV (account allocation, verification, entry)
- Ensuring monthly and annual closings are on time, including reconciliation of closing entries – our monthly closings are reliably ready by the 11th of the following month
- Handling and monitoring payment transactions
- Central point of contact for all accounting matters – internally and with our tax office and auditors
- Supporting the annual audit: you provide documentation and clarify auditor queries
- Continuously developing and automating our accounting processes, especially by introducing and driving AI tools

**What we're looking for**

- A commercial or accounting qualification, ideally as a certified tax clerk (Steuerfachangestellte*r), and experience financial accounting
- A strong affinity for numbers, an independent and structured way of working, and openness to digital, paperless processes
- Very good DATEV skills, good Excel skills and ideally experience with Spendesk
- Fluent in German and English
- You support the idea that banking needs value: for example an account that doesn't invest a cent in weapons or fossil fuels.

**What we offer**

It will be fun too, we promise! Together with more than 50 like-minded colleagues, you will work every day to win people over to Banking with value. Our culture is based on our [values](https://www.tomorrow.one/en-EU/about-us/our-values/), our active new work philosophy and clear, ambitious goals. But the best way to get to know us is to come and meet us and experience Tomorrow for yourself.

The office is available to you any time. It is wheelchair-accessible, has ground-level access and offers flexible options for designing your workspace.
