### About the Role

We're a seed-stage startup building Germany's most modern accounting and tax advisory platform — an AI-powered operating system for tax advisory firms. We acquire established tax firms and transform them into high-performing, technology-enabled businesses through proprietary software and centralised services.

As a **Founding Engineer – Backend & Platform**, you'll design and operate the core backend that powers our platform: enabling data flows, reliable workflows, and safe AI applications across dozens of firms. This is foundational, greenfield work — you'll define how data moves, how work gets done, and how we scale. Your code ships to real tax firms from day one.

Visa sponsorship is available for this role.

### What You'll Do

-

Design, implement, and operate core backend services spanning accounting, payroll, reporting, and compliance domains.

-

Build robust data pipelines to ingest, normalise, and reconcile data from DATEV, banks, and document sources.

-

Design and operate workflow orchestration for long-running, stateful, and failure-prone processes.

-

Own correctness, observability, and performance of every system you ship.

-

Make pragmatic architectural decisions in a true greenfield environment — no legacy constraints to navigate.

-

Ship to production frequently; your work runs inside live advisory firms from the start.

-

Collaborate directly with the CTO on system design and core technical decisions.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

Demonstrated track record of building and operating production backend systems (language-agnostic background welcome; the stack is TypeScript-heavy today).

-

3+ years of experience building backend systems.

**Required skills:**

-

Strong TypeScript skills.

-

Solid experience designing systems around data integrity, reliability, and workflows.

-

Strong observability, monitoring, and performance optimisation skills — metrics, tracing, and logging in production environments.

-

Hands-on experience integrating DATEV and/or other accounting software into a production platform.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience building data pipelines to ingest, normalise, and reconcile data from DATEV, banks, and document sources.

-

Experience designing and operating workflow orchestration for long-running, stateful, failure-prone processes.

**Who thrives here:** Engineers who take full operational ownership, prefer pragmatic solutions over theoretical elegance, and are energised by messy, real-world infrastructure challenges at an early-stage company.
