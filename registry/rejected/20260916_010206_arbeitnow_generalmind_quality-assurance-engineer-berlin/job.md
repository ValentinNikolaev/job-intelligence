## Tasks

- Test the platform from a real user's perspective, running through both typical flows and the unusual ones ("what happens if I enter the wrong thing?").

- Track down bugs, crashes, display issues, and illogical behaviour, and document them clearly with steps to reproduce.

- Judge whether features work the way they should, and flag anything confusing, clunky, or inconsistent (usability).

- Check different devices, browsers, and screen sizes to make sure everything works everywhere.

- Rank bugs by severity (critical vs. cosmetic) and prioritise them so the team knows what to fix first.

- Retest fixed bugs and confirm that the fixes didn't break anything else (regression testing).

- Work closely with engineering and product, answer their questions, and help reproduce issues.

## Requirements

- A sharp, thorough eye for detail and the patience to get to the bottom of things.

- A structured, clear way of documenting bugs so anyone can follow them.

- The ability to think like a user and to use software "the wrong way" on purpose to find its limits.

- Clear, easy-to-understand communication, both written and spoken.

- First experience with software testing, QA tools, or ticketing systems (e.g. Jira, Linear) is a plus, but not a must depending on focus.
