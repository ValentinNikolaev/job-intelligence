- 5+ years of hands-on development experience with MS Dynamics Business Central or Dynamics NAV, ideally within a Microsoft Partner or enterprise environment
- Deep expertise in AL programming, Visual Studio Code, and Business Central development frameworks
- Proven track record of delivering scalable, high-quality technical solutions for complex business processes

- Strong knowledge of modern integration techniques, including APIs, Web Services, and Power Platform

- Practical experience leveraging AI-powered tools and services to enhance ERP functionality
