## **About TACTILIA:**

At TACTILIA, we are building the industry-ready robotic hand that closes one of the biggest gaps in Physical AI. Spun out of SCHUNK, the global market leader in gripping technology, we combine a decade of robotic-hand expertise and real industrial access with the speed and ambition of a deep-tech startup.

This isn't a research project waiting for its first customer. We already have a product, customers are ready to use it, and we are launching now. Your work will directly shape the electronics, actuators, and embedded systems that make that possible.

You will join at the moment when the hard engineering questions become real product decisions: how do we make a highly capable robotic hand reliable, manufacturable, serviceable, and simple enough to deploy on a real shop floor?

**The Role:**

TACTILIA is entering a new chapter as an independent company, following its spin-off from SCHUNK. As Chief of Staff, you will work closely with the leadership team as a key operational and strategic partner, helping shape and drive the company’s next phase of growth.

This is a high-ownership role, you will operate across strategy, technology, product, business and operations, taking on the TACTILIA’s most important and ambiguous problems and driving them from idea to outcome.

You will help build the operating infrastructure that allows TACTILIA to scale without losing its speed, ambition or quality.
