🎯 Your main goal will be to own the forward-looking financial plan for n8n’s commercial engine and help Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Partnerships make sharper, data-driven investment decisions.

To make that happen, you’ll co-own the GTM plan, turn commercial strategy into actionable financial insight, and build the models and operating rhythms that support n8n’s next stage of growth:

### DRIVE GTM PLANNING AND FORECASTING

-

Lead n8n’s annual planning and regular forecasting process for GTM, from top-down target setting and cross-functional alignment through to consolidation into the company plan.

-

Build and maintain an integrated sales-led top-line model that reflects the evolving strategies of Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Partnerships.

-

Partner with other FP&A Managers and Controlling to ensure the company’s forward plan and actual performance are connected, consistent, and interpreted with rigor.

### MODEL COMMERCIAL PERFORMANCE

-

Model pipeline health, sales dynamics, funnel economics, and enterprise sales performance to help leadership understand the drivers behind growth.

-

Translate key growth levers such as pipeline generation, conversion, and ACV into clear financial impact and investment trade-offs.

-

Support pricing and packaging analysis, including the impact of commercial decisions on revenue and margin.

### PARTNER WITH GTM LEADERSHIP

-

Own the financial partnership with GTM budget owners, helping them right-size investment and make informed decisions before committing resources.

-

Define the KPIs, reporting cadence, and frameworks that improve decision-making, including a consistent approach to marketing ROI, CAC, conversion, and marketing-sourced bookings.

-

Present clear, concise recommendations to Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and executive leadership, including board-level audiences when required.

### STRENGTHEN GTM FINANCE OPERATIONS

-

Partner with RevOps on capacity planning, funnel performance, and the financial implications of GTM strategy.

-

Work with Data and Controlling to turn GTM financial reporting into tangible insight and recommendations rather than simply explaining variances.

-

Partner with RevOps and Controlling to forecast sales commission expense as part of the overall GTM budget.

-

Use AI and automation to create repeatable, scalable planning and reporting workflows that reflect how an automation company should operate.
