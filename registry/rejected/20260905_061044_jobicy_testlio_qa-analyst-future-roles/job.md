### What Your Day Looks Like

- Design and execute smoke, scripted, and exploratory test cases on mobile, web and desktop.

- Conduct functional, localization, regression, and payments testing to ensure software meets quality standards.

- Report, reproduce, and track issues found during testing.

- Document and share test results, including bugs and potential risks.

- Analyze test results, logs, and related data to identify patterns, trends, and areas for improvement.

- Collaborate with developers, product managers, and other stakeholders to align on goals, timelines, and deliverables.

- Communicate test progress, issues, and risks clearly and proactively with stakeholders.

- Generate test reports that provide actionable insights.

- Design and execute API tests using tools like Postman, SoapUI, or similar.

- Create, maintain, and review test cases and test suites aligned with business priorities and industry best practices.

- Design and optimize effective test data.

- Ensure the test library remains relevant by refactoring, updating, and deprecating tests as needed.

- Develop and maintain a deep understanding of the systems under test.

- Stay up to date with trends and advancements in software testing tools and methodologies.

### What You’ll Need to Succeed

**Technical Skills:**

- Proven experience (2+ years) as a QA Analyst or similar role, with a strong understanding of software testing principles, methodologies, and tools (such as JIRA, Bugzilla, TestRail, Xray or similar.)

- Experience with mobile and web applications, frontend and backend testing (including API testing.)

- Proficiency in identifying and documenting software bugs and issues.

- Ability to solve technical tasks required during testing - such as test data generation, log capturing and basic analysis, working with browser developer tools, network logs analyzers, etc.

- Experience in working in agile teams in a remote environment - proven track record of communication and collaboration with different stakeholders such as Scrum Masters, Product Owners and others.

- Good knowledge of SDLC, CI/CD, and DevOps, as well as an understanding of test automation principles in effective SDLC.

**Human Skills:**

- Effective English communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate and work effectively in a team environment.

- Drive to learn quickly and continuously expand knowledge.

- Comfortable with a frequently changing environment and able to adapt fast.

- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.

- Strong attention to detail and the ability to prioritize and manage multiple tasks.
