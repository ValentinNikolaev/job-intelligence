About us

Satispay began by rethinking the simple act of a payment to remove the friction from our daily routines. But we didn't stop there. Today, we are building a complete financial platform designed to empower people and concretely improve their lives. By giving our 6 million users a clear, open path to pay, save, and invest, we are evolving into the definitive destination for every financial need.



What you'll be doing

As a Software Engineer, Backend, in the Business Team, you'll play a key role in shaping the future of digital payments by designing and building scalable, high-quality systems. Here's what your day-to-day will look like:

 - Own the Story Lifecycle: Complete the entire story lifecycle from intake to production release, ensuring all requirements are met for product stories.

 - Build Business Financial Systems: Develop and maintain services for fee calculations, invoicing, payout orchestration, and settlement flows to ensure accuracy for thousands of businesses.

 - Deliver Compliance & Reporting Solutions: Contribute to systems handling periodic reviews, risk assessments, and transaction reporting to support regulatory requirements.

 - Contribute to Technical Excellence: Conduct technical analysis for standard scope stories and implement robust, scalable back-end solutions.

 - Work in an AI Engineering Culture: Use AI as a core part of your engineering workflow to accelerate development and deepen analysis while keeping quality and judgment at the center.

 - Grow Your Technical Voice: Share your vision with the team through code reviews and technical discussions, building the skills to become a technical reference for the pod.

 - Ensure Quality and Stability: Accurately estimate effort, resolve blockers promptly, and participate in incident resolution and root cause analysis.

 - Document and Communicate: Create comprehensive technical documentation for the features you build and collaborate effectively with stakeholders.



Who we're looking for

We need a problem solver who is eager to learn, owns their work, and is ready to collaborate and to take real ownership. Does it sound like you?

 - Experience & Technical Foundations: You have at least 3 years of industry experience working with Java and a foundational understanding of cloud platforms (AWS is a plus).

 - Teamwork and Communication: You are able to communicate clearly and professionally, contributing to team-level knowledge sharing and building trust with peers.

 - Financial Domain Awareness: You have an interest in – or experience with – financial systems such as billing, invoicing, payouts, or settlement processes.

 - User Focus: You care about the end result and how the back-end supports a great experience for businesses. A genuine interest in FinTech is a plus.

 - Language proficiency: You are fluent in English, both written and spoken.
