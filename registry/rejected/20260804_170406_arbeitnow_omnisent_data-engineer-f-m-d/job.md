## Tasks

We are looking for a **Data Engineer** to build and maintain the data infrastructure behind our flight testing and acoustic sensor development – turning raw field data into structured, actionable insights for our hardware and ML teams.

- Design, build, and maintain databases and pipelines for acoustic device parameters and flight test data using SQL and Postgres

- Structure and manage large volumes of field-collected sensor and flight test data

- Build tools and processes to make test data accessible and usable across hardware, ML, and flight test teams

- Translate raw flight test results into structured, actionable datasets and insights

- Collaborate with flight test engineers and ML engineers to ensure data quality and completeness from collection through analysis

## Requirements

- Experience working with sensor, test, or field-collected data

- Comfortable structuring and cleaning large, messy real-world datasets

- Strong collaboration skills, able to work closely with hardware, flight test, and ML teams

**Bonus:**
