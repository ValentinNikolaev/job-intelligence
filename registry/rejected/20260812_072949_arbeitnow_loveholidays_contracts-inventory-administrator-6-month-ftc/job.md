**The impact you’ll have:**

Reporting to the Contracts and Inventory Manager Contracts and Inventory Administrator will provide administrative support to our contractors, direct hotels and transfer suppliers.

**Your day-to-day:**

-

Ensure Contracts, Special Offers and Stop Sales are loaded correctly, and any discrepancies corrected as a matter of urgency.

-

Provide support on cost queries, inventory, and availability queries.

-

Ensure, that pricing queries on all bookings and all relative requirements are applied with minimal delay

-

Ensure all rejected bookings will get accepted with minimal financial impact through negotiation with suppliers.

-

Brief the business on additional content requirements, and or quality control issues with content that have business consequences.

-

Report on errors and look at route causes to escalate issues and ensure mistakes are not repeated
