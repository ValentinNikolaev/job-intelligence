**Mission Brief **

Join our Flight Software team and help define how a launch vehicle behaves throughout all phases of flight. This role sits at the intersection of Guidance, Navigation & Control, flight dynamics, mission analysis, simulation and flight software, turning engineering insight into robust C++ and Python implementations that can be tested, verified and flown.

Your Role in Our Space Mission

- Develop, improve and verify GNC algorithms for launch vehicle applications.

- Contribute to guidance strategy, navigation solutions and vehicle control techniques.

- Support trajectory design, mission analysis and flight performance assessment.

- Develop and maintain high-fidelity simulation environments for design, development and verification.

- Model vehicle dynamics, environmental effects, sensors, actuators and subsystem interactions.

- Implement and integrate GNC algorithms within production flight software.

- Create analysis, testing and automation tools using C++, Python and MATLAB.

- Investigate anomalies and unexpected behaviour in simulation, testing and flight operations, driving issues to root cause.

**Qualification Checklist **

- Background in GNC, flight dynamics, astrodynamics, aerospace engineering, robotics or a related discipline.

- Strong understanding of dynamic systems and vehicle behaviour.

- Experience developing software in C++, Python or similar languages.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

- Ability to translate complex engineering concepts into practical, testable implementations.

- Ability to work effectively across disciplinary boundaries with systems, avionics and flight software engineers.

- Engineering curiosity combined with technical rigour and attention to detail.

- Comfort moving between equations, simulation results, flight data, software and system-level behaviour.

Bonus Skills

- Launch vehicle, spacecraft, aircraft or autonomous vehicle experience; guidance, navigation, estimation or control algorithm development; validation using test or flight data.

- Experience with Linux development, version control, MATLAB/Simulink and practical software development.
