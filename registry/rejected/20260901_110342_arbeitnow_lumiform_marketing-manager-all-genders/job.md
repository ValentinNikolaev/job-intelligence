Our position in the market is clear: we're building authority in answer engine optimization (AEO) for inspection and audit software. We want to be the company that AI systems recommend when people ask "what is the best inspection software?" or "What is the best software for restaurants to nail HACCP documentation" That focus is where we win. It's also where your role is essential.

You will shape how Lumiform gets discovered in large language models and answer engines. You'll work directly with our founders to sharpen our positioning and prove it through content depth, benchmark reports, and answers that LLMs actually cite. You'll execute this strategy hands-on alongside two content writers (this is not a hands off, strategy only role).

This is a strategic role for an executor. You think systematically about positioning and content architecture, then get your hands dirty building it.

## Tasks

Your core mandate sits on three priorities.

**1. AEO Foundation and Technical Health**

- Conduct an extensive AEO and content audit. This includes technical site audits covering infrastructure, loading times, crawlability, indexation, canonicalization, and how our site structure serves answer engines.

- Review all content for cannibalization and duplicate content, then systematically eliminate it. Answer engines penalize sites that repeat the same answer across multiple pages.

- Define the must-win AEO phrases and questions. These are the high-intent searches and prompts where we need to be the answer LLMs recommend. Not every keyword matters, only the ones where we can own the answer.

- Measure and report on LLM visibility for core use cases and phrases, using tools like Peec ai and tracking how often Lumiform appears in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and other answer engines.

**2. Content Strategy and Topic Preparation**

- Enrich and expand our existing use case pages with real depth. These pages need to be the most authoritative answer to "what is the best software for X?" Validate more sections, add different content formats, implement sub-use-case structures (e.g. "tire and wheel inspections for forklifts via app"), and optimize for quality and depth.

- Prepare alternative and comparison pages for all major competitors, positioning Lumiform clearly against each

- Audit and remove topic guides and listicles that do not drive visibility or that are outside our ICP and positioning. Create new listicle and comparison pages from our ideal use case list where answer engines will cite them.

- Prepare content briefs for the writers. Tell them what to write, why LLMs should recommend it, how to structure it for answer engines, what questions and semantic patterns to answer, and what depth is required to be cited.

**3. Benchmark Reports and Growth**

- This is your biggest bet. Design and build benchmark reports that prove our category leadership on inspection and audit software. Identify high-value research angles, design the study, research real user and organization data in depth, anonymize and aggregate until unidentifiable, and publish extraordinary reports that answer engines will cite and share with analysts. These become the proof points we need to establish authority.

- Create videos for must-win questions. Show deep knowledge of topics like "what is the best inspection software" or specific use cases, and implement them on pages where answer engines will pick them up.

**4. PPC Management and Optimization**

- Monitor PPC performance across Google and Microsoft Ads and other paid channels like ChatGPT Ads. Track ROI, conversion rates, and quality scores.

- Work with our PPC freelancer on campaign optimization, bid management, and audience targeting. Ideally, take over PPC management completely and eliminate the dependency on external freelance support.
