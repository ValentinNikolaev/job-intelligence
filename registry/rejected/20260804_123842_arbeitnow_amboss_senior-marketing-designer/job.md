## **About AMBOSS**

AMBOSS is the copilot for medical professionals, empowering them to provide the best possible care through our learning and clinical decision support tool. Our team brings together care, innovative thinking, and cutting-edge AI medical technology. Our founders launched AMBOSS in 2012 with the goal of creating a tool they wished they had during medical school and clinical practice. Since then, we've grown to over 600 employees and are active in over 180 countries - primarily in Germany and the US. Our offices are located in Berlin, Cologne, New York, Cagliari and Cape Town.

Want to learn more about our culture? Watch our[ video](https://youtu.be/gTPDn5XpeJg) to explore what makes us unique

## Why can this position be exciting for you?

As a **Senior Marketing Designer** at AMBOSS, you’ll play a key role in shaping how healthcare professionals around the world experience our brand. You’ll transform complex medical and educational concepts into clear, impactful visuals that drive user engagement and trust. Your work will directly influence how students, doctors, and partners connect with AMBOSS, helping us grow globally while staying true to our mission of improving medical education and patient care. This is an opportunity to put your design skills to work on projects that make a real difference.

## **You will:**

-

**Own and evolve AMBOSS’s marketing design language** across campaigns, channels, and markets, ensuring consistency and excellence in visual storytelling.

-

**Design and deliver high-impact marketing assets** (e.g., campaign concepts, landing pages, ads, social content, event materials, sales enablement tools, printed collaterals) that inspire and convert.

-

**Translate complex medical and educational concepts** into accessible, engaging visuals that resonate with students, physicians, and partners worldwide.

-

**Collaborate closely with Marketing, Product Design, and Markets** to align creative work with strategy, messaging, and user needs.

-

**Lead creative direction on key projects,** mentoring junior designers and setting quality standards within the marketing design team.

-

**Continuously experiment and improve,** contributing ideas for new formats, storytelling approaches, and creative strategies, workflow optimisation that strengthen the AMBOSS brand.

**Strong understanding of branding and storytelling,** with the ability to adapt visuals to different audiences and markets.

**Ability to simplify complexity** — translating technical or educational topics into engaging, clear, user-centered design.

**Project ownership and leadership skills** — able to drive projects independently, set quality standards, and mentor junior designers.
