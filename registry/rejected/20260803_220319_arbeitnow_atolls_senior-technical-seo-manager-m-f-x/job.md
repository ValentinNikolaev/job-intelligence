### **Responsibilities:**

In this role, you will:

- Conduct technical audits, crawl/log-file analyses, and manage indexation (noindex, canonicals, robots.txt) to resolve issues on a platform with large URL volumes.

- Strategically advance our Entity Optimization and Semantic Search capabilities through structured data (Schema.org) and internal linking to build a machine-readable Knowledge Graph.

- Drive the technical readiness of the platform for generative search engines (GEO) and AI-powered answers (AIO), including managing AI crawlers.

- Build and automate internal tools, apps, and monitoring workflows using an AI-first approach with tools like Claude, Lovable, v0, Cursor, and n8n.

- Establish workflows to measure and analyze our visibility within AI answers and translate SEO goals into actionable technical requirements for Product & Tech teams.

### ** ****Your Profile:**

We’re looking for someone with deep experience in technical SEO for large-scale websites, who has a proven ability to leverage AI and automation to create scalable systems.

- Proven experience working with Screaming Frog, GSC, Ahrefs/SISTRIX/SEMrush; HTML/CSS, JS rendering, Schema.org;

- Deep understanding of entity-based search and Knowledge Graphs; AI-first building with LLMs (Claude, Lovable, etc.).

- Nice to have: Dashboarding, database experience, programmatic SEO, and experience building agent/tooling infrastructures (n8n).

- Experience isolating platform-wide patterns and algorithmic anomalies across all markets, distinguishing business impact from digital noise.

- Systematically dissects root causes when algorithms change, finding creative workarounds where standard tools reach their limits.

- Experience translating highly complex technical SEO dependencies into clear business value for Product Owners and developers.

- Independently identifies and prioritizes high-impact actions and documents workflows to ensure team resilience.

- Strong communication skills, both written and spoken in English; any other language is a plus
