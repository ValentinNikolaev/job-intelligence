**The** **AI Teams at Agile Robots** are looking for a (**Senior) Full-Stack Robotics Software Engineer (m/f/d)**, who will build and maintain the software stack that runs vision-language-action (VLA) models on humanoid and industrial robots. You will help make sure VLA models run reliably once they reach these robots.

### **Responsibilities**

- **VLA Integration:** Integrate VLA models into the robots' software stack to drive manipulation and task execution on humanoid and industrial platforms.

- **Software Architecture:** Design and implement software components and architectures for AI-enhanced robotics applications.

- **Platform Integration:** Develop applications that keep working across a changing set of robots and sensors.

- **Stack Maintenance:** Review and maintain the shared software stack used across humanoid and close-to-industry robot projects.

- **Research Integration:** Track new developments in robotics and AI software, and bring promising external components into the stack alongside in-house development.

### **Essential Skills**

- **Background:** Degree in robotics, computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, or a related field, or equivalent qualification, together with around four years building software for robotic systems.

- **VLA Models:** Hands-on experience building or integrating vision-language-action models on physical robots.

- **Core Stack:** Proficiency in C++, CMake, Python, shell scripting, and Git for building and maintaining production robotics software.

- **ROS 2:** Experience building robot software on ROS 2.

### **Beneficial Skills**

- **Robot Platforms:** Experience working with dual-arm or humanoid robot platforms.

- **Computer Vision:** Working knowledge of computer vision and robot control methods.
