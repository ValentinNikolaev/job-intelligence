**About the Project**
The project is a cloud-based property operations platform designed for the housing sector. It connects housing providers, property managers, residents, contractors and service providers within a single ecosystem. The platform helps automate repairs, maintenance, compliance, contractor coordination and resident communication while providing real-time reporting and analytics. It also integrates with existing property management systems to improve service delivery, reduce operational costs and streamline day-to-day workflows.

- 1+ year of commercial experience with Vue.js
- Upper-Intermediate (B2+) English
