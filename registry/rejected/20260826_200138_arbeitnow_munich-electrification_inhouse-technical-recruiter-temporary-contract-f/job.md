Munich Electrification is an innovative company founded with the aim of accelerating the transition to electric and connected mobility. Our international team is composed of highly motivated and exceptional engineers. As a small and specialized team we are well-aware of the importance of each individual colleague and support and promote each employee according to their abilities and needs. **Your role**

We are looking for an Inhouse Technical Recruiter (f/m/d) to join our People team** for 12 months**. You will identify and attract the best talent for **technical/engineering roles**, as well as consistently build up a talent pool. You will support our hiring managers in finding innovative ways to improve our recruitment process, and develop sourcing strategies that best meet our needs. You will represent Munich Electrification authentically on relevant platforms, using your creative communication skills to attract and engage the most talented candidates.

**Your future tasks at a glance**

-

Manage and execute the** full recruitment cycle **(sourcing, arranging interviews, CV screening, interviewing…) for a variety of roles and departments (particularly **hardware and software development**).

-

Be** on site **in the office at least **three times a week** for final interviews, to organize the setup and welcome candidates.

-

Continuously **work together with the Hiring Managers and Department Leads **to understand our recruitment goals and define the ideal candidate profile.

-

Independently develop and execute the sourcing strategy (market mapping, search criteria, etc.) for our roles.

-

**Active sourcing **on a daily basis to identify and attract passive suitable candidates, inspire them and gain their interest in our roles and company.

-

Consistently **promote our roles **on relevant social media channels (and other employer branding activities).

-

Build a **strong network of qualified engineers** in the fields of electronics, e-mobility and software development.
