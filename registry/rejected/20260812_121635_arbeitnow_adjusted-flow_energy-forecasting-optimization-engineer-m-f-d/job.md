## Aufgaben

You will work on the forecasting and optimization core of EOIS for industrial energy optimization. The role combines deterministic and probabilistic forecasting, uncertainty modeling, mathematical optimization, simulation, and production integration for live industrial systems.

- Develop and improve time-series forecasting models for industrial electricity consumption, PV generation, and other energy-relevant signals

- Extend and improve forecast services with probabilistic outputs

- Develop and improve optimization models for industrial energy flexibility, production-related decisions, storage systems, and other controllable assets

- Evaluate forecasting and optimization quality on noisy, incomplete, and non-stationary industrial data, including their actual operational and economic impact

- Design how forecasts, uncertainty measures, physical constraints, and system states are used in downstream optimization workflows

- Build and improve simulation, replay, benchmarking, and validation workflows to test model behavior before and alongside deployment in live systems

- Improve robustness through plausibility checks, fallback behavior, re-forecasting, and handling of low-confidence or missing data

- Support validation and technical interpretation of model behavior in real customer environments
