**Mission Brief**

As part of our HSE team, you will support the collection, management, and preparation of environmental data required for Life Cycle Assessments (LCA). Your primary responsibility will be coordinating data collection across different departments, ensuring data quality and consistency, and preparing datasets for integration into openLCA. Through your work, you will help establish the environmental data foundation needed for current and future LCA activities.

Your Role in Our Space Mission:

- **Environmental Data Collection:** Collect environmental, operational, and material-related data from various departments across the company. Coordinate and follow up on data requests with Engineering, Procurement, Manufacturing, Facilities, and Supply Chain teams. Support the establishment of efficient environmental data collection processes.

- **Data Validation & Preparation:** Review, validate, and organize collected datasets. Ensure data consistency, completeness, and traceability. Structure environmental datasets according to LCA requirements.

- **Life Cycle Assessment Data Management:** Prepare and transfer environmental data into openLCA. Maintain and update LCA-related databases and datasets. Support the creation and maintenance of lifecycle inventory data. Assist in collecting and organizing background and supplier-related environmental information.

**Qualification Checklist **

- You are currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program.

- Strong interest in working with data and structured datasets.

- Highly organized, reliable, and detail-oriented working style.

- Ability to independently drive data collection activities and proactively engage with internal stakeholders.

- Strong communication skills and confidence when interacting with different departments.

- Fluent in English and German.

Bonus Skills

- Experience handling large or complex datasets is a plus.

- Interest in sustainability and environmental topics is an advantage.

- Previous exposure to Life Cycle Assessments would be beneficial.

- Knowledge of openLCA or similar software is highly appreciated.

- Experience in the aerospace sector is a plus.
