### What we actually do

We run and maintain WooCommerce stores and everything sitting behind them: ERP, CRM, warehouse and accounting integrations, order and stock sync, custom admin tooling. Some of it we inherited. Some of it we built. Right now we’re moving store backends off WooCommerce/PHP onto **NestJS**, service by service, without taking anyone’s shop offline.

It’s real, messy, revenue-carrying infrastructure. Not a greenfield playground, and not a body-shop ticket queue either.

### The role

We’re looking for one or two people who are somewhere between a very strong senior and a fractional CTO. Concretely, that means you can:

- **Read a broken system and explain it.** To another engineer, and to a client’s finance director, in the language each of them needs.
- **Own scope.** Break vague requests into estimable pieces, give a number, and defend it when reality pushes back.
- **Lead people.** Review code, mentor mid-level devs, run a small team without turning into a bottleneck.
- **Decide.** When to patch the PHP, when to strangle it out into a Nest service, when to leave it alone because the ROI isn’t there.

### What you’ll work on

- Maintaining and extending production WooCommerce stores (plugins, hooks, custom order flows, performance)
- Designing and building NestJS services that progressively replace Woo backend functionality
- Integrations with ERP, CRM, payment, logistics and accounting systems — REST, SOAP, webhooks, queues, whatever the vendor forces on us
- Data migration and dual-run strategies where old and new systems have to agree for a while
- Technical discovery with clients: audits, estimates, roadmaps.

### What we need from you

- **7+ years** building and running production web systems
- **Deep PHP** — WooCommerce/WordPress internals specifically, not just “I’ve used Laravel”
- **Deep Node/TypeScript** — NestJS in production, module architecture, queues, testing
- MySQL/PostgreSQL performance work: you can read a slow query log and know what to do next
- Comfort with legacy: you’ve untangled someone else’s code without rewriting everything out of spite
- **You work with AI tooling daily** — Claude / Claude Code, Cursor, or equivalent — as a real part of your workflow, not a demo. You know what to delegate, how to review what comes back, and where it will confidently lie to you.
