**What you’ll be doing:**

-

Build a solid understanding of Hospitality's business processes, customers, and data so you can answer questions accurately and with the right context.

-

Perform analyses such as segmentation, customer health checks, and impact measurement to help teams understand how the business is performing.

-

Write SQL queries and build datasets to answer stakeholder questions, pulling from multiple data sources as needed.

-

Build and maintain dashboards and reports that make it easy for business partners to track KPIs and spot trends.

-

Work with stakeholders to understand what they're really asking, turning loosely defined questions into a clear analysis plan, with support from senior colleagues on trickier requests.

-

Present findings clearly, with visualizations and a short narrative that explains what the data shows and why it matters.

-

Help spot and investigate data quality issues, documenting what you find and flagging root causes to the right people.

**And a lttle bit of:**

-

Stay up to date with developments, innovations, and trends in data, including state-of-the-art machine learning models and large language models (LLMs).

-

Contributing as part of the wider team to achieve organisational objectives even if this means doing things that aren’t strictly within the scope of your role.

**What you need to bring:**

-

2–3+ years of experience as a Data Analyst or in a similar analytics role.

-

Solid SQL skills, comfortable querying and joining data across multiple sources.

-

Experience with Python for data analysis is a plus.

-

Experience building dashboards or reports using Looker or a similar BI tool.

-

Exposure to Hospitality, Finance, Accounting, Revenue Operations, Sales, or another operational business environment is a plus.

-

Comfortable turning a business question into an analysis plan, and asking clarifying questions when the ask isn't fully defined.

-

Careful with data accuracy, with a good eye for spotting things that look off.

-

Strong verbal and written communication skills, able to explain findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

-

Curious, organized, and a self-starter, comfortable in a fast-paced environment.

-

Familiarity with GCP, BigQuery, Dataform, GitHub, or Salesforce is a plus.

-

Familiarity with Segment, Heap type of tools
