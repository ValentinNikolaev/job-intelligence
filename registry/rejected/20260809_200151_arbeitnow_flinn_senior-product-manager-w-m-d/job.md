As Senior Product Manager in the Regulatory Group at Flinn, you'll be owning a product at a major inflection point. It has a deep data foundation, a task-based workflow that saves regulatory experts time, and a growing base of enterprise customers. Your job is to take our regulatory product from early traction to enterprise-ready: benchmarking what's working, closing workflow gaps that matter to highly specialized users, and finding the improvement vectors that could change how regulatory teams work. The path there is earned through sharp prioritization, close customer proximity, and the ability to turn complex problems into concrete steps.
