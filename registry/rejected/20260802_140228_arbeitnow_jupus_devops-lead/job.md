We’re seeking a proactive DevOps Lead to take ownership of the infrastructure and platform strategy behind one of the fastest-growing AI legal tech platforms in Europe.

This role focuses on scaling and professionalizing our infrastructure across cloud, CI/CD, Kubernetes, observability, and security as we continue to grow rapidly year over year.

For someone who thrives in highly autonomous environments and enjoys combining technical depth with leadership, this is an opportunity to shape the future of infrastructure at JUPUS from the ground up.

# Your Challenge and Opportunity in This Role

As our DevOps Lead, you’ll own the infrastructure roadmap and help evolve our platform from a strong technical foundation into a highly scalable, reliable, and proactive engineering environment.

You’ll lead initiatives across Kubernetes migration, CI/CD modernization, observability, infrastructure automation, and cloud scalability while coaching developers and helping establish operational best practices across the company.

Expect broad ownership, fast decision-making, and visible impact on how JUPUS scales its platform and engineering organization.
