**Could your legal expertise help build the contractual foundations of Europe’s next financial management platform?**

At Pennylane, we believe legal should be more than a safeguard. It should be a business enabler.

Our ambition is to equip millions of small and medium-sized businesses across Europe with a complete, high-performing financial management platform. As Pennylane continues to grow rapidly in France and expand internationally, we are looking for a **Senior Legal Counsel, Contracts** to join our Legal, Compliance & Risk team.

Within a Legal department of 10 professionals, the Contracts team secures Pennylane’s commercial and operational relationships with customers, suppliers, partner firms, resellers, integrators and strategic partners.

Reporting to the **Lead Legal Counsel, Contracts**, you will work closely with our Sales, Partnerships, Security, Finance and Product teams. This is a hands-on role where legal rigor, creativity and business pragmatism are equally valued.

## **🎯 Your role**

You will act as a trusted legal partner for teams across Pennylane, managing a broad range of commercial and operational agreements from drafting through to signature and post-signature follow-up.

You will also help make our contracting processes more efficient and scalable, contributing to the tools, templates, playbooks and legal culture that support Pennylane’s growth.

## **📋 Your responsibilities**

**International contracts**

-

Draft and negotiate purchase orders and related agreements with partner firms, primarily outside France, with a current focus on Germany.

-

Manage contracts with strategic international suppliers.

-

Maintain and improve templates in line with regulatory, product and business developments.

-

Work with Sales and Partnerships to streamline the contracting process.

**Customer, reseller and integrator agreements**

-

Improve customer terms and conditions for SME customers across France and Europe.

-

Support Sales with the contracting process and automated quotations.

-

Review and negotiate agreements with Pennylane integrators and resellers.

-

Anticipate the contractual impact of key regulations, including e-invoicing, payment services and the EU AI Act.

-

Contribute to contract templates and playbooks.

**Legal operations and collaboration**

-

Structure and improve internal contracting processes to increase efficiency and scalability.

-

Optimize our contract management tools, including Tomorro, Pivot and Notion.

-

Identify opportunities to automate repetitive, low-value tasks, including through AI.

-

Provide clear, pragmatic advice to Sales, Partnerships, Finance, Security and Product teams.

-

Train non-legal teams and help build a strong legal culture across Pennylane.

## **🙋 The profile we are looking for**

-

You are a qualified lawyer or legal counsel with **extensive experience** in contract law, gained in a law firm, particularly in IP/IT, technology or SaaS, and/or in a fast-growing tech company.

-

You are fluent in English and have a working knowledge of Franch and German.

-

You have strong expertise in **B2B SaaS agreements**, including IP, indemnification, liability caps, SLAs and data-related provisions.

-

You are autonomous and comfortable managing matters from start to finish.

-

You have experience working in a multi-jurisdictional environment, particularly across France and Europe.

-

You are interested in legal operations and technology, and have experience structuring processes or optimizing contract management tools.

-

You are a genuine **business partner**, able to help teams move quickly while protecting the company.

-

You are rigorous, concise and able to make complex legal topics clear to non-legal audiences.

-

You enjoy building in a fast-moving environment where ambiguity, prioritization and rapid iteration are part of the job.
