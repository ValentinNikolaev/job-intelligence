**The Role**

We’re looking for a Working Student - Talent Acquisition & Office Support (f/m/d) to join our team in Berlin. In this role, you’ll support our day-to-day recruitment activities while also helping to keep our Berlin office organised and running smoothly.

You’ll work closely with our Talent Acquisition and wider People & Culture team, gaining practical experience across recruitment and office coordination. You should be available to work from our Berlin office at least two days per week.

**To join our team, you should be excited to**

-

Support the end-to-end recruitment process, including drafting and posting job advertisements, screening applications, coordinating interviews, and providing timely updates to candidates.

-

Support candidate sourcing through LinkedIn, job boards, and university networks, gradually taking ownership of sourcing and interviews for working-student positions across different departments.

-

Maintain accurate candidate information in our applicant tracking system and recruitment trackers.

-

Support onboarding activities by preparing documents and coordinating first-day logistics.

-

Work closely with recruiters and other members of the People & Culture team on ongoing and ad hoc tasks.

-

Support the day-to-day operations of our Berlin office, including organising office supplies, equipment, shared spaces, deliveries, and maintenance.

-

Coordinate with external vendors and service providers when needed.

**Your Qualifications**

-

You are currently enrolled at a university and are interested in gaining practical experience in Talent Acquisition, Human Resources, or office coordination.

-

You are reliable and available to work onsite at our Berlin office at least two days per week.

-

You are organised and able to manage your time and priorities effectively.

-

You can work independently once you have been briefed and are comfortable taking ownership of assigned tasks.

-

You have strong communication and collaboration skills and enjoy working as part of a team.

-

Previous experience in recruitment, HR, administration, or office support is helpful but not required.

-

You are comfortable using Microsoft Office tools such as Outlook, Excel, and PowerPoint.

-

Previous experience with an applicant tracking system or other recruitment tools would be a plus.
