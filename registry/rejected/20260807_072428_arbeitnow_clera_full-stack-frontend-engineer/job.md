### About the Role

We're a **Series A AI-powered go-to-market SaaS company** building intelligent infrastructure for B2B sales and marketing teams. Our platform uses AI agents to help GTM teams find, qualify, and engage their best-fit accounts — and we're looking for a **Full Stack Frontend Engineer** to lead the development of the high-performance, elegant user experiences that bring those capabilities to life.

You'll work closely with product, design, and AI research teams — reporting to the Director of ML & Platform Engineering — to translate ambitious ideas into polished, scalable interfaces. This role sits at the intersection of AI and UX, and is central to how users interact with intelligent systems every day.

### What You'll Do

-

Own end-to-end development of highly interactive, performant, and elegant frontend experiences for AI-powered web applications, from concept to production.

-

Collaborate with product and design teams to translate ambiguous ideas into polished, user-friendly interfaces.

-

Make thoughtful trade-offs between functionality, polish, and delivery speed — shipping features fast to validate user value quickly.

-

Set the standard for clean, maintainable, and scalable frontend architecture.

-

Integrate AI capabilities into seamless, human-centered user interactions.

-

Implement analytics and feedback loops to measure impact and guide iteration.

-

Optimize performance and reliability through strong CI/CD practices and frontend testing frameworks.

-

Evolve the design system and component library to ensure consistency, scalability, and rapid product development.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

2+ years of frontend development experience with a proven track record delivering production-grade web applications.

-

Hands-on experience with **React, TypeScript, Next.js, TailwindCSS, Node.js, and Python**.

-

Experience leading frontend projects or mentoring junior engineers.

-

Strong collaboration skills across product, design, and AI/ML research teams.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Background in B2B SaaS, sales tech, or data-intensive web applications.

-

Experience integrating AI/ML features into consumer-facing interfaces.

-

Familiarity with design systems and component library development.
