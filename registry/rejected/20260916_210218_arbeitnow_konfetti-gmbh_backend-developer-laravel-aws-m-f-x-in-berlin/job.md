**Your next step → konfetti!**

## Tasks

- You **own backend features end to end**, from initial analysis through deployment and monitoring, including our partner-facing booking software (Backoffice)

- You **shape our architecture**: you identify what is holding the product back, propose a direction, and then build it

- You work across service boundaries, data models, queues, integrations, and performance, and you **improve monitoring and address technical debt** without being asked

- You use **AI tooling as a core part of how you work**, including on parts of the stack outside your specialization

- You think critically about requirements and **identify edge cases early**, drawing on your experience of what can go wrong in production

- You **actively collaborate** with our tight-knit team of engineers, designer, and product lead in daily standups, planning, and technical discussions: asking questions when something is unclear, sharing blockers early, and contributing your perspective

## Requirements

- Several years of production backend experience, ideally with PHP/Laravel, and **a track record of systems you built** that other people still run

- **Strong opinions about architecture**, backed by reasons and by having lived with the consequences of your past decisions

- Solid experience with **AWS services** such as Lambda, SQS, EC2, ECS/Fargate, CloudFront, and Bedrock, and comfort owning what you ship in production

- **Real fluency with AI-assisted development**. We use Claude Code day to day and can also offer Cursor. We want someone who already works this way and can push the rest of the team forward

- A **maker mindset and a can-do attitude**: you would rather build the thing than write a document about building the thing

- The **confidence to challenge how things are done today**, the respect to understand why they were done that way first, and the honesty to say when you do not know something
