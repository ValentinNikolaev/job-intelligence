**About us**

Since 2000, [ITA Logistics Group](https://www.italogistics.com/) has been making shipping simpler, more transparent, and more efficient for businesses. With our platforms [LetMeShip](https://www.letmeship.com/) and [ParcelParcel](https://www.parcelparcel.com/nl_EN/en-home/), companies can quickly compare and directly book their daily shipping needs. By bringing together a wide range of carriers on our platforms, we combine modern technology with personal service.

Across several locations in Europe, our teams work every day to simplify shipping processes in a sustainable way. We believe in open collaboration and fast decision-making. That’s why we are looking for people who take ownership, contribute ideas, and want to actively shape the future with us.

**Your mission**

As our (Technical) Product Owner (m/f/d), you will be part of our Product Team and take ownership of key technical interfaces and integrations across our platforms. Your main focus will be on our Customer APIs, the Unified Carrier Interface (UCI), and, going forward, the Unified Financial Platform (UFP) as well as our HubSpot integration.

You will work at the intersection of Product, Engineering, and Business. You understand technical dependencies, translate different requirements into clear priorities, and work closely with our teams to ensure that our integrations are reliable and continuously evolving.

You will collaborate closely with our Product Owners, Technical Business Analyst, Software Engineers, and Data Insights team while also acting as an important point of contact for internal stakeholders, customers, carriers, and external partners.

**Your Key Responsibilities**

-

You own and continuously develop the roadmap for our Customer APIs across LMS Enterprise and LMS Next.

-

You drive the migration of our API customers from LMS Enterprise to LMS Next, identifying and prioritizing the features and carrier integrations required to make the transition successful.

-

You act as a technical point of contact for our API customers, supporting them with questions, troubleshooting, and technical challenges.

-

You take ownership of the development of our Unified Carrier Interface (UCI) in close collaboration with internal and external stakeholders.

-

You analyze requirements for carrier features and services, translate them into clear requirements for Engineering, and support QA in defining relevant test cases.

-

You communicate directly with our carriers to clarify technical requirements and integration specifics.

-

Once live, you take ownership of our Unified Financial Platform (UFP) and manage its backlog, requirements, and further development.

-

You continuously monitor our existing HubSpot integration and prioritize adjustments based on evolving business needs and feedback.

-

Across all these areas, you connect Engineering, business stakeholders, carriers, and external partners, ensuring clear requirements, priorities, and decisions.
