**About the Role: **

We are seeking a versatile Senior Accountant to serve as a key bridge between our newly established Berlin manufacturing facility and our US financial operations. In this dual-impact role, you will lead the cost accounting workflows and local compliance for our German entity (GmbH) while simultaneously supporting global cost accounting tasks, cross-border subsidiary reconciliations, and capital expenditure tracking. You will partner with operational leaders to establish scalable standard costing methodologies, maintain inventory integrity across multiple locations, and ensure seamless financial alignment between EMEA and US teams. Ideal candidates are detail-oriented professionals with background in multi-entity global structures who thrive in cross-functional environments.

This is a full-time, hybrid role which will require you to work from our Berlin office 3 days per week.

**Impact You’ll Own:**

- Partners with Berlin Operations to establish scalable cost accounting workflows, standard costing methodologies, and manufacturing time-tracking for our local production line.

- Leads monthly close processes for GmbH inventory, including raw materials, WIP, and finished goods reconciliations, while overseeing physical cycle counts and analyzing adjustments.

- Ensures local accounting activities comply with German GAAP (HGB), prepares cost breakdowns for German R&D tax incentives (e.g., *Forschungszulage*), and manages financial documentation for local government grants and subsidies.

- Manages PCard reconciliations for lab equipment and flight materials while reviewing capital expenditure vs. operational expenses (CIP/Expense classifications) for the German entity.

- Serves as the dedicated backup for US cost accountants during time-off, high-volume periods, and initial US-hours coverage through cross-training on US month-end workflows.

- Reviews and clears consolidated transfer orders between international subsidiaries, managing movements between locations for both PBC and GmbH entities.

- Maintains costs associated with satellite materials, launch, and build processes while tracking movements across inventory, WIP, and internal fixed asset materials.

- Assists in labor capitalization tracking across internal software development, satellite construction, and commissioning efforts.

- Performs purchase price variance (PPV), production variance, scrap, and Excess & Obsolete (E&O) analysis while preparing balance sheet reconciliations, journal entries, and accrued purchases/prepaid manufacturing schedules for US and EMEA operations.

- Supports internal and external audits across US GAAP, local German GAAP, and SOX controls.

**What You Bring:**

- 4 to 6 years of relevant cost accounting experience in a manufacturing or high-tech operational environment.

- Experience with inventory controls and multi-entity global structures.

- Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or Business Administration (or a relevant German *Ausbildung*, e.g., *Industriekaufmann/-frau*, with specialized focus on accounting/controlling).

- Proficiency with modern ERP systems and advanced capability in Excel or Google Sheets.

- Professional fluency in both English and German.

**What Makes You Stand Out:**

- Working knowledge of both US GAAP and German GAAP (HGB).

- Direct hands-on experience with NetSuite ERP.
