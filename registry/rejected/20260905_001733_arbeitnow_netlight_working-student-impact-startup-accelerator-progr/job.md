### Your Role

Our Impact & Startup Accelerator Program connects consultants who are currently between client engagements to two kinds of work: pro-bono/impact projects for NGOs and social ventures, and subsidized consulting projects for early-stage startups. You'll join us in help building the operational backbone that lets the program scale.

As a working student, you will own the operational engine of the program: tracking, documentation, first-pass triage, and dashboard upkeep.

Your responsibilities will include:

-

Building and maintaining a structured overview of incoming client requests and available consultants, and orchestrating an initial matching process

-

Ownership for tracking key program data (capacity status, commitments, deadlines) and flagging anything that requires action or attention

-

Supporting communication with stakeholders such as preparing briefing materials, status updates, notetaking and taking part in meetings.

-

Maintaining a dashboard of program metrics and compiling regular reporting

-

Documenting and continuously improving how the program runs, as processes are still being defined — an interest in AI-driven automation is a plus
