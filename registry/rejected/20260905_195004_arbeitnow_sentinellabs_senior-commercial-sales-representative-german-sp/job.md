### What Will You Do?

Primary responsibilities include:

- Own the full sales cycle from prospecting through to close, targeting accounts across Germany, qualifying opportunities, negotiating with key decision-makers, and delivering results through disciplined forecasting, pipeline management, and proactive account planning.

- Build and manage a high-performing pipeline by leveraging multiple lead sources, outbound strategies, and channel partnerships, while collaborating with field sales teams and cross-functional stakeholders to amplify reach and accelerate deal velocity.

- Act as a trusted advisor to customers by developing deep knowledge of SentinelOne's solutions and the broader cybersecurity landscape, delivering tailored, value-driven conversations that address each client's specific needs and challenges.

- Stay ahead of industry trends and competitive dynamics to continuously refine your consultative sales approach, and provide timely and accurate forecasts, insights, and strategic feedback to sales leadership.

### What Skills and Knowledge Will You Bring?

Ideal candidates will have:

- Fluency in both English and German, with excellent written and verbal communication skills and exceptional presentation, storytelling, and objection-handling skills that build trust and inspire customers.

- At least 2 years of direct sales experience at a software vendor, with a proven record of closing net new logos, driving territory growth, and consistently exceeding targets in the commercial segment.

- Experience selling into commercial organizations, with a background in working with or through channel partners to maximize collaboration and reach.

- The ability to manage complex sales cycles from initial qualification through to executive-level negotiations, with comfort operating in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

- Prior cybersecurity experience is a strong advantage, combined with a results-driven, competitive, and self-motivated mindset and a Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience.
