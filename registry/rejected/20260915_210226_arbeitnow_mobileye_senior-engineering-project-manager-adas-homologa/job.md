We are looking for a **Senior Technical Project Manager** to lead the homologation of Advanced Driver‑Assistance and semi‑autonomous driving systems in Europe and China.
You will work at the intersection of **engineering, regulation, and customer engagement**, ensuring our **L2++ safety and comfort features** meet regulatory, safety, and customer expectations on the path to mass production.

**How your job will look like:**

- Initiate, plan, and execute semi‑autonomous vehicle programs for **globally known OEMs**

- Own the **end‑to‑end homologation** of ADAS and automated driving systems (EU & China), including **UNECE regulations, Euro NCAP, and type approval**

- Act as the **single point of contact** for homologation affairs across engineering and commercial domains, enabling fast decision‑making and alignment

- Manage type approval **roadmap, timeline, integration, delivery, and monitoring** from concept to SOP

- Translate regulatory and customer requirements into execution by leading **technical workshops, test drives, system tuning, and validation activities** for driving and safety functions

- Coordinate with OEMs, technical services, and authorities throughout certification and approval phases

- Drive resolution of **internal and external escalations**, reducing delivery risk and maintaining customer confidence

**All you need is:**

- Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Computer Science, Electrical, Mechanical, or similar)

- 7+ years of experience in technical project or program management (automotive or high‑tech)

- Strong understanding of **ADAS and automated driving development lifecycles**

- Solid knowledge of **European regulations (UNECE)**; Chinese standards are a strong plus

- Experience leading multi‑site, cross‑disciplinary teams and working directly with OEMs or certification bodies

- Strong organizational, communication, and problem‑solving skills

- Fluent in **English** (German is a plus)

- Willingness to travel within Europe and to China
