**Who we need**

You’re a movie buff with strong opinions, and you can’t help but dig into *why* something is getting attention. Now imagine turning that instinct into global media coverage.

At JustWatch, we’re looking for someone with a sharp, data-driven mindset; someone who understands (or is eager to learn) **technical SEO, digital PR, and how content earns attention at scale**. You’re not just creative; you know how to back ideas with data, spot patterns, and turn insights into campaigns that land in top-tier publications and generate high-quality backlinks**.**

You have a strong gut feeling for what works, but you don’t rely on gut alone. You can read data, extract clear takeaways, and iterate quickly. You’re comfortable sharing ideas, testing them in the real world, and pushing for **high-quality execution over endless perfectionism**.

If you’re excited about building stories that get picked up globally - and helping JustWatch reach **100M monthly organic users** - you’ll fit right in.

We value people who challenge themselves, move fast, and get things done. We follow the 80/20 rule, favor pragmatism over theory, and care about impact above all.

### **Who we are**

JustWatch ([www.justwatch.com](http://www.justwatch.com)) is already the world's largest streaming guide for movies, TV shows & sports, and we aim to become even bigger by reaching 100 million monthly SEO visits with your support.

We deliver data-driven digital marketing for the global entertainment business; from the biggest blockbusters and award-winning shows to major sporting events. We’re entertainment obsessives ourselves, and as owners of the world's largest streaming guide, we have unparalleled data and insights into audience behaviour and content tastes.

**What You Will Be Doing**

-

**Ideate, Execute & Report Digital PR Campaigns**:

-

From infographics to interactive landing pages, you’ll craft campaigns journalists want to share about movies, TV shows, streaming services, and major industry moments

-

You’ll turn data into headlines, identifying trends in our datasets and shaping them into stories that drive coverage

-

React quickly to industry news and major releases; sometimes preparing outreach the same day

-

Research, execute, and report on link-building campaigns with our prospecting team and designers

-

**Build Lasting Relationships**: Grow your own network of journalist contacts; your personal “friends list” that eagerly covers JustWatch’s product updates and data-led stories

-

**Drive Outreach & Partnerships**: Identify and pitch API and content partnerships to leading entertainment publishers, guiding partners from first conversations through to implementation. Work closely with affiliates, support integrations where needed, and collaborate with social and creators to extend coverage across channels

**What We Need from You**

-

**Digital PR expertise:** You have experience in / know how to create and execute a successful digital PR campaign, from ideation and research to drafting, pitching, and securing high-quality coverage and links.

-

**SEO & growth mindset:** You understand how to measure success from an SEO perspective, including link quality, referring domains, and overall organic impact. You think beyond coverage and focus on how PR drives sustainable organic growth.

-

**Data storytelling skills:** You’re comfortable working with Excel/Google Sheets and turning data into compelling stories and clear reports. You can craft pitches that grab attention and resonate with journalists

-

**Strong task & project management:** You’re able to manage multiple campaigns and deadlines at once, communicate timelines clearly, and prioritise effectively.

-

**Hands-on & persistent:** You bring a proactive, execution-focused mindset and stay resilient even when outreach gets challenging. You’re comfortable with digital tools and enjoy working in a fast-paced, online-first environment.

Taking over responsibilities fast with first successes

The opportunity to grow your position at JustWatch
