We are looking for a **Senior Infrastructure Automation / DevSecOps Engineer** to support the further development and automation of an existing vulnerability management platform for a public-sector client in Germany.

The role focuses on **Infrastructure as Code, Linux automation, virtual machine provisioning, API integrations, security, and infrastructure connectivity**.

You will work closely with existing infrastructure and operations teams to further develop the automation environment and ensure reliable, secure, and well-documented infrastructure.

## Tasks

- Take over and validate technical infrastructure specifications

- Further develop and maintain the **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)** environment

- Create and maintain **Linux images and configurations**

- Automate virtual machine provisioning using **Terraform**

- Integrate existing **Vault and credential management** processes

- Develop **REST API integrations and automation scripts**

- Perform network integration and connectivity testing

- Carry out functional, performance, and security testing

- Produce technical documentation and support the handover to operations

## Requirements

- Strong hands-on experience with **Proxmox**

- Strong experience with **Terraform**

- Experience with **Ansible**

- Strong Linux experience, particularly **Ubuntu and/or RHEL**

- Programming and scripting experience with **Python and Bash**

- Experience working with **REST APIs**

- Solid networking knowledge, including:

- VLAN

- Routing

- Multi-NIC environments

- MTU

- Experience with **Git**

- Knowledge of **security hardening, RBAC, and secrets management**

**Nice to Have**

- **Packer**

- PAM and credential management

- ITIL knowledge

- Certifications such as **RHCSA, RHCE, Terraform Associate**, or equivalent
