## **Overview**

We are seeking native-level French speakers with a linguistics background to serve as the primary language resource for voice AI in their language. You will be the trusted point of contact for all questions, quality decisions, and language-specific work related to French.

This is not a standard annotation engagement. You will own your language end to end, which includes managing annotation quality, auditing data, monitoring product performance, investigating customer-reported issues, and executing projects that require deep linguistic expertise and attention to detail. You will operate independently across these workstreams and serve as the team's go-to resource for anything related to your language.

This is an ongoing contractor engagement. Availability during some US Pacific business hours is preferred to allow for team syncs and real-time support.

## **About the Engagement**

You will coordinate with the Human Evals Manager on project deliverables and timelines. Some examples of what your day-to-day work may include:

-

Answer questions from annotators about guidelines, edge cases, or language-specific issues via an agreed project communication channel.

-

Cascade new feedback, instructions, or rubric updates to annotators working on your projects.

-

Listen to annotator-rated audio files and check whether their ratings and written feedback align with the rubric.

-

Prepare written quality assessments for annotators with specific examples and recommendations.

-

Listen to TTS output and provide ground truth assessments of pronunciation, prosody, and naturalness in your language.

-

Update or expand sections of the annotation guidelines when you find gaps or edge cases.

-

Prep and run calibration meetings with annotators.

-

Hold office hours on a mutually agreed schedule during active studies so annotators can ask questions in real time.

-

Develop onboarding materials and resources for new annotators.

-

Write QA reports on annotation quality and calibration outcomes.

-

Review production audio in your language and flag quality issues to the team.

-

Investigate language-specific issues reported by customers and identify root causes.

-

Audit datasets for linguistic accuracy, completeness, and consistency.

## **Commitment**

Roughly 40 hours per week with flexibility in scheduling. Project hours may vary depending on active studies, data projects, and product needs. Participation in periodic coordination meetings during US Pacific hours is expected.

## **Requirements**

-

Native-level proficiency in French as spoken in France.

-

Experience with data annotation, transcription, or language quality assurance.

-

Strong foundation in linguistics or a related field, with the ability to apply concepts like phonetics, prosody, and intonation when evaluating speech and writing annotation guidelines.

-

Strong written English proficiency. All guidelines, feedback, reports, and team communication are in English.

-

Ability to write clear, structured instructions and deliver specific, actionable feedback to annotators.

-

Comfortable working with data and metrics such as inter-rater agreement scores, audit spreadsheets, and structured feedback templates.

-

Self-directed and able to manage feedback cycles, QA reviews, and data projects without close oversight.

## **Preferred**

-

Experience writing or maintaining annotation guidelines or rubrics.

-

Experience training, mentoring, or managing other annotators.

-

Experience with audio evaluation, TTS, or STT.

-

Conversational spoken English proficiency for meetings and calibration syncs.

-

Experience working with distributed, remote teams.
