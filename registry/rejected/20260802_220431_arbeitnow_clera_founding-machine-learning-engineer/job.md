### About the Role

This is a founding-level, hands-on ML engineering role at a fast-growing **Series A AI startup** in the Bay Area, specializing in high-quality training data, post-training data pipelines, and model evaluation for frontier AI labs and enterprises. You'll work closely with leadership to design, train, and ship production-grade ML systems — while helping shape the technical culture and infrastructure from the ground up.

**This role is fully on-site in Mountain View, CA. Candidates must be authorized to work in the US without visa sponsorship.**

### What You'll Do

-

Build and optimize end-to-end ML pipelines, from data ingestion through to production deployment.

-

Implement and fine-tune LLMs, embeddings, and generative models for real-world applications.

-

Develop efficient training and inference systems leveraging distributed compute at scale.

-

Partner with data and product teams to translate ideas into measurable ML impact.

-

Contribute to model monitoring, evaluation, and continual learning frameworks.

-

Establish best practices in model versioning, reproducibility, and scalability across the organization.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

3–10 years of hands-on experience as an ML Engineer, Applied Scientist, or Research Engineer.

-

Proficiency in **Python** and at least one of **PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX**.

-

Proven experience building production-grade, end-to-end ML pipelines (data ingestion, training, deployment).

-

Experience implementing and fine-tuning LLMs, embeddings, and generative models for real-world use cases.

-

Hands-on experience with distributed training/inference and scalable ML systems.

-

Familiarity with cloud platforms (**AWS, GCP, or Azure**) and ML tooling such as **MLflow** or **Weights & Biases**.

-

Strong collaboration skills with cross-functional data and product teams.

-

Bias for action, ability to work autonomously, and eagerness to build from scratch.

**Nice to Have**

-

Experience with RL environments or evaluation frameworks for AI models.

-

Background in agentic systems, tool-use pipelines, or multi-step execution trajectories.

-

Prior experience at an early-stage startup or in a founding-engineer capacity.

-

Equity participation as a founding team member.

-

### Location

**On-site** in Mountain View, California.

No visa sponsorship available — candidates must have existing US work authorization.
