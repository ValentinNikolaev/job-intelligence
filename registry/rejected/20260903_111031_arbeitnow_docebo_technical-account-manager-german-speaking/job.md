## ⚡ The Day-to-Day

-

**Architect the Future:** Translate customer business and eLearning goals into long-term, scalable technical strategies using Docebo’s robust feature set.

-

**Own the Technical Relationship:** Take full technical and product ownership of your assigned enterprise accounts while collaborating closely with Account Teams on commercial growth.

-

**Serve as the Trusted Advisor:** Guide executive and technical stakeholders through platform innovations, API integrations, and AI-assisted workflows to maximize operational efficiency.

-

**Proactively Protect Platform Health:** Monitor technical performance metrics—like API usage and login trends—to catch and mitigate potential risks long before they hit platform stability.

-

**Lead Executive Technical Reviews:** Deliver high-impact performance presentations on SLAs directly to customer leadership and champion customer feedback internally to Docebo teams.

-

**Drive Systemic Platform Improvements:** Partner with Premium Advisors and internal resources to analyze support case patterns, run proof-of-concept pilot projects, and streamline overall platform health.

## 💪 Your Superpowers

-

**Enterprise SaaS Expertise:** Bring 5+ years of SaaS technical troubleshooting and 2+ years of enterprise/large account management experience.

-

**Integration & Auth Mastery:** Hold a solid foundation in back-end practices, RESTful APIs, OAuth 2.0, SAML, and front-end essentials like JavaScript, CSS, and HTML.

-

**Data & Middleware Savvy:** Possess hands-on skills in at least two key areas: iPaaS tools (e.g., Workato, Zapier), SQL queries, or data warehousing/BI tools (e.g., Snowflake, Tableau, Power BI).

-

**Executive Translator:** Master the art of taking complex, highly technical concepts and translating them into simple, high-value business conversations for executive decision-makers.

-

**Global Flexibility & Travel:** Ready to support EMEA regional time zones and comfortable traveling internationally up to 25% of the time.

-

**Project Management Mindset:** Bring proactive ownership, analytical decision-making, and experience forecasting product release impacts or advising on AI/automation integrations.

-

**German language proficiency is required;** however, English is the primary language used for day-to-day communication and collaboration.
