### About Us

Soda's mission is to monitor the world's decisions. We're building the first Data & AI performance monitoring platform that spans data collection to automated decision-making. Soda helps customers find, understand, and fix data quality issues by combining automated post-production monitoring with pre-production pipeline data testing.

Soda was founded in 2019 by Maarten (employee #5 at Collibra) and Tom (creator of multiple open-source projects generating hundreds of millions in value). We commercialize through Soda Cloud and we're well-funded, with investors like Point 9 Capital, Hummingbird, Singular, and Eurazeo.

### The Role

You'll join the Finance team that covers Soda Data NV (Belgium), Soda Data Inc. (US) and NannyML NV, the Belgian company we acquired in 2025. The work is real from week one: invoice processing, month-end reporting, payroll data preparation, expense management, and intercompany recharges across three entities, two currencies and three accounting systems.

The second half of the job is making that work disappear. Finance at Soda builds agentically: Claude and other AI tools are part of the daily workflow, not an experiment on the side. You'll learn a process, run it until you know it cold, and then automate it. Sometimes that means an AI agent on GitHub, sometimes a well-designed formula - you pick the right tool and defend the choice. Accuracy is the bar for both: in finance, 99% right is wrong, so every automation ships with the checks and reconciliations that prove its output.

### What You'll Do

- Run core finance processes: invoice processing, month-end reporting, payroll data preparation, expense management, and intercompany recharges. Learn them, run them, own them.
- Automate them: with Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub and the Soda MCP, or with smart spreadsheet design when that is the better tool.
- Build in the checks: reconciliations and controls in everything you automate, so the output is verified before anyone else has to look at it.
- Document as you go: process documentation that a colleague and an AI agent can both follow.
- Support the wider Finance team: reporting, analysis, and ad-hoc projects across our Belgian and US entities.
