**Your tasks**

**Focus: Technical support and troubleshooting for LLM and AI applications in client projects**

- You support users with technical questions related to LLM and AI applications and handle support requests at L1 and L2 level
- You analyze technical issues in a structured manner, identify potential root causes, resolve them independently where possible, and escalate more complex cases to the appropriate experts
- You communicate clearly and professionally with users in German and English and ensure transparent handling of their support requests
- You work primarily with Langdock and develop a solid understanding of the platform and its underlying LLM and AI functionalities
- You document recurring questions and solutions and contribute to the continuous development of our knowledge base
- You actively contribute insights from day-to-day support to continuously improve our support processes, documentation, and Langdock Support Bot
- You collaborate closely with our technical teams and gain practical insights into the production use of modern LLM and AI solutions for our clients
- You initially support a specific client project and will have the opportunity to apply your knowledge to additional AI support and client projects in the future

**Your profile**

- You are currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Data Science & AI, Business Administration, Economics, Business and Economics, or a comparable field and have a strong interest in technical topics
- You have a solid technical understanding and initial hands-on experience with digital applications, technical tools, or support processes
- You are interested in LLMs and modern AI applications and have already gained practical experience using relevant tools
- Initial experience with Langdock is a plus, but not a requirement
- You approach technical problems systematically, are able to narrow down potential causes, and enjoy independently familiarizing yourself with new topics and tools
- You bring a strong hands-on mentality and take a pragmatic approach to solving problems rather than simply passing them on
- You are able to explain technical topics clearly and communicate professionally and service-oriented with different types of users
- You work reliably, thoroughly, and in a structured manner and maintain a clear overview even when handling multiple support requests in parallel
- You have very good German and English skills at least at C1 level
