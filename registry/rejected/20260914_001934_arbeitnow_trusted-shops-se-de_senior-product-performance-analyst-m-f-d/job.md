## Senior Product Performance Analyst (m/f/d)

(Hybrid in Cologne or Berlin / Full-time / Permanent contract)

As a Senior Product Performance Analyst, you'll turn scattered data into clear direction: unified KPIs, consistent dashboards, and actionable insights that influence product strategy and prioritization. Working closely with Product Managers and Product leadership, you'll help create a scalable data foundation that enables measurable decision-making across our Product organization. Your work will ensure that product investments, outcomes, and customer impact are guided by reliable data and transparent performance measurements.

### Your Responsibilities

-

Identify business requirements for data streams based on business processes and customer journeys and manage them independently.

-

Turn data into clear stories: build high-quality standard reports, dashboards, and visualizations for a wide range of stakeholder groups.

-

Standardize reporting, KPI definitions, and tracking approaches across Product teams to improve consistency and data quality.

-

Manage projects related to data provisioning from the data lake and ensure reliable access to relevant product data.

-

Partner with Product Managers and Product leadership to develop new KPIs and evaluate product impact.

-

Act as a trusted advisor on measurement, tracking concepts, and performance evaluation within the Product organization.

-

Support the product teams in the early stages of product development in formulating the right hypotheses and success metrics to shape the development phase and create the needed data points.

### Your Profile

-

Degree in Business Analytics, Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, or equivalent practical experience.

-

Several years of experience in data analytics or product analytics, ideally within a product organization.

-

Strong SQL skills and hands-on experience working with data lakes or data warehouses.

-

Proficiency with BI, reporting, and data visualization tools.

-

Proven experience building KPI frameworks, reporting standards, and scalable measurement structures.

-

Independent, project-oriented working style with the ability to communicate complex data insights to diverse stakeholders.

-

Analytical thinking and statistical skills.

-

Business fluent English; additional language skills are a plus.
