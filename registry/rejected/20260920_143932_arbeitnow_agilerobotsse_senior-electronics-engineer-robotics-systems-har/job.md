#### About the role

Join our development team at our Munich R&D site, where you will be responsible for designing, implementing, and validating electronic circuits and software for development and certification testing.

#### Your Responsibilities

- Design and implement electronic circuits and software for testing robot prototypes and subassemblies.

- Evaluate and analyse test results, providing feedback within a multidisciplinary team

- Conduct root cause analysis for production-related quality issues.

- Create and maintain documentation for test procedures and results

- Identify and implement improvements to existing test procedures to enhance efficiency and reliability

- Support the production team in troubleshooting and resolving test-related issues

#### Essential Skills

- Deep understanding of electronic systems (e.g. PCB design, field oriented control, sensors and actuators)

- Hands-on experience in electronics lab work (e.g. Soldering, Oscilloscopes, Cable design and manufacturing)

- Industry experience in testing, verification, and analysis of electronic and robotic systems

- Degree in electrical engineering, or a related technical field

- Proficiency in Python for data analysis and test bench automation

- Ability to collaborate and handle diverse responsibilities

- System-oriented thinking and a structured working approach

- Excellent written and spoken English skills

- Driving License

#### Beneficial Skills

- Experience with robotic systems and test bench automation (e.g. PLC)

- Familiarity with middleware systems (e.g., OPCUA) and bus systems (e.g., EtherCAT)

- Experience with Linux/Unix (e.g., Bash), embedded controllers, and interfaces (e.g., CAN, SPI)

- German language skills are a plus
