# Director Marketing DACH (m/f/x)

Posted: 2026-08-12T11:20:15Z

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement

We seek a strategic and execution-driven **Director of Marketing** to lead our marketing activities across the DACH region and reinforce our position as a full-service payment provider.

This role is responsible for developing and executing a localized marketing strategy that drives brand awareness, customer acquisition, and revenue growth across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. You own marketing outcomes for DACH end-to-end. While operating within a global organization, your primary focus is to ensure strong regional impact by translating broader strategic direction into locally effective execution..

You act as a senior sparring partner to leadership and take clear ownership of decisions in a dynamic, competitive, and highly regulated market.

**Requirements**

### 🚀 **Key Responsibilities**

**Regional Marketing Strategy**

- Develop and execute a comprehensive DACH marketing strategy aligned with regional and global objectives and regional market dynamics
- Identify growth opportunities, customer segments, and key verticals within the payments ecosystem
- Own the regional product marketing function for DACH, ensuring market requirements, customer feedback, and competitive insights are translated into product strategy and roadmap priorities
- Continuously adapt strategy based on local market developments, competition, and regulatory changes

**Demand Generation & Pipeline Growth**

- Own regional pipeline targets and drive demand generation through integrated campaigns (digital, events, partnerships, content)
- Optimize performance marketing channels and lead lifecycle management

**Brand & Positioning**

- Strengthen brand presence and thought leadership in the DACH fintech and payments landscape
- Develop and execute the DACH events strategy, including industry conferences, customer events to support brand awareness, customer engagement, and pipeline generation
- Own DACH public relations activities, including media relations, press releases, executive communications, thought leadership programs, and reputation management

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**

- Partner with Sales to align on go-to-market strategies and account-based marketing initiatives
- Work closely with Global Product teams to translate market requirements and product capabilities into compelling value propositions and commercial opportunities.

**Team Leadership**

- Build, lead, and mentor a high-performing regional marketing team across Martinsried and Hamburg
- Drive a performance-oriented, accountable, and hands-on team culture
- Manage external agencies and partners

**Market Intelligence**

- Monitor competitive landscape, industry trends, and regulatory developments in fintech and payments
- Translate insights into actionable strategies

**Budget & Performance Management, Internal Events**

- Own regional marketing budget and ensure efficient allocation of resources aligned with regional priorities
- Track, analyze, and report on KPIs, ROI, and campaign effectiveness
- Establish clear planning cycles and performance transparency across stakeholders
- Organisation of internal events

### **🧭​ Leadership & Operating Context**

- Act as the clear marketing owner for DACH, with end-to-end accountability for outcomes
- Operate effectively in a complex environment with multiple stakeholders and priorities
- Make decisions with confidence, even in incomplete or ambiguous situations
- Balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution
- Adapt quickly to changing market, regulatory, and business conditions
- Provide clear, data-driven recommendations and act as a trusted advisor to leadership
- Maintain focus, structure, and impact in a fast-paced and evolving environment

### 💫 **Qualifications & Experience**

- 8 - 12+ years of experience in B2B marketing, ideally within fintech, payments, SaaS, or financial services
- Proven track record of driving growth and pipeline generation in the DACH region
- Deep understanding of the payments ecosystem (e.g., PSPs, acquiring, issuing, digital wallets, open banking)
- Experience operating in complex and matrixed organizations
- Strong leadership experience with the ability to build and develop high performing teams
- Data-driven mindset with expertise in marketing analytics and automation tools
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills with the ability to influence across functions
- Fluent in German and English (mandatory)
- Experience in high-growth or scale-up environments
- Familiarity with regulatory frameworks in financial services (e.g., PSD2, AML, KYC)
- Strong network within the DACH fintech/payments community
