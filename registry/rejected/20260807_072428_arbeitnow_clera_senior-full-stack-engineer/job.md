### About the Role

We're a fast-growing, Munich-based AI-powered B2B SaaS company (founded 2022, ~25 people) building privacy-first productivity tools for sales teams. Our product spans a web app, desktop client, iOS app, and backend services — all centered around real-time, low-latency data processing and seamless CRM integrations.

As a **Senior Full-Stack Engineer**, you'll take technical ownership of a major platform surface, work directly with the CTO and founding engineers to shape product and infrastructure strategy, and help define how we build and ship software at scale. This is a high-impact, high-autonomy role at the heart of our engineering team. The role is **hybrid** and based in **Munich, Germany**. *Visa sponsorship is not available.*

### What You'll Do

-

Own a platform — web, desktop, iOS, or backend — and deliver real-time, low-latency features end-to-end.

-

Architect scalable services and define cross-stack best practices that balance performance, reliability, and product velocity.

-

Collaborate closely with the CTO and founding engineers to shape product direction and infrastructure strategy.

-

Mentor junior engineers, lead code reviews, and foster a high-performing, learning-oriented engineering culture.

-

Build and maintain a robust delivery pipeline, including CI/CD, test automation, and deployment strategies.

-

Document architectural decisions and communicate clearly across engineering, product, and infrastructure teams.
