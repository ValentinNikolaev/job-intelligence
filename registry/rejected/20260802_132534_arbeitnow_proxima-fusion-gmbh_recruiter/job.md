**YOUR IMPACT**

As a Recruiter, you’ll be joining Proxima Fusion’s People team during a pivotal period of growth. You’ll be responsible for identifying, attracting, and hiring world-class talent, ensuring each new joiner is not just a fit for today but a multiplier for the future. Your work will shape the caliber of the company and directly influence our ability to deliver fusion energy to the world.

Hence, being a Recruiter during our hyper-growth stages towards building a fusion power plant will not be a walk in the park, but will require dedication, hard work and thinking outside of the box so that we continue building an outstanding team. This is what makes this role special - it will stretch you and help you grow professionally.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

-

Own and execute full-cycle recruitment for critical roles across engineering, science, operations, etc.

-

Partner closely with hiring managers to define hiring needs,build unique sourcing strategies beyond LinkedIn, and deliver top-tier candidates.

-

Drive top-of-funnel strategy: proactively source and engage exceptional passive talent globally via recruitment events, conferences and talent communities.

-

Champion an outstanding candidate experience at every touchpoint.

-

Track, improve, and automate recruitment workflows and tooling to keep us fast, organized, and effective.

-

Build a high-trust, high-rigor process that supports scaling without compromising on quality.

-

Beyond filling roles, manage TA projects such as implementing a new system, or improving our process, or delivering interview training.

-

You thrive in fast-paced, high-stakes environments and take ownership very seriously.
