## **Our player community**

PlaytestCloud runs one of the largest proprietary player panels in games: a large, growing, global community of players who take part because they care about making the games they play better.

This isn't a role where you're building a panel from scratch. You'll be advising a mature Research Operations team that's already investing in the long-term health, quality, and strategic development of that community.

Our goal is simple: help game studios make better decisions through real player insight. You'll play a central role in keeping that community trusted, representative, engaged, and ready for what game developers need next.

## **About the role**

We're hiring an experienced, analytical **Panel Manager (f/m/d)** to shape the next stage of PlaytestCloud's proprietary player panel. Your job: figure out what makes a panel strong and scalable, then turn that understanding into measurable action. You'll identify the metrics that matter most, find opportunities to improve panel quality and availability, and build a practical framework that helps us serve studios better as we grow. This is a freelance contract (3–6 months) with a clear mission: build a stronger foundation for how we measure, manage, and grow our player panel.

*Key Responsibilities:*

-

**Panel strategy and ownership. **Take ownership of the health and growth of PlaytestCloud's global player panel.

-

**Metrics and insight. **Define and track the metrics that connect panel performance, player experience, studio needs, and business growth.

-

**Opportunity identification.** Analyze panel data to find the highest-impact opportunities, constraints, and risks.

-

**Actionable improvements. **Turn insight into practical recommendations, and work with the right teams to implement and measure them.

-

**Cross-functional collaboration.** Partner closely with Research Operations, Product, and GTM to make sure the panel supports great outcomes for our studios.

-

**Sustainable foundations.** Set up a reporting rhythm, performance framework, and roadmap the team can keep using after the contract ends.
