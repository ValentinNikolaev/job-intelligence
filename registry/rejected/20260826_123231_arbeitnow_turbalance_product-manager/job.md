**Your mission**

To support our growing team, we are looking for an experienced Product Manager. In this role, you will take a strategic and hands-on approach to defining the product vision and expanding our product suite. You will develop frameworks and processes that bring together insights from engineers, executives, and customers to strengthen our cutting-edge, easy-to-use, high-performance AI infrastructure design and management platform.

- Develop the multi-year vision and roadmap for our AI Scheduling product, from job queuing and resource allocation to latency optimization and reliability at scale.
- Collaborate closely with engineering, infrastructure, and platform teams to define and deliver capabilities that enterprise and developer customers rely on every day.
- Engage directly with customers to identify unmet needs, validate product direction, and turn complex or conflicting input into clear product decisions.
- Monitor the competitive and technical landscape, including distributed systems, orchestration tools, and AI workload patterns, and translate relevant trends into roadmap implications.
- Work with leadership to support product launches, roadmap reviews, and the broader product narrative.
- Create tight feedback loops between customers and engineering to continuously improve performance, usability, and reliability.
- Support go-to-market activities by contributing to product messaging, documentation, and technical content, including blogs, white papers, and presentations.

**Your profile**

** This role is open to candidates based in Germany who are able to work in a hybrid setup from our Heidelberg office, or candidates based in the San Francisco Bay Area. **

- 5+ years of product management experience, including significant ownership of infrastructure, platform, or developer tooling products.
- BS or MS in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent hands-on technical experience.
- Proven track record of defining and delivering multi-year product roadmaps, not just quarterly plans.
- Product management experience with cloud or cluster management technologies such as Kubernetes, OpenStack, Slurm, VMware, or similar platforms.
- Working understanding of machine learning systems, including compute hardware, communication layers, and AI software toolchains.
- Working understanding of common machine learning software, including models, inference engines, orchestration tools, and typical user and developer workflows.
- Comfortable operating in ambiguity and able to make clear decisions with incomplete information.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with engineers, executives, and enterprise customers.
- Comfortable working as an individual contributor, with impact driven by insight, ownership, and cross-functional influence rather than team size.
- Excellent organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple workstreams without losing quality or momentum.
- Willingness and ability to travel up to 30% between our German and US locations, as business needs require.

**Nice to have**

- Experience in an AI/ML infrastructure or MLOps environment, including an understanding of how scheduling supports training, inference, and evaluation pipelines.
- A portfolio of technical writing, product specifications, or public content demonstrating an ability to communicate complex topics clearly.
- Measurable outcomes you can point to and own, such as shipped products, adoption metrics, customer wins, or performance improvements.
