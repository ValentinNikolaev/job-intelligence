### About the Role

We are a fast-scaling, Series A-backed AI SaaS company operating in the procurement intelligence and supply chain space — helping industrial enterprises across Europe modernize and optimize how they buy. With strong investor backing and a growing customer base in the industrial sector, we are expanding our Go-to-Market team in Munich and looking for a **Senior Business Development Representative** to help drive pipeline growth across European markets.

This is a hands-on, high-impact role for a motivated sales professional who thrives in a fast-paced, feedback-driven environment and is excited to leverage AI tools to supercharge their prospecting workflows. **On-site presence in Munich and fluency in German are required for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Own the full prospecting pipeline end-to-end — identifying, engaging, and converting new leads via phone, email, and social media.

-

Run discovery conversations and build well-qualified opportunities for the wider sales team.

-

Execute and continuously iterate on both outbound and inbound lead generation strategies.

-

Conduct independent market research to identify B2B prospects and tailor outreach for the industrial sector.

-

Develop and refine persuasive sales materials, call scripts, and communication templates.

-

Organize and deliver internal training sessions and workshops to share prospecting best practices with teammates.

-

Identify and experiment with AI/ML tools to accelerate prospecting workflows and optimize team efficiency.

-

Contribute to a high-velocity, performance-driven sales culture with clear, measurable goals.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

5+ years of experience in B2B SaaS sales, with a strong track record of building and delivering prospecting pipelines and validating/closing leads.

-

Excellent written and verbal communication skills in **German** (native or near-native level required).

-

Demonstrated experience owning outbound and inbound lead generation strategies in a B2B SaaS environment.

-

Experience creating and refining call scripts and templates, and organizing internal sales training or workshops.

-

Ability to conduct independent market research and develop compelling sales materials and presentations.

-

Comfort identifying and applying AI tools to accelerate sales processes, with a habit of sharing successful approaches with peers.

-

Willingness and ability to work on-site in Munich.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Prior exposure to the industrial sector, manufacturing, or procurement-related domains.

-

Experience in a startup or high-growth environment.

-

Hands-on familiarity with AI/ML tools applied in a sales context.

**Soft skills we value:**

-

Resilience and goal orientation — you're energized by ambitious targets, not intimidated by them.

-

Strong sense of ownership and a "build from scratch" mentality.

-

Collaborative mindset and genuine eagerness to elevate the whole team.
