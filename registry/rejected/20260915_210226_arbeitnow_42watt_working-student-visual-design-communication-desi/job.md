## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement

**What You'll Do**

- You help us bring the topics of heat pumps, energy-efficient renovations, and 42watt to life.
- You work on exciting communication formats for web, social media, and print — from (info) graphics and layouts to templates and animations.
- You expand our visual language with concrete assets and strengthen the visual foundation of our brand.
- You translate briefs and content into production-ready files.

**Your profile**

- You are studying communication design, graphic design, visual communication, or a related field.
- You have a strong visual sense for branding and work consistently within existing brand guidelines.
- You are proficient in Figma and the Adobe Creative Suite (InDesign, Illustrator, and ideally Photoshop).
- You enjoy working hands-on on clearly defined tasks with regular output.
- You are interested in the energy transition and sustainable technologies.

Nice to have:

- Initial experience with motion graphics (After Effects, Figma).
- You enjoy experimenting with creative workflows beyond the standard — for example, using AI-powered tools for image generation, asset variation, or automating routine tasks.

- **Modern work environment:** You’ll work with a latest-generation MacBook in a modern office featuring call zones, quiet zones, and phone booths. Espresso machine coffee, drinks, and fresh fruit are always available.
- **Monthly tax-free benefit:** €50 per month (from 30 hrs/week) or €25 (under 30 hrs/week) — flexible to spend on what matters most to you.
- **Team culture with real exchange: **Regular team events and our biweekly breakfast meet-up foster transparency, collaboration, and a strong sense of togetherness.

- **Meaningful work with measurable impact: **You actively contribute to the energy transition in the building sector and work on solutions that create lasting sustainable impact.
