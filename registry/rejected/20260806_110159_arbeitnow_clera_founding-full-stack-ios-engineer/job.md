### About the Role

A Y Combinator-backed consumer wearable AI startup is hiring a **Founding Full-Stack iOS Engineer** to own the company's companion iOS app from the ground up. This is a high-ownership, founding-team role at the intersection of consumer hardware, real-time AI inference, and emotional intelligence. The company is building a first-of-its-kind wearable that reads and interprets a user's emotional state in real time — think a fitness tracker, but for your emotions and cognitive performance.

You'll be the primary builder of the consumer-facing mobile experience, translating sophisticated AI model outputs (energy, mood, focus signals derived from audio and biometric data) into intuitive, beautiful interfaces. This role comes with significant equity upside and a clear path to leading mobile engineering as the company scales.

The team is distributed, with presence in **San Francisco** and **London**. This role is based in **London**, with flexibility for hybrid or remote working arrangements.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the companion iOS app end-to-end — architecture, development, deployment, and ongoing iteration.

-

Surface real-time emotional-state outputs from the company's proprietary AI inference model through clean, intuitive app interfaces.

-

Build and ship a polished, consumer-grade mobile experience that meets high product and design standards.

-

Integrate backend APIs covering authentication, data sync, and data models.

-

Make autonomous technical decisions as a founding engineer, setting patterns and standards for the mobile codebase.

-

Collaborate across time zones with a small, fast-moving distributed team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

3+ years of professional iOS development experience with **Swift**, with a proven track record of shipping production-ready, consumer iOS apps.

-

Demonstrated end-to-end ownership of mobile features — from concept through release and ongoing maintenance.

-

Strong full-stack capabilities including mobile app development and backend API integration.

-

Experience integrating machine learning model outputs or inferences into a mobile app UX.

-

Ability to deliver polished, consumer-grade UI/UX with attention to performance and smoothness.

-

Comfortable working independently and across time zones as part of a remote/hybrid team.

-

Work authorization to be employed in the **UK or US** — the company does not offer new visa sponsorship (H-1B transfers considered where applicable).

**Nice to Have:**

-

Background in data-driven apps that surface complex model outputs through accessible design.

-

Experience at a consumer hardware, wearable, or health/wellness tech company.

-

Strong CS fundamentals and comfort owning architectural decisions autonomously.
