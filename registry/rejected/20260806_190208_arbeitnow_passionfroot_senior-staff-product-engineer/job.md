# About the role

We’re looking for a Senior or Staff Product Engineer to help build the next generation of Passionfroot.

You’ll work across the full stack on both our core platform and Zest, our AI Campaign Manager for creator marketing. That could mean designing a new workflow for managing creator campaigns, making collaboration data feel instant, or turning models, search, and product context into a reliable AI experience that customers can trust.

We don’t separate “AI engineering” from “product engineering.” AI is becoming part of how the product works, but great product fundamentals still matter: understanding the user, shaping the problem, choosing the right architecture, building a thoughtful experience, and operating it reliably in production.

You’ll work closely with the founders, design, and customers to take important problems from early exploration to shipped product.

We’re a team of six engineers, and staying small is deliberate. It means you can have an idea on Monday and have customers using it by Thursday—without layers of sprint ceremonies or alignment meetings in between. The flip side is real ownership: you need to exercise strong judgment, communicate your decisions, and think through their consequences rather than wait for someone else to catch them.

# Our technology

Passionfroot is built primarily in TypeScript across the full stack.

Our core product uses React, Remix, PostgreSQL, Prisma, and Zero sync for real-time product experiences. Our AI systems use foundation models from multiple providers alongside the AI SDK, Typesense, and Laminar for search, agent workflows, evaluations, and observability.

You don’t need experience with everything we use. We care more about strong fundamentals, relevant product experience, and your ability to learn quickly.

# Our interview process

We want the process to be useful, respectful, and representative of how we actually work. We don’t use take-home challenges or algorithmic whiteboard interviews.

The process typically includes:

-

A short introductory call.

-

A conversation with Lorenzo about your experience, ambitions, and the work you are most proud of.

-

A technical walkthrough of a project you know deeply, focused on the decisions and trade-offs you made.

-

A collaborative product and technical problem-solving session with members of the team.

-

A paid work trial based on a realistic problem, which can be completed flexibly around your current commitments.

-

References and offer.

We aim to complete the process within one to two weeks.
