**About Zeit AI**

Zeit AI builds the agent framework for business intelligence. We connect to the systems companies already run (ERP exports, CRM data, spreadsheets, internal databases) and put agents on top, so operators get reliable answers without hiring a data team. Founded by Marvin and Leopold, both x-Palantir. YC S24, backed by Oxford Seed Fund and Sequoia Scout. Customers in manufacturing, agriculture and aviation. $1M+ ARR in year one. Small team, one room in Munich, regular offsites in San Francisco.

**What This Role Is**

We have revenue, customers and a product that works. What we do not have is a brand that carries that story for us. Most customers and hires still come from outbound. Your job is to change that.

Inbound moves fast. You own the website and change it as often as we change the product: keywords, SEA campaigns, landing pages per use case, the blog, and tracking what converts. The goal is qualified meetings that arrive without a cold call.

Brand builds on what we have. We already have organic reach, now we push further: press, LinkedIn, conferences. Events are yours end to end, which ones are worth it, what the stand looks like, negotiating the speaker slot, and sometimes standing on stage yourself.

The website, in Webflow: content, structure, landing pages, conversion, tracking

SEA: Google Ads and LinkedIn, measured on qualified inbound, not impressions

-

Brand guidelines that stick: fonts, colours, templates, tone

Events end to end: selection, stand, materials, speaker slots, follow-up
