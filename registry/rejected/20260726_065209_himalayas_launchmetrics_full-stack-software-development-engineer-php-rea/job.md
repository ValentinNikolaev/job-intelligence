#### **About [Launchmetrics](https://himalayas.app/companies/launchmetrics)**

[Launchmetrics](https://himalayas.app/companies/launchmetrics) is the leading Brand Performance Cloud used by Fashion, Lifestyle and Beauty (FLB) executives to make smarter decisions around their branding efforts.

With over a decade of industry expertise, we have helped more than 1,200 customers create inspiring, impactful and measurable experiences.

Founded in NYC and with operating headquarters in Paris, we have employees in ten markets worldwide and offer support in five languages. We have been the trusted brand performance technology to brands worldwide such as Dior, Fendi, Shiseido, NET-A-PORTER and Adidas as well as industry partners like IMG, the Council of Fashion Designers of America, the British Fashion Council, and Camera Nazionale Della Moda Italiana.

#### **The Team & the Pods organization**

We are a team of passionate engineers distributed across the world, always eager to learn new things. We are building state-of-the-art modern applications and scale it on the cloud. We innovate to solve customers problems, focusing on high-performance implementations without neglecting the user experience.

We recently moved our Product and Tech teams to a pod model, and it changes the shape of this job in ways worth understanding before you apply.

-

A pod is a small group, 1 to 4 people, that owns a single mission end to end: from scoping and building through cleaning, enablement and watching adoption. Pods form around a mission and dissolve when it's done. Everyone can be a pod owner, from the most junior person to the most senior, because ownership is the point.

-

Your domain is your product-area home, the part of our software suite you know best. Pods change with every mission; your craft and your domain don't.

#### **The role**

The Launch domain (Samples and Events) builds the operational platforms brands rely on to run their collection launches and their in-person moments. The team owns:

-

Events, the platform behind the world's most important fashion shows: it handles the planning (seating and floor plans, invitee lists, invitations) and the live logistics, checking in over a million attendees every season

-

Samples, the tooling brands use to manage and track their physical assets across a launch, so the right pieces reach the right influencers and celebrities and fewer opportunities, and fewer samples, are lost.

You'll work on a system built for scale and reliability under real-world pressure, where the roadmap is pushing toward Enterprise customers, new event types beyond the runway (store openings, exclusive dinners, parties), deeper integration with clients' enterprise systems, and hundreds of people collaborating on a single event.

##### **What you'll do**

-

Build and maintain back-end systems using the latest versions of PHP, MySQL, and the Laravel framework.

-

Develop and maintain front-end components and features using React, and implement efficient bundling/building processes using Esbuild, Vite or equivalent tools.

-

Collaborate with the design and product teams to deliver user-friendly interfaces and ensure seamless user experiences.

-

Design, develop, and maintain APIs to support front-end functionalities and ensure high performance and responsiveness.

-

Perform code reviews, write clean, maintainable, and efficient code, and actively participate in problem-solving and architectural decisions with the rest of the team.

-

Write and maintain unit tests for both front-end and back-end code to ensure code quality, reliability, and maintainability.

-

Monitor and maintain the tech stack and dependencies using npm & composer, ensuring their stability, security, and efficiency.

-

Keep documentation up to date, ensuring clear and comprehensive information for the codebase, APIs, and system architecture.

-

Use AI-assisted development tools (Claude Code) as a core part of the daily workflow — from scaffolding and refactors to test generation and documentation — while maintaining full ownership and critical review of anything AI-generated before it ships.

-

Build and maintain reusable "skills" / prompt playbooks / agent workflows that encode team conventions, architectural patterns, and coding standards, so AI tooling produces consistent, production-ready output across the team.

-

Continuously evaluate and help define internal AI usage standards (code review criteria for AI-assisted PRs, guardrails for sensitive data in prompts, approved tools and models) to keep velocity gains from becoming technical debt or security risk.
