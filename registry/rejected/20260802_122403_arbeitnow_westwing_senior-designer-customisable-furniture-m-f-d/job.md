**JOIN OUR TEAM**

As a **Senior Designer Customisable Furniture**, you’ll drive the creative vision for the newest category within our Westwing Collection, bringing innovative design, modular thinking, and strategic product development to life. You’ll play a key role in shaping this category from the ground up, ensuring a seamless blend of style, configurability, quality, and sustainability. This position is **permanent** and based in **Munich**.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

- Play a key role in the in-house design team, creating on-trend, visually appealing designs for modular and customisable furniture - the newest category within the Westwing Collection
- Drive product development with an emphasis on premium design, quality, sustainability, and materials, balancing aesthetics with the manufacturability of configurable systems
- Translate modular system architecture into detailed CAD specifications, technical drawings, and prototypes, ensuring components work coherently across configurations
- Collaborate with engineering and tech to ensure the digital customization experience faithfully reflects design intent
- Bring an innovative, problem-solving mindset to the design challenges unique to a brand-new, still-evolving category
- Effectively communicate and convey creative visions to both internal and external stakeholders
- Align creative decisions with assortment strategies and commercial goals

**YOU COME WITH**

- 7+ years of professional experience in product design, with proven expertise in furniture design and development, including materials, quality, and sustainability
- Experience designing modular or configurable product systems, with proficiency in CAD/3D modeling tools (e.g. Rhino, SolidWorks, AutoCAD)
- In-depth knowledge of industry trends, brands, and IP, coupled with a passion for design
- Comfortable navigating ambiguity and building processes from scratch as part of a brand-new category
- Exceptional relationship-building and communication skills, able to work closely with engineering, buying, and commercial teams
- Hands-on attitude and experience in a fast-paced, international environment
- Fluency in English
