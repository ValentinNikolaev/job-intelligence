**Responsibilities**

As an **Infrastructure Engineer**, you will be responsible for the reliable delivery and deployment of our software systems to customer environments. You will ensure that deployments are automated, resilient, and adaptable to diverse on-premise and hybrid infrastructures.
Operating at the intersection of infrastructure, software, and customer environments, you will play a key role in enabling smooth commissioning, stable operations, and continuous improvement of our deployment processes.

**Your Tasks & Responsibilities
**

- Own and execute end-to-end deployments across on-premise and hybrid environments, including software rollout, commissioning, and go-live support
- Configure and validate infrastructure (compute, storage, clusters, networking, mTLS) to ensure production readiness
- Troubleshoot and resolve deployment and infrastructure issues; support Customer Success team during escalations
- Develop and maintain deployment automation, runbooks, and standardized procedures
- Implement automated rollback, recovery workflows, and improve CI/CD-driven deployment processes
- Monitor system health and capacity; manage lifecycle activities such as patching, upgrades, and maintenance
- Implement and test backup and recovery strategies, ensuring compliance with recovery objectives
- Coordinate with customer IT and internal teams on network, security, and hardware integration requirements
- Document site-specific configurations and feed insights back to improve architecture, tooling, and deployment processes
- Contribute to continuous improvement by optimizing workflows and supporting internal test environments

**Qualifications**

- Strong experience with Linux systems administration and container runtimes (Docker, Docker Compose)
- Hands-on experience deploying and operating Kubernetes-based systems in production
- Solid knowledge of networking (VLANs, firewalls, VPNs, DNS, OT/IT environments)
- Experience with infrastructure as code and automation tools (Ansible, Terraform)
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines (e.g., GitLab) and version control (Git)
- Experience with monitoring and observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki)
- Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills in complex environments
- Experience with certificate management (PKI)

**Nice to haves**

- Experience with air-gapped or restricted-network deployments
- Familiarity with Helm and GitOps tools (e.g., ArgoCD, FluxCD)
- Knowledge of industrial networks and OT/IT convergence
- Experience with Debian/Linux packaging
- Relevant certifications (e.g., RHCE, LPIC, CKA)
