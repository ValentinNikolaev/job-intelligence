## About the Role

Founded in 2023, REMATIQ is changing how MedTech product development teams operate with a highly specialised AI platform that helps companies move faster while maintaining quality and compliance.

We already work with some of the world’s leading Life Sciences companies, including **Bayer, B. Braun, and Novartis**, and have raised **€5.4M in Seed funding** from experienced investors.

We are hiring a full-cycle Enterprise Account Executive to own the complete sales process for large Life Sciences accounts, from prospecting through close. This is a quota-carrying role with uncapped variable compensation tied to performance.

We believe in the value of in-person collaboration and work in a hybrid setup, with the team coming together in our office in the heart of **Berlin-Mitte** typically **four** days per week.

## Responsibilities

-

Manage the full sales cycle for enterprise accounts, from initial engagement through negotiation and close

-

Position REMATIQ as the leading AI compliance & documentation solution for Life Sciences to VP and C-suite decision makers

-

Demo existing and new product capabilities; build compelling ROI cases to win over multi-stakeholder buying committees

-

Co-own and continuously improve our GTM playbook

-

Collaborate with our broader team, take ownership of improving our product and processes
