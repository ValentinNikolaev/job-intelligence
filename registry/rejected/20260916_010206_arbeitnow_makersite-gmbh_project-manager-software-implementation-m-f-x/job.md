#### **The role:**

** **

-

Lead the planning and implementation of software projects related to system connections with our customers, ensuring they are delivered on time and within scope.

-

Maintain and update project plans, proactively identifying and managing any project risks.

-

Ensure the availability of the right expertise for each project phase, coordinating with internal and external resources as necessary.

-

Manage stakeholder expectations, delivering regular and comprehensive project updates and effectively resolving any arising issues.

-

Contribute to the standardization of project implementation methodology and project templates to improve project standardization and efficiency.

Generate thorough Project/Solution Documentation, outlining project goals, deliverables, and timelines.

Handle all customer communication across various projects, ensuring clear, consistent, and timely communication with all stakeholders.
