**Your Responsibilities**

-

-

Impact and stress tests

-

Accuracy and repeatability measurements

-

Set up test rigs and measurement setups

-

Investigate and analyze failures or unexpected behavior

-

Document test procedures, results, and conclusions

-

Collaborate closely with hardware to improve designs
