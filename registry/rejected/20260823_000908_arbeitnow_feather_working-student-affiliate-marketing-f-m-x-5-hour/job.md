**Intro**

As our working student - Affiliate marketing for one of our key insurance partnerships, you will be responsible for managing the existing affiliate network as well as onboarding new affiliates into the network. You’ll manage the day-to-day relationship with affiliates in German and English.

This is a working student role (~5 hours/week) on a one-year contract - a great fit if you're currently enrolled at a university and looking for hands-on, real-world marketing experience alongside your studies.

At Feather, we're building insurance that actually matters to our customers. It rests on three principles: a fully digital experience, modern and flexible plans, and reliable human support. And because we build it all for expats living abroad, we're a genuinely different kind of insurer.

**Why work at Feather?**

- **We’re small, lean, and we love what we do.** The usual buzzwords here, except we actually mean them. Staying small helps us focus on what matters, and we want to keep it that way.
- **You’ll work on a product that is loved by customers.** Our excellent [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/feather-insurance.com) and [Google](https://www.google.com/search?q=feather+insurance&oq=feather+insurance) reviews speak for themselves.
- **There's plenty of space to grow.** We trust you to take ownership and make decisions from day one, and we will give you all the support and mentorship you need to succeed.
- **Flexible remote work policy.** We offer a remote-first work environment with an office in Berlin. We host company retreats, team dinners, and meetups to spend in-person time together.

**What you bring to the table**

- You are an advanced Bachelor’s or early Master’s student enrolled at a German university (eligible for Werkstudent status)
- You have some experience in affiliate marketing, partnership management, or a similar relationship-focused role.
- You're comfortable maintaining relationships day-to-day over email, calls, and video calls.
- You're comfortable using everyday software tools (Slack, Notion, Google Suite, and similar) and can pick up new tools quickly.
- You're someone who takes initiative and can manage your own workload independently, without needing close supervision once you're settled in.
- You're fluent in German and English.

**You'll be responsible for**

- You will manage our existing affiliate network for this partnership, in German and English, acting as the main point of contact for day-to-day questions and onboarding new affiliates as they join.
- You will build and maintain strong relationships with our affiliates to keep the partnership running smoothly.
- You will monitor affiliate performance and flag issues early, sharing clear, simple reporting with the team.
- You will use good judgment to know when to handle something directly versus when to escalate without needing close supervision.
- You will handle commission payments, as well as regular financial reporting and reconciliation for the partnership, keeping records accurate and up to date.

**What will the interview process look like? **

- **CV screening**
- **Initial video call -** We’ll have a chat and get to know each other (25min)
- **Take-home task** - We'll give you a task to work on, which will give you an idea of the type of projects you'd take on at Feather (1-2h)
- **Take-home task discussion** - We'll go through the task to work on together, so we make sure that we understand your reasoning and approach (60min)
- **Expectations & alignment conversation** - A conversation with the Head of Marketing to align on expectations, ways of working, and next steps, including a review of insights from the previous interviews (~30min)
