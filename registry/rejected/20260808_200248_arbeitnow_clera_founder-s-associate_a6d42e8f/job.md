### About the Role

This is a founding-stage role sitting at the heart of a fast-growing **personalized health diagnostics** startup based in Berlin. You'll work directly with the co-founders to move ideas to production quickly, eliminate manual work through AI and automation, and build the repeatable, measurable systems that improve member outcomes — all within a regulated health context.

The engagement is **3–6 months** with real potential for conversion to a full-time role based on fit. It's hands-on, technical, and high-impact from day one — low bureaucracy, high ownership.

### What You'll Do

-

Work directly with founders in a fast-moving environment to ship ideas quickly and reduce operational drag.

-

Automate and eliminate manual ops work using AI, code, and clean processes — not manage tasks by hand.

-

Own **customer onboarding and technical setup** to move members through the funnel efficiently.

-

Run day-to-day operations including acquiring blood testing locations and managing logistics (sample flow, shipping, escalations).

-

Recruit, onboard, and schedule **mobile field staff** (e.g., mobile nurses).

-

Manage customer support and concierge workflows — handling member requests and action plans.

-

Structure pipelines for product and sales to surface member feedback and patterns to the team.

-

Build internal tools and automations using **AI agents, n8n, Lovable, Retool, Zapier, and Claude/GPT APIs**.

-

Track ops KPIs with **SQL** (cycle times, error rates, stockouts) and convert ad hoc processes into measurable systems.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Strong ownership mindset with a bias to action — hands-on, structured, and clear about process.

-

Proven ability to turn manual processes into measurable, repeatable, automated systems.

-

Experience recruiting, onboarding, and scheduling field staff and managing logistics operations.

-

Technically capable: comfortable with basic SQL, APIs, and building tools with Lovable / Retool / n8n.

-

**AI-native**: daily use of Claude, GPT, Cursor or similar; automations-first mindset.

-

Strong communication skills in both **German and English**.

-

Background studying or working in Computer Science, Engineering, Life Sciences, or a related field.

-

Evidence of side projects, prototypes, or code (e.g., GitHub activity).

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience in healthtech or other regulated environments.

-

Prior exposure to early-stage startup or founder-led environments.
