### About the Role

A fast-growing, Y Combinator–backed startup in the AI marketing space is hiring their **first engineer** in Munich. This is a true founding-scope role: you will own full product features end to end, working side by side with the founders and directly with customers. The company builds a marketing suite that helps brands understand, monitor, and improve their visibility across AI agents and AI-powered search platforms — a category that is growing rapidly.

You will span product thinking, frontend, backend systems, LLM pipelines, data, and customer conversations. If you thrive in ambiguity, love shipping things that matter, and want real ownership from day one, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Own full product features from ambiguous problem through to live, customer-validated product

-

Work directly with founders and customers to figure out what to build next

-

Design and implement LLM pipelines and agentic workflows that power core product capabilities

-

Build across the full stack — frontend UI, backend services, and data layers

-

Design simple, intuitive UX for complex workflows

-

Talk to customers regularly and translate feedback into concrete product improvements

-

Continuously iterate until features create measurable customer value
