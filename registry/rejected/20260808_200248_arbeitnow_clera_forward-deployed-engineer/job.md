Experience deploying on cloud platforms (**Azure, AWS, or GCP**) and managing scalable AI workloads.

Production-grade testing, monitoring, and reliability practices for AI systems.

Strong client-facing communication and stakeholder management skills — able to translate messy requirements into clear deliverables.

Ability to collaborate across engineering, product, and customer success to ship impactful AI solutions.
