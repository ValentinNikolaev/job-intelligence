#### **The opportunity:**

The Enterprise Account Executive (ENT) is responsible for driving complex, high-value enterprise deals and establishing long-term strategic partnerships with large customers. This role owns advanced, multi-stakeholder sales cycles that often span regions, business units, and decision layers. The ENT AE operates with a high degree of autonomy, shaping deal strategy, influencing executive stakeholders, and orchestrating cross-functional teams to deliver differentiated, value-driven solutions.

Your responsibilities as an** Account Executive, ENT **include, but are not limited to the following:

Pipeline & Opportunity Ownership

- Own and close high-value, complex enterprise opportunities, consistently meeting or exceeding individual quotas.

- Build a strategic pipeline through self-prospecting, account planning, and SDR partnership.

- Proactively identify whitespace, expansion potential, and multi-entity opportunities within target accounts.

- Maintain forecasting, opportunity hygiene, and deal governance in Salesforce (SFDC).

Strategic Discovery & Solution Architecture

- Lead executive-level discovery to understand enterprise supply chain strategy, risk posture, and long-term business objectives.

- Navigate complex buying groups across procurement, ops, finance, IT, and senior leadership.

- Design integrated, multi-product Flexport solutions aligned to customer priorities and business outcomes.

- Position Flexport as a strategic partner through compelling value narratives, business cases, and solution roadmaps.

Deal Strategy, Negotiation & Closing

- Own end-to-end deal strategy for complex opportunities, including stakeholder mapping, decision criteria, and close plans.

- Lead advanced commercial negotiation, pricing, contracts, and non-standard deal structures.

- Orchestrate internal stakeholders (Ops, Finance, Legal, Product) to progress deals efficiently

Onboarding, Expansion & Relationship Leadership

- Own the onboarding motion, ensuring enterprise customers are set up for scalable success.

- Establish and maintain trusted relationships with senior and executive customer stakeholders.

- Identify expansion opportunities together with Account Management to drive sustained growth.

- Serve as a point of escalation and strategic advisor during the customer lifecycle.

Enterprise Leadership & Market Impact

- Act as a senior commercial voice in enterprise pursuits, sharing market insight and customer feedback internally.

- Contribute to enterprise sales best practices, deal reviews, and peer coaching.

- Represent Flexport with credibility and confidence in high-stakes customer interactions.

#### **You should have:
**

- 5+ years of experience managing complex, enterprise-level sales cycles with consistent quota attainment.

- Proven ability to close large, multi-stakeholder deals with long sales cycles and high deal complexity.

- Strong executive presence with the ability to influence senior decision-makers.

- Advanced discovery, negotiation, and value-based selling skills.

- High degree of autonomy, strategic thinking, and commercial judgment.

- Strong logistics, freight forwarding, or supply chain industry experience preferred.

- Intermediate in English; additional languages are a plus depending on region.
