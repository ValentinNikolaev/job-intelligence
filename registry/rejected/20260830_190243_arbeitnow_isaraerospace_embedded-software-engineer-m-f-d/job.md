**Mission Brief **

As an Embedded Software Engineer in the Flight Software Testing team, your mission is to ensure the reliability and performance of the software that powers our spectrum launch vehicle. By leveraging your skills, you work with the rest of the team to automate improve and maintain the flight software testing infrastructure and pipelines.

Your Role in Our Space Mission

- Work to implement and automate software tests, data analysis and verification/validation.

- Work to build and maintain test infrastructure such as HIL setups.

- Develop tools to support our flight software development and testing.

- Recognize bugs and drive the effort to resolve them.

- Help track the performance of our software in the field (e.g. during launch and test campaigns) and be able to provide live support and expertise.

**Qualification Checklist **

- BSc/MSc in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Embedded Systems or comparable

- Experience developing with Python, C/C++

- Experience working with Real-time systems

- Proficient with Git

- Proficient at debugging software

- Proficient in data analysis and visualization

- Experience working with Linux

- Experience working with CI/CD pipelines

- Fluent in English

Bonus Skills

- Experience developing or testing software for Aerospace applications.

- Experience working with MATLAB/Simulink

- Experience with real-time hardware-in-the-loop test systems

- Experience working with hardware such as microcontrollers and FPGAs
