## About Eye Security

Eye Security is providing cyber security with embedded cyber insurance solutions for organisations across Europe. Headquartered in the Netherlands, we're over 190 FTEs and growing internationally, having secured Series C funding of 60 million.

We combine cutting-edge technology with hands-on expertise to detect, respond to, and recover from cyber threats in real time. Our team brings together talent from intelligence, military, tech, and consulting backgrounds, all united by one shared mission: to make enterprise-grade cyber security accessible to every business, not just the big players.

At Eye, you'll work on projects with an international footprint, solving real-world challenges and helping to build a safer digital future for our SME clients and our partners.

## About this role

We are looking for a **Junior Cyber Security Consultant** to join our **Service Delivery (SD)** team, the heart of Eye Security. In this role, you'll be part of a team that guides organisations through their security journey, helping them understand and improve their cyber posture.

You'll execute core tasks within a predefined process: onboarding new customers, supporting them with security questions, delivering periodic cyber-security reviews, and working closely with our technical experts. Your role is hands-on and focused, allowing you to develop real expertise in a structured, supportive environment whilst gradually taking on more responsibility.

Service Delivery sits at the intersection of product, operations, and customers, so you'll work across different teams and grow in multiple aspects of cyber security. This is a strong early-career role with a clear path to grow into more advanced consultant roles over time. You'll solve real problems for real customers, supported by experienced colleagues who want to see you succeed.

## What you will do

-

Guide new customers through Eye's onboarding process, helping them implement EDR, cloud monitoring, and other security controls

-

Assess customer security questions and concerns, working out how Eye's resources can best support them

-

Deliver periodic security reviews, providing tailored advice and helping customers understand which measures to prioritise

-

Work closely with our Cyber Security Specialists, Product, Customer Success, Engineering, Security Operations, and Threat Intelligence teams to solve customer challenges

-

Spot opportunities to improve how Eye operates, both technically and in how we build relationships with customers

-

Learn from experienced colleagues and contribute ideas to real projects, taking ownership of well-defined deliverables

-

Support second-line technical challenges alongside our first-line support team and specialists
