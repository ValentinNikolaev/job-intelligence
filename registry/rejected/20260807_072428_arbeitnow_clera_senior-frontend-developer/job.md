### About the Role

A fast-growing **AI search analytics platform for marketing teams** is looking for a **Senior Frontend Developer** to play a central role in shaping the product experience. You'll work at the intersection of design, data, and AI — translating complex insights into clean, intuitive, and high-performance interfaces used by thousands of brands and agencies.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the development and optimization of web applications, delivering fast, polished, and accessible user experiences.

-

Collaborate closely with design, product, and data teams to translate requirements into elegant, functional interfaces.

-

Turn complex AI-driven insights into intuitive and visually compelling UI.

-

Drive frontend performance optimization and profiling to ensure lightning-fast responsiveness at scale.

-

Uphold and improve web accessibility standards across the product (WCAG-compliant UI).

-

Iterate on designs independently when needed, bringing both technical and aesthetic sensibility to the work.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Strong computer science fundamentals.

-

Proficiency with **TypeScript** and **React** to build scalable frontend applications.

-

Hands-on experience with **Tailwind CSS** and component libraries (e.g., **shadcn/ui**).

-

Proven experience with frontend performance optimization and profiling techniques.

-

Strong knowledge of web accessibility principles and **WCAG-compliant** UI development.

-

A genuine design sense — you can iterate on designs yourself without waiting for specs.

-

Ability to collaborate effectively across design, product, and data teams.

-

Willingness and availability to work **on-site in Berlin, Germany, 5 days per week** (this is a firm requirement).

**Nice to Have:**

-

Familiarity with AI tooling and integrating AI insights into frontend applications (e.g., OpenAI, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Llama).
