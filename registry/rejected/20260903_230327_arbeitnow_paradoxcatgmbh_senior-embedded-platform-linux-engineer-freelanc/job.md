## Senior Embedded Platform & Linux Engineer FREELANCER (f/m/d) - Munich

## You will:

- Develop and integrate Embedded C/C++ software for ARM-based platforms

- Bring up target hardware and integrate Yocto-based Board Support Packages, including bootloader, Linux Kernel, Device Tree, drivers and peripheral interfaces

- Create and maintain Yocto layers, BitBake recipes and reproducible build, flashing and deployment environments

- Debug complex hardware and software issues using GDB, JTAG, logs, traces and Linux performance tools

- Analyze system performance, memory usage, startup behavior and I/O bottlenecks

- Support display, GPU, network and system integration in close collaboration with software, graphics and QA engineers

- Coordinate technical topics with customers, hardware suppliers and platform vendors, document results and share knowledge within the team

## You have:

- A degree in computer science, electrical engineering, embedded systems, a comparable qualification, or equivalent experience

- Several years of professional embedded software development experience with strong Embedded C and Embedded C++ skills

- Practical experience with ARM-based SoCs or microcontrollers, board bring-up and hardware-near development

- Strong knowledge of Embedded Linux, Yocto, BitBake, OpenEmbedded, Device Tree, BSP integration and cross-compilation

- Experience with Linux Kernel configuration, device drivers and bootloaders such as U-Boot

- Practical target-hardware debugging experience using GDB and JTAG, SWD or comparable technologies

- A structured approach to root-cause analysis and experience with Git and agile development

- An excellent standard of written and spoken English

## Nice to have:

- Experience with NXP i.MX platforms, ideally i.MX 95, heterogeneous Cortex-A/Cortex-M systems or vendor-specific BSPs and SDKs

- Knowledge of FreeRTOS, secure boot, Ethernet, TSN, IEEE 1588 or embedded performance tools such as perf, ftrace and kgdb

- Experience integrating display controllers, GPU drivers, OpenGL ES, EGL or Vulkan

- Experience with CI/CD, automated image creation, flashing and deployment

- Automotive, industrial, commercial vehicle or functional safety experience

- Knowledge of written and spoken German
