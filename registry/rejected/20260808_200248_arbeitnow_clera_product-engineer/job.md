### About the Role

A fast-moving, pre-seed healthtech startup building a modern preventive health platform is looking for a highly autonomous **Product Engineer** to join their small team in Berlin. The platform combines advanced biomarker diagnostics, clinician-reviewed health plans, AI-powered concierge experiences, and interactive customer dashboards into a single seamless product.

This is a **founder-mode role** — no PMs, no hand-offs, no tickets handed to you. You'll work directly with the founders, own features end-to-end, and ship real product from day one.

### What You'll Do

-

Ship product end-to-end across the web app, backend, and third-party integrations (lab partners, supplement manufacturers)

-

Build and iterate on customer-facing experiences: biomarker dashboards, personalised action plans, and subscription flows

-

Own and improve engineering infrastructure and AI development tooling as the product scales

-

Work directly with the founding team to translate product vision into working software — fast

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

Up to 1 year of software development experience, with **shipped, real-world software** — startup, founder, or indie hacker contexts are a strong signal

-

Hands-on experience with modern AI development workflows and tooling (e.g. Cursor, Claude Code, evals, agentic pipelines)

-

Experience designing and implementing customer-facing dashboards and subscription flows for web applications

-

Experience building API integrations with external partners (e.g. lab or manufacturing providers)

-

Genuine full-stack capability: comfortable owning frontend, backend, and integrations without relying on specialists

-

Professional working proficiency in English

-

Ability to operate autonomously in a high-trust, low-process environment

**Nice to Have**

-

Proficiency in **TypeScript** and **Next.js** (or equivalent); experience with **React**, **PostgreSQL**, **Supabase**, and **Vercel**

-

Genuine interest in or background relating to preventive health, longevity, or biomarker science
