## Tasks

- Design and implement motion planning algorithms that generate safe and efficient vehicle trajectories under diverse driving conditions

- Optimize planned trajectories for safety, comfort, and efficiency, balancing competing constraints in real time

- Validate motion planning behavior in simulation and on real-world test data, identifying and resolving edge cases

- Work closely with perception and software integration teams to ensure motion planning outputs integrate reliably into the overall driving stack

- Ensure motion planning algorithms meet defined performance benchmarks and safety requirements for autonomous operation

## Requirements

- A completed degree in computer science, robotics, engineering, applied mathematics, or a comparable field - or equivalent vocational training/apprenticeship in a relevant technical discipline. Hands-on experience counts at least as much as the formal qualification

- 3+ years of experience in motion planning, robotics, or autonomous vehicle software

- Proven experience developing motion planning/trajectory generation algorithms for robotics or autonomous vehicles, with a solid background in optimization techniques (e.g. model predictive control, sampling-based planners)

- Strong C++ and/or Python skills, with experience in motion planning frameworks/libraries (e.g. OMPL, MoveIt) and optimization solvers for trajectory optimization/MPC (e.g. CasADi, OSQP, IPOPT)

- Experience with ROS/ROS2 and simulation environments (e.g. CARLA, Gazebo), plus a solid grounding in control theory and state estimation (e.g. Kalman filters), linear algebra, and numerical optimization

- Experience validating algorithms via simulation and real-world test data, including edge-case analysis, and comfortable working with Git/CI-CD and real-time/embedded system constraints

- Experience working cross-functionally with perception and software integration teams, with an understanding of functional safety requirements for autonomous systems

- An analytical, structured problem-solving mindset, a safety-conscious approach, and the ability to clearly communicate algorithmic trade-offs to technical and non-technical stakeholders

- English C1 is required; German is a plus
