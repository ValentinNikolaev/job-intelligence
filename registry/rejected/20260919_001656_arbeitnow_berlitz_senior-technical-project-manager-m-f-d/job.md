# Senior Technical Project Manager (m/f/d)

We're building a new global Berlitz, and this is your opportunity to help shape it. Nearly 150 years of expertise, meeting the ambition and technology to power a new era of learning.

If you are the kind of person who thrives in a fast-paced environment, isn’t afraid of a blank page, and is excited to help shape the future of a global company undergoing a significant transformation, **you might belong at Berlitz!**

We're looking for people with software engineering backgrounds who are ready to move into technical project management, owning the business systems work behind our transition from a legacy stack to a new one. These are growth paths, not fully-formed PM seats.

Our legacy stack involves over 100 systems across the globe including proprietary invoicing/billing/scheduling systems, two Salesforce instances and NetSuite, among others. Our new stack is HubSpot, Metronome, Stripe, NetSuite, and Workato. Getting from one to the other — while the business keeps running on both — is the core challenge of both tracks.

**We're hiring two people into this role.** The skills profile is similar for both, but each hire will own one of two distinct tracks: **O2C** **Integrations** or **CRM Projects**. These tracks will eventually mature into a larger scope of internal tooling and enterprise customization. Track assignment will be based on candidate strengths and interest during onboarding, rather than decided in advance.

###

Track 1: Order-to-Cash Integrations Projects

Focused on **building and connecting**

-

**Own the Order-to-Cash flow end-to-end** — Across both tech stacks. You're accountable for how orders, invoicing, billing, and revenue data move through the business, regardless of which systems they touch.

-

**Set integration ecosystem strategy** — Define how integrations across business systems should be architected as a whole: point-to-point vs. centralized (e.g., via Workato), reusable patterns, and standards for how new systems connect in.

-

**Co-build the Legacy system replacement** s— Partner with the Enterprise product team as technical owner of how the new platform's O2C integration layer is architected, including data models and API contracts.

-

**Control integration vendors** — Own technical evaluation, SLAs, and build-vs-buy decisions for integration tooling and vendors across the ecosystem.

-

**Bridge legacy and new-stack engineering teams** — Be the technical point of contact ensuring O2C data contracts and dependencies are understood on both sides.

Track 2: CRM & Martech Projects

Focused on **retiring and migrating CRM systems**

-

**Lead the Salesforce 2A sunset project** — Own the plan, timeline, and execution for retiring this legacy Salesforce instance, coordinating data migration and cutover with dependent teams.

-

**Lead the Salesforce STAR sunset project** — Same ownership model, applied to the STAR instance.

-

**Control the HubSpot vendor relationship** — Own vendor management for HubSpot, including contract terms, SLA performance, and roadmap alignment with vendor capabilities.

-

**Own marketing automations** — Own the roadmap and execution for marketing automation workflows (built largely on HubSpot). Campaign strategy sits with Marketing; you own the tooling and execution layer those campaigns run on.

-

**Manage sunset risk** — Identify what breaks if a legacy system goes away too early, and build technically sound mitigation and rollback plans.

### **What We're Looking For**

-

Software engineering background — you've built, maintained, or integrated production systems, ideally including APIs, middleware, or data pipelines.

-

Strong communication skills — able to explain technical tradeoffs to engineers, vendors, and business stakeholders alike.

-

Detail oriented and structured approach — planning and running complex, multi-stakeholder technical projects to completion, including sequencing, risk management, and timeline ownership.

-

Comfortable reading and reasoning about system architecture and data models across multiple connected systems without translation from engineering.

-

Comfortable with ambiguity, especially around evolving scope boundaries between adjacent teams during a platform migration.

### **Nice to Have**

-

Familiarity with billing/invoicing/CRM systems (Salesforce, NetSuite, HubSpot, Stripe, Metronome, Workato, or similar).

-

Experience with vendor management, including contract and SLA negotiation.

-

Background in a company undergoing a legacy-to-modern platform migration.

-

Experience defining integration strategy or standards at an organizational level, not just shipping individual integrations; exposure to integration architecture patterns (point-to-point, iPaaS/middleware, event-driven).

-

Formal project management certification (PMP, PRINCE2, or similar) — helpful but not required with structured ways of working

### **Why You Should Join**

This is a rare, explicitly structured path out of pure engineering — you're hired as an engineer, judged on engineering judgment, and grow into project management on the job. You'll get unusually broad system exposure fast, which is the kind of company-wide operational map most engineers wait years to see. You will be joining a project management and product operations team and receive ongoing coaching for your career development.
