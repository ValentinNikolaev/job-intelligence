**The Robotics and Mechatronics Teams at Agile Robots** are looking for a **C++ Software Engineer (m/f/d)**, who will build and maintain the C++ platform infrastructure our robot runs on. You will help us ensure it runs reliably and safely once it's out of the lab and in production.

### Responsibilities

- **Platform Development:** Design, implement, and maintain C++ software components for the robot's platform infrastructure, including Linux-based runtime services, communication layers, configuration handling, and developer tooling.

- **APIs & Interfaces:** Define and improve the APIs and interfaces that connect robot applications, backend services, and hardware-facing components, working directly with the firmware, control, backend, DevOps, QA, and application teams that consume them.

- **Real-Time Processing:** Contribute to real-time and near-real-time data processing pipelines, accounting for latency, determinism, resource usage, and failure behavior.

- **Hardware Integration:** Support integration with real robotic hardware and investigate issues that cross software, OS, network, and hardware boundaries.

- **Reliability & Observability:** Improve system reliability through better error handling, observability, monitoring, and recovery mechanisms.

- **Architecture & Standards:** Contribute component-level input to software architecture decisions and technical standards, working within direction set by senior engineers and technical leads.

### Essential Skills

- **Background:** Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of training and professional experience, with 2 to 5 years building production C++ systems.

- **Modern C++:** Professional experience writing production C++ software using modern C++ practices (C++17/C++20).

- **Systems Programming:** Solid understanding of Linux system programming — processes, threads, IPC, sockets, scheduling, file systems — and networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, UDP, latency, buffering, and packet flow.

- **Debugging & Concurrency:** Practical debugging skills (GDB, sanitizers, or similar) and some experience with multi-threaded or concurrent programming.

- **Engineering Practices:** Understanding of maintainable software architecture principles (SOLID, separation of concerns, testability) and experience writing automated tests, particularly unit and integration tests.

### Beneficial Skills

- **Robotics/Industrial Background:** Experience with robotics, industrial automation, or embedded systems, including industrial communication protocols such as EtherCAT, CAN, Modbus, or Profinet.

- **Deep Systems & Deployment:** Knowledge of real-time Linux and kernel internals — scheduling, IRQ handling, IPC mechanisms — and experience with firmware update flows, containerized environments, or build/package systems such as Conan or CMake.

- **Python Tooling:** Experience with Python for tooling, automation, testing, or code generation.

- **Platform SDK Design:** Exposure to designing internal frameworks, SDKs, or reusable platform libraries.

- **Mentoring:** Experience mentoring interns or peers, or contributing to technical discussions.
