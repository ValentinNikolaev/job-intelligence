## Aufgaben

This role provides combined FP&A and operational finance support to the Analytics business, with a strong focus on reporting, budgeting, inventory, cost, and operational performance management. Reporting solid-line into Global FP&A and operationally supporting the Ops Finance in Germany, the role ensures accurate reporting, disciplined inventory and cost controls, and effective execution of global and local finance processes.

**Business Partnership & Support**

- Support Global FP&A and the Ops Finance with financial analysis and data preparation

- Act as execution bridge between global FP&A requirements and local Operations Finance needs

- Prepare insights and analyses for operational and financial reviews

**Budgeting, Forecasting & Planning**

- Support budgeting, forecasting, and monthly reporting processes

- Prepare variance analysis and identify key cost and performance drivers

- Support backlog reporting and integration into FP&A forecasts

**Reporting, Analytics & Controllership**

- Prepare or support reconciliations, validations, and accuracy checks

- Support collaboration with Accounting on close and reporting topics

**Operations Finance, Inventory & Cost**

- Monitor stock balances, inventory movements and absorption

- Perform inventory valuation including standard cost recalculations, usage, E&O and reconciliations

- Support inventory spot checks and monitoring of inventory days

- Monitor purchasing volumes including material and non-material

- Track productivity figures from production

**ERP, Master Data & Systems**

- Monitor ERP master data including but not limited to items and cost

- Act as contact for item master and POC model topics

- Monitor working orders history, incoming orders and backlog
