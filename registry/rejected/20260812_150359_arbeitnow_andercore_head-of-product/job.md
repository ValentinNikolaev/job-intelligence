## **About Andercore**

Andercore is the AI-native supplier of industrial materials for energy, infrastructure, and construction in wholesale and beyond.

We trade with global suppliers and distribute to European customers on our own account. Our AI runs the full trade and distribution end-to-end: sourcing, quality, pricing, sales, logistics, and embedded financing. For the customer, it feels like buying from their preferred local supplier; for our partners, it is the most convenient and safe way to do business across borders. Behind it, our software and agentic AI do the heavy lifting that used to take an asset-intensive supply chain with four or five intermediaries and weeks of manual coordination.

Where we are today:

-

Strong triple-digit-million euro turnover

-

Seven European markets live

-

80+ people across Berlin (HQ), offices in London, Mumbai, and Shanghai

$40M Series B just closed, $75M raised to date from Atomico, Project A, and Inven Capital, institutional financing from international banks.

We are building the world's first and last industrial-grade AI operating system for materials, redefining how global trade works in one of the largest and most essential industries on earth.

**Overview**

We are hiring a **Head of Produc**t to define, build, and scale the product vision behind the company’s AI operating system for industrial supply. This is a **mission-critical leadership role with full ownership to lead the product organization** across multiple markets. You will shape the architecture, roadmap, and execution machinery that powers sourcing, sales, logistics, financing, and automation across six markets.

This role requires a product leader who combines **technical depth, AI-first thinking, commercial intuition, and strong cross-functional alignment**. You will lead Product Management, Product Design, and cross-functional Discovery, working closely with Engineering, AI/ML, Data, Sales, Procurement, Logistics, and Finance to drive product development from concept to execution.

Your mandate: **make the company’s platform the most reliable, predictable, and autonomous operating system in the industry**, delivering high-quality, scalable solutions that meet customer needs and drive measurable business impact.

As part of our management leadership team, you will have a pathway to multi-region oversight, a highly competitive compensation package with performance-based incentives, and the opportunity to shape a high-performance organization from our Berlin headquarters, backed by top-tier investors.
