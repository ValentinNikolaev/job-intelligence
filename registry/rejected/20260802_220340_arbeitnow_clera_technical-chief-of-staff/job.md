### About the Role

A fast-moving, Y Combinator–backed AI-native CRM startup in Stockholm is hiring a **Technical Chief of Staff** to work directly alongside the founding team. This is a high-impact, on-site role designed for someone who thrives in an early-growth environment, brings strong technical judgment, and is energized by cross-functional execution at speed.

This role is an exceptional launchpad for future founders — you'll have direct exposure to product strategy, go-to-market execution, and operational decision-making from day one. The company is building an AI-native productivity platform for sales and customer-facing teams, automating the messy parts of CRM so teams can focus entirely on their customers.

### What You'll Do

-

Partner directly with the founders across product, go-to-market, and operations as a trusted strategic and executional counterpart.

-

Coordinate cross-functional initiatives, aligning priorities and driving execution across teams.

-

Translate high-level strategic goals into concrete plans and track delivery against tight deadlines.

-

Support founder decision-making by providing technical context, project-level insights, and clear recommendations.

-

Own high-impact projects end-to-end — identifying what moves the needle and making it happen.

-

Operate effectively in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment, adapting fluidly as priorities evolve.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

Strong technical background with solid CS fundamentals — computer science training or equivalent hands-on experience.

-

Demonstrated ability to coordinate cross-functional initiatives and work closely with executive stakeholders.

-

Strong product mindset with the ability to contribute meaningfully to product strategy and decisions.

-

Excellent written and verbal communication skills; able to articulate strategy clearly to diverse audiences.

-

High ownership mentality — comfortable driving initiatives forward through ambiguity with minimal direction.

-

Prior startup experience or meaningful exposure to fast-paced, entrepreneurial environments.

-

Eligibility to work in Sweden and ability to work on-site in Stockholm full-time (**no visa sponsorship available**).

**Nice to Have:**

Interest in branding, design, or managing social channels.

-

Experience working in early-stage (seed or Series A) companies.
