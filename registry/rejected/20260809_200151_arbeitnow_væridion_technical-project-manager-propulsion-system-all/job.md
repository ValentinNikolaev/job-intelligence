**Your Mission:**

As Project Manager – Propulsion System, you will lead the development and integration of VÆRIDION's electric propulsion system for next-generation electric aircraft. You will manage cross-functional activities across engineering, testing, certification, manufacturing, and supply chain to ensure propulsion system milestones are delivered on time, within budget, and in accordance with programme and airworthiness requirements.

**Your Day-to-Day:**

-

Lead the end-to-end propulsion system project from concept through certification and industrialisation

-

Develop and maintain integrated project plans, schedules, budgets, and resource forecasts

-

Coordinate propulsion system design, development, testing, and integration activities across internal teams and suppliers

-

Drive technical reviews, programme milestones, risk management, and change control processes

-

Track technical maturity, project performance, and key deliverables against programme objectives

-

Manage external partners and suppliers, ensuring delivery to scope, schedule, quality, and cost targets

-

Facilitate alignment between propulsion, systems, certification, manufacturing, and supply chain teams

-

Support verification, validation, and certification activities for the propulsion system

-

Proactively identify and resolve technical, programme, and organisational risks

-

Provide clear status reporting and decision support to leadership and stakeholders

**Your Profile:**

-

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Aerospace, Mechanical, Electrical, Systems Engineering, or a related field

-

Several years of project management experience in aerospace, aviation, automotive, or another safety-critical industry

-

Proven experience managing complex multidisciplinary engineering development projects

-

Strong understanding of systems engineering, product development, verification, and configuration management

-

Experience coordinating internal stakeholders, suppliers, and external development partners

-

Excellent planning, communication, risk management, and stakeholder management skills

-

Knowledge of electric propulsion, powertrain, high-voltage, or aerospace systems is desirable

-

Familiarity with aerospace certification processes is advantageous

-

Fluent in English; German is a plus

-

Proficiency with project management and product lifecycle management tools
