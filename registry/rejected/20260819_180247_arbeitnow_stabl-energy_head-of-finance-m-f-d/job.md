## Your Role

We are looking for an experienced **Head of Finance** to build and develop our Finance organization and support STABL Energy through its next phase of growth.

Working closely with the CFO and leadership team, you will strengthen financial planning, forecasting, business controlling, cash management, reporting, and Finance processes. You will combine strategic thinking with a hands-on approach and use financial insights to support better business decisions across the organization.

This is a business-focused Finance leadership role with significant ownership and the opportunity to shape the future Finance function at STABL.

## Your mission

- Lead **FP&A**, including budgeting, forecasting, long-term planning, and financial modelling

- Build transparency around business performance through meaningful KPIs, reporting, and profitability analysis

- Act as a **Finance Business Partner** to Sales, Operations, Supply Chain, Engineering, and the leadership team

- Own cash flow forecasting, liquidity planning, and working capital management

- Support the CFO with management and board reporting, fundraising, financing activities, and strategic finance topics

- Drive the development, digitalization, and automation of Finance processes, systems, and reporting

- Lead and develop the Finance team and help shape a scalable Finance organization for STABL’s next stage of growth

## Your profile

- 7+ years of relevant experience in **FP&A, Corporate Finance, Business Controlling, or Finance Management**

- Strong expertise in financial planning, forecasting, financial modelling, and performance management

- Experience with cash flow management and working capital

- Strong analytical and business partnering skills, with the ability to turn financial data into actionable insights

- Experience working with senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams

- Strong Excel skills and experience with BI and ERP systems

- Strategic thinker with a hands-on and entrepreneurial mindset

- Excellent German and English communication skills and the ability to build structures in a fast-growing environment
Experience in **manufacturing, industrial, energy, renewable energy, or technology environments** is a plus.

A degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field is preferred.
