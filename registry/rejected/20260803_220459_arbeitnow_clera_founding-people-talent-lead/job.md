### About the Role

A fast-growing, seed-stage AI company in Munich is hiring a **Founding People & Talent Lead** to build the Talent and People function from the ground up. This is a high-impact, hands-on founding role working directly with company leadership to solve one of the most critical scaling challenges in a high-growth AI enterprise software business targeting a large, underserved market in the DACH region and beyond.

You'll own end-to-end recruiting while progressively shaping how the company onboards, develops, and retains exceptional people. If you thrive in high-agency environments, enjoy building systems from scratch, and want to grow into a broader People leadership role, this is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Build and own the full hiring engine — leading recruiting end-to-end from role definition through sourcing, assessment, and closing offers.

-

Spend the majority of your early time on Talent, sourcing and closing exceptional candidates across all functions.

-

Design and scale recruiting processes, analytics, workflows, and operating rhythms that enable fast, high-quality hiring.

-

Partner with founders and team leads to define role expectations, calibrate hiring decisions, and challenge org design.

-

Build out People systems over time — including onboarding, performance calibration, career progression frameworks, and compensation benchmarking.

-

Develop and own employer branding to attract top talent across the DACH region and internationally.

-

Build and nurture a talent network across universities, startups, and technical communities.

-

Leverage AI tools and experiment with new workflows to increase recruiting velocity and quality.

-

Uphold a high bar for hiring excellence and maintain standards as the team scales.

-

Contribute actively to company culture, hiring for long-term retention and growth.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

2–5 years of experience with meaningful time in Talent, People, or org development at a high-growth startup or scaleup.

-

Demonstrated experience leading end-to-end recruitment across all functions (role definition, sourcing, assessment, closing) in a high-growth environment — ideally through a hypergrowth phase (e.g., 10→50 or 30→100 people).

-

Proven ability to build scalable recruiting systems, processes, workflows, and analytics independently.

-

Strong storytelling and relationship-building skills with a track record of sourcing and closing senior candidates.

-

Daily use of AI tools and a genuine curiosity to experiment with new recruiting workflows.

-

Ability to build employer branding and a talent network to attract top candidates in the DACH region and beyond.

-

Fluency in English.

-

Comfort with ambiguity, high agency, and on-site work in Munich.

**Nice to Have:**

-

German language skills (a meaningful plus for this market).

-

Experience with performance calibration and compensation benchmarking as part of scaling People operations.

-

Background in enterprise software, AI, or B2B SaaS environments.

### Location

This role is based **on-site in Munich, Germany**. Candidates must be willing to work from the Munich office full-time. **Visa sponsorship is not available** — candidates must already have the right to work in Germany.
