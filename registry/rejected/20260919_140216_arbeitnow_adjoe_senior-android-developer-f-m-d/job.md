**Where 50 Billion Daily Predictions Begin
**
The User Experience team is the origin point of adjoe Ads. Everything the system knows about a user, every prediction the ML models make, every bid placed in an auction. It all starts with code this team ships.

The team owns two responsibilities. On the SDK side, a native **Kotlin** and **Swift **code runs inside hundreds of publisher apps. It participates in **120 million daily auctions**, renders ad creatives across multiple formats, and captures the behavioral signals that power** 50 billion predictions per day**. The constraint that defines this work: you're executing inside someone else's app, on someone else's device, with zero tolerance for instability. Every ad impression has to feel native and fast to the user.

On the analytics side, the dashboards, investigation tools, and data frameworks that turn raw data into decisions: real-time revenue performance across active campaigns, infrastructure cost visibility, and data quality assessments. If a number matters to the business or to engineering, this team makes it accessible.

**Your Mission & Who We Are Looking For:**

-

**Kotlin & Android Internals Expertise:** You will own the core development and optimization of the adjoe Ads SDK, which runs inside hundreds of publisher apps. -

**High-Performance Network Design:** You will collaborate with backend teams to design APIs and capture behavioral signals that power 50 billion daily ML predictions. -

**Resource-Constrained Optimization:** You will ensure every ad impression feels native and fast while participating in 120 million daily auctions. -

**Build Automation & Delivery:** You will maintain and improve our CI/CD pipelines to guarantee efficient, reliable SDK delivery. This requires hands-on experience with Gradle and GitLab CI to automate the testing, building, and packaging of our mobile libraries.

-

**Plus – AdTech & Low-Level Systems:** It is a major plus if you bring experience in ad-tech or programmatic advertising (aligning products with IAB specifications). Additionally, experience with NDK/JNI (C/C++ interop on Android) or parallel iOS development experience is highly valued to help support our cross-platform ecosystem.
