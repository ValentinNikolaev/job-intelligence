### About the Role

You will be the primary technical partner for the most strategic enterprise accounts at a fast-growing, engineering-led AI observability platform — think Datadog, but purpose-built for LLM applications and agents. Your mandate is straightforward: make the best customers impossible to churn.

You'll drive net revenue retention through deep technical engagement, proactive risk management, and a tight feedback loop back to product. You understand AI architectures well enough to have opinions, you write documentation that engineers actually follow, and you close loops rather than escalate them.

### What You'll Do

-

**Own 10–20 strategic accounts** — lead onboarding, build success plans, conduct deep-dives into customer AI architectures, and drive adoption and expansion across the full lifecycle.

-

**Guide production readiness** — advise on instrumentation strategy, evaluation setup, data modeling, security and privacy configurations, and operational playbooks.

-

**Troubleshoot and unblock** — debug SDK/API integrations, resolve production issues, support self-hosted deployments, and be the first technical escalation point before engineering.

-

**Create feedback loops** — synthesize patterns across accounts into actionable product insights; work cross-functionally with engineering and product teams to influence the roadmap.

-

**Enable at scale** — produce technical content (guides, runbooks, integration examples) that multiplies your impact across the full customer base, including open-source community contributors.

-

**Monitor account health** — track adoption signals, usage telemetry, and risk indicators to intervene proactively before problems become churn.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

5+ years in technical account management, solutions engineering, or customer-success engineering supporting enterprise SaaS or open-source software customers.

-

Hands-on experience supporting production LLM/AI applications or developer-facing AI platforms — including integrations, prompt management, model evaluation, and production troubleshooting.

-

Solid grounding in observability, tracing, and monitoring for distributed systems or ML services (distributed tracing, metrics, logging, APM) and the ability to apply those signals to reduce customer risk.

-

Proven track record of driving customer adoption and improving NRR through technical enablement and proactive, outcome-focused engagements — be ready to share metrics and examples.

-

Ability to troubleshoot SDK and API integrations, debug production issues, and support self-hosted/on-prem deployments (logs, incident response, etc.).

-

Familiarity with ClickHouse or other columnar/analytics databases; experience working with large-scale telemetry or metrics data.

-

Fluent English with strong written and verbal communication skills — equally comfortable presenting to engineers and to executive stakeholders.

-

Experience collaborating cross-functionally with engineering and product teams in a small, engineering-heavy organisation.

-

**Right to work in the EU without visa sponsorship** (sponsorship is not available).

-

Availability to work EU timezones and willingness to spend approximately one week per month on-site in Berlin.

**Nice to Have**

-

Experience engaging with open-source communities at scale — issue triage, community support, documentation, and evangelism.

-

Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes) and experience supporting customer deployments in cloud or on-prem environments.

-

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field (or equivalent practical experience).
