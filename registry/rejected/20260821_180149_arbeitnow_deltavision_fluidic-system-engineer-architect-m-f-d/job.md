**Deine Mission**

As a full-time member of our Orbital Systems team in Munich/Germany, you will work on defining the architecture of fluidic systems for spacecraft propulsion and other functions, working closely with a multi-disciplinary team. We work on projects ranging from launcher propulsion systems to orbital refuelling systems.
**
Your role will include:**

- Defining fluidic systems' architectures, working closely with other domain architects
- Performing fluidic systems' simulations (performance calculations, steady and transient CFD, thermal and others)
- Systems Engineering, incl. Requirements Engineering and MBSE
- Technical leadership
- Driving MAIT, incl. hands-on support
- Co-ordinating design and mechanical engineers
- Direct interfacing with customers and suppliers
- Supporting proposal preparation

**Dein Profil**

We are seeking engineers with demonstrated expertise in fluidic systems. Candidates with 3+ years of relevant experience are encouraged to apply. We are also interested in more experienced professionals with 10+ years of experience in fluidic system architecture and leadership. The position level, scope, and compensation will be determined based on the candidate's experience and demonstrated impact.

Ideally, your experience includes:

- Knowledge of space systems and subsystems
- Propulsion systems architecture, including HTP and/or hydrazine/MMH
- Propellants ground handling experience including safety requirements
- Fluidic system simulations, with knowledge of ECOSIM / ESPSS, Simulink, Matlab
- Familiarity with MBSE concepts and requirements engineering principles; ECSS
- Strong analytical and communication skills
- Hands-on mindset for hardware related activities
- Proactive, structured, and able to work independently and in a team
