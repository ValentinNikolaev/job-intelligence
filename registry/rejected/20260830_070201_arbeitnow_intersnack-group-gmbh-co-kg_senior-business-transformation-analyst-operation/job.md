#### **Why This Role Matters**

As a **Senior Business Transformation Analyst Operations Execution**, you will deliver transformation projects within the Transformation Management sub-department, working independently in at least one functional area. You will apply expertise in project management, process mapping, and solution design, support the implementation of Business Transformation’s methodologies and tools, and drive process improvements and IT solutions for one end-to-end process.

As part of the Operations Execution team, you will contribute to driving projects that optimize manufacturing, supply chain, and order fulfilment processes. You will help enhance operational effectiveness and efficiency for downstream supply chain.

#### Working with us

Intersnack has become one of Europe’s leading savoury snacks producers by ‘creating happy snacking moments’ in people’s lives. Being privately owned, we operate with a long-term view and commit ourselves to a more sustainable world.

Successful and sustainably growing, we generated sales of more than 4.6 billion euros in 2025. We now have 12 regional Management Units with more than 40 production sites in Europe, Asia, Australia and New Zealand and a total workforce of more than 15,000 people.
