## Tasks

Option A – Regulatory Reporting

- Prepare and submit regulatory and statistical reports in accordance with

- German and European banking regulations including reports tot eh relevant

- deposit protection scheme.

- Ensure reporting accuracy and compliance with regulatory deadlines.

- Validate and reconcile financial data between banking systems and regulatory

- reporting systems.

- Monitor developments in CRR, CRD VI and other regulatory requirements

- and advise the senior management in this regard.

- Support implementation of new regulatory reporting requirements.

- Cooperate with the IT team to maintain the requisite reporting systems and

- access to reporting platforms from relevant supervisory authorities.

- Respond to external and internal audit requests related to regulatory matters.

- Liaise with supervisory authorities and external service providers where

- required.

Option B –Taxation

- Preparation of annual accounts under German GAAP

- Prepare tax calculations, tax returns and tax filings under German tax law.

- Support corporate tax, trade tax and VAT compliance.

- Monitor withholding tax obligations relating to cross-border transactions.

- Support transfer pricing documentation.

- Assist with implementation of OECD Pillar Two and other present and

- upcoming, national and international requirements.

- Coordinate tax audits and communicate with tax authorities and external tax

- advisers.

- Respond to external and internal audit requests related to accounting and tax

- matters.
