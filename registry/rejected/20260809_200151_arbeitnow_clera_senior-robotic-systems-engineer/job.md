### About the Role

This is a **foundational senior engineering hire** at an early-stage industrial robotics startup in Munich. You will own the core robotics software stack end-to-end — from motion planning and control through to real-world deployment of an intelligent robotic work cell built for the hardest manual tasks in industry: **surface finishing, welding, and coating**.

This is a high-ownership, hands-on role for someone who thrives on turning real hardware into reliable, production-grade systems. You'll shape architecture decisions from day one on a small, ambitious team pushing the boundaries of industrial automation.

### What You'll Do

-

Own and evolve the core robotics software stack of the work cell

-

Develop and optimize motion planning, trajectory generation, and control algorithms for industrial robot arms

-

Integrate perception systems — cameras, force/torque sensors, LiDAR — into real-time control loops

-

Implement industrial safety concepts and fail-safe behaviors

-

Lead on-site testing, calibration, and commissioning activities

-

Drive system architecture decisions and establish engineering best practices

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

5+ years of hands-on experience in robotics engineering, with a strong focus on **real-world hardware** (simulation-only backgrounds are not a fit)

-

Demonstrated experience with motion planning and trajectory generation for industrial robot arms in production environments (e.g. KUKA, ABB, Fanuc, Universal Robots)

-

Proficiency in **C++** and/or **Python**

-

Experience with **ROS 2**

-

Hands-on integration of force/torque sensors and/or vision systems

-

Solid grounding in kinematics, dynamics, and control theory

-

Eligible to work in Germany — **visa sponsorship is not available**

**Nice to Have:**

-

Background in surface finishing, welding, or industrial coating applications

-

Experience with **3D CAD** tools (SolidWorks, CATIA)

-

Familiarity with industrial communication protocols such as **EtherCAT** or **OPC-UA**

-

German language skills (not required)
