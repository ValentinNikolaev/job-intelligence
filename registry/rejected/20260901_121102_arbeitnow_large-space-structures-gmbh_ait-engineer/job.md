# AIT Engineer

Posted: 2026-09-01T10:30:34Z

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement
- Reason: vacancy is explicitly non-remote

Would you like to work on one of the most exciting space projects (Conical Imaging Microwave Radiometer for the Copernicus Mission) and be directly involved in assembling, integrating, and testing flight hardware bound for orbit? Are you driven by hands-on challenges and precision engineering? Do you want to be part of a diverse, dynamic team that shares your passion for space?

Then LSS is the right place for you!

We are at the forefront of European innovation in deployable antenna reflector technologies, serving both domestic and international markets. We’re now looking for a dedicated and detail-oriented **AIT Engineer (m/w/d)** to join our growing team of space professionals.

As an AIT Engineer at LSS, you’ll play a vital role in bringing our cutting-edge space systems to life — from cleanroom integration to rigorous testing that ensures mission success. This is your chance to work on high-impact space hardware, contribute to groundbreaking missions, and be part of a collaborative, future-focused environment. If you’re ready to combine your technical skills with your passion for space, we invite you to join us and help shape the next generation of space exploration.

## Tasks

- Working as part of the AIT team

- Preparation, execution, and documentation of AIT activities

- Use and support ISO8 conditions during the entire AIT process

- Assembly, integration, and testing of qualification and flight hardware according to AIT procedures and general AIT practices

- Define and prepare integration of thermistors, thermostats, other thermal hardware.

- Responsible operation of cranes and MGSE.

- Preparation of procedures for AIT operations

- Preparation of technical reports and presentations for and with the AIT team.

- Interact with project partners (internal and external) in the coordination and execution of AIT activities at LSS

- Contribution to continuous improvement actions aiming at the increase of performance and efficiency of AIT operations according to business needs.

## Requirements

- [M.Sc](http://M.Sc). or PhD in a relevant field and/or certification or similar within space or aerospace.

- 3+ years’ experience in space manufacturing, engineering, or prototype environment.

- 5+ years management experience within a production environment.

- Excellent communication skills in English (oral and written), and German is beneficial.

- Excellent time management skills to meet tight deadlines and demanding expectations of the projects.

- Ability to learn quickly and understand highly complex designs.

- Solution-oriented working style combined with ability to work in a team

- Detail oriented individual with excellent communication skills.

- Familiarity with ESA and ECSS standards is highly desirable.

- Willingness to travel to various test centres around Europe.
