## **JOB PURPOSE**

*This role is part of our ****Experience & Engineering**** team in Germany. We combine technology and experience design to create emotive yet conversion-focused ideas, products, and campaigns at scale and speed. Our specialty is digital experiences and communication that change perceptions and shape new behaviours to make a difference in the market.*

In this key leadership position, you are responsible for the strategic direction and growth of our Engineering teams. You will lead a team of approximately 7-9 professionals, including **Team Leads and Senior Engineers.** Your focus shifts from writing code to **building systems**: you will optimize processes, develop the next generation of leaders, and actively drive the cultural change toward **AI-driven development**.

## **WHAT YOU’LL DO:**

- **Leadership of Leaders: **You lead, coach, and mentor Team Leads and Senior Engineers. You own their career paths and empower them to lead their own sub-teams to excellence.

- **AI Change Management: **You define the roadmap for integrating Artificial Intelligence into the daily engineering lifecycle (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Agentic Workflows). You act as a change agent, transforming our workflows for maximum efficiency and innovation.

- **Operational Excellence: **You take full ownership of your department's operational performance (resource planning, budget monitoring, utilization) and ensure a profitable and scalable engineering organization.

- **Strategic Partnering: **You work closely with Directors and VPs to sharpen DEPT®’s technological vision in the DACH region and act as a high-level technical sparring partner for our key accounts.

- **Cultural Stewardship: **You foster an inclusive, motivating, high-performance culture and establish modern department rituals that promote knowledge sharing and innovation.

## **WHAT YOU BRING:**

- **Proven People Manager: **You have a track record of leading teams in a "manager-of-managers" structure or significant experience leading larger engineering departments.

- **Change Agent:** You are passionate about the potential of AI in the software lifecycle and have a clear vision of how modern engineering organizations must evolve.

- **Frontend Background:** You possess a deep technical history in frontend development, allowing you to validate architectural decisions and act as a mentor on eye-level.

- **Business Acumen:** You understand the economic drivers of an agency or consultancy and can translate KPIs into actionable success.

- **Communication: **You are a confident communicator in both German and English, able to present complex technical topics to both stakeholders and clients.
