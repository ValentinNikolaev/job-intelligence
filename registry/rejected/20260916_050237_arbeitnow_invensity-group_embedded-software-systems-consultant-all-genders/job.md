- **Per****sonal and professional development**: Grow in a technologically leading environment, build connections, and help drive innovation across a wide variety of industries

**Your profile**

- **A successfully completed degree in computer science, electrical engineering, communications and information technology**, or a comparable field
- **Several years of professional experience **in** hardware-oriented embedded software development** using C/C++ under real-time operating systems (RTOS), as well as in-depth knowledge of Python and Git-based development environments
- **In-depth knowledge **of** Ethernet-based communication protocols** such as TCP/IP, TLS, and PTP; ideally supplemented by **experience with industry standards** such as IEEE 1588, TSN, and X.509
- Knowledge of C#, SQL, GoogleTest, and Jenkins, as well as experience with Azure DevOps/Pipelines, Visual Studio Code, or modern CI/CD processes, is a plus
- **Strong conceptual and analytical skills**, as well as the ability to communicate complex technical issues to various stakeholders in a way that is tailored to their needs, understandable, and practical
