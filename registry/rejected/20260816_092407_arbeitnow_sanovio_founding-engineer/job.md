**About the Role**

As a **Founding Engineer** at SANOVIO, you will play a key role in building the AI-powered procurement infrastructure that tackles one of the biggest and most complex cost problems in European healthcare.

This is a fast-paced, hands-on role for engineers with **high agency**, strong technical depth, and a founder mindset. We’re looking for a **full-stack generalist** with a strong **frontend affinity**. You excel at **designing user flows** and implementing them in **React and TypeScript**, while owning outcomes end-to-end across **backend, data, AI-enabled workflows, and cloud deployment**. You thrive amid **uncertainty and incomplete requirements**, move quickly, and still care deeply about **architecture, clean code, and long-term quality**.

If you are a passionate builder who thrives at the intersection of full-stack development and AI, this is your opportunity to work closely with the founders, shape the product, and help us scale toward **€10M ARR in 2026**.

**About the interview process**

**Step 1: Intro call**

Casual intro meeting with one of the founders to get to know each other and introduce SANOVIO.

**Step 2: Technical interview**

The technical interview consists of two parts:

-

**A take-home exercise, including a short presentation**

-

**A system design interview**

For the take-home exercise, we’d provide you with a task. You’ll have 2–3 hours to complete it, focusing on a real SANOVIO workflow and user flow in React and TypeScript, with a light end-to-end angle such as API integration, data modeling, edge cases, and clean architecture. Later, you’ll present your results to us.

**Step 3: Personal Fit**

Meet another co-founder to get a feel for our culture and working style.

**Step 4: Offer & Reference Checks**

If we see a great fit, you will get an offer, including a competitive compensation package. At the same time, we’ll ask you to provide 2–3 references so we can gather additional insights into your strengths and working style.
