As a **Senior Controller – Reporting & Insights**, you will own and continuously elevate the company’s **management reporting and insight generation**. Your mission is to transform financial and operational data into **clear narratives, actionable insights, and decision-ready reporting** for senior leadership.

You will be responsible for building and refining **state-of-the-art reporting standards**, ensuring consistency, transparency, and relevance across all outputs. This role goes beyond compiling numbers — you will **connect the dots across the business**, challenge assumptions, and provide the “so what” behind performance.

Working closely with FP&A, Accounting, and business stakeholders, you will ensure that reporting becomes a **true steering tool**, not just a retrospective exercise.

#### **What is your Day to Day Mission:**

**Management Reporting & Storytelling**

-

Own and continuously improve the **monthly management reporting**, ensuring clarity, accuracy, and relevance

-

Drive the **collection, consolidation, and quality of business commentary** across all functions

-

Translate financial and operational results into a **clear, structured narrative**, highlighting key drivers, risks, and opportunities

-

Provide the **“so what” behind the numbers**, turning data into actionable insights for leadership

-

Prepare high-quality **management and board-level presentations**

**Insights & Performance Transparency**

-

Identify trends, patterns, and deviations across financial and operational data

-

Generate **deep-dive analyses** and ad-hoc insights on business performance

-

Act as a central point for **cross-functional insight generation**, connecting data across departments

-

Ensure full transparency on business performance, enabling fact-based decision-making

**Operational Excellence & Automation**

-

Continuously improve **reporting processes, structures, and outputs** towards best-in-class standards

-

Drive **automation and standardisation** of reporting workflows to reduce manual effort and increase speed

-

Collaborate with BI and data teams to enhance reporting capabilities and data models

-

Ensure data consistency and integrity across all reporting outputs

**Stakeholder Collaboration**

-

Work closely with FP&A, Accounting, and business teams to align on data, definitions, and messaging

-

Challenge and refine inputs from stakeholders to ensure high-quality, decision-relevant reporting

-

Act as a trusted partner to senior management for performance analysis and communication
