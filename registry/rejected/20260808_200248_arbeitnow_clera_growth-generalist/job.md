### About the Role

Join a fast-moving, Berlin-based B2B performance marketing and lead generation agency that helps companies across industries acquire more qualified leads and customers. In this role, you won't just advise — you'll develop growth strategies **and execute them yourself**, using proprietary in-house tools built specifically to outpace anything available on the open market.

You'll work across a wide variety of business models and markets, gaining rare behind-the-scenes access to how diverse companies grow — and directly contributing to their results.

### What You'll Do

-

Develop and execute multichannel growth strategies for B2B clients across industries

-

Run outbound and performance marketing campaigns across channels such as LinkedIn, Google, and email

-

Build and manage automations using tools like Clay, Make, and OpenAI-powered workflows

-

Integrate with and optimize client CRM systems (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive)

-

Build reporting infrastructure to track pipeline value, lead quality, and campaign ROI

-

Use signal-based outreach techniques to generate qualified first meetings for clients

-

Challenge existing strategies, identify key growth levers, and drive measurable outcomes
