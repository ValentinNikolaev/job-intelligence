### **Our Story**

At Endear, we’re building the modern CRM for retail teams — starting with the frontline.

Our software helps retail teams build stronger customer relationships through thoughtful, intuitive technology. From clienteling and customer capture to appointments and in-store experiences, Endear gives brands the tools they need to create more personal interactions and drive measurable growth.

We work with brands like Allbirds, Reformation, and Alo Yoga, and we’re continuing to evolve Endear into an increasingly AI-first product built around how modern retail teams actually work.

### **Position Overview**

We’re looking for a Product Designer to join our IRL (in real life) team, which owns the in-store side of Endear — including appointments, events, customer check-ins, and the administrative experiences behind those workflows.

You’ll design for store associates and managers running clienteling day to day, as well as admin users who help shape how those teams operate and manage their workflows.

In this role, you’ll own features and projects from understanding the problem through research, testing, iteration, and production-ready design, while partnering closely with our Head of Product Design on vision and direction.

The IRL roadmap is early. Many of these features don’t exist yet, so you’ll be defining problems through research before designing solutions.

This role is well suited for someone comfortable creating clarity when the solution isn’t obvious.

-

### **About the Team**

Endear’s product organization is structured across three product teams: AI, IRL, and Core Flows, with one designer dedicated to each.

As the IRL designer, you’ll work closely with a Product Manager and 2–3 Engineers while also being part of Endear’s three-person design team. You’ll report to the Head of Product Design, who will provide vision, direction, mentorship, and support as you grow in the role.

You’ll partner most closely with:

-

**Head of Product Design: **vision, direction, mentorship, feedback, and design leadership

-

**Product Team:** roadmap alignment, priorities, customer needs, and business context

-

**Engineering Team:** feasibility, implementation, and iteration

You’ll have access to mentorship, an education budget, and support around Endear’s AI processes to help you continue developing your craft and expanding how you use AI in your work.

We’re a small team with big ambitions — collaborative, customer-obsessed, and deeply excited about the problems we’re solving in retail.
