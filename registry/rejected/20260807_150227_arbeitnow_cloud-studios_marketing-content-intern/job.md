# Marketing & Content Intern

Posted: 2026-08-07T13:30:31Z

## Rejection

- Category: tech_stack
- Reason: role does not mention Go/Golang or PHP

Cloud Studios **turns raw ideas into products people actually rely on, then builds the strongest ones into real, independent businesses.**

Our current priority is an AI visibility platform helping companies understand and improve how they appear across platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

We are looking for a **creative and proactive Marketing Intern** to help us build the content engine around Cloud Studios, our products and the founder behind them.

You will **work directly with the founder** and take real ownership of how we communicate across LinkedIn, Instagram and potentially newsletters.

The goal is not simply to “post more content”. We want to build a system that turns ideas, insights and what is happening inside the company into content that **builds attention, trust and ultimately generates opportunities for the business**.

## Tasks

- Support and grow the content presence of Cloud Studios, our products and the founder

- Create content for **LinkedIn and Instagram**, including posts, carousels and graphics

- Turn ideas, conversations and product developments into clear, engaging content

- Build a **content waterfall system** that repurposes one strong idea into multiple formats and channels

- Create visual content using Figma, Canva and existing templates

- Support newsletters and other longer-form content

- Track what performs, improve future content and help turn attention into leads

**Bonus:** If you enjoy short-form video, you can also help capture and edit behind-the-scenes content from the team and founder.

## Requirements

- Currently enrolled at a university or similar programme where a mandatory internship is part of your studies

- Strong written English

- Good understanding of **LinkedIn, Instagram and digital content**

- Able to turn rough ideas into clear, engaging content

- Good visual judgement and comfortable using Figma, Canva or similar tools

- Proactive, reliable and comfortable taking ownership

- Curious about AI, startups and using new tools to work faster and better

## Benefits

- Work **directly with the founder** and have real ownership from day one

- Create content that is actually published and used to grow the business

- Learn how **founder-led marketing, content and lead generation** work in practice

- Gain hands-on experience with AI-native marketing workflows and tools

- Work closely with product, sales and growth inside an early-stage company

- Flexible hybrid setup with Berlin as the preferred base

- Opportunity to capture real behind-the-scenes startup content rather than relying purely on stock or templated material

- Strong performance may lead to a **paid working-student or ongoing role** after the internship

If you enjoy turning ideas into content, have a good instinct for what makes people stop scrolling and want to understand how content can actually contribute to building a company, we would like to hear from you.

We are not looking for someone who just wants to follow a social media calendar.

We want someone who will eventually hear an interesting idea in a meeting, notice something happening inside the company or see the founder building something and immediately think:

**“There is a piece of content in that.”**

Please send us:

- A short introduction

- Something you have created that you are proud of - this can be content, design, a personal project or something completely different

- A LinkedIn or Instagram account whose content you think is particularly good, and why
