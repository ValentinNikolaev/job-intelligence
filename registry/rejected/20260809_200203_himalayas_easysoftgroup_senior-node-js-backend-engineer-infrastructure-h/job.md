- Design, build, and maintain high-load backend systems capable of handling large-scale traffic and ensuring high availability.

- Participate in technical discussions and contribute to architectural decisions around backend services, infrastructure, and platform scalability.

- Degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related technical field.

- **7+ years of commercial backend development experience.**

- Strong expertise in Node.js and TypeScript.

- Hands-on experience with MongoDB and Redis.

- **Proven track record of building and supporting high-load production systems.**

- Solid understanding of backend architecture, API design, and scalable system design principles.

- **Hands-on experience with infrastructure setup, configuration, and support in cloud environments.**

- **Experience contributing to architectural decisions for scalable and reliable backend systems.**

- Strong debugging, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.

- Ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.

**Nice to Have**

**We Value**
