**Location: Remote — Europe or US East Coast (Virginia, New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Florida only)**

🎯 Your main goal will be to build n8n's global revenue enablement engine — installing one enterprise selling methodology across every region, compressing ramp for every revenue team worldwide, and turning our community-led momentum into repeatable 7–8 figure enterprise revenue.

You'll report directly to our senior commercial leadership, inherit a small enablement team with basic onboarding in place, and have the mandate (and explicit VP trajectory) to build the function from the ground up: for a remote-first company selling across EMEA, the US, and beyond.

To do so, here are your responsibilities:

### METHODOLOGY & ENTERPRISE SELLING EXCELLENCE

-

Own the global install of one enterprise methodology (MEDDICC / value-selling framework): selection, rollout, certification, and continuous reinforcement across every rep and manager, in every region.

-

Build the PLG-to-enterprise playbook: teach sellers to convert community and self-serve signals into qualified 7-figure pipeline.

-

Partner with PMM and Product to arm the field with AI-led narratives, competitive plays, and launch enablement.

### RAMP, ONBOARDING & MANAGER DEVELOPMENT

-

Build one global onboarding engine, pre-boarding through certification to first-deal support, and compress time-to-quota for hiring waves in every region.

-

Develop frontline sales managers: coaching frameworks, deal inspection, and forecast rigor that make the methodology stick.

-

Define enablement's scorecard in revenue terms: ramp time, win rate, forecast accuracy, not course completions.

### BUILDING THE GLOBAL FUNCTION

-

Lead and grow the enablement team (starting with 1–2 ICs) across sales, SDR, CS, and partner enablement.

-

Stand up the enablement infrastructure: tooling, content systems, certification tracking, and measurement with RevOps.

-

Operate as a trusted peer to regional sales leaders across EMEA, the US, and future markets: in deal rooms, QBRs, and forecast reviews.
