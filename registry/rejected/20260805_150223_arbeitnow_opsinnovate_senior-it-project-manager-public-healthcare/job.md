We are recruiting on behalf of our client for a Senior IT Project Manager to support a large-scale digital transformation programme within Germany's public healthcare sector. The programme focuses on acute and emergency care digitalisation, including cross-sector care, telemedicine, emergency assessment (SmED), eAppointment services, and the integration of emergency control centres.

## Tasks

- Lead multiple sub-projects in parallel within the acute and emergency care programme.

- Build or adapt a stable, flexible and integrated IT service architecture that enables the changed business processes, with explicit service targets on cost and benefit, user acceptance, scalability, and compliance with information security and data protection requirements.

- Run tender and procurement processes and manage external providers.

- Maintain continuous alignment with business stakeholders, the programme management function, and external project partners.

- Create the conditions for permanent operation and support of the IT services, including enabling internal IT staff and end users.
