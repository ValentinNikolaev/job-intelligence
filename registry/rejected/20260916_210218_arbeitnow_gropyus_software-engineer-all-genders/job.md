**About The Company**

GROPYUS is a technology-based construction company focused on building multi-story residential buildings. Thanks to its prefabricated building system with various design options, industrial offsite construction, and fully digitalized processes, the company manufactures aspirational, sustainable, and affordable homes using timber construction methods. GROPYUS is using scalable construction and manufacturing solutions to tap into a future market, boost Europe's strength in innovation, while also playing a substantial role in improving sustainability.

## **About the Role:**

As a Software Engineer (f/m/d), you will be part of a highly collaborative product vertical working at the cutting-edge of a complex, evolving domain.

We build cross-collaborative and diverse teams that focus on products and are not tethered by ancient legacy systems or reporting structures. In this role you’ll help us design, build and maintain our Kotlin services and React applications. You’ll be working on a git-like version control system to allow architects and structural engineers to manage system rules and other components of GROPYUS’s Building Systems.

We live proactive, agile, and test-driven development and value automation not just in CI/CD but in every aspect of work. GROPYUS is the right home for you if you want to build products that profoundly impact people’s lives, if you value sustainability and take ownership.

## **What you bring:**

- Experience with Kotlin or Java in a professional environment

- Cloud-native experience: Hands-on experience working with applications on Azure, GCP, AWS, or a similar cloud platform.

- Experience with React and Typescript in a professional environment

- Experience with relational databases (e.g. PostgreSQL).

- A plus if you had experience with Spring Boot

- End-to-end software ownership: Experience designing, building, running, and maintaining production software.

- Understanding of modern engineering practices (e.g. CI/CD, Microservices) and experience or interest in pair programming.

- Collaboration & planning: Interest in contributing to team roadmap and sprint planning.

- Curiosity and openness to learn about new Domains and Business Cases and to work across the whole technology stack (Frontend, Backend, Infrastructure)

- You have experience with or are interested in leveraging state-of-the-art AI tooling to support your development workflows
