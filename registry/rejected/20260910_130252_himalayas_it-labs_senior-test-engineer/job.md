### Responsibilities

- Design, document, and execute comprehensive automated testing strategies spanning **APIs**, frontend applications, backend services, and data flows

- Create and maintain reliable automated test scripts using frameworks such as **Selenium**, **Playwright**, or **Cypress**

- Develop verification frameworks for validating LLM prompt behaviors, model outputs, response consistency, and hallucination rates

- Execute system integration testing, regression testing, and performance/load testing ahead of platform releases

- Validate end-to-end workflows and automated actions against defined business logic, system behavior, and performance SLAs

- Document detailed bug reports, analyze issues to identify root causes, and collaborate closely with developers on resolution

- Contribute to quality standards, test coverage practices, release readiness processes, and continuous improvement of QA methodologies

- Integrate test automation into CI/CD pipelines and support quality assurance across cloud-hosted applications and services

### Required Skills & Experience

-
**5–10 years** of experience in **QA engineering**, **test automation**, or **software quality assurance**

- Strong experience designing and implementing automated test strategies for complex web applications and distributed systems

- Hands-on expertise with test automation frameworks such as **Selenium**, **Playwright**, or **Cypress**

- Experience testing **APIs**, frontend interfaces, backend workflows, and end-to-end system integrations

- Familiarity with performance testing, regression testing, and release validation in enterprise environments

- Experience validating **AI/LLM-driven systems**, including prompt behavior, output quality, and response consistency, is strongly preferred

- Hands-on experience with **Git**, **CI/CD pipelines**, defect tracking, and automated test reporting

- Prior engagement in large-scale enterprise environments and mission-critical systems

- Candidate **must be located in an EU member country**

- Excellent communication skills in **English**
