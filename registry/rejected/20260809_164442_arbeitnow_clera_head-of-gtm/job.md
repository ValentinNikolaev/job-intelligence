### About the Role

An early-stage ambient AI / personal productivity startup is hiring a **Head of GTM** to fully own go-to-market strategy and brand — from funnel logic to narrative — across both strategy and execution. This is a founding-level, **player/coach role** that is critical to launching and scaling the product, driving acquisition, and building market presence from zero.

The company is building an ambient intelligence system — combining hardware, software, memory, and AI — designed to seamlessly integrate into daily life, reduce cognitive load, and help people focus on what matters most. Think of it as a continuous context layer that learns user patterns, manages follow-ups, and handles the administrative "small loops" that drain attention.

This is a **0→1 hire** for a company at early/seed stage. You will shape how the product enters the market and how the brand is perceived by early adopters.

### What You'll Do

-

Own end-to-end GTM strategy, including funnel logic, beta-to-launch sequencing, and channel mix (founder-led outreach, KOL activation, community platforms, Product Hunt, Hacker News, and more).

-

Develop the brand narrative, voice, and positioning from scratch — identifying and resonating with early-adopter audiences.

-

Build and optimize beta acquisition funnels; use conversion data to continuously reallocate effort across channels.

-

Direct and coach all individuals involved in GTM and marketing efforts in a true hands-on player/coach capacity.

-

Create a comprehensive, repeatable GTM and brand playbook for the team to leverage post-launch.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

10–12+ years of experience, with a proven track record owning end-to-end GTM execution *and* brand/positioning for a startup launch (Seed through Series B).

-

Demonstrated ability to grow a product from zero awareness and zero brand equity.

-

Experience sequencing multi-channel beta-to-launch plans and crafting compelling brand narratives.

-

Comfort with ambiguity and prior experience as a first/founding GTM hire.

-

Experience directing and coaching GTM or marketing personnel alongside doing the hands-on work yourself.

-

Ability to build repeatable playbooks for post-launch scaling.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience with consumer AI or hardware-adjacent startups.

-

Familiarity with community-led or Product-Led Growth (PLG) models.

-

Awareness of the "trust gap" dynamics around the origin of consumer tech products.

-

An existing operator network or professional following on X/LinkedIn.

-

Bilingual capabilities.

-

Understanding of "second brain" / Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) personas.

**Not a fit if:** You are a single-discipline specialist (brand-only or GTM-only), primarily a large-company operator, management-only without hands-on execution experience, or have no 0→1 startup background.
