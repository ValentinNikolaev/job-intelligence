# Business Development Manager (m/f/d)

Posted: 2026-09-17T13:00:51Z

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement
- Reason: vacancy is explicitly non-remote

To further strengthen our growth and unlock new business potential, we are looking for an entrepreneurial, strategically minded and persuasive personality to join us as soon as possible as a Business Development Manager (m/f/d). In this role, you will actively help expand our business activities with market insight, strong negotiation skills and enthusiasm for innovative energy solutions.

## Tasks

- Identifying, evaluating, qualifying and developing new business opportunities in the fields of hydrogen, energy storage and renewable energies

- Identifying and developing new customer, partner and project structures as well as maintaining long-term stakeholder relationships, including the development of formal partnerships

- Analysing markets, customer needs, competition and regulatory conditions to derive concrete growth opportunities and actions

- Developing go-to-market, partnership and market-entry strategies in national and international environments

- Supporting the acquisition, conceptualisation and commercial development of hydrogen and storage projects

- Preparing business cases, decision-making materials as well as market and profitability analyses

- Preparing and conducting customer and partner meetings, presentations and negotiations

- Working closely with Engineering, Project Management and the Management Board to implement new business approaches

- Representing RCT GH at conferences and trade fairs

## Requirements

- A completed degree in industrial engineering, energy technology, mechanical engineering, chemical or process engineering or a comparable field

- Several years of experience in business development, strategic sales, partner management or project business, ideally in the energy sector, hydrogen, gas process plants or comparable fields

- Strong understanding of complex sales cycles, capital equipment and/or process technology sales

- Sound understanding of energy markets, business models and commercial and legal interrelationships

- Interest in hydrogen technologies, renewable energies and the energy transition

- Entrepreneurial mindset, strong analytical skills and a high level of initiative

- Confident presence, strong communication skills and negotiation ability

- Independent, structured and goal-oriented way of working

- Business-fluent German and English skills as well as willingness to travel. Additional languages are a plus

- Familiarity with Salesforce or other CRM tools

## Benefits

- A varied and responsible role in a future-oriented technology environment

- The opportunity to actively contribute to innovative solutions for the energy supply of the future

- A dynamic, committed team with short decision-making paths and an open, collegial company culture

- A challenging area of responsibility with a high degree of autonomy and plenty of room to shape your work

- Attractive and performance-based remuneration

- An attractive workplace in Konstanz within a high-growth company environment

- The chance to actively help shape market expansion in hydrogen storage technology

We offer you the opportunity to apply your business development experience in a technologically demanding and forward-looking market environment, actively develop new business potential and work with us to drive the expansion of innovative hydrogen solutions.
