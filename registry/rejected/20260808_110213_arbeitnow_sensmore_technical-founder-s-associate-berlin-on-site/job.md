## Role Overview:

We are looking for a hands-on Founder's Associate to work directly with our founders in Berlin. This role suits a recent graduate who combines quantitative and economic thinking with real technical ability – the kind of person who can size a market in the morning, pull the numbers themselves in the afternoon, and spend the next day in the workshop or on-site getting hardware onto a machine. A meaningful part of this job is physical: moving machines, equipment, and hardware. You will do the work rather than coordinate it.

## Key Responsibilities:

-

Own high-priority projects end to end on behalf of the founders, from customer pilots and field deployments to internal tooling and process automation.

-

Get hands-on with hardware – preparing and assembling sensor and compute kits, moving machines and equipment, and supporting installation, testing, and demos on-site.

-

Build the analysis behind key decisions – unit economics, pricing, market sizing, and machine performance data – pulling and cleaning the data yourself.

-

Prepare material for investors, grant programmes, and customers, and work across engineering, commercial, and operations teams to unblock execution.

## Required Qualifications:

-

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Economics, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related quantitative field – a combination of the above is ideal.

-

Able to ship working software with AI coding tools such as Claude Code or Cursor – scripts, prototypes, and automations – with enough Python and SQL to review, debug, and trust what comes out.

-

Valid category B driving licence, and the willingness to drive to customer sites, transport equipment, and move machines and hardware.

-

Evidence of getting things done without supervision – a startup, a side project, a student venture, or a demanding internship.

-

Based in Berlin (or willing to relocate), happy to work on-site, and comfortable spending time in a workshop and around heavy machinery in operation.

## Preferred Skills:

-

Practical workshop experience – wiring, mechanical assembly, prototyping, or vehicle work, whether from a robotics team, Formula Student, or personal projects.

-

Exposure to robotics, autonomous systems, machine learning, or industrial hardware.

-

Experience operating vans, trailers, forklifts, or heavy equipment, or a willingness to be trained.

-

Practical experience building internal tools, dashboards, or automations with modern AI tooling.

-

Working knowledge of German, and interest in customers in mining, construction, or heavy industry.
