**About the role**

You are the engineer on the ground at our biggest DACH customers, based in Munich, working hand in hand with our Deployment Strategist.

When a multibillion-dollar company decides to go AI-native with Dataleap, you show up at their office, sit with their teams, find the workflows that matter, and ship agents that run in production. You also build on our core product. When you hit a platform limitation at a customer, you don't file a ticket, you work directly with engineering to close the gap, most often by writing the fix yourself.
