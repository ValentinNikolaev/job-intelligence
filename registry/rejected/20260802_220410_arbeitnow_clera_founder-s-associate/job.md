### About the Role

A fast-growing, early-stage consumer nutrition brand in the men's health space is hiring its first full-time **Founder's Associate** based in Berlin. This is a high-impact generalist role working directly with the founder across brand, product, and go-to-market — making you a core shaper of the company's culture, direction, and Germany-first launch. The right candidate may be considered for a co-founder path.

This is an on-site role in Berlin. You must be **fluent in German** and eligible to work in Germany without visa sponsorship.

### What You'll Do

-

Ramp up quickly and support the founder across operations, brand, and product tasks day-to-day.

-

Own go-to-market activities with a focus on Germany.

-

Contribute to brand building, product taste, style, and positioning for a consumer lifestyle product.

-

Coordinate a small, scrappy team including a Werkstudent, an incoming intern, and a fractional head of performance.

-

Execute ad-hoc operational projects with high autonomy and low supervision.

-

Help define early customer experiences and establish feedback loops for a consumer product.

-

Collaborate closely with the founder and contractors on-site in Berlin.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

Based in Berlin and fully available to work on-site.

-

Fluent in German (hard requirement for the Germany-first go-to-market).

-

Eligible to work in Germany — **no visa sponsorship is available**.

-

Strong generalist drive with the ability to learn quickly across domains in a startup environment.

-

Genuine affinity for brand building and consumer products — this is *not* a pure SaaS or analytics role.

-

Demonstrated taste, style, and feel for what makes a compelling consumer brand.

-

Comfortable operating with high autonomy and executing both strategic and operational tasks.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Prior experience in a startup, consumer brand, or early-stage company.

-

A bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience (helpful, not required).

-

0–4 years of experience in relevant areas; entry-level candidates with strong potential are very welcome.
