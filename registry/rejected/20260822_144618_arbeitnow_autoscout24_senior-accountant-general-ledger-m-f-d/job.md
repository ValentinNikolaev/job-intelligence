**What you'll do**

- Maintain the **General Ledger** for German legal entities, ensuring transactions are recorded accurately and in line with Group accounting policies, IFRS, HGB and local statutory requirements.

- Support the preparation of statutory financial statements, annual audits and other local reporting requirements.

- Prepare balance sheet reconciliations and ensure reconciling items are investigated and resolved.

- Assist with monthly **VAT** returns and related tax compliance activities.

- Support the global intercompany reconciliation process and collaborate with finance teams across the Group.

- Contribute to system improvements and finance projects that enhance efficiency and strengthen internal controls.

**What you'll bring**

- University degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field.

- Professional accounting qualification or active studies towards ACCA, ACA, CPA, Bilanzbuchhalter, Steuerfachwirt or an equivalent qualification.

- Solid knowledge of **IFRS** and **HGB**.

- Experience in **General Ledger Accounting** or Legal Entity Accounting within a medium to large international organisation.

- Experience with ERP systems; Workday Financials is a plus.

- Fluent German and English.
