**ABOUT US**

Tozero is Europe's leading deep-tech startup specializing in the recycling of lithium-ion batteries.
Committed to accelerating decarbonization and transforming the material life cycle, we’re actively
crafting sustainable recycling solutions for a cleaner and more innovative future.

**WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR**

This role combines maintenance engineering, spare parts management, CMMS administration
and hands-on support in our production facility. The successful candidate will work closely with
both the engineering team in Munich and the operations team in Gendorf to improve equipment
reliability and maintenance processes.
Reporting to the Maintenance department lead, the successful candidate will work closely with the
Head of operations, Maintenance mechanical engineer, and external equipment suppliers to
support reliable day-to-day plant operation and continuous improvement initiatives

**WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT**

**1. Operational coordination & spare parts logistics**
Inventory & supplier management:
Identification of critical spare parts, inventory optimization to minimize downtime, and direct
communication with equipment manufacturers for parts procurement and technical support.
Equipment standardization:
Analyzing and unifying components (e.g., pumps and seals) to reduce spare parts inventory
levels and enable faster maintenance interventions.
**2. CMMS & technical documentation**
CMMS (computerized maintenance management system) management:
Entering new equipment, updating the database according to field conditions, and tracking
preventive maintenance records (full training on our CMMS system will be provided).
Technical archiving:
Maintaining technical manuals, CE certifications, intervention reports, and tracking
contracts with external service providers and inspectors.
**3. Hands-on support & Gendorf operations**
Field work (Gendorf ~ 2 days per week):
On-site presence to monitor ongoing operations and act as a direct liaison with
management in Munich.
Assistance to mechanical engineer:
Hands-on assistance during the servicing and installation of mechanical subsystems
(pumps, piping, gearboxes, mixers, filter presses, heat exchangers).
**4. Process improvement & SOPs (standard operating procedures)**
Equipment reliability:
Data-driven failure analysis (transitioning from subjective assessment to component
lifespan analysis).
SOP & design for maintenance:
Creating and reviewing standard operating procedures and participating in the early stages
of new system development to ensure future maintainability.

**Your profile**

- Structured way of working
- Curiosity and willingness to learn
- Interest in mechanical systems
- Comfortable working both in the office and on the shop floor
- Strong communication skills and ability to work independently
- Category B driver’s license (required for trips to the Gendorf facility, company car provided).
- Nice to have:
- Welding experience or knowledge of welding techniques.
- Prior exposure to CMMS software or experience in the process/manufacturing industry.
