####
About the role

You will own the EEA growth campaign engine, which mainly comprises of 4 types: Affiliate, Listing, Trade & Earn, and New Product Launches. This is both a coordination and execution role. You will be the single accountable owner who turns a campaign concept into a shipped, measured, and reflected-upon outcome by orchestrating creative, media, lifecycle, BD, KOL ops, product marketing, and legal. The number on the campaign is yours. You drive the outcome.

**
What you will own**

-

Affiliate campaigns: Activation pulses on top of the always-on affiliate channel. Coordinated with BD and BD Operations.

-

Listing campaigns: New token listing campaigns shipped within 24 hours of listing decisions. Tight loop with Listing PM, legal, comms, BD/KOL Ops, exchange ops.

-

Trade & Earn campaigns: Volume incentives, leaderboards, zero fee promos. Built around clear unit economics and incrementality, not vanity volume.

-

Launch campaigns: Product and feature launches. Coordinated with product marketing, product team, social team, and lifecycle team.

#### What you will do

-

Translate fuzzy stakeholder asks into clear campaign briefs that creative, paid, and lifecycle can act on without a six-revision loop.

-

Build and own campaign plans with clear gates, dependencies, owners, and dates. Hold stakeholders accountable when slots slip.

-

Define success metrics with analytics before launch, not after. Run holdouts where possible. Run reflection sessions within 7 days of campaign close. Publish learnings the team can reuse.

-

Navigate MiCA and MiFID II constraints with legal. You will not be the lawyer, but you will know enough to flag the risks early and propose alternatives.

-

Be the connective tissue between EEA Growth and Global Campaigns. Localise global playbooks to EEA markets. Push EEA-originated wins back to global for global execution.

#### What we are looking for

##### Must-haves:

-

Four to seven years total experience, two-plus years coordinating end-to-end campaigns at a tier 1 regulated crypto exchange (Binance, Kraken, Bitpanda, Coinbase, or similar). You have shipped at least 10 campaigns with named numbers attached. And you have the receipts.

-

Fluency in English and German, French, or Dutch.

-

Hands-on familiarity with EEA regulator dynamics under MiCA and MiFID II. You have shipped campaigns under regulator review and lived to tell the tales.

-

Strong brief-writing muscle. Strong project management muscle with strong stakeholder management across senior and peer levels.

-

Analytical literacy: you can read a campaign dashboard, spot the wrong attribution, ask the right question of analytics, and present results to leadership.

-

Budget discipline. You understand CAC payback, ROAS, incremental volume, LTV.

### Who you are

-

Competitive. You want your campaign on the leaderboard against Global. You want EEA to outperform.

-

Crypto-native. You trade. You have a wallet. You know which tokens listed last week and which exchange ran the best campaign around it. You have opinions.

-

Detail-oriented. You catch the wrong token contract address in a launch comms draft before legal does.

-

Fast. The good campaigns ship within hours of the news, not days.
