## Our mission

Constructor’s mission is to enable all educational organisations to provide high-quality digital education to 10x people with 10x efficiency.

With strong expertise in machine intelligence and data science, Constructor’s all-in-one platform for education and research addresses today’s pressing educational challenges: access inequality, tech clutter, and low engagement of students.

### **Please send your resume in English only.**

As the **Senior Technical Product Manager for Platform**, you will own the product strategy for the shared services layer that powers Constructor's learning and research applications. You will drive the evolution of Platform capabilities - including identity, data infrastructure, AI/ML services, and developer tools - into a modular foundation. Your work enables internal teams and external partners to build and ship faster while maintaining enterprise-grade security, compliance, and global readiness.

### **Duties and Responsibilities:**

- Define and drive the product vision for a multi-instance, scalable foundation that supports SaaS and on-premise deployments

- Collaborate with application product teams to consolidate fragmented components and requirements into unified platform services

- Stay up-to-date with industry developments and technologies to inform Platform strategy

- Communicate Platform insights and recommendations to stakeholders and the leadership team

- Act as a point of contact for cross-functional teams and stakeholders

- Analyse performance data to identify areas for improvement and optimisation

- Collaborate with the development team to identify and prioritise new features and capabilities

- Drive a roadmap with dependencies across the wider organisation, balancing technical constraints with business needs

### Qualifications and experience:

- 5+ years of experience working as a program or product manager for a platform, SaaS infrastructure, or API products

- Computer Science or similar degree

- **A strong technical background.** You are comfortable discussing API design, architecture, and authentication flows.

- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills

- Strong communication and collaboration skills

- Experience leading teams and working with cross-functional stakeholders

**Nice to Have:**

- Experience with developer platforms or platform-as-a-service products

- Experience managing AI/ML infrastructure or platforms
