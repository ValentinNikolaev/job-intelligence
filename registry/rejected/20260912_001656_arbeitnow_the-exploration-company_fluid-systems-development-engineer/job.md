##
Description

We are seeking a talented Fluid Systems Development Engineer to join the Storm engine project. As a key member of our System Team, you will play a central role in developing key fluid systems of the engine.

## Key Responsibilities

As a Fluid Systems Development Engineer, your responsibilities will include:

-

Translate engine-level functional and performance requirements into fluid-system requirements, architectures, and development priorities.

-

Lead architecture trade-off studies, sensitivity analyses, and risk assessments to support robust design decisions.

-

Define and manage the development roadmap for fluid-system sub-assemblies, from concept through maturation and qualification.

-

Lead the design of fluid systems and the selection, development, and integration of their components, including make-or-buy decisions.

-

Define and manage the verification and validation strategy, including requirements, test specifications, acceptance criteria, and evidence of compliance.

-

Design and specify the pneumatic equipment required to support demonstrator test campaigns and test-article operations.

-

Analyse test data to assess system performance, identify discrepancies, and validate and anchor fluid-system models.

-

Feed test results and lessons learned back into system design, requirements, and future development activities.

##
What we would love to see from you

-

Master's Degree or higher (or NZ and AUS "Bachelor with honors") in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related and applicable technical discipline. Alternatively, a bachelor's degree with substantial relevant experience

-

Minimum 2 years of proven experience developing propulsion fluid systems

-

Strong knowledge of fluid dynamics, thermodynamics and cryogenic fluids

-

Good knowledge of fluid components (check-valves, pressure regulators...)

-

Fluency in English; proficiency in German or French is an asset

-

Basic knowledge of aerospace standards (e.g. ECSS, NASA guidelines)

-

Experience writing scripts in Python

-

Experience using 3D CAD software (Siemens NX is an asset)

-

(Optional) Experience with modelling and simulation tools is a plus (e.g. EcosimPro)

-

(Optional) Hands-on experience developing hardware from concept to working prototypes is an asset
