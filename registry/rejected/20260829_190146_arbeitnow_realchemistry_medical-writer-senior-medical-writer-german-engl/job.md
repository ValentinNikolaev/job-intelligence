**What is the role?**

We're looking for a **Medical Writer/Senior Medical Writer **to join our team at **Real Chemistry Germany**. You will be the first hire in our German-based content team, and will be responsible for developing materials across medical affairs and marketing projects in a range of therapy areas for top pharma clients.

As this role will be working on international and global work, we will require fluency in both German and English.

**What will you be doing? **

- Taking responsibility for the day-to-day delivery of scientific content across medical affairs and marketing accounts

- Developing and maintaining a thorough understanding of assigned therapeutic areas

- Liaising directly with clients and external experts on therapy area and content strategy (with occasional on-site travel)

- Working with the client services, strategy and creative teams on strategy development

- Taking an interest in AI tools to enhance efficiency

- Developing and mantaining a knowledge of regulations across Germany and wider Europe

- Supporting the senior team with developing pitch materials and proposals

- Identifying new business opportunities

**Who are you?**

- Already working as Medical Writer or Senior Medical Writer in a medical affairs or healthcare advertising/marketing agency.

- A strong scientific background with a degree in a scientific or medical discipline (post-graduate degree preferred).

- Passion and profound understanding of, healthcare, science, medicine, and the pharmaceutical industry is essential

- Experience interacting with clients and KOLs

- Full fluency in English and German
