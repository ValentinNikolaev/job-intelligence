## 💡 Your Mission: One source of truth for a company that runs on data

As** Senior Analytics Engineer**, you own voize's company data from the warehouse to the dashboards. You'll be part of our Data & AI function - ensuring the data voize and our AI layer runs on are reliable, safe & scalable. The modern stack (Redshift, dbt, Fivetran, Lightdash) is already live: you make it rock-solid and turn it into the single source of truth the whole company runs on, from ARR to product adoption. As our first dedicated analytics hire, you partner directly with Finance, RevOps, Product, and Customer Success, turning their questions into numbers everyone trusts.

## 🚀 Your Daily Business - No two days are alike

-

Model company data in dbt on Redshift: layered, tested, documented models built to outlive you

-

Ship and run the company KPI dashboard in Lightdash, plus self-serve analytics for every team

-

Own metric modeling and propose the definitions the whole company runs on. Sign-off sits with data leadership, and you make every number defensible

-

Keep the ingestion layer running day to day (Fivetran connectors, existing ETL jobs), in close coordination with engineering

-

Act as a partner to our teams and turn ambiguous business questions from Finance, RevOps, Product, and CS into trusted, self-serve answers

-

Shape the data foundation before the function scales. As a Head of (Internal) Data & AI and a further Engineers join, you set the standards they inherit and go deeper on the architecture the whole company trusts.

## 🤝 Your Skillset - What you bring to the table

-

4+ years owning analytics from the warehouse onwards (modelling, reporting, BI) in production, ideally as an early or founding data hire

-

Deep SQL above all, plus strong dbt (layered models, tests, CI, semantic / metric layers)

-

A cloud data warehouse in production (Redshift ideal; Snowflake or BigQuery with willingness to switch) and a BI tool people actually use

-

Business-facing autonomy: you partner with non-technical stakeholders, propose and defend metric definitions, and ship without waiting for a data team to form around you

-

You document, test, and monitor by default, and finishing the 10% that makes a model trustworthy

-

You know how to leverage AI, you have a track record of automating & building workflows with AI.
