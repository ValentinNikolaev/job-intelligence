**Dein Impact**

We’re building the AI platform for energy installers and want to change how the industry works. Founded in 2023, graduated from Y Combinator in 2024, and now a team of 70 in our Berlin office near Weinbergspark. And we have much more ahead.
As **Team Lead Sales Development**, you’ll continuously develop our SDR team, processes, and sales playbook. You’ll coach, set ambitious targets, and pick up the phone yourself. In doing so, you’ll build a strong outbound pipeline and drive our growth.

**Location:** Berlin, on-site
**Employment type:** Full-time

- **Lead and develop our SDR team.** Help each rep improve through regular 1:1s, call reviews, practical coaching, and clear development goals.
- **Own pipeline generation.** Translate monthly and quarterly targets into actionable plans, tracking both performance and opportunity quality.
- **Lead by example.** Make cold calls, test new approaches, and show your team how to run effective conversations with decision-makers.
- **Build a strong outbound culture.** Make preparation, consistent prospecting, structured follow-ups, and shared learning part of the team’s daily routine.
- **Develop our sales playbook.** Refine messaging, call scripts, and outreach sequences for heating and solar installation businesses.
- **Partner with Account Executives and Sales Leadership.** Align on target customers, qualification criteria, and smooth handoffs.
- **Improve our processes.** Use CRM data, automation, and AI tools to reduce repetitive tasks and give your team more time with prospects.

**Requirements**

- At least one year of outbound sales experience, with a track record of generating qualified opportunities through cold calling.
- One to two years of sales leadership experience, including coaching and managing team performance.
- An understanding of SMB sales and the ability to connect with business owners and decision-makers.
- A structured approach to targets and metrics, with the judgment to identify where your team needs support.
- Initiative, resilience, and the ability to turn ideas into action quickly.
- Excellent English communication skills, plus C2 proficiency in German, Italian, or French.
- Enthusiasm for AI, software, and the energy transition. Experience in SaaS sales or the installation industry is a plus.
- Confidence using CRM systems. Experience with HubSpot and sales automation is an advantage.
