FAMILY FLOW

Saarbrücken / Homburg, Saarland, Germany | On-site | Fulltime | Digital Health (DiGA)

About Family Flow

Family Flow is a scientifically grounded, trauma-sensitive parenting app that helps parents with their own history of adverse childhood experiences (ACEs) manage the moment between a trigger and their reaction to their child — building a personalized 'Flow Plan' (trigger → warning signs → pause → skill → parenting strategy) instead of another generic parenting app.

The opportunity

The concept is built on more than 20 years of clinical and scientific work on child maltreatment led by Prof. Dr. med. Eva Möhler, Chair of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry at Saarland University, alongside DFKI researchers Patrick Gebhard and Jan Alexandersson. The idea and clinical content are finalized — what's missing is the team and funding to build and launch the app. We are looking for a CEO / Business co-founder (fundraising, tech and operations) to turn a validated clinical concept into a real product.

## Tasks

What you will own

Company build-up & fundraising

- Lead incorporation of Family Flow and raise the funding needed for app development (German, French and English versions).

- Own the path to the German DiGA directory (digital health app reimbursement) and the parallel free-market / B2C launch.

Product & delivery

- Translate the finalized clinical concept and psychotherapeutic material into a software roadmap, in partnership with a tech/development team or subcontractor (e.g. potential partnerships with Pioneo, Pfeffermind or Charamel).

- Own day-to-day operations, as the current interdisciplinary team (DFKI + Child and Adolescent Psychiatry) is part-time only.

Go-to-market

- Build the launch plan for Germany and France, then wider Europe, including social-media-led B2C acquisition alongside the DiGA pathway.
