## **Department Overview**

The Facility & Engineering team ensures safe, reliable, and efficient daily operations of fulfillment centers by maintaining infrastructure, equipment, and systems. We minimize operational risks and enable continuous performance through preventive and corrective maintenance.

As Technician, you will own the maintenance and reliability of warehouse equipment and infrastructure. You will perform preventive maintenance, resolve breakdowns, and ensure compliance with safety standards. By securing equipment uptime and functionality, you will directly support the smooth operation of fulfillment center processes.
