**YOUR IMPACT**

As a Workplace Experience & Team Assistant, you will provide reliable and detail-oriented administrative support to the Leadership Team. Your main responsibilities will include coordinating calendars, booking travel, handling logistics, and supporting work-related administrative errands.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, being in the middle of how organizations work managing multiple priorities and stakeholders, and takes pride in operational excellence. The role offers a unique view into the way Proxima’s leadership team operates, creating human connections with multiple individuals whose performance is critical to Proxima’s success.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

-

Coordinate and manage the Leadership Team’s calendars for internal and external meetings

-

Book travel (flights, trains, hotels, transfers) based on guidance and preferences

-

Support logistics such as dinner reservations, appointments, and work-related errands

-

Assist with expense tracking and reimbursement submissions

-

Prepare basic meeting documentation (e.g. formatting documents, taking meeting notes when required)

-

Take ownership of recurring internal meeting scheduling (e.g., 1:1s, weekly team meetings)

-

Possibly own company-wide travel management - this is something that is currently evolving and being flexible is very important

**WHO YOU ARE**

-

Highly organized with strong attention to detail

-

Reliable, structured, and comfortable handling recurring administrative tasks

-

A clear and professional communicator in English (German is a plus)

-

Comfortable working with Google Workspace, Slack, and other productivity tools

-

Discreet and trustworthy when handling sensitive information

-

Proactive and service-oriented, with a “can-do” mindset
