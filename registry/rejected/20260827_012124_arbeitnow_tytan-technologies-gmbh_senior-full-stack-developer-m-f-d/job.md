### TYTAN Technologies

At **TYTAN**, we aim to set a global standard in autonomous air defense with AI-driven, cost-efficient systems that counter unmanned aerial threats at scale. We protect people. We protect critical infrastructure. We protect Europe’s sovereignty.

We are looking for a new team member who is eager to make a real difference! As a Senior Full-Stack Developer, you will be responsible for designing and developing scalable military ground control station applications coordinating various effectors and integrating with external systems in real-time mission-critical environments.

### Your roadmap to make an impact

-

**Architect full-stack applications** for ground control stations, balancing performance, reliability, and operator usability.

-

**Build real-time communication layers** between C2 systems, autonomous platforms, and sensor networks.

-

**Design low-latency data pipelines** for target tracking, sensor fusion, and tactical decision support.

-

**Ensure system observability** through comprehensive logging, distributed tracing, and real-time metrics.

-

**Develop tactical interfaces** (desktop and web) that give operators clear situational awareness.

### **Who you are**

-

Bachelors or Masters degree in Computer Science or similar technical field.

-

5+ years full stack development with exposure to real-time, mission-critical applications.

-

Experience in building modern frontend applications using **React / Angular (JavaScript / TypeScript),** and able to build dense, data-heavy interfaces that stay responsive under load.

-

Experience in backend development using **Node.js or Python**, with hands-on experience designing REST APIs and WebSocket-based real-time communication.

-

Comfortable working with relational, NoSQL, in-memory, and time-series databases (e.g. PostgreSQL/MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, InfluxDB).

-

Solid understanding of **networking fundamentals:** solid grasp of TCP/IP and UDP fundamentals (sockets, multicast, packet loss, latency), and experience with serialization and messaging formats such as Protocol Buffers, MQTT, etc. Familiarity with MAVLink or comparable telemetry protocols is a plus.

-

Experience working with **Linux-based environments**, as well as tools such as **Docker, Nginx, and CI/CD pipelines** to build, deploy, and maintain production systems.

-

Domain knowledge in **real-time systems**, including state machines, coordinate frames, **sensor data fusion, Kalman filtering**.

-

You want **end-to-end ownership,** including **in the field.** Shipping the feature is only half the job. You're motivated to take it to the flight line, see it run on real hardware in real conditions, debug it there, and bring the lessons back into the code. This involves regular travel to test sites across Germany and occasionally abroad - and you see that as one of the exciting parts of the role!
