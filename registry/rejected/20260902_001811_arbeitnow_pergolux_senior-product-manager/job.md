# Senior Product Manager

Posted: 2026-09-01T20:40:09Z

- Category: location_requirement

As a Senior Product Manager (m/w/d), you will own eCommerce storefront initiatives end to end, from concept and discovery through launch and optimization, with a global scope across PERGOLUX markets. You will define product solutions, scope, and success metrics for content-rich, UX-heavy customer journeys, and you will drive measurable improvements in storefront performance such as add-to-cart and checkout conversion. Working closely with UX, development, analytics, performance marketing, and country teams, you will structure complex product information into clear, attractive pages that reflect a premium brand experience.

PERGOLUX designs and sells Scandinavian-inspired outdoor living products, from pergolas to garden furniture, directly to consumers. With around 300 employees, 9-figure revenue, and operations across 8 markets, we have grown consistently across Europe and beyond, building a collaborative international team along the way.

**What You'll Do**

• Own eCommerce product initiatives from concept to launch and optimization, defining scope, risks, requirements, and success metrics.

• Conduct product discovery through user research, competitor analysis, usability testing, and synthesizing customer feedback into product decisions.

• Create page concepts, wireframes, prototypes, copy drafts, and visual briefs, and write clear specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria.

• Plan delivery with development, managing priorities, dependencies, QA, releases, rollout logic, toggles, and rollback plans.

• Monitor launches and use analytics, A/B testing, and experimentation to drive rapid fixes and follow-up improvements to storefront performance.

**What You'll Bring**

• 5+ years in product management for eCommerce products, with strong experience in UX-heavy, content-rich customer journeys.

• Proven ability to structure complex product information into clear, simple, and attractive pages, backed by customer research and insight-driven decisions.

• Strong visual taste and an understanding of what makes a premium digital brand experience, plus the ability to create concepts, wireframes, and copy drafts independently.

• Solid command of CRO, analytics, A/B testing, and storefront performance optimization, and a track record of aligning UX, developers, analytics, and marketing.

• Nice to have: experience with Shopify or other storefront platforms, high-ticket or considered-purchase eCommerce, premium/lifestyle brands, and building repeatable product processes or templates.
