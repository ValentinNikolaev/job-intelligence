**Ready to kickstart your Product Management career at the crossroads of Marketing Tech and Entertainment?**

JustWatch provides data-driven digital marketing for the global entertainment industry. We build internal tools that help our campaign managers automate workflows and unlock innovative capabilities in entertainment marketing.

We’re hiring a **Working Student Product Manager** for our **MarTech** team. This role is ideal for someone who is organised, curious, proactive, and excited to learn how Product Management works in a fast-moving environment.

**What you will be doing**

- Test new product releases and help document bugs or unexpected behaviour.

- Monitor product metrics and help identify issues early.

- Coordinate with developers and campaign managers to help keep projects on track.

- Gradually take ownership of smaller features or workflows as you grow into the role.

**What you will NOT be doing**

- Jira administration or ticket shuffling - we work lightweight, and we optimise for speed.

- Wait for work to fall on your desk - you’ll know what needs to be prioritised next.

- Wait around for instructions all day. We value people who are proactive and curious.

**What we need from you**

- You enjoy solving problems and **bringing structure to projects.**

- You’re** organised and reliable**. You follow up on tasks and help keep everything moving.

- You’re comfortable using tools like Google Docs, Sheets, or Notion to organise information.

- You enjoy** working with data** and are curious about how metrics help teams make decisions.

- You’re not afraid of technical topics and are interested in learning how APIs, tracking, automations, and internal systems work.

- You like learning by doing and** taking initiative** when something needs attention.

- You’re interested in product development, marketing technology, automation, or digital products.

- You’ve shipped a side project, automation, or dashboard yourself.

**What we offer you**

- A hands-on introduction to Product Management in a real business environment.

- Direct impact on how global entertainment marketing campaigns are run.

- A fast-learning environment with lots of **ownership** and responsibility.

- A culture built on autonomy, trust, and continuous improvement.

If you’re missing some hard skills, that’s okay. We hire for attitude, curiosity, motivation, and growth potential.
