### About the Role

A fast-growing, Series A/B EdTech startup based in Berlin is looking for a **Visiting Finance Analyst** to join its Finance team on a full-time, on-site basis. This is a rare early-career opportunity to work directly alongside senior leadership — including the Director of Finance & People — and gain hands-on experience building a modern finance function from the ground up. You'll have real ownership of workstreams, direct access to decision-makers, and a front-row seat to how a high-growth tech company manages its finances. The company operates a widely used AI-powered learning platform with tens of millions of users globally across more than 30 countries.

### What You'll Do

-

Work directly alongside the Director of Finance & People as a hands-on contributor, acting as a trusted right hand across projects and priorities.

-

Prepare decision briefs and support cross-functional initiatives, keeping projects on track across Finance, Growth, Engineering, and Legal.

-

Support monthly, quarterly, and annual financial close processes and coordinate with external auditors.

-

Contribute to accurate, reliable financial reporting across multiple jurisdictions.

-

Assist with operational controlling, including budget planning, financial forecasting, and liquidity tracking.

-

Build monthly reports, conduct ad hoc deep dives, and translate data into clear, decision-ready analysis.

-

Identify and implement automation opportunities using AI tools, low-code platforms, and smart integrations to reduce manual workflows.

-

Help evolve the finance function from static reporting toward dynamic, AI-enhanced financial intelligence.

-

Coordinate with external tax advisors, auditors, and legal providers.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

Currently enrolled in or recently graduated from a degree program in business, finance, economics, or a related field.

-

Professional working proficiency in both **German and English**, written and spoken.

-

Foundational knowledge of accounting principles sufficient to support financial close and reporting processes.

-

Proficiency in Excel and hands-on experience analyzing financial data to generate reports and inform decisions.

-

Experience supporting month-end, quarter-end, or year-end close processes, audit coordination, or multi-jurisdiction financial reporting.

-

Experience with financial planning tasks such as budgeting, forecasting, or liquidity tracking.

-

Experience using automation tools, AI applications, or low-code platforms to streamline finance or operational workflows.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Prior internship, coursework, or project experience in finance, accounting, controlling, or consulting.

-

Familiarity with German accounting standards (HGB) or IFRS reporting requirements.

-

Familiarity with BI tools or data visualization platforms for financial dashboards.

-

Experience working in a high-growth startup or technology environment.

**Mindset & soft skills:**

-

Proactive, self-driven, and pragmatic — you take ownership without being asked.

-

Strong analytical thinker who can turn messy data into clear, actionable insights.

-

Genuinely curious about AI tools and the future of finance automation.

-

Comfortable working closely with leadership in a lean, fast-moving team.

### Location & Work Arrangement

This role is based **on-site in Berlin, Germany**. Candidates must be able to work from the Berlin office full-time. **Visa sponsorship is not available** for this position.
