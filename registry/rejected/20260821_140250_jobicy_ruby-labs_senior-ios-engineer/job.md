## **About the role**

As a** Senior iOS Engineer**, you will play a key role in developing and maintaining our mobile app, ensuring high performance, scalability, and a seamless user experience. You will collaborate closely with the product manager and fellow developers to bring new features to life and continuously optimize our app. Your expertise in **Swift and iOS **development best practices will be instrumental in driving innovation and maintaining our position as a leader in the App Store.

## **Key Responsibilities**

-

Develop and maintain a high-quality iOS application with a focus on performance, UI, and UX.

-

Take ownership of development processes, ensuring clean, maintainable, and scalable code.

-

Work with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and launch new features.

-

Implement CI/CD pipelines and deployment best practices.

-

Conduct code reviews and enforce coding standards.

-

Profile and optimize app performance (memory, launch time, rendering, battery).

-

Stay up to date with the latest iOS development trends and integrate relevant advancements.

## **Qualifications**

-

**5+ years of professional iOS** development experience.

-

**Experience with B2C mobile apps** built on **subscription business models.**

-

Expert proficiency in **Swift **(protocol-oriented design, generics, value semantics, async/await, structured concurrency, actors).

-

Strong command of **StoreKit 2 **and subscription lifecycle: purchase flows, receipt/transaction validation, entitlement management, offer codes, and server-to-server notifications (with RevenueCat or a custom equivalent).

-

Deep understanding of **UIKit** and **SwiftUI**, including interoperability, when to use each, and SwiftUI state management (@State, @Observable, @Environment).

-

Solid grasp of architecture patterns (MVVM, Clean Architecture, Composable Architecture) and modularization for large codebases (SPM modules, feature isolation).

-

Experience with Combine and/or Swift Concurrency for reactive and async data flows.

-

Proficiency with** CI/CD** (Xcode Cloud, Fastlane, Bitrise, or GitHub Actions), Gitflow, code signing/provisioning automation, and TestFlight distribution.

-

**Automated testing experience:** XCTest, XCUITest, snapshot testing, and dependency injection for testability.

-

Strong debugging and profiling skills with Instruments (Time Profiler, Allocations, Leaks, Hangs).

-

Experience integrating AI-driven features and/or AI-assisted development workflows.

-

Familiarity with analytics, crash reporting, and A/B testing / feature-flag tooling
