**About the Venture**

We're building next-generation HPC and AI infrastructure for a world where brute-force compute is reaching its limits. Our chip and system architecture is designed to exploit sparsity at scale, focusing compute where it matters most and eliminating unnecessary computation everywhere else. That means orders-of-magnitude gains in performance and efficiency. We're based in Dresden and working at the intersection of deep tech, European industrial policy, and the global AI race.

**About the Role**

This is a senior hire at the centre of how we engage with government, regulators, and the broader European policy landscape. You'll work directly with the CEO and leadership team, with real ownership over how we show up in Berlin and Brussels.
