## Tasks

**You will play a key role in building and scaling our hardware production. You will be responsible for the interface between product development, manufacturing, suppliers, purchasing, quality, inventory and management.**

**Your goal is to transform our production from small-series manufacturing into a predictable, efficient and quality-controlled serial production setup. This role is not only about coordination, but also about industrialization, cost optimization, process development, supplier management and continuous improvement.**

**Your responsibilities include:**

**1. Strategic Management & Optimization**

- **Operational leadership, with a focus on production and supplier management**

- **Scaling our production from one to multiple locations**

- **Ensuring high quality and targeting zero down time in our fleet**

- **Setting up a near to perfect quality control system after production**

**2. Financial Planning & Management**

- **Oversight of the financials including budgeting and controlling**

- **Optimization of purchasing conditions and cost management**

- **Ensuring financial stability while scaling**

- **Setup of a clear KPI driven reporting**

**3. Support Customer Growth**

- **Improvement of coordination and execution of our services for B2B clients**

- **Building efficient processes to ensure the highest level of customer satisfaction**

**4. Team & Collaboration**

- **Building and leading a high-performing team across all fields**

- **Close collaboration with CEO & CTO to achieve strategic goals**

- **Direct responsibility with decision-making authority at the highest level**

- **You will work closely with the founding team and management, ensuring short communication paths and fast decision-making.**
