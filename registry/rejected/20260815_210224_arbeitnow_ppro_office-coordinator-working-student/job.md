We are looking for an energetic, organized, and proactive **Working Student - Office Coordinator** to become the heartbeat of our Berlin workspace. In this role, you will be the driving force behind a smooth, welcoming, and highly efficient daily office environment. Balancing 20 hours a week around your studies (3 days per week) you will manage day-to-day office operations, support our team, and help cultivate a vibrant workplace culture. This is a fantastic opportunity to gain hands-on operational and administrative experience in a fast-paced, international business environment.

**What you'll be doing:**

#### **Office Operations & Hospitality**

-

**Workspace Management:** Act as the main point of contact for the Berlin office, ensuring the workspace remains professional, organized, welcoming, and tidy at all times.

-

**Inventory & Supplies:** Proactively manage and restock office inventory, including kitchen supplies, snacks, beverages, and stationery.

-

**Vendor Relations:** Liaise and build positive relationships with external service providers, including cleaning staff, couriers, building management, and suppliers.

#### **Facilities & Workplace Experience**

-

**Facilities Maintenance:** Oversee the functionality of office equipment (printers, coffee machines, meeting room tech) and promptly coordinate maintenance or repairs when needed.

-

**Health & Safety:** Assist in maintaining basic workplace health, safety, and security standards, ensuring a safe environment for all employees.

-

**Visitor Management:** Warmly welcome guests, candidates, and visiting international colleagues, ensuring they have an exceptional on-site experience.

#### **People, Culture & Events**

-

**Event Coordination:** Take a lead role in bringing our team together by planning and organizing weekly team lunches, office celebrations, seasonal parties, and cultural events.

-

**Community Building:** Act as a culture champion, brainstorming new ideas to make the Berlin office an inspiring and fun place to work.

#### **Administration & Onboarding Logistics**

-

**Access Control:** Coordinate the logistics for office access, managing key cards, fobs, and desk allocations for new starters, guests, and departing employees.

-

**Financial Admin:** Assist with basic office finances by organizing and processing office-related invoices, receipts, and maintaining simple trackers for monthly office expenses.
