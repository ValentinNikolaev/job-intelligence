**Short Description**

INERATEC’s innovative chemical reactor technology is a key enabler of the energy transition. We build chemical plants that use greenhouse gases such as CO₂ and renewable electricity to produce synthetic fuels and sustainable products.
As Engineering Manager (m/f/d), you will act as the central technical interface for the planning, execution, and operational realization of complex Power-to-X and e-fuels plants within an EPC/EPCM environment. You will lead engineering activities across disciplines and coordinate interfaces between process engineering, plant design, electrical/instrumentation engineering, automation, and permitting. #sort: 140#PES#
