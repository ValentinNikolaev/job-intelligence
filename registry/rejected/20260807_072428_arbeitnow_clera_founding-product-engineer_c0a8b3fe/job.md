### About the Role

We're a fast-growing, Vienna-headquartered AI communication startup building an AI phone assistant platform for SMEs across Europe — and we're looking for a **Founding Product Engineer** to join our engineering team. This is a high-ownership, end-to-end role at the intersection of product thinking and full-stack engineering. You'll help shape the product from hypothesis to production, working closely with customers, partners, and sales to deliver features that create real, measurable business value.

We're a **remote-first team** with an optional office in Vienna. You'll need meaningful overlap with Central European Time (CET). Our product is German-language-first, so **native-level German proficiency is essential**.

### What You'll Do

-

Own features end-to-end — from hypothesis and prototype through architecture, implementation, and production release.

-

Lead product discovery by designing and running rapid experiments to validate ideas before committing to large-scale builds.

-

Maintain close, recurring contact with customers, partners, and the sales team to surface real pain points and translate them into product decisions.

-

Design and implement tracking and analytics to enable data-driven iteration.

-

Drive architecture choices across the full stack and take responsibility through to release.

-

Prioritize pragmatic, scalable solutions that balance speed of validation with long-term quality.

-

Take radical ownership of your initiatives — move things forward without waiting for sign-offs.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

3+ years of experience in Product Engineering delivering end-to-end software features.

-

Strong full-stack proficiency: **Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS, and React**.

-

**Native-level German fluency** (Deutsch auf Muttersprache-Niveau) — essential for our German-language product and customer interactions.

**Required:**

-

Experience integrating software with external systems (CRMs, calendar tools, databases, APIs).

-

Ability to gather customer feedback and convert pain points into prioritized product decisions.

-

Comfort identifying, tracking, and analyzing product metrics to drive data-informed choices.

-

Proven experience owning the full development lifecycle from architecture to production.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience working with AI/LLM integrations (e.g., OpenAI or similar) — this is a strong advantage.

-

Familiarity with GDPR compliance and EU AI Act considerations in product development.

-

Background working in remote-first, asynchronous environments at early-stage or high-growth startups.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

**Salary:** €80,000 – €120,000 annually, depending on experience.

-

**Equity:** Meaningful equity stake as part of a high-growth founding team environment.

-

Remote-first flexibility with optional office access in Vienna.

*Please note: visa sponsorship is not available for this role.*

### Location

Fully remote, with a preference for candidates based in **Germany** or able to work with significant CET overlap. An optional co-working office is available in Vienna, Austria.
