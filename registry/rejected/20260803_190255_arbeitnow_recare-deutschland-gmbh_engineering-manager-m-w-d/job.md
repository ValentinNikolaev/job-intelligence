**In this role, you can make an impact and grow with us as Engineering Manager (m/w/d):**

As we continue to grow our engineering organization, the role of Engineering Manager becomes key to keeping teams aligned, healthy, and effective. Your mission is to develop people, strengthen collaboration across teams, and create the structures that allow engineers and Tech Leads to thrive while building products that truly improve healthcare.

-

You are **responsible for the growth and development** of engineers in your team cluster – running regular 1:1s, supporting career development, giving actionable feedback, and addressing conflicts early.

-

You are **the driver of team health**, making sure teams collaborate effectively, identifying friction early, and stepping in when challenges start affecting delivery or morale.

-

You **shape how teams work together** by facilitating discussions across teams and ensuring the right people are involved when decisions impact multiple areas.

-

You **support Tech Leads** by enabling them to focus on technical leadership and mentoring, while you make sure decisions are documented, communicated, and actually land in the teams.

-

You **ensure that key engineering processes run smoothly** – from planning cycles and cross-team dependency alignment to meaningful retrospectives.

-

You **develop engineering beyond your immediate team** by contributing to cross-team initiatives such as onboarding programs, engineering-wide practices, or collaboration with Product.

-

You **analyze signals around team health and delivery**, spotting potential problems early and helping teams address them before they escalate.

-

You **accompany teams through operational situations** by ensuring clear escalation paths, reliable incident workflows, and strong documentation practices.

**Here’s how we picture you as our Engineering Manager (m/w/d):**

-

You have **experience in engineering leadership roles**, focusing on people management, and have worked closely with modern engineering teams in fast-growing product organizations.

-

You **understand and dive deep in technical discussions and engineering trade-offs** well enough to follow and facilitate conversations, even though your focus is on leadership rather than writing production code.

-

You are **very familiar with the current state of AI-native engineering**. You stay close to the frontier and adjust accordingly.

-

You bring **strong emotional intelligence** and the ability to give honest feedback, navigate conflicts, and hold people accountable in a constructive way.

-

You **think like a coach** – helping people grow and solve problems themselves instead of stepping in to solve everything for them.

-

You **communicate clearly and proactively**, making sure decisions, expectations, and challenges are transparent across teams and stakeholders.

-

You are **comfortable working with multiple Tech Leads** across teams while maintaining clarity around ownership and responsibilities.

-

You **communicate confidently in English** and are comfortable operating in an international environment.
