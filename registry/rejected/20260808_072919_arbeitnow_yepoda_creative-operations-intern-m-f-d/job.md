### **About Yepoda**

**Clean K-Beauty Innovation. No compromises.**

Yepoda is a clean K-Beauty brand that actually proves it works. We make Korean skincare without any of the compromises. Everything is clinically tested, 100% vegan, and formulated above EU standards. So you get the innovation and the results that K-Beauty is known for, plus a real commitment to sustainability. And we make it genuinely fun and easy to use — skincare you actually look forward to.

We are currently looking for a Creative Operations Intern to join our Creative team in Berlin for a 6-month internship. In this role, you'll help the creative team stay organized and on track, supporting the Creative Operations Lead with the day-to-day coordination that keeps projects, deadlines, and deliverables moving smoothly.

### **Your Tasks**

-

Support the Creative Operations Lead with the day-to-day coordination and tracking of creative projects, deadlines, feedback, approvals, and deliverables.

-

Maintain and organize the creative team’s Notion ticketing system, including statuses, due dates, assignments, and project information.

-

Review incoming tickets for missing information and help keep project data accurate and up to date.

-

Assist with weekly planning, project status updates, meeting notes, action items, and stakeholder follow-ups.

-

Collect and organize data for reporting on project volume, delivery timelines, overdue work, and team capacity.

-

Support project management and administrative tasks across campaigns, launches, and ongoing creative requests.

-

Assist with the setup and migration of a digital asset management system, including file auditing, naming, tagging, organization, and quality checks.

-

Maintain process documentation, trackers, templates, and other Creative Operations resources.

### **Your Profile**

-

You are organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable managing multiple tasks.

-

You communicate clearly and follow through reliably on agreed actions.

-

You enjoy working with structured information, databases, spreadsheets, and project trackers.

-

You are interested in creative operations, project management, marketing, design, or digital asset management.

-

You have a proactive, collaborative, and service-oriented approach.

-

You are confident asking questions when information is unclear or incomplete.

-

Experience with Notion, particularly databases, dashboards, or project boards, is a plus.

-

Familiarity with digital asset management systems is a plus.

-

Experience with creative tools such as Figma, FigJam, Adobe Creative Cloud, Frame.io, or Miro is a plus.

-

Basic knowledge of creative production workflows, file formats, or marketing channels is beneficial.

-

You are fluent in English.
