### About the Role

We're building an AI-native operating system that helps nurse practitioners launch and run independent clinical practices — and we're looking for a **Founding Product Engineer** to own and shape the clinician-facing product experience. This is a high-impact, early-team role at a seed-stage healthtech company where you'll work directly with founders, designers, and clinicians to turn complex clinical workflows into polished, reliable software that improves care delivery.

You'll be a defining voice in engineering culture, product direction, and technical architecture — not just an executor of finished specs. If you thrive at the intersection of strong engineering craft and genuine product ownership, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Own major clinician-facing workflows end-to-end — from whiteboard through implementation, testing, rollout, and iteration.

-

Collaborate closely with designers, founders, and clinicians to define product behavior rather than waiting for finished specs.

-

Drive frontend architecture and technical direction across provider and patient applications.

-

Influence APIs, data models, and overall system behavior while primarily owning the frontend.

-

Iterate quickly from rough concepts to production-quality experiences alongside a product designer.

-

Debug complex production issues spanning browsers, APIs, third-party integrations, and infrastructure.

-

Contribute to engineering culture through thoughtful code reviews, technical discussions, and pragmatic decision-making.

-

Leverage AI development tools as part of your workflow while maintaining full ownership of system design and production quality.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

Demonstrated experience leading significant technical initiatives or owning major product areas.

-

Strong hands-on experience with **React** and **Next.js** (TypeScript familiarity expected).

-

Proven track record of owning user-facing features from concept through production.

-

Comfort collaborating across the frontend/backend boundary — including working with APIs and debugging backend behavior.

-

Experience working directly with designers, customers, or domain experts to shape product direction.

-

Experience delivering workflows in a fast-moving, early-stage startup environment.

-

Strong product intuition, engineering judgment, and debugging skills.

-

Willingness to commute to a Midtown Manhattan office **3 days per week** (hybrid).

-

Comfort using AI-assisted development tools responsibly while maintaining ownership of what you build.

**Nice to haves:**

-

Experience in healthcare, with **FHIR**, or other regulated software environments.

-

Prior experience as an early or founding engineer at a startup.

-

Experience building products used throughout the workday, where usability and workflow continuity matter as much as correctness.

-

Experience evolving or maintaining a design system.
