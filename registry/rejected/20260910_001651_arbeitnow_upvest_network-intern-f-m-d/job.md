# **About the role:**

The Network team establishes sound partner and operational risk management practices and strategically enhances our Network of Partners. Our vision is to have an autonomous ecosystem of network partners that empowers Upvest's growth. The Network Intern plays a supportive role within the team, overseeing vendor documentation, questions from internal teams and supporting ongoing initiatives. This role focuses on ensuring accurate and timely execution of various network tasks. A key aspect of this role will be working across internal teams in order to fulfill vendor requirements. Internship length is 6 months and location is Berlin.

# **What you’ll do:**

-

Learn the ins and outs of vendor relationship management from due diligence process and account openings to BAU and strategic discussions & project management.

-

Support the network team throughout the lifecycle of vendor relationships from initial selection and onboarding to ongoing relationship management, issue resolution, and successful contractual and commercial negotiations.

-

Providing support for ongoing network initiatives and projects.

-

Working cross-functionally with operations, product and banking teams to resolve vendor requests.

-

Update and complete vendor documentation in case of new functionality or accounts.

-

Solve problems efficiently with calmness and creativity.

-

Addressing and resolving internal inquiries regarding vendor management.
