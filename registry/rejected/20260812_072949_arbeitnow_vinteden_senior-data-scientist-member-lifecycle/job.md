## **Information about the position **

Vinted sends over a **billion messages every month to millions of users**, across multiple channels (e.g. push, email, in-app) and with multiple goals (marketing, transactional, legal). We are looking for an ambitious Data Scientist to join an **early stage** initiative to build Vinted’s communication decisioning layer from the ground up: A unified "brain" that evaluates eligible message candidates and **decides whether to send, hold, drop, bundle, when to deliver, and through which channel**, or whether silence is the most valuable action to protect long-term user trust. In this role, you will take ownership of the core ML algorithms, uplift models, and policy optimisation frameworks that govern Vinted’s communication flows.

## **In this position, you’ll **

- **Own core decisioning & modelling logic:** Design, prototype, and refine multi-objective recommendation and delivery models (e.g., contextual bandits, uplift modelling, ranking, capping) that govern message selection, timing, and channel routing.

- **Formulate the optimisation problem**: Develop policies that maximise long-term member value and marketplace health (GMV, active buyers/sellers) while strictly controlling negative externalities like unsubscribes and notification fatigue.

- **Evaluate impact**: Establish reliable offline policy evaluation, and partner with Decision Scientists to design and run large-scale global holdout experiments, attributing incremental business impact and engagement quality.

- **Bridge science to production**: Collaborate closely with Engineers and Product to deploy prototype models into production pipelines.

- **Build the future**: Contribute to the long-term vision of a Vinted Communication Recommender.
