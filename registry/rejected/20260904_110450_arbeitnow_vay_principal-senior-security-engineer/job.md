#### ***Why this role is exciting***

Security at Vay is different. You’ll be working across a real-world technology platform combining vehicles, embedded systems, software, cloud infrastructure, connectivity and Remote Driving.

As a **Senior / Principal Security Engineer**, you’ll help shape the cybersecurity foundation of our technology platform, influencing architecture, engineering decisions and security practices across the company.

This role is ideal for someone who can operate strategically while staying technically hands-on. Depending on your experience and scope of ownership, the role can be hired at either Senior or Principal level.

#### ***What you'll be driving***

- Own and evolve cybersecurity engineering practices across Vay’s Remote Driving platform and vehicle ecosystem.

- Perform threat modelling, security risk assessments and technical security analysis across products and systems.

- Define cybersecurity requirements and work with engineering teams to implement practical mitigations.

- Influence architecture and system design across Software, Systems, Embedded, Infrastructure, Safety and Validation teams.

- Build security into the full engineering lifecycle, from early design through implementation, testing and deployment.

- Shape security testing and validation strategies across Vay’s technology stack.

- Support secure approaches to authentication, authorization, communications, software updates, identity and trust.

- Assess vulnerabilities, prioritize real-world risk and drive effective remediation.

- Represent cybersecurity in technical reviews, architecture discussions and internal and external conversations.

- Build scalable security processes that can support the continued growth of Vay’s commercial fleet and technology platform.

#### ***This might be a great fit, if***

- Around **8+ years of experience** across cybersecurity engineering, product security, application security, platform security or related areas.

- Strong knowledge of security architecture, secure software development, threat modelling, vulnerability management and security risk assessment.

- Experience securing complex products involving multiple software, infrastructure, networking, hardware or device components.

- Experience translating security risks into clear technical requirements and practical mitigations.

- Good understanding of authentication, authorization, cryptography, secure communications and trust models.

- Experience working with security standards, regulatory frameworks or structured cybersecurity engineering processes.

- Strong technical depth and the ability to understand unfamiliar systems quickly.

- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills across technical and non-technical audiences.

- Comfortable owning ambiguous problems and operating both strategically and hands-on.

- For **Principal-level scope**, significant experience driving security architecture, technical direction and cybersecurity topics across multiple engineering teams or an organization.

#### *Nice to have*

- Experience securing embedded systems, firmware, robotics, IoT, industrial technology, aerospace, medical devices, automotive systems or other cyber-physical systems.

- Automotive cybersecurity or vehicle systems security experience.

- Familiarity with **ISO 21434, UNECE R155/R156, IEC 62443, the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** or similar standards and regulations.

- Experience conducting **TARA** or comparable structured security risk assessments.

- Knowledge of embedded Linux, secure boot, secure software updates, hardware-backed security or device lifecycle security.
