# What We Do

Rillet serves accounting and finance teams, the financial brains of their companies. Our job is to help them run the numbers with impossible speed, accuracy, and insight.

Rillet is the AI-native ERP built for modern finance teams. We help companies automate the most critical parts of finance, from closing the books and recognizing revenue to consolidating entities and reporting to investors. More than 600 companies run on Rillet, including Mercor, Scribe, and Function Health, and we partner with leading accounting firms including EY and KPMG.

This huge market is ours to take. We’ve raised $200M+ from Sequoia, a16z, ICONIQ, Bain Capital Ventures, Oak HC/FT, and other leading investors to build the financial infrastructure for the next generation of companies and help every business run its numbers at the speed of AI.

## Who We Need

We are building industry-defining accounting and finance software. We are looking for a proactive, detail-oriented Accounting Solutions Consultant to join our team and help lead the successful onboarding of new customers onto Rillet.

This role is ideal for someone with a strong accounting foundation who is excited to work directly with customers and help finance teams modernize how they operate. You may come from Big 4 audit, accounting advisory, finance transformation, or an operational accounting role at a high-growth technology company. You do not need to have formally led ERP implementations before, but you do need to be systems-minded, highly organized, customer-oriented, and eager to learn quickly in a fast-paced startup environment.

We’re primarily looking for teammates who are based near our New York City or San Francisco offices, or open to relocating.** For this role, we’re also open to remote candidates based anywhere in the United States.** Our hybrid schedule brings office-based team members together in person on Tuesdays and Thursdays, plus one additional flexible day each week. Some roles may spend more time in the office depending on the needs of the team.

## What You'll Do

-

Lead key parts of customer onboarding and deployment, helping ensure a smooth and timely implementation experience

-

Partner with internal teams across Product, Engineering, and Customer Success to execute implementation plans and solve customer problems quickly

-

Learn and analyze client accounting and finance workflows, then translate those needs into clear requirements and practical solutions

-

Guide customers through onboarding, training, change management, and adoption

-

Support data migration, reconciliations, systems setup, and integration coordination

-

Track milestones, drive follow-through, and keep internal and external stakeholders aligned throughout the onboarding process

-

Identify risks early, escalate issues quickly, and help keep customer launches on track

-

Build trust with customers by being responsive, thoughtful, and solutions-oriented

-

Maintain clear documentation and provide regular status updates to customers and internal stakeholders
