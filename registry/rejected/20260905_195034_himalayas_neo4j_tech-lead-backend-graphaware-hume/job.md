You'll be sought out across the organisation for technical guidance on graph architecture, and expected to anticipate issues at the product level before they surface. [Neo4j](https://himalayas.app/companies/neo4j) sits at the heart of the product, we need someone who goes beyond using it well, and actively tracks new features, tests them against real workloads, and identifies where they can move the product forward. Working hands-on with [Neo4j](https://himalayas.app/companies/neo4j) at the heart of the product, you will drive the architecture that lets us model, query, and scale the knowledge graphs our users depend on.**
