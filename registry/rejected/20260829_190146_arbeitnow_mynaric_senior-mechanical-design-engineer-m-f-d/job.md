**Your way into the future**

Join us as a **Senior ****Mechanical Design Engineer (m/f/d) **and work with us on changing the future!

**What awaits you at Mynaric**

- You develop and design assemblies for our optical communication terminals in a multidisciplinary team,
- You work independently with a high level of responsibility,
- You create and maintain the assembly models throughout their life cycle,
- Tolerancing and verification of the systems are part of your tasks,
- You derive system and subsystem requirements and define interfaces and envelopes,
- You prepare the technical documentation including manufacturing documents,
- You perform simple strength and modal analysis for initial dimensioning of the parts,
- You excel in documenting your results and convincingly present them to the project team and in reviews.

**What you bring with you**

- University Degree in mechanical engineering, aerospace engineering or related field.
- 5 or more years' experience in the design of optomechanical systems, with knowledge of design principles and tolerance analysis.
- Passion for finding technical solutions for high-precision applications in challenging environments.
- Hands-on mentality and willingness to build and test your own designs.
- Your design solutions reflect your knowledge of manufacturing processes and serial production.
- Solid understanding of design calculations and GD&T.
- Strong knowledge of 3D CAD software and PLM systems, ideally Siemens Teamcenter/NX.
- Results-oriented, reliable, and pro-active individual, who works independently and responsibly.
- Absolute team player with strong organizational, communication and documentation skills.
- Good communication skills in written and spoken English.
- You enjoy working in an innovative, dynamic, and team-oriented environment.
