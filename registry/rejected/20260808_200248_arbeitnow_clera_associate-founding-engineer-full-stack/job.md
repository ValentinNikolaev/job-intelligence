### About the Role

Join a small, fast-moving team building an **AI-powered digital assistant for B2B sales teams** — a multi-platform product spanning a React Web App, an Electron Desktop Client, a Swift Mobile App, and two backend services. As an **Associate Founding Engineer (Full-Stack)**, you'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with founding engineers from the very early days, receive direct mentorship from senior technical leaders, and grow your ownership and responsibility as you demonstrate your strengths. This is a rare opportunity to shape a product at the ground floor.

### What You'll Do

-

Support and learn from founding engineers across the full product stack.

-

Contribute to core features spanning Web, Desktop, Mobile, and backend services.

-

Receive hands-on mentorship from senior technical leaders.

-

Participate in product and architectural discussions from day one.

-

Take on increasing responsibility as you build a track record within the team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Solid foundation in **TypeScript** and modern **React**.

**Nice to have / additional strengths:**

-

Demonstrable experience in one or more of: **mobile (Swift)**, **desktop (Electron/React)**, or **web backend (Node.js, REST/WebSockets)**.

-

Proven track record delivering features across multiple platforms (Web, Electron/React Desktop, and Swift Mobile) within a single product.

-

Comfort working in GitHub-based teams with code reviews and CI/CD pipelines.

-

Experience participating in early-stage product discussions and influencing architectural decisions.

-

Open-source contributions or showcaseable side projects that demonstrate initiative.

-

University degree in Mathematics, Computer Science, or an adjacent discipline.

-

Enthusiasm for fast-paced, early-stage startup environments.

*Note: Visa sponsorship is **not available** for this role.*

### Location & Work Arrangement

This is a **hybrid role** based in **Munich, Germany**. Regular on-site presence at the Munich office is required as part of the role.

### About the Company

The company is an early-stage, privately held SaaS startup founded in 2022 with a team of around 20 people. It builds AI-driven tooling that automates the administrative burden for B2B sales teams — including real-time note-taking (without audio recording, GDPR-compliant), CRM updates, meeting preparation, and follow-up management. The platform integrates with leading CRM and calendar tools and is designed to improve CRM adoption, conversion rates, and revenue forecasting accuracy.
