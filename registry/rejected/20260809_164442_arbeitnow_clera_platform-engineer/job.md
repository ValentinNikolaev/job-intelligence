### About the Role

A fast-growing, Berlin-based enterprise AI platform startup is looking for a **Platform Engineer** to help build and maintain the backbone of their multi-tenant SaaS infrastructure. Founded in 2023 and backed by notable investors, the company serves thousands of enterprise customers and is scaling rapidly. You'll be a critical hire on a small, high-calibre team, working on-site in Berlin.

This role sits at the intersection of cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and security — partnering closely with both internal Product Engineers and external customer IT teams to enable reliable, compliant, and scalable AI deployments across diverse cloud environments.

**Visa sponsorship is available for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Manage and continuously improve CI/CD pipelines for automated testing, building, and deployment

-

Deploy and maintain the platform across multiple customer cloud environments, including **GCP, AWS, Azure, and OpenShift**

-

Automate infrastructure provisioning using Infrastructure as Code tools (**Terraform, Bicep**)

-

Monitor and optimize application performance and infrastructure costs

-

Ensure security and compliance standards are upheld across all cloud environments

-

Collaborate with Product Engineers to support the rollout of new features

-

Troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure and deployment issues promptly

-

Document deployment processes and maintain runbooks for different customer environments

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

3+ years of software or platform engineering experience

-

Native-level German or **minimum B2 German proficiency** — required for direct customer communications with enterprise IT teams

-

Proven hands-on experience with platform-level engineering responsibilities (deployment, infrastructure ops, reliability)

-

Experience deploying and operating enterprise platforms across **Azure, AWS, GCP, and/or OpenShift**

-

Hands-on experience with **Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Azure Container Apps, and GitHub Actions**

-

Experience with **PostgreSQL and Redis** in production environments

-

Solid understanding of security best practices in cloud deployments

-

Strong CS fundamentals and ability to implement and uphold platform reliability standards

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience with infrastructure cost optimisation and observability tooling

-

Familiarity with Bicep for Azure infrastructure automation

-

Background working with enterprise customers or in a customer-facing infrastructure capacity
