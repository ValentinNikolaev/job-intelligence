🎯 Your main goal will be to make n8n’s IT operations reliable, secure, and scalable while reducing manual work through thoughtful, maintainable automation.

You’ll combine hands-on IT operations with continuous process improvement across the following areas:

### IDENTITY, ACCESS, AND SAAS OPERATIONS

-

Own day-to-day identity and access management across Okta, Google Workspace, GitHub, and connected SaaS applications.

-

Manage employee and application lifecycle workflows, including provisioning, access changes, group assignments, troubleshooting, and deprovisioning.

-

Maintain an accurate SaaS inventory and support application administration, lifecycle management, access governance, and account reconciliation.

### EMPLOYEE AND ENDPOINT LIFECYCLE

-

Deliver reliable and timely onboarding and offboarding experiences in close partnership with People, Finance, managers, and application owners.

-

Manage and troubleshoot n8n’s primarily macOS device fleet through our endpoint management tooling.

-

Support hardware procurement, device lifecycle workflows, and secure endpoint operations for a growing distributed team.

### AUTOMATION AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

-

Design and build secure automations for recurring IT processes using n8n, APIs, scripting, and AI-assisted tooling where appropriate.

-

Redesign manual workflows so they become observable, documented, reliable, and easier to operate at scale.

-

Build automations with appropriate logging, error handling, monitoring, ownership, and human approval steps for sensitive decisions.

### SUPPORT, DOCUMENTATION, AND COMPLIANCE

-

Provide responsive L1/L2 IT support while using recurring issues to identify opportunities for systemic improvement.

-

Create and maintain clear runbooks, process documentation, escalation paths, and operational controls.

-

Support security and compliance activities, including access reviews, audit evidence collection, SOC 2, and ISO 27001-related processes.

## REQUIREMENTS

### **Must-haves**

-

**🧰 Modern IT operations experience:** You have 2–4 years of experience in IT systems administration, SaaS administration, IAM, workplace engineering, or a similar role within a SaaS, cloud-first, or distributed company.

-

**🔐 Identity and access management:** You have hands-on experience with Google Workspace and an identity provider such as Okta or Microsoft Entra ID.

-

**🔄 Employee lifecycle management:** You have managed onboarding, offboarding, groups, roles, access changes, application assignments, and secure deprovisioning.

-

**💻 macOS endpoint management:** You have administered macOS devices through an MDM platform such as Kandji, Jamf, JumpCloud, Workspace ONE, or a comparable tool.

-

**⚙️ Workflow automation:** You can demonstrate how you have improved or automated an operational process using APIs, scripting, workflow automation platforms, or AI-assisted tooling.

-

**🔌 Integration fundamentals:** You have working knowledge of REST APIs, webhooks, JSON, authentication concepts, and integration troubleshooting.

-

**🔍 Technical troubleshooting:** You can diagnose workflow and systems issues using logs, API responses, system events, and other operational data.

-

**🧭 Ownership and judgment:** You’re known for strong ownership, sound judgment, rigor, and a thoughtful approach to privileged access, sensitive data, and high-impact changes.

-

**🤝 Service mindset:** You communicate clearly, document your work thoroughly, and care about delivering a reliable experience for internal users.

-

**🌍 Autonomous working style:** You’re comfortable prioritising and making progress independently in an evolving, distributed environment without relying on highly defined procedures for every task.

### **Nice-to-haves**

-

**🔗 Workflow automation platforms:** You have experience with n8n or another workflow automation platform such as Zapier or Make.

-

**⌨️ Scripting:** You have basic scripting experience using Python, JavaScript, or Bash.

-

**🪪 Identity protocols:** You have worked with SAML, OIDC, SSO, and SCIM.

-

**🐙 GitHub administration:** You have experience managing GitHub organisations, teams, repositories, and permissions.

-

**🤖 Reliable AI workflows:** You have designed AI-assisted workflows that include validation, human approvals, monitoring, error handling, and fallback paths.

-

**🛡️ Automation reliability:** You understand concepts such as retries, idempotency, secrets management, auditability, and failure handling.

-

**📋 Security and compliance:** You’re familiar with SOC 2, ISO 27001, access reviews, or audit evidence collection.

-

**🚀 Scaling environments:** You have worked in an early-stage or rapidly growing IT function where processes and systems were still being established.
