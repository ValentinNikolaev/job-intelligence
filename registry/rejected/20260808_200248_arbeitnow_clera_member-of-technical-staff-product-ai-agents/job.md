### About the Role

We are an early-stage enterprise AI automation company headquartered in Munich, Germany. Our platform creates AI coworkers that observe, learn, and take over complex but repeatable workflows — freeing teams in professional services, insurance, financial services, and customer support from hours of manual busywork.

As a **Member of Technical Staff, Product**, you will turn observed workflow data into production-grade AI agents that work autonomously on behalf of customers. You'll own the full lifecycle — from talking directly with customers to understand their operations, through building and deploying agents, to monitoring and iterating in production. This is a high-ownership, hands-on role at the core of what we ship.

### What You'll Do

-

Convert workflow observations into AI agents that perform work autonomously and reliably.

-

Partner closely with customers to understand their operations and surface high-value automation opportunities.

-

Ship automations that replace hours of repetitive manual effort and measurably improve team efficiency.

-

Own production AI agents end-to-end: design, build, deploy, monitor, and continuously improve them.

-

Run rapid experiments with new models, prompting techniques, and use cases; benchmark results and ship what works.

-

Design thoughtful AI × Human interfaces that make automation practical and trustworthy for real users.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

5+ years of experience in ML/AI product development, with a track record of building and delivering production-grade AI agents.

-

Hands-on experience shipping LLM-based systems in production — not just prototypes.

-

Strong Python programming skills; comfortable owning a full production Python codebase.

-

Experience owning AI agents end-to-end: build, deploy, monitor, and improve.

-

Demonstrated ability to turn observed real-world workflows into deployable AI automations.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Experience designing AI interfaces for effective human-AI collaboration.

-

Familiarity with model experimentation, benchmarking, and systematic selection of approaches.

-

Experience with observability and monitoring for AI systems (metrics, dashboards, alerting).

-

Comfort operating in early-stage, fast-moving startup environments with high autonomy.

-

Customer-facing experience gathering requirements and iterating on solutions based on real feedback.

### Location

This role is **on-site in Munich, Bavaria, Germany**. Candidates must be willing to work from our Munich office. **Visa sponsorship is not available** — candidates must already have the right to work in Germany.
