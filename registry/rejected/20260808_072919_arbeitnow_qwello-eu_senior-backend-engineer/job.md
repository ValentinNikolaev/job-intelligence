Our mission at **Qwello** is to revolutionize the charging industry; we are committed to turning the world from combustion to electric. We design, build, and operate charging infrastructure for the public space. We excel in simplicity, user focus, and engineering excellence. We value diversity of thought, background, and approaches, and foster a culture of belonging. We understand that the best products are built by excellent teams, not individual superstars. Be part of shaping the E-mobility of the future!

To reach our goal, we are looking for a **Senior Backend Engineer **to join our Backend Team. At Qwello, you will architect, design, and implement new backend services (typically in Rust and Python) to support our growing fleet of self-designed/built charging stations being deployed across Europe. You will also design and implement backend-to-backend systems connecting the Qwello systems with the services of Qwello's partners. Reliability and craftsmanship are important to you, so you design with automatic testability, resilience, introspection & observability in mind. Security is important to you. You are an efficient engineer, focused on the fast execution of your craft. You are a good communicator, a free thinker, have an inquisitive mindset, and an eye for detail. You enjoy sharing your knowledge with your colleagues and mentoring junior team members. You are comfortable working in a startup, collaborating in an interdisciplinary team, and willing to contribute, with your skills, experience, and enthusiasm, to help us build a successful business. As a proactive and committed team member, we continuously motivate you to continue learning and developing your skills.

Let's power the future together! ⚡

**Your Responsibilities 🚀 **

- Design and implement new backend services and serverless code in Rust and Python

- Drive the transformation of our system architecture to meet our business needs and scale to our goals

- Take part in strategic and execution planning, code reviews, design reviews, and hiring

- Maintain and improve the Qwello backend as part of the backend team

- Work in close collaboration with our partners to integrate our services with theirs

- Take ownership, along with the rest of the team, for building and refining our service as the business expands

**Your Profile ✨**

- Proven experience as a Software Engineer implementing complex, larger projects

- Expert-level Python implementation skills

- Experience with Rust as a development language would be a big plus; the willingness to learn it is a must

- Solid experience with architecting & implementing distributed systems

- Hands-on off-premise cloud experience, preferably AWS, but Azure, Google Cloud, or similar via laC (Terraform) is also great

- A make-it-happen attitude, helping the team to deliver on changing the world from combustion to electric

- Ability to self-organize and lead in an environment of changing priorities; willingness to work on multiple tasks/content switching

- Fluency in English; German language knowledge is a nice-to-have
