**Senior Accountant (m/f/d) – Yepoda, Berlin (Hybrid)**

**About Yepoda**

At Yepoda, Korean effectiveness meets sustainable solutions: our mindfully produced skincare products are made with love in Korea. Our mission is to bring the best beauty innovations from Korea straight to Europe – without ever compromising on our values, quality, and sustainability. All our products are made with the best natural and active ingredients, are vegan and cruelty-free. We aim to make the K-Beauty routine easy, effective, and fun.

We are a beauty start-up founded in 2020 and located in the heart of Berlin. Our team is composed of over 130 brilliant human beings who came from all over the world to write Yepoda’s story actively.

We are looking for a Senior Accountant to join our team at Yepoda. This role will involve overseeing financial operations, ensuring compliance with German accounting standards, and supporting the team as we continue to scale.

**Key Responsibilities:**

-

Oversee day-to-day financial operations including bookkeeping, invoicing, and payment processing

-

Assist and take ownership with monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements preparation

-

Support during the statutory audits and ensure compliance with tax and legal regulations

-

Support on implementing ERP system and setting up new projects and flows from scratch on

-

Prepare cash flow statements and monitor cash on hand

-

Collaborate cross-functionally to support other departments with financial insights

**Requirements**:

-

Degree in Finance, Accounting, or related field

-

6+ years of relevant work experience in finance and accounting

-

Fluent in English, German/Italian/French/Spanish are a plus

-

Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and hands-on mentality

-

Process driven and excited about setting up new projects and flows from scratch on

-

Experience in e-commerce and cross-border VAT is a plus

-

Knowledge of German GAAP (HGB) and experience with Oracle Netsuite are a plus
