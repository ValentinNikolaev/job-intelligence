## About Us

Constructor is the only search and product discovery platform tailor-made for enterprise e-commerce where conversions matter. Constructor's AI-first solutions make it easier for shoppers to discover products they want to buy and for e-commerce teams to deliver highly personalized experiences that drive impressive results. Optimizing specifically for e-commerce metrics like revenue, conversion rate, and profit, Constructor generates consistent $10M+ lifts for some of the biggest brands in e-commerce, such as Sephora, Petco, home24, Maxeda Brands, Birkenstock, and The Very Group.

Constructor is a U.S. based company that was founded in 2015 by Eli Finkelshteyn and Dan McCormick. For more, visit: [constructor.io](http://constructor.io).

## About The Role

We're looking for a passionate and skilled backend engineer to join our Searchandizing team. You'll be responsible for creating and maintaining the tools used by merchandisers of global e-commerce brands to manage their product discovery experience. You'll focus on delivering features for our Searchandizing dashboard, finding innovative ways to improve its usability, and ultimately enhancing the product discovery experience for shoppers worldwide.
