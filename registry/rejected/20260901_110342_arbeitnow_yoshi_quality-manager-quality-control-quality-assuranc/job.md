**About the role:**

We are a niche beauty startup dedicated to developing high-quality cosmetic products with a strong focus on natural ingredients and modern formulation science. We combine scientific standards with a conscious approach to sustainability, ingredient transparency, and performance-driven skincare.

**So, what can you expect to work on?**

-

Own incoming quality control for raw materials and packaging, including sampling plans, specification checks, and supplier certificate of analysis review.

-

Manage batch release: reviewing production records, in-process checks, and finished-product testing before a batch is cleared for sale.

-

Coordinate microbiological, stability, and preservative-efficacy (challenge) testing with external laboratories, and interpret results against acceptance criteria.

-

Lead investigation of out-of-specification results and contamination events, including root cause analysis, containment, and corrective and preventive action (CAPA) plans, in coordination with suppliers.

-

Maintain and continuously improve Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for quality control and quality assurance processes.

-

Own the customer complaint process: logging, root cause analysis, corrective action, and closing the loop with customer service and production.

-

Maintain quality records and documentation in line with ISO 22716 (Cosmetics GMP) principles.

-

Support internal audits of quality processes and prepare documentation for external audits and certification bodies.

-

Evaluate new raw materials and suppliers from a quality and testing perspective ahead of onboarding, working closely with Sourcing and Development.

-

Act as the day-to-day quality contact for Production and Development, flagging quality risks before they reach the market.

**What you offer us**

-

3+ years of experience in Quality Control or Quality Assurance within cosmetics, personal care, or a comparable regulated industry.

-

Degree in a natural science field (Chemistry, Cosmetic Science, Biology, or comparable), or equivalent hands-on experience.

-

Working knowledge of ISO 22716 (Cosmetics GMP) and cosmetic microbiological testing and challenge testing (e.g. ISO 11930) principles.

-

Practical experience with root cause analysis and CAPA processes.

-

Comfortable reviewing batch records, certificates of analysis, and laboratory test reports independently.

-

Experience working with external testing laboratories and coordinating results against acceptance criteria.

-

Structured, hands-on, and comfortable owning documentation and quality decisions in a small-team startup environment.

-

Business-fluent **English and German**, other languages are always a plus.
