Hi there! We are Testlio, a global software testing company with its own freelance network. Our freelancers test apps from companies across the globe. Testlio is a great place for people who value the freedom and flexibility of remote work.

At the moment, we are looking for Business Owners based in Italy to collaborate in testing a popular platform's new user onboarding flow for businesses.

Your testing will help identify and resolve issues for business owners across Italy who wish to create PayPal business accounts. We're specifically looking for individuals who own **Limited Companies (Società a Responsabilità Limitata - S.R.L. / S.R.L.S.)** in **Italy **and speak **Italian**. Your task would be to create a Paypal Business account for your company and record any bugs along the way. You will be acting like a new PayPal user, sharing your honest feedback about the experience. With your help, the PayPal team will be amble to improve the experience of new users!

Note: We cannot collaborate if you have an existing PayPal account tied to your Limited Company. It is OK if you have a personal PayPal account, as this will not disrupt the testing flow.

To participate in this project, you will need to create an account on our platform. Through this account, we can assign you testing tasks. You will be asked to use our website for defect tracking and will receive materials to prepare you for testing.

- Schedule: You can create your schedule and work on other testing runs that interest you.

## Requirements

- Age: You have to be at least 18 years old.

- Location: Must be physically located in Italy.

- Availability: 30 minutes for your Testlio account setup and 2 hours for the actual PayPal testing task, which will be paid.

- Devices: iOS device (iPhone or iPad), Android device (Android Tablet or Phone), Laptop/Desktop.

- Experience: Software testing experience is a plus, but if you are new to testing, we offer free courses to help you learn and grow. We offer a preparatory course that will help you learn more about testing on our website.

- Type of work: short-term, freelance.

- Pay rate: The pay rate for this is **30 USD/hr**.
