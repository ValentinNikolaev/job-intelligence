#### About the role

We are seeking a detail-oriented SAP S/4HANA Data Controller to ensure the accuracy, integrity, and governance of master data across our SAP S/4HANA environment. You will play a key role in maintaining high-quality business data, supporting business operations, and enabling reliable reporting and decision-making.

Working closely with Business, Sales, Procurement, Supply Chain, R&D, Manufacturing, Planning, and IT teams, you will ensure that master data complies with company standards and SAP best practices. SAP S/4HANA relies heavily on accurate master data to support end-to-end business processes across finance, procurement, manufacturing, and sales.

#### Your Responsibilities

- Create, maintain, and validate SAP S/4HANA master data, including:

- *Material Master *

- *Business Partners *

- *Customers and Vendors *

- *Cost Centers *

- *Profit Centers *

- *General Ledger accounts *

- *Bills of Materials (BOMs) *

- Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and completeness across SAP modules

- Monitor data quality through regular audits and validation checks

- Support data governance policies, standards, and procedures

- Investigate and resolve master data issues in collaboration with business stakeholders

- Develop and maintain master data documentation and process guides

- Support users with data-related queries and provide training where required

- Work with IT and business teams to improve master data processes and automation

- Compliance with internal controls, audit requirements, and regulatory standards

#### Your Profile

- Apprenticeship or Studies in a technical field, state-certified technician or comparable qualification

- Very good SAP S/4HANA knowledge

- First professional experience in a comparable position

- Very good knowledge of German (C-Level) and good English skills (B-Level)

- High-quality awareness

- You are characterized by your strong teamwork and communication skills

- Your target-oriented way of working rounds off your profile
