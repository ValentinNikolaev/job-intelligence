### About the Role

This is a founding-team software engineering role at an early-stage, AI-powered construction tech company based in Berlin. The company builds browser-based software that automates the generation of building design plans — covering technical building equipment and electrical schematics — dramatically reducing planning timelines from weeks to hours.

As one of the first Software Engineers on the team, you will have a direct impact on core architecture, infrastructure, and plugin/backend design decisions. You'll help shape the tech stack, deliver prototypes, and drive the MVP to production across multiple ecosystems — all within a fast-moving, high-ownership environment.

### What You'll Do

-

Contribute to the design and delivery of early-stage prototypes.

-

Ensure the MVP is production-ready and built to scale.

-

Deliver robust solutions across multiple software ecosystems.

-

Write clean, maintainable, and well-structured code.

-

Process and handle large volumes of CAD data efficiently.

-

Participate in backend and plugin architecture decisions alongside the founding technical team.

-

Contribute to infrastructure setup and tooling decisions.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

Proficiency in **C# or Python** for backend software development, underpinned by strong computer science fundamentals.

**Strongly valued:**

-

Experience with **CAD data processing** and implementing data structures and algorithms for scalable software.

-

Proven ability to design, build, and ship **production-ready MVPs**.

-

Hands-on experience contributing to **infrastructure setup** and backend/plugin architecture.

-

Comfort working across multiple ecosystems and with large, complex datasets.

-

An early-stage mindset — you're energised by ambiguity, ownership, and building from scratch.

Background in AEC (Architecture, Engineering & Construction) software, or experience with tools such as Revit or similar CAD/BIM platforms, is a plus.
