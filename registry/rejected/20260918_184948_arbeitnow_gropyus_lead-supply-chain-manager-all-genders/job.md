**About The Company**

GROPYUS is a technology-based construction company focused on building multi-story residential buildings. Thanks to its prefabricated building system with various design options, industrial offsite construction, and fully digitalized processes, the company manufactures aspirational, sustainable, and affordable homes using timber construction methods. GROPYUS is using scalable construction and manufacturing solutions to tap into a future market, boost Europe's strength in innovation, while also playing a substantial role in improving sustainability.

### **About the position**

As **Lead Supply Chain Manager**, you will take on a key role at our production site in **Eppingen-Richen**. GROPYUS develops and manufactures innovative timber-hybrid modules for serial and sustainable construction. To ensure our modules are produced and delivered on time, efficiently, and to the highest quality standards, we rely on a high-performing and resilient supply chain.

In this strategic leadership role, you will be responsible for the continuous development and management of our supply chain processes across the entire value chain. Your primary objective is to ensure a stable supply chain within the context of modular construction, guaranteeing reliable material availability, efficient production operations, and on-time delivery of our projects.

You will lead a team of approximately **15 to 20 employees **(5 direct reports), with further growth potential, covering administration, planning and scheduling, production planning, warehouse management, and operational logistics, including forklift operators and loading personnel. In doing so, you will create the conditions for seamless collaboration between production, procurement, logistics, and external partners.

You will be responsible for:

- Strategic and operational responsibility for the supply chain at the production site

- Ensuring a stable and efficient material flow from procurement through production to final delivery

- Functional and disciplinary leadership of a team of currently 15 to 20 employees across supply chain, warehouse, and operational logistics functions

- Continuous improvement of planning, scheduling, and logistics processes to increase transparency, efficiency, and delivery performance

- Close collaboration with procurement, production, and other stakeholders to optimize the value chain

- Analysis of market, sourcing, and supplier developments and implementation of appropriate measures

- Identification and execution of optimization opportunities related to costs, inventory, and processes

- Ensuring compliance with quality, safety, and regulatory requirements within the supply chain

- Building and maintaining strong relationships with suppliers and logistics partners

- Developing KPIs and management mechanisms to drive sustainable performance improvements
