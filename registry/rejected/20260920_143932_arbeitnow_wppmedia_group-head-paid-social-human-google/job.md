## **This is what you move:**

As **Group Head Paid Social**, you will lead the strategic direction and high-level delivery of cross-market campaigns for our global key account **Google**, serving as the primary strategic interface between senior clients, platform partners, and internal specialist teams.

- **Strategic Account Leadership:** Owning the overarching paid social roadmap for Google workstreams, ensuring all campaign strategies perfectly align with business KPIs, measurement priorities, and agency-wide best practices.

- **Senior Stakeholder Management:** Building and nurturing high-level relationships with key client stakeholders, platform partners (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snap, Pinterest), and internal leadership teams.

- **Process & Workflow Governance:** Driving operational excellence by identifying and implementing improvements in paid social processes, briefing standards, and campaign workflows across all markets.

- **Strategic Client Advisory:** Acting as an expert partner to internal teams, media and marketing teams on client side to challenge briefs, refine objectives, and guide stakeholders toward the most impactful media approaches.

- **Innovation & Testing Leadership:** Directing the development of advanced testing plans and pro-actively identifying industry trends, alpha/beta product releases, and growth opportunities.

- **Regional Quality Assurance:** Taking ultimate accountability for channel controls, campaign compliance, and leading the regional development of paid social standards within your team.

## **The following qualities distinguish you:**

- **Proven Agency Background:** Extensive (4–6 years) hands-on digital agency experience managing multi-million dollar paid social campaigns across the EMEA region.

- **Social Platform Mastery:** In-depth, expert-level knowledge of Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snap, and Pinterest, ideally backed by industry credentials (e.g., Meta Blueprint Certified).

- **Bilingual Communication Skills:** Professional-level fluency in English (written and spoken) is mandatory to confidently lead daily communication across diverse, global teams and senior client structures.

- **Data-Driven Mindset:** Strong analytical skills with a natural affinity for numbers and the ability to extract actionable strategic insights from complex, raw data sets.

- **Translational Capability:** Outstanding interpersonal skills and the unique ability to act as a bridge, translating highly technical platform data into simplified, high-level strategic advice.

- **AdTech & Automation Expertise:** Deep understanding of programmatic optimization levers, ideally complemented by experience with third-party tools like Smartly.io or Sprinklr.
