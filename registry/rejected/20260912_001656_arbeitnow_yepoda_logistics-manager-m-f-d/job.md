We are hiring a Logistics Manager to help scale Yepoda's global inbound logistics operations across air and ocean freight. In this hands-on role, you will own end-to-end shipment flow from manufacturing sites to warehouses, and balance strategic freight forwarder management with day-to-day operational execution.

###
**Your Tasks**

-

Manage end-to-end inbound logistics from manufacturing sites to warehouses, covering international air and ocean freight.

-

Lead freight forwarder management, including rate negotiations, provider nominations, shipment bookings, service performance, and issue resolution.

-

Ensure proactive shipment tracking and exception management, addressing delays, capacity constraints, documentation issues, and other logistics disruptions.

-

Provide customs brokers with accurate and timely shipment, product, and commercial data required for customs clearance, and support resolution of clearance-related queries.

-

Maintain accurate shipment, transport, and inventory movement data in the ERP system.

-

Coordinate inter-warehouse transfers based on transfer requirements defined by Procurement, including transport arrangements, system transactions, and follow-up through to completion.

-

Monitor and report key logistics KPIs, including freight cost, transit time, on-time delivery, and service-provider performance.

-

Drive logistics optimization and operational excellence through strategic, data-driven decision-making and continuous process improvement.

-

Act as the main point of contact for logistics topics, coordinating with manufacturing sites, warehouses, freight forwarders, customs brokers, and internal stakeholders.

-

Mentor and develop a junior colleague, fostering knowledge sharing, capability building, and continuous growth within the team.

-

Act as backup for the Fulfillment Manager during planned absence, ensuring continuity of critical fulfillment activities.

### **Your Profile **

-

Bachelor’s or master’s degree in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, or a related field.

-

Min. 4 years of professional experience in an international logistics or supply chain environment.

-

Good understanding of global transportation, Incoterms, and customs-related requirements.

-

Commercial mindset with confidence in negotiations and supplier/service-provider discussions.

-

Strong analytical skills and confidence working with ERP systems, data, KPIs, and Google Workspace.

-

Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.

-

Proactive and solution-oriented, with a strong sense of ownership and sound decision-making skills.

-

Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills across different functions and external partners.

-

Fluent in English; additional languages are an advantage.
