**TL;DR** — Construction software looks like it was designed in 2000. Because most of it was. As Product Asociate, you define how alago looks, feels and behaves.

alago brings AI-powered project management to the construction industry. As our **Product Associate**, you'll own a core product areas end to end: how messy project data becomes an early warning that a €200M project is about to go wrong.

**That's what we're fixing.** 15 people, 30+ customers, **€1M+ pre-seed** from leading construction-tech VCs and one of Germany's largest project developers. The product is live and used daily on real construction sites — which means the interesting work is only starting.

## Aufgaben

**What you'll actually do**

- **Design the product.** Take features from rough idea to high-fidelity UI. You'll work on the tools construction teams open every single day and make them feel effortless.

- **Make AI feel right.** Define how AI shows up in Alago and what it feels like to work with it. Nobody has solved this well for construction yet.

- **Prototype and spec.** Build clickable prototypes in Figma or with AI tools, and contribute to specs so the team can ship fast.

- **Run discovery.** Talk to users, research how other products solve similar problems, and turn what you find into concepts the team can build on.

- **Grow the design system.** Design components in Figma and work with engineers to build them.

- **Make Alago look good everywhere.** Brand, landing pages, launch materials, everything that carries the product outward.

## Qualifikation

**Must-haves**

- Enrolled student in design, HCI, media informatics, architecture, or a related field.

- C1 German: you'll talk to German construction project managers.

- Figma proficiency: components, prototyping, systems thinking.

- Technical enough to collaborate: you won't write production code, but you understand how web apps work and can work alongside engineers.

**Bonus points**

- You've designed for AI products before and have a take on how generative features should feel.

- You have strong opinions on products you love and hate. You notice details other people miss.

- Experience with Figma Make, Claude, Cursor, Lovable or similar.

- Past work in B2B SaaS or complex enterprise tools.

- Interest in animation (Rive, Lottie), iconography or illustration.

- Side projects that show you care about craft.
