**TL;DR:** We’re looking for a hands-on Talent Lead to own and build our hiring function from scratch. You’ll personally drive hiring across the company, build scalable recruiting processes, track hiring performance, and work closely with the Head of People and hiring managers across GTM, Ops, Research, and Product.

# **About us**

[White Circle](https://whitecircle.ai) is an AI Safety company building the safety, reliability, and optimization layer for AI systems. At the core of our platform are policies – simple natural-language rules that define what an AI model should and shouldn’t do. We automatically test, enforce, and continuously improve these policies at scale.

-

We’ve raised $11M from top funds, founders, and senior leaders at OpenAI, Anthropic, HuggingFace, Mistral, DeepMind, Datadog, Sentry, and others

-

We process over one hundred million API calls every month

-

We fine-tune and train our own LLMs so they run faster and cheaper than any open or proprietary model

We’re a small, highly focused team. If you want to work deeply on hard problems, see your work ship to production quickly, and influence how AI safety is actually built – you’re the one we need.

# **You will:**

-

Own and run the hiring process end-to-end (sourcing, screening, interviews, and closing)

-

Personally source and hire candidates across the company (this is a hands-on recruiting role)

-

Build our hiring processes and infrastructure from scratch, including interview loops, scorecards, pipelines, and candidate tracking

-

Work closely with hiring managers to define roles, hiring priorities, and candidate profiles

-

Drive hiring across a wide range of functions, including GTM, Ops, Research, Product, and Engineering

-

Lead international hiring across the US, EU, UK, and other markets as we expand

-

Build and maintain hiring metrics and reporting, including pipeline conversion, time to hire, source performance, and offer acceptance

-

Use hiring data to identify bottlenecks and continuously improve the recruiting process

-

Build and maintain strong sourcing channels across different functions and geographies

-

Develop a strong candidate experience and represent White Circle throughout the hiring process

-

Help define and improve our approach to hiring, including how we assess candidates and make hiring decisions

-

Partner with leadership on long-term talent planning and hiring strategy
