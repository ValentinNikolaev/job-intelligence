## **What you will do:**

-

Being part of the Office team, you are responsible for the well-being of our 380+ employees in Hamburg and creating a superb work environment for them

-

You will be one of the contact persons for all colleagues with any office-related questions or feedback

-

You keep things in order and are always aware of what’s needed to maintain a great office environment

-

You monitor stock levels of office supplies, food & drinks and make timely orders to ensure that essentials are always available

-

You help to organize exciting events, from team get-togethers to big celebrations like our annual Christmas party

-

You coordinate repairs and maintenance in the offices, keep in contact with facility managers and landlords, as well as cleaning services

-

You manage our in-house gym and our trainer schedule

-

You welcome national and international guests, ensuring they feel comfortable and support our internal travel management

-

You manage onboarding for new employees and provide them with what they need

-

You support the expansion of our offices (not only in Hamburg)

-

Depending on your experience and interests, you take over responsibility for your own projects

## **Who you are:**

-

You like to help, and you are service- and solution-oriented

-

You are engaged, proactive, and you like to take over responsibility for your own projects

-

You love working in a team and jumping in where a hand is needed

-

You are well organized and can adjust multiple tasks flexibly

-

You are diligent and enjoy bringing things into order - whether it’s organizing events, sorting things out, or stocking our office fridges

-

You have an eye for detail and also understand the big picture of office processes

-

You have at least 5 years of experience working in Office Management or similar roles

-

Ideally, you have expertise in relevant areas, e.g. event management, building and office administration, and contract negotiation

-

You communicate in conversational level German and have fluent English abilities

-

On-site Commitment: This role is 100% on-site at our Hamburg headquarters to ensure effective communication and hands-on management. But no worries, our office is amazing!
