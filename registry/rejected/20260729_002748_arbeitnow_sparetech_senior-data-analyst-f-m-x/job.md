# **Your Mission**

We are seeking a Senior Data Analyst to lead data quality, maintenance, and data import processes while helping advance the organization’s analytics capability. You will take ownership of SPARETECH's heart—the Golden Layer—by acquiring, importing, and maintaining spare parts product data to increase its value. You will ensure data accuracy and integrity, design and lead reporting and analytics frameworks, and deliver actionable insights to support business decision-making.

# **We offer you the opportunity to**

- Manage the acquisition, import, and maintenance of product data in SPARETECH's proprietary database while ensuring accuracy and quality.

- Conduct thorough quality checks on incoming catalogues and ensure high database integrity by investigating and resolving reported product issues in collaboration with the Data and Partner Management teams.

- Work collaboratively across teams to gather business requirements and lead the development of core data reporting and analytics that drive better decision-making throughout the organization.

- Take ownership of Product Data Management and Data Acquisition topics, constantly seeking opportunities to improve and optimize our existing structures.

# **Become a part of our team if you**

- 5+ years of experience in data analysis, data management, or business intelligence roles.

- Experience developing analytics functions or data capabilities within a growing organization.

- Experience designing and implementing data quality checks and governance processes.

- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

- Proficiency in SQL and advanced Excel skills.

- Experience with Python or R for data analysis and automation.

- Familiarity with relational databases, non-relational databases and Metabase.

- Familiarity with data processing techniques and tools.

- Experience mentoring junior analysts and promote a data-driven culture across the organization.

# **About SPARETECH**

SPARETECH's vision is to empower the zero-waste industrial sharing economy by enabling maintenance and procurement teams at manufacturing leaders like Bosch, Porsche, and Nestlé to reduce MRO spend and optimize inventory through accurate part information, internal transparency, and market visibility.

To achieve this, we have built, and continue to build, an AI-powered MRO software that connects all players in the spare parts ecosystem, from manufacturers and suppliers to the people working behind the machines. By facilitating the exchange of data, knowledge, and expertise, we create shared visibility that empowers smarter decisions, reduces waste, and drives seamless collaboration. This connected spare parts intelligence unlocks value that extends far beyond software.

Backed by [Insight Partners](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/sparetech-raises-10-million-in-series-a), SPARETECH is accelerating its growth with a strong focus on product innovation and team excellence. We take pride in our inclusive and collaborative culture, as well as our energetic and committed team.
