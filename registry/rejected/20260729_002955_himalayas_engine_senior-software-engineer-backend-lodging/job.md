### ****The Role****

**The Lodging team owns the entire backend behind [Engine](https://himalayas.app/companies/engine)'s highest-demand vertical: Search, Integrations, and Bookings. Three systems, one team, one set of priorities. Node.js powers Search and Integrations; Kotlin runs Bookings.**

**The business is scaling fast. Our Partner API is live and large external companies are integrating directly with [Engine](https://himalayas.app/companies/engine) — which means moving business logic out of the frontend and building clean backend wrappers our partners can rely on. Work varies week to week: a pricing team request one week, a new supplier integration the next, a tricky data bug after that. High volume, high stakes, real ownership.**

### **What You'll Do**

- **Contribute across all three backend systems: Search, Integrations, and Bookings**

- **Build and maintain business logic for the Partner API integration layer**

- **Handle cross-team requests from Pricing, FinTech, Content, and Data — clarifying scope, driving to solutions**

- **Partner closely with the PM, frontend lodging team, and supplier integrations lead**

- **Own projects end to end: surface blockers, keep things moving, deliver**
