**Role:**

We are looking for a talented Frontend Engineer to join our new Berlin AI lab.

In this role, you will build and maintain clean, scalable web applications, such as intuitive AI Agent interfaces that seamlessly integrate into our broader enterprise system. Because we are a small, agile team, you will have ownership of your work; you are highly encouraged to actively participate in UI/UX and product decision-making.

You will work closely within a tight-knit product squad alongside another frontend engineer and a PM/Designer. You will report to the Head of Agentic AI Engineering while collaborating across a compact, cross-functional team of backend and AI engineers.

**Responsibilities:**

-

Build, maintain, and scale frontend applications and services using React and TypeScript.

-

Develop reliable integrations between our frontend, backend APIs, and AI systems.

-

Work with modern state management like Jotai and component libraries like Mantine to build responsive interfaces.

-

Help implement and maintain clean, component-based frontend architecture.

-

Collaborate closely with AI engineers to integrate and productionize AI-powered product features.

-

Contribute to pragmatic engineering decisions around performance, accessibility, responsiveness, and user experience.

**Our evolving tech stack:**

-

Core: React, TypeScript, Jotai, Mantine, Playwright

-

Ecosystem: Claude Code, GitHub, REST APIs

We deeply value being an AI-native team. Embracing tools like Claude Code is an encouraged part of our daily engineering workflow.

Strong practical experience building real-world React and TypeScript applications through work, internships, freelancing, open-source contributions, or personal projects.
