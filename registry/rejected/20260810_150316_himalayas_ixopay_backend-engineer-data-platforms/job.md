### About the Role

We're looking for a Back-End Engineer (Data Platforms)to help build the next generation of [IXOPAY](https://himalayas.app/companies/ixopay)'s AI Payments Intelligence platform.

In this role, you'll design and develop the backend services and data infrastructure that power AI-driven insights, analytics, and payment optimization. Working closely with Product, Data Science, and Engineering teams, you'll build scalable systems that help enterprise merchants improve payment performance, reduce costs, and make smarter decisions using their payment data.

This is a hands-on engineering role with significant ownership and the opportunity to influence both technical architecture and product direction.

### What You'll Do

-

Design, build, and maintain scalable backend services for our AI Payments Intelligence platform.

-

Develop data pipelines, ETL processes, and cloud-native infrastructure that process large volumes of payment transaction data.

-

Build the backend services that power analytics, dashboards, AI-driven insights, and customer-facing reporting.

-

Collaborate with enterprise customers to integrate payment data from PSPs, acquirers, and other external systems.

-

Partner with Product, Data Science, and Engineering teams to design new platform capabilities and contribute to architectural decisions.

-

Develop secure, reliable APIs and integration services for payment gateways, reporting APIs, webhooks, and other data sources.

-

Optimize backend systems for performance, scalability, security, and high availability.

-

Stay current with emerging technologies and contribute to engineering best practices across the platform.
