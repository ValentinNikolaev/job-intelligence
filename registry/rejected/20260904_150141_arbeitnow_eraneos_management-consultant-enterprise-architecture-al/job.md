**What you can expect from us**

As part of our Sourcing & IT Advisory team, you will help clients shape and deliver complex business and IT transformations across industries.

Our tasks focus on complex transformation projects:

- **Enterprise Architecture & Strategy:** Define enterprise architecture strategies, governance models, target architectures, and roadmaps aligned with business objectives and value streams
- **Business & Application Architecture:** Design business capabilities, processes, application portfolios, and platform landscapes to enable scalable digital operating models
- **Data & Integration Architecture:** Develop modern information, data, API-first, integration, and event-driven architectures that ensure interoperability and business value
- **Legacy Modernization & Technical Debt:** Lead the transition from legacy and monolithic environments to cloud-native, modular, microservices-based architectures. Help clients to harmonize their diverse technology landscapes, work on their technical debt, and guide their IT migrations
- **Technology Transformation:** Design multi and hybrid cloud environments and new platforms reflecting requirements such as cloud migration and cloud exit options, business process resilience, open-source solutions, maintainability, and DevOps organizations and processes
- **AI Architecture & Innovation:** Drive AI readiness, define architectures for AI-enabled and agentic systems, and champion the effective use of AI tools to accelerate architecture, delivery, and decision-making
- **Architecture Governance:** Establish architecture principles, standards, processes, and decision frameworks to ensure sustainable enterprise-wide adoption
- **Transformation Delivery:** Advise business and IT stakeholders up to executive level, facilitate workshops, apply agile methodologies (e.g., SAFe), and ensure successful implementation of architectural change

**Who you are**

- University degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Information Systems, Business Administration, or a related field
- At least 4 years proven experience in Enterprise and IT Architecture, ideally across multiple industries
- Proficient in leveraging modern AI tools to improve architecture design, consulting, and delivery, with a mindset of continuously exploring and adopting emerging AI capabilities
- Excellent communication skills in English and German
- Willingness to travel as required for client engagements

Strong expertise in:

- Enterprise Architecture frameworks (e.g., TOGAF), Architecture Governance, and Architecture Maturity Assessments
- Business Capability Management, Business-IT Alignment, IT Strategy, and IT Governance
- Business, Application, Information, Data, Integration, Technology, and Security Architecture
- API-first, event-driven, service-oriented, cloud-native, and microservices architectures
- Data management concepts (e.g., master data management, data lakes, data mesh)
- Enterprise platforms (e.g., SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow)
- Architecture modeling standards (e.g., ArchiMate)
- AI readiness, agentic AI architectures, and modern AI-enabled enterprise platforms
