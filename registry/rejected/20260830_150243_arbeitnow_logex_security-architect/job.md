**Our mission**

At LOGEX, we help healthcare providers improve outcomes through data-driven insights. Every day, hospitals and healthcare organizations across Europe rely on our financial, operational, clinical, and patient pathway data to make better decisions.

With that responsibility comes a clear commitment: keeping sensitive healthcare data secure, protected, and trusted.

We are looking for a **Security Architect** who is not only able to design secure solutions but also enjoys implementing them. This is a hands-on role for someone who wants to make a tangible impact on security across our platforms, cloud environment, and engineering practices.

**Your impact and responsibilities**

As Security Architect, you will report directly to the **Head of Information Security & Compliance** and play a key role in strengthening LOGEX's security maturity. This is not a traditional enterprise architecture role focused on creating frameworks and presentations. We are looking for a security practitioner who enjoys rolling up their sleeves, working closely with engineering teams, solving real security challenges, and driving measurable risk reduction. You will work alongside software engineers, data engineers, and platform teams across multiple countries to ensure security is built into everything we do.

**Your team**

**Security Architecture & Standards**

-

Define practical and scalable security architecture standards, patterns, and best practices

-

Ensure security requirements can be realistically implemented by engineering teams

-

Drive secure-by-design principles across platforms and applications

**Secure Software Development**

-

Embed security throughout the SDLC

-

Perform threat modeling and security design reviews

-

Support secure coding practices

-

Implement effective security controls within CI/CD pipelines

**Cloud & Platform Security**

-

Improve and harden cloud environments and platforms

-

Manage security across identity and access management, networking, secrets management, encryption, and configuration management

-

Partner with engineering teams to strengthen our security posture

**Vulnerability Management**

-

Identify, prioritize, and drive remediation of security vulnerabilities

-

Work directly with engineers to resolve issues

-

Ensure vulnerabilities are not only discovered but effectively closed

**Detection & Incident Response**

-

Improve logging, monitoring, and threat detection capabilities

-

Support security incident investigations and response activities

-

Help strengthen operational security processes

**Compliance & Risk Management**

-

Translate requirements from GDPR, ISO 27001, and customer security standards into practical technical controls

-

Collaborate with Information Security, Legal, and Data Protection teams

-

Support audits and security assurance activities
