### About the Role

A fast-growing, Y Combinator–backed conversational AI startup is hiring its **founding Sales Development Representative** to join the Growth team in Berlin. This is a ground-floor opportunity to own pipeline generation for a voice AI product with strong, proven market demand — and a clear path to full-cycle deal ownership as you ramp up.

You'll help shape the sales playbook, build repeatable go-to-market motions, and play a direct role in scaling revenue quickly. If you thrive in high-energy, low-ego environments and want your work to have a measurable impact from day one, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Generate qualified pipeline to drive aggressive revenue growth over the next year.

-

Identify the right prospects and engage them through strategic outreach.

-

Drive multi-channel outreach across email, LinkedIn, and phone.

-

Build and manage relationships with customers from first touch onward.

-

Manage pipeline and maintain CRM tooling with rigor and discipline.

Transition into owning and closing deals end-to-end after strong early performance.

Help shape the GTM motion, build a repeatable sales process, and refine outreach strategies.

Contribute to hiring and growing the sales team, and building out the sales playbook.

Proficiency with multi-channel outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone) and CRM tools.

**Near-native / C2-level German** (non-negotiable) and strong English (B2–C1).

Willingness to work **on-site in Berlin five days a week**.
