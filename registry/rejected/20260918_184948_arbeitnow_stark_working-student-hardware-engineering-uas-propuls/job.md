**About Us**

STARK is a European defence technology company building the next generation of unmanned systems. We
design, develop and manufacture software-defined, mass-scalable and cost-effective platforms that give
NATO operators a decisive edge in highly contested environments. We are focused on delivering
deployable, high-performance capability — not future promises. In a time of rising threats, our work
strengthens the technological edge of NATO Allies and their Partners.

**Your mission**

Your mission is to work hands-on with the parts of the aircraft that turn stored energy into flight: the
motors, the batteries, and the propulsion and power components that decide how far, how fast, and how
long our platforms fly. You support the hardware team from bench characterisation through data analysis
in MATLAB and Python.

You aren't just swapping components on a bench; you are helping generate the measured evidence that
decides what actually flies — and learning how unmanned hardware is designed, tested, and pushed to its
limits.

**Responsibilities**

● Support characterisation and bench testing of motors, propellers, batteries, and power
components for our UAS platforms.
● Help set up test rigs and instrumentation, and capture clean, repeatable measurement data.
● Assist with component selection and trade-offs by turning test data into clear comparisons.
● Reduce and analyse test data in MATLAB and Python — efficiency, thrust, endurance, thermal and
discharge behaviour.
● Visualise results so the engineering team can make decisions from them quickly.
● Keep test setups, measurements, and findings documented and traceable.

**Qualifications**

● Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's in Electrical, Mechanical, Aerospace, or a comparable
engineering field.
● Working knowledge of MATLAB and Python for data analysis.
● Interest in and some exposure to UAS, electric propulsion (motors / ESCs), or battery / power
systems.
● Must be a citizen of a NATO member state (due to export control and security regulations).
● Practical, hands-on mindset — comfortable at the bench and with a multimeter as much as in a
script.
● Good written and verbal communication skills in English; German is a plus
● Hands-on: You like building, measuring, and finding out what really happens.
● Rigorous: You trust clean measurements over assumptions.
● Curious: You want to understand why a component behaves the way it does.

**Nice to have**

● Experience with test benches, DAQ, or measurement instrumentation.
● Familiarity with battery testing, thermal behaviour, or motor/propeller characterisation.
● Personal projects with drones, RC aircraft, batteries, or robotics
