### About the Role

We're a fast-growing, Munich-based AI-powered B2B SaaS startup building a privacy-first digital assistant for sales teams. Our platform automates the administrative burden of the sales cycle — from real-time meeting notes and CRM updates to voice-guided follow-ups and coaching — helping sales professionals focus on selling rather than admin work. Founded in 2022 and now ~25 people strong, we work with enterprise clients and integrate with leading CRMs including Salesforce, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and HubSpot.

We're looking for a **Senior Full-Stack Engineer** to take meaningful technical ownership across our product surfaces: web app, desktop client, iOS app, and backend services. You'll work closely with the CTO and founding engineers to shape architecture and technical strategy, while also mentoring teammates and raising the engineering bar across the team. This is a hybrid role based in Munich, Germany. **Visa sponsorship is not available.**

### What You'll Do

-

Take end-to-end technical ownership of a core platform (web, desktop, iOS, or backend) and ship real-time, low-latency features.

-

Architect scalable services and define cross-stack best practices, balancing performance, reliability, and product velocity.

-

Collaborate directly with the CTO and founding engineers to shape product and infrastructure strategy.

-

Mentor junior engineers, lead code reviews, and foster a high-performing, learning-oriented engineering culture.

-

Build and maintain a robust delivery pipeline — CI/CD, test automation, and deployment strategies.

-

Document architectural decisions and communicate effectively across engineering, product, and infrastructure teams.
