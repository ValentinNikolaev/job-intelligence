**This is what you move:**

As a **Senior Programmatic Manager (Mensch)**, you are responsible for both the further development of programmatic activities and their operational implementation for our clients.

- **Best Practices:** You regularly analyze campaign results and derive appropriate optimization measures. You confidently apply existing best practices and collaboratively evolve them with the team.

- **Monitoring:** You keep a close eye on ongoing campaigns within multiple DSPs (Demand-Side Platforms) and ensure properly configured as well as continuously optimized setups.

- **Strategy:** You contribute to the selection of suitable target audiences, technologies, and inventory for programmatic buying and you are able to explain your strategy to relevant stakeholders in an intelligible way.

- **Data Acumen:** You optimize programmatic media buying and always keep relevant KPIs in focus. The goal is to reliably achieve agreed-upon performance targets.

- **Collaboration:** You work closely with specialist teams and external partners in an international environment. You lead a small team of experts and are responsible for knowledge transfer within the team.

**The following qualities distinguish you:**

- **Qualifications: **You hold a university degree with a focus on Marketing, Media Studies, or a comparable education, and have more than 3-4 years of professional experience in online marketing.

- **Data & Tech:** The digital media landscape (DSPs, Data, Inventories, Ad- & Audience-Verification technologies, etc.) are not just terms for you; you have already been able to implement them in practice.

- **Curiosity & Eagerness to Learn:** Always moving forward is not just a motto! It is essential for you to always stay "UpToDate" in the programmatic field.

- **Data Presentation:** Thanks to your strong organizational skills (in Excel as well as PowerPoint), you can easily present complex information in an understandable way.

- **Language Skills:** You can communicate confidently in both German and English.

Furthermore you are: **#Teamplayer #Communicative #SolutionOriented**
