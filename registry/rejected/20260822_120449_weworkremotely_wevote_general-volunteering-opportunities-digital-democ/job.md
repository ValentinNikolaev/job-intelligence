**About Us**

WeVote is a nonpartisan get-out-the-vote nonprofit startup and a celebrated Fast Forward nonprofit technology grantee. WeVote is building a movement of excellent, passionate, part-time volunteers (starting with the founders) who build mobile technologies that will touch and mobilize millions of voters on Election Day. We believe that many voters are busy, distracted, and impatient. We have a goal of providing a positive voting experience that helps people decide how to vote. More information at https://www.wevote.us or on Twitter @WeVote.

**Interested in other areas to join the WeVote Movement?**

WeVote is always interested in connecting with people who are passionate about politics and excited to use technology to educate voters and increase voter turnout. If you have 4+ hours a week to devote to WeVote and can meet remotely at least 3-4 times a month with your team ([see when teams meet here](https://sites.google.com/wevoteeducation.org/wevote/teams)), then please apply and be sure to include a letter letting us know where/how you would like to volunteer, any past volunteer activities, etc.

**Who you are: Must-haves.**

- You want to work with an established and successful team. You are committed to strengthening American Democracy.

- 4+ hours per week during our core team hours M-F 8 a.m. - 6 p.m.
- A willingness to work with and support other volunteers.
- Ability to work remotely from a US location (50 states, PR, etc.)
