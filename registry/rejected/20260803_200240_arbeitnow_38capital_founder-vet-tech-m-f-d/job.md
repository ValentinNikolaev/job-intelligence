## Tasks

We are looking for entrepreneurial individuals interested in building companies in the vettech space together with ThirtyEight Capital.

Our thesis:

The German veterinary market is consolidating. Capital is buying up practices, and the people who actually do the work are increasingly employed by someone whose interests aren't theirs. Most of them didn't spend six years studying to become someone else's headcount — but the alternative, going out alone, has become close to impossible: financing, buildout, staffing, admin, regulation, and a business skillset nobody taught them. We think that gap is the opportunity, and we think it's addressable with infrastructure rather than capital alone.

This is for you if you might already:

- have identified inefficiencies in the market

- have industry experience

- or want to start a company but are looking for the right partner and setup

Together, we would:

- build first commercial traction

- and potentially launch a venture together

Our approach focuses on building real businesses with strong fundamentals:

- operational excellence

Rather than “growth at all costs”, we believe in building durable companies with long-term value creation.
