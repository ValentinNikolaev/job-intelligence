### About the Role

We are a fast-growing, **Y Combinator-backed SaaS startup** operating at the intersection of AI and enterprise sales automation. Our platform helps distributors and manufacturers eliminate lost deals by automating quoting and order processing end-to-end — integrating directly with major ERP and CRM systems. We're a small, high-output team and we move fast.

We're looking for a **Full-Stack Engineer** with a founder mindset to build AI-powered systems end-to-end. This is a hands-on, high-agency role for someone who loves owning architecture decisions, shipping production-grade software, and working across the entire stack.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the architecture and design of AI systems from concept to production.

-

Collaborate with a talented cross-functional engineering team across the full stack.

-

Build robust data pipelines to process large-scale unstructured data efficiently.

-

Deploy and manage software in cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or GCP).

-

Make the backend scalable, stable, and performant.

-

Develop scalable, reusable frontend components and API structures.

-

Write clean, maintainable code with well-considered testing strategies (unit and integration).

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Proficiency building end-to-end AI-powered web applications using **Next.js / React** on the frontend and **Python** on the backend.

-

Strong proficiency with **TypeScript** in frontend development.

-

Demonstrated product-minded mindset with a track record of shipping complex web applications.

-

Experience designing and implementing scalable **RESTful APIs** and API structures.

-

Experience building and maintaining **data pipelines** for processing large-scale unstructured data.

-

Proficiency with cloud platforms (**AWS, Azure, or GCP**) and modern CI/CD practices.

-

Strong hands-on experience with **Docker and Kubernetes** for containerization and deployment.

-

Ability to own architecture and design decisions with clear documentation and code quality standards.

-

Hands-on experience building and validating unit and integration testing strategies.

-

No visa sponsorship required — candidates must be authorized to work in Germany.

**Nice to Have:**

Hands-on experience with **ML/NLP technologies**, including embeddings, vector databases, and RLHF, with production deployments.

-

Experience with **ML model serving and inference pipelines** in production environments.

-

Proficiency with **PostgreSQL** and relational data modeling.
