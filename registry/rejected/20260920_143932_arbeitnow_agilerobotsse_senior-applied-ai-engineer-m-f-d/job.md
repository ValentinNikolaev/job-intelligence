### About the role

As a Senior Applied AI Engineer (Intelligent Application Focus) at Agile Robots SE, you will design, build, and deploy AI solutions that enable our robotic systems to solve real-world problems. The role requires industry experience and deep technical expertise to apply novel machine learning and AI techniques under real-world constraints. The focus is on delivering impactful, reliable AI systems that demonstrate measurable value in practical, industrial environments.

### Your Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain AI-driven software applications (including Vision Language Action Models (VLAs)) for robotic systems, ensuring high performance and reliability

- Collaborate with hardware engineers, system architects, and other software engineers to integrate AI applications with robotic hardware

- Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code following best practices and industry standards

- Test and validate AI applications in real-world environments, ensuring they meet performance and safety requirements

- Provide technical support and guidance to other team members and stakeholders

- Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI and robotics, and integrate relevant innovations into the development process

### Essential Skills

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical Engineering, or a related field

- At least 5 years experience working with real robotic hardware and AI applications in industrial environments

- Strong understanding of multimodal learning concepts (e.g. vision-language-action), including model architectures, training paradigms, and advantages/limitations

- Strong foundation in machine learning and deep learning, with hands-on experience deploying AI models in industrial or real-world use cases (e.g., reinforcement learning or world models).

- Proficiency in programming languages such as C++ and Python

- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, collaborative environment

- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal

### Beneficial Skills

- Familiarity with ROS

- Experience with simulation tools such as Mujoco, Nvidia Isaac-Sim, or similar

- Familiarity with hardware interfacing and real-time operating systems

- Knowledge of software development tools and practices, including version control systems (e.g., Git), continuous integration, and Agile methodologies
