Your main goal will be to become the founding owner of Sales Compensation at n8n, building from scratch how we pay and motivate every revenue team across SMB, Mid-Market, Enterprise, CS, SE, SDR, and Partnerships, spanning EMEA and AMER. You'll have the mandate to make it AI-first from day one, partnering with our internal AI team to build the agents that run it at scale.

To do so, here are your responsibilities:

### Compensation Plan Design & Governance

-

Design, cost, and maintain variable compensation plans for quota-carrying reps and overlay roles (BDR, Partners, SE, CS), aligned with GTM strategy and financial targets.

-

Model plan costs and payout scenarios ahead of annual and mid-year changes, partnering with Finance on affordability and ROI.

-

Own plan documentation, rep-facing comp letters, and FAQs.

### Quota & Crediting Operations

-

Implement quotas, targets, and crediting rules correctly across the commissions platform and Salesforce.

-

Own crediting logic for expansions, amendments, multi-year deals, proration, and FX.

-

Partner with RevOps and Deal Desk to keep crediting aligned with how deals are structured.

### Commission Calculation & Payments

-

Run monthly and quarterly commission calculations, reconciliation, and dispute resolution.

-

Own the payroll handoff process, ensuring accurate and on-time payouts.

-

Maintain audit readiness and strong internal controls across the full compensation cycle.

### Reporting & Analytics

-

Build and maintain reliable reporting on attainment, payouts, and plan effectiveness for Sales, Finance, and RevOps leadership.

-

Surface insights on plan performance and recommend adjustments to keep incentives driving the right behaviors.

### Cross-Functional Collaboration

-

Partner with Sales leadership, RevOps, Finance/FP&A, Payroll, People, and Legal on compensation governance.

-

Serve as the go-to expert on comp mechanics, explaining plan design and payouts to reps and executives alike.

-

Support internal-controls work, responding to audit requests related to compensation.

## REQUIREMENTS

### **Must-haves**

-

**💼 Comp/RevOps experience:** You bring 4-8+ years in Sales Compensation, Incentive Compensation, RevOps, Sales Ops, or Finance within a high-growth B2B SaaS environment.

**📐 Plan design expertise:** You've designed and operated variable compensation plans, including quota-carrying and overlay roles, accelerators, and SPIFFs.

-

**📈 SaaS commercial fluency:** You're fluent in SaaS commercial concepts like bookings/ARR, expansions and amendments, multi-year deals, proration, and FX.
