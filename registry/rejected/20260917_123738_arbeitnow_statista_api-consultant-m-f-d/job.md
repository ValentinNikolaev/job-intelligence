**Your Role**

As an API Consultant, you will be responsible for ensuring customers achieve rapid and sustained success with our API and MCP solution — and just as importantly, for making them *excited* about it. You will be the face of Statista Connect in front of customers: pitching the solution, building and demoing custom agents live, and turning technical capability into a compelling story that wins hearts and budgets. You'll guide customers from initial setup to advanced use cases, drive adoption by crafting tailored prompts and agents, and help them seamlessly integrate our API solution into their workflows.

This is a hybrid role where technical expertise meets showmanship. If you light up when you get to demo something you built and watch a customer's eyes widen — this role is for you. If presenting to customers feels like a chore you'd rather delegate, it is not.

**Key responsibilities**

-

**Pitching & Demoing (core of the role):** Deliver compelling pitches and live demos of Statista Connect to prospects and customers, from analysts to C-level. Independently build and iterate on demo agents, prompts, and workflows tailored to each customer's use case — and use them to create genuine excitement and "I need this" moments in every meeting.

-

**Customer Adoption & Enablement:** Guide customers from initial setup to advanced use cases with tailored prompts, agents, onboarding flows, documentation, and tutorials — carrying the excitement from the pitch through to daily usage.

-

**Data-Driven Engagement:** Use product usage data, API analytics, and customer health scores to spot adoption blockers and expansion opportunities, and act on them with proactive guidance, lifecycle outreach, and new pitch angles.

-

**Integration Success:** Ensure smooth API integrations, resolve blockers quickly, document best practices, and collaborate with engineering on technical challenges.

-

**Commercial Support:** Identify upsell and expansion opportunities and support sales teams with demos and technical storytelling in renewal and growth conversations.

-

Feedback Loop: Share insights from pitches, demos, and usage data with product and engineering to sharpen the pitch, refine demo agents, and shape the roadmap.
