As a **Data Analyst** on the **Reporting Partnerships team**, you will be an integral part of our **Commercial Data and Analytics organization**, working to generate physician-level reporting that powers client measurement and analysis.

***This role can be filled in our San Francisco headquarters OR remotely in the U.S.***

**How you’ll make an impact**

- Generate and maintain Python-based client reporting used to power client measurement.

- Deliver new Python scripts for reporting use cases, from adapting existing client templates to building custom client scripts end-to-end.

- Conduct daily QA and act as a point-of-contact for teammates’ SQL and Python questions, helping raise the overall bar for code quality and consistency.

- Create and maintain both Python and SQL-based aggregate reports as needed.

**What we’re looking for**

- At least **2 years of professional experience** working in business analytics or performance reporting.

- Excellent **SQL** skills to create and evaluate complex statements involving numerous tables and data relationships.

- Fluency in **Python**, with hands-on experience building, maintaining, and debugging scripts for data processing or reporting.

- Experience stepping into** client-facing** roles, especially to translate technical solutions for non-technical audiences.

- Strong organizational skills and **attention to detail**; ability to stay ahead of multiple deadlines at once.

- A **proactive**, ownership-oriented mindset: comfortable working through scripting updates largely on your own, escalating questions or issues when necessary.

- A **continuous-improvement **focus, always looking to automate operational processes, elevate code quality, and deliver faster, more accurate reporting while strengthening value to clients.
