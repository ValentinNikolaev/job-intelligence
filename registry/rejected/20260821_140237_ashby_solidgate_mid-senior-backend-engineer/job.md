ABOUT THE TEAM AND ROLE

You will join the Banking team — a small, focused group building financial infrastructure for digital companies that struggle to access EU banking. Many businesses, especially from Ukraine and CEE, can't open EU business accounts at all, or wait months to do so. Our product removes that blocker: companies get a business account, can send SEPA and SWIFT payments, receive payouts to cards, exchange currencies, and manage everything through one portal.



Your immediate team: a Senior Backend Engineer with 10+ years in fintech and AI, a Senior Backend Engineer with 8+ years including crypto, a Middle Frontend Engineer with 6+ years, and a Junior AQA Engineer. We're growing it now to launch corporate cards — a new product that lets business clients issue and manage cards directly through the platform.

Explore our technology stack ➡️ here https://solidgate-tech.github.io/.
