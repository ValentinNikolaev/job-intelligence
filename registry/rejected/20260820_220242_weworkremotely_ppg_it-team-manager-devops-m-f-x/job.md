## As IT Manager, you will define and architect the deployment, automation, and management of our cloud applications portfolio and infrastructure. The DevOps team works closely with platform engineering teams, infrastructure architects, product owners, and stakeholders to establish and maintain efficient CI/CD pipelines, deployments. Supports the DevOps team with scripting and automating all environments, software deployments, and it's supporting infrastructure.
