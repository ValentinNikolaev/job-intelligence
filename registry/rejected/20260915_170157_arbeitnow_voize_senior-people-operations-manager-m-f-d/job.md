## 💡 Your Mission: Build the People Ops function voize will run on

As **Senior People Operations Manager**, you’ll own People Operations at voize - and build the function from the ground up.

This is our first dedicated People Ops role, so there’s no legacy setup to inherit and no standard playbook to follow. Instead, you’ll have the space to design how People Ops should work at a modern, fast-growing company: automated where it can be, self-service where it should be, and scalable enough to still work when we’ve doubled in size.

We build AI that gives nurses more time for what matters. Your job is to bring that same mindset to our People function - cutting admin, removing friction, and creating an employee experience that sets the standard for how modern companies should operate.

## 🚀 What You'll Own - the mandate in detail

-

**Own the employee lifecycle end to end:** Onboarding to offboarding with the mandate to redesign how it works, not just run it.

-

**Be the trusted point of contact:** Own the people topics that need judgment and discretion - and build the self-service, automation and agents that make routine questions disappear.

-

**Build a first-class People Ops foundation:** Modern, AI-native processes, clear ownership and documentation, and a seamless employee experience from day one.

-

**Automate what shouldn’t be manual:** Build a reliable source of truth and the workflows and AI agents around it - so documents, deadlines, data checks and repetitive admin run without human intervention.

-

**Make us global-ready:** Entity vs. EOR decisions, global payroll architecture and local employment compliance across every market we operate in - with audit-proof documentation the whole way.
