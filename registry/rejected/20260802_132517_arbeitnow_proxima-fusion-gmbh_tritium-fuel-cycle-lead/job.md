**WHO WE ARE**

Proxima Fusion is Europe’s fastest-growing fusion company and the continent’s best-funded fusion player, as well as the first spin-out from the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (IPP). Backed by over €650M and powered by a growing team across Munich, Zurich, and Oxford, we are developing the hardware and infrastructure needed to deliver the world’s first commercial stellarator fusion power plant.

Our concept advances the most mature fusion technology out there, the Wendelstein 7-X stellarator, through two next-generation machines: Alpha and Stellaris. Our work combines stellarator optimization, advanced computation, machine learning, and high-temperature superconducting magnets to unlock higher-performance designs that were previously out of reach.

Turning these designs into a functioning fusion power plant requires excellence and ownership across every discipline, from physics and engineering to software, manufacturing, law, and business functions.

**YOUR IMPACT**

-

Lead the design and integration of the tritium fuel cycle systems for Alpha, Proxima's scientific breakeven stellarator

-

-

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

-

Lead the specification of the tritium fuel cycle equipment required to operate the Alpha stellarator including exhaust gas processing, tritium storage, tritium accountancy and tritium fueling.

-

Own and develop the industry contracts and partnerships required to deliver the Alpha fuel cycle.

-

Develop and lead on tritium accountancy in Alpha including quantification of tritium permeation and retention; and its impact on the Alpha plant and program.

-

Technically lead first principles reasoning, modelling, tests and prototyping to detail and validate the design of the Alpha fuel cycle system.

-

Provide expert input to the Alpha safety case related to tritium containment best practice.

-

Build an innovative and inclusive team within Proxima and strong collaborations with external suppliers, to achieve the ambitious Alpha milestones.

**WHO YOU ARE**

-

Visionary team player with the ability to lead internal and external work packages within multidisciplinary technical teams in fast-paced, dynamic settings.

-

Deep understanding of fusion fuel cycles, with knowledge of tritium gas exhaust processing, separation, storage, accountancy and containment.

-

Proven success in procuring and commissioning tritium equipment.

-

Experience developing and owning tritium accountancy frameworks, including quantification of permeation and retention.

-
