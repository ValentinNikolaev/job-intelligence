## **Your role**

This is a role with real impact, ownership, and visibility from day one. As **Junior App Product Owner (all genders)** You will join one of Sunday Natural’s most important digital initiatives as the first version of our app launches and help shape its future product and customer experience.

Working closely with our Senior Product Owner, you will own defined app areas end to end – from identifying customer problems and defining requirements to coordinating development, launching features, and measuring their impact.

You will collaborate with our Staff Engineer, external development partners, and cross-functional teams, while gaining exposure to key product, leadership, and selected C-level discussions. You will be trusted to bring structure to ambiguity, align stakeholders, and independently drive your areas forward.

## **Your responsibilities**

-

Own defined app features end to end – from identifying customer problems and defining solutions to development, launch, performance analysis, and continuous improvement.

-

Support the Senior Product Owner in shaping, prioritizing, and delivering the app roadmap.

-

Translate customer needs and business objectives into clear product requirements, user stories, priorities, and next steps.

-

Coordinate feature delivery across internal teams and external development partners, ensuring clear responsibilities, dependencies, and timelines.

-

Conduct user research in German and English, analyze customer journeys, app usage, funnels, and feedback, and turn insights into actionable improvements.

-

Support experiments and A/B tests, explore AI-powered features and agents, and prepare product updates and follow-ups for senior leadership.

## **Your profile**

First and foremost, you are ambitious, smart, and eager to take responsibility. You are a self-starter who works independently, learns quickly, and does not wait for every next step to be definedYou are highly structured, proactive, and able to turn complex or ambiguous topics into clear priorities, decisions, and actions.

-

You take ownership, drive topics forward independently, and know when to involve others, ask for support, or challenge assumptions.

-

You work in an agile way, adapt quickly to changing priorities, and confidently speak up when something is unclear.

-

You are passionate about digital products, customer-centricity, and developments in health, technology, and AI.

-

You are analytical, comfortable with numbers, and interested in product metrics, conversion funnels, experimentation, and A/B testing.

-

You communicate clearly, bring stakeholders together, are comfortable interviewing customers in German, and have gained initial experience in a relevant field; you are fluent in **German and English at C1 level**

## **What you can expect**

-

Join a major digital initiative from the beginning and actively shape a completely new platform.

-

Take on real responsibility, visibility, and end-to-end ownership of meaningful product areas from day one.

-

Work closely with and receive mentoring from an experienced Senior Product Owner.

-

Collaborate with a Staff Engineer, external app development specialists, and teams across IT, Design, CRM, Data, Marketing, Product Development, and Customer Experience.

-

Participate in selected senior leadership and C-level product discussions.

-

Build expertise in product ownership, customer research, digital analytics, experimentation, stakeholder management, and AI, with clear growth potential into a broader Product Owner or Product Manager role.

The lack of natural, high-quality products on the market was the original motivation for founding Sunday Natural in 2013. Our diverse team in Berlin consists of over 350 employees from more than 45 nationalities and continues to grow – but at a sustainable pace. Our goal is to foster a collaborative, supportive, and inclusive work environment with a diverse group of motivated, talented, and visionary team members.
