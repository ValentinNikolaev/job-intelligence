# **Your Team and Role**

Working on the **Data Science** Functional Team, you'll drive product and business impact through improving our search engine ranking and results. Recent projects include:

-

*Improving first-phase ranking quality and precision without sacrificing latency*

-

*Developing new lexical-matching and document-authority signals*

-

*Expanding our search evaluations to measure relevancy readiness and surface ranking blindspots *

-

*Investigating LLMs for feature generation and ranking*

Our data meets rigorous privacy standards and is necessarily sparse, so you'll need to think creatively to surface actionable insights in an evidence-first culture where quantitative rigor is the baseline, not the exception.
