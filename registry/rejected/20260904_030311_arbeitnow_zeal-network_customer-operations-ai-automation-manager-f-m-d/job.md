# **About the Role**

We're looking for an **Customer Operations AI & Automation Manager (f/m/d)** to own the AI agenda across Customer Operations. You will be the driving force behind identifying, implementing, and monitoring AI-powered features and projects that improve efficiency, quality, and customer experience across the Customer Operations Department. This is a hands-on, highly cross-functional role for someone who thrives on running multiple projects at once, loves working with diverse stakeholders, and gets genuine satisfaction from tidying up messy processes and finding smarter ways of working.

The Operational Excellence team sits at the center of Customer Operations, partnering closely with our two frontline teams **Customer Support** and **Payments, Fraud & Verification**, to continuously improve how we work. Our mission is to make operations faster, smarter, and more scalable, with AI and automation as key levers.

-

**AI Implementation & Lifecycle Management**

-

Drive the end-to-end lifecycle of AI features across Customer Operations: From ideation and business case, through implementation, to ongoing monitoring and optimization.

Manage multiple simultaneous projects for different stakeholders across the department, keeping timelines, priorities, and expectations aligned.

**Operational Excellence**

Proactively identify inefficiencies and opportunities for process improvement, not just where AI applies, but general process "tidy-ups" that make operations run more smoothly.

Bring a "think outside the box" mindset to problem-solving, challenging the status quo of how things are currently done.
