### Your Roadmap to Make an Impact

**Systems Architecture & Roadmap**

-

Build and maintain the enterprise architecture across business capabilities, processes, data, and applications, building a lean digital thread from Requirements and Engineering through to Production and Service.

-

Define and enforce standards across the stack: single source of truth, SSO, clean integrations, and compliance with ISO 27001, ISO 9001, and Export Control requirements.

-

Anticipate when the current tool landscape needs to evolve, knowing when Odoo alone is not enough and when dedicated PLM, MES, or other systems are needed.

**Odoo Development & Customization**

-

Configure, customize, and extend Odoo modules using Python to match evolving business needs, owning upgrades, testing, and deployment across environments.

-

Ensure data integrity, security, and performance of the Odoo platform while managing integrations with tools across CRM, finance, project management, and communication.

**Engineering, Production & Process**

-

Support and optimize workflows for BOM management, production planning, work orders, and quality control, ensuring system processes reflect how hardware and software are actually built.

-

Work with stakeholders to map, document, and improve business processes eliminating bottlenecks, reducing manual workarounds, and maintaining documentation as the business evolves.

-

Manage product lifecycle processes from prototyping and engineering changes through to serial production, while ensuring full traceability and documentation standards are met.

**AI & Automation**

-

Identify and implement AI and automation opportunities from Odoo's built-in capabilities to third-party tools and custom solutions to reduce manual work and improve accuracy

-

Stay current on emerging AI and automation technologies and bring relevant ideas to the table

-

3–5 years of experience with ERP systems, with hands-on Odoo experience strongly preferred

-

Solid Python development skills and familiarity with Odoo's OWL framework for customization and module development

-

Proven experience mapping, documenting, and improving business processes with a strong understanding of how sales, procurement, inventory, production, and finance translate into system workflows

-

Background in a manufacturing, engineering, or hardware-building environment is a strong plus

-

Experience building and maintaining integrations between business systems via APIs, middleware, or native connectors, you see the full landscape and know how the pieces fit together

-

Familiarity with AI/ML tools and workflow automation platforms such as n8n, Make, or Zapier

-

Awareness of ISO 27001, ISO 9001, and export control compliance in a systems context;

-

Clear communicator with both technical and non-technical stakeholders; fluent in English, German is a plus
