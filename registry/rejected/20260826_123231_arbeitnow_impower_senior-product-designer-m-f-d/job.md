**Einleitung**

At **Impower GmbH**, we are shaping the future of **property management — simple, fast, and digital**. More than 100,000 people manage over 12 million apartments using processes that are still largely manual, complex, and time-consuming. We are changing that with modern technology, clear workflows, and real practical value.

Our Cloud ERP brings complex processes such as accounting, banking, and property management into one intuitive platform. As our product continues to grow, great design plays a key role in turning this complexity into simple and efficient user experiences.

As a **Senior Product Designer**, you take ownership of key areas of our core product and shape experiences that property managers rely on every day. Working closely with our Head of Design, Product Owners, and Engineers, you turn complex workflows into intuitive solutions and drive design topics hands-on from the initial problem to the shipped feature.

**Deine Aufgaben**

- Own and drive product design across key areas of our core platform, from problem discovery to final implementation
- Translate complex, data-heavy workflows into simple, intuitive, and scalable user experiences
- Develop user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and high-quality UI designs that clearly communicate your solutions
- Work closely with Product and Engineering to turn concepts into technically feasible, shippable features
- Contribute to and continuously improve our design system, including reusable components, patterns, and documentation
- Use customer insights, product context, and feedback to challenge assumptions and continuously improve existing experiences
- Support other designers by sharing expertise, providing feedback, and helping strengthen design quality across the team

**Dein Profil**

- You bring 7–9 years of hands-on Product Design experience, ideally with SaaS products
- You have experience designing complex, data-heavy products such as ERP systems, business software, or comparable applications
- You combine strong UX thinking with excellent UI skills and experience working with scalable design systems
- You are highly proficient in Figma and comfortable using modern AI and prototyping tools to accelerate your design process
- You understand front-end development constraints and enjoy collaborating closely with engineers to bring designs into production
- You take ownership of complex problems and confidently drive them from initial exploration through to a high-quality solution
- You communicate confidently in both German and English and enjoy working in an international, cross-functional environment

**Warum wir?**

- Meaningful impact: Shape a core product that helps thousands of people simplify complex property management processes every day
- Real ownership: Take responsibility for important product areas and influence how our platform evolves
- Close collaboration: Work directly with our Head of Design, Product, and Engineering in a highly cross-functional environment
- Growth opportunities: Develop your expertise while helping shape our design practices and product experience as we scale
- Modern environment: Work with state-of-the-art tools and choose the hardware that works best for you (Mac or Windows)
- Hybrid working: Enjoy the flexibility of working remotely while collaborating regularly with the team in our central Munich office
