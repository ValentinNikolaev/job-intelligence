### **ABOUT US**
Unzer is a leading European fintech company with a mission to simplify international payments for e-commerce and retail businesses. Our brand was formed from 13 companies that now contribute to building a unique product covering the entire payment flow.

At Unzer, we are driven by the belief that customers should enjoy a seamless shopping experience, no matter where they choose to shop. We are a team of over 750 experts from 70 different nationalities, dedicated to creating a state-of-the-art unified commerce platform. Our goal is to enable businesses to delight their customers with a seamless payment experience.

Whether you're a tech enthusiast, payment expert, or dedicated support professional, we are looking for individuals who are passionate about making a difference.

Our offices: We are based across Austria, Denmark, Germany, and Luxembourg with a HQ in Berlin.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

As a Product Manager at Unzer, your mission is to deliver product value. You will collaborate closely with stakeholders across the organization, analyze customer feedback, and serve as the subject-matter expert in your domain. Your focus will be to translate insights into actionable specifications and guide product development within your team to ensure the on-time delivery of real, measurable impact that supports company goals.

You will lead platform-wide initiatives in our Foundations group. The team owns the core components of UnzerOne – a modern platform enabling seamless unified commerce solutions for our merchants and fully automated end-to-end (onboarding to settlement) flows to empower our internal stakeholders.

**What your work will look like:**

-

**Merchant journey: **Establish a deep understanding of our merchants’ journey throughout the Unzer platform, from onboarding to settlement, opportunities and pain-points.

-

**Manage and Deliver the Product Roadmap: **Maintain and own the product roadmap for your area of responsibility, ensuring alignment with organizational goals and on-time delivery of product enhancements and features.

- **Own Product Initiatives:** Plan and execute product initiatives end to end, demonstrating full product ownership and autonomy.

- **KPI Definition & Performance Tracking:** Define and track KPIs to measure product success and drive continuous improvement.

- **Solve Complex Challenges: **Proactively resolve complex challenges across varied product contexts by in-depth analysis from technical and business perspectives and focusing teams on delivering solutions.

-

**Manage Communication and Collaboration: **Enable collaboration within your team and communication with stakeholders outside your team, ensuring a shared understanding and effective product delivery.

**What you need to be successful in this role:**

- 3+ years’ product management experience in Tech, ideally in Payments on billing and settlement solutions - a plus if gained within a financially regulated company.

- You are skilled at planning, driving, and monitoring product development to meet objectives in high quality and on time.

- Your decisions are informed by data, and you are experienced with investigating and resolving a broad range of complex issues in the technology space.

- You facilitate collaboration and knowledge sharing within cross-functional teams, engaging stakeholders and ensuring user or customer needs are met.

- You communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical audiences, comfortably explaining complex concepts, gaining insights and supporting decision-making.

- You bring creativity and an improvement mindset, proposing new solutions, encouraging team enhancements, and implementing changes.

- You demonstrate leadership and adaptability by embracing change and actively promoting new technologies and the use of AI in daily work.
