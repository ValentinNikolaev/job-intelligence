### **ABOUT US**

Unzer is a leading European fintech company with a mission to simplify international payments for e-commerce and retail businesses. Our brand was formed from 13 companies that now contribute to building a unique product covering the entire payment flow.

At Unzer, we are driven by the belief that customers should enjoy a seamless shopping experience, no matter where they choose to shop. We are a team of over 750 experts from 70 different nationalities, dedicated to creating a state-of-the-art unified commerce platform. Our goal is to enable businesses to delight their customers with a seamless payment experience.

Whether you're a tech enthusiast, payment expert, or dedicated support professional, we are looking for individuals who are passionate about making a difference.

Our offices: We are based across Austria, Denmark, Germany, and Luxembourg with a HQ in Berlin.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

You will drive the strategy, development, and continuous improvement of our POS product. You will work closely with engineering, design, commercial teams, and customers to create a product that is intuitive, scalable, and valuable for businesses in our target verticals.

As Lead Product Manager for POS, you will take ownership of a modern point-of-sale product that helps businesses manage sales, payments, operations, and customer interactions in one seamless experience. You will lead and develop a team of two product managers, and act as the key product partner to our commercial and sales organization.

**What your work will look like:**

-

Own and lead the product vision, roadmap, and development priorities for our POS product.

-

Directly manage, coach, and grow a team of two product managers, setting priorities and ensuring their focus areas ladder up to a coherent POS strategy.

-

Work closely with your dedicated development team to plan, build, launch, and improve new features.

-

Define product requirements based on customer needs, business goals, market trends, and data insights.

-

Collaborate with other Product Managers to ensure the POS product integrates smoothly with our SaaS and back-office solutions.

-

Partner closely with commercial and sales leadership to align on priorities, protecting product roadmap integrity while staying embedded in business conversations rather than working around them.

-

Gather feedback from users, internal teams, and stakeholders to continuously improve the product experience.

-

Monitor product performance, adoption, and customer satisfaction to identify growth opportunities.

-

Manage timelines, priorities, and delivery expectations to ensure product goals are met.

-

Support the overall product strategy for our vertical SaaS ecosystem, with a strong focus on POS, payments, and operational workflows.
