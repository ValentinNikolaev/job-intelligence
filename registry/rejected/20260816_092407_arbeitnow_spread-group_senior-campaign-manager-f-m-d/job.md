### **Who We Are**

Spread Group is a renowned global provider of customized apparel and lifestyle products, distinguished by our commitment to made-to-order technology. Our core business model revolves around producing precisely what our customers desire, precisely when they desire it. As pioneers in this space, we combine sustainability with on-demand personalization, enabling individuals and businesses to express their unique identities through bespoke creations. Join us to be part of a global leader in the exciting world of personalized apparel and lifestyle products!

As we continue to expand our business, we are seeking a curious and passionate individual to join our team as the **(Senior) Campaign Manager (f/m/d)** and drive our business forward.

### **What Will Your Role Be**

We're looking for a Senior Campaign Manager to own marketing campaign planning and execution across our entire D2C business. You'll be the person who spots the right seasonal moments, turns them into compelling campaign concepts, and rallies our paid, CRM, SEO, and creative teams to bring them to life - on time and on target. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role with direct exposure to senior leadership.

### **What You´ll Do **

**Own the Campaign Calendar**

-

Identify seasonal events, cultural moments, and product opportunities worth building campaigns around

-

Maintain a forward-looking campaign roadmap 8–12 weeks ahead, balancing MP-specific, CYO-specific, and D2C-wide initiatives

**Plan Campaigns End-to-End**

-

Write clear campaign briefs covering objectives, audience, channels, messaging, KPIs, and timelines

-

Define the channel mix and coordinate with Paid Marketing, Paid Social, CRM, On-Page, and SEO teams to align on execution

**Drive Production**

-

Brief creative teams on asset needs - graphics, copy, landing pages, email templates - and manage the approval process

-

Initiate and oversee campaign-related photo shoots from concept to delivery

**Deliver and Measure**

-

Hold accountability for on-time, on-brief campaign delivery across all channels

-

Track campaign performance in real-time; run post-campaign analyses and share learnings

-

Report results and strategic recommendations with visibility regularly to the Head of Marketplace and Head of CYO
