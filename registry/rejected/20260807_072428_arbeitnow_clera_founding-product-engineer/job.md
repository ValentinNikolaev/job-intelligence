### About the Role

We're a seed-stage, AI-native healthtech startup building an operating system that empowers nurse practitioners to launch and run independent clinical practices. We're hiring a **Founding Product Engineer** to own and shape the clinician-facing product experience end-to-end.

You'll work closely with founders, designers, and clinicians to transform complex clinical workflows into polished, reliable software that directly improves care delivery. This is a high-ownership, high-impact role at a small, fast-moving team — you'll have an outsized influence on both the product and the engineering culture from day one.

### What You'll Do

-

Own major clinician-facing workflows from whiteboard through production — including implementation, testing, rollout, and iteration.

-

Collaborate with designers, founders, and clinicians to define product behavior; you won't be waiting for finished specs.

-

Drive frontend architecture and technical direction across provider and patient applications.

-

Influence APIs, data models, and overall system design while primarily owning the frontend.

-

Iterate quickly from rough concepts to polished production experiences alongside a product designer.

-

Debug complex production issues spanning browsers, APIs, third-party integrations, and infrastructure.

-

Contribute to engineering culture through thoughtful code reviews, technical discussions, and pragmatic decision-making.

-

Leverage AI development tools as a core part of your workflow while maintaining full ownership of system design and production quality.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Demonstrated experience leading significant technical initiatives or owning major product areas end-to-end.

-

Strong hands-on experience with **React** and **Next.js** (TypeScript familiarity assumed).

-

Proven track record of owning user-facing features from concept through production.

-

Comfortable collaborating across the frontend/backend boundary — working with APIs and debugging backend behavior.

-

Experience working directly with designers, customers, or domain experts to shape product direction.

-

Strong product intuition, engineering judgment, and debugging skills.

-

Comfortable in a fast-moving startup environment and experienced using AI development tools responsibly.

-

Able to commute to our Midtown Manhattan office 3 days per week (hybrid).

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience in healthcare, with FHIR, or in other regulated-software environments.

-

Prior experience as an early or founding engineer at a startup.

-

Experience building products used throughout the workday, where usability and workflow matter as much as correctness.

-

Experience evolving and maintaining a design system.

**Note:** Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.
