## **About Peec AI**

Peec AI is the visibility platform for the age of AI search. The way people discover brands is being rewritten. ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Mode and Copilot now sit between companies and their customers, and most brands have no idea what those models say about them. We built the platform marketing teams use to measure, understand and improve how they show up inside AI answers.

We went from 0 to $10M ARR in 16 months. We're around 80 people, based in Berlin with a growing presence in New York, and we're moving fast enough that the people joining now are writing the playbook everyone after them will run.

Measuring the problem is only the first half. The second half is software that acts: agents that watch what a brand is doing across AI search, surface what matters, and then do the work. We're building that agent layer from the ground up, and this PM will define and own it.
