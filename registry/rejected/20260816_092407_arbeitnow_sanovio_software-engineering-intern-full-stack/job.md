**About the Role**

As a Software Engineering Intern at SANOVIO, you’ll get hands-on experience building the AI-powered procurement infrastructure that tackles one of the biggest and most complex cost problems in European healthcare.

This is a fast-paced, hands-on role for aspiring engineers with high agency, strong technical curiosity, and a builder mindset. We’re looking for a full-stack generalist with a frontend affinity. You’re eager to design user flows and implement them in React and TypeScript, while learning to own outcomes end-to-end across backend, data, AI-enabled workflows, and cloud deployment. You thrive amid uncertainty, move quickly, and care deeply about writing clean, high-quality code.

If you are a passionate builder who thrives at the intersection of full-stack development and AI, this is your opportunity to work directly with the founders, contribute to a real product, and grow rapidly in a startup scaling toward €10M ARR in 2026.

**Tasks**

-

Contributing to **end-to-end product workflows**, from frontend UX to backend systems, data pipelines, and edge cases

-

Building **frontend experiences** in React and TypeScript alongside scalable, reliable backend systems

-

Supporting the design and development of** AI-enabled systems**, including embeddings, retrieval, and agents that create real operational leverage

-

Working on **data pipelines** for large-scale unstructured data

-

Making pragmatic **technical decisions on architecture, tools, and frameworks** to balance speed and quality

-

Deploying and maintaining software in **cloud environments **with a “you ship it, you run it” mindset

-

Writing clean, maintainable, and well-tested code with **scalable, reusable components and APIs**

-

**Collaborating across the stack** and learning through code reviews, pair programming, and mentorship from senior engineers

**About the interview process**

**Step 1: Intro Call**

Casual intro meeting with one of the founders to get to know each other and introduce SANOVIO.

**Step 2: Technical Interview**

The technical interview consists of two parts:

-

**A take-home exercise, including a short presentation**

-

**A system design interview**

For the take-home exercise, we’ll provide you with a task focusing on a real SANOVIO workflow and user flow in React and TypeScript, with a light end-to-end angle such as API integration, data modeling, edge cases, and clean architecture. Later, you’ll present your results to us.

**Step 3: Personal Fit**

Meet another co-founder to get a feel for our culture and working style.

**Step 4: Offer**

If we see a great fit, you’ll receive an offer.
