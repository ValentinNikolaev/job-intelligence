Mercura (**YC W25**) helps the companies that build the real world move at software speed, automating the back-office work that runs the **$12 trillion industry** behind every hospital, school, and power plant. We started with one of their biggest bottlenecks - turning messy technical requests into accurate quotes - and **eightfolded our revenue in 12 months**. With a team of 16, we're now targeting $10M by the end of 2026 as well as our expansion into the US.

## About the role

We’re looking for a **Founder's Associate Intern (Marketing & Ops)** to help make Mercura one of the most visible and compelling B2B AI companies in Europe.

You will take real ownership of how Mercura shows up externally — to customers, candidates and the broader ecosystem. You’ll work closely with the founders and team to turn ideas, company developments and customer insights into content, campaigns, events and new growth initiatives.

This is not an internship where you execute a predefined marketing calendar. We want someone who comes up with ideas, launches them quickly, measures what works and doubles down.

If you want to learn how to build a company, enjoy creating things from scratch and care deeply about results, this is an opportunity to own a meaningful part of Mercura from day one.

## What you will be working on

-

**Company & Founder Marketing:** Own and grow Mercura's company and founder LinkedIn presence. Turn what happens inside Mercura into content that reaches prospective customers, exceptional candidates and the wider industry.

-

**Growth Marketing:** Develop and test new ways to generate awareness, inbound demand and talent. Come up with experiments, launch them quickly and measure whether they actually move the numbers.

-

**SEO, AI Visibility & Website:** Help grow Mercura's organic presence across Google and AI search. Work with experienced external experts on SEO, AI visibility, website structure, conversion and design — and turn their recommendations into execution.

-

**Events:** Build memorable events for both customers and talent — from recruiting events and community formats to intimate dinners with CEOs and strategic prospects. Own everything from the initial idea and guest list to invitations and execution.

-

**Brand & Content:** Create landing pages, customer stories, campaigns, event concepts and other assets that make Mercura stand out and explain why what we are building matters.

-

**New Initiatives:** Spot opportunities we haven't thought of yet. If you believe a campaign, partnership, event or completely different idea could create meaningful impact, you will have the freedom to make it happen.
