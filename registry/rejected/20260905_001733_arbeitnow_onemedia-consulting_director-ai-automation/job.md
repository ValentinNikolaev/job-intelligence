**Your Mission**

We are looking for a Director AI & Automation to join our management team and take full ownership of our technology strategy, architecture, and AI transformation. You will own technical decision making for OMC, and you will be the driving force behind embedding AI across everything we build and deliver, both internally and for our clients.

The foundation for this is already in place. We have been working with AI and Machine Learning for years, and have long implemented services like predictive lead scoring for our clients. What started as pockets of expertise across the consultancy has now grown into something central to our strategy, and we are professionalizing it accordingly. What we need now is someone to own this transformation end to end and take it to the next level, a role important enough to warrant its own dedicated seat in management.

You will work in direct, close collaboration with the rest of the management team on overall strategy, consulting/delivery alignment, and on how technology and AI translate into revenue and market positioning.

**What You'll Own**

Technology & Architecture

-

Full ownership of technical and architectural decisions across OMC's systems

-

Definition and evolution of our internal and client facing technology stack

AI Transformation

-

Design and drive OMC's AI first strategy end to end, from internal tooling to client facing service offerings

-

Build and codify AI first productized service offerings we can sell to enterprise clients, including methodology, pricing, and delivery model

Client Delivery

-

Lead client engagements on Marketo/Adobe and Molequle architecture, including integrations with CRM and other systems in the client's MarTech stack

-

Lead client engagements focused on AI adoption and AI augmented delivery within the Adobe/Marketo space

-

Embed AI into our delivery practice through tooling, playbooks, and governance frameworks

Team Enablement

-

Enable and upskill OMC consultants and delivery teams on AI tooling and methodology

-

Roll out internal AI tooling across our teams, with clear adoption targets

Thought Leadership

-

Represent OMC's AI first positioning externally, including speaking engagements at industry events such as Adobe Summit and Marketo User Groups

-

Produce technical articles and blog content that establish OMC as a thought leader in AI for MarTech
