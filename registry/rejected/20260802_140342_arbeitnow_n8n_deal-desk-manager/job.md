## **Responsibilities**

-

Build n8n's Deal Desk function from scratch, AI- and automation-first: intake, approval workflow, and pricing guardrails.

-

Advise Sales on deal structure and pricing to protect margin without slowing down the sales cycle.

-

Maintain a simple set of discount and pricing rules that Sales can apply on their own, without escalating every deal.

-

Run a monthly and quarterly deal war room to keep deal velocity high, especially around month-end and quarter-end close.

-

Handle contract redlines and non-standard terms directly, keeping most negotiations out of Legal's hands.

-

Build a redline clause library of accepted and rejected language, so AI can suggest fallback terms on routine deals.

-

Automate approvals and quoting in CPQ, and use AI to triage incoming requests and flag which deal changes actually need re-approval.

-

Own Salesforce data quality and executed-contract hygiene for every deal.

-

Support Customer Success on non-standard renewal and expansion structuring.

-

Coordinate security and compliance requests (DPA, SOC 2, security addenda) between Sales, Security, and Legal.

-

Report monthly on deal desk performance: cycle time, discounting, and exceptions.
