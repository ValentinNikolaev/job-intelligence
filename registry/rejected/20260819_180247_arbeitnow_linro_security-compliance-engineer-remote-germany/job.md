## **About Linro**

Linro is a cloud security company building a real-time, agentic cloud-security platform. **Teams ship with AI. AI ships with Linro.**

Cloud infrastructure changes constantly, and increasingly it's AI agents making those changes, not people. Most security tools only catch problems after the fact. Linro works differently. We enforce what's allowed at every stage, from commit to production. We stop bad changes before they ship, and fix problems in real time once they're live.

We have each been on the receiving end of this problem: running cloud security at scale, building the platforms underneath it, and shipping in regulated systems where a wrong change isn't an option. Founded in Berlin in 2026 and built fully remote across Europe, Linro has a platform that's real and working, with plenty still to shape.

## **The role**

You will own Linro's security and compliance program end to end, starting from a blank page. Your job is to build that from scratch: figure out where we actually stand, find the gaps, and close them across a multi-cloud environment. Automate what can be automated instead of redoing the same work by hand every quarter.

## **What you will do**

-

**Take SOC2, ISO 27001 and 42001 from a blank page to audit-ready**. Run the gap analysis, document controls and policies, gather and map evidence, track remediation to done, and get us through audit.

-

**Automate compliance** instead of just babysitting it: build checks and guardrails so we stay continuously compliant, and help ensure our multi-cloud infrastructure (AWS and beyond) is managed as Infrastructure-as-Code with Terraform.

-

**Own our compliance evidence** end to end: keep it current, mapped, and audit-ready across whatever tooling we run.

-

**Support security operations**: monitoring, alert triage, and documenting findings.

-

**Stay close to how the product is actually built**. Our compliance checks run on the same engine we sell customers, so you are testing the product, not filling out paperwork off to the side.

## **What we are looking for**

-

Hands-on experience with at least one of SOC 2, ISO 27001, or ISO 42001: you have run a gap analysis, built and mapped evidence, and taken a program through an actual audit, not just studied the framework. Experience across more than one is a plus.

-

Genuine interest in cloud and information security, governance, and compliance, and the drive to own outcomes, not just tasks.

-

Comfort reading cloud configurations and IaC across multiple providers. Ability to automate scales with level.

-

Careful, structured, and trustworthy. You will handle sensitive material.

## **Nice to have**

-

Hands-on Terraform, and experience with AWS, GCP, and/or Azure.

-

Exposure to a compliance automation platform.

-

Prior general software engineering experience, ideally in the stacks we use day to day (Go, SQL, and cloud infrastructure)
