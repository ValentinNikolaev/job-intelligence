This role is focused on execution — building the first production-grade POC that turns anti-cheat outputs into real, user-facing product experiences.

You will work closely with the Data / Anti-cheat team and integrate detection outputs into scalable backend services and intuitive frontend dashboards.

This is a hands-on role with direct impact on product delivery.
