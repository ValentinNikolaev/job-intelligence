## **What you will do:**

-

Together with the Office team, you are responsible for the well-being of our 380+ employees in Hamburg and creating a superb work environment for them

-

You will be one of the contact persons for all colleagues with any office-related questions or feedback

-

You keep things in order and are always aware of what’s needed to maintain a great office environment

-

You monitor stock levels of office supplies, food & drinks and make timely orders to ensure that essentials are always available

-

You help to organize exciting events, from team get-togethers to big celebrations like our annual Christmas party

-

You coordinate repairs and maintenance in the offices, keep in contact with facility managers as well as cleaning services

-

You help to manage our in-house gym and our trainer schedule

-

You welcome national and international guests, ensuring they feel comfortable and prepare meeting rooms accordingly

-

You support the onboarding of new employees and provide keys to them

-

Depending on your experience and interests, take over responsibility for your own projects
