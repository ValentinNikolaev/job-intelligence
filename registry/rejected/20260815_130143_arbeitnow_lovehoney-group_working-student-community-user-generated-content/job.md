We are currently looking for a Working Student - Community & User Generated Content Operations who will be integral to driving high-level performance across Lovehoney Group. In this role, your focus will be supporting the operational management and quality assurance of user-generated content across multiple e-commerce platforms and community channels. The role helps ensure a consistent, trustworthy, and high-quality customer feedback experience by coordinating review processes, monitoring platform performance, and supporting day-to-day content operations.

**What you will do **

- Support moderation and quality control of user-generated content (reviews, Q&A, community interactions).

- Handle escalated review cases according to moderation guidelines.

- Monitor review syndication and content quality across brand shops and partner platforms.

- Ensure new product launches are correctly integrated into review systems.

- Identify operational inconsistencies and escalate issues where needed.

- Assist with monitoring community/forum activity.

- Support operational aspects of user research and customer feedback initiatives.

- Contribute ideas to improve operational efficiency and review quality processes.

- Help optimize workflows and reporting structures.
