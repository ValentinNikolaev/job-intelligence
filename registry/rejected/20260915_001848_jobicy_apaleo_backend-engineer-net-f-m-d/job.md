### Join Apaleo and Shape the Future of Hospitality Tech!

Apaleo is the world's most open, API-first property management platform, powering the next generation of hospitality operations. Our modular, AI-powered infrastructure lets hoteliers customise their tech stack, automate routine work and deliver seamless, personalised guest experiences, backed by best-in-class apps and autonomous AI agents that adapt to every stay.

We're looking for a **Mid-Level Backend Engineer** to join our Invoice & Tax team. This is where hospitality meets regulation: every property Apaleo powers needs to invoice correctly and handle tax right, in every country it operates in. If you like the idea of solving real compliance problems with clean, well-tested code, and owning your work end to end, read on.

**What You’ll Be Up To**

- Own backend projects end to end: from validating requirements with the product manager, through architecture and technical breakdown, to implementation and release.

- Build and extend backend services using .NET 10, running in Docker containers.

- Develop features from database design through business logic to RESTful APIs, with a direct line to how tax and invoicing rules across multiple countries shape the implementation.

- Write clean, well-tested code, including unit and end-to-end tests. We don't have a dedicated QA function, so you'll take full ownership of making sure what you ship holds up in production.

- Monitor your own services in production, and identify and resolve issues early.

- Work against dual deadlines on invoicing and tax, driven by regulatory requirements as well as customer commitments, and escalate early when something's at risk.

- Collaborate closely with your product manager, frontend engineers and other backend engineers in an agile team where your ideas and feedback are valued.

**What You’ll Bring to the Team**

- A degree in Computer Science, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

- Solid, independent experience delivering backend features end to end in a cloud environment.

- Strong .NET / C# backend development experience.

- A genuine tolerance for tax, legal and compliance subject matter. You don't need deep expertise from day one, but multi-country tax and invoicing legislation is core to this role, not a side detail, so it needs to hold your interest.

- A meticulous, detail-driven working style. Compliance work leaves little room for loose ends.

- A proactive approach to communication: comfortable clarifying requirements, flagging risk and escalating early rather than waiting to be asked.

- Hands-on experience with at least some of the following:

- Relational databases such as PostgreSQL.

- Docker and containerised workflows.

- Cloud platforms such as AWS (Azure experience is also welcome) and message-based or asynchronous systems, such as Kafka or Azure Service Bus.

- Familiarity with microservices concepts and API-driven architecture.

- Comfort breaking down tasks, estimating work and delivering features incrementally.

- Experience with, or interest in, AI-assisted coding tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Copilot or similiar.

- Fluent English.

**Nice to have**

- First experience with **Kafka** or other streaming / messaging systems.

- Exposure to **Kubernetes / ECS** or cloud-native DevOps practices.

- Basic experience with performance tuning or database optimizations.

- Experience working in SaaS or API-first products.
