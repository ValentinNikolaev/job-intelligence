You’re not just designing websites, you’re building and shipping the experiences that shape how Lio shows up to the world.

As our Webflow & Visual Specialist, you’ll work closely with our Founding Brand Operator, CEO, and GTM team to continuously build and improve Lio’s website. ## **What You’ll Do**

In this role, you will:

-

Build, launch, and continuously improve Lio’s website in Webflow or Framer

-

Work closely with our Founding Brand Operator, CEO, and GTM team to turn ideas and Figma designs into polished web experiences

-

Build new landing pages, campaign pages, and other web experiences from concept to launch

-

Create responsive layouts, interactions, and animations that make the website feel polished across devices

-

Help maintain and improve the structure, components, and consistency of our website as it grows

-

Turn complex ideas around AI and enterprise technology into simple and engaging digital experiences

-

Create visuals and assets for our website, social media, campaigns, events, and other brand touchpoints

-

Experiment with new tools, interactions, and web technologies to continuously improve Lio’s digital presence

##
**Your Profile**

You’ll thrive in this role if you:

-

Are highly comfortable building and shipping websites in Webflow or Framer

-

Are proficient in Figma and can confidently translate designs into responsive, production-ready pages

-

Understand responsive design, components, interactions, animations, and the fundamentals of modern web experiences

-

Have a strong visual eye for typography, layout, spacing, and composition

-

Can take a rough idea or Figma concept and turn it into a polished live experience

-

Enjoy both the creative and technical sides of building for the web

-

Care about the details and like figuring out how to make things work, not just how they should look

-

Learn new tools quickly and enjoy experimenting with new technologies

-

Feel comfortable working in a fast-moving environment and collaborating closely with others

Your experience can come from professional, freelance, or personal projects — what matters most to us is what you can build.
