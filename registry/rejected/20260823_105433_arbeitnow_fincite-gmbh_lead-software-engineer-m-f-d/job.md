**Your responsibilities**

• Together with two fellow Lead Engineers, you will help lead an engineering organization of around 20 people.
• You will provide technical and functional leadership for a team, with no more than five direct reports.
• You will build robust backend solutions for sophisticated wealth management products and actively shape their architecture.

**Your profile**

• Several years of backend development experience with Python and Django.
• Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL and Redis, together with experience in cloud-based architectures like Microsoft Azure or AWS
• A genuine passion for excellent software design and clean, maintainable solutions.
• Initial or proven experience in technical or functional leadership.
• A genuine interest in financial markets, investing and wealth management, enabling you to understand our users and build a lasting connection with the product.
• A growth mindset, curiosity and the motivation to develop both technically and as a leader.
