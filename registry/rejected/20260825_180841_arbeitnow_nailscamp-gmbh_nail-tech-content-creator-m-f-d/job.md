Your nails. Your creativity. Your camera.

## Aufgaben

Your responsibilities

💅 Nail Tech Expertise

You will be our technical nail expert, creating professional nail looks and demonstrating our products across different techniques and levels of expertise.

Your work will include:

- Classic Gel Polish / gel manicure

- Soft Gel Nail Tips / Full Cover Tips

- Builder Gel / BIAB

- Polyacryl / Polygel

- Acrylic

- Popits / Dual Forms

- **Russian Manicure – this is particularly important to us**

- Nail extensions and refills

- Nail Art and creative techniques

- Professional nail preparation

- Different nail shapes and structures

- Clean, precise and technically correct application

🎥 Content Creation

You will create engaging content for Instagram, TikTok and other social platforms.

This includes:

- Short-form videos and Reels

- Full step-by-step tutorials

- Educational content

- Product demonstrations

- Nail transformations

- Nail Art content

- Trend-based content

- Tips, tricks and professional techniques

- Before & after content

You won't just demonstrate a technique – you'll **explain it clearly and make people want to try it themselves**.

🎬 Be the face of our content

You are comfortable being on camera and showing your personality.

You don't need to be a professional presenter. **Authenticity, personality and confidence are more important to us.**

💡 Content & Product Development

You will work closely with our Marketing and Product teams to:

- Develop new content formats

- Test and review products

- Provide professional technical feedback

- Identify new nail trends

- Create new nail looks

- Turn customer questions and trends into useful content

## Qualifikation

What we're looking for

Nail expertise

You have **strong professional Nail Tech skills** and are comfortable working across multiple techniques.

**Russian Manicure is particularly important for this position.**

You have a strong understanding of nail preparation, product application, structure, shaping and overall nail health.

Most importantly, you have **high technical standards and an eye for detail**.

Experience with video editing is a plus, but **you don't need to be a professional videographer**.

Personality

You are creative, curious, reliable and hands-on.

You enjoy experimenting with new techniques and trends, but you also care about technical quality.

You're someone who can look at a nail trend and think:

**"I know exactly how we could make this into great content."**
