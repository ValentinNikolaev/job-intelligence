**eleQtron** develops and operates full-stack quantum computers based on trapped-ion technology. Quantum computing is a new computing paradigm rapidly approaching commercial viability. Our unique approach combines laser-cooled trapped ions with microwave control and tailored magnetic fields. Our next-generation computing machines under development will be able to find solutions to problems that are out of reach today, thus enabling new innovative products and services for a brighter future.

**About the Role:**

We are looking for a highly experienced and technically strong **Senior Mechanical Engineer (f/m/d)** who can take ownership of complex mechanical designs from concept through detailed engineering, manufacturing, assembly, and validation.

This is a hands-on engineering role for someone with strong mechanical engineering fundamentals, extensive practical design experience, and advanced SolidWorks capabilities.

**Responsibilities:**

-

Take technical ownership of complex mechanical components, assemblies, and subsystems from concept through manufacturing, assembly, and validation

-

Develop robust mechanical designs for high-precision scientific and industrial equipment

-

Create and maintain high-quality SolidWorks parts, assemblies, manufacturing drawings, BOMs, and technical documentation

-

Perform tolerance analysis and define appropriate fits, interfaces, geometric tolerances, and dimensional requirements

-

Translate system-level requirements into clear mechanical requirements, interfaces, and manufacturable design solutions

-

Define manufacturing requirements and work closely with suppliers and Procurement on manufacturability, materials, tolerances, and quality

-

Develop, test, and improve prototypes and actively contribute to design reviews and technical decision-making

**Required skills:**

-

BSc or MSc degree in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Precision Engineering, or a closely related discipline

-

Minimum 8 years of professional mechanical engineering experience

-

Advanced professional proficiency in SolidWorks is mandatory

-

Demonstrable experience designing complex mechanical assemblies, bespoke machinery, or technically demanding systems

-

Strong mechanical engineering fundamentals, including manufacturing methods, materials, tolerance analysis, GD&T, DfM/DfA, structural design, and mechanical interface

-

Strong ownership of mechanical designs and technical decisions

**We value:**

-

CSWP – Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional certification

-

Experience in industries such as scientific instrumentation, semiconductor equipment, aerospace, precision engineering, special-purpose machinery, optics, or similar fields

-

Experience with high-precision systems and/or exposure to vacuum, cryogenic, thermal, optical, or vibration-sensitive applications

-

Excellent engineering judgment and the ability to identify and improve weak or risky design solutions

-

Practical understanding of how components are manufactured, assembled, tested, and serviced
