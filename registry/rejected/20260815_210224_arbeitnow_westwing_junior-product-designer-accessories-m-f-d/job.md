**JOIN OUR TEAM**

As a **Junior Product Designer **for Accessories, you will support the creation of inspiring and on-trend products across our most diverse category including Deco, Tabletop, Kids, and Bathroom. Working closely with the development teams, you will contribute to shaping the Westwing Collection with a strong focus on design, color, materials, and finishings. This is a unique opportunity to grow within a fast-paced, international environment and develop your design skills while contributing to a premium and design-led portfolio. This position is **permanent** and based in **Munich.**

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

- Design and develop on-trend and visually appealing products across multiple categories
- Assist in managing the design pipeline and collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (e.g., Buying, Quality and Product Management)
- Conduct trend and market research to support collection building and design decisions, leveraging AI and automation where applicable
- Prepare design concepts, sketches and product documentation, including detailed CMF (Color, Material and Finish) specifications
- Support in maintaining consistency and quality across the Westwing Collection

**YOU COME WITH**

- 1+ years of professional experience or relevant internships in product, industrial, or graphic design, or a related field
- A deep passion for product, driven by a clear ambition for design excellence
- Strong interest in product development, materials, and design trends
- A good eye for aesthetics, detail, and proportions
- Understanding of materials, production processes, and sustainability is a plus
- Ability to work in a structured and collaborative way within a fast-paced environment
- Strong communication skills and a proactive, hands-on mindset
- Fluency in English
