**About the Role:** The Senior Talent Acquisition Partner is a sourcing-first recruiter and strategic advisor to hiring leaders, anchored in EMEA GTM hiring (Sales, Marketing, Customer Success) and built to flex as a utility player wherever the business needs coverage next. Your primary focus is finding and closing GTM talent across EMEA before it's actively looking; secondly you’ll be picking up other departments and levels when volume, timing, or gaps require it. You'll operate inside an AI-forward TA function, using Findem for talent intelligence and outbound sourcing, Ashby as our core Applicant Tracking System, and BrightHire for interview signals, to move faster and with better data than a traditional recruiting model allows.

This role directly shapes how Camunda builds its EMEA GTM engine and is a proof point for what a modern, sourcing-led TA function looks like in the region.

**What You Will Do:**

-

**Own EMEA GTM hiring end-to-end**, acting as a strategic advisor to Sales and Customer Success leaders on market realities, compensation structures, notice periods, and candidate expectations across different countries - and shaping hiring plans before reqs are even opened

-

**Build proactive sourcing pipelines in Findem**, surfacing GTM talent in untapped markets before they're actively looking, with regular market intelligence shared back to hiring leaders

-

**Keep our hiring infrastructure clean and disciplined:** use Ashby as our system of record with accurate stages and active scorecards; use BrightHire consistently to capture interview signal and coach hiring teams on feedback quality

-

**Be the trusted point of contact** hiring leaders reach out to for updates - not the other way around - while also flexing into other EMEA departments and levels when the business needs coverage without losing sourcing rigor

-

**Partner directly on role scope and market strategy** with EMEA hiring leaders before job descriptions are finalized, helping them hire smarter and faster

-

**Report pipeline health and sourcing effectiveness** to business leadership with a regular cadence, making GTM pipeline visibility a standing metric

-

**Create a candidate experience that is fast**, transparent, and consistent with a company operating at Camunda's pace

**What You Will Bring to the Role:**

**Must Haves:**

-

6+ years of full-cycle recruitment at a high-growth, scaling SaaS company with direct experience owning EMEA or multi-country hiring at scale

-

Proven track record recruiting GTM talent (Sales, Marketing, or Customer Success) across EMEA - with working knowledge of compensation structures, notice periods, and candidate expectations by country

-

A sourcing-first mindset: you can point to candidates you found and hired, not just candidates you processed through a pipeline

-

Experience partnering with hiring leaders as an advisor and strategist, shaping role scope and market strategy - not just fulfilling requisition requests

A solutions-driven, high-pace mindset suited to a company still building its operating model, with comfort operating autonomously in a remote, distributed environment

**Nice to Haves:**
