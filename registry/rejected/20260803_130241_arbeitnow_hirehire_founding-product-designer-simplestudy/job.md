## About SimpleStudy

SimpleStudy is building the exam prep experience students actually want to use. The numbers back it up: 1.5M+ students across the UK, Ireland, Brazil, South Africa, and Australia, growing by ~40k to 50k new users every week. The product runs on web, mobile, and desktop, and the team ships fast, with heavy A/B testing and product-led growth built in.

SimpleStudy is post-seed, backed by the first investors of Facebook and Revolut, and board members of Stripe and Intercom. The team is ~30 people, based mostly in Lisbon. Lean, product-obsessed, and deliberately not your typical edtech company.

They're now hiring the first founding designer to work directly with the co-founder on what comes next and develop the app as the best next generation of the consumer experience.

## Your impact

-

Own the full design process from understanding ambiguous product problems to designing and shipping solutions, without needing someone to frame the brief for you

-

Partner directly with the co-founder on product direction, priorities, and design philosophy. Bring your point of view, and defend it

-

Work in a fast, data-led environment: ship features, measure impact, iterate; use Mixpanel and product analytics fluently

-

Drive consumer experience design: onboarding, social features, viral loops, and retention, work that reaches hundreds of thousands of students from day one

## What you need to succeed

-

**Senior-level product thinking.** You've owned complex design problems end to end, from "what are we solving and why?" through to a shipped solution. You don't wait to be told what the problem is

-

**Consumer product background.** You've designed for B2C audiences at scale, and you understand growth, retention, and onboarding. The kind of products people find for themselves, rather than ones sold to companies

-

**Comfort with ambiguity and speed.** You come alive when you're figuring things out, rather than executing a roadmap someone else already defined

-

**A/B testing mindset.** You've worked in growth-oriented environments, and you design for experimentation. Every ship is a way to learn what's actually true

-

**Craft and taste.** Your design sense is a given. What matters is the thinking behind it

## What you get

-

**Direct impact at scale.** Your designs reach 1.5M+ students. A new feature ships to hundreds of thousands of people. Fast feedback loops, real stakes.

-

**Ownership from day one.** You'd work directly with the co-founder. What you design gets built.

-

**Equity.** SimpleStudy is generous with stock options, and you're joining early enough for it to mean something.

-

**Remote Europe.** Work from where you choose, with team offsites three times a year.

-

**Investors who've done it before.** First investors of Facebook and Revolut, plus board members of Stripe and Intercom.
