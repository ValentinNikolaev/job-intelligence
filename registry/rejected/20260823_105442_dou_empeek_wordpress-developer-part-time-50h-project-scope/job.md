- Hands-on experience with WooCommerce.
- Ability to customize checkout flows, product pages, and payment integrations.
- Experience with performance optimization and handling large product catalogs.
- Experience integrating Google Analytics (including GA4) and Google Search Console.
- Experience with Attentive (or similar marketing automation tools) is a plus.
- Experience integrating Stripe payments.
- Strong understanding of SEO best practices.
- Comfortable working independently and interpreting visual references/assets without formal design mockups.
- At least Upper-Intermediate English; fluent in Ukrainian.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Maintain and enhance an existing WordPress platform serving both patients and partner providers.
- Integrate third-party API snippets and services without disrupting existing functionality.
- Customize and extend WooCommerce features, including checkout flows and payment integrations.
- Configure and maintain Google Analytics (GA4), Google Search Console, and marketing automation tools.
- Implement visual and layout updates based on provided design assets and references.
- Apply performance optimization best practices across pages and product catalogs.
- Collaborate closely with the PM and a technical consultant to align on scope and priorities.
