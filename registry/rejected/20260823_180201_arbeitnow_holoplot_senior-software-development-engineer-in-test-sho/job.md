**The ideal candidate is**

We are looking for a Senior Software Development Engineer in Test who thrives at the intersection of software engineering, audio technology, and product quality.

You have a strong engineering mindset, deep curiosity about how complex systems behave, and practical experience testing audio software in real-world environments. You are comfortable working close to the code, designing robust test automation, debugging across software and hardware boundaries, and helping teams build products that are reliable, observable, and easy to validate.

At HOLOPLOT, software is central to the design, configuration, deployment, monitoring, and operation of sound systems.

Our show production team has been working on a wide range of audio applications/plugins for immersive audio content production that will work hand in hand with our sound system. Testing such applications and plugins requires more than checking whether features work. It requires an understanding of signal flow, timing, synchronization, real-time constraints, interoperability, and how audio systems fail in practice. It also requires creating representable mocks and setups that are effective, economical, and maintainable.

**Your role and responsibilities**

As a Senior Software Development Engineer in Test in this team, you will partner closely with software engineers and product designers to define test strategies, build automation, and validate complex workflows across desktop applications, audio plugins, networked audio systems, and spatial audio features. You will develop automated, scalable test frameworks, explore test infrastructure, and increase confidence in release cycles.

You will work on both black-box and white-box testing approaches, depending on the problem: from backend-driven end-to-end tests to signal-analysis-based tests, UI validation, and system-level regression tests.

You will help integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines and improve the reliability, runtime, debuggability, and maintainability of the test suite.

You will create test tools that make it easier to automatically capture, analyze, compare, and validate audio behavior.

You will help teams move from manual validation toward reproducible, deterministic, and observable test workflows.
