🎯 Your main goal will be to turn n8n's pricing and packaging into a deliberate, data-informed growth lever - one that scales revenue and adoption across our entire go-to-market, from our 1.5 million-strong community to global Enterprise accounts.

To do so, here's what you'll own:

**BUILD THE ANALYTICAL FOUNDATION FOR PRICING**

-

Build and own the model connecting unit economics, agent run cost, willingness-to-pay, gross margin, and customer value.

-

Maintain competitive and market intelligence so our pricing keeps pace with a category that's shifting fast.

-

Run pricing experiments and turn the results into documented, reusable learnings.

**DESIGN OUR PRICING & PACKAGING ARCHITECTURE**

-

Shape plan design and packaging across our community, self-serve, and Enterprise tiers, plus add-ons like our startup and charity plans.

-

Design pricing for new SKUs and consumption-based models as our product and cost-to-serve evolve around AI Assistant and n8n Connect.

-

Build packaging frameworks and decision tools that product teams use directly to launch new features.

**DRIVE AND ROLL OUT PRICING DECISIONS**

-

Lead cross-functional pricing decision-making and secure buy-in across Product, Sales, Marketing, Finance, RevOps, and Data.

-

Own the rollout of major pricing changes end-to-end, from decision to launch to measured impact.

-

Build and own our pricing frameworks: list pricing, discount guardrails, decision rubrics, grandfathering rules, and usage-based billing decisions.

**Requirements**

**Must-have**

-

📈 **Pricing leadership in B2B SaaS**: You have 6+ years in pricing, monetisation, or commercial strategy, including at least one full cycle leading a major pricing model transition — subscription to consumption, seat-based to usage-based, or a platform repricing.

-

⚡ **Hands-on with modern pricing models**: You've personally designed and rolled out consumption-based, outcome-based, or AI-native pricing at a company past $100M ARR.

-

🔀 **Both self-serve and Enterprise motions**: You've owned pricing on both sides of the funnel — self-serve/PLG (activation, conversion, tier design, funnel experimentation) *and* Enterprise, sales-led (packaging for large accounts, deal economics, discount governance). n8n monetises both, so fluency in one motion only isn't enough.

-

🧮 **Deep analytical rigour**: You build the financial model yourself and reason fluently about unit economics, cost-to-serve, gross margin, willingness-to-pay, and elasticity.

-

🔌 **Billing fluency, technical and operational**: You understand event metering, rating, entitlements, proration, overage, and revenue recognition well enough to design pricing that's actually operable — and you've partnered with a Billing function on invoicing, dunning, true-ups, and disputes.

-

🛠️ **Empathy for technical users and buyers**: You understand where, how, and why developers and technical buyers assign value across a product experience.

-

🧩 **Frameworks over bottlenecks**: You've built packaging frameworks and decision tools that product teams use independently, rather than routing every pricing decision through you.

-

🤝 **Cross-functional influence**: You bring Product, Finance, Sales, Customer Success, Engineering, and Billing into alignment on contentious commercial decisions.

-

🧭 **Motivated by scope, not headcount**: You're energised by owning a global, high-stakes topic as an individual contributor, with analytical support from Data and RevOps and rollout support from Program Management.

**Nice-to-have**

-

🎓 **Formal pricing training**: You've been trained at Simon-Kucher or the pricing practice of another consultancy, and you bring that rigour with you.

-

🧑‍💻 **Developer tools experience**: You've priced products sold to engineers and technical teams.

-

🌍 **Open source or freemium background**: You know how to design pricing that converts a free community into paying customers without breaking trust.
