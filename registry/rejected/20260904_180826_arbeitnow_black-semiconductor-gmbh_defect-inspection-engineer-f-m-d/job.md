**Your Role**

As a Defect Inspection Engineer/Expert, you will be responsible for ensuring the defect-free quality of semiconductor wafers using Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) systems and unpatterned wafer inspection.

You will detect defects such as particles, scratches, haze, and surface roughness that could impact the yield of our integrated graphene photonics process flow. Working in a cleanroom environment, you will collaborate with process, equipment and integration teams to set up, qualify and optimize inspection recipes, troubleshoot issues, and continuously improve defect detection and classification.

**What You’ll Do**

-

Perform AOI and unpatterned wafer inspection on materials such as bare silicon, epitaxial layers and polished wafers

-

Set up, qualify and optimize inspection recipes

-

Fine-tune inspection tool and recipe parameters to improve defect-detection sensitivity, inspection quality, repeatability and overall performance

-

Analyze defect data to identify trends, root causes and potential process excursions

-

Interpret defect data and translate findings into actionable insights for process teams

-

Generate defect maps, Pareto charts and reports to communicate findings

-

Participate in Design of Experiments (DOE) to optimize inspection parameters

-

Troubleshoot inspection-related issues and support continuous improvement in defect detection and classification

-

Collaborate with process, equipment and integration teams

**What You Bring**

-

A bachelor’s degree or above in electrical engineering, materials science, physics or a related field

-

5+ years of experience in semiconductor inspection, with a focus on AOI and unpatterned wafer inspection

-

Hands-on experience with unpatterned wafer inspection systems, such as SPx, and AOI tools such as Eagle or Dragonfly

-

Understanding of semiconductor process flows, including polishing, epitaxy and cleaning

-

Familiarity with defect classification, SEM review and Statistical Process Control (SPC)

-

Proficiency in AOI and defect-review software, such as KLA Klarity and Camtek Inspecta

-

Ability to optimize inspection tools and recipe parameters for sensitivity, inspection quality and repeatability

-

Ability to interpret defect data and identify relevant trends and improvement opportunities

-

Proficiency in JMP for statistical analysis, data visualization and DOE

-

Familiarity with MATLAB or Python is an advantage

-

Excellent communication and teamwork skills, with the ability to collaborate across functions

**What We Offer**

-

The opportunity to work on defect inspection for an integrated graphene photonics process flow

-

A cleanroom-based role with close collaboration across process, equipment and integration teams
