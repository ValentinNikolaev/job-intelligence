Senior UX/UI Designer (m/f/d), Banking & Insurance, Freelance, 100% Remote, starting immediately. Project duration: 6 months or longer, fulltime.

For our friendly team we are looking for an experienced Senior UX/UI Designer (m/f/d) on a freelance basis, who will support us in client projects in the banking and insurance sector, from research and concept to polished UI.

New Monday is a digital agency based in Berlin, working 100% remotely. Our clients include well-known corporations such as Atruvia, DAK, SAP and Lufthansa, as well as modern start-ups like NUMA Stays.

As a company, we stand out through flat hierarchies, an agile mindset and a New Work approach. All New Monday team members work 100% remotely (home office), but thanks to our positive remote culture we still stay closely connected (you can read more about this in the "Benefits" section).

## Tasks

- UX and UI design for digital products in the banking and insurance sector, from first concept to final handover.

- Planning, conducting and evaluating qualitative and quantitative user research (e.g. interviews, usability tests) and turning the results into concrete design decisions.

- Development of wireframes, user flows and prototypes based on research insights and user requirements.

- (Re-)design of digital products, tools and software solutions with a focus on usability and conversion.

- Working with and contributing to design systems, in close collaboration with development teams.

- Preparing and presenting design work to stakeholders on the client side, including senior management.

- Moderating workshops for ideation, requirements analysis and conception.

- Close collaboration with interdisciplinary teams in corporate structures, together with our internal design team.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of relevant professional experience in UX/UI design. This is essential for this position.

- An outstanding portfolio that shows both your design thinking and your visual craft. Applications without a portfolio cannot be considered.

- Excellent communication and presentation skills, including with stakeholders at senior level.

- Excellent written and spoken English.

- Advanced experience with Figma, including design systems, tokens, components and variables. This is essential for this position.

- Confident use of AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) and modern tooling as part of your daily design workflow.

- Experience in the banking or insurance sector, or in other regulated industries, is a big plus.

- Experience working in corporate structures is desirable.

- Available to start immediately and committed for a project duration of at least 6 months.
