**About ResearchGate**

ResearchGate is where science happens online. Over 26 million researchers globally use our platform to discover groundbreaking research, connect with peers, and advance their work.

**What we do matters.** By providing the world’s leading pharma, biotech, and scientific organizations with precision digital advertising and content solutions, we connect them directly with the global scientific community. The partnerships you build will bring vital tools, clinical data, and breakthrough technologies directly to the minds actively solving humanity's most complex medical and scientific challenges. Your work doesn't just drive revenue—it directly accelerates the discoveries that change the course of human history.

**The Role**

We are looking for a high-performing **Senior Sales Manager** to join our **Marketing Solutions** department, owning and growing a portfolio of enterprise life science accounts. You will drive full-cycle sales—from strategic outbound prospecting to close—**selling high-value digital advertising solutions and custom media campaigns directly to life science marketers and their media agencies.**

**This is a consultative, multi-stakeholder digital media sale with true deal ownership and a clear sense of purpose.** You won't just pitch and pass; you will architect partnerships that connect pioneering life science brands with the global scientific community.

**The Team**

Reporting to the VP of Sales, you will join a high-performing, international, and collaborative commercial team focused on connecting researchers with tools and solutions that accelerate scientific progress.

**What You’ll Actually Do:**

- **Drive Ad Revenue:** Close net-new and expansion digital advertising business across a dedicated portfolio of pharma, biotech, and scientific brands.

- **Consultative Media Selling:** Run structured, deeply analytical discovery sessions to understand brand objectives and build tailored, insight-led **digital media and content strategy proposals**.

- **Navigate Marketer Ecosystems:** Confidently pitch to and steer deals through complex brand teams, **digital marketing leads, agency partners**, and procurement.

- **Strategic Account Planning:** Build and execute long-term account roadmaps to scale our share of wallet and maximize ad spend within key organizations.

- **Collaborate for Campaign Success:** Partner closely with Account Management and Ad Ops to ensure **ad campaigns perform flawlessly against client KPIs**, securing renewals and long-term retention.

**What Success Looks Like**

- Consistently hitting and exceeding your quarterly advertising revenue quota.

- Growing key enterprise accounts year-over-year through strategic digital media upselling.

- Delivering high-ROI ad campaigns that naturally convert into predictable, repeat business.

**What We’re Looking For:**

-

**The Track Record**: 5+ years of closing experience in B2B digital advertising, ad-tech, digital media sales, or publisher solutions.

-

**Marketer Fluency**: Deep understanding of digital advertising metrics (CPM, CTR, lead generation, brand awareness) and how life science marketers allocate budgets.

-

**Industry Context:** Experience selling into pharma, biotech, or life sciences—or managing these verticals from an ad agency perspective—is a strong plus.

-

**Pipeline Ownership**: A proactive hunter mindset. You are comfortable prospecting, mapping out marketing departments, and building your own pipeline without relying on inbound leads.

-

**Commercial Polish**: Strong commercial instinct. You know how to qualify rigorously, multi-thread within complex marketing organizations, and close high-value ad contracts
