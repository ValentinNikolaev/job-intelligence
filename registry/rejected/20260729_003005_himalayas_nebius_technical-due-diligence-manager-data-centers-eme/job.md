### About the Role

As a Technical Due Diligence Manager, you'll evaluate the technical feasibility of AI infrastructure opportunities across the Americas before [Nebius](https://himalayas.app/companies/nebius) commits capital. Working within the Infrastructure Acquisition Group, you'll lead multidisciplinary engineering due diligence across powered land and build-to-suit developments, partnering with Site Selection, Engineering, Energy, Legal, and Project Development to identify technical risk, assess development feasibility, and support investment decisions.

This is a build-phase role on a build-phase team. Many of the standards, playbooks, consultant relationships, and technical review processes you'll use are still being written—in some cases, you'll help write them. We're looking for someone who can independently lead complex technical due diligence programs while helping build the systems that make every future evaluation faster, more consistent, and more scalable.

### What You'll Own

- Lead multidisciplinary technical due diligence for powered land, build-to-suit, and other strategic infrastructure opportunities.

- Define the scope of engineering, environmental, geotechnical, surveying, utility, and entitlement studies required to evaluate prospective sites.

- Procure, manage, and coordinate external engineering and technical consultants from scope development through final recommendation.

- Interpret technical findings and provide development recommendations supporting acquisition decisions.

- Prepare technical due diligence summaries and present risks, constraints, and recommendations to executive leadership.

### Technical Risk Assessment

- Evaluate geotechnical conditions, environmental constraints, utility capacity, network connectivity and fiber access, entitlement requirements, constructability, and development schedules.

- Identify technical risks that may impact project cost, schedule, or feasibility.

- Recommend mitigation strategies and additional investigations where appropriate.

- Quantify technical risks and communicate commercial impacts to Site Selection and Project Development.

### Studies & Consultant Management
