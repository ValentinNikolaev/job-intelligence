**Your Role **

We are looking for a motivated Working Student to support our Customer Success Team in Cologne.

In this role, you will gain hands-on experience working with customers, contribute to customer projects, and support experienced Customer Success Managers with standard trainings and webinars.

**How You Make an Impact**

- You support our Customer Success Managers in customer projects and help customers in a structured and solution-oriented way.
- You take ownership of your own tasks in customer interactions – from preparing and documenting information to preparing customer-specific content and following up on next steps.
- You support online trainings and webinars for new users and learn, together with experienced colleagues, how to communicate content clearly and in a customer-oriented way.
- You work closely with our Customer Success Team and gain hands-on insights into customer support, the digital energy industry, and our software solutions.

**Your Profile**

- You speak very good German and good English.
- You are pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree with a technical focus, e.g. Electrical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Energy Management, Renewable Energies, or a comparable field.
- You are hands-on, work independently, and enjoy finding practical solutions.
- You enjoy working with customers and users and have a good understanding of their needs.
- You enjoy preparing information, trainings, and meetings and documenting results clearly and in a structured way.
- You are confident using MS Office and ideally have experience with tools such as Notion, HubSpot, or similar CRM/documentation tools.
- You are interested in Customer Success, digital products, and the energy industry and are eager to grow in an open and collaborative team.
