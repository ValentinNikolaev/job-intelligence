### About the Role

A fast-growing, Series A-backed AI search analytics company in Berlin is looking for a **Senior Backend Developer** to architect and build the systems that power its AI-driven recommendations platform. You'll work at the intersection of scalable backend engineering and cutting-edge AI integrations, collaborating closely with product, data, and frontend teams to deliver reliable, high-performance insights for marketing teams worldwide.

Formal degrees or fixed years of experience are **not required** — demonstrated excellence and the ability to move fast are what matter most.

### What You'll Do

-

Architect and build backend systems powering AI-driven recommendations.

-

Design, implement, and maintain scalable APIs, data pipelines, and databases delivering reliable analytics insights.

-

Collaborate with product, data, and frontend teams to translate requirements into robust technical solutions.

-

Develop and maintain automated tests and CI/CD pipelines to ensure quality and rapid delivery.

-

Integrate with AI model APIs (OpenAI, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Llama) to support real-time recommendations.

-

Ensure security, authentication/authorization, and data privacy across backend services.

-

Implement end-to-end changes across backend services with clean, maintainable code.

-

Build and scale REST/GraphQL APIs and data models to support AI-driven product features.

-

Actively leverage AI-assisted developer tools (e.g. Cursor) to enhance productivity while keeping committed code concise and clear.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Deep proficiency in **TypeScript**.

-

Hands-on experience with the core stack: **GCP (Cloud Functions, Cloud Tasks), PostgreSQL, Firestore**.

-

Experience integrating with AI model APIs such as **OpenAI, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and/or Llama** for real-time use cases.

-

Strong system design and architectural decision-making abilities.

-

Experience designing and building scalable **REST and/or GraphQL APIs**, data pipelines, and data models.

-

Experience implementing **automated testing** (unit/integration) and **CI/CD pipelines**.

-

Solid knowledge of security best practices, authentication/authorization, and data privacy.

-

Ability to match the code style of an existing codebase and implement changes end-to-end.

-

Excellent collaboration skills across product, data, and frontend teams.

-

A demonstrated track record of exceptional performance or notable achievements in past projects.

-

Willingness to work on-site full-time in Berlin, including extended hours when needed.
