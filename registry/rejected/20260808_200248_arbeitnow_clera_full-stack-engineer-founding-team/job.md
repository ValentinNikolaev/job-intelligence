### About the Role

This is a founding-team engineering position at a pre-seed AI-powered SaaS startup operating in the construction technology (ConTech) space. As one of the first engineers, you will own the product technically from day one — partnering directly with the founders to build a category-defining platform that automates project documentation, decision tracking, workflow management, and risk analysis for the construction industry.

You will have end-to-end ownership across the full stack, from data models and backend systems to intuitive, responsive user interfaces, with direct and visible impact on customer outcomes in a rapidly evolving market.

**Important:** German language proficiency is a hard requirement for this role.

### What You'll Do

-

Build customer-facing features spanning database design through beautiful, responsive frontends.

-

Design robust, scalable systems capable of handling complex workflows and large, real-world datasets.

-

Ship code fast — work in a high-velocity environment where ideas go from concept to production in days.

-

Build sophisticated data workflows leveraging AI and vector data capabilities.

-

Collaborate closely with founders to shape product direction and key technical decisions.

### What We're Looking For

**Required skills & experience:**

-

Fluent German speaker (hard requirement).

-

Strong proficiency in **TypeScript** and **Next.js** for full-stack development.

-

Hands-on experience with **PostgreSQL** and **Supabase**, including vector data.

-

Familiarity with **LangChain**, **TanStack Query**, and **shadcn UI** for building UIs and data workflows.

-

A track record of stable tenure — preferably roles lasting 12+ months (internships excluded).

-

Strong computer science fundamentals; a university degree is valued but not required.

**Nice to have:**

-

Prior experience in a startup environment, shipping features rapidly.

-

Experience in or knowledge of the construction or project management industry.

-

Ability to take full end-to-end ownership from database to UI with a direct eye on customer impact.

-

Collaborative, founder-ready mindset — comfortable contributing to product and strategic decisions.

### Location & Work Arrangement

This role is based in **Munich, Germany** and follows a **hybrid** working model. You are expected to be in the office at least three days per week, with flexibility to work remotely for a few weeks at a time.

**Visa sponsorship is not available** for this position.
