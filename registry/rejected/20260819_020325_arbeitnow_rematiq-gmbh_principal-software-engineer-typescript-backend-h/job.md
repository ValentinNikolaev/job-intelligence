## About the job

As a **Principal Software Engineer, Platform**, you’ll build the services, workflows, and developer-facing APIs behind our AI requirements engine, document processing pipelines, and compliance workflows used by leading MedTech companies.

You’ll work closely with product, domain experts, and data teams to turn complex regulatory requirements into reliable, intuitive product experiences. Because we operate in a highly regulated industry, correctness, traceability, and reliability are central to how we build.

We believe in the value of in-person collaboration and work in a hybrid setup, with the team coming together in our office in the heart of **Berlin-Mitte** typically **four** days per week.

## Our stack

We work in a modern **TypeScript‑first monorepo**:

-

**Core:** Node.js 20+, TypeScript 5+, pnpm workspaces, Turborepo.

-

**Frontend:** React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, and design system.

-

**Backend & workflows:** Express-based services, with DBOS for durable workflows.

-

**Data & storage:** PostgreSQL with pgvector, Prisma ORM, Azure Blob Storage.

-

**AI & observability:** Azure OpenAI, Langfuse, Sentry, plus logging/tracing with tools like Winston and OpenTelemetry.

-

**Dev experience:** AI‑assisted workflows with Cursor and Claude Code, integrated into our TypeScript‑first monorepo and review process.
