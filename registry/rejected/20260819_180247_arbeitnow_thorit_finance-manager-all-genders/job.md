# **About the Role**

You'll be the person who brings order to Thorit's day-to-day bookkeeping — owning accounts payable, receivable, and general ledger end-to-end and building the processes that let the company scale cleanly. This is a hands-on, high-trust role: you'll work closely with our external tax advisor and take real ownership of how bookkeeping runs.

# What You'll Do

-

**Own** accounts payable, accounts receivable, and general ledger bookkeeping, including bank reconciliation

-

**Build** a documented, repeatable monthly close process where none exists today

-

**Clean up** aged debtor and creditor items and keep OPOS lists current

-

**Prepare and submit** monthly advance sales tax returns (Umsatzsteuervoranmeldungen) accurately and on time

-

**Support** the annual financial statements in direct collaboration with our external Steuerberater

-

**Handle** travel expenses for the team

-

**Document** Finance SOPs in Confluence so processes outlive any one person

# What You Bring

## **Must-haves**

-

Several years of hands-on experience in German financial bookkeeping (Finanzbuchhaltung)

-

Working fluency in DATEV

-

Solid general knowledge of German commercial and tax law as it applies to bookkeeping

-

Comfort working independently and building structure without close day-to-day supervision

-

Native-level German and working business English

## **Nice-to-haves**

-

Payroll experience (Lohn- und Gehaltsabrechnung) and knowledge of DEÜV/ELStAM/DATEV Lohn + Gehalt — a plus if payroll is ever brought in-house, but not required since it currently runs externally

-

Experience supporting the annual financial statement alongside an external tax advisor

-

Prior experience in a scaling company or consultancy environment

# Your Team

You'll report directly to our CEO and will be part of the Business Functions that runs Thorit internally, separate from our client-facing Practices.
