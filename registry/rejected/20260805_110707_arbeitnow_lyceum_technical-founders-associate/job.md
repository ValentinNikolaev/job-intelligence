You'll join our product and engineering team at the intersection of customers and code. This isn't a classic PM role - you'll work hands-on with some of the most senior engineers we have, helping build our GPU cloud and inference platform end to end. You'll figure out what needs to be built, talk to the customers using it, and help ship it.

We're moving fast on all fronts. You'll have real ownership from day one.

**What You'll Do**

-

Work directly with senior engineers to build and improve our inference platform - from API design to customer-facing features

-

Talk to ML engineers and AI companies using our platform - synthesise their feedback into product decisions

-

Write specs, user stories, and acceptance criteria for new features

-

Identify friction points in the product through usage data and customer conversations

-

Coordinate between engineering, GTM, and support to ensure smooth feature launches

-

Contribute directly to the product roadmap - your input shapes what gets built next

Based in Berlin or willing to relocate
