### About the Role

Join a lean, high-impact engineering team at a Series A PropTech company building an **AI-native operating system for short-term and holiday rental management** across Europe. This is a central, ownership-heavy role — you'll design and ship agentic AI systems that observe, decide, and act on real-world property operations with humans in the loop. Expect to go deep on the business, modernize legacy systems, and turn messy operational workflows into reliable, autonomous software.

### What You'll Do

-

Own features end-to-end — from requirement gathering through production deployment — with minimal hand-holding.

-

Design and build agentic AI workflows covering guest communication (chat and voice), operational decision-making, and property task management.

-

Modernize legacy systems and redesign data structures to meet evolving product needs.

-

Shadow real operational workflows to surface inefficiencies, hidden steps, and workarounds — then translate them into clean, production-ready software.

-

Scope, ship, and iterate on problems rather than working purely from a ticket queue.

-

Collaborate closely with internal teams to keep product and engineering tightly aligned.

-

Operate with high agency and sound judgment about speed vs. stability trade-offs.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-have:**

-

**TypeScript proficiency** — this is a dealbreaker requirement.

-

Demonstrated ability to own features end-to-end: requirements → production, translating messy workflows into reliable systems.

-

Track record of shipping quickly with sound judgment about what can safely break in a lean startup environment.

-

Familiarity with **React, Express, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and AWS**.

-

Comfort working in an agentic engineering style — running multiple agents and terminals in parallel, using AI coding tools (e.g., Claude Code, Codex).

-

Strong product taste: attention to UX details down to messaging and interaction wording.

-

Ability to self-manage: plan work, communicate progress, and flag blockers proactively.

-

**Fluent English** and strong written/verbal communication skills.

-

Willingness to relocate to Germany and work from the Berlin office.

**Nice to have:**

-

Founder mentality or prior startup founding experience.

-

Experience with Linear and Notion for work tracking and documentation.

-

Background in PropTech, hospitality tech, or operational SaaS.
