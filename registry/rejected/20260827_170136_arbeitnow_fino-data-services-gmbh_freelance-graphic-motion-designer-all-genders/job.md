We are the marketing team at fino data services. We build software for invoice, document and finance processes and work at a high pace.

We are looking for an experienced **freelance Graphic & Motion Designer (all genders)** for an ongoing, project-based collaboration. You will work independently on agreed deliverables and projects and bring your own creative and professional approach to the work.

## Tasks

- **Motion design:** Create explainer videos, product demos, social formats and other moving-image content from concept and storyboard through to the finished cut.

- **Graphic design:** Develop ad creatives, landing-page visuals, sales documents, presentations and partner materials.

- **Visual concepts:** Translate marketing objectives, product information and campaign ideas into clear, compelling visual concepts.

- **Multilingual assets:** Adapt and prepare content in German and English; from Q4 onwards, selected projects may also include Italian and Spanish content, primarily through subtitles and voice-over.

- **AI-assisted production:** Use AI tools where they add genuine value to creative development, design and production, and contribute ideas for efficient new workflows.

- **Independent project delivery:** Plan and execute agreed assignments independently, coordinate deliverables and communicate proactively about progress, dependencies and deadlines.

## Requirements

- **Relevant expertise:** Demonstrated professional expertise in graphic design, motion design, visual communication or a comparable field.

- **Portfolio:** A portfolio or showreel that demonstrates the quality and relevance of your graphic design, motion design and visual storytelling work.

- **Motion design:** Demonstrated ability to create explainer videos, product demos, social content or comparable formats from concept through to final production.

- **Graphic design:** Strong skills in layout, typography, composition and visual hierarchy across digital and presentation formats.

- **Relevant training or equivalent expertise:** Relevant education, vocational training, professional training or equivalent practical expertise in graphic, communication, media or design is welcome.

- **What matters most is demonstrable professional capability and the quality of your work.**

- **AI-assisted production:** Practical experience using AI tools in creative workflows and the ability to demonstrate how they have improved, accelerated or expanded your creative production.

- **Language skills:** Ability to work confidently with German- and English-language source material. German copywriting is not part of the role.

- **Independent project work:** Ability to organize and execute agreed projects and deliverables independently, reliably and professionally.

- **Project flexibility:** Ability to respond professionally to changing project requirements and priorities within agreed assignments.

- **Quality and delivery:** Ability to deliver consistently high-quality work within agreed project timelines.

How we work together:

This is a **freelance collaboration, not an employment position**.

- Projects, deliverables, deadlines and fees are agreed individually.

- You organize your own working hours, location and workflow independently.

- You use your own professional methods, tools and equipment unless otherwise agreed for a specific project.

- We provide the relevant project context, objectives, assets and feedback needed to achieve the agreed deliverables.

- You remain responsible for how you organize and execute the agreed work.

- There is no requirement for permanent availability or fixed working hours.

- Collaboration may take place remotely and, where useful for a project, in direct coordination with our marketing team.

- The scope and duration of individual assignments may vary depending on project requirements.

We are interested in a **long-term professional relationship with a freelance partner** and expect reliability, clear communication and consistently high-quality results.
