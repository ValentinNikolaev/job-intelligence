Hi, we’re AMBOSS and we’re searching for a **Working Student** to support the **Legal** team at AMBOSS!

## About AMBOSS

AMBOSS is the copilot for medical professionals, empowering them to provide the best possible care through our learning and clinical decision support tool. Our team brings together care, innovative thinking, and cutting-edge AI medical technology. Our founders launched AMBOSS in 2012 with the goal of creating a tool they wished they had during medical school and clinical practice. Since then, we've grown to over 600 employees and are active in over 180 countries - primarily in Germany and the US. Our offices are located in Berlin, Cologne, New York, Cagliari and Cape Town.

Want to learn more about our culture? Watch our[ video](https://youtu.be/gTPDn5XpeJg) to explore what makes us unique.

## **You will:**

-

**Review smaller vendor contracts:** check them against our standard criteria, flag anything that needs a closer look from our Legal Counsels, and take the routine cases through to approval yourself

-

**Bring structure to our contract landscape:** classify vendors into our vendor categories, build a clean filing logic, and make sure every agreement ends up where it belongs instead of in someone's inbox

-

**Support our data protection work in close collaboration with our Legal Counsel Privacy & IT: **reviewing DPAs and vendor privacy terms, keeping our records of processing activities up to date, and flagging where personal data is involved

-

**Draft and manage low-level partnership agreements** with student associations, from first draft to signature

-

**Support the team on civil law and IP topics**, from trademark and copyright questions to content usage

-

**Grow into handling simple B2B cases independently **over your first months with us

## **You bring:**

-

**You are enrolled in a law programme in Germany and in your 5th semester **or beyond — we're open on where in Germany you study and are located

-

**German at C2 level and English at C1 **or above: our contracts and counterparties are largely German, our company language is English, and you'll move between both daily

-

**An interest in data protection —** you don't need to be a GDPR expert, but you should be the kind of person who notices when something touches personal data and asks about it

-

**First exposure to or genuine interest in civil law and IP law**, whether from your studies or a previous role

-

**A structured, reliable way of working**: the kind of person who finishes the filing, not just the interesting part of the task

-

**Clear, precise writing — **you'll draft and comment on contracts that other AMBOSSians rely on

## **You enjoy:**

-

You work independently and with ambition, but in close collaboration with a team of three seniors — you don't need someone checking in on you to make progress

-

You pick things up hands-on and want to keep growing beyond the tasks you already know

-

You have good judgement about what you can decide yourself and what needs the owner of a senior team member and you'd rather ask once too often than once too little

-

You're curious about how legal work connects to the commercial side of a business, and you want to see contracts in their real context rather than only in a seminar
