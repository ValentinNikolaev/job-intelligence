### About the Role

We're a fast-moving, mission-driven energy technology company building the infrastructure that powers dynamic electricity plans for European households — and we're looking for a **Product Engineer** to sit at the intersection of customer discovery, end-to-end delivery, and engineering culture.

This is a high-ownership, zero-to-one role on a small team operating in a complex, regulated domain. You'll work directly with customers, shape what gets built, and ship production-grade solutions to real problems in energy retail — billing, procurement, customer retention, and cost optimization. We're an AI-first team and treat AI-native tooling as a core force multiplier, not an afterthought.

We're based in **Berlin (Mitte), Germany**. This role is fully on-site. **Visa sponsorship is not available.**

### What You'll Do

-

Sit with customers to dissect problems and develop product intuition that directly guides engineering decisions.

-

Ship end-to-end solutions across the full stack — from discovery through to production.

-

Use AI-native development tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor) to accelerate output and increase your impact.

-

Own zero-to-one problems such as dunning workflows, customer portal re-engineering, and other core product surfaces.

-

Prioritize ruthlessly — decide what to build, what to skip, and when to kill an initiative.

-

Solve core energy retail challenges: billing, procurement, customer retention, and cost optimization.

-

Shape engineering culture, tooling, and processes as the team grows.

-

Help recruit and raise the engineering bar across the team.

-

Adapt quickly to new tools and methods, and bring the team along to the frontier.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

Demonstrated track record of owning zero-to-one problems from discovery to production — we assess what you've shipped, not just years on a résumé.

-

2+ years of software engineering experience delivering end-to-end, full-stack products to production.

-

Hands-on experience with AI-native codebases and AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor).

**Strong requirements:**

-

Experience solving billing, procurement, customer retention, or cost-optimization problems in an energy retail or similar consumer-facing domain.

-

Ability to ship end-to-end features that measurably reduce costs or improve customer experiences in production.

-

Experience shaping engineering culture, tooling, and processes in a small or early-stage team.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience building or contributing to core products in high-engineering-bar environments or successful open-source projects.

-

Comfort operating with extreme ownership and thriving in ambiguity within a high-growth environment.
