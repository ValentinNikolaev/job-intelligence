# **Your Role**

At LIQID, we’re redefining modern wealth management, making investment solutions previously reserved for ultra-high-net-worth individuals accessible to affluent investors. As an **Intern to Chief of Staff (f/m/d)**, you will work closely with one of our Chiefs of Staff, supporting strategic planning, execution, and internal communication at the highest level.

**Your internship will include:**

-

**Cross-Functional Exposure**: Gain insights into collaboration across teams, high-stakes decision-making, and the operational backbone of a fast-growing fintech.

-

**Leadership Support**: Help structure leadership meetings and ensure that processes run smoothly at every stage.

-

**Operational Enablement**: Work hands-on with teams on operational tasks, from coordinating workflows to unblocking day-to-day execution challenges.

-

**Strategic Initiatives**: Drive forward key projects, contributing to planning, coordination, and implementation.

-

**Research & Analysis**: Conduct research, prepare analyses, and translate findings into actionable recommendations.

-

**Hands-On Learning**: Experience firsthand how strategy turns into execution in a company managing over €3 billion in assets.

# **What you bring:**

-

**Education**: You hold a Bachelor’s degree in Management, Economics, Information Systems, Industrial Engineering, or a related field, and are currently either in a gap year or enrolled in a Master’s program.

-

**Practical Experience**: You have already gained hands-on experience through a working student position or internship in a fast-paced environment, for example at a tech startup, scale-up, or consulting firm.

-

**Mindset**: You think analytically, work independently, and enjoy bringing structure to complex topics.

-

**Communication Skills**: You communicate clearly and confidently – in writing, in presentations, and in person.

-

**Tool Affinity**: You are comfortable with tools such as Google Sheets, PowerPoint, Notion, Slack, etc., and quickly pick up new systems.

-

**Motivation**: You are proactive, hands-on, and curious to learn how leadership operates in a high-growth fintech company.
