**What you'll do**

-

Own Avionics Assembly, Integration, and Test (AIT) delivery, ensuring avionics hardware is built, integrated, and verified correctly from component level through vehicle integration.

-

Develop avionics assembly, integration, and test procedures directly from requirements and engineering drawings.

-

Translate design intent into clear, executable build instructions, defining tooling, torque specifications, parts, consumables, work sequences, and verification steps.

-

Define and maintain avionics wiring, harness, and pinout diagrams, ensuring correct electrical interfaces and configuration control.

-

Develop and own avionics assembly procedures for components, sub‑assemblies, and vehicle‑level integration.

-

Create and execute avionics test procedures, validating functionality, interfaces, and readiness prior to higher‑level integration.

-

Ensure avionics AIT activities meet quality, reliability, and safety standards, suitable for aerospace and defence environments.

-

Work closely with avionics design, systems, and vehicle AIT teams to resolve integration issues and close design‑for‑integration gaps.

-

Identify and ensure availability of tools, fixtures, consumables, and test equipment required to support build and integration activities.

-

Support production of consistent, repeatable avionics builds by enforcing process discipline and configuration control.

-

Own avionics‑related non‑conformance identification, documentation, and resolution, feeding lessons learned back into design and process updates.

-

Enable smooth progression from bench‑level avionics testing to vehicle‑level integration

**Who you are**

-

An Avionics AIT Manager or Senior Avionics Integration Engineer with hands‑on experience in electronics or electrical assembly environments.

-

Background in aerospace avionics manufacturing and assembly is preferred, with automotive electronics experience also acceptable.

-

Experienced developing avionics wiring diagrams, harness definitions, and pinouts.

-

Comfortable creating and executing avionics assembly and test procedures from engineering drawings and requirements.

-

Strong understanding of electronic hardware build processes, tooling, torque control, and workmanship standards.

-

Detail‑oriented with a strong instinct for process discipline, quality, and repeatability.

-

Comfortable working across disciplines, coordinating with design, systems, and vehicle integration teams.

-

Hands‑on and pragmatic, able to support build and testing activities directly when required.

-

Confident managing multiple avionics workstreams and maintaining configuration control under schedule pressure.

-

Motivated by taking complex avionics systems from drawings to integrated, verified, flight‑ready hardware.
