### Role OverviewWe are looking for an experienced Senior .NET Engineer to join a strategic project focused on the customization and enhancement of the a SaaS solution built on Microsoft Dynamics 365, for a large enterprise customer.
In this role, you will lead the design and delivery of scalable customizations, support new product development, and drive alignment with a unified enterprise data model, while ensuring best practices across architecture, development, and quality.

### Key Responsibilities

- design and implementation of customizations on Microsoft Dynamics 365 (CE/Dataverse)

- Own solution architecture, ensuring scalability, maintainability, and alignment with enterprise standards

- Drive new product creation and feature development within SaaS platform (customer version + inner sourcing and to core version contribution)

- Oversee process automation initiatives (workflows, integrations, business rules)

- Ensure data model alignment with enterprise-wide data standards

- Guide and mentor developers; perform code reviews and enforce best practices

- Collaborate with stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical solutions

- Support integration with external systems and services

- Participate in knowledge transfer and contribute to documentation and governance processes

### Technology StackThe role requires hands-on expertise in technologies commonly used for Microsoft Dynamics 365 customization, including:

- Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Customer Engagement) / Dataverse

- C# / .NET (Plugins, Custom Workflow Activities)

- JavaScript / TypeScript (Client-side customizations, form scripting)

- Power Platform:

- Power Apps (model-driven and canvas apps)

- Power Automate (flows)

- REST APIs / Web API integrations

- Azure services (e.g., Azure Functions, Logic Apps – nice to have)

- SQL / Dataverse data modeling

- ALM tools (Azure DevOps, Git, CI/CD pipelines)

### Required Experience

- 7+ years in software engineering, with strong focus on Dynamics 365 / CRM development

- Proven experience as a Technical Lead or Senior Engineer in enterprise environments

- Strong understanding of Dynamics 365 architecture, extensibility, and limitations

- Experience designing scalable data models and integrations

- Hands-on experience with automation and business process optimization

- Ability to lead distributed teams and collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders

### Nice to Have

- Experience with large-scale SaaS platform customization

- Exposure to enterprise data model transformations / harmonization initiatives

- Knowledge of DevOps practices in Dynamics environments

- Experience working in phased transitions / knowledge transfer setups

### What You’ll Be Working On

- Building a scalable, future-ready solution aligned with enterprise data strategy

- Supporting transition to internal ownership and sustainable delivery model
