Role Purpose

Ensure the consistent quality of marketing assets, web services, and product marketing data through manual testing, implementation of automated quality checks, and the use of AI-powered tools to minimize defects, improve user experience reliability, and ensure data accuracy for business decision-making.
Responsibilities:

- Perform functional and regression testing of marketing assets, including landing pages, promotional pages, banners, email campaigns, and other user-facing content across different devices and browsers.

- Validate the accuracy of product marketing analytics, including event tracking, conversion funnels, UTM parameters, and other tracking mechanisms.

- Test marketing web services and integrations, including payment flows, subscriptions, CRM systems, email platforms, A/B testing tools, and related services.

- Create and maintain automated checks for recurring QA tasks, such as landing page validation, tracking verification, link validation, pricing and user account checks, localization validation, and other repetitive test scenarios.

- Leverage AI tools to generate test cases, create test data, validate content, analyze testing results, and optimize QA processes.

- Coordinate the localization process for marketing materials and perform quality assurance of localized content.

- Create and maintain QA documentation, including test cases, checklists, and bug reports.

- Collect QA metrics, analyze testing results, and prepare reports for the Product Marketing Lead.

- Collaborate closely with Marketing, Analytics, Engineering, and Design teams to ensure high-quality product and marketing releases.

- Partner with the Mobile Apps team to ensure a seamless and reliable user experience while minimizing the risk of data loss during cross-platform user journeys.

- Contribute to the continuous improvement of QA processes by proposing new automation approaches and testing best practices.

Requirements:

- Experience. 1–2+ years of experience in Manual QA, with hands-on experience testing web applications (frontend/backend), marketing websites, or web services.

- Web Testing. Solid understanding of functional, regression, and cross-browser testing, with experience using browser DevTools and analyzing network requests.

- Analytics & Tracking. Basic understanding of web analytics (Google Analytics, conversion tracking, UTM parameters) or the ability to quickly learn these tools.

- Test Automation. Basic hands-on experience or a strong interest in test automation using Playwright, Cypress, or similar frameworks, preferably with JavaScript.

- AI Literacy. Experience using two or more AI tools to create test scenarios, analyze testing results, and improve personal productivity.

- QA Documentation. Experience creating test cases, checklists, and detailed bug reports.

- Analytical Thinking. Strong attention to detail, problem-solving skills, and the ability to identify root causes and work effectively with data.

- English. Intermediate level or higher.

- Data Skills. Proficiency in Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel for analyzing testing results, maintaining checklists, and preparing basic reports.

- Development Processes. Understanding of Agile methodologies and the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
