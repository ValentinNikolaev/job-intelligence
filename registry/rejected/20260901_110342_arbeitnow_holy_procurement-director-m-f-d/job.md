**About the Position**

As Procurement Director, you will build one of HOLY's most important competitive advantages: a world-class supplier ecosystem that enables us to scale faster, improve margins and consistently deliver outstanding products to our customers.

Procurement at HOLY is far more than buying ingredients. You will own our sourcing strategy across ingredients, packaging, merchandising and co-manufacturing while continuously improving cost, quality, supplier performance, flexibility and speed.

This is a highly strategic leadership position reporting directly to our COO, Markus. You will work closely with Product Development, Supply Chain Planning, Operations, Finance and our founders to build supplier partnerships capable of supporting HOLY's ambitious growth journey toward becoming a global consumer brand.

We are not necessarily looking for a traditional procurement leader. We are looking for an exceptional builder with outstanding commercial judgment who enjoys solving complex business problems and creating long-term competitive advantages.

If you combine strategic thinking with a hands-on mindset, thrive in fast-paced environments and enjoy building things from scratch, this role is for you.

**Responsibilities: **

**Strategic procurement
**

- Own HOLY's sourcing strategy across ingredients, packaging, merchandising and co-manufacturing
- Develop category strategies that improve competitiveness, resilience and long-term scalability
- Drive structural COGS improvements through strategic sourcing, commercial excellence and value engineering
- Identify new sourcing opportunities, technologies and supplier capabilities to strengthen HOLY's competitive position
- Build long-term strategic partnerships with HOLY's key suppliers
- Develop suppliers across capacity, quality, innovation, flexibility and operational excellence
- Ensure our suppliers scale alongside HOLY's ambitious growth plans

**Supply Chain & Operational Excellence
**

- Improve end-to-end supply chain performance by reducing lead times, increasing supplier responsiveness and improving planning reliability
- Partner closely with Supply Chain Planning and Operations to align supplier capabilities with demand forecasts, inventory targets and growth plans
- Improve working capital through stronger commercial terms, better supplier collaboration and optimized replenishment models
- Support the development of a scalable and resilient manufacturing network
**Supplier Performance & Quality
**

- Own supplier performance across quality, OTIF, responsiveness, compliance and continuous improvement
- Establish structured Supplier Business Reviews, measurable KPIs and clear accountability with strategic suppliers
- Proactively identify and mitigate supply risks before they impact the business
- Ensure suppliers consistently meet HOLY's quality, food safety and regulatory standards
**Leadership & Organization Building

- Lead and develop HOLY's Strategic Procurement and Operational Buying teams
- Build a high-performance organization with strong commercial capabilities, clear ownership and a continuous improvement mindset
- Develop future leaders and continuously raise the maturity of HOLY's procurement function
