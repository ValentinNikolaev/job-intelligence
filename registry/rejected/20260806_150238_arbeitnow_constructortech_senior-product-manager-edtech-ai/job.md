## Our mission

Constructor’s mission is to enable all educational organisations to provide high-quality digital education to 10x people with 10x efficiency.

With strong expertise in machine intelligence and data science, Constructor’s all-in-one platform for education and research addresses today’s pressing educational challenges: access inequality, tech clutter, and low engagement of students.

### **Please send your resume in English only.**

### **Duties and Responsibilities:**

- **Strategic Roadmap Ownership:** Define and drive a multi-year, outcome-based roadmap that aligns with global business goals and North Star metrics.

- **Market Intelligence:** Lead comprehensive market research to identify emerging trends in AI and EdTech, ensuring our product strategy remains ahead of the curve.

- **Monetization & Pricing:** Own the pricing strategy for international markets. Conduct analysis to determine price floors and ceilings, and optimize monetization models to maximize LTV.

- **Scientific & Data Leadership:** Apply a scientific approach to product discovery. Analyze performance data to validate hypotheses and drive data-informed decision-making across the organization.

- **Stakeholder Management:** Act as a key partner for the Leadership Team, delivering clear, data-backed insights and recommendations that influence company-wide strategy.

- **Team Mentorship:** Lead, mentor, and scale a team of product managers, fostering a culture of accountability, growth, and high-quality PRD standards.

- **Cross-functional Collaboration:** Serve as the primary point of contact for Engineering, Data Science, and Design teams to prioritize features and resolve technical/business trade-offs.

### **Qualifications and Experience:**

- **5+ years of experience** as a Product Manager in software development (SaaS, EdTech, or AI-driven products).

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical/scientific field.

- Proven ability to manage products using complex data analysis and scientific research methodologies.

- Expert knowledge of prioritization frameworks (e.g., RICE, WSJF) and experience building outcome-driven (not just feature-driven) roadmaps.

- Strong track record of writing high-quality PRDs and managing sprints in a fast-paced, agile environment.

- Experience leading cross-functional stakeholders and directly mentoring other product managers.

- Fluent English with the ability to articulate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders clearly.

### **Nice to Have:**

- Academic background in Business (MBA/BBA) or completion of advanced product strategy programs (e.g., Reforge, Kellogg, or similar).

- Experience in building and scaling **AI/ML-powered products**.

- Background in **Science** or experience with academic/research-focused software.

- Experience managing products with **millions of users** across multiple geographical regions.
