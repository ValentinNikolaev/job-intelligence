## **Your Mission**

As an **Operations & Strategy Intern**, you’ll become part of our core Ops team - solving real problems across private market investing. You’ll take ownership in one of four focus areas depending on your strengths and interests, and support process execution, improvement, and scale. While track placement will reflect business needs, you’ll remain part of the broader Ops team with opportunities to explore other areas during your time with us. This is your opportunity to learn fast, work hard, and make your mark.

# **Focus Areas**

**Investor Due Diligence
**Help us maintain trust with our clients by supporting robust investor onboarding and compliance processes. You’ll work within clearly structured workflows (like KYC/AML checks), flag inconsistencies, and keep our controls running smoothly. Ideal for detail-oriented individuals with a calm, structured working style - and the ability to speak up when it matters.

➡️ Previous experience in banking, law, or regulatory settings is a plus.

**Where this can take you**: Careers in compliance, risk operations or legal/finance operations.

**Fund Administration
**Support our fund onboarding process by coordinating investor support, client onboarding, document preparation, and troubleshooting operational issues as funds transition onto our platform. You’ll liaise with internal and external stakeholders, map fund structures, and help ensure a smooth and scalable onboarding experience. . This focus is ideal for structured, proactive problem-solvers who enjoy bringing clarity to complexity and improving systems sustainably.

➡️ A background in VC, high-growth startups or consulting is a plus.

**Where this can take you**: Careers in fund administration or client and investor operations.

**Venture Operations
**Get a front-row seat to how unregulated investment vehicles are executed on our platform. You’ll manage investor onboarding, investment documentation, capital calls, and more - helping us scale deal execution with quality and speed across jurisdictions, focusing on process efficiency and end-to-end automation across jurisdictions. This is for you if you’re a fast learner, clear communicator, and want to work at the intersection of product and operations.

➡️ A background in VC, high-growth startups or consulting is a plus.

**Where this can take you**: Careers in venture capital operations, business development or project management.

**Fund Accounting
**Take ownership of investor reporting cycles, a regulatory requirement for funds in each of our jurisdictions. You’ll work with large datasets, external accountants and our product team to help ensure that investors receive accurate, timely, and clear reports. Perfect for structured and detail-driven minds who can turn complex data into meaningful, compliant communications.

➡️ Strong Excel skills and a background in finance or or accounting are a plus.

**Where this can take you**: Careers in fund accounting, financial reporting or finance operations.
