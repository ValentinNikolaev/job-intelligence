### About the Role

A fast-growing Berlin-based B2B performance marketing agency is looking for a creative, self-driven **Werkstudent Content & Brand (m/w/d)** to own its entire brand presence from the ground up. This is a real ownership role — not an assistant position. You'll shape how the company shows up across LinkedIn, video, design, and every sales and marketing touchpoint that matters.

You'll work **16–20 hours per week** on a Werkstudent basis, ideally for **12+ months**, on-site in **Berlin (Mitte)**.

### What You'll Do

-

Write compelling LinkedIn content for company founders and the company account — hooks, storylines, carousels — calibrated to the tone a B2B niche actually responds to.

-

Produce image and video content end-to-end: concept, shoot, edit, design. Think Reels, talking heads, customer testimonials, and behind-the-scenes.

-

Own brand consistency across all channels: website, pitch decks, sales materials, and templates — everything should feel cohesive.

-

Build and maintain a content calendar and keep publishing cadence consistent — shipping beats perfecting.

-

Track performance data, identify winning formats, and scale what resonates.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves (dealbreakers):**

-

Currently enrolled student with 16–20 hours/week available, ideally for 12+ months.

-

Proficiency in video editing tools (CapCut, Premiere, or equivalent) — concept to final delivery.

-

Proficiency in design/graphics tools (Figma, Canva, or equivalent) for visual assets, pitch decks, and sales materials.

-

Demonstrated experience producing LinkedIn content (posts, carousels, hooks) with an understanding of what performs on the platform.

**Strongly preferred:**

-

Experience writing marketing and brand content for B2B or SaaS audiences.

-

Experience managing content calendars and executing consistent publishing schedules.

-

Experience analyzing content performance metrics and using data to inform strategy.

-

Experience producing video content end-to-end, including filming, editing, and post-production.

-

Experience building brand consistency across multiple touchpoints (website, decks, templates, marketing materials).

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience with photography, lighting, and on-location content production.

-

Exposure to GTM, sales enablement, or marketing for B2B software/SaaS companies.

-

A portfolio or examples of personal content creation projects or published work.

**You're a great fit if you:**

-

Move independently — you generate ideas, execute them, and seek feedback proactively.

-

Want to build a brand from scratch rather than iterate within a corporate process.

-

Live on LinkedIn and genuinely understand what makes content land.

-

Have a portfolio or samples ready to share.
