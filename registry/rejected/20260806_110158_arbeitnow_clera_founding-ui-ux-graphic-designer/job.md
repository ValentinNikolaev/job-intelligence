### About the Role

A Y Combinator–backed consumer wearable startup is hiring a **Founding UI/UX & Graphic Designer** to own its entire visual presence. This is a rare, foundational opportunity: you will define how users experience a premium AI-powered wearable brand across every digital touchpoint — from the iOS app to the website, social channels, and beyond.

The company is building a wearable device that reads and analyzes real-time emotional states, helping users develop their emotional intelligence and consistently reach peak flow. The founding team brings deep experience from world-class consumer hardware backgrounds. Prototypes are already on users' wrists. This is a ground-floor role with real ownership and a direct path to leading the design org as the company scales.

### What You'll Do

-

Own end-to-end UI/UX design for the iOS app.

-

Lead design across the website, social channels, marketing communications, and email campaigns.

-

Create event invitations, launch collateral, and campaign assets that embody the brand.

-

Define and maintain a unified, premium consumer design language across all touchpoints.

### What We're Looking For

-

**Outstanding portfolio** — this is the single most important factor. It must demonstrate exceptional taste and strong graphic design fundamentals.

-

Experience delivering polished, user-facing design at a consumer company, or equivalent training in a rigorous design environment (e.g. top design school graduate).

-

Exceptional aesthetic sensibility suited to a premium consumer hardware brand.

-

Ability to independently own a broad consumer design stack spanning mobile UI/UX, web, and marketing collateral.

-

Proficiency with Figma and standard brand/graphic design tooling.

-

Comfortable working on-site in London (or San Francisco).

**Nice to have:** Background at a consumer company known for tasteful design; education from a top design school (e.g. RCA, RISD, or equivalent).
