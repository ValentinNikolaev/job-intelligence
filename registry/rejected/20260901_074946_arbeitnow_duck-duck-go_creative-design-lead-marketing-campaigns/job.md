# **Your Team and Role**

Working on the Design Functional Team, you'll shape marketing creative for DuckDuckGo's growing portfolio spanning Duck.ai, search, browsers, and subscriptions, using tools like Figma and the Adobe Creative Suite, and work on related projects. Recent projects include:

-

Design and lead creative campaigns introducing App Tracking Protection to help people block hidden trackers on Android

-

Design marketing creative supporting the launch and growth of Duck.ai, our free, private way to chat with multiple AI models

As a Creative Design Lead (Senior Designer II) you'll be a hands-on creative force and idea generator, taking full ownership of design work from concept through execution, across multiple concurrent campaigns. You'll drive high-craft, on-brand creative for Duck.ai, search, browsers and subscriptions with minimal oversight, while advising fellow designers on marketing and brand execution. You'll navigate cross-functional stakeholders during scoping and ideation, evolve our brand language with fresh creative concepts, and help build reusable systems that make marketing production more efficient, growing into a go-to advisor for brand and marketing design across the team.
