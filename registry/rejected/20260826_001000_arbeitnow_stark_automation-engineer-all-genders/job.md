**Your mission**

As Automation Engineer, you build and deploy automations that eliminate manual effort across operations and the back-office. You work across RPA, LLM-based tooling, and API integrations — and you are as likely to be automating an invoice approval chain as a production line data feed. You measure success in hours saved and errors eliminated.

**Responsibilities**

-

Design and build RPA workflows for operational and back-office processes — UiPath, Power Automate, or equivalent

-

Develop LLM-based automation tools for document-heavy workflows: invoice extraction, quality certificates, order processing, email triage

-

Integrate automation solutions with ERP, MES, and business applications via APIs

-

Build agent-based workflows where multi-step reasoning or decision-making is required

-

Test, deploy, and maintain automations in production — including monitoring, alerting, and incident response

-

Document automation logic, dependencies, and maintenance procedures thoroughly

-

Work with operational and back-office teams to identify, scope, and prioritise new automation opportunities

3–6 years building automations in an operational or business process context

-

Has shipped working automations into production — not just proof-of-concept or demo environments

-

RPA tools — UiPath, Power Automate, or equivalent (hands-on production experience)

-

Python scripting — for custom automation logic, API integration, and LLM tool use

-

API integration — REST APIs, webhooks, data exchange across ERP and business systems

-

Process analysis and QA mindset — automations must be reliable, not just functional

-

BSc in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or similar
