**About the Role at RobCo**

RobCo is building the category of Autonomous Industrial Robotics and we're doing it fast. We believe that automating the ordinary frees humans to do the extraordinary, and we're building the end-to-end robotics infrastructure to make that possible - hardware, software, and AI in one stack that just works.

As our Regional Director of Enterprise Sales, you act as the primary catalyst for RobCo's enterprise growth across the German market, driving large-scale industrial adoption of our Autonomous Manufacturing Platform. You will lead high-impact sales strategies, build strategic relationships with executive stakeholders in key industrial sectors, and scale our enterprise footprint from the ground up.

**Your Responsibilities**

-

Drive Regional Revenue: Lead and execute the German enterprise sales strategy to achieve and exceed ambitious revenue and market-penetration targets.

-

Build Executive Relationships: Target, engage, and secure long-term partnerships with C-level executives and key decision-makers across mid-market and large-scale manufacturing and industrial enterprises.

-

Champion Technical Solutions: Direct complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise sales cycles by clearly communicating the strategic, financial, and technical ROI of RobCo's modular robotics and physical AI solutions.

-

Scale Enterprise Footprint: Identify and unlock new market verticals and geographic opportunities across the DACH region to continually expand the sales pipeline.

-

Partner Cross-Functionally: Collaborate closely with Solution Engineering and Product teams to align client requirements with product capabilities and deliver seamless rollouts.

-

Forecast with Accuracy: Maintain meticulous pipeline hygiene, actionable sales reporting, and highly accurate quarterly/annual revenue forecasts in Salesforce.

-

Represent the Brand: Position RobCo as the premier AI-driven automation provider through representation at key industry events, panel discussions, and executive roundtables.

-

Team Leadership & Enablement: Lead, mentor, and coach a high-performing team of Senior Account Executives, driving quota attainment, pipeline growth, and continuous performance management.

**About RobCo**

Founded in Munich in 2020, RobCo leads the transition toward fully Autonomous Industrial Robotics. Our mission is to deploy RobCo in every factory - solving the industrial labor shortage through end-to-end robotics that just works. Powered by Physical AI, our solutions combine a scalable modular hardware architecture with intelligent software that adapts to your use case, improves over time, and requires zero upfront investment through our Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) model.

We solve our customers' most pressing pain points - labor shortage, cost pressure, workplace safety, and national reshoring - across the full spectrum of Autonomous Industrial Robotics. With $160M raised from investors including Lightspeed, Lingotto Innovation, and Sequoia, 200+ people globally, and 2.5M+ operating hours in the field, we are deploying robots in factories across Europe and the United States - with our US operations headquartered in Austin, TX, and our RobCo Bay Area Demo Lab in San Francisco.
