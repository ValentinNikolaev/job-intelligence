At **CesiumAstro**, we are developers and pioneers of out-of-the-box communication systems for satellites, UAVs, launch vehicles, and other space and airborne platforms. We take pride in our dynamic and cross-functional work environment, which allows us to learn, develop, and engage across our organization. If you are looking for hands-on, interactive, and autonomous work, CesiumAstro is the place for you. We are actively seeking passionate, collaborative, energetic, and forward-thinking individuals to join our team.

We are looking to add a **Talent Acquisition Manager** to our team. If you enjoy working in a dynamic, rapidly changing environment and are passionate about technology and helping a company scale, we would like to hear from you.

In this position, you will lead full-cycle recruiting efforts while managing and developing a team of recruiters. This role is both strategic and hands-on, partnering closely with business leaders to execute hiring plans, support workforce planning, and deliver high-quality hiring outcomes across technical and business functions.

**JOB DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

- Manage, coach, and develop a team of Talent Acquisition professionals to drive consistent hiring results.

- Set clear goals, track performance, and provide ongoing feedback and development support.

- Oversee requisition allocation, workload balance, and day-to-day team execution.

- Support onboarding and growth of new team members.

- Lead end-to-end recruiting for complex, high-priority, and leadership roles.

- Partner with hiring managers to define role requirements, success profiles, and hiring strategies.

- Ensure a high-quality, consistent candidate experience throughout the hiring lifecycle.

- Drive structured interview processes, candidate evaluation, and offer execution.

- Collaborate with hiring managers, HR, and leadership to align on hiring priorities.

- Support workforce planning, including headcount tracking and hiring forecasts.

- Provide market insights on talent availability and competitive landscape.

- Build strong relationships with hiring managers, executives, and cross-functional partners.

- Communicate regularly on hiring progress, challenges, and opportunities.

- Influence stakeholders and guide decision-making to improve hiring outcomes.

**JOB REQUIREMENTS AND MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS**

- Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, or related field (or equivalent experience).

- 7–9 years of experience in Talent Acquisition, including full-cycle recruiting.

- 1–3 years of experience managing or leading recruiting teams.

- Experience hiring for technical roles, preferably within aerospace, defense, or engineering-driven environments.

- Strong understanding of recruiting best practices and hiring strategies for complex roles.

- Expertise with ATS platforms (e.g., Greenhouse, Lever, Workday) and sourcing tools (LinkedIn Recruiter, SeekOut).

- Experience leveraging data and metrics to drive decisions and improve recruiting performance.

- Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines in a fast-paced environment.

- Strong analytical thinking and decision-making capabilities.
