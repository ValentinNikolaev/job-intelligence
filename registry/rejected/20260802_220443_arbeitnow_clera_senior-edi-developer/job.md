### About the Role

We are looking for a **Senior EDI Developer** to join a US-based IT services and technology consultancy on a **10-month W-2 contract** engagement. In this fully remote role, you will perform solution-level technical analysis, design, and implementation of EDI integrations, while leading the day-to-day technical execution of integration and API projects across globally distributed teams.

**Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.** Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship.

-

Translate solution designs into production-ready EDI integration implementations.

-

Ensure code quality and adherence to development standards across all deliverables.

-

Lead technical work streams and provide hands-on technical guidance to the broader team.

-

Mentor junior developers and conduct thorough code reviews.

-

Collaborate effectively with globally distributed teams in a remote-first environment.

-

Troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues in a timely and methodical manner.

### What We're Looking For

**Required — Dealbreakers:**

-

**8–10 years of experience** with IBM Sterling Integrator (v5.2.6 or above).

-

Proven EDI development, maintenance, support, and monitoring experience.

-

Mastery of mapping formats: **X12, EDIFACT, XML, cXML, CSV, JSON, and Flat Files**.

-

Experience with communication protocols: **FTP, SFTP, AS2, and VAN**.

-

Proficiency with **DB2, SQL, XML, XSLT, Web Services, and REST APIs**.

-

Solid understanding of EDI architecture including encryption, authentication, monitoring, scalability, and availability.

-

Must be authorized to work in the United States without visa sponsorship.

**Also Required:**

-

Ability to lead technical work streams, mentor junior developers, and conduct code reviews.

-

Experience collaborating with globally distributed teams and providing technical leadership.

-

Excellent communication and problem-solving skills.

-

Availability for a W-2 contract engagement (not C2C or 1099).
