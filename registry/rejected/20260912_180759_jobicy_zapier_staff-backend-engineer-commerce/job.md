### Hi there!

Zapier's mission is to make automation work for everyone. That mission is funded by revenue — and every dollar of it flows through our Commerce Zone. We build and operate the infrastructure behind billing, subscriptions, payments, pricing, entitlements, and checkout across everything Zapier ships: self-serve plans, enterprise contracts, and ecosystem offerings. When a customer upgrades, renews, changes plans, or pays an invoice, our systems make it happen — correctly, instantly, and at scale.

We're hiring a **Staff Engineer** to own the technical vision and long-term architecture for the Commerce Zone. You'll define the pricing primitives, service boundaries, and data models that let Zapier ship pricing and packaging changes in days instead of months — while keeping every dollar accounted for accurately. That means designing the framework and abstractions product teams plug into so they can launch new SKUs without becoming billing experts, building configuration-driven infrastructure for pricing experimentation, and ensuring the platform evolves without foundational rewrites.

This is production-critical, finance-grade infrastructure where correctness is non-negotiable. You'll drive alignment across teams in a space defined by high ambiguity and competing constraints, working closely with Product, Engineering, Finance, and Sales. The role is particularly exciting because decisions here ripple across Zapier — shaping how the company monetizes its next phase of growth.

### **Things You’ll Do**

-

**Serve as a technical leader within the Commerce Zone:** You’ll operate as part of the zone’s leadership team, partnering closely with Engineering, Product, and Design to shape strategy and roadmap decisions. You’ll translate business context into technical direction—and ensure engineering tradeoffs are clearly understood at the business level.

-

**Shape the long-term platform vision:** You’ll define and evolve the architectural direction of the Commerce Zone, balancing near-term delivery with long-term durability, extensibility, and operational excellence.

-

**Lead high-impact technical initiatives:** You’ll drive the strategic evolution of our monetization platform—decoupling billing from account systems, clarifying system boundaries, and consolidating pricing infrastructure. You’ll build flexible, configurable foundations that enable fast Pricing and Packaging experimentation and seamless launch moments across the customer lifecycle, while supporting clean, reliable data for Finance and Pricing & Packaging.

-

**Own and strengthen revenue-critical systems:** You’ll be responsible for systems across the Commerce Zone—Billing, Pricing, Payments, Checkout, and Entitlements—ensuring they are resilient, observable, and correct. You’ll raise the bar on proactive detection, safe rollback mechanisms, and revenue accuracy—catching issues before customers do. You’ll systematically reduce structural and technical debt, eliminate recurring operational pain points, and strengthen architectural foundations to prevent revenue-impacting incidents before they emerge.

-

**Guide high-impact technical decisions:** You’ll influence API design, data modeling, caching strategies, and system architecture—optimizing for scalability, developer experience, and rapid pricing and packaging iteration.

-

**Partner deeply across Zapier:** You’ll collaborate closely with Pricing & Packaging, Product, and Design teams to drive ARR growth and craft seamless, intuitive, and lovable monetization journeys for self-serve, enterprise, and ecosystem customers. You’ll also work with Support and Operations to ensure robust admin tooling and exceptional customer experiences across upgrades, billing changes, and subscription management.

-

**Drive measurable improvements:** You’ll identify performance bottlenecks and deliver optimizations that show up in the metrics—reduced latency, improved reliability, stronger customer experiences, and improved conversion and retention.

-

**Mentor engineers and raise the technical bar:** You’ll share patterns and tooling, conduct thoughtful code reviews, and grow engineering capability across the zone. You’ll be a go-to resource for engineers tackling complex monetization and platform challenges.

-

**Champion documentation and developer experience:** You’ll ensure our systems are well-documented and easy to adopt, reducing friction and accelerating development across the organization.
