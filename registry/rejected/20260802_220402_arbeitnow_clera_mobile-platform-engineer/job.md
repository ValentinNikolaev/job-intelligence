### About the Role

Join the Foundation team at a fast-growing **Series A/B EdTech platform** serving tens of millions of students worldwide. This team builds the technical base that product teams rely on — from the Flutter mobile app and core backend services to developer experience and AI infrastructure.

As a **Mobile Platform Engineer**, you will own the core of the Flutter app: shared components, architecture, and performance. You'll shape the standards that keep the wider engineering organisation productive. This is a high-leverage role — the systems you maintain determine how confidently other teams can ship features.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the core Flutter app, including shared components, the design system, performance monitoring, and tooling.

-

Define how features are built by establishing patterns for state management and backend integration, and guide product teams in applying them.

-

Improve the end-to-end developer workflow — from CI/CD and store distribution to internal tooling — removing recurring friction that slows other teams down.

-

Integrate AI tooling into the codebase and workflows, and help determine its role in the product over time.

-

Keep the existing foundation healthy and dependable so product teams can build on it with confidence.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Several years of experience delivering production software at scale, ideally including responsibility for the architecture of a mobile application.

-

Strong, multi-year hands-on experience building mobile apps with **Flutter and Dart**.

-

Solid mobile DevOps experience: CI/CD pipelines, app store distribution, and release management.

-

A genuine interest in AI as an engineering surface — curiosity matters more than a formal background.

-

A systems-thinker mindset: you reason clearly about trade-offs, communicate effectively across teams, and take satisfaction in foundational work whether or not your name is attached to a shipped feature.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

Work in a collaborative, mission-driven engineering team in a scaling EdTech company.
