**What you'll do**

- **Own the CRM roadmap:** Define and execute the CRM strategy for LeasingMarkt, aligning CRM initiatives with business objectives and customer lifecycle priorities.

- **Own and optimize CRM engagement:** Manage the end-to-end delivery of multi-channel CRM communications across Email, WhatsApp, and App Push. Continuously improve engagement, conversion, and retention by optimizing campaigns, customer journeys, and lifecycle communications.

- **Lead the Salesforce Marketing Cloud → Iterable migration:** Own the migration from planning through implementation, journey design, QA, testing, launch, and post-migration optimization, while ensuring business continuity.

- **Drive CRM innovation:** Identify and champion new opportunities in automation, AI, and personalization to continuously enhance the customer experience and scale CRM performance.

- **Measure and improve performance:** Monitor CRM health through dashboards and KPIs, analyze campaign performance, run A/B tests, and translate insights into actionable improvements.

- **Collaborate across teams:** Partner closely with Product Managers, Engineers, Data Analysts, and Marketing stakeholders to deliver CRM initiatives and continuously improve the customer experience.
