#### **Your Impact**

As Head of Product – Agentic Offerings, you will lead the product and design vision for enabling next-generation commerce experiences powered by AI agents. This role is central to our strategy to enable our enterprise customers to be and win where their customers are in the Age of AI and to leverage agents within their commerce ecosystem.

You will drive the creation of the **AI Hub**, which powers Discoverability-> Discovery and Conversion on the leading GenAI Channels and the **AgentSPHERE** recently announced which is where you can manage these agents, their access and a potential future marketplace, including enterprise governance, authentication, and secure agent orchestration. You will be a key member of the leadership team, working closely with the CPO, Engineering VP and other leaders.

**Your Mission:**

- **Drive the product strategy **for the Agentic Offerings Domain; **AI Hub: focusing on Discoverability and conversion** and and **AgentSPHERE; **focusing on Agent governance and control leading to the creation of an Agent Marketplace

- **Lead a multidisciplinary team** to build commerce-native tools and intuitive management interfaces that allow merchants to configure and secure their agentic ecosystems.

- **Serve as a key advocate** for enterprise-grade governance, ensuring every AI agent interaction is authenticated, auditable, and controlled.

- **Partner with Engineering and cross-functional peers** to evolve the AgentSPHERE into a comprehensive gate for agents while optimizing AI Hub for multi-channel discovery (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot).

- **Ability to balance vision with execution**, leading teams to deliver iterative business value rapidly while LLM specifications and the Agentic Ecosystem continue to evolve.

- **Define and evolve the agentic narrative** in collaboration with Product Marketing, ensuring our role as the definitive bridge between commerce engines and LLMs is understood.

- **Hire, lead, and mentor as needed** PMs and Designers, fostering a small team that can navigate the high ambiguity and rapid pace of the AI landscape.

- **Be a thought leader**, anticipating trends in Agentic AI, Model Context Protocol (MCP) standards, and the evolution of LLM-based shopping.
