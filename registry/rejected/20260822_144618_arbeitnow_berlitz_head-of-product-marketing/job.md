We're building a new global Berlitz, and this is your opportunity to help shape it. Nearly 150 years of expertise, meeting the ambition and technology to power a new era of learning.

If you are the kind of person who thrives in a fast-paced environment, isn’t afraid of a blank page, and is excited to help shape the future of a global company undergoing a significant transformation, **you might belong at Berlitz!**

Berlitz is transforming how the world learns languages, moving from a legacy education brand into a modern, digital-first B2B platform. As we roll out the Berlitz App, Compass, and a new credit-based commercial model, we need a **Head of Product Marketing** to turn that transformation into a story buyers actually believe, and a go-to-market engine that delivers it. Reporting to the CPTO, you'll own positioning, messaging, and enablement for the business, and be the direct link between market reality and our product roadmap.

**Your responsibilities**

-

Own and evolve segment positioning and messaging (HR, L&D, enterprise vs. SMB) as our product and commercial model transforms, keeping it consistent across every buyer touchpoint.

-

Define the narrative architecture for new products and packaging, including the credit-based commercial model, Berlitz App, and Compass, in partnership with the CPTO and Product leadership.

-

Lead GTM strategy and execution end-to-end for major product and feature launches, directing cross-functional coordination across marketing, sales, and product.

-

Build, manage, and develop the product marketing team, setting standards for output quality and prioritisation.

-

Own the sales enablement library, battlecards, decks, case studies, built globally and localised for major regions.

-

Direct market and competitive research and convert findings into product implications, USPs, and pricing input.

-

Own win/loss analysis as a standing discipline, feeding structured insight into positioning and product planning.

-

Partner with Brand & Activation, ABM, Paid Performance, and Product leadership, and represent product marketing in CPTO-level planning forums.

**Who you are**

-

You have 10+ years in product marketing, including 3+ years leading or managing a team.

-

You've built and scaled a product marketing function, including sales enablement infrastructure and cross-functional launches at company-wide scale.

-

You write and message at an executive level, translating technical and commercial complexity into buyer value.

-

You're comfortable operating at leadership level across segments and geographies in a global, matrixed organisation, partnering directly with C-suite stakeholders.

-

You're analytical and fluent in using competitive, win/loss, and customer data to inform and defend strategic decisions.

**You stand out if...**

-

You've worked in HR tech, L&D, language learning, or education.

-

You've operated through a major commercial or platform transformation, such as a system migration, pricing model change, or entity restructuring.

-

You have deep familiarity with ABM and demand generation motions and how product marketing shapes them.

-

You've worked in a global-local model spanning multiple regions, such as DE/EU and North America.

**Why you should join**

-

Set the positioning and GTM strategy for Berlitz's next-generation B2B, digital-first offering.

-

Partner directly with the CPTO on product and commercial transformation.

-

Build and lead a product marketing team and enablement function from the ground up.

-

Join a collaborative international team shaping how the world learns.

Remote work but occasional travel may be required for business engagements or events.
