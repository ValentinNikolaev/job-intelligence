## **The role**

We are seeking a Senior R&D Chef to join the culinary team of our newest brand, **Factor**: a healthy, ready-to-heat meal delivery service. At Factor, our goal is to revolutionize the way consumers eat: we make it more convenient to get delicious, healthy meals delivered to your doorstep; we cater to distinct dietary lifestyles and provide an ever-changing menu that appeals to a wide population of people. We are growing exponentially and are looking to continue to build our team with entrepreneurial-minded people who have a passion for culinary innovation, nutrition, and helping people achieve their goals.

Reporting to the Culinary Lead, the Senior R&D Chef will be responsible for developing delicious new recipes and innovating on existing recipes in order to drive excitement across our customer base. This role is required to develop recipes across all diet and meal preference types, while maintaining specific cost and nutritional targets. You will be expected to deliver on end-to-end culinary recipe development from ideation through initial editorial steps. The role is cross-functional and will work closely with our Operations, Nutrition, Procurement, and Menu Planning teams to develop the best possible product for our customers.

## **What you’ll do**

**You will...**

- Develop new recipes weekly that meet Factor’s product priorities, nutritional targets, cost targets, and production constraints

- Continuously improve existing recipes by analyzing customer feedback and performance data

- Dive deep into customer data, market trends, and competitor activity to inform future development

- Bench-test new recipes and run panel tastings to refine products through peer feedback

- Input and track recipes in our development tools to ensure clear documentation and iteration history

- Partner with Sourcing to evaluate and approve new ingredients that improve the customer experience, and meet quality / cost standards

- Collaborate with our Commercialization Chefs to ensure recipes are thoroughly tested and designed for large scale production

- Collaborate with kitchen production team to adapt recipes or make ingredient substitutions when required, ensuring customers still get the best quality and tastiest meals

## **What you’ll bring**

- 6+ years experience creating recipes, and/or running a commissary or large restaurant/banquet/catering

- A strong understanding of recipe execution at large scale and the ability to quickly adapt recipes and processes for improved output

- A degree in Culinary Science or a similar culinary-related field

- Curiosity and open-mindedness: you are always interested in learning new and better ways of doing things

- Outstanding communication and organizational skill: you excel at planning, prioritizing, and executing projects, are highly collaborative and can manage diverse opinions and competing priorities, and never miss a deadline

- An entrepreneurial mindset: you take initiative, are highly accountable, and love to get things done, all with a high standard of excellence

- An obsession with food and a passion for helping people achieve their goals!
