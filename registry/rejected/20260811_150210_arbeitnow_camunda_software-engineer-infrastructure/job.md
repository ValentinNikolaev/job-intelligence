**About the role:**

We are hiring for the **Self-Managed domain**, which owns the full software development lifecycle of our **self-managed product artifacts**.
This includes everything from the *getting started* experience to *production deployments* and *day-2 operations*.

We are looking for a **talented and motivated individual contributor** who thrives on ownership and technical depth - someone eager to push our artifacts and processes to the next level. **What You’ll Be Doing:**

-

**Build, maintain, and release C8 distributions** for our self-managed customers, covering Helm Charts (Kubernetes), C8Run, Docker Compose, and related documentation.

-

**Improve reliability and usability** of our deployment artifacts, ensuring a seamless experience from local setups to production clusters and day-2 operations.

-

**Collaborate cross-functionally** with Development Teams, Product Management, Quality Assurance, Documentation, and Support to deliver integrated, tested, and documented releases.

-

**Handle engineering-level support cases** together with our support team to understand our customers issues and feed learnings back into development.

-

**Drive documentation and onboarding improvements** - turning complex systems into clear, accessible instructions that help customers succeed faster.

The Distribution team currently consists of **1 Senior and 3 Mid-level Software Engineers**, operating with a strong manager-of-one mindset and cross-team collaboration.

-

**Improved onboarding and documentation**, transforming your own learning experience into concrete improvements.

-
