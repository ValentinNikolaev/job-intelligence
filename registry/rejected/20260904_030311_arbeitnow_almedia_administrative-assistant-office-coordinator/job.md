# **What You'll Do 🎯**

-

Own front desk and guest relations, serving as the first point of contact for candidates, guests, and employees while ensuring a professional, welcoming experience.

-

Own candidate coordination and end-to-end onsite interviews, scheduling and confirming panels and rooms, and hosting candidates on the day so every interview feels considered and well-run.

-

Manage guest check-in and hospitality (visitor system, tours, refreshments), protecting confidentiality and accessibility needs.

-

Provide light scheduling and calendar support to the leadership team, growing into broader responsibilities over time.

-

Take on ad hoc projects, event support, and admin tasks as they come up, with this part of the role growing gradually as you settle in.

-

Keep meeting-room calendars organised and up to date, and close the loop on requests with clear ETAs.

-

Mail, packages, and facilities requests sit separately with our workplace team, so this role can stay focused on people, not logistics.

# **What You'll Bring 💡**

-

Experience in coordination, team assistance, front of house, hospitality, or similar admin support, with a hospitality-first mindset and interest in growing into a wider administrative support role.

-

Ownership mentality, seeing things through once something is picked up and closing every loop rather than leaving it half-finished. Bringing questions, ideas, and solutions rather than just problems. Precise as well as fast, with small details like names, times, rooms, and confirmations never slipping.

-

Strong prioritisation across competing demands, candidate needs, leadership's asks, and office operations, with a clear sense of what to put down first when they collide.

-

Comfort with Google Workspace, Slack, and calendar scheduling, with a quick ability to pick up new tools like visitor systems, ATS, and Notion independently. Using AI tools to draft, summarise, and stay organised is a plus.

-

Clear, professional communication with candidates, employees, leaders, and external partners, along with discretion handling sensitive information.

-

Genuine comfort with ambiguity and fast-changing priorities, likely built in an environment that moved quickly and reshuffled plans at short notice, coming out of it composed rather than rattled.

# **What Makes You a Great Fit**

-

You've worked in environments that move quickly and change plans often. You keep calm when priorities shift or several things land at once. You can absorb a shifted priority or a last-minute change without losing momentum.

-

You move quickly, set clear expectations, and follow through until resolution.

-

You take initiative to streamline scheduling or check-in, and you want a role that grows with you.
