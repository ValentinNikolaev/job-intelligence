We're looking for a **Senior Product Designer** to join one of Finom’s core product domains and help shape the experience for tens of thousands of customers across Europe. You’ll own design from discovery through delivery, turning complex financial workflows into intuitive, elegant, and scalable product experiences.

This role is for a designer who thrives on complexity, demands excellence, and is ready to translate ambitious business goals into simple, high-quality user experiences. You’ll work closely with Product Managers, Engineers, Analysts, Research, and Design leadership to define problems clearly, explore solutions rigorously, and ship work that materially improves the lives of entrepreneurs, freelancers, and small businesses.

**What You’ll Do**

- **Own design across the full product lifecycle**, from discovery and problem framing to final implementation, QA, and polish.

- **Turn complex fintech and SMB workflows into clear, user-centered experiences**, using research, product data, and customer feedback to guide decisions.

- **Collaborate closely with Product Managers, Engineers, Analysts, and fellow Designers** to scope work, define outcomes, and ship high-quality solutions.

- **Create essential design artifacts** including flows, wireframes, prototypes, high-fidelity mockups, and clear product specs.

- **Contribute to our design system and product quality bar**, improving consistency, accessibility, scalability, and craft across the interface.

- **Support high-priority initiatives** such as modular pricing, onboarding UX transformation, and AI Copilot features.

**Who You Are**

- **4+ years of experience in product design**, ideally in fintech, SaaS, or complex digital product environments.

- **Formal education in design** — such as Product Design, Interaction Design, UX/UI, Visual Communication, HCI, or a related field.

- A **strong portfolio** showing user-centered thinking, strong interaction and visual design craft, and polished execution.

- Experience designing **complex platforms** with multiple user flows, states, permissions, edge cases, and dependencies.

- Confidence working in **cross-functional squads** and explaining design decisions using research, product data, business context, and customer feedback.

- Strong English communication skills; German language skills or experience designing for German-speaking users are highly valued.

- **AI-native mindset**: you actively use AI tools for exploration, synthesis, prototyping, critique, documentation, and faster iteration.
