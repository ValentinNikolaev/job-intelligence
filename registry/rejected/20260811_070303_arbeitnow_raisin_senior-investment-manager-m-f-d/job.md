Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the **FinTech** connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers **better interest rates** and banks a **diversified form of refinancing**. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.

Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from **more than one million investors** which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.

**Team**

Our Investment and Pension Products (IPP) team is all about helping customers earn a higher return than they can expect from safe bank deposits. Building products that maximize return per unit of risk taken and guide customers towards the risk level that’s optimal for them is the IPP team’s job.

**Your Responsibilities**

- You have the unusual opportunity to take responsibility for topics along the entire investment products value chain.

- You’ll be accountable for strategic projects such as the introduction of new investment products, asset classes, strategies, etc.

- You’ll be a key stakeholder in the development of financial and non-financial features of our products, including the web app and native mobile apps.

- You’ll contribute to communication related to investments and capital markets for CRM, webinars, and our website.

- You have shared responsibility for finding the best ways to give our customers exposure to new asset classes, including instrument screening and selection.

- You’ll analyse data using quantitative and qualitative methods to learn more about our customers and their investment behavior.

- You serve as a point of contact for all things capital markets for all Raisin stakeholders.

- You represent the team’s investment philosophy throughout the organization and externally.**
**

**Your Profile**

- You are a generalist with strong subject matter expertise in investments; experience with pension products is a plus.

- A degree in economics, finance, mathematics, or the natural sciences is preferred.

- Alternatively, you have a degree in another subject (including business) and are committed to skeptical empiricism, both in investments and in business more broadly.

- You trust your judgment and can work independently. You feel comfortable taking risk-based decisions.

- You are precise in your language.

- You have a sense of humor and don’t take yourself too seriously.

- You care about both processes and results, but much more about results.

- Exposure to programming languages and basic software development workflows is a plus.

- Experience with pension products is a plus.
