robust, user-ready products.

If you enjoy solving real-world engineering challenges, moving quickly, and building meaningful technology in a highly

collaborative startup environment, we would love to hear from you. You will work on everything from the user app

interface to communication protocols and data security

## Aufgaben

Your Responsibilities

• Develop, test, and maintain user-facing app for our wearable robotic systems

• Translate user needs and clinical workflows into intuitive, accessible interface designs

• Ensure data security and privacy compliance in all app-device data handling

• Implement communication between the app and embedded device firmware

• Conduct usability testing with end users and iterate on designs based on feedback

• Document UI/UX architecture, design decisions, and app-device interfaces in a clear and structured way

• Work closely with cross-functional teammates to rapidly test, improve, and refine product performance
