#### About the role
Agile Robots SE is an innovative company with a clear mission: bringing intelligent robotic solutions to our customers. We combine the latest AI technology with powerful robotic systems, from industrial robot arms and mobile manipulators to humanoids, to solve tasks that classical automation cannot address. At the heart of our offering is a versatile software platform for orchestrating complex robotic systems. It allows efficient customization for specific customer requirements: configuring systems, developing reusable skills, parameterizing processes, and executing tasks reliably in production. Our solutions are deployed with international customers across a wide range of industries. Seamless integration and scalable deployment are at the core of our mission.

We are looking for a passionate Forward Deployed Engineer for Robotics to bridge cutting-edge robotics technology with real-world customer success. In this role, you will work directly with international customers to deploy, customize, and optimize advanced robotic automation solutions. You will become a trusted technical partner, translating customer challenges into scalable, production-ready robotics applications. The role is based in Munich, Germany.

#### Your Responsibilities

- Deploy, integrate, and commission complex robotic systems for industrial automation and mobile manipulation applications

- Customize and extend our robotic workcell automation framework by implementing customer-specific features, integrations, and workflows

- Integrate and fine-tune AI models for robotic applications, including Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, end-to-end learned policies, and general machine learning components

- Work closely with customers to understand technical requirements and translate them into robust software solutions

- Diagnose, troubleshoot, and resolve software and system integration issues both remotely and on-site

- Travel to international customer sites for workshops, deployments, training, and technical support (up to 40% travel)
