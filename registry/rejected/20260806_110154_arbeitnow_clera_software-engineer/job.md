### About the Role

Join a small, fast-moving engineering team at a **Y Combinator-backed conversational AI voice startup** building the infrastructure that powers thousands of AI-driven phone calls every day. This is a high-impact, full-stack engineering role where you'll own projects end-to-end, influence core architecture decisions, and work directly alongside customers to ship meaningful product experiences in an AI-first, communications-enabled platform.

The company is **on-site in Berlin, Germany** — visa sponsorship is available for the right candidate.

### What You'll Do

-

Work closely with customers to deeply understand their problems and design product experiences that solve real challenges.

-

Build and scale backend infrastructure capable of handling thousands of concurrent AI phone calls per day.

-

Craft intuitive front-end user experiences tailored to an AI-first product paradigm.

-

Own high-stakes projects from initial concept through production launch and post-release support.

-

Contribute to core architectural decisions alongside a senior, driven engineering team.
