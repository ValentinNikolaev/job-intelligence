**Aufgaben**

Your tasks - this is what awaits you in detail

- You plan and run research across the full spectrum, from foundational studies that shape immediate product decisions to strategic work that influences our long-term user experience vision and goals
- You work closely with product managers, product designers and engineers across several product areas and get involved early, while problems are still being defined
- You own your projects end to end: scoping the question, choosing the method, running the study and turning findings into a clear recommendation
- You combine qualitative and quantitative methods (user interviews, usability testing, surveys, unmoderated testing, behavioural and market data) and triangulate them instead of relying on a single source
- You use AI in your daily research work and help shape how our team applies it
- You contribute to our research repository and make insights reusable, so teams can build on what we already know
- You benchmark competitors and triangulate those findings with primary customer research to show where our real opportunities are
- You run workshops, find formats that make insights travel and follow through until your findings have influenced a decision

**Profil**

Your profile - this is what we expect from you

- You speak German at native or near-native level and English fluently, as the majority of our customer and seller interviews are conducted in German
- You have several years of experience in qualitative and quantitative user research, in-depth expertise in UX methods and a comprehensive understanding of customer-centric agile product development
- You are strong in either qualitative or quantitative research and actively building the other, with a good understanding of statistical methods and analytical thinking
- You know how to conduct research at the different stages of the product development process (generative setting, solution validation or end-to-end)
- You are proactive, enjoy taking ownership of your topics and feel confident presenting your findings and recommendations to stakeholders at all levels
- You are pragmatic in how you scope your work, balancing speed and depth for the decision at hand, and you already use AI tools in your own practice
- You enjoy working in a small, close-knit team where people share work early, support each other and learn from one another
