**Director of Revenue Operations & Strategic Finance**

**Location: **Remote from Spain, UK or Germany (optional Hybrid in Berlin)

**Reporting to: **VP of Finance

**About the Role**

As the **Director of Revenue Operations & Strategic Finance**, you will be responsible for shaping and steering CoachHub’s financial plan and go-to-market strategy.

Reporting directly to the VP Finance, you will act as the dedicated business partner to our Sales organization, while also partnering closely with Customer Success, Product & Tech and Support functions. Your focus will be on driving sustainable, profitable growth by connecting commercial performance, operational reality and financial outcomes.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can **fly high and dive deep** - acting as an eye-level sparring partner to senior commercial and finance leaders while also being willing to get into the details and drive important topics personally.

You will lead and develop a team of Revenue Operations and FP&A professionals while helping shape and further build the function as CoachHub continues to scale.

**Key Responsibilities**

Revenue Operations & Commercial Partnering:

-

**Revenue forecasting & steering:** Own pipeline and bookings forecasting, develop the latest view of expected progress on deals and work with Sales leadership to define measures to reach targets.

-

**Sales & Marketing resource allocation:** Advise on staffing and deployment of commercial resources to maximise return on investment.

-

**Sales incentives design:** Partner with the compensation team on quota and variable compensation topics, ensuring commercial incentives are aligned with business objectives.

-

**Commercial performance improvement:** Turn complex commercial and financial data into clear insights and recommendations that drive commercial business decisions.

-

**Revenue operations improvements:** Partner with the Go-to-market systems team to improve processes, tools and ways of working across the commercial organization, independently identifying and driving opportunities to increase efficiency.

Strategic Finance & Planning:

-

**Financial planning:** Develop an integrated financial plan from contract value (ACV / TCV), through the P&L to cash flow, working closely with stakeholders across the business.

-

**Forecasting & steering:** Translate financial and operational performance into clear insights and recommendations for senior leadership.

-

**Business partnering:** Partner with the VP Finance and senior stakeholders on strategic business decisions, ensuring commercial initiatives are grounded in the broader financial picture.

-

**Reporting:** Lead the team to efficiently deliver management, investor and bank reporting

Leadership:

-

**People leader:** Lead and develop a diverse team of Revenue Operations and FP&A professionals across financial and operational disciplines.

-

**Trusted advisor:** Act as a dedicated business partner to senior commercial leadership and role model strong business partnering for your team.

-

**Pragmatic operator:** Bring structure and best practices where needed, while adapting them to what is actually fit-for-purpose for CoachHub rather than simply applying established blueprints.

-

**Independent driver:** Set your own agenda, identify opportunities and drive meaningful change independently in an environment where ambiguity is part of the job.

**Your Profile**

-

**Revenue & Commercial Expertise:** Strong experience partnering with Sales or commercial leadership — owning or driving revenue forecasting, pipeline, GTM planning and commercial performance.

-

**Finance Foundation:** Solid finance background with strong analytical and commercial-modelling skills — you're comfortable in raw data and can synthesise it into clear strategic insight. Deep FP&A or strategic-finance expertise is valued but can be further developed on the job where the commercial and finance-partnering foundation is already strong.

-

**Business Partnering:** Proven track record partnering VP- and C-level stakeholders and influencing decisions without direct authority.

-

**Leadership:** Experience building and developing high-performing analytical teams, including at least 2–3 years directly leading a team.

-

**Builder & Scale-up Mindset:** Demonstrated ability to independently build, improve or reshape processes, teams or ways of working — setting your own agenda and adapting best practices pragmatically to a scaling business, rather than applying a big-company blueprint.

-

**Communication:** Exceptional communication and storytelling — you make complex financial and commercial concepts simple and engaging for senior stakeholders.

-
