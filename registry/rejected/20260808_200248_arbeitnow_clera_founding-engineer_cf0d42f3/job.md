### About the Role

Join a small, ambitious team building the core software platform that controls and optimises industrial battery storage assets across Europe. As a **Founding Engineer**, you will have outsized ownership across the full stack — from real-time optimisation algorithms and backend services to operator-facing dashboards and cloud infrastructure. Your work will directly shape both the product and the trajectory of the company at a critical early stage.

This is an on-site, full-time position based in **Munich, Germany**. Visa sponsorship is available.

### What You'll Do

-

Own and build the core product powering real-time optimisation and forecasting for industrial energy assets.

-

Develop backend services and mixed-integer optimisation algorithms to process high-frequency data in real time.

-

Design and scale distributed infrastructure to support thousands of battery systems in production.

-

Build intuitive frontend dashboards that allow battery operators to monitor, control, and analyse their assets.

-

Implement containerised, CI/CD-driven deployment pipelines and maintain production observability.

-

Collaborate directly with founders and customers to define product features and translate operational requirements into engineering solutions.

-

Iterate rapidly based on feedback from live deployments.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Experience with real-time data processing and optimisation or forecasting systems.

-

Proven ability to design and operate scalable distributed backend systems for large-scale deployments.

-

Solid Python backend development skills, including mixed-integer optimisation.

-

Frontend development proficiency with JavaScript/TypeScript and React (Next.js preferred); experience with Tailwind CSS.

-

Containerisation and modern deployment practices — Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD for production services.

-

Experience with observability and monitoring tooling (e.g. Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).

-

Database design and data modelling experience (e.g. PostgreSQL or MySQL).

-

Cloud infrastructure experience with AWS, GCP, or Azure.

-

Startup experience (company under 3 years old and fewer than 50 employees qualifies).

-

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or Electrical Engineering with a grade better than 2.0 (German grading scale).

-

**Fluency in German** — the majority of customers communicate in German.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Background or hands-on experience in the energy industry (battery storage, renewables, grid management, or similar).

-

Demonstrated track record of deploying optimisations in live production systems.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

Founding-stage equity and meaningful ownership in the company's success.

-

High-impact role with direct influence over product and company direction.

-

Visa sponsorship available for qualified candidates.

### Location
