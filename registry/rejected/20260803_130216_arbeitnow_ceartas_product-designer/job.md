At **Ceartas**, we stand at the forefront of AI-powered brand protection, copyright law, and digital security, dedicated to safeguarding the integrity of content creators, brands, and enterprises worldwide. As we accelerate our growth and expand our global footprint, we're seeking a **Mid-Level UX/UI Designer** who is passionate about crafting intuitive and engaging user experiences and contributing to our design system and product innovation.

This role is a unique opportunity to make a significant impact on the future of digital content protection. You will work closely with our Senior Product Designer, engineers, and business stakeholders as part of the Product Team to develop cutting-edge solutions that address the challenges of brand protection.

🚀 **Your Role: Beyond the High-Level**

You'll be a key contributor to our product team, ensuring our user experience meets industry standards and aligns with our business goals. In this role, you will:

**Enhance User Experience & Engagement:** You will play a critical role in designing intuitive and engaging user journeys. From conducting user research and developing personas to creating wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity UI, your work will help define our product's overall experience.

**Contribute to Our Design System:** You will help maintain and enhance our design system, ensuring consistency across our digital platforms. Your contributions will help shape a scalable and structured approach to design at Ceartas.

**Take Ownership of Features:** You will be responsible for taking features from concept to completion, considering all user touchpoints and edge cases. You will work collaboratively with engineers and stakeholders to ensure smooth implementation.

**Support Research-Driven Design:** You will participate in user research efforts, gathering qualitative and quantitative insights to inform design decisions. Your ability to analyze and interpret research findings will contribute to a more user-centric product.

**Work Cross-Functionally:** You will collaborate with engineers, business stakeholders, and other designers to ensure a seamless handoff between design and development. Understanding basic coding principles and technical feasibility will be a plus in this role.

🗒️ **Responsibilities:**

Contribute to and maintain the design system to ensure consistency and scalability across products.

-

-

Take ownership of features from discovery to delivery, ensuring a holistic user experience.

-

Assist in refining the UX architecture of our product, including mind mapping and journey mapping.
