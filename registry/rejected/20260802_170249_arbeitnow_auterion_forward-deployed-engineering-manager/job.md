As the Forward Deployed Engineering Manager, you will lead a specialized team of engineers who bridge the gap between our core products and real-world, field-deployed environments. This role is uniquely challenging: you aren't just managing software deployments; you are overseeing the physical integration of our technology into complex, diverse hardware ecosystems.

You will lead your team through the entire lifecycle of a customer engagement—from initial discovery and site surveys to the final hardware/software "handshake" in the field.

### **Responsibilities and Duties**

- **Team Leadership & Mentorship:** Lead and scale a team of Solutions Engineers. You will provide technical guidance on complex integrations and foster a culture of proactive problem-solving

- **Accountability: **Work directly with stakeholders throughout the company and keep the bar high for your exceptional team. You will have a close relationship with the Director of Hardware, CTO, CEO and VP of Engineering

- **Field Deployment Oversight:** Manage the technical strategy for deploying applications in "non-standard" environments. This includes overseeing hardware site readiness, connectivity challenges, and environmental constraints

- **Architectural Strategy:** Act as the lead architect for high-stakes customer integrations. You will ensure that the hardware-to-software interface (API, firmware, or edge computing layers) is robust and scalable

- **Stakeholder Management:** Serve as the primary technical liaison for enterprise customers. You will translate customer business requirements into technical deployment plans that account for hardware latency, power constraints, and data throughput

- **Feedback Loop Optimization:** Work closely with Product and R&D teams to communicate field-testing results and hardware compatibility issues, ensuring our product evolves to meet real-world edge cases

### **Qualifications and Skills**

- **Hardware/Software Fluency**: 8+ years of experience in technical roles, with at least 3 years specifically focused on Hardware/Software Integration (e.g., IoT, robotics, industrial automation, or edge computing)

- **Leadership Experience**: 3+ years managing a technical, customer-facing team (Solutions Architects, Field Engineers, or Sales Engineers)

- **Field Deployment Expertise**: Hands-on experience with field-deployed applications where "turning it off and on again" isn't always an option. Understanding of networking protocols is a major plus

- **Full-Stack Understanding**: While hardware-focused, you must understand the modern software stack (Cloud services, APIs, and Database management) to ensure data flows correctly from the field to the dashboard

- **Crisis Management**: Proven ability to troubleshoot critical failures in the field under high pressure and with limited physical access to the hardware

- **Work Authorization:** Must hold EU citizenship and be eligible to work in the European Union without restriction.
