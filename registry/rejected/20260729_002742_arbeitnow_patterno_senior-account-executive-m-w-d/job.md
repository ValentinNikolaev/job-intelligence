**The role**

You are a Senior Account Executive at Patterno. You own complex deals end-to-end, from discovery to closed-won, and you help turn a working motion into a predictable, scalable revenue engine.

You run sharp discovery, lead multi-stakeholder processes, and drive executive-level outcomes. You generate pipeline, build trust with senior stakeholders, and raise the bar on how we sell—especially for enterprise contracts.

**What success looks like (first 90 days)**

- Build a strong pipeline across inbound, partner signals, and outbound; run tight next steps and mutual action plans

- Establish a repeatable cadence: CRM hygiene, pipeline reviews, and accurate forecasting you can defend

- Turn deal learnings into scalable assets: ICP refinement, objection handling, competitive intel, and messaging that converts

## Aufgaben

**What you’ll do**

- Own the full sales cycle from discovery to closed-won across mid-market and enterprise-style buying committees

- Navigate procurement, legal, and compliance-heavy processes and keep momentum through complex deal cycles

- Drive clear next steps and timelines, manage mutual action plans, and keep momentum through complex sales processes

- Lead late-stage deal strategy (qualification, value framing, negotiation, close plans) and drive clean execution

- Partner with the founders on positioning, messaging, and pricing. Turn what you learn in deals into better GTM materials

- Keep a clean CRM, forecast accurately, and make performance visible through simple reporting

- Generate your own pipeline and help define the outbound + inbound operating system as the team scales

- Feed market insights back to the product team. Every deal is product input and competitive intelligence

## Qualifikation

**What you bring**

**Must-haves**

- German + English fluency

- 4–7+ years in B2B sales with measurable results (quota attainment, win rate, multi-stakeholder deal velocity, and enterprise deal size)

- You’ve done both SDR work and full-cycle AE closing (you can prospect and you can close)

- Strong track record selling enterprise / complex contracts and navigating procurement-heavy environments

- Strong commercial judgment: you qualify hard, push for clarity, and can say no to bad-fit deals

- Tool- and process-driven: you run a clean CRM, build repeatable workflows, and use modern tools (including AI) to automate busywork

- Numbers-first operator: you manage your funnel by conversion, cycle length, and pipeline coverage

**Nice-to-haves**

- B2B SaaS background (or similar high-velocity sales environment)

- Experience selling into procurement-heavy or compliance-driven buying committees

- Experience in an early-stage or fast-scaling environment (build while you sell)

**You might be a fit if you**

- Love turning a strong discovery call into a structured process that closes

- Enjoy working in ambiguity and improving the system while executing on it

- Want to be part of building a sales engine, not just inheriting someone else’s playbook

- Are hungry for ownership and not interested in waiting for permission

- Care about winning but stay humble about what you don’t know yet

- Have something outside of work that shows you don’t do things halfway, whether that’s competitive sports, a side project, or something completely different

- Want to be a senior closer and help build a world-class sales machine
