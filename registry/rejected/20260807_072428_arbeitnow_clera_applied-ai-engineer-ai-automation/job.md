### About the Role

We're a fast-growing, technology-first financial services group building the operating system for the German tax advisory industry. Our platform combines AI-powered automation with deep accounting integrations to help tax firms dramatically improve productivity and service quality for their SME clients.

As an **Applied AI Engineer** on our AI & Automation team, you'll sit at the heart of this transformation — turning complex, regulated accounting and advisory workflows into scalable, reliable software. You'll work directly with founders and product teams to ship solutions that make a real difference across our network of partner firms.

### What You'll Do

-

Build automation and infrastructure tooling that converts repetitive, manual work into fast and reliable execution.

-

Translate accounting and tax advisory processes into scalable, AI-powered software.

-

Integrate systems and data across multiple firms to create a coherent, unified platform.

-

Collaborate closely with founders, advisors, and product teams to design and ship high-impact features.

-

Help shape the overall technical architecture for speed, reliability, and scale.
