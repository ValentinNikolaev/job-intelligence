# **Your Role**

As the Senior Product Manager for Capital Management, you will play a critical role in building the infrastructure that powers how private funds move and track capital—from capital calls to distributions, treasury workflows to accounting integrations. These are some of the most high-stakes and operationally intensive processes fund managers handle, and you’ll help make them seamless, scalable, and audit-ready. Your work will directly contribute to our mission of reshaping private markets through modern, intuitive software.

This is a high-impact position where you’ll collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to build products that deliver tangible value, including engineering, design, operations, and customer success.

# **Top responsibilities**

-

Define and drive the product roadmap for capital-related workflows, including capital calls, distributions, treasury, and accounting integrations

-

Identify opportunities to transform complex operational processes into intuitive product experiences, automation logic, and scalable infrastructure

-

-

-

-

-

-

Continuously gather feedback to iterate and improve our product

-

Stay ahead of industry trends in fund finance, treasury tools, and accounting systems to identify new opportunities for innovation
