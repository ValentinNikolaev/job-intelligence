### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to join a small, elite design team at a Series B AI-powered EdTech platform that reaches **tens of millions of students** across more than 30 countries. You'll work at the centre of a major shift toward AI-native product experiences, with direct access to C-level leadership to shape the product vision from day one. Your work will be seen — and felt — by millions of learners every day.

We're going all-in on design craft and AI-native experiences. If you believe details *are* the design, ship faster than you deck, and see AI as a genuine multiplier rather than a buzzword, this role is built for you.

**Note: Visa sponsorship is not available for this role. On-site in Berlin, Germany.**

### What You'll Do

-

**Own entire product areas end-to-end** — from conception to delivery — working directly with C-level and engineering to ship ideas to millions of students within days.

-

**Raise the quality bar** on everything students see, touch, and feel — world-class UI, motion design, and micro-interactions that set a new standard.

-

**Build and scale a design system from the ground up** — curated references, stellar components, and sharp reviews that make quality easy to replicate across the team.

-

**Pioneer AI-native design** — develop AI-assisted workflows and AI-native product experiences that change how the team researches, ideates, and ships.

-

**Set the reference point** — create UIs students want to screenshot and details they feel but can't name.

-

**Mentor and guide** more junior designers, raising the collective craft of the team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Several years of product design experience with a portfolio that demonstrates shipped work — work that *moved metrics* and delighted real users (portfolio matters more than tenure).

-

Actively uses AI tools across the full design loop: prototyping, research, and shipping — not experimenting, *multiplying*.

-

Systems thinker who scales quality across a product, not just a pretty component library — code-first design systems, AI-powered design reviews, whatever works at speed and scale.

-

Proven ability to simplify complex, messy user journeys into experiences that feel obvious.

-

Bias toward action: prototypes over presentations, experiments over endless debate.

-

Experience guiding and levelling up more junior designers.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Builder mentality — bonus if you've shipped a side project end-to-end or opened a merge request.

-

Background in consumer apps, EdTech, or fast-paced B2C environments.

-

Experience at companies like Quizlet, Khan Academy, Brainly, Memrise, Chegg, or similar consumer/EdTech products.

**You won't be a fit if you:**

-

Prefer slow deliberation over fast shipping.

-

Are sceptical of or resistant to AI tools in your workflow.

-

Work best as a solo, territorial artist rather than a team-first collaborator.

-

Default to decks and presentations rather than prototypes and experiments.

### What We're Like

-

51–200 person company, growing fast across a global student audience.

-

Direct feedback culture — no preciousness, no territory.

-

Builders who ship: ideas go from concept to millions of users in days, not quarters.

-

AI is central to how the product is built *and* how the team works.
