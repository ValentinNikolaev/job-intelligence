**Job description**

We are seeking an experienced **Senior DevOps Consultant **to support a defined project at our Headquarters in Berlin.

The assignment is focused on the set-up and further development of our data management and infrastructure platform. The initial consulting engagement is planned for six months, with the possibility of extending the assignment for a further period, potentially up to 12 months in total, subject to project requirements and mutual agreement. The consultant will provide specialist DevOps and data-platform expertise within the scope of the project. The assignment is structured as an independent consulting engagement, with responsibility for delivering agreed project outcomes and technical solutions.

The project supports our Climate Policy Team, which is engaged in various projects on climate change mitigation, in particular on energy system transformation, evaluation of emissions scenarios, the 1.5°C global warming limit and evaluation of national climate action. This includes flagship projects such as the Climate Action Tracker and the National Pathways Explorer, for which data is processed and analysed in-house and then published on the respective project websites.

As part of the assignment, the consultant will work on the shared data platform and infrastructure used across the organisation. Our platform is self-hosted on our own hardware and currently runs PostgreSQL, MinIO, GitLab CI/CD and containerised workloads on Linux. The consultant is expected to organise and perform the assignment independently, within the agreed project scope and in coordination with relevant project stakeholders.
**Your responsibilities**

- Build and operate the data platform for advanced analytics and climate modelling, spanning DevOps and data engineering work.
- Take the platform from single-server deployments to properly separated environments, with orchestration and infrastructure as code.
- Establish and improve the platform's operational practices, including monitoring, alerting, incident response, capacity planning and performance optimisation.
- Design and implement the access, identity and secrets model across platform and shared infrastructure.
- Contribute to the standardisation of production data workflows and platform standards across the organisation.
- Document what you build (runbooks, architecture notes and handover material) to ensure continuity.
- Provide technical guidance to data analysts and scientists and support the adoption of good practice in DataOps, security and governance.
- Evaluate new tools and technologies, run proof-of-concepts, and help define the technical roadmap for our infrastructure.
