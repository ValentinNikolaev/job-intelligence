### **The Opportunity**

As a Senior Backend Engineer, you will design and build the services that provide our customers with fast, reliable, and scalable Node API and Web3 Data access. You will have an opportunity to explore new product offerings, dive deep into the details of the different blockchains we support, and engineer new solutions to scaling a globally distributed, low-latency platform.

You’ll join an engineering team that is a diverse group of individuals with a shared passion for problem-solving and innovation. We believe in open communication and knowledge-sharing, and our engineers are encouraged to work cross-functionally across the company and to specialize based on their interests.
