**🎯 Your main goal will be to become n8n's dedicated architect of Partner Ecosystem strategy and operations, building the systems, data, and processes that let this fast-growing partner network scale alongside n8n's growth.**

To do so, here are your responsibilities:

### Channel Program Operations

-

Own the operational infrastructure powering n8n's global partner ecosystem across reseller and referral segments.

-

Design and maintain partner-attributed pipeline tracking, keeping sourced, influenced, and co-sell deals accurately categorized in Salesforce.

-

Build and maintain channel reporting frameworks covering partner-sourced MRR, activation rates, deal registration, and tier performance.

### Partner Performance & Pipeline

-

Support partner pipeline inspection and forecast roll-ups alongside Channel Sales leadership.

-

Develop reporting and insights that drive partner growth and sharpen program performance.

-

Build channel-specific territory and routing logic so partner-sourced leads and registered deals are prioritized and attributed correctly.

### Program & Systems Administration

-

Own configuration and adoption of partner-facing tools, including the partner portal and Salesforce's channel-specific objects.

-

Maintain documentation and training materials covering channel processes, rules of engagement, and partner program policies.

-

Manage partner tier qualification, tracking certifications and activity thresholds, while using AI-enabled tools to streamline and scale the work.

### Cross-Functional Collaboration

-

Partner with Channel Sales, Revenue Operations, Marketing, Legal, and Finance to operationalize GTM motions like co-marketing campaigns and partner enablement launches.

-

Serve as the channel operations subject matter expert across cross-functional planning cycles, from annual planning to QBRs and GTM launches.

-

Triage and support ad hoc requests, partner escalations, and troubleshooting as they come up.

## REQUIREMENTS

-
