**Your Role**

As an Engineering Manager with a hands-on mindset, you will lead a cross-functional team responsible for Statista's internal Content Tooling platform - a key enabler for our in-house researchers and data engineers. In close collaboration with a strong Product Manager, you'll be at the forefront of building AI-enabled content production at scale. Together, you’ll shape the roadmap, empower a senior engineering team, and deliver high-quality, scalable, and cost-efficient solutions.

**Your Tasks**

-

Lead a team of backend, frontend, and QA engineers, creating a supportive environment where people can grow and do their best work.

-

Carry a clear 12-18 month direction for the team and keep it intact when org priorities move.

-

Own cross-team interfaces and dependencies so engineers can stay focused on the work.

-

Drive engineering excellence and accountability for the stability, performance, and maintainability of the tools you build and own.

-

Lead and deliver migration projects - Statista has a long history of building internal tools; you'll work to consolidate what’s still valuable and phase out the obsolete.

-

Bring modern AI-assisted engineering practices into the team's workflow, with responsible review and clear ownership.

**Your Profile**

-

3+ years of experience leading cross-functional, product-oriented engineering teams

-

Solid grasp of full-stack development, ideally with Java, React, and both relational and NoSQL databases

-

Strong understanding of distributed system design, particularly microservice architectures

-

Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset - you build strong relationships with senior engineers, product peers, other engineering managers, and various stakeholders

-

Familiarity with building applications on AWS

-

Bonus: experience developing GUI-heavy internal tools or platforms
