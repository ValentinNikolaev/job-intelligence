**Your mission**

As our **Product Manager – Android Integration & Ecosystem**, you will define the product what and why for how ProGlove devices connect, integrate and create value within the enterprise Android ecosystem.

ProGlove is evolving from a wearable scanner company into a Worker Companion company, bringing together ergonomic wearable hardware, software, connectivity, workflow guidance, data and intelligence. With the MAI Wearable Companion, we are entering a broader ecosystem of enterprise applications, technologies, standards, partners and customer infrastructure.

Your mission will be to make our products easy to adopt, integrate and deploy at scale – without turning every customer opportunity into a bespoke engineering project. You will identify recurring customer needs, translate them into scalable capabilities and propositions, and decide where ProGlove should own, enable or partner. In doing so, you will help turn integration from a technical necessity into a source of differentiation, customer value and commercial opportunity.

You will work closely with the Product Manager responsible for the Wearable Companion device. While they own the device proposition, you will own how the device connects to and creates value in the wider ecosystem. Together, you will shape a coherent end-to-end proposition for customers, developers, integrators and partners.

**Your day-to-day **

- Define and evolve the product strategy and priorities for ProGlove’s enterprise Android integration and ecosystem capabilities, aligned with customer needs and business goals.
- Work directly with customers, Sales and Solution Architecture to identify recurring problems, distinguish individual requests from broader market needs and turn the right opportunities into scalable solutions.
- Translate recurring integration needs into repeatable product capabilities such as APIs, SDKs, tools, interfaces, documentation, integration patterns and deployment solutions.
- Shape the product experience around device setup, provisioning, application distribution, device management and other capabilities required to deploy and operate connected device fleets at scale.
- Make clear, pragmatic decisions about where ProGlove should **own, enable or partner**, balancing customer value, strategic control, speed, scalability and technical complexity.
- Identify the platforms, applications, standards and ecosystem players that matter to our customers, and shape the integrations, certifications and technology partnerships that strengthen our proposition.
- Explore where integration capabilities, software, services and ecosystem propositions can create differentiated value and support commercial or recurring-revenue opportunities.
- Partner closely with Engineering and the Product Manager for the Wearable Companion to create alignment on customer problems, priorities, trade-offs and desired outcomes.
- Enable Sales and other customer-facing teams to clearly explain our Android proposition, supported capabilities, integrations and common deployment patterns.
- Maintain a forward-looking view of enterprise Android, AOSP, enterprise mobility, AI and adjacent technologies, and translate relevant developments into strategic options and product priorities.

**Your profile**

At ProGlove, technical expertise matters, but how you work is just as important. We are looking for someone who takes ownership, stays curious, collaborates openly and turns complexity into clear, pragmatic decisions.

- You bring strong Product Management experience in B2B or enterprise technology and can define strategy, discover meaningful customer problems, prioritize opportunities and turn them into scalable products or propositions.
- You are commercially minded and naturally connect product decisions with customer value, differentiation, business impact and opportunities to capture value.
- You are comfortable working with enterprise software, platforms, connected devices, integrations or technology ecosystems, and understand the realities of complex customer IT environments.
- You have enough technical depth to understand architecture, interfaces and trade-offs, contribute credibly to platform and integration discussions, and recognize when deeper engineering expertise is needed.
- You think in systems and patterns: when faced with a customer requirement, you look beyond the immediate request to understand why it exists, how broadly it applies and what a simple, repeatable solution could look like.
- You take end-to-end ownership, move topics forward without waiting for perfect instructions and remain effective when the product space or path ahead is not yet fully defined.
- You collaborate generously and build trust across Product, Engineering, Sales, Solution Architecture, customers and external partners. You can create alignment and drive decisions without relying on formal authority.
- You communicate clearly and transparently, raise risks early and can make complex technical and commercial topics understandable to different audiences.
- You are curious about customers, the business and emerging technologies, and continuously test assumptions rather than pursuing technology for its own sake.
- You use pragmatic judgment to balance speed, simplicity, quality and long-term value.
- You are fluent in English and comfortable working in an international environment.

Experience in one or more of the following areas would be valuable, but is not required:

- Enterprise Android, AOSP or adjacent Android ecosystems, including environments operating outside standard Google Mobile Services.
- Enterprise mobility management, MDM/EMM, device provisioning, application deployment or connected fleet management.
- Rugged or industrial devices, IoT or edge devices, connected hardware or other B2B technology ecosystems.
- APIs, SDKs, developer or integrator experiences and enterprise integrations.
- Platform or ecosystem strategy, technology partnerships, certification programs or developer and partner ecosystems.
- Enterprise customers deploying technology at scale.
Direct Android Product Management experience is an advantage, but not essential if you bring strong Product Management expertise and relevant experience from an adjacent enterprise technology ecosystem.

**Why us?**

This is an opportunity to shape an important part of a category that is still being defined.
Android gives ProGlove access to a much broader technology ecosystem, but the opportunity goes far beyond compatibility. You will help determine what role ProGlove should play in that ecosystem, which capabilities we should own, where we should partner, what customers and developers should be able to build around us, and where integration itself can become a source of differentiation and recurring business value.
ProGlove already has the customer base, market credibility, technology and industrial expertise to build on. Our products are used in some of the world's most demanding operational environments. What we are now building is the next layer.
Over time, success will mean more than making our own products easier to integrate. It will mean creating capabilities, interfaces and ecosystem relationships that customers, application providers and partners actively choose to build around - moving ProGlove from ecosystem participant toward ecosystem shaper.
If you are excited by the idea of turning complex technology ecosystems into simple, valuable products - and helping shape how the next generation of frontline technology connects with the digital world - we would love to meet you.
Join us in building the future of frontline work.

**What success looks like**

- ProGlove has a clear, customer-backed strategy and prioritized roadmap for Android integration and ecosystem capabilities.
- Recurring customer and integration needs are turned into scalable, repeatable solutions rather than one-off engineering projects.
- Customers can adopt, integrate, deploy and manage ProGlove devices more easily within their existing enterprise environments.
- Decisions about what ProGlove should own, enable or partner are made transparently and based on customer value, business impact and long-term scalability.
- Product, Engineering, Sales and Solution Architecture are aligned on priorities, supported capabilities and key trade-offs.
- Customer-facing teams can confidently communicate our Android proposition and common deployment patterns without relying on Product or Engineering in every opportunity.
- Relevant integrations, partnerships and commercial opportunities are identified, validated and translated into tangible product or business outcomes.

**Meet your team**

You will join our Product team and report directly to the CPO. You will work especially closely with the Product Manager responsible for the Wearable Companion, as well as colleagues across Engineering, Sales, Solution Architecture and Product Marketing.

This is a highly cross-functional role. You will bring together customer needs, technical realities and commercial opportunities, while also building relationships with external technology partners, platform providers and integrators. The team values open collaboration, clear communication and shared ownership of outcomes.
