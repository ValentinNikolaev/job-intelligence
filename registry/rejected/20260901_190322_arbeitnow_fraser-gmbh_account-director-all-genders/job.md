**Your role**

FRASER is an independent, owner-led creative agency with teams across Berlin, Zurich, Cape Town and Dubai. We work across the full funnel — from creative strategy and brand to campaigns, content and performance marketing. We bring senior creative talent and different perspectives together to create work that delivers real results for ambitious brands.

**Your role **

- Lead client relationships across key FRASER accounts, acting as a trusted strategic and commercial partner.
- Own projects and campaigns from initial brief through strategy, creative development, production and delivery.
- Translate client needs and business objectives into clear briefs and actionable direction for internal teams.
- Work closely with Creative, Strategy, Social, Production and Performance teams to develop integrated work across channels.
- Manage project scopes, timelines, budgets and deliverables, ensuring projects stay on track and commercially sound.
- Lead client presentations, status meetings and project kick-offs with confidence and clarity.
- Build strong relationships with clients and understand their business, challenges, audiences and ambitions beyond the immediate brief.
- Anticipate challenges, identify opportunities and proactively bring solutions to clients and internal teams.
- Help ensure the quality and ambition of FRASER's work remains high throughout the client and creative process.
- Contribute to pitches, new business opportunities and the growth of existing client relationships.
- Support and mentor more junior account team members, helping to establish strong ways of working across the agency.

**Your profile**

- **7+ years of experience in account management / client services**, ideally within a creative, advertising or integrated agency.
- Deep understanding of how **creative agencies and advertising work**, from briefing and strategy through creative development, production and delivery.
- Proven experience managing significant client relationships and complex integrated projects.
- Strong understanding of creative processes and the ability to work confidently with Creative Directors, Art Directors, Copywriters, Strategists and Producers.
- Experience managing budgets, timelines, scopes and multiple stakeholders simultaneously.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the confidence to challenge clients and internal teams when needed.
- Commercially minded and able to identify opportunities for account growth and new business.
- Highly organised, proactive and comfortable working in a fast-moving agency environment.
- **Fluent German and excellent English** are essential.
- Based in **Berlin** and comfortable working closely with the team in the office.
- International agency experience is a plus.

**What we offer**

- The opportunity to shape a globally relevant social media arm of a growing agency with relevant big clients and substantial business
- A senior leadership team that is close and passionately ambitious
- Fantastic colleagues and a collaborative environment
- Competitive salary
- Employee benefits program
- Chance to help shape the agency and influence its growth
- Flat hierarchy fostering a transparent and collaborative work culture
- International environment with diverse employees, clients, and locations
- Experienced team: work alongside seasoned professionals
- 30 days of vacation per year (based on a 5-day workweek)
- Opportunity for workation from European countries or South Africa
- Flexible working hours and locations, including 3 days on site in our great office and 2 home office
- A great roof terrace in the heart of Kreuzberg and always a fridge full of cool drinks
