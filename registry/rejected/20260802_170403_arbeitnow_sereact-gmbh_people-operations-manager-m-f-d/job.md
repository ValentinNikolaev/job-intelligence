**Overview:**

As a People Operations Manager, you will own and support the day-to-day people operations functions that help our organization scale effectively. This is a highly operational, hands-on role focused on recruiting coordination, onboarding, employee experience, organizational processes, and supporting leadership across people-related initiatives.

This role is ideal for someone who is highly organized, proactive, detail-oriented, and excited to help build strong operational foundations in a fast-paced startup environment. While this role will help shape processes and improve how the organization operates, this is currently an individual contributor role focused on execution rather than building or managing a large HR team.

**Onboarding & Employee Experience**

Own onboarding coordination and ensure employees have a smooth and organized onboarding experience.

Help drive employee engagement initiatives, team events, and internal communication efforts.

-

Support employees throughout their lifecycle with the company.

-

-

Help establish scalable people operations processes and best practices.

Ensure consistency and follow-through across internal people-related processes.

-

Support leadership with scheduling, coordination, and follow-up on people operations initiatives.

Assist with office operations and administrative coordination as needed.

**Cross-Functional Support**

Support ad hoc operational projects and company initiatives as the organization grows.
