**Position Overview**

CLO Virtual Fashion is looking for a creative Brand Communications Working Student to join our Brand Communications team. This individual will support research, copywriting, and event planning in collaboration with the Brand Communication Manager. The ideal candidate will be someone eager to explore digital fashion and deepen their understanding of marketing strategies within a global organization.

**Please note that this is a **working student position**. A minimum availability of **20 hours per week** is required for this role.**

**Responsibilities**

- Work closely with the Brand Communications Manager to grow brand awareness across the EMEA region

- Monitor social media and market trends and the competitive landscape

- Support the creation and implementation of marketing campaigns across multiple channels, such as social media, email, PR, and influencer partnerships

- Assist with the planning and execution of events and software launches

- Provide support to the Marketing team as needed

**Requirements**

- Strong interest in marketing and the fashion tech industry

- Currently pursuing a degree in Marketing, Communications, or a related field at a university in the Munich area

- Excellent verbal and written communication skills

- Familiarity with social media platforms, including Instagram, Linkedin and YouTube

- Knowledge of Mailchimp and WordPress a plus

- Good command of spoken and written English

- Students majoring in marketing, communications, or a related field

If you are interested in the role, you must submit a CV and resume **MUST **be in English.

The duration of the working student position will be 6 months.
