**Your Contribution**

Are you passionate about designing scalable data platforms and helping others grow along the way? As a Senior Data Engineer, you will shape the architecture and technical direction of our Google Cloud data platform while enabling a team of engineers to deliver reliable, high-quality data products.

Working closely with the Head of Data Platform, product teams, data scientists, analysts, and engineers across DISH and METRO, you will build the foundation for data-driven decision-making across the business. This is a highly influential role combining hands-on engineering, architectural leadership, mentoring, and continuous improvement.

**Menu of Responsibilities**

- Define and evolve the architecture, standards, and roadmap of our Google Cloud data platform, ensuring alignment with business goals and long-term scalability.
- Design and deliver reliable, high-performance data pipelines and data products using modern cloud-native tools and engineering practices.
- Establish data quality, governance, testing, monitoring, and operational excellence practices that enable trusted analytics and machine learning outcomes.
- Mentor engineers across different experience levels through coaching, code reviews, technical guidance, and knowledge sharing.
- Lead the resolution of complex technical challenges, production incidents, and cross-functional initiatives while fostering collaboration and shared ownership.
- Drive the adoption of modern engineering practices, including AI-assisted development, with clear quality, security, and reliability guardrails.

**Ingredients for Success**

- Proven expertise in designing and operating scalable data platforms and distributed data systems in a cloud environment.
- Deep knowledge of data architecture, data modeling, and modern warehouse or lakehouse approaches, with strong Python and SQL skills.
- Extensive hands-on experience with Google Cloud Platform services such as BigQuery, Cloud Storage, Cloud Composer, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, and related technologies.
- Strong experience building data orchestration, transformation, and testing solutions using tools such as Airflow and dbt.
- Demonstrated ability to define technical direction, influence architectural decisions, and communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Collaborative leadership style with a passion for mentoring others, driving continuous improvement, and balancing innovation with operational excellence.
