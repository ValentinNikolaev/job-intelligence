- Coordinate with Regional and Corporate Finance teams to maintain transparency and operational efficiency.

- Experience working with external tax advisors and auditors.

- Experience in finance operations, financial planning, budgeting, and reporting (monthly, quarterly, annual).

- ERP experience (SAP / Microsoft Dynamics / Oracle / Datev / One Stop Shop).

- Strong attention to detail, analytical thinking, and organizational skills.

- Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities under tight deadlines.

- Excellent communication and coordination skills for cross-functional and regional collaboration.

- Fluent in German and English, both written and spoken; Chinese language skills are an advantage.
