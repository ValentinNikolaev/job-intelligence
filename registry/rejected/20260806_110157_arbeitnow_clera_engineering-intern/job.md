### About the Role

Join a fast-growing, Series A/B-backed EdTech company building an AI-powered learning platform used by tens of millions of students across 30+ countries. As an **Engineering Intern**, you'll work alongside a talented and international engineering team to contribute to real product features that help students learn more effectively — from AI tutoring tools to study resources and beyond.

This is a hands-on internship where you'll be embedded in the team and expected to ship meaningful work. The internship runs for **3–6 months** and is based in **Berlin, Germany**.

### What You'll Do

-

Contribute to the development and improvement of features across a mobile-first, AI-driven learning platform

-

Write clean, well-tested code in one or more languages (e.g. Python, Java, or JavaScript)

-

Collaborate with engineers and product managers in an agile environment

-

Participate in code reviews and engineering discussions

-

Take ownership of scoped tasks and deliver them end-to-end with guidance from senior engineers

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a closely related field

-

Proficiency in at least one programming language (e.g. Python, Java, or JavaScript), demonstrated through coursework, personal projects, or a prior internship

-

Ability to commit to the full internship duration (3–6 months) with a flexible schedule to accommodate project needs

-

A portfolio or examples of coding work (e.g. a GitHub profile or project repository)

**Nice to Have:**

-

Prior experience in a tech startup or product-focused engineering environment

-

Familiarity with mobile app development (iOS/Android) or web development

-

Interest in EdTech, AI, or consumer-facing applications
