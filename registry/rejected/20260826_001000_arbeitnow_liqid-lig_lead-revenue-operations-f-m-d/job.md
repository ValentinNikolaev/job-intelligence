## Your Role

We’re looking for a Lead Revenue Operations (f/m/d) to take ownership of our commercial operations and help drive our revenue engine. You’ll work at the intersection of Sales, Relationship Management, Marketing, Finance, Tech and Product with a clear mission: to build a scalable, data-driven commercial infrastructure that fuels growth and efficiency at LIQID.

## What Will Keep You Challenged?

-

Design, implement and continuously improve sales & relationship management processes, funnels, KPIs and reports and own data governance for the revenue organization: a single authoritative definition for every commercial KPI, data quality standards and their enforcement.

-

Act as functional owner of the commercial toolkit incl. CRM (data model, object structure, field logic, workflows, permissions and AI-integration)

-

Drive operational excellence and change through automation and system optimization (CRM, automation, VOIP, BI etc.).

-

Assess funnel performance and attribution jointly with Marketing, including each channel's contribution to closed business, and enable growth initiatives with the data and infrastructure they require.

-

Drive the revenue forecast end to end—methodology, cadence, model maintenance and accountability for forecast accuracy—and turn data into recommendations for the management team: risks, opportunities and trend breaks, with a clear point of view.

-

Design and maintain how targets and client portfolios are allocated across the commercial team—customer segmentation, coverage segments, assignment rules and individual targets—and create transparency on existing-client economics: churn, portfolio expansion, and net inflows and outflows.

-

Collaborate deeply across Marketing and Finance teams with regular touch points in Product and Tech, removing blockers and elevating the commercial execution engine.

## What You Bring

-

5+ years' experience in Revenue Ops, Sales Ops or similar roles, ideally in advisory-led, relationship-driven commercial models such as Wealth/Fintech, or SaaS environments

-

Clear communicator with a structured, ownership-driven, and pragmatic working style (full professional fluency required in both **German and English**)

-

Deep sales funnel and CRM expertise, especially HubSpot, plus practical experience with commercial tool stacks, (workflow) automation tools and process performance monitoring

-

Strong grasp of sales KPIs, funnel metrics, dashboard building, data visualization and process mapping; an analytically trained background is a strong plus (for example: consulting, controlling or FP&A)

-

Proven track record building forecast models and owning forecast accuracy over time, and building commercial operating infrastructure in a scaling organization

-

Operative mindset with cross-functional collaboration experience across Marketing, Sales, Relationship Management and the Management team: you steer change processes successfully without formal authority and hold sales and relationship management leadership accountable to process and data standards

-

First leadership experience, functional or disciplinary, and the ambition to take on a small team as the revenue organization grows.
