**The Purpose:**

Senior Fullstack Engineers (m/f/d) at PPRO drive core technical decisions, solving complex technical problems, whilst participating in decisions on architecture and technical direction. Supporting and mentoring engineering colleagues within a hands-on environment. A strong understanding of business context, customer needs, and technical constraints is essential to designing and implementing pragmatic, scalable solutions that address the right problems and deliver sustainable value.

**What you’ll do:**

- Own end-to-end software development processes, from requirements to deployment in our production systems for our key products and services (e.g. accounting and reporting systems)

- Be an integral part of the team’s culture and ways of working, continuously improving development processes and practices together with your colleagues based on data, feedback, and changing business needs.

- Use the latest technologies (e.g. React Query, gRPC) to solve real problems for our customers at a massive scale

- Take e2e operational responsibility for the team-owned components

- Share technical expertise and architect creative solutions while advancing team best practices

**What we look for in you:**

- Ability to collaborate and grow dynamic and autonomous environments, whilst in a small, agile team.

- Impact-oriented, collaborative, empathetic, focused, curious, well-organized, having a continuous improvement mindset and strong sense of responsibility

- Experience leading design solutions from scratch to impact, heavily supporting them hands-on

- Working proficiency in English

- Confident AI-augmented development experience with a focus on a solution quality and cost-efficient usage (Ideally Claude Code)

- Accounting/Fintech Domain: Experience with—or a strong interest in—the complexities of accounting systems or ledgers is a distinct advantage

**Technical Requirements: **

- 5+ years of professional experience working with Java/Kotlin and JS/TS

- Modern frameworks (Spring Boot, React)

- Strong AWS experience, including efficient cost-management (SNS/SQS, Kinesis, Lambdas; Terraform is a plus)

- Event-driven + Microservice architectures (Anti-corruption layer, Event Sourcing, CQRS)
