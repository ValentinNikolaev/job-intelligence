**Your mission as a Senior Backend Engineer**

We are looking for a passionate (**Senior)** **Backend Engineer (m/f/d)** to join our amazing backend team. In this role, you will be responsible for developing new features and enhancing our existing code base using NodeJs, Express, and MongoDB.

**What you’ll be doing**

- Design and maintain server-side applications and database architecture.

- Collaborate with front-end developers for a seamless user experience.

- Regularly review and improve code and system efficiency.

- Develop and deploy reusable, testable code and automated tests

**You will thrive in this role if**

- You have at least 3 years of hands-on experience with Node.js, along with strong proficiency in TypeScript and Express.

- You have practical experience working with NoSQL databases, preferably MongoDB.

- You have a solid understanding of object-oriented programming principles.

- You hold a degree in Computer Science or a related field – or have completed a comparable vocational training

- Having AWS (S3, ECS, EC2), Docker and Terraform experience is a plus

- Having previous experience with NestJS is a plus

- You are a proactive problem solver, always seeking innovative solutions and continuously improving processes.

- You are willing and able to get familiar with new systems.

- You are a team player and enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams.

- You thrive in a collaborative environment, valuing team input and ensuring alignment with project goals.

**How we work**

- We work in small, agile product teams with lots of ownership and fast decision-making.

- You’ll collaborate with smart, driven, and genuinely nice people.

- We keep meetings to a minimum – focus time matters.

- We use a modern tech stack that supports us in building great products:

- Backend: Node.js (TypeScript), Express, MongoDB, AWS, Docker, Terraform

- Frontend: Angular, Flutter

- Tools we love: GitHub, ClickUp, Slack
