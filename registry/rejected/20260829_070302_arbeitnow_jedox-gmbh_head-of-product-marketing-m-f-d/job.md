**What is Jedox?**

Jedox is a leading provider of Enterprise Performance Management solutions, helping organizations streamline planning, budgeting, forecasting, and reporting. Our intelligent platform empowers finance teams and business leaders to make confident, data-driven decisions while adapting quickly to change.
At Jedox, we believe that great results come from working as #OneTeam. We foster a people-centric environment where curiosity, innovation, a growth mindset, and a commitment to executing with excellence enable our teams to create meaningful impact.

**Your Responsibilities**

As Director Product Marketing, you will shape the positioning and go-to-market strategy for Jedox product offerings. You will serve as a strategic bridge between Product, R&D, Sales, Sales Engineering, Marketing, executive stakeholders, customers, and external industry audiences. With a hands-on approach and strong ownership mindset, you will lead cross-functional product launches, refine company positioning, enable the sales organization, and communicate the value of Jedox solutions throughout the customer buying journey.
**What You’ll Do**

- **Lead cross-functional product launches: **Develop and execute multi-channel launch plans for new Jedox product offerings, ensuring effective internal readiness and strong external market activation.
- **Shape positioning and thought leadership: **Lead and support the development of company and product positioning, informed by market research, analysis, and relevant industry perspectives.
- **Optimize the customer buying journey:** Build a strong understanding of why organizations choose Jedox and create a content library that delivers the right value to the right prospect at the right time, helping improve sales-stage conversion rates.
- **Translate technology into customer value:** Partner with R&D, Product Management, and Sales Engineering to turn complex technical concepts into clear, specific, and compelling customer value propositions.
- **Enable sales success:** Create high-quality sales collateral that equips the global sales organization to communicate Jedox’s value consistently and effectively.
- **Deliver engaging product webinars:** Coordinate with internal and external subject-matter experts to develop and present Jedox product webinars for relevant audiences.
- **Drive analyst and influencer engagement: **Identify priority analysts and influencers, relevant upcoming research, and published reports to maximize opportunities for meaningful engagement.
- **Build strategic industry relationships: **Work directly with Jedox C-level executives as well as Product Management and Marketing teams to educate and engage industry analysts, including firms such as Gartner and BARC.
- **Create impactful results across teams: **Bring together stakeholders with a #OneTeam mindset, establish clear priorities, and execute with excellence across multiple initiatives in a fast-paced environment.

**Your Profile**

- 5-7 years of product marketing experience in B2B SaaS environments, supporting mid-market and enterprise customers.
- Strong knowledge of Enterprise Performance Management (EPM), finance organizations, and Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) processes.
- Proven track record of owning and successfully delivering at least three product or service launches.
- Hands-on experience in technology product marketing, analyst relations, or public relations, with the ability to translate complex technology into clear customer value.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to engage diverse audiences and create compelling content; fluent in English.
- Self-starter mindset with strong ownership, the ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment, and a willingness to embrace AI as a productivity catalyst.

**What’s in It for You**

- **Strategic market impact:** Play a central role in shaping how customers, prospects, analysts, and influencers understand the Jedox platform.
- **Executive and cross-functional exposure:** Collaborate directly with C-level executives and work closely with Product Management, R&D, Sales Engineering, Sales, and Marketing.
- **End-to-end ownership: **Influence the full product marketing lifecycle, from positioning and launch strategy to sales enablement, webinars, and analyst engagement.
- **Room to innovate:** Use technology and AI to enhance productivity, develop new approaches, and continuously improve how Jedox brings its products to market.
- **A values-led environment:** Work with colleagues who value #OneTeam collaboration, people centricity, continuous growth, innovation, execution excellence, and meaningful outcomes.
