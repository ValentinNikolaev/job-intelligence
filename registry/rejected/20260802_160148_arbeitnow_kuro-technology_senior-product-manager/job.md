# **The Opportunity**

As **Senior Product Manager**, you'll work on the platform at the heart of Kuro's product, building the foundational capabilities that enable our teams to deliver faster and scale with confidence. You'll help defining the strategy and execution, from the data domain model, project graphs, and ontologies to workflow abstractions and essential services such as permissions, document versioning, lineage, and collaboration.

Working closely with the Co-Founder & CPO, platform engineering, design, and our Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), you'll ensure every investment accelerates the development of future use cases, making the platform more extensible, scalable, and powerful with every release.

# **What You'll Do**

-

Be an active voice in roadmap creation, balancing immediate platform needs with long-term strategic investments.

-

Lead strategic initiatives to capture key decisions across the project lifecycle and evolve Kuro's scalable data domain model.

-

Define success metrics, use production data to iterate on platform capabilities, and communicate releases across the organization.

-

Make informed decisions on prioritization, scope, and sequencing, partnering with leadership on major architectural and new product bets.

-

Discover opportunities with Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) and translate complex operational challenges into clear product requirements.
