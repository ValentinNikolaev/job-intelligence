**Join Our Team as a Senior SAP Master Data Management Consultant (m/f/d)**

Are you passionate about driving SAP S/4HANA transformations and shaping master data strategies? In this role, you will lead critical projects, guide clients through complex data challenges, and ensure seamless integration across processes. As a trusted expert, you will take ownership of sub-projects, deliver hands-on solutions for data migration and quality, and collaborate closely with functional teams in a dynamic, international environment.

**Your Role **

- **Independent Project Delivery: **Leverage your expertise in the execution of SAP projects in SAP Master Data Management (MDM) and become a valuable team member in a dynamic, international environment

- **Master Data Management Strategy:** Develop and define comprehensive master data management strategies for our clients

- **SAP S/4HANA Transformations:** Guide and implement key master data topics such as Customer Vendor Integration for Business Partners, Material Ledger, and master data quality, taking ownership of solutions from design to execution.

- **Data Profiling, Scoping & Quality: **Profiling of data in current ERP system, defining scope of migration, assessing data quality and guiding customers to improve data quality and readiness before the move to SAP S/4HANA.

- **Sub-Project Leadership:** Take ownership of sub-projects in the master data management field, leading their planning, execution, and coordination, while applying your experience in the MDM space

- **Data Migration:** Plan and drive data migration processes with a focus on ensuring data quality, accuracy, and integrity, including the use of external tools for data migration.

- **Collaborate** closely with Functional Stream Leads (FI/CO, SD/MM) to ensure seamless integration across processes

**Your Profile**

- Degree in (Business) Informatics, Business Mathematics, or a comparable qualification

- Extensive experience in guiding customers through their SAP S/4HANA transformations from a master data management perspective

- Experience with topics such as Customer Vendor Integrations (Business Partners) and Material Ledger

- Background in any SAP functional module (MM/SD/PP/FI, etc.)

- Experience with SAP S/4HANA Transformation projects, including knowledge of best practices for data governance and data migration

- Understand various transition paths from SAP ECC to SAP S/4HANA such as Greenfield, Bluefield, Brownfield

- Basic knowledge of SAP Business Transformation tools such as SAP Readiness Check, SAP CALM / WalkMe, SAP Business Transformation Center is an advantage

- Knowledge in SAP MDG is considered an advantage

- Excellent communication skills in both **English** and** German**

- Willingness to travel
