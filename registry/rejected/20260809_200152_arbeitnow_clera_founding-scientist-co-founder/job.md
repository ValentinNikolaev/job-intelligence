### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to join as a **Founding Scientist / Co-Founder** at an early-stage AI-for-biology startup based in Germany. This role sits at the intersection of wet-lab science and computational AI — you will help shape the company's scientific strategy, build core technology, and bridge hands-on experimental work with AI-driven workflows to accelerate biological discovery. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.

### What You'll Do

-

Define and lead the scientific vision and research roadmap for early-stage AI + wet-lab products.

-

Design and execute key molecular biology, biochemistry, or genomics experiments to validate models and assays.

-

Collaborate closely with engineers and ML researchers to translate experimental needs into computational requirements.

-

Implement and oversee lab automation and data pipelines to scale experiments and improve reproducibility.

-

Prototype computational tools and analyses; contribute code or notebooks integrating wet-lab data with predictive models.

-

Mentor and recruit early scientific hires; establish laboratory best practices and safety standards.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Hands-on experience designing, executing, and interpreting molecular biology, biochemistry, or genomics experiments.

-

Experience leading scientific teams, mentoring early-career scientists, and recruiting for scientific roles.

-

Proficiency in programming for data analysis (e.g., Python) and fluency with notebooks for data-driven biology workflows.

-

Experience designing and implementing data pipelines that integrate wet-lab data with computational models and analyses.

-

Senior-level research background — PhD strongly preferred, or equivalent industry experience.

-

Training or research experience at a leading research institution or university.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Prior experience at AI-for-biology companies, biotech startups, or similar industry environments.

-

Experience with ML applied to biological data.

-

Hands-on experience with lab automation systems.

-

Prior founder experience or direct experience scaling early-stage teams and operations.
