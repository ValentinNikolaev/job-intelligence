### Qualifications

**To connect with ecosio it is important to have…**

- several years of work experience in e-invoicing and/or EDI

- experience with the integration of ERP import and export interfaces based on XML, CSV and IDocs

- experience with EDI onboardings, EDI service providers and Value Added Networks (VANs)

- experience with national e-invoicing mandates around the globe

- in-depth knowledge of typical EDI and integration formats like XML, EDIFACT, ANSI X12, and VDA as well as EDI protocols like AS2, OFTP2 or PEPPOL

- excellent communication skills in English; proficiency in additional languages is an advantage (French, German, Italian, Chinese, etc)

- know-how in project management, supply chain management and/or logistics

- strong organisational skills and enjoy working independently
