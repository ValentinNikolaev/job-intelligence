**Become a part of the 4screen team!**

As a **Business Ops Intern – People & Culture** at 4screen, you will support our team across the full employee lifecycle – from recruiting and onboarding to offboarding and beyond. You'll help optimize P&C processes, support P&C administration and contract creation, and strengthen our company culture through initiatives like team events and employer branding. In addition, you'll support taking care of our office environment, ensuring it remains a welcoming space for collaboration and wellbeing. As part of our broader Business Operations team, you'll also gain exposure to various cross-functional projects along the way, gaining valuable insights into a dynamic and innovative workplace.

## Tasks

**You will contribute to our success by:**

- **Supporting recruiting and hiring**: You help manage job boards, monitor and respond to our recruiting inbox, and support candidates through the hiring process, including efforts to streamline our recruitment process.

- **Shaping onboarding and offboarding**: You own key parts of the onboarding and offboarding admin process, keep our onboarding materials up to date, support exit interviews, and help create employment references.

- **Driving P&C administration and contracts**: You support contract creation, help optimize internal and company-wide processes using AI and automation, and help collect and maintain personnel data in our P&C system (Personio).

- **Strengthening employee experience and culture**: You contribute to internal communication formats and employer branding and help organize regular team and company initiatives that strengthen cohesion and team spirit.

- **Managing our office**: You support day-to-day office management and help shape an office environment where the team feels comfortable and our culture is visible – bringing your own ideas to make it a hub for collaboration.

- **Drive cross-functional projects**: You support cross-functional projects, building skills in project planning, stakeholder management, and communication, with insight into how Business Operations functions end-to-end at a scale-up.

## Requirements

- What makes you stand out:

**Completed or ongoing Master's degree,** ideally in Business, Economics, or a related field

- **A genuine interest in processes, projects,** and how People & Culture functions

- Availability for a 6-month internship

- **Excellent German and English skills** (at least B2+)

- **Previous internship or working student experience in HR or Operations** (ideally in consulting, start-up, tech, or automotive) is a plus

- **Structured and independent working style,** with strong attention to detail

- **Hands-on and can-do mindset,** comfortable owning administrative and operational tasks alongside project work

- **Strong communication skills,** initiative and an open and approachable manner

- **A true team player** with curiosity and eagerness to learn
