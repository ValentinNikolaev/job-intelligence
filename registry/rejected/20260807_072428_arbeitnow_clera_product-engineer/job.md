### About the Role

We're a fast-moving, pre-seed healthtech startup building a modern preventive health platform — combining advanced diagnostics, biomarker-driven insights, AI-powered concierge experiences, and personalized health dashboards into one seamless product. We're looking for a highly autonomous **Product Engineer** to join us on-site in Berlin and help shape the product from the ground up.

This is a founder-mode role: no PMs, no hand-offs, no tickets handed down from above. You'll work directly with the founding team, own features end-to-end, and ship real product that reaches real users.

### What You'll Do

-

Ship product end-to-end across the web app, backend, and third-party integrations (lab partners, supplement manufacturers)

-

Build and iterate on customer-facing experiences — biomarker dashboards, personalized action plans, and subscription flows

-

Own engineering infrastructure and AI development tooling as the team and product scale

-

Work directly with the founders to translate product ideas into working software, fast

-

Integrate external APIs from lab and manufacturer partners into a cohesive platform experience

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Up to 1 year of software development experience — what matters most is that you've *shipped real software*, ideally in a startup, founder, or indie-hacker context

-

Hands-on experience with modern AI development workflows and tooling (e.g., Cursor, Claude Code, agentic workflows, evals)

-

Demonstrated experience designing and building customer-facing dashboards and subscription flows for web applications

-

Experience building integrations with external partners via APIs

-

Full-stack ownership mindset — comfortable across frontend, backend, and integrations

-

Professional working proficiency in English

-

Ability to operate autonomously in a fast-paced, founder-style environment

-

**Must be eligible to work in Germany without employer visa sponsorship** (no sponsorship available)

**Nice to Have:**

-

Proficiency in **TypeScript** and **Next.js** with **React** and **PostgreSQL**; familiarity with **Supabase** and **Vercel** is a plus

-

Genuine interest or background in preventive health, longevity, or biomarker science
