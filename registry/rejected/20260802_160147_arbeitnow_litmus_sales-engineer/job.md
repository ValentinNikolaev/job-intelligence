# **Who is Litmus**

Litmus is building the data foundation that powers industrial AI.

AI doesn’t work without real-world, contextualized data - Litmus makes that data usable. As AI adoption accelerates, most industrial environments still can’t access or use their operational data. We solve that gap.

We’re a growth-stage software company helping manufacturers access, structure, and use real-time data from machines, systems, and sensors at the edge. Our platform sits at the intersection of edge computing, AI, and industrial operations, enabling some of the world’s largest companies to run operations in real time, reduce downtime, and optimize production.

Backed by leading investors and trusted by global manufacturers and partners like Google, Microsoft, Dell, Oracle, and Mitsubishi, Litmus is powering the shift toward software-defined manufacturing.

## **About the Role**

We’re hiring a Sales Engineer to play a central role in the Litmus sales cycle. You’ll partner closely with Account Executives to understand customer challenges in OT/IT integration and Industry 4.0, then demonstrate how the Litmus platform delivers measurable value. You bring hands-on knowledge of the factory floor — discrete and batch production, industrial software ecosystems, and the realities of connecting OT to IT.

## **What You’ll Do**

• Lead technical discovery with prospects — uncovering business and technical requirements to shape solution presentations

• Deliver tailored product demos and solution presentations that address customer-specific use cases

• Deploy and support small pilots and proof-of-concept projects

• Work alongside Account Executives throughout the sales cycle to provide technical depth and credibility

• Respond to RFIs and RFPs with accurate, compelling technical content

• Relay market and customer feedback to Product Management — gaps, feature requests, and competitive insights

## **What You’ll Bring**
