**About GlassFlow:**

GlassFlow is the data infrastructure for AI agents in production. It has two products: GlassFlow Tares, which feeds agents correlated data from every system they touch, and GlassFlow Rius, which traces and debugs what agents do once they're running.

We’re a Berlin startup with a Silicon Valley mentality, backed with $5.9m from Upfront Ventures, the CEO of GitHub, the ex-CTO of Aiven, and more world-class investors.

The founders are serial entrepreneurs with more +10y of experience in building and selling data products.

## Tasks

**Why is this role special**

As one of the first fullstack engineers, you'll get to significant impact at GlassFlow. As a fullstack engineer, you will design and implement our web applications and collaborate directly with our CTO. You will join a small team solving challenging problems in the field of Data engineering and building a product that will be used by developers across the world.

## Requirements

**What we’re looking for**

As our Senior Cloud Engineer, you won't just keep the lights on; you will take complete ownership of our cloud environment and play a pivotal role in implementing a mature DevOps culture across our engineering team.

**You are:**

- Continuously refining and optimizing our cloud infrastructure with a sharp focus on scalability, maintainability, and security.

- Managing, monitoring, and scaling SQL & Linux servers, and Kubernetes (EKS) clusters to guarantee high availability.

- Leading technical infrastructure initiatives (architecture design, testing, monitoring, security) and partnering with product engineering teams on complex architecture needs.

- Collaborate closely with our Platform Team to evaluate, iterate, and execute strategies that drive DevOps best practices organization-wide.
