### **The Team**

We're looking for a **Senior Python Engineer to join our Content engineering team**, responsible for one of the most complex and impactful areas of the PandaDoc platform.

We own the content model, the editor backend, data persistence, and the real-time collaboration infrastructure that allows multiple users to edit a document simultaneously with consistency and reliability. Our backend processes a continuous stream of events from the frontend, translating user interactions into consistent, durable state across a distributed fleet of services and a high volume of concurrent sessions.
We ship product features and maintain the systems that underpin them. Product mindset and operational excellence are not in tension here: we expect engineers who care equally about moving the product forward for customers and about the long-term health and reliability of what they build.

### **What you'll work on**

- Design and evolve the backend systems that power real-time collaborative document editing for tens of thousands of concurrent users

- Own the content model and data persistence layer: schema design, migrations, compatibility, and long-term maintainability as the product grows

- Build and maintain event-driven pipelines (Kafka, NATS) that process high volumes of frontend interactions and keep document state consistent across services

- Contribute to the reliability, observability, and operational excellence of a large fleet of Python microservices

- Ship product features end-to-end, from backend architecture to production monitoring, and care about how they behave after they ship

- Contribute to our growing investment in AI-powered capabilities, including agentic document workflows

### **Our stack**

- **Core language:** Python

- **Relational + caching:** PostgreSQL, Redis

- **Messaging & events:** Kafka, NATS

- **Async programming:** AsyncIO throughout

- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, AWS

- **Observability:** Grafana stack (Loki, Tempo, Mimir)
