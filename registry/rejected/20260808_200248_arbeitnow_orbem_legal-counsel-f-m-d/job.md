# **Your Role**

**You will build Orbem's legal and compliance function largely from the ground up, at a pivotal moment.** As we scale across Europe, into the US, and toward new geographies and verticals, legal and compliance touch nearly every part of the business — commercial contracts, corporate matters, employment, data protection, and product & AI regulation. Much of this exists today only in pieces. You will inherit that reality and the mandate to shape it into a coherent function.

This is a role for a hands-on legal counsel who wants to *build* a function, not just run one — someone comfortable with ambiguity, who makes pragmatic, risk-balanced calls with incomplete information, and does the substantive legal work themselves while orchestrating a lean stack of external counsel.

A central part of the role is building Orbem's compliance function: a cross-functional, integrated approach to knowing and managing our risks — across intellectual property, product compliance, information security, data protection, and regulatory readiness. We treat compliance as an enabler of speed, not a brake on it — applying the right level of rigour for our stage and moment, so Orbem stays compliant *and* moves fast.

# **Your Day-to-Day**

**Legal & Commercial (≈ 40%)**

-

**Draft and negotiate our commercial contracts** — the integrated hardware sale/lease, software/AI licensing, and service/maintenance agreements at the core of our B2B business, plus supplier and partner agreements.

-

**Build and own our contract toolkit** — templates, playbooks, and a clean review-to-signing workflow that lets the business move quickly without losing rigour.

-

**Own corporate and governance matters**, including corporate housekeeping, board and shareholder support, and our employee equity program.

-

**Advise on employment matters and manage disputes and litigation** in partnership with external counsel.

**Building the Compliance Function (≈ 40%)**

-

**Stand up Orbem's cross-functional compliance function** — an integrated approach to identifying, aligning on, and managing risk across the business, rather than in silos.

-

**Partner across IP, InfoSec, Product Compliance, and Data Protection** to translate obligations into practical, proportionate standards people can actually work with.

-

**Drive regulatory readiness** for the frameworks that matter to us — EU AI Act, Cyber Resilience Act, export control, data protection — sequencing what we do to our stage.

-

**Own the foundational building blocks** — Code of Conduct and core policies as enablers of trust and speed.

**Orchestrating External Counsel & Lateral Leadership (≈ 20%)**

-

**Manage a lean stack of external firms** (e.g. corporate, US, employment counsel), optimizing for cost, consistency, and a coherent global strategy.

-

**Be the central hub for legal and compliance** across Orbem — looped in early on decisions, not as a final gatekeeper.

-

**Embed legal as a proactive enabler** in fast-moving business workflows, and leverage AI and legal tooling aggressively to keep the function lean and scalable.

## **How You Fit In**

This is an individual contributor role at Principal level, reporting to the COO. Your influence is lateral, not hierarchical — you work across the whole company rather than through a team of your own. You partner closely with Information Security, Intellectual Property, and Product Compliance on the integrated compliance approach, and work day to day with Finance, People & Culture, Supply Chain, Business Development, Field Operations, and our Product teams. You advise the full executive team on legal- and compliance-relevant decisions.
