The Finance Manager (f/m/d) will play a central role in shaping the financial foundation of our fast‑growing scale‑up. As the company expands, this position will grow into a broader role with increasing strategic and operational responsibility. Working closely with the finance leadership at our Swiss headquarters, you will help establish a structured, reliable, and future‑proof financial environment that supports sustainable growth and sound decision‑making. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys building systems, improving processes, and taking ownership in a dynamic, international setting.

## Aufgaben

- Lead day‑to‑day Finance & Controlling operations and ensure fully HGB‑compliant bookkeeping, reporting, and documentation.

- Drive monthly, quarterly, and an in annual closing activities hands‑on, including budgeting, forecasting, and performance analysis in cooperation with Swiss headquarter.

- Build and continuously improve controlling structures (KPIs, cost tracking, variance analysis) and ensure transparency for all stakeholders.

- Manage liquidity and cash flow in a very operational way, including banking coordination and short‑term financial planning.

- Coordinate audits, tax filings, and statutory requirements together with external partners and advisors.

- Support implementation and execution of the group's transfer pricing framework and ensure local compliance and coordinate intercompany transactions, reconciliations and reporting with sister companies and headquarters.

- Implement and strengthen internal controls, financial processes, and risk‑management routines suited for a fast‑growing scale‑up.

- Develop and maintain financial models and forecasts to support strategic and operational decisions.

- Build scalable finance processes, workflows, and tools — from invoice handling to reporting automation — and continuously improve efficiency as the organization grows.

- Coordinate contract-, insurance- and legal management together with external advisors.
