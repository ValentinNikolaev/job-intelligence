## **About the role: What's in the Box**

You'll work with HelloFresh's **GenAI Enablement Squad**, a cross-functional team helping teams across the company unlock the practical value of AI. The mission is simple: identify high-impact problems, build AI-powered solutions that solve them, and scale what works. As a Staff Engineer, you'll work at the intersection of engineering, product, and business. One week you might be embedded with the HR or Finance teams understanding how they work; the next you'll be building an agentic workflow, integrating it into the internal tooling, or scaling a successful prototype into a production system used across the company.

This is a highly hands-on role. You'll work on problems from discovery to production, working directly with engineers, product managers, and non-technical stakeholders. Rather than waiting for requirements, you'll help define them—identifying where AI can create real leverage and shipping solutions that deliver measurable business outcomes.

While you'll support and advise other engineers and influence technical direction, your primary impact comes from building products people actually use.
