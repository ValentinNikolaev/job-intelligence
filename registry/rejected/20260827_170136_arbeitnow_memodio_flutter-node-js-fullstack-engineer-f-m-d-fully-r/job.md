**Where you sit:** anywhere in the world. We're a small distributed team, not a company with a remote policy bolted on. The one requirement is overlap: at least four hours of your working day between 09:00 and 18:00 Berlin time, because we solve problems together, not by throwing tickets over a wall.

## Tasks

Our core product is live and helping thousands of people, but it's far from finished. A big part of this role is evolving the existing app to better fit the people who actually use it: adults aged 50+, often dealing with early-stage cognitive decline. Every improvement you make has a direct, visible effect on someone's day.

Beyond the core product we're building new and adjacent products, including ones with AI capabilities. If working with LLMs excites you, there's real room to shape that. If it doesn't, that's fine too.

**How the role works**

You won't get finished designs and implementation specs to execute. You'll get problems, context, and user needs and the freedom to figure out the best solution. That's deliberate: it's where the creativity and the ownership live. It also means the job asks you to think about *what* to build, not only *how*.

Today our CTO is hands-on in the codebase, supported by a part-time engineer. You'd join as the next full-time engineer on a small team where your decisions carry real weight.

**Day to day**

- Frontend: evolve our Flutter app (iOS & Android), with a strong focus on good UX and usability for people aged 50+

- Backend: design and build our Node.js APIs and services for new features

- Product: work closely with our medical and product colleagues: your voice matters in what we build and how

- Quality: help us keep code review, testing, and engineering standards at the level a regulated medical product demands

- Optional: if DevOps interests you, there's room to shape our cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, and deployment but it isn't the core of the job
