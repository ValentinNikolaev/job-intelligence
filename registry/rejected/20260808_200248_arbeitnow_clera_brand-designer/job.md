### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to own brand design end-to-end at a fast-growing, **seed-stage enterprise AI platform** based in Berlin. You'll shape how people experience the company across every touchpoint — from the website and design system to merchandise, office spaces, and event activations. You'll work with significant creative latitude on a small, high-trust team where your decisions have immediate, visible impact.

The company builds a secure, model-agnostic AI platform that helps enterprises roll out AI adoption at scale. The team is backed by well-regarded investors and is trusted by organisations across Europe. This is an on-site role in Berlin.

### What You'll Do

-

**Own and evolve the design system** — maintain components, tokens, and visual patterns for reuse across the brand.

-

**Ship website updates directly** — maintain visual and frontend quality without a ticket queue or developer handoff.

-

**Design and produce merchandise** — brief suppliers, iterate on samples, and make quality and materials decisions so every piece represents the brand with intention.

-

**Shape physical spaces** — design the office environment, conference booths, and event setups to craft coherent, on-brand experiences.

-

**Lead creative projects end-to-end** — from launch moments to one-off campaigns and collaborations, from brief to delivery.

-

**Move fluidly between tools and formats** — Figma, vendor briefs, physical environments, and production code as needed.

-

**Use AI tools to accelerate your workflow** — identify where automation adds speed without compromising judgment or quality.

-

Act with creative ownership and iterate quickly, seeing the real-world impact of your work on a short feedback loop.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

5+ years in brand design with a track record of delivering end-to-end brand experiences — design systems, merchandise, and physical spaces.

-

Strong Figma skills and comfort working in code to ship production-ready designs without developer handoff.

-

Demonstrated experience owning a design system: components, tokens, and scalable visual patterns.

-

Proven ability to lead high-visibility creative projects from concept through delivery.

-

Experience designing physical artefacts — merch, event setups, spatial design for offices or exhibitions.

-

Comfortable operating in ambiguity — able to write your own brief when none exists.

-

Practical, thoughtful use of AI tools in a design workflow.

-

Collaborative, kind, and able to defend creative decisions with clarity and taste.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience with color token creation or accessibility considerations within design systems.

-

Motion design or animation skills to enhance brand storytelling.

### Location & Work Arrangement

This role is **on-site in Berlin, Germany**. Visa sponsorship is **not available** — candidates must have the right to work in Germany.

### Compensation & Benefits

Compensation will be discussed during the hiring process and is competitive for a seed-stage startup. You'll join a small, senior team with real creative ownership and the chance to build something lasting from the ground up.
