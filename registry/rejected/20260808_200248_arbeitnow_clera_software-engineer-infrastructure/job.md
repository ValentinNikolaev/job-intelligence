### About the Role

We're a seed-stage deeptech startup accelerating the discovery of new materials through physics-informed AI, robotics, and real-world experimental data — with a focus on the energy sector and other critical industries. Our software is the coordination layer between scientific intent and physical execution, and we treat infrastructure as a first-class product.

As a **Software Engineer, Infrastructure**, you'll build and maintain the systems that connect AI models, automated experiments, and robotic platforms. Your work directly shapes scientific outcomes — reliability, observability, and usability aren't afterthoughts here, they're core product priorities. This is an on-site role based in **Berlin, Germany**.

*Please note: visa sponsorship is not available for this position.*

### What You'll Do

-

Build backend services in **Python** (FastAPI, Pydantic) to orchestrate real scientific workflows.

-

Develop data pipelines that convert raw experimental output into actionable signals.

-

Create internal dashboards and interfaces (React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Streamlit) used daily by scientists and engineers.

-

Maintain containerized environments and CI/CD pipelines to keep systems running smoothly.

-

Improve observability so teams understand system state proactively — before users notice problems.

-

Drive reliability improvements grounded in real failure modes and production experience.

-

Support job orchestration and ML systems, including integration with **SLURM-based simulation clusters**.

-

Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams of scientists and engineers.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

2–5 years of software engineering experience with solid Python fundamentals.

-

Experience with containerized applications and foundational DevOps practices (CI/CD, Docker, etc.).

-

Comfort working across the stack and a willingness to dig into unfamiliar territory.

-

Excellent written and verbal communication skills — you'll work daily with both scientists and engineers.

-

A habit of writing clean, maintainable, well-documented code.

-

English fluency; additional languages are a plus.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Exposure to scientific computing, ML infrastructure, or data-intensive systems.

-

Familiarity with SLURM or HPC environments.

-

Experience with React / TypeScript front-end development.

-

Background or strong interest in deeptech, cleantech, or materials science domains.

### Location

This is a **full-time, on-site position** based in **Berlin, Germany**. Candidates must be eligible to work in Germany without visa sponsorship.
