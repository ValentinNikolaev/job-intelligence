**JOB PURPOSE**

We are looking for a hybrid AI-Enabled Campaign Manager who combines strategic campaign ownership with hands-on execution, operational excellence, and a strong interest in AI-driven marketing innovation.

This role sits at the intersection of campaign strategy, CRM execution, marketing technology, data, and creative production. You will lead and execute customer-facing campaigns across eBay’s owned channels (email, push, onsite, in-app, landing pages), while leveraging AI tools and creative capabilities to scale personalization, improve efficiency, and drive performance.

You are both a campaign owner and an operator: comfortable managing timelines and stakeholders, while also building, QA-ing, and optimizing campaigns yourself. You bring a test-and-learn mindset, curiosity for AI, and the ability to turn insights into impactful customer experiences.

- Manage timelines, stakeholders, approvals, and delivery across global teams

- Ensure flawless execution through QA, troubleshooting, targeting accuracy, and optimisation

- Help Shape AI-Driven Marketing

- Use AI tools to improve campaign creation, personalisation, and testing

- Experiment with AI-generated copy, subject lines, creative concepts, and workflows

- Support AI prompt development and scalable marketing automation

- Improve workflows and operational efficiency through AI-driven ways of working

- Bring a test-and-learn mindset to experimentation and optimisation

- Ensure responsible AI usage and quality control

- Support Creative & Performance Optimisations

- Brief, review, and optimise CRM creative assets

- Adapt visuals across channels

- Run A/B tests and turn insights into measurable improvements

- Help improve engagement, efficiency, and marketing KPIs

**WHAT YOU BRING:**

- 3+ years in CRM, campaign management, or marketing operations

- Hands-on experience with owned channels (email, push, onsite, in-app)
