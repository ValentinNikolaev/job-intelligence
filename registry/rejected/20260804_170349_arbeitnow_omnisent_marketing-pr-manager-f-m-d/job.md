- Own and grow Omnisent's LinkedIn presence and other relevant channels

- Develop marketing materials, including product sheets, presentations, and other external-facing content

- Coordinate with leadership and technical teams to ensure marketing content is accurate and on-brand

- Track and report on the impact of marketing and PR activities

**Bonus:**
