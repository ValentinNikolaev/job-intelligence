Kraken is seeking an exceptional Senior Account Manager to manage and grow a portfolio of Flexibility clients across Europe.

The role is responsible for building trusted stakeholder relationships, coordinating priorities across Kraken teams and helping clients grow and realise value from Kraken's portfolio of Flexibility products and services.

Working across multiple clients and markets, you'll need strong stakeholder management, prioritisation and communication skills. We're looking for a highly organised, proactive self-starter who can build trust, create clarity from complexity and drive progress in a fast-moving environment.

Experience of flexibility markets is highly advantageous. However, we also welcome exceptional candidates who can demonstrate strong stakeholder management, commercial awareness and an ability to drive outcomes in complex environments.

Fluency in one or more European languages, particularly German, would be valuable given the importance of the German market within our European client portfolio.

The role offers an opportunity to work with some of the most innovative flexibility clients in Europe and play a key role in supporting Kraken's continued international growth.

What you'll do

-

Own and develop a portfolio of Flexibility client accounts across Europe.

-

Act as the primary point of coordination for your portfolio of clients, primarily Residential Flex and, where appropriate, Generation Flex services.

-

Identify opportunities to help clients grow their flexibility programmes and increase the value delivered through Kraken's products and services.

-

Act as a cross-functional leader, aligning priorities across Delivery, Product, Engineering and Customer Success, bringing together the right people to deliver successful client outcomes.

-

Be the client advocate within Kraken whilst balancing the needs of the wider business.

-

Maintain a deep understanding of high-priority features, client commitments and critical incidents so that you can communicate confidently with stakeholders and proactively drive delivery progress by ensuring risks, dependencies and blockers are actively managed.

-

Provide clear communication, challenge constructively and hold stakeholders accountable for agreed outcomes.

-

Facilitate client governance, including QBRs, service reviews, strategic planning sessions and product roadmap reviews appropriate to the needs and maturity of each client.

-

Prepare client reporting, account health and forecasting updates.

-

Lead commercial discussions where required.

-

Ensure clients achieve meaningful outcomes and realise value from Kraken's Flexibility products and services.

What you'll need

-

Experience managing client relationships in a client-facing environment.

-

Comfortable managing multiple clients and priorities simultaneously.

-

Ability to influence and align Product, Engineering, Delivery and Customer Success teams around shared client outcomes.

-

Strong stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills.

-

Highly organised, proactive and able to operate independently.

-

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

-

Ability to lead through influence and create clarity in ambiguous situations.

-

Comfortable navigating difficult conversations and holding others accountable.

-

Resilient, accountable and delivery focused

-

Demonstrated ability to build trusted relationships.

Preferred Experience

-

Energy industry experience.

-

Flexibility experience.

-

Experience of commercial negotiations.

-

Fluency in one or more European languages, particularly German.

What success looks like

-

Clients view you as a trusted advisor and advocate.

-

Client priorities are clearly understood and effectively represented within Kraken.

-

Delivery challenges are identified early and managed proactively.

-

Contract and commercial discussions are managed successfully.

-

Clients continue to realise increasing value from Kraken's products and services, supporting both customer success and business portfolio growth.

-

Flex priorities are effectively represented across Kraken, ensuring client needs are reflected in Product, Delivery and Customer Success.
