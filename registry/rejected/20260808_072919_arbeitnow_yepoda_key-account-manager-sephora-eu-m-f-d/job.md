## **About Yepoda**

### **Clean K-Beauty Innovation. No compromises.**

Yepoda is a clean K-Beauty brand that actually proves it works. We make Korean skincare without any of the compromises. Everything is clinically tested, 100% vegan, and formulated above EU standards. So you get the innovation and the results that K-Beauty is known for, plus a real commitment to sustainability. And we make it genuinely fun and easy to use — skincare you actually look forward to.

Founded in 2020 by Sander Joonyoung van Bladel and Veronika Strotmann, Yepoda is a **certified B Corp™** and 1% for the Planet member, based in the heart of Berlin. Our products are available through our own online shop and, since March 2025, at Sephora Europe — online and in over 650 stores. With a team of 140+ brilliant individuals from around the world, we are actively shaping Yepoda's story every day.

We’re currently looking for a **Key Account Manager** to own and develop Yepoda’s strategic partnership with Sephora within the EU. This role will be fully dedicated to Sephora and will play a key part in driving our retail growth, brand visibility, and commercial performance across this channel.

### **Your Tasks**

-

Own the day to day of Yepoda's relationship with Sephora EU across commercial, category, and operational topics

-

Live in the sell out data. Track performance at market and door level, diagnose issues quickly, and build action plans that move the numbers

-

Support and execute commercial discussions across assortment, launch plans, promotional calendars, and growth opportunities

-

Build strong working relationships with Sephora buying, category, and retail teams across markets

-

Ensure strong brand presence and execution across all Sephora touchpoints, both in store and online, working closely with in store teams on merchandising and customer experience

-

Manage forecasting and sell through planning to ensure optimal stock levels and operational efficiency

-

Work cross functionally with Marketing, Supply Chain, Finance, and Product teams to deliver smooth launches and campaigns

-

Proactively identify opportunities and drive them through to completion with clear timelines and follow through

-

Monitor competitor activity and category trends to strengthen Yepoda's positioning

-

Help build the retail operating model from the ground up as we scale, contributing to how we plan, execute, and report

### **Your Profile**

-

Experience in key account management OR trade marketing within beauty or premium retail, ideally with direct exposure to Sephora

-

Hands on operator. You get energy from being deep in the detail on sell out, door execution, and promotional planning, not from sitting above it

-

Genuinely numbers driven, comfortable turning data into clear commercial actions and confident presenting insights to internal and external stakeholders

-

Strong understanding of Sephora's retail model, promotional calendar, and commercial KPIs

-

Experience from a scaling, challenger, or emerging brand where you have built ways of working from scratch rather than inherited them is a strong plus

-

Strong analytical, organisational and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to take full ownership and drive topics independently

-

Comfortable challenging external partners and pushing initiatives forward in a structured and professional way

-

Entrepreneurial mindset with a strong sense of ownership

-

Experience working in fast paced environments where proactivity and accountability are critical

-

French language skills are a plus
