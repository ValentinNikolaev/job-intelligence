## **About the role: What's in the Box**

As the Senior Product Manager II for Menu Planning within the Operations Mega Alliance, you will own the product layer that decides what lands on HelloFresh menus every week, across markets, brands, and millions of customers. You will drive the systems and workflows that let our culinary, commercial, and operations teams plan, version, and scale an ever-expanding menu, working back from their needs to build the automation and data foundations that make a larger and more personalized assortment possible.

This is a domain in the middle of a significant platform transformation: you will help move menu planning into a smarter baseline, all while keeping weekly menu delivery running without disruption. You will partner closely with engineering, culinary, procurement, and stakeholders across our largest markets to define a data-driven roadmap, untangle real complexity around regionality and menu customization, and set the long-term vision for how HelloFresh builds its menu at scale.

## **What you’ll do: The Recipe**

- **Own** the vision and multi-quarter roadmap for menu planning, turning the needs of culinary, commercial, and operations partners into prioritized initiatives tied to clear business impact.

- **Build** scalable systems and data foundations for menu versioning, regionality, and customization that make a larger, more personalized assortment possible.

- **Leverage** AI and machine learning to automate menu and editorial workflows and sharpen assortment decisions, partnering with data science to take models from concept to production.

- **Collaborate** with engineers, data scientists, and senior stakeholders across our largest markets to gather requirements, align in direction, and secure buy-in for strategic bets.

- **Apply** a data-driven approach, using both qualitative and quantitative signals to validate hypotheses, prioritize, and measure outcomes.

- **Champion** product craft as the hands-on expert for menu planning, raising the bar on discovery, prioritization, and execution across the tribe.

## **What you’ll bring: The Ingredients**

- **Seasoned product experience** building and scaling technical or platform products, ideally in a domain with real operational or supply-chain complexity.

- **Ability to navigate ambiguity**, shaping strategy in undefined problem spaces without waiting for a predefined solution.

- **Data-driven decision-making**, comfortable using both qualitative and quantitative signals to validate hypotheses, prioritize, and prove impact.

- **Fluency working alongside engineers, designers and data people**, enough to understand technical trade-offs, feasibility, and how AI and machine learning can be applied to real workflows.

- **Strong communication and stakeholder skills**, with a knack for aligning and influencing partners at every level and steering cross-functional teams toward shared goals.

- **Proven knack for structure**, breaking multifaceted problems into parts others can act on.
