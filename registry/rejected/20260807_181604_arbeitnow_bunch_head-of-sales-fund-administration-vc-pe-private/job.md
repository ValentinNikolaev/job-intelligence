## **Your Role**

As **Head of Sales** at bunch, you will strengthen, lead and scale our sales team across key European markets — UK, Luxembourg and Germany. Reporting directly to the Co-Founder, you will turn our high-touch executive commercial motion into a structured, repeatable sales engine across our key European markets.

You will lead Account Executives and Commercial Associates selling to sophisticated decision-makers across VC, PE, and alternative asset managers (GPs, CFOs, COOs, and Fund Ops teams). Because our business model combines scalable SaaS capabilities with high-touch fund services, sales cycles are relationship-driven, multi-stakeholder, and often long. This role demands a leader who can combine structured organisation and process building, relentless deal execution and strong coaching with domain adaptability.

You will:

-

Lead, coach, and develop Account Executives across UK, Luxembourg and Germany.

-

Set a high bar for sales quality, customer understanding, pipeline discipline, commercial judgment, and performance

-

Define and execute market-by-market sales plans in close partnership with the leadership team

-

Build a predictable sales operating cadence across pipeline reviews, forecasting, deal inspection, CRM discipline, and conversion tracking

-

Set up scalable sales processes across the full funnel — from lead routing, qualification, discovery, mutual action plans, pricing, proposal creation, negotiation, handover, and post-sale feedback loops

-

Own the commercial operating system: CRM hygiene, reporting, dashboarding, pipeline definitions, stage exit criteria, forecasting methodology, activity tracking, and tooling adoption

-

Create and maintain sales handbooks, enablement materials, talk tracks, qualification guides, objection-handling resources, market playbooks, and deal review templates so the team has a clear source of truth

-

Coach the team on complex enterprise sales: discovery, qualification, stakeholder mapping, business-case building, negotiation, pricing, executive communication, and multi-threading

-

Build a regular coaching rhythm through call reviews, deal reviews, pipeline inspection, 1:1s, performance feedback, and structured development plans

-

Stay close to strategic deals where customer trust, market context, executive engagement, or commercial judgment are critical

-

Partner closely with other teams, including Marketing, Customer Success, Product Development, Operations, Legal & Finance, and our Co-Founders to advance commercial outcomes

-

Bring structured market feedback into company planning, including customer needs, competitor patterns, pricing insights, product gaps, and operational blockers

-

Partner with Talent Acquisition to hire, onboard, ramp, and manage performance as the sales team expands

## **What success looks like**

In your first months, you will build a clear understanding of bunch’s commercial motion, ICP, product, implementation model, pricing, and market dynamics. You will establish trust with the existing sales team, founders, and key cross-functional partners, while introducing a clear cadence for pipeline reviews, deal coaching, forecasting, and performance visibility.

Over time, you will strengthen the team’s execution across priority markets, improve pipeline quality and forecast accuracy, standardise the sales playbook, support hiring and onboarding, and build stronger cross-functional routines with Marketing, Solutions, Operations, Customer Success, Product, and RevOps. You will also establish the core sales infrastructure the team needs to scale: clear funnel processes, reliable CRM workflows, useful dashboards, practical handbooks, repeatable coaching routines, and a shared source of truth for how we sell.

Ultimately, you will help build a high-performing and scalable sales organisation across UK, Luxembourg and Germany, improving predictable revenue generation and constantly raising the quality bar for complex private-markets selling.
