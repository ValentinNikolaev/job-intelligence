### About the Role

Join an early-stage industrial robotics startup at the forefront of intelligent automation. This is an **entry-level AI Engineer** position embedded within the ML and robotics engineering team in Munich. You'll work hands-on developing, training, and deploying deep learning models that give robotic work cells the perception and decision-making capabilities needed to operate in real industrial environments — think surface finishing, welding, and protective coating.

As an early team member, you'll have meaningful impact from day one, with a clear path to grow into deployment and MLOps responsibilities as the product and stack mature. Equity participation is included.

**Note:** This role is fully on-site in Munich, Germany. Visa sponsorship is not available.

### What You'll Do

-

Train and implement deep learning models as a core part of the robotics product.

-

Iterate on model architectures and training pipelines to improve real-world performance.

-

Support deployment of trained models to customer and edge environments.

-

Collaborate closely with the ML and robotics team on the core technical roadmap.

-

Contribute to getting models running reliably in on-site customer and edge settings.

-

Design and run reproducible experiments; track and evaluate results rigorously.

### What We're Looking For

**Required (dealbreakers):**

-

1+ year of hands-on experience training deep learning models using Python and PyTorch (academic projects, thesis work, or industry experience all count).

-

Experience with Docker for model deployment and environment replication.

**Also required:**

-

Experience deploying trained models to customer or production environments.

-

Comfort working in a Linux/command-line environment.

-

Documented results and evaluation metrics from prior model training work.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience with ONNX or other model export/interoperability tools.

-

Familiarity with data versioning tools (e.g., DVC).

-

Background or strong interest in computer vision or robotics.

-

Cloud deployment experience.

-

Experience with Slurm or other cluster job scheduling systems.

-

Interest in edge computing and on-device deployment.

-

Fluent English required; German language skills are a plus.
