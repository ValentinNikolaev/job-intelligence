-

Support the preparation of group consolidation in accordance with HGB and IFRS, including monthly, quarterly, and annual consolidated financial statements

-

Perform monthly consolidation of income, expenses, and balances to ensure a consistent and reliable data basis across the group

-

Contribute to internal reporting and prepare insightful ad-hoc analyses and management reports for decision-making purposes

-

Compile, analyze, and interpret financial data for reporting

-

Analyze and reconcile balance sheet and P&L positions as a basis for management decisions

-

Analyze and resolve intercompany differences in close collaboration with group entities

-

Lead and participate in projects to improve accounting processes, systems, and methodologies

-

Degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, or a comparable qualification

-

Solid knowledge of accounting standards under HGB and IFRS

Experience in Manufacturing is mandatory (automotive is an advantage)

Fluent in English, (both written and spoken) German knowledge is an advantage

Strong analytical skills and a structured, detail-oriented working style

High affinity for numbers, together with initiative and a solution-driven approach

Hands-on mentality, strong sense of responsibility, and enthusiasm for improving processes and systems
