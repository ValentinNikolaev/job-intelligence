**About Peec AI**

Peec AI is a Berlin-based Series A startup building analytics and intelligence tools for modern SEO and growth teams. As AI reshapes how people discover information online, companies need new ways to understand where they appear, how they perform, and where the next growth opportunities lie. Peec AI helps teams track their visibility across AI search, measure performance, and uncover opportunities to grow their organic presence in an increasingly complex search landscape.

**About the role**

We’re looking for a **Visiting Finance Associate** to work closely with our founders and finance team on the financial and strategic foundations of Peec AI as we scale. In this role, you’ll help build financial models, analyze key SaaS metrics, support budgeting and forecasting, and contribute to investor reporting and strategic projects. You’ll gain hands-on exposure to how a high-growth startup operates, from financial planning and operational analysis to fundraising and board reporting, while taking ownership of meaningful finance projects from analysis through to insights and recommendations.

### What you’ll do

-

Support the founders and leadership team on financial planning, analysis, and strategic decision-making at Peec AI

-

Work closely with the founders and our Head of Finance on budgeting, forecasting, performance tracking, and day-to-day finance operations such as month-end close processes and invoice tracking.

-

Build and maintain financial models related to revenue, pricing, unit economics, and growth scenarios

-

Analyze key financial and operational metrics (ARR, churn, CAC, LTV, burn, runway) and turn data into clear insights and recommendations

-

Assist with monthly reporting, board materials, and investor updates

-

Support (external) fundraising and grant applications, investor diligence, and ad-hoc strategic analyses as needed

-

Take ownership of finance-related projects end-to-end, from problem framing to execution and presentation

### What we’re looking for

-

Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, ideally in Finance, Economics, Business, Mathematics, or a related field

-

Proven experience through 1–2 internships or roles in venture capital, private equity, investment banking, consulting

-

Strong analytical and structured thinking, with the ability to break down complex problems and work with numbers confidently

-

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, you can clearly explain financial insights to non-finance stakeholders

-

Curiosity about startups, technology, and how high-growth companies are built and scaled

-

Low-ego team player who’s comfortable working closely with founders and getting hands-on with operational work

### Bonus points

-

-

Familiarity with accounting basics, financial statements, or reporting processes

-

Experience in a fast-paced or high-ownership environment

-

Strong interest in venture-backed companies, and the startup ecosystem
