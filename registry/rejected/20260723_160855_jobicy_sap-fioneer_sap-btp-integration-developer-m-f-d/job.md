## About the role

As an SAP BTP Integration Developer, you design, build, and deliver integration-driven product features on SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP). You work closely with customers, product owners, and technical stakeholders to ensure robust, secure, and scalable integrations aligned with SAP Fioneer standards.

## Key responsibilities

- Work directly with customers and solution or product owners to deliver product features based on user stories and tasks in Jira
- Own the end-to-end development of software features, including coding, testing, bug fixing, and technical documentation
- Support sales opportunities by providing reliable effort estimations for user stories
- Create transparency through regular reporting on progress, utilization, risks, and blockers
- Collaborate with the CTO office on SAP BTP and S/4HANA infrastructure setup, ensuring compliance with internal standards and guidelines

**Requirements**

- 5+ years of development experience with at least 3 years with SAP technologies
- Bachelor’s degree or above in IT/ Computer Science/ Physics/Mathematics/Engineering or related discipline
- Deep knowledge in SAP Business Technology Platform architecture, solution design, best practice, and handling critical issue resolution
- Proven experience with SAP ERP integration using ALE/SAP IDocs/RFC/Web Services/REST/oData, Process Integration/Orchestration, SAP Integration Suite.
- Experience of developing new APIs using SAP Integration Suite preferred
- Knowledgeable in SAP UI5, SAP Fiori, SAP Business Application studio and SAP’s Cloud Application Programming Model
- Expertise in Agile tools such as: Confluence, Jira and Github.
- Fluency in written and spoken English
