## Your tasks

- Own the roadmap for our Data, Analytics and AI products and prioritize initiatives based on business value and strategic relevance

- Identify data-driven opportunities together with business stakeholders and translate business requirements into AI and Machine Learning solutions

- Analyze complex datasets, develop predictive models and derive actionable recommendations

- Design, build and evaluate Machine Learning models and modern AI solutions using LLMs, RAG and agent-based workflows

- Manage the full product lifecycle, from problem definition and development to validation and production deployment

- Ensure the operation, monitoring and continuous improvement of AI systems in modern cloud environments

- Collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, Analytics and other stakeholders while communicating progress, risks and results transparently

## Your profile

- Several years of professional experience in Machine Learning, Data Science, AI Engineering or Advanced Analytics

- Experience as a Product Owner, Product Manager or in a similar role with responsibility for products, projects or complex initiatives

- Strong knowledge of Python, SQL and modern Data Science and Machine Learning frameworks

- Hands-on experience with Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Prompt Engineering and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

- Experience working with cloud platforms, ideally AWS, and operating AI and ML solutions in production environments

- Knowledge of MLOps, CI/CD, monitoring and the continuous improvement of production systems

- Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to connect technical and business requirements

- Excellent written and spoken German and English language skills
