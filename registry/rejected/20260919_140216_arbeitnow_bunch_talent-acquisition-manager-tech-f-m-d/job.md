# Your role

As Talent Acquisition Manager — Tech at bunch, you will own end-to-end hiring across Engineering, Product, Design and Analytics. You will build high-quality technical pipelines, translate business needs into structured hiring strategies, and partner with technical leaders to make sound, evidence-based hiring decisions.

You will:

-

Own assigned technical searches from kick-off through accepted offer and onboarding handover, turning role outcomes into clear scorecards, interview plans, sourcing strategies and delivery forecasts

-

Build targeted outbound pipelines, engage passive technical talent with relevant communication and develop reusable talent maps and relationships across referrals and technical communities

-

Partner with hiring managers on talent availability, location, compensation, level and search feasibility, while maintaining a high bar for interview quality, timely feedback and consistent assessment

-

Manage competing searches through explicit priorities, milestones, pipeline coverage and risk signals, keeping Ashby workflows, reporting and role-level forecasts accurate

-

Create a strong candidate experience and close effectively by understanding motivation, concerns and decision criteria early, then communicating clearly throughout the process

-

Use AI and automation to improve research, preparation, reporting and follow-up while retaining human judgment, data quality and accountability

## About you

You have 3+ years of experience in end-to-end recruitment, with meaningful ownership of technical roles in a fast-moving environment

-

You are a creative sourcer who develops new talent pools, messages and routes to market when the standard process is not enough

## What success looks like

-

**Own it:** Take end-to-end accountability for searches, candidate commitments and stakeholder follow-through. Surface risks early with a proposed solution and learn openly from mistakes.

-

**Aim for impact:** Focus on hiring work that moves the needle. Connect priorities to business and team outcomes, make explicit trade-offs and keep quality as part of speed.

-

**Empower others:** Invest time, empathy and recruiting expertise in candidates, hiring managers and teammates. Improve interview quality and help others make better decisions.

-

**Always be curious:** Ask why, challenge assumptions and continuously learn about technical roles, the talent market and bunch’s business. Turn each search into reusable insight.

-

**Enjoy the ride:** Bring warmth, authenticity and constructive energy to demanding work. Stay calm under pressure, recognise progress and help the team navigate setbacks together.
