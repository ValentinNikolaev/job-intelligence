**JOIN OUR TEAM**

As a **Senior Manager Cross Channel Operations**, you will be part of our Content Team, leading the planning and execution of Westwing's onsite customer experience across homepage, newsletters, landing pages, and campaigns. - Own the cross-channel planning and execution of the homepage, newsletters, landing pages, and onsite campaigns
- Optimise product sorting for landing pages to maximise relevance, engagement, and conversion
- Lead cross-functional planning with Sales, Marketing, and Brand Buying to align priorities and execution
- Drive operational excellence by optimising processes, workflows, and scalable planning frameworks through AI and automation
- Execute market expansions and coordinate cross-functional rollout plans
- Contribute to strategic planning, OKRs, and continuous improvement of onsite performance

- Strong project management and organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously
- Commercial mindset and a good understanding of customer behaviour, onsite merchandising, and conversion drivers
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills across interdisciplinary teams
- A proactive, hands-on attitude with a strong sense of ownership and continuous improvement
- Experience leading projects and mentoring team members in a fast-paced environment
