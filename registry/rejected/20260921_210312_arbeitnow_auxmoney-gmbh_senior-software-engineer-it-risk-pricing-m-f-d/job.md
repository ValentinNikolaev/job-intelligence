**Your new team: **

As a Senior Software Engineer (m/f/d) at auxmoney, you’ll join our IT Risk & Pricing team, where we build backend systems and services that support risk decisioning, pricing, and data-driven product logic. **Your profile**

- **Backend Engineering Expertise:** Several years of hands-on backend development experience with preferably strong proficiency in PHP, particularly using the Symfony framework and Python.

- **SQL Expertise:** Hands-on SQL experience in application development, with strong proficiency in relational data modelling (table design, keys, and constraints) and writing complex, performant queries to support business features.
- **Cloud & DevOps Practices:** Experience working with cloud platforms (preferably AWS) and modern DevOps practices such as CI/CD pipelines, containerization, and infrastructure automation. Experience with Docker and Kubernetes is highly valued.

- **Product-Oriented Mindset: **A strong focus on building solutions that deliver measurable value for users and business outcomes.
