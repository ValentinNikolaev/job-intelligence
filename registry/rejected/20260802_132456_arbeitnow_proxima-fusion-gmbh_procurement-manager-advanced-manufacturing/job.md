**WHO WE ARE**

Proxima Fusion is Europe’s fastest-growing fusion company and the continent’s best-funded fusion player, as well as the first spin-out from the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (IPP). Backed by over €650M and powered by a growing team across Munich, Zurich, and Oxford, we are developing the hardware and infrastructure needed to deliver the world’s first commercial stellarator fusion power plant.

Our concept advances the most mature fusion technology out there, the Wendelstein 7-X stellarator, through two next-generation machines: Alpha and Stellaris. Our work combines stellarator optimization, advanced computation, machine learning, and high-temperature superconducting magnets to unlock higher-performance designs that were previously out of reach.

Turning these designs into a functioning fusion power plant requires excellence and ownership across every discipline, from physics and engineering to software, manufacturing, law, and business functions.

**YOUR IMPACT**
At Proxima Fusion, we're developing the world's leading stellarator technology. To turn cutting-edge designs into reality, we need a supply chain capable of manufacturing components that have never been built before. As Procurement Manager for Advanced Manufacturing, you'll lead sourcing for Proxima-designed components, magnets, machined parts, castings, forgings, welded assemblies and other critical components. Working closely with Engineering and Quality, you'll identify the right manufacturing partners, negotiate contracts and help industrialize Alpha from prototype to production. This is a unique opportunity to shape the supply chain behind one of Europe's most ambitious engineering projects.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

-

Own procurement for advanced manufacturing, engineered components and fabrication.

-

Develop sourcing strategies for build-to-print components and identify the best manufacturing partners.

-

Lead RFIs, RFPs and supplier negotiations from market assessment through contract award.

-

Work with Engineering and Quality to ensure suppliers can manufacture, inspect and deliver components to specification.

-

Lead supplier industrialisation activities, including manufacturing-readiness reviews, design-for-manufacture feedback, capacity planning and first-article qualification.

Manage contract execution, supplier performance, delivery risks and commercial escalations.

Review quotations, understand manufacturing cost drivers and identify value-for-money opportunities.
