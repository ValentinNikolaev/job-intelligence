🎯 Your main goal will be to build and scale integrated campaigns that generate measurable pipeline for our sales-led growth motion and help the sales team engage and nurture high-value accounts.

To make that happen, you’ll own campaigns end to end and bring teams together around a clear strategy, timeline, and revenue goal:

### **CAMPAIGN STRATEGY & EXECUTION**

-

Own integrated campaign strategy and execution from the initial brief and goal-setting through launch, optimization, and post-campaign reporting.

-

Build and maintain campaign plans covering timelines, budgets, deliverables, owners, and dependencies.

-

Keep stakeholders aligned and accountable so campaigns launch on time and support shared pipeline objectives.

### **CROSS-FUNCTIONAL CAMPAIGN ORCHESTRATION**

-

Act as the connective tissue across Product Marketing, Enterprise Marketing, Paid, Lifecycle, Web, Events, Content, and Sales.

-

Brief and coordinate each channel so messaging, audiences, assets, and timelines support one consistent campaign goal.

-

Partner closely with Sales and SDR teams to ensure campaigns support effective account engagement and follow-up.

### **CHANNEL ACTIVATION**

-

Partner with Paid Media on channel selection, audience targeting, and budget pacing across search, social, and display.

-

Work with Lifecycle and Email teams to develop nurture sequences and campaign-specific communications.

-

Coordinate with Web, Events, and Content teams on landing pages, forms, conversion optimization, webinars, follow-up, blog posts, guides, and supporting assets.

### **PERFORMANCE & OPTIMIZATION**

-

Track campaign engagement, responses, pipeline attribution, and revenue impact against agreed goals.

-

Report clearly on what worked, what did not, and which changes should be made in future campaigns.

-

Run a consistent test-and-learn process so every campaign improves on the one before it.

## **REQUIREMENTS**

-

**🧑‍💻 Technical audience expertise:** You have successfully created campaigns for technical audiences and understand how to make complex software propositions relevant and compelling.

-

**🌍 ABM experience:** You have run account-based marketing campaigns across both EMEA and the United States.

-

**📧 Marketing automation knowledge:** You have practical experience with email automation platforms such as Marketo or [Customer.io](http://Customer.io) and understand how to use their capabilities within integrated campaigns.

-

**📊 Data-driven approach:** You are comfortable using Salesforce reporting and campaign data to evaluate engagement, responses, pipeline attribution, and revenue impact.

-

**🤝 Cross-functional collaboration:** You can coordinate multiple specialist teams, create clarity around ownership and timelines, and hold stakeholders accountable.

-

**🛠️ Hands-on execution:** You enjoy working directly in the details and are ready to build, launch, measure, and optimize campaigns yourself.

### **Nice-to-haves**
