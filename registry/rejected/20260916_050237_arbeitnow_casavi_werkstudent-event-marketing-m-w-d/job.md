**About the role**

We are looking for a reliable and organized person to support our marketing team in Munich. As a Working Student in Event Management, you will support the preparation, execution and follow-up of events. Your main responsibilities will be operational and administrative, ensuring that materials, data and organizational processes are prepared on time. In doing so, you will help lay the foundation for professional events and support the team in its day-to-day work.

Preparing, organizing, packing and shipping event materials

Supporting events on site

Maintaining event, participant and CRM data

Supporting participant management, from organizational preparation through to follow-up

Handling the organizational preparation and follow-up of events

Maintaining checklists, overviews and processes

Providing administrative support in the day-to-day event business
