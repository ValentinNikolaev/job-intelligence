As a Senior iOS Engineer at idealo, you drive the evolution of our award-winning e-commerce apps. We are a fully integrated and autonomous team that balances proven technologies with cutting-edge experimentation, delivering best-in-class user experiences that drive idealo's mission.. We own our code and decide on the technical direction of our products. We're looking for a Senior iOS Engineer to help us take our apps to the next level.

### About your new role

-

At idealo, you are part of a team of highly motivated iOS developers who strive for excellence in product and software development.

-

You work closely with product owners, user experience designers, data analysts, Android engineers and the engineering team responsible for our app-focused GraphQL backend.

-

We work as an agile team where you will be involved in product development from ideation to operating a product at scale.

-

You'll work with modern technologies and practices on a high-quality Swift codebase, allowing us to innovate quickly and efficiently.

-

A refined user interface is important to you, and you work closely with your UX colleagues to make features even better during implementation.

-

You apply pragmatic solutions, find the right trade-offs, and seek to reduce complexity to help your team deliver effectively and scale engineering impact across the organisation.

### **Skills & Requirements**

-

You have a deep understanding of native iOS development and extensive experience working with Swift, UIKit and SwiftUI.

-

Ideally, you have experience working with GraphQL APIs.

-

You are passionate about writing readable, reliable, maintainable, and testable code.

-

You are an open and communicative team player, curious about solving interesting and sometimes complex problems.

-

You are a self-sufficient and well-organized software engineer, and you can expect the same from the team around you.
