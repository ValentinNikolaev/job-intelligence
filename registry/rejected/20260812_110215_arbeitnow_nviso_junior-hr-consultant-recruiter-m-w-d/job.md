## Tasks

You have a strong passion for talent acquisition and interesting ideas how to achieve our growth plans?

As a a **(Junior) HR Consultant / Recruiter (m/w/d), located in or close to Frankfurt am Main**, these would be your tasks :

- Very close collaboration with the hiring managers of the technical departments;

- Evaluation and consulting in setting up the job descriptions and requirements;

- Responsibility for a smooth running recruitment process and a high level of candidate’s satisfaction;

- Direct approach of suitable candidates via tools like LinkedIn;

- Organizing interesting trade shows at universities, job fairs, etc.;

- Implementation of additional recruitment channels and taking ownership for following those up;

- Building up an international network of cyber security experts on a global basis;

- Advertisement and publishment of job descriptions via different social media channels;

- Supporting with innovative ideas regarding the topics Employer Branding or Well-Being or Learning & Development;

- Onboarding of new joiners incl. preparation of welcome packages and presentation of HR introduction;

- Setting up contract proposals, offer packages and clarifying questions in the offer stage;

- Daily support in local Office Management tasks;

- Supporting in general HR administration on a daily basis.

## Requirements

- **You hold citizenship in one of the 32 NATO member states** **or the Austrian citizenship;**

- You finished an apprenticeship or you hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant field;

- You have 1 year or more of experience in Recruitment that has equipped you with the skills, the mindset and the knowledge to support the recruitment of our teams;

- You have done recruitment in IT and / or Consulting and are acquainted with using LinkedIn & other job boards and recruitment tools to manage our pool of applicants;

- You are a “people person” and concerned about your colleagues;

- You are results-oriented and able to deliver within preset deadlines. You value quality and client-satisfaction; above all, and appreciate the value of outstanding deliverables;

- You are able to be on-site (at the Frankfurt office) at least 2 days per week;

- Fluent business English and German language skills (at least C1 level).
