### About the Role

A fast-growing Series A AI model training and evaluation company — headquartered in Mountain View, CA and working with top frontier AI labs — is hiring a **Founding ML Engineer focused on Demand Generation**. You'll own the end-to-end development of AI-powered demand generation infrastructure: from lead scoring models and behavioral analytics pipelines to LLM-driven personalization and campaign optimization. Think of it as engineering meets growth — where the models you ship create measurable business impact.

**This role is on-site in Mountain View, CA. No remote or hybrid option is available. Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States without employer sponsorship.**

Build and deploy ML models for lead scoring, conversion prediction, and campaign performance optimization.
