#### Senior Backend Software Engineer

**About Leaf Space**

Leaf Space is a rapidly growing scale-up company and a leading provider of ground segment as-a-service (GSaaS) solutions. Our innovative and proprietary concept is focused on providing satellite and launch vehicle connectivity as-a-service, enabling clients to efficiently manage their assets and fully exploit data. Our GSaaS solutions have been recognized by the market for their efficiency, security, and effectiveness in supporting different applications, from remote sensing to IoT communications. 

**JOB OVERVIEW**

We're building a satellite-based mesh network that builds on top of our Leaf Line service, and we need a Software Engineer to design and build the software at its core — the ground-segment services, protocol implementations, and network layers that move data between the mesh nodes. You'll take ownership of complex software components end to end, contribute to technical decisions, and help drive engineering quality across the team. This is a hands-on senior role for someone who is as comfortable in protocol details and network internals as in system design.

**RESPONSIBILITIES**

-

Design, build and maintain core software components for the mesh network's ground segment and data-handling services.

-

Implement and integrate **protocols and services** for space data systems (e.g., telemetry/telecommand, file delivery) across the link between spacecraft and ground.

-

Develop **network software** — protocol handling, routing, and data transport — for reliable, high-throughput communication across a dynamic constellation.

-

Design and operate **cloud-based, distributed services** that are scalable, observable, and resilient.

-

Contribute to technical design through design reviews, interface definition and architecture discussions.

-

Write high-quality, well-tested, maintainable code and champion engineering best practices (testing, CI/CD, code review).

-

Share knowledge and support other engineers through code reviews and technical collaboration.

-

Collaborate closely with mission analysis, network, and platform teams to turn requirements into robust software.
