**Your mission**

As our Working Student Brand Ambassador, you bring Mushroots directly to the people. Our products sit in the premium segment, and first-time buyers need to taste them before they commit. You make that happen — at supermarkets, festivals, trade fairs, and on our sampling bike across Hamburg.

- Plan and coordinate tastings with supermarkets, festivals, and trade fairs independently
- Run tastings end-to-end: set up the stand, engage with customers, break down, and clean all equipment
- Take the Mushroots sampling bike to high-traffic spots in Hamburg and create hands-on brand experiences
- Track and share feedback from tastings to help the team understand what resonates with customers

**Your profile**

- Currently enrolled at a university (any field of study)
- German C1, English B2 or above
- You enjoy approaching people you've never met and starting a conversation
- Self-starter who plans, coordinates, and executes without needing step-by-step guidance
- Valid driver's license (Class B)
- Comfortable with a physical role: carrying equipment, standing for extended periods, cycling the sampling bike
- Previous experience as a promoter or in event sampling is a plus, not a requirement
- Available to occasionally work on weekends when tastings call for it
- Genuine interest in Mushroots products and what Infinite Roots stands for
