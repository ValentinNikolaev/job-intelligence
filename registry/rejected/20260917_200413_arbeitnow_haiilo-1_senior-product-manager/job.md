At Haiilo, we're building the digital home that connects employees with the information that matters to them — reliably, efficiently, every day.

As our **Senior Product Manager **for the Analytics team, you own the strategy and execution for our analytics product area end-to-end. You work hands-on with a dedicated product team - partnering closely with engineering and design to turn data into meaningful product experiences that help our customers understand and act on what's happening in their organisation.

This role is hybrid with two office days per week in Hamburg.

**🌟 What's in it for you:**

- Access to OpenUp for you, your family and friends
- Urban Sports Club or Wellpass membership
- Lease a Bike scheme
- Deutschlandticket subsidy
- 30 days holiday + 2 additional self-care days
- Lots of room to make your mark in a growing software company
- Modern processes and short decision-making paths — no endless approval loops

**Who we're looking for**

- 5+ years of proven experience as a Product Manager in a B2B SaaS environment, ideally with a focus on analytics or data products
- Solid understanding of modern product discovery and product management practices
- Strong communication, analytical thinking, and ability to work in a global, fast-moving environment
- Comfortable owning a product roadmap - from identifying opportunities to communicating changes to stakeholders
- Fluent english skills, german is a nice to have

**What you'll do**

- Own and drive the day-to-day roadmap for the analytics product area, balancing strategic direction with hands-on execution
- Work closely with your engineering lead and UI/UX designer to deliver high-quality analytics experiences
- Develop and maintain a clear product roadmap aligned to Haiilo's overall product strategy
- Identify market trends, customer needs and opportunities to drive meaningful innovation in analytics and data
- Act as the key liaison between your product team and internal stakeholders — keeping communication structured, clear and consistent
- Be the go-to person for product requests and ensure they're handled in a structured, transparent way
- Foster a culture of agility, collaboration and continuous improvement within your team
