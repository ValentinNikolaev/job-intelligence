# Senior Spacecraft Systems Engineer – Avionics & Electrical Systems (m/f/d)

- Category: location_requirement

**Deine Mission**

As part of our Avionics & Software team in Munich, you will help define and mature the avionics and electrical systems of our spacecraft platforms. You will translate mission and spacecraft-level needs into clear requirements, interfaces and equipment decisions. Working closely with our Systems Architects and specialist teams in electronics, embedded software and controls, you will ensure that avionics equipment and subsystems form a coherent and verifiable overall system. This is a systems engineering role with a strong technical focus. You are not expected to be the implementation expert for every avionics domain.

**Your role will include**

- Define, manage and maintain spacecraft avionics and electrical system requirements.
- Coordinate interfaces between spacecraft equipment, electronics, embedded software, control systems and higher-level spacecraft functions.
- Support avionics architecture development together with Systems Architects and technical specialists.
- Perform and document equipment and make-or-buy trade studies, for example for OBCs, PCDUs, solar arrays, sensors, communication equipment and remote I/O.
- Translate selected equipment into clear electrical, data and software interface requirements.
- Develop and maintain interface control documents, architecture descriptions and verification concepts.
- Coordinate technical topics with suppliers and assess compliance, development status, risks and open points.
- Support proposal activities through requirements analysis, preliminary architectures, supplier inputs, effort estimates and technical risk assessments.
- Support subsystem and spacecraft-level integration, verification and troubleshooting.
- Contribute to MBSE models and continuously improve our systems engineering processes.

**Dein Profil**

- Several years of experience in spacecraft avionics, electrical systems or a comparable safety-critical engineering environment.
- Experience in requirements engineering, interface definition and technical trade studies.
- Broad understanding of spacecraft avionics and electrical equipment, without the expectation of being a specialist in every domain.
- Ability to work effectively with electronics, software, controls and systems engineering teams.
- Experience working with suppliers and evaluating technical proposals or equipment.
- Familiarity with verification planning, integration testing and relevant systems engineering standards such as ECSS.
- Strong analytical and communication skills, with a structured and accountable way of working.
- Motivation to develop systems for space logistics and orbital servicing.
**
Nice to have**

- Experience with spacecraft power systems, data handling, sensors or communication equipment.
- Experience with MBSE tools and methods.
- Familiarity with SiL, HiL or spacecraft system integration.
- Experience with autonomous spacecraft, RPOD or fluidic systems.
