## **About us**

At GotPhoto, we are freeing photographers to be more successful.

Through our platforms [GotPhoto.com](http://GotPhoto.com) and [fotograf.de](http://fotograf.de), we help professional photographers simplify and transform the way they work — from photo management and client communication to ordering and payment processing. Our end-to-end workflow and e-commerce solution gives photographers more time to focus on what matters most: being behind the lens.

Since 2012, we’ve built a sustainable and profitable business driven by entrepreneurial spirit, innovation, and a deep understanding of photographers’ everyday challenges. Today, thousands of photographers trust our software , creating their own [success stories](https://gotphoto.com/success-stories/) along the way.

As the leading e-commerce engine for professional volume photographers and a business backed by EQT, one of the world’s leading private equity firms, we’re powered by a team of 150+ talented and international people building technology that simplifies photographers’ everyday work and helps them focus on capturing meaningful moments.

Join us on our journey to empower photographers like never before.

**Your Role**

Do you want to move beyond traditional Excel modeling and define the future of data-driven finance operations?

Are you ready to be a key architect of the financial narrative for a high-growth SaaS business backed by world-class PE investors?

**...then you are our perfect match!**

Prepare for a steep learning curve and the opportunity to leave a lasting legacy by evolving our FP&A infrastructure for the next stage of scale. This is not a 'run the model and report back' role; we want someone who interrogates the numbers, surfaces the story behind them, and is willing to challenge assumptions at every level of the organisation.

Please note: This is a 12 month maternity cover.

**What You'll Do**

-

**Drive Strategic Visibility:** Build, maintain, and present meaningful dashboards and reports (e.g., Monthly Flash, Management Pack, Board Pack) that enable the Board, CFO, and Head of FP&A to monitor performance and take action.

-

**Modernize the Stack:** Support the migration of our FP&A tool/platform by defining requirements, mapping data flows, validating systems, and training stakeholders.

-

**Architect Planning Frameworks:** Implement end-to-end forecasting and budgeting templates and processes to enable efficient, accurate quarterly and annual planning cycles.

-

**Define the Truth:** Partner with the Data team to validate SaaS KPIs, ensuring a single version of truth across all business reporting.

-

**Sales & Marketing Partnering:** Support pipeline-to-forecast conversion modeling and commission tracking for Sales, while collaborating with Marketing to optimize segment profitability and ROI.

-

**Operational Ownership:** Manage and forecast global OPEX directly with department heads and support the Accounting team with month-end reviews and ad-hoc strategic business cases.

**What You'll Bring**

**Experience**

-

**The Foundation:** Minimum 4+ years’ experience in FP&A, business finance, or a similar analytical role, ideally within a high-growth SaaS or subscription business.

-

**The "Scar Tissue":** Proven track record of building complex financial models and managing investor-grade reporting (Private Equity/PE experience is a significant plus).

-

**The Builder:** Experience with platform/tool migrations (FP&A or ERP systems) and a history of driving process improvements.

**Skills**

-

**Technical Mastery:** Highly proficient in Excel/G-Sheets; SQL experience is a plus.

-

**AI & Automation:** Proven track record of leveraging AI-driven tools and methodologies to streamline analytical workflows and optimize repetitive tasks.

-

**SaaS Fluency:** Deep understanding of SaaS economics and metrics (ARR, Churn, LTV/CAC).

-

**Communication:** Excellent verbal and written English; German is a bonus but not required.

**Mindset**

-

**The Interrogator:** You don't just report numbers; you surface the story behind them and challenge assumptions at every level.

-

**The Owner Mindset:** You take full ownership of your workstreams and drive change in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment.

-

**Bias to Action:** You prefer a "shipped" automated solution over a "perfect" manual one.

-

**Keep it Simple:** You champion the value of simplicity, identifying process improvements and eliminating non-value-added tasks.
