## Your mission

With the Bikeleasing Group, we are shaping the future of modern mobility and working environments. As a leading group for employer benefits and mobility solutions, we bring together five specialised companies under one roof – from multi-benefit providers and leasing and insurance services to dealers in refurbished bikes. We are growing profitably and are on an exciting technical transformation journey. As **Team Lead Data Products & Analytics (gn)**, you will have a significant impact on how we turn data into real business value. You will lead a cross-functional analytics team, build the team lead function from scratch, and connect strategic data requirements with operational excellence. You can expect a modern tech stack, a direct line to the Head of Data, and group-wide impact across BLS, Probonio, and Bike2Future. If you want real ownership, drive things forward pragmatically, and thrive in a professional environment with short decision-making paths – let's talk.

- You lead a team of Analytics Engineers and Analysts – both disciplinarily and professionally – and actively support each person's career development

- You own the team backlog: you prioritise stakeholder requests, protect team capacity, and communicate decisions transparently

- You drive the quality and evolution of our data products – from KPI definitions and self-service dashboards to semantic layers and AI products

- You build lean processes for refinement, planning, and delivery, keeping overhead consistently low

- You work closely with the Head of Data and technical peers, translate priorities into concrete team goals, and represent the data team in alignment sessions with Marketing, Sales, and Product

## Your profile

- You have several years of experience in analytics or BI and have already led a team or teams – professionally or disciplinarily

- You think in stakeholder value, not tickets – and know when a request needs to be prioritised, reshaped, or declined

- You bring along engineers and non-technical stakeholders equally, without having to translate between both worlds

- You prioritise consistently and keep the overview when multiple projects are running in parallel

- You communicate fluently in German and English (C1 or C2)

- Familiarity with modern data stacks (e.g. dbt, Redshift, Superset) and solid SQL skills are a plus – not a must
