**Are you ready to change the market?**

PAIR Finance is reimagining debt collection: We've developed the most advanced AI technology in receivables management, fundamentally transforming the entire collections industry. Today, with over 260 employees, 600+ clients, and 10+ European entities, we're leading the way in AI-powered receivables management - delivering higher recovery rates while protecting our clients customer relationships.

You're responsible for the **quality of our collection processes and teams**. You develop trainings and document processes that enable your colleagues to deliver consistently high-quality work. Based on sample insights, you analyze gaps, identify improvement opportunities, and directly translate these into optimized trainings and processes. You're the bridge between operations and quality assurance - with a focus on measurable results and continuous improvement.

You work internationally with teams across different markets, expanding our documentation and training materials to new European markets.

**Ready to join us? **We'd love to see your application!

**Your Responsibilities: **

- **Training Design, Delivery & Quality Improvement:** You develop, implement, and optimize trainings and learning paths. Through regular sample analyses conducted by the team, you identify gaps and directly translate improvements into new trainings
- **Process documentation & expansion:** You document, update, and expand our process documentation to additional European markets (such as the UK)
- **Knowledge management:** You build and structure a knowledge database that serves as a foundation for AI-powered tools and support the quality management department in AI-driven processes
- **Data-driven optimization:** You leverage our KPI reporting for continuous improvement of our quality standards
- **International collaboration:** You work cross-functionally with teams across other entities and squads to anchor processes and documentation locally

**What we're looking for:**

**Must-Haves**

- **Experience:** Minimum 3+ years at senior level in Training & Documentation and/or Quality Management
- **Languages:** Fluent in English, German knowledge is beneficial
- **Technical competence:** Experience with Learning Management Systems or learning path design; familiarity with process documentation and process optimization
- **Industry knowledge:** Strong understanding of internationally varying regulatory requirements, ideally in Collections and/or Data Protection
- **AI tools:** Basic understanding of AI-powered tools such as Claude and their practical application
- **Soft skills:** Independent, structured work style with ownership mentality and strong written and verbal communication skills
**Nice-to-Haves**

- Experience in the collections industry or other heavily regulated sectors (Legal, Finance, Compliance)
- Knowledge of another European language
- Experience with Debt Collection in the UK and applicable regulatory requirements
- Familiarity with SharePoint and the Microsoft 365 ecosystem
- Experience with Instructional Design (ID) Frameworks, Content Management, or Knowledge Engineering
