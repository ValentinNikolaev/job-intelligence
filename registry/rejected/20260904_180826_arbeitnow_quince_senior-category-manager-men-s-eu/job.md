Our mission is simple: make really high quality essentials for really low prices, fairly and sustainably. **THE ROLE**

**Senior Category Manager, Men’s EU**

We are seeking a **Senior Category Manager, Men’s EU **to join our growing team reporting into the Head of Merchandising, Men’s UK. This role is suited to a data-driven product obsessed all-rounder. It supports the execution of merchandising strategy across selected menswear categories, with responsibility for driving regional performance through strong product execution, trading, and customer insight. In this role, you will play a key part in scaling Quince’s value proposition in the UK & EU by delivering a compelling regional assortment, optimizing the customer experience, and partnering cross-functionally to bring high-quality products to market.

**Responsibilities**

- Support end-to-end merchandising for selected menswear categories in the UK & EU, including assortment execution, launches, and performance tracking

- Execute regional merchandising strategy in partnership with US Product Merchandising and international regional merchandising peers

- Contribute to product ideation, helping shape new product opportunities and business cases

- Work cross-functionally with Product and Regional Merchandising, Planning, Marketing, Technical Design, and Sourcing to deliver successful product launches—balancing inventory, go-to-market execution, and product quality (fit, sizing, and margin)

- Analyse trading performance (sales, returns, margin) and take ownership of actions to improve results

- Support pricing and assortment decisions for the region

- Own product catalogue management and administration, including product setup, data accuracy, and system maintenance

- Build site merchandising strategy, including PDP improvements roadmap and customer journey optimisations

- Monitor ratings, reviews, and returns to identify and support product quality improvements

- Manage regional product samples and merchandising assets

- Maintain merchandising reports, tools, trackers, and pricing files

- Take ownership of evaluating regional product compliance requirements and partner with Sourcing and Quality teams to execute

**Qualifications required: **

- Role is based in one of our UK/EU offices (London, Madrid, or Berlin), with 4 days per week in-office requirement

- 8 years of retail merchandising or buying experience in menswear categories, with demonstrated ownership of category performance

- E-commerce and/or DTC experience, with exposure to both startup and established (blue-chip) brands preferred

- Proven track record of progressing within an organization and demonstrating continuous growth in roles and responsibilities

- Strong product awareness, with a commercial mindset and attention to detail

- Product obsessed, with experience leveraging data tools and external sources to drive results

- Critical eye to anticipate customer needs (both the “science” and “art” of retail merchandising)

- Strong analytical skills, with experience using data to inform trading and merchandising decisions (e.g. sales, margin, return rates)

- Demonstrated ability to operate effectively in both executional and strategic support roles

- Experience working cross-functionally with teams such as Planning, Design, Sourcing, or Marketing

- Excellent written and verbal communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills with particular ability to influence through concise written narrative

- Motivated by working in a fast-paced collaborative environment, where flexibility to stretch into or out of functional comfort zones is crucial for team success

- Adaptable and comfortable with ambiguity and change

- Bachelor’s Degree, preferred
