**Who we are**

## **The Team**

Document-oriented workloads are at the heart of many modern applications, and in this role you’ll help shape how developers build them using Redis. You will own and evolve Redis’s **document data capabilities**, enabling developers to work with JSON-based application data in a fast, intuitive, and scalable way across a wide range of real-world use cases.

You’ll work on a highly visible and deeply technical product area, collaborating closely with experienced engineers, architects, and customer-facing teams. The challenges you’ll tackle span data modelling, performance, memory efficiency, scalability, and developer experience, with direct feedback from customers running document-centric workloads at scale.

This role offers a unique opportunity to influence how Redis is used beyond caching, while balancing simplicity and flexibility with the needs of open source users and enterprise customers in cloud and self-managed environments. If you enjoy turning complex data problems into clear, intuitive product experiences and seeing your work adopted quickly and widely, this is the role for you

## **The Role**

What type of work will you be doing:

Define and evolve the product vision and roadmap for Redis’s document-oriented data capabilities as well as advanced ways to query them

-

Identify and prioritize document-centric use cases where Redis delivers strong customer value

-

Work with our alliances team to establish relationships with key partners in the document-centric application and data ecosystem, and drive joint customer value

-

Translate customer and market needs around JSON-based data models, document access patterns, scalability, and reliability into clear product requirements

-

Partner with GTM, sales, and marketing to help position and explain Redis’s document use cases

-

Gather customer feedback through direct engagement, usage data, and market research

-

Drive successful launches and measure adoption and impact
