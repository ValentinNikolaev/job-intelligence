### About Us

Soda's mission is to monitor the world's decisions. We're building the first Data & AI performance monitoring platform that spans data collection to automated decision-making. Soda helps customers find, understand, and fix data quality issues by combining automated post-production monitoring with pre-production pipeline data testing.

Soda was founded in 2019 by Maarten (employee #5 at Collibra) and Tom (creator of multiple open-source projects generating hundreds of millions in value). We commercialize through Soda Cloud and we're well-funded, with investors like Point 9 Capital, Hummingbird, Singular, and Eurazeo.

### The Role

You'll lead frontend work in one of our product teams: a TypeScript/Angular application with Storybook-driven components and Cypress e2e coverage, in front of a Java backend. You own the user experience end-to-end, from component architecture and state management down to the API shape, because the backend is yours to touch too. We're product engineers, not ticket-takers: you'll talk to users, shape the design with your PM, and ship without waiting for hand-offs.

Frontend is your craft; full-stack is your reach. You'll spend most of your time in the frontend, and you can ship a backend endpoint when the feature needs it rather than filing a ticket and waiting. We also build agentically: AI coding agents are part of the daily workflow here, not an experiment on the side.

### What You'll Do

- Own complex, data-dense UIs: dashboards, monitoring views, large tables, diffs and drill-downs that stay fast and legible.
- Drive component architecture, state management, and frontend performance.
- Build with a component library and Storybook; keep the design system coherent as the product grows.
- Write tests at every layer. You'll help keep our Cypress e2e suite meaningful.
- Build the frontend of agentic features: interfaces where users direct, review, and act on LLM-powered workflows.
- Improve our systems for agentic development on the frontend: conventions, Storybook coverage, and tests that agents can build against reliably.
- Shape the APIs you consume, and implement backend changes (Java) when your feature needs them.
- Mentor backend-focused engineers on frontend, helping the team grow more full-stack and deliver features end to end.
- Work directly with product, design, and customers to decide what to build. Your opinion matters here.
