### **Responsibilities:**

In this role, you will:

#### **Reader Value and Deal Judgement:**

- Identify why an offer matters to someone trying to save money, and name the concrete saving lever behind the price, use community signals as reporting evidence.

#### **Content Creation and Management:**

- Write strong and engaging headlines and copy, and develop story ideas and coverage plans.

- Own the weekly editorial plan for the German edition and make sure stories land on time and at standard.

- Own recurring shopping events end to end: planning, hub pages, live coverage, post-mortem.

- Choose images, videos, and other media with a keen eye to increase the quality of our content.

#### **Proofreading and Editing:**

- Own keyword research, title and heading structure, internal linking and structured data.

- Edit articles for grammatical accuracy, factual consistency, and style consistency according to our guidelines.

- Brief, review and sign off articles from staff editors and freelance contributors, with written feedback.

- Optimize content for search engines (SEO/GEO) and digital platforms to maximize reach and engagement.

#### **Performance Analysis:**

- Track conversion rates, organic (Google) traffic, and click-through rates.

- Turn those numbers into a weekly decision: what to repeat, what to fix, what to stop.

#### **Collaboration and Coordination:**

- Work with the platform's Editorial department, PR, Commercial and Social to gather insights and coordinate coverage.

#### **Expertise and Author Credibility:**

- Track the market you cover, from launches and price cycles to retailer moves, and turn it into a topic pipeline rather than a reading list.

- Keep a network you can actually call: manufacturers, independent testers, and the mydealz Community. Representing us publicly is welcome, not required.

#### **AI knowledge and Expansion:**

- Maintain a comprehensive understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI), its current applications, and potential future developments, particularly in the context of journalism and content creation.

- Use AI tooling in daily production within our editorial guidelines, and build and maintain the desk's prompts and skills.

#### **Crisis Management:**

- Stay calm and efficient under pressure.

#### **Compliance Monitoring:**

- Ensure all stories comply with legal guidelines and journalistic standards.

### ** ****Your Profile:**

We're looking for someone with at least **3 years of editorial experience in an online newsroom,** who is dedicated to creating exceptional user experiences and driving innovation.

- Editorial: Published work you can point to, ability to find the story in a price, clean handling of numbers and sources.

- Technical Skills: CMS routine (ideally WordPress with Gutenberg), Google Search Console, an SEO suite (Ahrefs or Semrush), price-history tools (Idealo, Keepa, CamelCamelCamel), hands-on AI tooling in a production workflow.

- Soft Skills: Judgement under time pressure, giving and taking feedback, clear communication across departments, ownership without being asked.

- Language requirements: **Fluent German **and English.
