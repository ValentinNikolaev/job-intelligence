The Associate Director, Europe Business Analytics & Insights, is responsible for delivering high‑impact analytics and insights to support Corcept’s Oncology commercial and medical strategies across European markets.

This role serves as a regional thought partner transforming complex data into actionable insights that inform decision‑making across sales, marketing, market access, and early commercialization activities. The AD operates closely with cross‑functional stakeholders to ensure analytics efforts are aligned to regional priorities, launch needs, and evolving market dynamics across Europe.

**Responsibilities:**

Business Analytics & Insight Generation

- Lead the application of statistical methods, modeling, and analytical techniques to support regional Oncology commercial and medical initiatives

- Develop and deliver actionable insights that inform prioritization, investment decisions, and execution across Europe

- Identify emerging trends, risks, and opportunities through rigorous quantitative and qualitative analysis

Commercial & Early Commercialization Support

- Provide analytics and insight support to regional commercial planning, brand strategy, and go‑to‑market activities

- Partner with global and regional teams on early commercialization and scenario planning efforts relevant to Europe

- Support opportunity assessments, forecasts, and market sizing to inform strategic decisions

Market Landscape & Competitive Insights

- Evaluate the European market landscape, including competitive dynamics, access environments, and evolving healthcare trends

- Monitor changes in market structure, customer behavior, and policy that may impact Oncology strategy

- Translate complex market intelligence into clear, actionable implications for non‑technical stakeholders

Data Enablement & Insight Delivery

- Produce and curate data sets required to generate insights across Commercial and Medical stakeholders

- Partner with internal teams to ensure data integrity, consistency, and relevance for regional needs

- Present insights in clear, compelling narratives tailored to senior regional stakeholders

Cross‑Functional Collaboration

- Collaborate closely with regional Sales, Marketing, Market Access, Medical, and Finance teams to understand insight needs

- Influence regional and global stakeholders through data‑driven recommendations

- Act as a trusted analytics partner to regional leadership, supporting both strategic and operational decisions

- Demonstrates strong judgment, problem‑solving skills, and intellectual curiosity

- Thrives in a matrixed, fast‑paced, and evolving regional environment

- Uphold Corcept’s ethics and patient‑first principles by embedding collaboration, evidence‑based decision‑making, and accountable leadership into all aspects of strategy and execution. Operates with cultural awareness and effectiveness across diverse European markets

**Preferred Skills, Qualifications and Technical Proficiencies:**

- Strong background in commercial analytics, business insights, or data science within biopharma, biotech, or specialty pharma

- Experience supporting sales, marketing, or market access decision‑making

- Demonstrated ability to influence cross‑functionally without direct authority

- Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to convert data into clear, relevant, executive‑ready stories

- Strong business and financial acumen; understands how analytics drives commercial outcomes

- Occasional travel within Europe to support regional meetings and stakeholder engagement

**Preferred Education and Experience:**

- Bachelor’s degree required; advanced degree preferred

- 8+ years of relevant experience in analytics, insights, commercial strategy, or related roles

- Prior experience supporting regional or multi‑country teams; Europe experience strongly preferred

- Experience in oncology or specialty therapeutics preferred

- Some leadership experience (project or functional leadership), with increasing scope over time
