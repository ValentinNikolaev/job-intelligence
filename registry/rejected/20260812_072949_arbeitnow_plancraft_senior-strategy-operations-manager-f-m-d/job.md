**YOUR MISSION AT PLANCRAFT**

As a Senior Strategy & Operations Manager (f/m/d), you are a **strategic operator, sparring partner, system builder, and connector** helping plancraft make better decisions, align around priorities, and turn strategy into coordinated execution.

Working closely with our Strategy & Operations team, Management, and functional leaders, you operate across three core areas: **insights & decision groundwork, strategy translation & integrated planning, and execution enablement & company operating systems**. You bring structure to complex company-wide topics, connect the right stakeholders and information, and turn ambiguity into pragmatic systems that work in practice.

As plancraft enters its next growth phase on the path to Series C, this role is central to strengthening how we steer and scale the organization. You take end-to-end ownership of critical company systems and selected high-impact, cross-functional initiatives — from diagnosing a problem and designing the target setup to stakeholder alignment, implementation, tooling, communication, and continuous improvement.

The role combines two operating modes: **running the engine** by operating and improving recurring company systems, and **upgrading the engine** by building the next capabilities and operating models plancraft needs. Your work creates the backbone for greater clarity, alignment, speed, and follow-through across the organization.

**YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES**

-

**Act as a trusted strategic partner and connector:** Build strong relationships across Management and Leadership, connect perspectives across the organization, and help bring the right people, information, and decisions together at the right time.

-

**Strengthen company decision-making:** Connect relevant internal and external inputs, create transparency, and turn complexity into clear insights and decision groundwork for Management and key stakeholders.

-

**Translate strategy into coordinated execution:** Design and facilitate the systems that connect company direction with priorities, plans, goals, ownership, dependencies, and follow-through.

-

**Evolve planning, steering & Management systems:** Help shape annual planning, goal-setting, company steering, Management rhythms, decision forums, information flows, and follow-up to enable better focus and faster, well-informed decisions.

-

**Build scalable company operating systems:** Improve shared processes, communication formats, tooling, automation, AI-enabled workflows, and ways of working that reduce coordination overhead and increase organizational effectiveness.

-

**Lead high-impact cross-functional initiatives:** Structure ambiguous company-critical problems, orchestrate stakeholders and workstreams, and drive initiatives from diagnosis and design through implementation and adoption.

-

**Create clarity and durable impact:** Improve visibility into priorities, progress, risks, dependencies, and decision needs, while building pragmatic systems that are adopted by their users and continue to work beyond individual projects.
