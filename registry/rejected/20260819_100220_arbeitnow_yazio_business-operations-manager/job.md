**HEY THERE**

Help us scale up Yazio, one of the world's leading nutrition apps.

As our Business Operations Manager, you'll improve how Yazio works, from AI-powered automations to company-wide operational initiatives. If you're someone who spots opportunities before others do and enjoys turning complexity into simple, scalable processes, **this role is for you.**

This role is 100% remote and open to candidates based in Germany, Spain, Portugal or the U.K.

**YOUR MISSION**

-

Drive operational projects from idea to implementation, including planning, stakeholder management, delivery and post-project reviews

-

Identify inefficient workflows and redesign them using AI, automation and smarter processes to create measurable business impact

-

Build and implement automation solutions that reduce manual work and help teams work more efficiently

-

Collaborate with teams across Yazio to roll out operational improvements and ensure lasting adoption

-

Own company-wide operational topics such as hardware, software licenses, tool access, subscriptions, insurance and workplace resources, enabling teams to focus on what they do best

-

Ensure compliance with internal processes, vendor documentation, data privacy and legal requirements while proactively identifying and mitigating operational risks

-

Develop scalable processes and operational infrastructure that support Yazio's continued growth

**YOUR PROFILE**

-

Relevant experience in Business Operations, Project Management or a similar operational role, ideally in a technology company, startup or scale-up

-

Exceptional organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple initiatives and priorities simultaneously

-

Hands-on experience improving business processes through AI and automation, with a proven track record of delivering solutions that reduce manual work

-

Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, fostering alignment across cross-functional teams

-

Comfortable using AI assistants as part of your daily workflow and always looking for opportunities to work smarter

-

Curiosity about legal, compliance, data protection and operational topics, combined with a willingness to continuously learn and adapt

-

A university degree in Business Management, Operations Management or a related field

-

Fluency in English and German, paired with excellent written communication skills

**WHY US?**

-

An exciting product with millions of users in over 150 countries, driven by our mission to transform the world through healthy eating

-

A remote-first setup: work 100% from home, join a co-working space, or work abroad for several weeks per year

-

30 vacation days to relax and recharge, plus 5 child sick days if your little one needs you

-

A focus-driven culture that values efficiency over long hours - no overtime expectations, and if it happens, you get that time back!

-

A high-impact environment where your work drives real change - short decision-making processes and no micromanagement

-

A team culture where ambition and balance go hand in hand, supported by strong connections - from our fully covered company retreat to team meetups and virtual coffee breaks

-

Tools and support to do your best work: MacBook, monitor, and €1,000 annual learning budget, plus regular feedback and career development

-

A truly international team with English as our company language and a shared commitment to growth and well-being - supported by Sunday Supplements and Nilo Health
