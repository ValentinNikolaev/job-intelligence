### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to be the **first engineering hire** at a fast-moving, Y Combinator-backed startup building the marketing suite for the agentic web. The company helps brands understand and improve how AI platforms — think large language model-powered search and content tools — mention and surface them. You'll work **directly with both founders and customers**, owning product features from ambiguous idea all the way to live, value-delivering product.

The work spans product thinking, frontend, backend systems, LLM pipelines, data, and real customer conversations. If you thrive in early-stage environments where you shape what gets built and how, this role is for you.

### What You'll Do

-

Own full product features end to end — identify what to build, ship it, and keep iterating until it creates real customer value.

-

Work side by side with the founders and customers on a daily basis.

-

Span the full stack: frontend UI, backend systems, LLM/agentic pipelines, and data infrastructure.

-

Talk directly to customers, translate those conversations into product decisions, and close the loop.

-

Design simple, intuitive UX for complex AI-driven workflows.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Demonstrated track record of shipping production SaaS features — you know what "done" looks like in a real product.

-

Comfortable owning full-stack product work from an ambiguous problem all the way to a live feature.

-

Strong instinct for designing clean, usable UX on top of complex workflows.

-

Solid experience with **TypeScript** and **React**.

-

Has shipped a product from 0 to 1 before (side project, startup, or early-stage role counts).

**Nice to Have:**

-

Hands-on experience with **Vite, Supabase, Vercel,** and/or **ClickHouse**.

-

Exposure to **LLMs and agentic workflows** — either in production or in the way you personally work.

-

Comfortable with the pace and ambiguity of an early-stage startup environment.

**Soft skills:**

-

Product-minded generalist who wants to own outcomes, not just tasks.

-

Enjoys direct customer contact and turning feedback into product.

-

Based in Munich or genuinely willing to relocate — this is an on-site role.
