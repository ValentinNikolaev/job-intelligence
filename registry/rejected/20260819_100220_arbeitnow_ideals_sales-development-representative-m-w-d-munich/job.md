### Get to know us

Ideals is a global B2B SaaS product company recognized as[ the most highly rated and customer-centric brand](https://www.g2.com/categories/virtual-data-room?tab=highest_rated) in the secure business collaboration market. Trusted by over 2,000,000 users from 300,000 companies globally, we help people run high-stakes processes and make important decisions with less stress, higher quality, and shorter hours.

[Ideals Virtual Data Room (VDR)](https://www.idealsvdr.com/): Secure document sharing and collaboration for due diligence, fundraising, corporate reporting, licensing, clinical trials, and other complex transactions.

After 18 years in the market, we grow sustainably at 30%+ YoY and are now scaling 5x globally to disrupt the VDR category and reach unicorn status by 2029.

### The role

We continue to grow our Sales team in Munich and are hiring **Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)** to support the rapid expansion of the Ideals VDR business unit across the Corporate segment in Germany.

Ideals ranks in the top 10% of all companies on RepVue, as rated by sales professionals. Backed by a top-tier product, supportive leadership, and a proven global go-to-market playbook, you will focus on driving new customer adoption in the DACH region, targeting key decision-makers in Corporate Development, Legal, M&A, Strategy, and C-Suite, where secure, high-stakes deal-making and collaboration are critical.

This is your opportunity to:

-

Represent a market-leading VDR product trusted by 300,000+ companies worldwide

-

Pioneer our DACH corporate growth while fast-tracking your tech-sales career through dedicated mentorship and enablement

-

Work hybrid out of Munich in a high-performing, global team of 500+ people across 30+ countries

The role is mostly remote, with weekly (once per week) meet-ups in our co-working space in Munich.

**Tech stack we use:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, HubSpot, Apollo, PhantomBuster, Woodpecker, Aircall.

*Please apply with an English version of your CV. The hiring process will be conducted in English as this is Ideals’ business language. *
