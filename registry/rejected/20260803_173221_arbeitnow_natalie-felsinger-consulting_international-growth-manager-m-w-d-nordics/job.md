On behalf of a fast-growing European HealthTech company, we are looking for an International Growth Manager (m/w/d) to lead its market entry and expansion across the **Nordic region**.

- **Fluency in English and Swedish or Danish**

- Finnish and German language skills are a strong advantage

- A proven track record of launching or scaling businesses in new markets

- Ideally, hands-on experience in Sweden, Denmark, or Finland

- A strong understanding of the commercial, cultural, and regulatory dynamics of the Nordic markets

- Excellent analytical skills and confidence in developing business cases and unit-economics models

- The ability to translate data and complex market insights into clear strategic recommendations

- A structured and systems-oriented approach, with a focus on creating scalable solutions rather than one-off processes

- An entrepreneurial mindset, strong ownership, and a clear bias for action

- The ability to operate effectively in ambiguous and fast-changing environments

- Confidence in working with senior stakeholders and aligning cross-functional teams

This is a key position within the international growth strategy of an ambitious European HealthTech scale-up.
