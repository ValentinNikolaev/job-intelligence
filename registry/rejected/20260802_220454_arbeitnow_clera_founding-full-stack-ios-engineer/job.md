### About the Role

This is a founding engineering opportunity at a Y Combinator–backed consumer wearable startup operating at the intersection of biometrics, emotional intelligence, and mobile software. The company is building a wearable device that reads emotional states in real time — think of it as a fitness tracker, but for your EQ — powered by a proprietary computational neuroscience inference model processing over 150 audio and biometric signals.

As the **Founding Full-Stack iOS Engineer**, you will own the companion iOS app end-to-end. This is a high-ownership, high-impact role: you'll be one of the first engineers at the company, expected to grow into a leadership position as the team scales. Prototypes are already on users' wrists — this is a real product with real momentum.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the companion iOS app end-to-end, from architecture decisions to pixel-perfect UI delivery

-

Surface emotional-state outputs (energy, mood, focus scores) from the backend inference model in a clear, compelling, and polished consumer experience

-

Collaborate closely with the founding team — including hardware, neuroscience, and design — to shape the full product experience

-

Build and maintain full-stack integrations between the iOS app and backend services

-

Set engineering standards and best practices as the mobile org grows around you

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

Several years of professional experience shipping consumer-grade iOS apps (no fresh graduates)

-

Strong proficiency in **Swift** and the iOS ecosystem

-

Full-stack capability — comfortable working across mobile and backend layers

-

Track record of owning and delivering polished, production-quality mobile products

-

Self-directed and comfortable working in a fast-moving, early-stage environment

**Nice to Have**

-

Experience with health, fitness, or wearable consumer apps

-

Familiarity with biometric data, Bluetooth/BLE hardware integration, or real-time data streaming

-

Prior founding or very early-stage startup experience
