**The role **

We are seeking a skilled **Analog/Mixed-Signal Design Engineer** at Black Semiconductor to design, simulate, and validate analog and mixed-signal building blocks that support our next-generation Optical Compute Interconnect (OCI) products. You will develop power management, biasing, monitoring, and auxiliary analog circuits that enable the reliable operation of high-speed optical transceivers and integrated photonic systems.

Working closely with analog, digital, photonics, packaging, and systems teams, you will contribute from concept through silicon validation, helping deliver robust analog IP for future OCI platforms.

**To join our team, you should be excited to **

-

Design analog and mixed-signal IP including LDOs, bandgap references, bias generators, voltage/current references, temperature sensors, POR circuits, oscillators, and monitoring circuits.

-

Develop mixed-signal control blocks such as DACs, ADCs, calibration circuits, state machines, and digital control interfaces where required.

-

Perform transistor-level simulations including DC, AC, transient, noise, stability, PVT, Monte Carlo, and post-layout verification.

-

Optimize circuits for power, area, accuracy, noise, PSRR, transient response, and robustness across process and temperature variations.

-

Support silicon bring-up, characterization, debugging, and correlation of measured results with simulation.

-

Work closely with analog, digital, photonics, packaging, layout, and test teams to deliver robust analog IP from concept through silicon validation.

**Your Qualifications**

-

Bachelor’s, Master’s, or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering or a related discipline with 4–5 years of industry experience in analog or mixed-signal IC design (or equivalent demonstrated impact).

-

Strong understanding of CMOS analog design fundamentals, including feedback, stability, noise, matching, power management, device characteristics, layout effects, parasitics, and post-layout verification.

-

Proven experience designing analog and mixed-signal blocks such as LDOs, bandgap references, bias circuits, calibration and monitoring circuits, temperature sensors, DACs, or ADCs.

-

Experience with transistor-level simulation and verification flows, including DC/AC, transient, noise, stability, PVT, Monte Carlo analysis, and pre-/post-layout verification.

-

Proficiency with analog and mixed-signal design tools, including Cadence Virtuoso/ADE/Spectre or equivalent SPICE simulators, Verilog-A/Verilog-AMS, Calibre DRC/LVS and parasitic extraction flows, and MATLAB and/or Python for analysis and automation.

-

Experience with silicon bring-up, characterization, debugging, and correlation of measured results with simulation data.

-

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across analog, digital, photonics, packaging, layout, and test teams.

-

Experience with high-speed optical transceivers, integrated photonic systems, mixed-signal SoCs, or developing calibration, monitoring, and power management circuits for complex SoCs **will be considered a plus.**

-

Experience taking analog IP through tapeout, silicon validation, and production readiness **will be considered a plus.**
