## **About the role**

As a **Lead iOS Engineer**, you will own the technical direction of our iOS platform, set engineering standards, and mentor a team of engineers while remaining hands-on with the codebase. You will drive architecture decisions, ensure scalability and performance, and partner with product and cross-functional leadership to shape the mobile roadmap.

## **Key Responsibilities**

-

**Define and own** the technical vision, architecture, and long-term roadmap for the iOS platform.

-

**Lead, mentor, and grow** a team of iOS engineers; set coding standards and review practices.

-

**Make high-impact **architectural decisions and drive modularization/scalability across the codebase.

-

**Own CI/CD strategy**, release management, and deployment reliability.

-

**Partner with product and design leadership** to translate business goals into technical execution.

-

**Establish **observability, performance budgets, and quality gates.

-

**Remain hands-on**: contribute to critical features and complex problem-solving.

## **Qualifications**

-

**8+ years of iOS development experience, including 2+ years in a lead** or tech-lead capacity.

-

Proven track record shipping and scaling** B2C subscription-based apps** at significant user volume.

-

Deep expertise in **Swift, Swift Concurrency**, and advanced language features; ability to set idiomatic standards across a team.

-

Mastery of **StoreKit 2,** **subscription monetization architecture**, entitlement/receipt validation at scale, paywall experimentation, and pricing/offer strategy.

-

**Strong architectural leadership:** large-scale modular architectures, dependency management (SPM), design systems, and cross-team API contracts.

-

**Experience owning CI/CD** at scale (Xcode Cloud, Fastlane, GitHub Actions/Bitrise), automated release trains, phased rollouts, and code-signing automation.

-

**Deep performance engineering:** app startup, memory optimization, rendering pipeline, and Instruments-driven diagnosis.

-

**Comprehensive testing strategy ownership: **unit, UI, snapshot, and contract testing with meaningful coverage goals.

-

**Experience integrating AI-driven product features **and championing AI-assisted development practices across a team.

-

Strong technical leadership, mentorship, stakeholder communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills.
