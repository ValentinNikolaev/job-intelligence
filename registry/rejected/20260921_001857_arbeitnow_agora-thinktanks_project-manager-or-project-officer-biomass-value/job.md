Reaching a climate-neutral industry will require unprecedented investments in a wide range of GHG mitigation technologies, due to the sector’s inherent complexity and regional diversity. While a significant share of emissions can be reduced by advancing energy efficiency and expanding direct electrification powered by renewable energy, there is also the need for more complex and costly measures as well as alternative feedstocks for processes, where biomass and hydrogen technologies can be implemented. To tap into this potential, climate-effective biomass uses in industry require thorough understanding of availability and costs across different regions to give recommendations on policies that help to decarbonise industry in a competitive way, incorporating ecological sustainability boundaries. Under the umbrella of the Agora Think Tanks, Agora Industry develops politically feasible and science-based strategies and solutions for the transformation to climate neutrality – in Germany, Europe, and internationally. As a think tank and policy lab we facilitate a productive exchange of ideas amongst stakeholders from politics, business, science and civil society. **Project Manager or Project Officer Biomass Value Chains (f/m/d)**
Full- or part-time (min. 32 hours)– based in Berlin

You will work on the supply side of biomass as a feedstock for hard-to-electrify applications in industry. The geographical scope will be global, covering different conditions and contexts that vary across geographies. This position is part of Agora Industry’s central Technologies & Analysis team, working closely with regional teams.

**Your responsibilities include**

- Contributing to the development and implementation of international projects focused on climate-effective biomass solutions for industrial decarbonisation, encompassing farm- and forest-level availability, logistics and conversion technologies, as well as biomass supply for industrial carbon dioxide removal (CDR).
- Analysing biomass supply costs, availability and potentials for different agriculture and forestry products across multiple geographies, taking into account competing uses in different sectors and market implications.
- Conducting and coordinating projects on biomass for industrial applications and related technological enablers and policy support mechanisms
- Analysing policy and regulatory gaps that hinder biomass use in various regions with a focus on economic, regulatory, and governance frameworks.
- Monitoring the development of technology and business models related to the scaling, mobilization and system integration of biomass value chains and logistics.
- Supporting stakeholder engagement with policymakers, industry, civil society, funders, among others, to inform discussions around biomass applications across different industry sectors.
- Pro-actively contributing to building partnerships and working closely with network partners across the globe.
- Liaising with expert colleagues across the Agora Think Tanks (including Agora Agriculture) to facilitate their contributions to projects and knowledge sharing across different regions.
- Actively contributing to internal discussions.
- Managing subcontractors including preparing agreements and work plans, supervising and guiding project progress, and managing the quality of deliverables.
- As **Project Officer, **the focus is on contributing to the technical management of specific projects with smaller budget, largely independent research, contributing to the conceptualisation and development of complex topics, and of project-related reporting and communication.
- As **Project Manager**, the focus is on technical management and responsibility for specific projects with smaller budgets, independent comprehensive research, conceptualisation and development of complex topics, technical supervision of students and interns, and project-related reporting and communication.

**We are looking for**

- A Master’s degree in energy, environmental engineering or forestry/agriculture engineering, natural sciences, renewable energies, agricultural economist, or similar
- As **Project Officer**: Preferably 2 years of experience (including internships, student work or post-graduate roles) in the field of quantitative analysis of biomass systems.
- As **Project Manager**: At least 4 years of overall professional experience, with 2 years specifically in the field of biomass
- A proven track record and expertise in biomass supply cost, transport logistics and conversion technologies.
- Solid knowledge of the farming and forestry sectors.
- Familiarity with agricultural systems and ideally forest systems in different world regions is a plus.
- Good background understanding of industrial transformation is a plus.
- Experience with Life-Cycle Assessment is a plus.
- Familiarity with sustainable biomass certification and deforestation-free frameworks is a plus.
- Strong networking skills and the ability to engage with business, industry associations, research partners and policy makers.
- A creative and result-oriented mind, capable of developing new ideas and proposals with a view to having impact.
- Outstanding communication skills, both verbal and written; track-record in writing high quality documents.
- Full command of the English language in reading and writing, knowledge of other languages is a plus.
- Willingness to travel outside Europe.
