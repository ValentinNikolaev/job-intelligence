**Your mission**

As a **working student in Marketing & Communications**, you'll be part of our marketing team and help shape how Rail-Flow shows up in the market – on LinkedIn, at fairs and events, in our newsletter, and in the way we talk about our products. In a small team, you'll take on real tasks, see your work go live, and get regular feedback.

- **Content creation:** You create content for LinkedIn, from copywriting to visuals and short animated videos.
- **Fairs & events:** You help organise our presence at industry fairs and support internal and external events – including railXchange, our own conference on digitalisation and AI in rail logistics.

- **Newsletter & CRM:** You manage our monthly newsletter and maintain data in our CRM system.

Short decision paths, real ownership, AI as a daily companion. You bring what you already know – you'll pick up the rest here.
