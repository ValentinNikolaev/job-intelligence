### About the Role

We're a fast-growing AdTech company (50–100 people) that combines human-made ad creative production with AI-driven testing and learning to help brands scale winning paid social campaigns. Think of us as a creative intelligence layer for performance marketing — we make ads, and every ad teaches the next one.

As our **Growth Marketing Lead**, you'll sit at the intersection of strategy, media, and creative intelligence. You'll own high-impact client accounts, steer substantial media budgets, and work cross-functionally with forward-deployed creative teams and media buyers to drive efficient, scalable growth. This is a primarily client-facing and strategic role — but you're equally comfortable rolling up your sleeves and getting into the tactical weeds.

**This role is based on-site in New York City. Visa sponsorship is not available.**

### What You'll Do

-

Oversee annual media budgets ranging from **$5M to $50M** across Meta, TikTok, and Google — driving efficient spend and account scalability

-

Lead client relationships through regular meetings, strategy sessions, and quarterly business reviews (QBRs)

-

Develop cohesive growth strategies that place **creative at the center** of paid advertising programs

-

Translate creative performance data into clear, actionable insights for clients, creative teams, and media buyers

-

Identify and execute optimizations across funnel strategy, signal quality, offer testing, and measurement frameworks

-

Partner closely with on-site creatives and media buyers to turn performance insights into high-performing content and scaled campaigns

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

**4–8 years** of paid media experience; agency and/or consumer app background strongly preferred

-

Deep expertise in **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** paid advertising platforms and campaign management

-

Proven track record managing accounts with **seven-figure+ annual media budgets** ($1M+)

-

Experience running paid media campaigns across **Meta, TikTok, and Google**

-

Strong analytical skills — ability to translate performance data into strategic recommendations

-

Experience developing growth strategies that tightly integrate creative performance with media buying

-

Excellent presentation and communication skills; comfortable leading QBRs and executive stakeholder meetings

-

Experience optimizing media efficiency beyond standard dashboard metrics (funnel strategy, offer testing, incrementality, etc.)

**Nice to Have:**

-

Deep understanding of mobile advertising measurement systems including **SKAN, AEM, and MMPs**

-

Experience working in agency environments managing multiple client accounts simultaneously

-

Background in consumer app or mobile-first product growth marketing
