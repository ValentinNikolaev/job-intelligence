## **Product Marketing Intern (f/m/d)**

**Overview**

We are looking for a motivated **Product Marketing Intern (m/f/d)** to support how our AI robotics products are positioned and brought to market. You will work closely with our Product, Sales, and Engineering teams to help communicate the value of our technology and support go-to-market activities.

This internship is an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in **B2B product marketing for cutting-edge AI and robotics systems** used by enterprise logistics companies. You will contribute to messaging, content creation, market research, and sales enablement while learning how technical products are successfully introduced to the market.

# **Your Responsibilities**

## **Messaging & Positioning**

Support the development of product messaging and value propositions for different use cases and customer segments

Help translate technical AI and robotics capabilities into clear and engaging communication materials

Assist in maintaining consistent messaging across presentations, website content, and marketing materials

## **Go-to-Market Support**

Assist with product and feature launch preparations

Help coordinate internal communications between Product, Sales, and Marketing teams

Support campaign execution and rollout activities

## **Sales Enablement**

Help create and update sales materials such as pitch decks, one-pagers, and product summaries

Support the preparation of demo narratives and use-case explanations

Assist in maintaining documentation used by Sales and Business Development teams

## **Content & Market Education**

Support the creation of product-related content such as blog posts, presentations, and solution briefs

Assist in preparing materials for webinars, technical talks, and events

Help communicate product updates and key features to internal and external audiences

## **Customer & Market Insights**

Conduct research on competitors, market trends, and emerging technologies in robotics and logistics automation

Support the collection and organization of feedback from customers and sales teams

Help summarize insights that can inform product positioning and marketing initiatives

# **Qualifications**

## **Education**

Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in **Business, Marketing, Engineering, Robotics, or a related field**

## **Skills & Interests**

Strong interest in **technology, robotics, AI, or logistics automation**

Clear communication and storytelling skills

Ability to structure information and present ideas clearly

Comfort working with tools such as **Google Slides, Notion, or similar tools**

Curiosity to learn about complex technical products and how they create value for customers

## **Soft Skills**

Proactive and hands-on mindset

Structured and detail-oriented work style

Strong collaboration skills

Fluent in **English** (German is a plus)

# **Our Interview Process**

**Application**

**Take-home assignment**

You’ll work on a short, role-relevant task with clear instructions and a deadline.

**Cultural fit interview (40 min)**

A conversation about how you work, what motivates you, and what you’re looking for. Expect CV highlights, your “why,” and time for your questions.

**Technical fit interview (45 min)**

A deeper discussion about how you approach problems and communicate ideas.

**On-site visit**

Meet the team in our office and collaborate on a real challenge similar to the work you would do in the role. You’ll also meet one of our founders.
