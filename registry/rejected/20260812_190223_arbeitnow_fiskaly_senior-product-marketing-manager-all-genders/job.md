## **🚀 Join fiskaly! Help Us Simplify Tax Compliance for Millions**

Millions of people interact with fiskaly every day, even if they never notice it. We're the company behind compliance at the point of sale and e-invoicing — the invisible layer that keeps receipts tamper-proof, fights tax fraud, and helps merchants across Europe ditch paper for good.

## **💼 The Role**

European fiscalization and e-invoicing are fragmented, technical and deadline-driven. Every country has its own rules, its own timelines and its own competitive dynamics. Our buyers: POS manufacturers, PMS and ERP vendors, retailers, resellers are not tax experts, and they do not want to become them. They want to know what a mandate means for them and what to do about it.

That translation is your job. You will own positioning and messaging for our products across markets, drive go-to-market for new launches and country expansions, and turn dense regulatory requirements into value propositions that land with a technical partner in Munich and a retail chain in Madrid alike.

This is a senior individual contributor role with a wide surface area. You will work closely with Product, Compliance, Demand Gen, Content, Sales and our country teams.

**🛠️ What You’ll Do**

- Own product positioning and messaging for fiskaly's fiscalization and e-invoicing products (SIGN, RECEIPT, E-INVOICE, SAFE) across multiple European markets

- Define and continuously sharpen our ICPs and buyer personas, POS manufacturers, retailers, resellers, PMS and ERP vendors, and equip Demand Gen with the segmentation, pain points and messaging they need to convert them

- Drive go-to-market strategy for new product launches and country/regulation expansions

- Translate complex regulatory and technical requirements into clear, accurate value propositions

- Build and maintain sales enablement material, battlecards, one-pagers, demo scripts with Sales and Customer Success

- Own competitive intelligence and market research across the fragmented European compliance landscape

- Feed market and customer signal into the product roadmap in partnership with Product Management

- Keep messaging consistent across DACH, Spain, Italy and our other country teams, with an ear for local nuance

- Supply the Content team with ideas at scale and shape campaign concepts with Demand Generation

- Track regulatory calendars (KassenSichV, RKSV, Verifactu, NF525, and what comes next) these are effectively our market events
