### About the Role

We're a small, mission-driven energy technology company building a full-stack platform that enables hardware manufacturers and energy providers to offer dynamic electricity plans to European households. Our team operates at the frontier of AI-assisted development, licensed electricity retail, and complex domain engineering — and we're looking for a **product-focused engineer** who thrives at the intersection of customer discovery, end-to-end delivery, and engineering culture.

This is a high-ownership, zero-to-one role. You'll work directly with customers, shape the product, and ship production-grade experiences that move the business in a measurable way.

### What You'll Do

-

Sit with customers to dissect problems and develop product intuition that drives engineering decisions.

-

Ship end-to-end solutions across the full stack, taking features from discovery through to production.

-

Use AI-native development tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor) as genuine force multipliers for your output.

-

Own zero-to-one problems — think dunning workflows, customer portal re-engineering, billing infrastructure.

-

Prioritize ruthlessly: decide what to build, what to skip, and when to kill an initiative.

-

Solve core energy retail challenges: billing, procurement, customer retention, and cost optimization.

-

Shape engineering culture, tooling, and processes, and help raise the bar as the team grows.

-

Adapt quickly to new tools and methods, and bring teammates along to the frontier.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

A track record of shipping real product work — we assess what you've built, not just years on a résumé.

-

2+ years of software engineering experience delivering end-to-end, full-stack products to production.

-

Proven end-to-end ownership of zero-to-one problems, from discovery to live product.

-

Hands-on experience with AI-native codebases and AI-assisted development tools.

-

Comfort solving billing, procurement, customer retention, or cost-optimization problems in a consumer-facing or energy retail domain.

-

Experience shaping engineering culture, tooling, and processes in a small or early-stage team.

-

High ownership mindset with strong judgment and an ability to thrive in ambiguity.

**Nice to have:**

-

Background in energy retail, utilities, or adjacent regulated industries.

-

Contributions to high-engineering-bar environments or notable open-source projects.

-

Experience operating with extreme ownership in a high-growth, fast-moving startup.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

Early-stage equity participation.

-

Direct influence over product direction and engineering culture at a pivotal stage.
