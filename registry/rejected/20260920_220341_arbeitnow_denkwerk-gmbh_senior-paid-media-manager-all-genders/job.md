**You are the one we miss!**

- As a Senior Paid Media Manager, you will be independently responsible for the strategic development, implementation, and continuous optimization of our paid media campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, TikTok Ads, and in native advertising-across the entire funnel, from awareness to performance
- You have more than 6 years of professional experience in paid media, including several years of independent responsibility for campaign budgets in the high six- to seven-figure range
- You will be independently responsible to the client for media planning and strategic campaign planning across channels and funnel stages, from reach and brand goals to conversion optimization
