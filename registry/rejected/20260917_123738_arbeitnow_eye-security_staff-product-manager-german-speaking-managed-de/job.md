## About this role

We're looking for a Staff Product Manager – Managed Detection and Incident Response (m/f/x) to join our Product team.

As our client base continues to grow rapidly across Europe, this role will play a key strategic part in scaling our Managed Detection and Response (MDR), Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR), and Incident Response (IR) capabilities — Eye Security's most strategic product area.

You'll take full ownership of the product strategy and roadmap for this space. Collaborating with our engineers, product designers, security operations, product marketing, and cross-functional leadership, you will ensure that our platform evolves with our customers' needs and delivers maximum impact at scale.

This is a senior individual contributor and thought leadership role designed for someone who thrives on autonomy, discovery, and leading cross-functional product development in a fast-growing environment. You'll be the strategic voice shaping our product direction, mentoring product managers, and influencing broader company strategy.
