### About the Role

Join a fast-moving, early-stage **logistics technology startup** as a **Founding Engineer** and help build the AI platform that is making global freight forwarding autonomous. As one of the first engineers, you will own critical architecture decisions, ship full-stack features end-to-end, and push the boundaries of applied AI in a production environment — all while working closely with the founding team in Munich.

This is a high-ownership, high-impact role suited to someone with a founder mentality who thrives in ambiguity and wants to shape the technical DNA of a company from the ground up.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the design, development, and maintenance of the most critical parts of the core product.

-

Build reliable, scalable full-stack features that make users radically more efficient.

-

Evaluate, benchmark, and fine-tune AI/ML capabilities to maximise domain accuracy and context.

-

Continuously experiment with new model releases, frameworks, and agentic techniques in production.

-

Ship promising approaches quickly and iterate based on data and real user feedback.

-

Navigate and improve large-scale, complex legacy codebases, adding structure and scalability.

-

Engineer infrastructure and automation pipelines to support heavy data and AI workloads.

-

Collaborate directly with founders and teammates as a hands-on, high-ownership contributor.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

2–3+ years of professional software development experience (strong personal projects or an entrepreneurial track record may substitute).

-

Strong proficiency in **Python**; familiarity with **Go** is a significant plus.

-

Solid full-stack engineering skills with the ability to move fluidly across backend and cloud environments.

-

Hands-on production experience with AI/ML systems: benchmarking, fine-tuning, integrating LLMs, and running experiments in live environments.

-

Experience designing and maintaining production-grade systems with clean, scalable architecture.

-

Proven end-to-end ownership of features from initial design through to production deployment.

-

Experience building cloud-scale systems with a focus on scalability, reliability, and observability.

-

Strong CS fundamentals, intellectual humility, and exceptional self-discipline.

-

Founder-level drive, adaptability, and a results-oriented, autonomous work style.

-

Based in Munich or willing to relocate and work on-site.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Prior startup or entrepreneurial experience.

-

Experience or genuine interest in logistics, freight forwarding, or autonomous systems.

-

Opportunity to define and own the technical direction of a product with real market traction
