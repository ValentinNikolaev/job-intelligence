**Mission Brief**

We deliver the half of the rocket that stays on the ground. As Senior Mechanical Ground Systems Engineer at the Ground Systems department of Isar Aerospace, you will take part in shaping the development of the Ground Systems infrastructure. This includes ground equipment such as the Launch Table, Transporter/Erector, and Hold-Down-Release Mechanism as fundamental parts of the Launchpad, but also the design of equipment for Testing Facilities, R&D, qualification and acceptance of propulsion systems and rocket stages. You will work in close cooperation with other engineering departments as well, such as Propulsion Technology and Launch Vehicle, since their requirements drive the design of the ground support equipment.

Your Role in Our Space Mission:

Reporting directly to the Manager, Mechanical Ground Systems the engineer's tasks are focused on but not necessarily limited to:

- Taking responsibility, ownership and accountability for mechanical (sub)systems as part of larger projects

- Managing requirements proactively between stakeholders for own (sub)systems as part of larger projects and defining Engineering Change Requests

- Providing technical guidance to and mentor junior/mid-level mechanical ground systems engineers and advising colleagues on matters of design of mechanical/structural ground systems

- Designing, planning and iterating mechanical and/or structural (sub)systems by means of calculation/CAD tools and numerical simulations

- Creating manufacturing and integration drawings including BOMs for practically manufacturable (sub)systems

- Performing trade-offs from subsystem level up to entire architectural approaches

- Selecting and procuring suitable hardware for (sub)systems based on requirements

- Be the technical point of contact for externals regarding realization of mechanical systems

- Writing technical documentation, conducting (sub)system design reviews and risk assessments

- Evaluating (sub)systems for compliance with environmental and safety regulations

- Planning of commissioning procedures/activities and providing support to operation crews with respect to commissioning activities during commissioning of owned (sub)systems

- Travel activity to construction and test sites for on-site coordination tasks
