Good campaign design needs to work beyond one key visual. It also needs to stay strong when adapted into banners, social media assets, and other formats.

At idealo, you support our design team in turning campaign ideas into consistent, well-crafted visual assets.

### About your new Role

-

You develop and produce campaign creatives, banners, and social media assets.

-

You systematically adapt existing design concepts to different formats and prepare files for final delivery.

-

You create print materials such as postcards, giveaways, and materials for events and trade shows.

-

You design infographics and illustrations that make complex content easy to understand. When your designs work consistently across different formats while keeping the brand clearly recognizable, you can directly see the result of your work.

-

You support the design team in developing our brand further and applying it consistently.

### **Skills & Requirements**

#### Must-have

-

You are confident using Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign and have a strong eye for typography, layout, and composition.

-

You speak fluent German and can communicate in English at a minimum B1 level.

-

You take ownership and independently drive topics forward.

-

The confidence to speak up and challenge ideas constructively, paired with openness to diverse perspectives.

#### Nice-to-have

-

First experience with After Effects or Figma is a plus.
