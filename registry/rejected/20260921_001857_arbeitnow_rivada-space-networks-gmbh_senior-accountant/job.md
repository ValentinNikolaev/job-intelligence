**Your mission**

• Independently managing day-to-day financial accounting

• Responsibility for monthly advance sales tax returns and other tax filings

• Preparing monthly financial statements in accordance with the German Commercial Code (HGB) in collaboration with external consultants

• Assisting in the preparation of annual financial statements in accordance with the German Commercial Code (HGB) and U.S. GAAP

• Serve as the liaison between accounts payable and accounts receivable, as well as other financial departments such as Treasury and Controlling

• Act as the point of contact for general accounting and tax issues

• Coordinate and manage financial matters with international subsidiaries

• Contribute to process design in the accounting department

**What you bring on the table**

• Successfully completed training as a certified accountant, tax specialist, or comparable qualification; alternatively, a bachelor’s degree in business administration with a focus on auditing, taxation, accounting, or financial auditing

• At least 3–5 years of relevant professional experience in finance and accounting

• In-depth knowledge of financial accounting and the preparation of monthly and annual financial statements in accordance with the German Commercial Code (HGB)

• Nice to have: initial experience in US GAAP reporting

• Good knowledge of German tax law, particularly in the area of value-added tax

• Proficiency in using common accounting, financial reporting, and tax software such as DATEV and NetSuite (and interfaces to these)

• Good command of English and very good written and spoken German

• A structured, independent, and solution-oriented approach to work

• A strong hands-on mindset and a passion for taking on responsibility in a dynamic startup or growth environment

• A high degree of initiative and interest in actively shaping and further developing processes
