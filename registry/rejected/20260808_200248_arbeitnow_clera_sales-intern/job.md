### About the Role

Join a fast-growing, early-stage AI content tech startup as a **Sales Intern** based on-site in Munich. You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with experienced sales professionals, gaining hands-on exposure to B2B sales processes, tools, and strategy — while making a real impact on customer acquisition and revenue growth. This is a great opportunity for a motivated student or recent graduate eager to build a foundation in B2B sales within an innovative, AI-driven environment.

### What You'll Do

-

Identify and qualify inbound and outbound leads, tracking outreach progress throughout the pipeline.

-

Conduct prospect outreach via email and phone under the guidance of senior sales team members.

-

Maintain and update CRM records (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) to ensure accurate and up-to-date pipeline management.

-

Create and tailor sales collateral, presentations, and bespoke proposals for prospective customers.

-

Coordinate meetings and product demos between prospects and account teams.

-

Track key sales metrics and generate simple reports to inform team decisions.

-

Collaborate with marketing and product teams to align messaging and materials.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Currently enrolled in or recently completed a degree in business, marketing, economics, or a related field.

-

0–2 years of professional sales or customer-facing experience.

-

Proficiency in English (verbal and written) for professional communication with prospects.

-

Experience using or maintaining CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) and managing sales pipelines, or a strong willingness to learn quickly.

-

Ability to create and adapt sales collateral, presentations, and proposals for different prospect needs.

-

Experience with or strong understanding of lead generation and qualification (inbound and outbound).

**Nice to Have:**

-

Additional language skills beyond English (German is a strong plus).

-

Interest in AI, search technologies, or content marketing.

-

Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and a results-oriented mindset.

-

Comfort working in a small, fast-paced startup environment.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

**Compensation:** €22,200 per year (internship).

-

Practical, real-world B2B sales experience at an innovative AI-focused company.

-

Close mentorship from experienced sales and business professionals.

-

Exposure to cross-functional collaboration across sales, marketing, and product teams.

-

**Visa sponsorship:** Not available — candidates must have existing authorization to work in Germany.

### Location

This role is **on-site in Munich, Bavaria, Germany**. Candidates must be available to work from the Munich office. Remote work is not available for this position.
