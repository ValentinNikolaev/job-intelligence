**Build the product engine behind the next generation of MedTech software.**

REMATIQ is building AI software that helps life sciences and medical device companies bring products to market faster, without compromising the quality, traceability and compliance their industry demands.

We work with leading companies including Philips, Siemens Healthineers, B. Braun and Novartis, and have recently closed our Series A.

Now we’re looking for a **hands-on Product Lead / Head of Product** to help us turn ambitious product direction into exceptional shipped product.

## The role

This is a **player-coach role** for someone who wants real product ownership without moving away from the product itself.

Our founder will continue to lead long-term product strategy, major capability bets and the frontier of where REMATIQ should go.

**You will own how we get there.**

You’ll take responsibility for the full product execution loop:

**Customer insight → product ideas → prioritization → definition → engineering refinement → sprint execution → shipped product → learning.**

You’ll lead a growing team of **2–3 Product Managers**, raise the quality bar across the product organization, and still directly own one or two of our most important and complex product areas yourself.

You won’t be managing the process from the sidelines. You’ll be in the details with customers, PMs, engineers and designers, making decisions and helping the team ship.

## What you’ll do

-

**Own product execution end to end:** Turn strategic direction and customer insight into clear priorities, well-defined product work and shipped outcomes.

-

**Lead and develop the Product team:** Manage 2–3 PMs, help them sharpen their judgment and execution, and establish a consistently high bar for product work.

-

**Stay hands-on:** Personally lead discovery, define product requirements, make prioritization decisions and own some of our hardest product areas.

-

**Run product across multiple squads:** Create clarity across Product, Engineering and Design and ensure teams move from idea to execution without unnecessary process or ambiguity.

-

**Make the hard calls:** Navigate competing customer needs, technical constraints and incomplete information, and make clear product decisions rather than simply facilitating consensus.

-

**Build an AI-native product organization:** Use AI deeply in your own work and help rethink how discovery, specification, prototyping, analysis and product development should work in an AI-first company.

-

**Stay close to customers:** Understand how sophisticated enterprise teams actually work, identify the problems worth solving and turn customer insight into scalable product rather than one-off features.

-

**Continuously improve how we build:** Identify where our product process needs more structure, where it needs less, and build the operating rhythm that helps REMATIQ ship better and faster as we scale.

## What we’re looking for

You may be a strong fit if you:

-

Have led product for a **B2B enterprise software product**.

-

Have directly managed **at least 2 Product Managers**.

-

Are still deeply **hands-on** and comfortable running discovery, writing and refining product requirements, prioritizing trade-offs and working closely with Engineering.

-

Have owned product delivery across **multiple squads or product areas**.

-

Have strong product judgment and are comfortable making decisions with incomplete information.

-

Are genuinely **AI-forward**: you actively use AI in your work, understand how it changes product development, and have thoughtful views on what makes a product truly AI-native.

-

Communicate exceptionally well, both in writing and in conversation.

-

Operate with high ownership, pace and reliability.

## We’ll be especially excited if you

-

Have been a **founder, founding PM or very early product leader** and can demonstrate founder-like ownership.

-

Are technically strong enough to work deeply with engineers on **APIs, data, AI systems, workflows and technical trade-offs**.

-

Learned product craft inside an **excellent product organization** with a high bar for execution.

-

Have experienced both sides of the journey: building product in an early-stage environment and later helping a product organization scale.

## Why REMATIQ?

-

**A rare product moment:** We have enterprise customers, strong demand and fresh Series A funding, while many of the product systems and ways of working for our next stage are still yours to shape.

-

**Real product ownership:** You’ll have genuine authority over prioritization, product definition and execution, while staying close enough to the work to personally influence what gets built.

-

**Player-coach by design:** This isn’t a role where leadership means leaving product craft behind. You’ll build the team while continuing to own meaningful product problems yourself.

-

**AI is the product and the way we work:** You’ll help define what an AI-native product organization should actually look like, rather than simply adding AI tools to traditional processes.

-

**Complex problems worth solving:** Our customers operate in environments where workflows are technical, regulated and mission-critical. The product challenges are correspondingly deep.

-

**Work directly with the people shaping the company:** Partner closely with the founders, Engineering and Design on the decisions that determine what REMATIQ becomes.

-

**Meaningful impact:** Better software can help medical innovators reduce manual work, navigate complexity and bring important products to market faster and more safely.

-

**Compensation and ownership:** Competitive salary plus equity, giving you a meaningful stake in the company you’re helping to build.
