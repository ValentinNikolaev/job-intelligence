### About the Role

This is a hands-on engineering role at the heart of a fast-growing, **agentic-first tax advisory technology platform** targeting the German SME market. You'll join a small, high-calibre AI & Automation team and work directly with founders and product leads to build the infrastructure that transforms how tax advisory firms operate — turning repetitive, manual processes into fast, reliable, AI-powered execution.

The platform sits at the intersection of regulated financial services and modern software engineering, giving you real-world exposure to complex domain problems where accuracy and compliance truly matter. This is an on-site role based in **Berlin, Germany**.

*Please note: visa sponsorship is not available for this position.*

### What You'll Do

-

Design and build automation and infrastructure tools that eliminate repetitive work across accounting and advisory workflows.

-

Translate complex accounting, tax, and advisory processes into scalable, AI-powered software solutions.

-

Integrate systems and data across multiple partner firms to create a coherent, unified platform experience.

-

Collaborate closely with founders, domain advisors, and product teams to ship high-impact features quickly.

-

Contribute to shaping the technical architecture — ensuring the stack is built for speed, reliability, and long-term scale.

-

Strong proficiency in **TypeScript and Python** — you're comfortable shipping clean, pragmatic code in both.

-

-

Solid computer science fundamentals and an instinct for clean, maintainable engineering.

Genuine interest in how businesses operate — accounting, finance, and process optimisation excite rather than intimidate you.

Experience at a consulting firm, fintech, or professional-services technology company.
