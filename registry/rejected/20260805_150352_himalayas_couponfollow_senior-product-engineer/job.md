### **A Different Kind of Engineering Role**

Software engineering is changing, and we're changing with it. At [CouponFollow](https://himalayas.app/companies/couponfollow), we're moving beyond the traditional senior software engineer model to something we think is the future: the **Product Engineer.**

**Product Engineers don't implement tickets. They own outcomes.** They understand the business deeply, work project-by-project on the problems that matter most, and use AI throughout the development lifecycle to operate with a scope and speed that wasn't possible a few years ago.

This is a new model, and we're learning as we go. We're looking for people who find that exciting, who want to **help define what next-generation engineering looks like** rather than follow a playbook someone else wrote.

### **About the Role**

We're expanding our portfolio of internal products that power one of [CouponFollow](https://himalayas.app/companies/couponfollow)'s key revenue-generating businesses. **As a****Senior Product Engineer**, **you'll own the next generation of tools that make our Paid Traffic operations****faster, smarter, and more scalable**.

**Your scope is edge-to-edge**: from sitting with business stakeholders to understand a problem, through designing and building the solution with modern AI-assisted tooling, to deploying it, measuring its impact, and continuously improving it in production. You'll partner closely with a dedicated Technical Product Manager and business stakeholders to decide **what gets built, why it matters, and how technology can create measurable value for the business and our customers**.

### **The Team**

You'll join our **Revenue & Partnerships Engineering team**, made up of Product Engineers and a QA Engineer, reporting to the Engineering Manager. Ownership on the team takes different shapes: some engineers own new products from concept through production, while others focus on making our existing stable of products great. In every case, engineers are expected to understand the business problems behind their work and continuously improve the systems they run.
