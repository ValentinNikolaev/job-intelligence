### About the Role

A fast-growing, seed-stage enterprise AI platform startup based in Berlin is looking for a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** to join their small, high-impact engineering team. Founded in 2023 and backed by prominent European and international investors, the company builds a comprehensive, security-first platform that helps organizations adopt and integrate generative AI across their entire operations — including chat, workflows, custom agents, and deep integrations with existing tooling.

This is a **full-time, on-site role in Berlin, Germany**. Visa sponsorship is available. You'll have substantial ownership over features from conception through production, and direct influence on product and architecture decisions at an early stage.

### What You'll Do

-

Design and implement robust backend systems and APIs powering an enterprise-grade AI platform.

-

Build end-to-end solutions spanning backend (Node.js), database layers, and frontend integration.

-

Develop and optimize database schemas, queries, and data models for performance and scalability.

-

Implement API endpoints and integrate with third-party services and AI/ML providers.

-

Collaborate with the team on technical architecture decisions and system design.

-

Maintain and optimize the codebase for performance, reliability, and maintainability.

-

Participate in rotating **Support Engineer** shifts, where engineers across the team periodically focus on supporting customers and the customer support team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

3+ years of full-stack web development experience.

-

Strong hands-on experience with **Node.js**, **React / Next.js**, and **TypeScript**.

-

Proficiency in **PostgreSQL** and SQL — data modeling, querying, and query optimization.

-

Proficiency with **Tailwind CSS** for frontend styling.

-

Experience owning features end-to-end, from conception to production deployment.

-

Experience building AI-powered features or integrating AI/ML services into production applications.

-

Solid understanding of API design and integration with third-party services.

-

Strong CS fundamentals and an ability to contribute to technical architecture and system design discussions.

-

Excellent collaboration and communication skills across product and engineering teams.

-

Willingness to work **on-site in Berlin, Germany**.
