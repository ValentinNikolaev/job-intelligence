**Your mission**

**As JUNA's Working Student for People & Office Experience**, you'll be the glue that keeps our Berlin office running smoothly and our team feeling supported — acting as the first point of contact for people experience topics while owning day-to-day office management.

**People Experience
**Reporting to our Head of Culture & Comms, you'll be the first line for people experience matters, such as:

- Answering everyday employee questions (office logistics, onboarding basics, general HR queries) and knowing when to escalate
- Supporting onboarding and offboarding logistics for new joiners and leavers
- Helping coordinate team events, celebrations, and culture initiatives
- Acting as a friendly, approachable point of contact so people know where to turn

**Marketing**
You'll also lend a hand in shaping how JUNA shows up, internally and externally:

- Supporting employer branding content — LinkedIn posts, career page updates, job ad drafts
- Capturing and sharing moments from team events and culture initiatives (photos, short recaps, social posts)
- Assisting with internal communications, such as newsletters and team updates
- Helping put together simple marketing materials for events or campaigns

**Office Management**
**Supplies & Facilities**

- Consumables ordering (REWE, kitchen, toilet paper) — incl. automation
- Pfand & waste management & other suppliers
- Handyman / maintenance coordination — must be on-site to supervise
- Office cleanliness standards
- Rent & landlord relationship
**Safety & Compliance**

- BG Verkehr and administrative requirements — registration, contribution reporting, incident reporting
- Administrative audits — preparation, documentation, on-site coordination
**IT Equipment**

- Order laptops for new hires (standard spec)
- Set up accounts, email, and software licenses for new hires (automate where we can)
- Manage software licenses for the tools we use
- Track hardware assets (serial numbers etc.) so we know what's out there

**What That Looks Like
****In the beginning:**

- Ramp Up Quickly: Get familiar with JUNA's office setup, tools, and key contacts (landlord, suppliers, IT).
- Own the Basics: Independently handle recurring office management tasks (supplies, IT equipment ordering).
- Build Relationships: Become a known, trusted face for people experience questions across the team.
**Looking ahead:**

- Reliable Owner: Run office operations with minimal oversight, flagging issues before they become problems.
- Process Improver: Identify opportunities to automate or streamline recurring admin tasks.
- Culture Contributor: Play an active role in shaping team events and people initiatives alongside the Head of Culture & Comms.

**Your profile**

**What you bring:**

- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program, ideally in Business Administration, HR, Office Management, or a related field.
- Available to work on-site in Berlin (Kreuzberg) for at least 15–20 hours/week, as several tasks (maintenance coordination, audits, supplies) require physical presence.
- Professional fluency in English required.

**Who you are:**

- Organized and detail-oriented — nothing falls through the cracks on your watch.
- Proactive and hands-on — you don't wait to be told, you notice what needs doing.
- Approachable and discreet — colleagues feel comfortable coming to you with people-related questions.
- Comfortable juggling admin, logistics, and people topics in the same day.

**Bonus points if you have:**

- Experience planning or coordinating events (team offsites, celebrations, etc.).
- German at B2 level or above (not a hard requirement, but helpful for supplier and landlord communication).
