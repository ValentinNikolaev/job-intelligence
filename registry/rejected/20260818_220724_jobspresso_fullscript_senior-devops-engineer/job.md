Founded in 2011, Fullscript began by helping practitioners access and prescribe the products they trust to support better outcomes. What started with one practitioner and a simple idea has grown into a modern platform used by more than 125,000 practitioners and 10 million patients across North America.

Our ambition is clear: deliver the perfect patient experience and build everything around it. The best health journeys are practitioner-guided and connected from start to finish.

Fullscript brings together labs, supplements, clinical tools, and operational workflows into one system designed to support personalized, prevention-focused practice at scale.

At the core of everything we do is our mission: helping people get better.

**The Opportunity**

You don’t just manage infrastructure, you build the systems that power everything we do.

As a Senior Developer, DevOps on our Platform Engineering team, you’ll play a critical role in designing and scaling the infrastructure that supports our platform. You’ll work across the full DevOps lifecycle from infrastructure as code to observability to CI/CD; enabling our engineering teams to move quickly while maintaining high standards for reliability, security, and performance.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a collaborative environment, enjoys solving complex technical challenges, and is passionate about building scalable systems that empower developers.

**What you’ll do**

- Design and maintain scalable, reliable infrastructure using AWS (ECS, RDS, and related services)

- Build and manage infrastructure as code using Terraform

- Develop and optimize CI/CD pipelines to support efficient, secure software delivery

- Improve system observability using tools such as Datadog, Sentry, and Coralogix

- Enable a seamless developer experience by improving tooling, workflows, and platform usability

- Collaborate with engineering teams to support fast, safe, and reliable deployments

- Troubleshoot production issues and contribute to system reliability improvements

- Document infrastructure and share knowledge to improve long-term maintainability

**What you bring to the table**

- 5+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or related roles

- Strong experience with AWS services, particularly ECS and RDS

- Hands-on experience with Terraform and infrastructure-as-code practices

- Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines (e.g., GitHub Actions, GitLab CI)

- Experience with observability tools such as Datadog, Sentry, or Coralogix

- Strong understanding of cloud infrastructure, reliability, and performance optimization

- Proficiency with scripting or programming languages (e.g., Python, Bash)

- Clear communication skills and the ability to document complex systems

**Bonus if you have**

- Experience with Kubernetes, Helm, or container orchestration tools

- Familiarity with Ruby on Rails and/or JavaScript applications

- Experience with MySQL, PostgreSQL, or similar databases

- Experience with Linux/Unix systems administration
