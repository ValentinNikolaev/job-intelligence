### Job Description

**You'll connect with the role if you enjoy...**

- working on EDI and e-invoicing projects, from implementation to support

- analysing and creating convertible data formats and protocols (mappings) to make data exchange as easy as possible for our customers

- supporting the team in initiating and managing customer projects

- solving problems in an organised structure, with the help of clean documentation and tickets

- working closely with your colleagues in Integration Engineering. Direct contact with customers is not required.

### Qualifications

**To connect with ecosio it is important to have…**

- experience with structured data formats (e.g. CSV, XML, JSON)

- experience with a mapping tool such as Altova MapForce, Seeburger BIS, Lobster

- experince with implementing e-invoicing country regulations to the mapping structure

- good verbal and written technical English skills

- a strong team orientation and commitment

- the drive to learn new things

- ability to implement critical thinking to the mapping process

**And nice to have...**

- experience with EDI formats such as EDIFACT or ANSI X12

- a diploma from a HTL (technical college) or a higher IT education (FH or Uni) or equivalent EDI work experience

- basic programming skills to do troubleshooting
