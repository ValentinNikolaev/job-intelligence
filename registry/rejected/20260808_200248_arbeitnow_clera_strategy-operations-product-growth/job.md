### About the Role

This role sits at the intersection of product, growth, and operations at a fast-moving, founder-led AI-powered B2B SaaS company serving the industrial procurement sector. You'll design and ship product-led acquisition strategies that create durable, compounding growth — moving quickly from hypothesis to prototype to scale. Roughly **80% execution, 20% strategy**, this is a high-ownership position where you'll help shape company direction while driving day-to-day impact.

You'll be joining a small, high-performance team at **Series A stage**, operating with founder-level pace and ambiguity in a mission-driven environment focused on transforming procurement for European industry.

### What You'll Do

-

Identify and build the next compounding growth loop (e.g., supplier referrals, community effects, embedded workflows) end-to-end.

-

Own organic acquisition through **technical SEO and AI-search (GEO) infrastructure** to convert procurement queries into pipeline.

-

Concept and ship **product-led wedges** that drive user acquisition without proportional sales effort.

-

Run rapid 0-to-1 cycles: whiteboard → prototype → validate → scale, and pivot decisively when needed.

-

Define strategy alongside founders (~20% of time) and execute independently (~80% of time).

-

Own roadmaps and outcomes with extreme ownership — no permission-seeking.

-

Contribute to a collaborative, ego-free culture that consistently raises team performance.
