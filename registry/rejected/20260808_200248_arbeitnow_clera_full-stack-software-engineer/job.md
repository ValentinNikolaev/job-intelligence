### About the Role

A fast-growing, seed-stage enterprise AI platform company based in Berlin is looking for a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** to join their small, high-impact engineering team. Founded in 2023, the company builds an all-in-one platform that enables organisations to securely adopt and integrate generative AI — including AI chat, workflows, and custom agents — across their entire operations.

This is a full-time, on-site role in Berlin with substantial ownership from day one. You'll shape product features, drive architecture decisions, and ship quickly in a fast-paced early-stage environment. **Visa sponsorship is available.**

### What You'll Do

-

Design and implement robust backend systems and APIs powering a scalable AI platform.

-

Build end-to-end solutions spanning backend (Node.js), database layers, and frontend integration.

-

Develop and optimize database schemas, queries, and data models for performance and scalability.

-

Implement API endpoints and integrate with third-party services and AI/ML providers.

-

Collaborate with the team on technical architecture decisions and system design.

-

Maintain and optimize the codebase for performance, reliability, and maintainability.

-

Participate in rotating **Support Engineer** shifts, in which engineers periodically focus on supporting customers and the customer success team.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

3+ years of full-stack web development experience.

-

Strong proficiency with **Node.js**, **React / Next.js**, and **TypeScript**.

-

Solid command of **PostgreSQL** and SQL — data modeling, querying, and optimization.

-

Experience with **Tailwind CSS** for frontend styling and implementation.

-

Proven track record owning features end-to-end, from conception through production deployment.

-

Experience building AI-powered features or integrating AI/ML services into applications.

-

Experience designing robust APIs and integrating with third-party services and AI providers.

-

Strong CS fundamentals and communication skills across product and cross-functional teams.

-

Willingness to participate in periodic support engineer rotations.

-

Ability to work on-site in **Berlin, Germany** (visa sponsorship available).
