**Requirements**

-

Define and drive the product vision and roadmap for Statista's core platform capabilities, aligning technical investments with company-wide product and business goals

-

Translate complex platform challenges into clear, prioritized requirements and work closely with engineering teams to deliver high-impact solutions across areas such as data infrastructure, APIs, and internal tooling

-

Identify opportunities to leverage AI and automation to accelerate platform development, improve developer experience, and unlock new capabilities for product teams

-

Act as the bridge between technical and non-technical stakeholders, ensuring alignment on platform priorities, trade-offs, and delivery timelines

-

Evaluate and shape integration strategies for emerging technologies, including AI/ML services, MCP protocols, and data pipeline architectures

-

Continuously assess platform performance, scalability, and reliability, driving improvements through data-informed decision-making and close collaboration with engineering leadership

**Responsabilities**

-

7+ years of experience in the tech industry with a strong understanding of product and engineering workflows, team dynamics, and modern software development practices

At least 2 years of recent, hands-on experience in a heavily technical product domain such as platform engineering, data infrastructure, AI/ML, or API development

-

Proven background in platform or technical product management; experience in data platforms or data engineering is a strong plus

-

You actively integrate AI into your daily workflows and are continuously refining and expanding how you use AI-powered tools to increase your effectiveness

Excellent communication skills with the ability to translate between deeply technical concepts and strategic business language

-

Strong analytical mindset with a structured approach to prioritization, problem-solving, and stakeholder alignment

You thrive in cross-functional environments and are comfortable navigating ambiguity, legacy systems, and evolving technical landscapes
