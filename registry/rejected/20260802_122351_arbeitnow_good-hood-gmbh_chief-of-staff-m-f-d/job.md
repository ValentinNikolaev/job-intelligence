**Deine Aufgaben**

We're looking for a results-driven Chief of Staff to build the operating backbone behind our leadership team. This role exists to support our COO and the wider C-level move faster: turning strategy into structured decisions, taking high-priority initiatives from idea to delivery, and making sure the way we work as a company actually keeps up with how we want to work.
You'll sit at the intersection of strategy, organizational design, and daily execution, with real ownership over how well the company runs, reporting directly to the COO.
**Turning strategy into action**

- Help teams move and settle into our new team structure, support them in working together effectively, and shape how our enabling teams support other teams
- Own high-priority initiatives end-to-end, partnering directly with senior leadership and department heads from first concept to delivery
- Steer and track cross-departmental projects, keeping them accountable to their timelines
Organizational design

- Assess our organizational structure, spot capability gaps, and lead the work to close them
- Design and refine the internal processes, workflows, formats, and meeting rhythms that keep the company moving
- Prepare & steer internal leadership meetings, keep track of deliverables
- Support the build-out and optimization of processes, tools, and automation
- Improve cross-team collaboration and introduce new ways of working
Daily operations & project management

- Work closely with the C-level on delivering key priorities and operational topics in day-to-day business
- Take on high-visibility administrative and organizational special topics, stepping in with hands-on coordination when leadership needs it to unblock something important
Executive communication

- Prepare analyses, presentations, decision papers, and management reporting for leadership
- Organize and follow up on management meetings, workshops, and steering sessions
