# **About the role**

Luminovo builds software that powers the electronics supply chain — and we're growing our OEM segment across the DACH market. As a BDR based in Munich or Berlin, you'll own the top of the funnel: turning cold outreach into qualified pipeline for our Account Executives.

This is a high-impact role for someone who believes cold calling isn't dead, thrives on outbound, and wants to grow into a closing role in B2B SaaS.

**What you'll do**

-

Run multichannel outbound sequences — email, phone, LinkedIn, and events — targeting technical buyers at OEMs across the DACH region

-

Use tools like Apollo, Clay, Lusha, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator to build and work targeted lead lists

-

Qualify prospects through cold call sequences to identify ICP fit

-

Hand off warm opportunities to Account Executives with clean notes and context

-

Track your own metrics and bring frontline market insights back to sales and marketing leadership

**Who you are**

-

Required BDR/SDR experience in B2B SaaS (or strong hunger to break in)

-

Fluent in German and English — you'll be reaching buyers across the DACH region

-

Comfortable with rejection and energized by outbound

-

Driven to grow into an Account Executive role and willing to earn it

-

Bonus: experience or genuine curiosity in electronics, manufacturing, or hardware

-

Nice to have: you use AI as more than a better search engine. Ideally you've built your own automations, workflows, or tools — we care about what you've actually shipped, not what you've read them

**Work setup**

Remote from Germany. Munich is our global HQ; we also have a Berlin hub.

**Travel**

We meet regularly for team retreats and selected industry events, so expect occasional travel. We cover the costs.

**Why Luminovo**

-

Join a well-funded SaaS scaleup at a pivotal growth stage

-

Clear path to Account Executive with a team invested in your development

-

Hybrid in Munich or Berlin with an international, high-performance team
