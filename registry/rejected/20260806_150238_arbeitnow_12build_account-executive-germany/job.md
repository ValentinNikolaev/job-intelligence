**As a Account Executive, you play a key role in our growth in Germany and the wider DACH region.**

**What will you do? **

-

Take full responsibility for managing the entire sales cycle with potential customers — from lead generation together with our SDRs, to presentations, all the way to closing deals (with both general contractors and subcontractors).

-

Conduct product demos online or on-site to convince general contractors of 12Build’s value.

-

Build and maintain long-term relationships with key decision-makers.

-

Help develop tailored features that meet our customers’ specific needs.

-

Work closely with SDRs, Marketing, Customer Success, and Product teams to shape our product and sales strategies.

-

Consistently achieve — and exceed — revenue targets and relevant KPIs.

**Sven’s tip for new 12Builders:**

*“Finding your own style and using it to inspire your colleagues is strongly encouraged!”*

**Who is 12Build?**

With a healthy dose of Drive, Happiness, and Openness, we’re building towards a transparent construction industry. We are not just a [SaaS platform](https://12build.com/en/) that connects construction companies and fosters collaboration, we are market leader in the Netherlands. We’ve been around for nearly twenty years — and we’re still growing. Our team now includes more than 90 colleagues, collaborating from three offices and several home offices across Europe. A large part of the construction world in the Benelux, Germany, and Denmark already works on our platform and uses our software on a daily basis. And we have the ambition to conquer all of Europe.

**What do we expect from you?**

-

**Language skills:** Native-level German (spoken and written) and very good English skills.

-

**Experience & expertise:** Several years of sales experience, ideally with a focus on B2B customer relationships.

-

**Commercial acumen:** Proven communication and sales skills, ability to identify customers, and a talent for thinking outside the box.

-

**Industry affinity:** Experience in or a strong interest in the construction industry is a big plus.

-

**Work style:** Independent, structured, and solution-oriented, with the ability to think strategically and execute operationally. Experience with CRM systems like HubSpot is an advantage.

-

**Mobility & presence:** Driver’s license (Class B) and willingness to spend about 20–30% of your time on-site with potential customers across the DACH region.

**What do we offer you?**

-

An exciting role in a **growing, international SaaS scale-up**

-

Attractive gross salary between **€5,500 and €6,500** per month

-

Participation in an attractive **bonus scheme and a 13th-month salary**

-

**Company car** for business and private use (including a charging or fuel card)

-

A high degree of autonomy, flat hierarchies, and short decision-making channels

-

A dedicated, supportive team that **enjoys working together**

-

Team events and **personal development opportunities**
