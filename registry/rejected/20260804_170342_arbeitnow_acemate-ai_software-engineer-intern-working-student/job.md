We're looking for a passionate **Software Engineer (Intern / Working Student)** to drive our product forward and help us scale.

About acemate

Founded in 2024, acemate has quickly become the leading AI learning platform for higher and further education, trusted by top-tier institutions across four countries, including **TU Munich**, **University of Cologne**, **WHU** and the **German Chambers of Commerce and Industry (IHKs)**.

- **Our Mission:** Democratizing access to personalized learning. We don't believe that AI will replace educators. Instead, we empower them to design and deliver personalized learning experiences at scale.

- **Our Growth:** We've raised pre-seed funding and are growing rapidly. While we focus on higher and further education in the DACH area today, we're already expanding into new regions and markets.

- **Our Culture:** We're a young, driven team passionate about tackling the toughest challenges in education. We embrace builder culture and minimize scheduled meetings for ad-hoc discussions and maximized focus on coding and creating.

## Tasks

**One role, a few possible directions**

Most of this role is product engineering: full-stack work on the features students and educators use every day. But we'd rather shape it around the person than force everyone through the same slot. Depending on where you're strong and what you want to get better at, you could lean towards:

- **Product:** Talking to users, building features end to end, making AI features feel genuinely useful rather than like another chatbot wrapper.

- **Mobile:** We're building our React Native app, and there's plenty to own if that's where your interest is.

- **Platform and architecture:** Effect, clean architecture, type-driven design, the AI pipelines behind everything. If that's the part you find interesting, there's real depth here.

You don't need to know which one you want on day one. Most people find out by building something.

**What You'll Be Working On**

You'll take ownership of your work and solve real user problems directly. Working alongside the founders and the engineering team, you'll connect what users actually need, how we design it and how we technically pull it off.

- **No endless spec docs:** Instead of writing long requirements, you'll speak directly with users to find their pain points and immediately start coding a solution.

- **Production prototyping:** Forget spending hours on Figma mocks when you can just prompt a functional UI quickly. You'll test your ideas by pushing live code rather than sharing static designs.

- **Data-backed intuition:** You'll use a mix of user empathy and live analytics (PostHog) to find where people get stuck and fix it right away.

- **Real ownership:** You'll take features from top to bottom, handling the architecture, the actual coding and the final deployment. Your code goes to real students at real institutions, not to a sandbox.

- **Ship fast, learn faster:** You'll push updates out early to see how users react and use that data to iterate.

**Past examples of things you might build:**

- A Duolingo-inspired daily quiz system that keeps students engaged with their university materials.

- Complex AI pipelines designed to pull text and visuals from various documents and map those topics into knowledge graphs.

- A custom authorization engine built to manage the increasing complexity of multiple institutional environments.

**Tech Stack:**

- **Frontend:** TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS

- **Mobile:** TypeScript, React Native, Expo

- **Backend:** Effect-based TypeScript services

- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)

- **Infrastructure:** Cloudflare (Workers, Pages, Workflows, R2, KV), Google Cloud (Gemini, Cloud Run)

- **Tools:** Sentry, Langfuse, PostHog, Stripe

If you don't know every single tool listed here from day one, that's completely fine. We trust your ability to pick things up quickly and learn new frameworks as we build.
