### About the Role

This is a hands-on sales leadership position at a fast-growing, Series A/B-stage AI-powered procurement intelligence platform serving the industrial sector. You will lead a team of ~5 Account Executives targeting the DACH market — a largely greenfield opportunity — while carrying a personal quota and working closely with senior leadership to shape go-to-market strategy. The company's platform helps industrial **Mittelstand** customers optimize procurement costs, reduce supply chain risk, and meet evolving regulatory requirements through AI-driven intelligence.

This role is based **on-site in Munich, Germany** and requires full-time in-office presence. **Visa sponsorship is not available.**

### What You'll Do

-

Own a defined customer segment and lead a team of ~5 Account Executives to capture a significant market opportunity across the DACH region.

-

Close deals against a personal quota while coaching your team to exceed collective revenue targets.

-

Partner with sales leadership to design and execute go-to-market strategy for a greenfield market, including market entry planning and GTM alignment.

-

Join high-stakes customer conversations to shape deal strategy, support negotiations, and develop compelling business cases for procurement teams.

-

Build and develop a high-performing sales team through structured training, hands-on deal coaching, and rigorous performance management.

-

Implement and coach structured sales methodologies (MEDDIC) to sharpen discovery, qualification, and negotiation across the team.

-

Drive performance through pipeline reviews, metrics tracking, and targeted action planning to improve win rates and deal velocity.

-

Deliver measurable procurement value to customers — cost optimization, supply chain risk reduction, and regulatory compliance.

-

Identify opportunities to leverage AI tools to accelerate team productivity and share effective workflows across the sales organization.

### What We're Looking For

**Required**

-

3+ years of experience as an Account Executive in **complex, non-transactional B2B SaaS** sales.

-

At least **1 year in a sales leadership role**, ideally within a high-growth SaaS environment.

-

Proven, consistent track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets.

-

Proficiency with **MEDDIC** sales methodology and demonstrated ability to coach others in MEDDIC-driven discovery and qualification.

-

Experience leading **GTM strategy development and execution** for a greenfield or nascent market.

-

Domain experience in **manufacturing procurement technology**, with a clear ability to articulate procurement value (cost optimization, risk reduction, regulatory alignment) to industrial customers.

-

**Business-level fluency in both German and English** — essential for engaging with DACH customers and stakeholders.

-

Strong strategic thinking paired with the ability to execute tactically on deals and GTM initiatives.

-

Based in Munich and available to work on-site full-time.

**Nice to Have**

-

Experience identifying and applying AI tools or workflows to improve sales team productivity.

-

Background at a consulting firm (e.g., strategy or Big 4) or enterprise software company serving industrial / manufacturing customers.
