## Aufgaben

You are the GTM Engineering Intern at ARC. While our BDRs are on the phone with CFOs, you build the machine behind them: the automations, data pipelines, and AI workflows that decide who we call, with what message, and when. We sell an AI-native platform, so our own go-to-market has to run AI-native too. You'll build our internal GTM OS from the ground up.

**What you'll do**

- Build automated prospecting pipelines: sourcing multi-entity Mittelstand groups and PE-backed companies that fit our ICP, at scale

- Enrich accounts and contacts with the signals that matter to us: legal entities, ERP landscape, PE ownership, trigger events like ERP migrations or acquisitions

- Automate outreach workflows across email, LinkedIn, and phone, and keep them personal enough that a German CFO actually responds

- Connect and orchestrate our GTM stack (HubSpot, Aircall, enrichment and outreach tools) into one system with clean data flowing through it

- Use AI models aggressively: for research, list building, message drafting, call analysis, and anything else that removes manual work from the pipeline

- Build dashboards and KPI tracking so we always know what's working in outbound and what isn't

- Prototype, test, and iterate: your experiments directly shape how ARC's GTM engine works as we scale the sales team

## Qualifikation

**What makes you a great fit**

- You love building things: with no-code tools, scripts, APIs, or AI agents, whatever gets it done fastest

- You're deep in the modern AI tool stack and use LLMs daily as a matter of course

- You're analytical and rigorous with data: messy lists and broken syncs bother you until they're fixed

- You have strong business sense and can think from the prospect's perspective, not just the pipeline's

- You're hands-on and not afraid of unglamorous work: cleaning a CRM included

- You communicate well in English; German is a plus since our market is the German Mittelstand

- You want to work with smart and friendly people with (self-reported) low ego

- You want to work on-site 5 days a week in our new Berlin office

- You need detailed instructions and shy away from taking responsibility

- You're not willing to deeply understand the product and the buyer behind your automations

- You're only interested in high-level "strategic" work

- You think GTM engineering means setting up one Zapier flow

- You've never built anything on your own initiative

- You prefer working remotely

- You prefer working with minimal collaboration

- You want to work a 9-5 job
