#### **TYTAN Technologies**

At **TYTAN**, we aim to set a global standard in autonomous air defense with AI-driven, cost-efficient systems that counter unmanned aerial threats at scale. We protect people. We protect critical infrastructure. We protect European sovereignty.

We are looking for a **Senior Procurement Specialist** to define and execute the **long-term procurement strategy** for our operations. This role goes beyond transactional purchasing and focuses on **supplier strategy, cost optimization, risk management, and scalability**, supporting both **current production and future growth**. You will work closely with **Manufacturing, Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, Finance, and Operations**, ensuring a resilient, cost-effective, and scalable supply base.

###

### **Your roadmap to make an impact**

**Procurement Strategy & Supplier Management**

-

Develop and execute sourcing strategies for key commodities, components, and services.

-

Select, onboard and manage global suppliers, building long-term strategic partnerships.

-

Monitor supplier performance and support continuous improvement initiatives.

**Cost, Risk & Contract Management**

-

Lead **cost reduction and value engineering initiatives** in collaboration with Engineering and Manufacturing.

-

Lead supplier negotiations for contracts and commercial agreements.

-

Identify, manage and mitigate supply chain risks through effective contingency and risk mitigation plans.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**

-

Act as the procurement interface to **Manufacturing, Engineering, Finance, Quality, and Operations for **selected commodities and projects.

-

Support new product introductions and supplier readiness.

-

Coordinate procurement activities to ensure material availability.

**Process Improvement**

-

Support procurement processes and KPI reporting.

-

Maintain supplier documentation and contracts.

-

Contribute to procurement system and process improvements.

3 - 6 years of experience in **strategic procurement or supply chain management**, preferably in a manufacturing or industrial environment

Proven experience managing **strategic suppliers and complex sourcing initiatives**

Strong negotiation, analytical, and commercial skills

Structured, data-driven working style with a high sense of ownership
