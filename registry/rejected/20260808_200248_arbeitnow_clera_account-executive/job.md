### About the Role

A fast-growing, Y Combinator–backed AI SaaS startup is looking for an **Account Executive** to join their Munich-based team on-site. The company builds AI-powered software that automates quoting and order processing for distributors and manufacturers — helping sales teams eliminate manual work, reduce lost deals, and close faster.

As an early AE, you'll own the full sales cycle from first meeting to close, selling to mid-market and enterprise customers primarily in Germany. You'll play a pivotal role in driving the company toward a significant ARR milestone by end of 2026, and have the opportunity to help build out the sales team and processes as the company scales.

### What You'll Do

-

**New Business:** Qualify and close new customers across mid-market and enterprise accounts.

-

**Pipeline Management:** Partner closely with the prospecting team to maintain a healthy pipeline and ensure a seamless customer journey.

-

**Customer Value:** Guide prospects through evaluation, build strong relationships, and develop compelling business cases that demonstrate clear ROI to executive stakeholders.

-

**Sales Execution:** Conduct product demos, lead negotiations, and close deals while continuously refining the sales process.

-

**Sales Strategy:** Design and implement strategies to win key accounts and expand into new markets and verticals.

-

**Customer Feedback:** Gather and relay customer insights to the engineering team to inform and accelerate product development.

-

**Team Building:** Help recruit, train, and develop a high-performing sales team and scalable sales processes.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

1+ years of closing experience in B2B SaaS or tech sales.

-

Proven track record of exceeding quota in mid-market and/or enterprise segments.

-

Experience owning end-to-end sales cycles: discovery, demos, negotiations, and close.

-

Proficiency with CRM and sales automation tools (e.g., Salesforce or equivalent) for pipeline management and forecasting.

-

Ability to build and present business cases and ROI models to executive-level stakeholders.

-

**Native German and fluent English — both are required.**

-

Based in Munich or willing to relocate; this is a fully on-site role.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience in construction, supply chain, HVAC, electrical, building materials, or the trades.

-

Experience building or leading a sales team and scaling sales processes.
