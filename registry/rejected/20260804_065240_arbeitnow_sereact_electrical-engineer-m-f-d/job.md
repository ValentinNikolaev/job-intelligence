# **Overview:**

We are looking for a skilled electrical engineer to design and develop circuits and control systems for robotics. You will work closely with the mechanical team to integrate electrical components into the robot’s physical structure, test and optimize these systems to meet performance requirements, and ensure the real-time operational reliability of our AI-driven robotic systems. Additionally, you'll troubleshoot and resolve electrical issues during both development and deployment.
