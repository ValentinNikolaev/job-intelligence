#### Tasks

- You lead and develop a software engineering team of approximately 15 engineers building our Website Builder Application – driving roadmap delivery, technical excellence and team performance.

- You are accountable for the architectural direction, code quality, reliability and continuous evolution of the Website Builder Application in close collaboration with Product Management and stakeholders.

- You educate, coach and grow your engineers, set clear goals and create an environment in which people do their best work and continuously develop.

- You drive our “AI first” initiative within your team and embed AI methods and tools deeply into daily engineering and product work.

- You design and run continuous learning, training and enablement formats so every team member builds AI fluency and applies it confidently in their day-to-day.

- You collaborate closely with Product Management, Design, other engineering teams and stakeholders to shape priorities, unblock delivery and create business impact.

- As a secondary responsibility, you act as the on-site representative for our Cologne development office and take care of selected operational and organizational topics around office, culture and employee experience.

#### Qualifications

- Several years of experience leading software engineering teams of 15+ people, ideally in web applications, SaaS or website builder products.

- Strong technical background in modern web technologies, distributed systems and agile delivery – credible and impactful with senior engineers.

- Proven track record of shipping high-quality web or SaaS products at scale and continuously improving engineering practices, quality and velocity.

- Hands-on experience with AI and GenAI tools and a clear vision for how AI changes engineering and product workflows.

- Strong people leadership skills: hiring, coaching, performance management and the ability to build a strong, inclusive team culture.

- Hands-on mentality and ownership combined with the ability to balance technical depth with strategic thinking and clear prioritization.

- **Strong verbal and written German skills **(English is a big plus), and willingness to take on light operational site-representative duties for the Cologne office.
