## 💡 Your Mission: Be the technical partner our hospital customers trust

As **Technical Account Manager - Hospital**, you will own the technical relationship with a portfolio of our strategic hospital customers. You will understand their systems, infrastructure, integrations, and stakeholders, and support them from go-live through releases and renewals; ensuring voize runs reliably across wards, sites, and clinical workflows. Your success will be measured by trust, retention, and long-term customer outcomes.

## 🚀 Your Daily Business - No two days are alike

-

**Own the technical relationship with a portfolio of hospitals and hospital groups, building a deep understanding of their HIS/KIS setup**, infrastructure, clinical workflows, open topics, and key stakeholders

-

**Maintain a technical account plan for every customer**, covering integrations, deployment architecture, security requirements, planned changes, open risks, and upcoming rollout waves

-

**Lead release pre-briefings and technical reviews**, helping customers assess the impact of changes across systems before they affect clinical operations

-

**Stay close to every issue across your accounts**: Technical Support manages the queue, while you manage the relationship and provide the technical and organizational context needed to accelerate resolution

-

**Identify recurring themes across hospital customers and turn them into actionable feedback** for Product and Engineering, improving reliability, integrations, and rollout readiness

## 🤝 Your Skillset - What you bring to the table

-

**Several years of experience in technical account management, solutions engineering, technical support**, or implementation, ideally in B2B SaaS, health tech, or hospital IT

-

**Hands-on technical knowledge of integrations, APIs, logs, networks, identity and access management**, and device or MDM setups; familiarity with healthcare interoperability standards such as HL7 or FHIR is a plus

-

G**reat communication skills across technical, clinical, and executive audiences**, from discussing an interface with hospital IT to aligning on a rollout with nursing leadership or senior management

-

**A structured, calm approach to managing complex organizations**, multiple sites, and open topics simultaneously, supported by data that demonstrates progress and impact

-

**Professional German and comfortable English, based in Germany, and willingness to occasionally travel** to customer sites when a rollout or escalation requires on-site support; experience with hospital information systems is a plus
