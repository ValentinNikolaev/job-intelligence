We’re looking for a Solutions Engineer UAS / Aviation who will play a pivotal role in the future of our company. Our Solutions Engineer will be responsible for the integration, validation, and operational readiness of Auterion-powered drone systems. You’ll collaborate closely with the production team, as well hardware engineering, firmware/software teams, and operations while ensuring that every platform transitions successfully from a physical airframe into a fully validated flying robot ready for deployment. This role is ideal for someone who lives and breathes drone technology and enjoys bridging the gap between hardware, firmware, and real-world flight performance.

**Responsibilities and Duties**

- Operate in a highly hands-on environment with evolving system requirements, rapidly iterating across hardware and software integration challenges

- Perform firmware flashing and configuration using PX4 and/or other autopilot ecosystems, including sensor calibration, ESC tuning, and system bring-up

- Execute End-of-Line (EoL) software validation and functional testing to ensure production readiness and reliability

- Conduct payload and camera load-seat testing, validating integration stability and operational performance under real-world conditions

- Troubleshoot embedded system issues spanning firmware, communication interfaces, sensors, and hardware-software interactions

- Work independently to diagnose integration and flight-readiness issues while collaborating closely with engineering and manufacturing teams

- Contribute to validation workflows for complete UAV systems, including components, integrated features, and operational flight configurations

- Support full-stack drone integration activities across hardware, embedded firmware, and flight software layers

- Solve complex integration and reliability challenges involving sensors, flight controllers, communication protocols, and power systems

- Drive measurable impact on product quality, manufacturing throughput, and deployment readiness through improved validation and testing processes

- Partner cross-functionally with hardware engineering, firmware/software teams, operations, and QC engineering to accelerate batch validation and reduce EoL cycle times

- Interface with external vendors and equipment providers to support testing infrastructure, hardware integration, and operational readiness

- Participate in hands-on flight testing activities to validate platform stability, safety, and mission performance

**Qualifications and Skills**

- B.Sc. in Aviation Technology, Robotics, Embedded Systems, Electrical Engineering, or related field (M.Sc. optional)

- Hands-on experience working with Autopilot, Mission planning and navigation Software, including firmware flashing, configuration, and flight validation

- Strong technical foundation in embedded systems, firmware debugging, and hardware-software integration

- Proficiency in C/C++ and/or MAVLink communication protocols or equivalent Datalink protocols

- Experience with UAV sensor calibration, ESC tuning, and flight controller configuration

- Familiarity with Linux-based development and debugging environments

- Experience with UAV flight testing, system validation, and troubleshooting in real-world operational environments

- Understanding of drone manufacturing, QC processes, and End-of-Line testing methodologies is a plus

- Strong collaboration and communication skills when working with cross-functional engineering and operations teams

- Ability to work independently in fast-paced environments with evolving technical challenges

- Passion for drone technology and a hands-on engineering mindset focused on building reliable flying systems

- EU citizenship required
