We are currently looking for a Director Performance Marketing who will be integral to driving high-level performance across Lovehoney Group. In this role, you will own the strategy, efficiency, and growth of our Group paid and earned acquisition engine across SEA, SEO/AIO, and Affiliates. You will combine your commercial and analytical expertise with leadership skills to manage channel teams, set the P&L, and translate market opportunities into measurable growth.

**What you will do**

- Own the blended performance marketing P&L across channels — spend, CRoI/ROAS, customer acquisition, and contribution.

- Set the multi-channel acquisition strategy across brands and markets, defining priorities, investment rationale, and exit decisions.

- Lead, develop, and performance-manage SEA, SEO/AIO, and Affiliate teams, building capability and commercial ownership.

- Partner with local Market Leads, CRM, Brand, and other functions to align channel activity with overall commercial priorities.

- Monitor performance daily, recommend actions to optimise revenue and margin, and feed learnings into global strategy.

- Manage senior agency, platform, and vendor relationships, holding partners to clear commercial standards.

**What you should bring**

- 10+ years in performance or digital marketing, with at least 7 years leading multi-channel acquisition at Director/Head level.

- Proven ownership of a large paid-media budget and blended acquisition P&L.

- Expertise across SEA, SEO, and Affiliates, with experience transitioning to AI-assisted acquisition.

- Track record of building and leading high-performing channel teams.

- Strong analytical and commercial skills — comfortable with attribution, incrementality, and channel economics.

- Experience coordinating acquisition with onsite trading, CRM, and brand activity in a multi-market or multi-brand environment.

- Exceptional executive communication and stakeholder management skills.
