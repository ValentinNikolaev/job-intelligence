### Job Summary:

We are seeking a highly skilled Manager, Software Engineering to lead our Tools & Frameworks team. This role is a unique opportunity to manage and scale teams working on infrastructure for core services while driving architectural excellence and ensuring operational efficiency. As an Engineering Manager, you will define and execute a roadmap aligned with company-wide goals, lead a team of talented engineers, and make impactful decisions that shape the future of our platform.

The Tools and Frameworks team serves as a critical force multiplier for the rest of the [SmithRx](https://himalayas.app/companies/smithrx) engineering organization. Composed primarily of backend-focused engineers, this team owns the shared libraries, core backend data, and internal tooling that provide the foundation for our product ecosystem.

As the manager of this team, you will champion developer productivity and systems architecture, ensuring your team designs resilient, scalable APIs and schemas intended for use across multiple business domains. You will play a vital role in balancing technical priorities with business needs, supporting our mission to lower healthcare costs through highly efficient internal infrastructure.
