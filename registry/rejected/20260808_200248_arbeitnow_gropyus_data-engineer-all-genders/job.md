**About The Role**

We are growing our Data Platform Team within the Gropyus Tech department. Our Platform team is responsible for features and operating our internal platform, the Gropyus Data Fabric.

Our team interacts with experts from various domains such as Digital Building Planning and Automation, Product Operations, Sustainability, AI, IoT, construction engineers, building architects, logistics experts, software engineering; solving complex challenges pertaining to end-to-end process for planning, building, and operating a building.

**As part of the Data Platform organization, you will:**

- Design, build, and maintain core data platform components, including ingestion frameworks, transformation pipelines, and data storage layers

- Own and improve data reliability through monitoring, alerting, SLAs, and data quality checks

- Collaborate with domain experts and software engineers to understand data needs and deliver high-quality datasets through managed data pipelines

- Contribute to initiatives to modernize or scale our data ecosystem (including query optimization, schema design, analytics, etc)

- Adhere to best practices and rigor in development including documentation, testing, and validation
