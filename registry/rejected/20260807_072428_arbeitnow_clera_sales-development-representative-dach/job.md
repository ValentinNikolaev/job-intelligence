### About the Role

Join a fast-growing **Construction Technology (ConTech) SaaS** company that uses artificial intelligence to transform how sales teams in the construction industry discover, qualify, and win new projects. As a **Sales Development Representative for the DACH region**, you'll be the first point of contact for prospective customers — playing a pivotal role in driving growth and building long-term partnerships across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

### What You'll Do

-

Proactively identify and engage prospects that match the ideal customer profile within the DACH construction market.

-

Conduct outbound outreach (email, phone, LinkedIn, and other channels) to generate qualified pipeline for the sales team.

-

Communicate the value of an AI-powered Revenue Engineering platform to construction industry decision-makers.

-

Qualify inbound and outbound leads through discovery conversations and pass them to Account Executives.

-

Maintain accurate records of activity and prospect data in the CRM.

-

Collaborate closely with marketing and sales leadership to iterate on messaging and targeting strategies for the DACH market.

-

Represent the company at industry events and help foster a strong market presence in the region.

### What We're Looking For

-

**0–2 years** of experience in sales, business development, or a customer-facing role (SaaS or B2B experience is a plus).

-

Native or near-native **German** language skills; strong English proficiency required.

-

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills — you're comfortable picking up the phone and starting conversations.

-

Self-motivated, curious, and eager to learn in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

-

Familiarity with CRM tools and sales engagement platforms is advantageous.

-

Interest in or exposure to the construction industry, AI, or SaaS is a bonus — but not required.

-

Ability to work from our Munich office in a hybrid setup.

**Visa sponsorship:** Available for eligible candidates who can obtain work authorization in Germany.

### Compensation & Benefits

-

**Base salary:** €45,000 – €60,000 per year, depending on experience.

-

Opportunity to grow quickly within the sales organization as the company scales.

-

Work alongside a high-calibre team with backgrounds at leading global tech, consulting, and AI research organizations.

-

Hybrid work environment with flexible arrangements.

### Location

This is a **hybrid role based in Munich, Germany**. Candidates should be able to commute to the Munich office regularly. Visa sponsorship is available for eligible applicants.
