### The Challenge

Commercial Advisory is a top team of management consultants responsible for localizing the company’s commercial strategy to their regional markets serving as advisors to Sales leadership. The Advisory team is also responsible for building, structuring and closing deals for the Enterprise market segment in close partnership with the Sales organization, contributing to achieve ACV and growth targets. We take ownership, anticipate challenges, identify opportunities, and leverage our expertise always considering the big picture. We are looking for a Commercial Advisory Director to join our team and transform the way that we go to market. This role reports to the Head of Commercial Advisory and aligns with one of our Sales RVPs.

### Your Mission

-

Own the commercial strategy for their regional market

-

Work with their partner Enterprise RVP to localize regional commercial strategies

-

Assess and prioritize opportunities based on potential

-

Work closely with account teams at opportunity level, building and negotiating the deals always looking for the best possible outcome for OneTrust and the customer

-

Build and deliver compelling investment justifications to secure customer funding for OneTrust

-

Put together attractive deal structures and articulate the value of our proposals
