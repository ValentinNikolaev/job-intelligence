**The opportunity**

• Ideal Start Date: October 1st.

• Contract type: Mini Job - Fixed-term (1 year).

• Schedule: 10 hours per week (5 hours per day, 2 days per week).

• Location: Onsite - Berlin

**You will:**

• Manage the cables: Route, bundle, and organize desk wiring, power strips, and cables across workstations and meeting rooms to ensure a clean, hazard-free setup.

• Hardware Maintenance & Repair: Diagnose, troubleshoot, and fix basic hardware issues with office IT equipment (e.g., monitors, peripherals, basic desk hardware).

• Equipment Organization: Keep IT storage spaces tidy, organize tech accessories (adapters, dongles, cables), and maintain basic stock order.

• Desk Maintenance: Assist with workstation adjustments, cable mounting, and setting up hardware for team members.

**You bring:**

• Prior experience troubleshooting, fixing, and repairing IT hardware/equipment.

• Practical, hands-on attitude with strong attention to detail.

• Familiarity with standard tech connections and basic hardware components (Ethernet, HDMI, USB-C, power cables).

• Ability to work neatly and stay organized independently.
