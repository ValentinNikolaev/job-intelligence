**ABOUT THE ROLE**

As a Senior Product Designer at WunderGraph, you are the connection between what our users will find most helpful and what our engineers build. Considering the users also happen to be other developers, this becomes an interesting challenge to think about the Developer Experience as the User Experience. You combine sharp user insight, craft, and pragmatism to deliver experiences that are elegant, usable, and built to scale.

This is a wide-reaching role, as design roles at a startup should be. One week you might be running user interviews to shape a new feature; the next, refining our design system or designing a marketing landing page. You ground your work in evidence rather than assumption, partner closely with product and engineering, and turn what you learn about users into a clear, informed UX strategy that guides where we go next.

-

**ROLE TASKS**

The role focuses on, but is not limited to:

### **Product Design**

-

Design and prototype user flows, interfaces, and interactions with scalability in mind.

-

Own design problems end to end. From initial concept through execution and iteration after launch.

-

Rapidly produce wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs, exploring multiple concepts and iterating quickly.

-

Define and evolve our design system and component library, keeping it consistent and maintainable.

-

Partner closely with engineers on feasibility, quality, and handoff, contributing to technical decisions (and coding where you're comfortable and it helps as a plus, not a requirement).

-

Introduce advanced prototyping, interaction design, and motion practices.

### **UX Research & Strategy**

-

Plan and conduct user research across discovery and validation via user interviews, surveys, usability testing, and competitor analysis.

-

Turn ambiguous product questions into decision-grade research that maps to real product and design decisions.

-

Synthesize findings into clear insights, personas, journey maps, and opportunity areas that the team can act on.

-

Build and maintain a shared insight repository so learning is reusable, searchable, and available to everyone.

-

Translate research into an informed, evolving UX strategy that guides the roadmap and anchors design direction in real user needs.

-

Advocate for the user in prioritization and roadmap discussions, and define what "better" looks like through clear UX success metrics.

-

Run discovery workshops and share insights so research shows up where decisions are made like in design reviews, product planning, and standups embedding user knowledge from the get-go.

### **Web & Brand Experience**

-

Own the design and evolution of WunderGraph's web presence.

-

Deliver assets that span product and marketing: landing pages, visuals, campaign graphics, and interactive components.

-

Shape and maintain a cohesive style guide, balancing design innovation with consistency.

-

Support brand storytelling with motion graphics and interactive elements.
