### Ready to build at the crossroads of Marketing Tech and Entertainment?

JustWatch provides data-driven digital marketing for the global entertainment industry. We build internal tools that help our campaign managers automate workflows and unlock innovative capabilities in entertainment marketing.

We’re hiring a **Product Manager** for our **MarTech** team. This role is perfect for someone who is highly organised and can create clarity in an unstructured environment.

**What you will be doing**

- Ensuring weekly progress toward quarterly goals.

- Testing new releases hands-on, verifying behaviour, tracking issues, and scheduling fixes.

- Monitoring product metrics and system health, flagging issues early.

- Using our internal automation tools to understand real user workflows.

- Stepping in quickly when issues surface to help coordinate a resolution.

- **Taking ownership **of smaller product areas as you** grow**.

**What you will NOT be doing**

- Jira administration or ticket shuffling - we work lightweight, and we optimise for speed.

- Make meetings for the sake of process or ceremony.

- Wait for work to fall on your desk - you’ll know what needs to be **prioritised next**.

**What we need from you**

- You’re comfortable working with unclear problems and incomplete information, and can **create structure on your own**.

- You keep work moving: follow up, unblock, prioritise, and **ensure things ship.**

- **You ask the right questions** to unlock core pain points and strategise solutions.

- You organise work using simple tools (Docs, Sheets), not PM frameworks or Jira workflows.

- You can write development-ready project documents **with no AI fluff**.

- You understand **MarTech fundamentals** (campaign workflows, tracking, automation, integrations) and why they’re complex.

- You’re **data-strong**: you explore datasets, can use BI tools (e.g. Lightdash), and conclude.

- You’re comfortable working close to technical systems: reading logs, debugging issues, understanding APIs, and supporting automation.

- **You’ve shipped before** - a dashboard, tool, workflow, script, automation, or project.

**What we offer you**

- You'll have a direct impact on how the global marketing campaigns are run.

- You’ll see immediately how improvements (and mistakes) affect our bottom line.

- Incredible learning opportunity. You’ll work closely with the MarTech PM, developers, and founders who are actively involved in shaping the company.

- A culture built on autonomy, trust, and continuous iteration - not corporate ladder climbing.

- A team that deeply **cares about delivering results**.
