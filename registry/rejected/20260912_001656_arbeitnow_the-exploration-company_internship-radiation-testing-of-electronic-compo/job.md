# Internship - Radiation Testing of Electronic Components and Test Automation

Posted: 2026-09-11T23:40:04Z

## Rejection

- Category: role_mismatch

Here at The Exploration Company, we are building innovative aerospace technologies that advance the future of space transportation.

We want you as a talented Radiation Test and Automation Engineer to help us in our mission by participating in the qualification of the electronic parts that will compose our Nyx Avionics system.

## Key Responsibilities

In your capacity as a Radiation Test and Automation Engineer, your role will be continuously evolving, but day to day your duties will include:

-

Assembly of radiation test PCBs for components to be tested

-

Test of the assembled PCBs and debugging of circuits in the laboratory

-

Soldering and adapting of parts if needed

-

Automation of radiation test setups and test report generation

-

Attending the radiation test campaign at the particle accelerator facility, if required

-

Understanding the effects of radiation to electronic circuits and create comprehensive test reports

## What we would love to see from you

In the role of Radiation Test and Automation Engineer ideally, you have collected some experience with the following:

-

A masters/bachelor's degree in electronic science or engineering / Physics / embedded systems design

-

Hands-on soldering and debugging experience of electronics circuits

-

Basic knowledge of verification and validation of avionics and embedded software

-

Experience with Python for test automation and control of devices and data visualization

-

Digital and Analog Electronic Design Knowledge

-

Experience with Altium Designer and LTSPICE or comparable software solutions is a plus

-

Basic understanding of space avionics is a plus
