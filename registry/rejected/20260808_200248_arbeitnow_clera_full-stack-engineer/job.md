### About the Role

Join a small, product-focused team at a **bootstrapped AI platform and consulting studio** based in Munich, Germany. You'll be a core contributor to the platform that enables thousands of users to interact with AI every day — building high-quality full-stack features and the scalable backend systems that power them. From synchronizing large client datasets and running massive LLM workloads to architecting secure sandboxed environments, you'll own meaningful, high-impact work end to end. This is a **hybrid** role based in Munich.

### What You'll Do

-

Build and ship high-quality full-stack features using **TypeScript** and **React**, focused on a seamless user experience.

-

Design and implement robust backend systems capable of handling massive data scale and millions of API requests.

-

Take full ownership of features from concept and deployment through to production operation, ensuring reliability and security.

-

Debug production incidents, identify root causes, and implement lasting solutions.

-

Mentor junior engineers and elevate the codebase through thorough, constructive code reviews.

-

Synchronize and index terabytes of client data while managing fine-grained permissions.

-

Operate and optimize systems that process millions of LLM calls per month with high reliability.

-

Architect and scale environments that spawn thousands of secure code sandboxes per day.

-

Build foundational infrastructure that enables rapid onboarding of custom integrations.

-

Shape how users interact with the platform by working across the core stack and AI integrations.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

3+ years of professional software engineering experience delivering full-stack features with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **PostgreSQL**.

-

Proven experience with **Kubernetes**-based deployment and container orchestration.

-

Hands-on experience building and running production-grade backend services at scale — high data volumes, millions of API requests, with a focus on reliability and security.

-

Comfortable working in **Dockerized** environments with robust **CI/CD pipelines**.

-

End-to-end product ownership mindset: from concept through deployment to live operation.

-

Strong debugging skills and experience resolving production incidents.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Experience with backend technologies such as **Python** or **tRPC**.

-

Experience working with GenAI platforms or LLM integrations.

-

Track record of mentoring junior engineers and leading code reviews.

-

Familiarity with high-throughput distributed services.

**You'll thrive here if you:**

-

Care deeply about user experience and long-term product quality.

-

Learn quickly and enjoy operating in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

-

Take pride in production ownership and have a strong instinct for reliability and security.
