**Your Role and Responsibilities:**

- Developing and planning the AI Factory’s (AIF) portfolio of education & training offers within a friendly and open work environment in a highly motivated, international team
- Designing and organizing educational events, trainings and training series, collaborating with other trainers
- Ability and willingness to contribute with your knowledge to educational (and consultancy) offers for, primarily, manufacturing and engineering SMEs and start-ups
- Analysing the requirements and needs of targeted user groups with respect to training (and consulting)
- Collaborating within the corresponding work package of the AIF
- Fostering contact with collaboration partners as well as stakeholders from the European AI ecosystem, relying on your friendly personality and communication skills
