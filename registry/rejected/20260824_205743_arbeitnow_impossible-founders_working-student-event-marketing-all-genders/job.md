**Your mission**

You help create experiences that people remember. Working closely with our Event Manager, you support the planning and execution of our events – from Impossible Founders' own formats to our presence at conferences and trade fairs. You keep the operational details under control so the team can focus on the bigger picture, and help turn it into content for our channels afterward.

**Event Coordination & Operations (your main focus)**Planning & vendors

- Research and coordinate logistics with vendors — venues, catering, equipment, tech, design, and decoration
- Maintain vendor contact lists and keep the CRM/database current
- Benchmark other events and scout venues
Guest & stakeholder communication

- Manage registration lists, RSVPs, and guest communications
- Support coordination with partners, sponsors, and institutions
- Support calendar scheduling and internal event announcements
On-site execution

- Prepare name badges, signage, and printed materials
- Order and track event supplies and giveaways
- Set up and break down event spaces (e.g. signage, seating, tech)
Wrap-up & reporting

- Take photos and notes for internal documentation
- Collect feedback forms and survey results, and help pull together simple success metrics (attendance, feedback, reach)
- Reconcile invoices and keep the budget tracker up to date
- Archive event materials and photos in the shared drive/Notion, and turn it all into reports on comms and press impact

**Marketing & Communication (supporting role)**

- Help grow our brand presence across our channels and different communication opportunities
- Support storytelling around our events and the journey of our startups, and help bring our brand to life through general marketing operation tasks
- Scout trends and support content research

**Your profile**

**What matters most to us**

- Enrolled in a degree program related to event management, communication, marketing, hospitality, or a comparable field
- First experience in events, hospitality, or project coordination (personal or through internships)
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail; comfortable juggling multiple tasks against tight deadlines, and staying calm and friendly even right before an event starts
- Structured, proactive, and solution-oriented way of working
- Reliable and curious to explore new tools and topics
- Excited about a steep learning curve in a meaningful environment
- Able to work independently and prioritize tasks
- Fluent in German and English
- Genuine interest in the startup, DeepTech, or innovation ecosystem

**Additionally, it would be a plus if you bring**

- Basic skills in Canva, video editing, or social media
- Copywriting skills
