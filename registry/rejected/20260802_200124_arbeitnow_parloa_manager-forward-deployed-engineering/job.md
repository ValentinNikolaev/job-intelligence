## **About the role:**

As a **Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering **at Parloa, you’ll be on the front lines of enterprise AI adoption — leading a high-impact team of FDEs that delivers our most strategic enterprise deployments. Your mission is to ensure our consistent deployment excellence: predictable delivery, strong engineering quality, and fast unblocking in complex enterprise environments — while staying technically credible yourself.

FDEs are our in-field engineers. They build what doesn’t exist yet, adapt architecture to enterprise realities, and engineer integrations across systems and APIs to make deployments successful. As a Manager, you make that work repeatable and reliable across multiple customer projects — through strong delivery leadership, clear priorities, and hands-on technical guidance.

## **Areas of ownership:**

- **Lead and develop the team:** hire, coach, and grow a team of FDEs; set clear expectations; run 1:1s, feedback cycles, and performance development.

- **Own capacity planning and staffing:** allocate engineers across parallel strategic projects, manage load and utilization, and anticipate delivery bottlenecks early.

- **Drive project delivery management:** own engineering delivery across deployments — milestones, dependencies, risk management, escalation paths, and stakeholder alignment.

- **Establish time tracking and delivery visibility:** implement lightweight tracking and reporting that creates trusted insight into effort, progress, and capacity (without bureaucracy).

- **Provide technical leadership and escalation support:** guide architecture and implementation choices, review designs/code when needed, and unblock high-severity issues in production environments.

- **Create scalable field engineering practices:** build reusable patterns, playbooks, and standards that improve reliability, maintainability, and speed across deployments.

- **Shape the product via field learnings: **translate recurring customer needs into clear productization proposals and partner with Product/Core Engineering to reduce one-offs over time.

- **Partner with customer engineering and enterprise stakeholders:** collaborate directly with customer teams (architecture, security, platform) to deliver outcomes and build long-term technical trust.

### **Our Tech Stack: **

## **Who you are:**

- **Experience level:** 8+ years in software engineering, systems integration, DevOps, or data engineering roles with meaningful production responsibility.

- **People leadership:** proven ability to lead and develop engineers (e.g., formal management, team lead responsibility, coaching, hiring, performance growth).

- **Enterprise delivery track record:** demonstrated success delivering complex technical projects in large enterprise environments with multiple stakeholders and constraints.

- **Technical breadth and depth:** strong capabilities across backend engineering, APIs, cloud platforms, Kubernetes, infrastructure-as-code, and databases — able to review designs and guide production decisions.

- **Hands-on effectiveness:** able to personally own and drive smaller technical workstreams end-to-end and to jump into debugging/implementation when necessary.

- **Operational excellence:** strong planning, prioritization, capacity thinking, and risk management; creates delivery clarity without unnecessary process overhead.

- **Customer-facing communication:** confident and credible with customer engineering teams and senior technical stakeholders; clear written and verbal communication.

- **Strong plus: experience building or integrating AI/LLM-powered systems (agent workflows, orchestration, evaluation, vector DBs, guardrails), ideally in forward-deployed or solutions engineering environments.**
