###

###
More Than a Role

We don't just need another Senior Software Engineer—we're looking for a builder who enjoys solving complex engineering challenges and delivering scalable, high-quality software that creates real business impact.

You'll play a key role in shaping and building the technology powering our Talent Operations platform, working on backend services while contributing to frontend development when needed. You'll influence technical decisions, contribute to system architecture, and collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, and cross-functional stakeholders to deliver reliable, scalable solutions that support our global business.

We're looking for engineers who embrace modern software development practices and actively leverage AI-powered development tools such as **Cursor** and **Claude Code** to improve productivity and code quality.

###
Where Responsibility Meets Real Opportunity…

- Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services while contributing to frontend features where required.
- Build high-quality, well-tested software following modern engineering and DevOps best practices.
- Contribute to architecture, system design, and technical decision-making across the platform.
- Collaborate closely with Product Managers, Designers, and Engineers to deliver impactful solutions.
- Review code, mentor teammates, and continuously improve engineering standards.
- Troubleshoot production issues and continuously enhance system performance, reliability, and scalability.

###
What It Takes to Make an Impact Here…

- 5–6+ years of professional software engineering experience with a strong backend focus and frontend exposure.
- Strong hands-on experience with **Java, Spring Boot, React, Microservices, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and AWS**.
- Experience designing scalable systems and distributed architectures.
- Daily experience using AI coding tools such as **Cursor** and **Claude Code**.
- Strong understanding of Test-Driven Development (TDD), software engineering best practices, and clean code principles.
- Excellent English communication skills, both written and spoken.

###
Bonus Points If You Bring…

- Experience working in Agile environments (Scrum or Kanban).
- Experience building highly scalable SaaS platforms.
- Passion for continuous learning and adopting modern engineering practices.
