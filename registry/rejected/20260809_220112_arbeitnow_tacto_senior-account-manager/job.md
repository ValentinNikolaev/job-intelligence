## Your Impact

We're strong at landing new customers. Now we need to get excellent at growing them.

Expanding within the customer base is one of Tacto's biggest growth levers, and we're building it out for the long term. Most mature B2B companies generate 50%+ of their revenue from existing customers - we want to capture more of that potential, and we're strengthening the team to get there. As Account Manager, you'll be a driving force behind our expansion motion: identifying and closing upsells, cross-sells, and strategic price increases across our customer base, and helping build and sharpen the playbook as we scale it. You'll work between Sales, Customer Value, and Product, spotting growth opportunities and turning them into revenue. This is your chance to build and grow a crucial commercial function at one of Europe's fastest growing industrial AI companies.

## Your Tasks

-

**Own deals end-to-end:** Identify opportunities, build business cases, run negotiations, close deals. Manage contracts and pricing logic.

-

**Master account intelligence:** Read customer dynamics, map stakeholders, understand politics. Know when to drive expansion yourself vs. when to bring in AEs or Customer Value. Identify perfect timing for upsells.

-

**Coordinate across functions:** Coordinate with Sales (pipeline), Customer Success (value-delivery), Product (what to sell), and Finance (forecasting) to drive expansion deals

-

**Own forecasting and planning:** Build accurate expansion forecasts. Coordinate deal timelines. Keep the business informed and on track.

-

**Translate roadmap into revenue**: Work with Product and Marketing to turn new modules into concrete expansion conversations with the right customers at the right time.

-

**Help building the function as it scales:** Turn what you learn into reusable process, qualification criteria, pricing guardrails, forecasting templates, rules of engagement with Sales and Customer Value. Where the playbook doesn't exist yet, you help writing it

## **Our Background**

Tacto is building the intelligence layer that connects AI to the physical world of manufacturing, starting with procurement. AI is transforming every industry, but it stops at the factory gate. The knowledge that holds manufacturing together - part costs, suppliers, materials - is scattered across systems and people. Over 50% of a product's cost originates from suppliers, making procurement the single largest cost lever in manufacturing and the missing connection between the digital and the physical world. Yet it still runs on Excel and legacy systems. We're changing that with our Procurement Intelligence Platform, trusted by hundreds of companies running billions of Euros of material.

Based in Munich, we are 100+ passionate builders who are in-office, with high expectations, fast growth, and a lot of fun. We are supported by >€50m from Sequoia, Index, and Europe's leading tech entrepreneurs and industry veterans.

## **Why It Matters**

Manufacturing forms Europe's economic backbone, driving a fourth of Europe's GDP and providing over 30% of jobs. These hidden champions and global market leaders manufacture the physical world around us, from the machines that build our cities to the medical devices that save lives.

But the world is changing. The US dominates the digital world of bits. China is scaling the world of atoms, manufacturing at unprecedented speed and scale. Geopolitics are more uncertain than ever. Europe's strength lies in its deep industrial knowledge through generations of engineering. Manufacturing worldwide runs on European machines and software. But that heritage is only an advantage if we leverage it in the age of AI.

Tacto builds the AI that connects Atoms and Bits, empowering our Industrial Base to Stay Stronger and building a lighthouse for European tech.
