*Join the founding team behind an immersive AI-powered VR training platform*

**About iACT**

iACT is an AI-powered immersive platform that helps international talent navigate social, professional, academic, and administrative life in Germany through realistic, interactive practice.

We have a functional prototype and are preparing for institutional pilots. We are looking for a backend and applied AI engineer who wants to shape the technical foundation of the product from an early stage.

**The opportunity**

This is not a standard salaried vacancy. You would join the prospective founding team and apply with us for the EXIST Business Start-up Grant. The commitment would begin only if the grant is approved. If approved, the programme provides a 12-month, full-time scholarship while the team develops the product and business.

## Tasks

**Your role**

- Own the backend and applied AI architecture connecting the Unity VR client with authentication, databases, speech services, and LLM/RAG pipelines.

- Design secure REST and WebSocket APIs and define reliable client-server contracts with our Unity/VR developer.

- Implement authentication, authorisation, role management, and GDPR-conscious data flows.

- Design and maintain data models for users, sessions, learning progress, and application data.

- Integrate and orchestrate LLM, RAG, Speech-to-Text, and Text-to-Speech services.

- Develop dialogue-state logic and systems for retrieving institution-specific knowledge.

- Optimise AI pipelines for latency, reliability, observability, and cost.

- Support secure, scalable cloud deployment and prepare the platform for institutional pilots and future commercial use.

## Requirements

**What you bring**

- A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.

- At least two years of hands-on backend development experience.

- Strong Python skills and experience with FastAPI, Django, or a comparable framework.

- Experience building REST and/or WebSocket services and working with databases such as PostgreSQL.

- A solid understanding of authentication, authorisation, API security, and scalable backend design.

- Practical experience integrating LLM APIs and/or RAG systems into real applications.

- Comfort designing systems for real-time or interactive clients.

- English proficiency at B1 level or higher

- Based in Germany or willing to relocate, and available for a full-time commitment if the grant is approved.

**Nice to have**

- Experience with vector databases, embeddings, and production RAG pipelines.

- Experience integrating Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech services.

- Docker and cloud deployment experience with AWS, Azure, GCP, Hetzner, or similar.

- Knowledge of GDPR and privacy-by-design principles.

- Experience with C#/.NET, Unity integrations, VR/XR, or other real-time interactive applications.

## Benefits

**What we offer**

- Ownership of the backend and applied AI architecture from prototype to pilot.

- A small, founder-led team with substantial influence over technical direction and product decisions.

- Flexible working hours and a high degree of creative freedom.

- If the EXIST application is approved, a 12-month monthly scholarship of €3,000 for doctorate holders, €2,500 for university graduates, €2,000 for team members with vocational qualifications, or €1,000 for students, plus €150 per child.

- Following the funding phase, the opportunity to discuss a long-term Co-Founder/CTO role with equity, subject to mutual fit and a separate agreement.
