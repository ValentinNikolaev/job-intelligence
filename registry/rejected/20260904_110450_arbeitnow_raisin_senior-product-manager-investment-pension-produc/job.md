Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the **FinTech** connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers **better interest rates** and banks a **diversified form of refinancing**. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.

Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from **more than one million investors** which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.

**Team**

The Investment and Pension Products tribe builds Raisin's capital markets–based products. We take complex, highly regulated financial systems and turn them into easy, transparent, and scalable product experiences. If you have prior experience in Product Management or related fields, have a passion for Fintech, and are eager to join us on this mission, this role is for you.

**Your Responsibilities**

- Own one of Raisin’s Investments and Pensions product areas end to end, from problem discovery to delivery, adoption, and measurable business impact.

- Develop a deep understanding of your product area (customers, market, regulation, competitive landscape) and identify the highest-leverage opportunities across the broader domain.

- Shape and maintain a value-driven 12-month roadmap, balancing strategic bets, improvements, and platform/operational needs; align stakeholders through clear narratives and trade-off decisions.

- Set and track quarterly OKRs with your Engineering Lead, ensuring plans are grounded, measurable, and connected to Raisin’s strategy; you’re accountable for outcomes, not just output.

- Lead cross-functional collaboration with Engineering, Design, Data/Analytics, Operations, Legal/Compliance, and other stakeholders to deliver a seamless customer experience.

- Communicate effectively through written product artifacts (e.g., PRDs, user journeys, wireframes, sequence diagrams, experiment plans, and release notes), providing enough clarity to enable autonomous team execution while keeping scope focused and aligned.

**Your Profile**

**Must haves**

- At least 3 years of Product Management hands on experience leading a cross functional team.

- Demonstrated success in defining, launching, and scaling complex products that combine deep technical systems, non-trivial business logic, and high operational or regulatory constraints.

- Proven ability to collaborate with diverse stakeholders (Engineering, Design, Business, Compliance, Legal, Marketing, Executive Leadership) and drive cross-functional alignment on product vision and prioritization. You have a sufficient grasp of these disciplines, such that you can sketch high-level solutions to product problems in software design artifacts.

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.

- Owner’s mentality: you tackle challenges head-on and drive solutions with conviction.

- Highly data-driven approach to prioritization and decision-making, including the ability to define KPIs, create product impact dashboards, and synthesize data into actionable product requirements.

- A track record of leveraging qualitative (customer interviews) and quantitative (product usage data) insights to validate assumptions and drive product strategy.

**Bonus qualifications**

- Fluency in German.

- Experience in Investment products, preferably with a leading European Fintech, investment firm or a Bank’s investment division.

- Experience with Looker or similar analytical tools.
