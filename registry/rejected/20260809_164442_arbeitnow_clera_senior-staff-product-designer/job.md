### About the Role

We're a well-funded, Series A AI-powered procurement intelligence platform serving the industrial sector — helping companies modernise how they manage spend, suppliers, and sourcing through a suite of AI agents. Our product is used daily by non-technical procurement professionals who need AI to feel **natural, trustworthy, and genuinely useful** — not like a black box bolted onto a legacy workflow.

We're hiring a **Senior or Staff Product Designer** to join a small, high-craft design team embedded closely with product managers, engineers, and domain experts. This is a pivotal role: you'll define how agentic AI capabilities surface in the product — from the moment an agent starts working, through to how findings and recommendations are presented and acted upon. Great design here doesn't just improve usability; it directly determines whether powerful AI features get adopted and whether customers realise real cost savings.

### What You'll Do

-

Define interaction patterns for agentic AI capabilities — covering status states ("the agent is working"), result surfaces ("here's what it found"), and human-in-the-loop collaboration moments.

-

Design workflows that position AI and human judgment as collaborators, not competitors, with a clear focus on trust and transparency.

-

Produce short-horizon vision work (3–6 month horizon) as runnable prototypes and tangible artefacts that align and guide the broader team.

-

Raise overall product craft and coherence as engineering velocity and feature output scale.

-

Work directly in code for last-mile polish — check out PRs and fine-tune interactions before features ship.

-

Partner with product managers, engineers, and procurement domain experts to translate complex, data-heavy workflows into clear, usable experiences.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Senior-level product design experience with a strong portfolio of *shipped* work — ideally in B2B SaaS, complex workflows, or data-heavy products.

-

A "block-shaped" designer: solid across visual design, interaction design, product thinking, *and* prototyping in code — not a single-specialty generalist.

-

Demonstrated ability to prototype beyond Figma — you build runnable prototypes that communicate intent, behaviour, and edge cases.

-

Comfortable checking out PRs and making direct code-level interaction tweaks before shipping (this is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have).

-

Thoughtful, well-formed opinions on how AI should appear in products — trust, transparency, appropriate automation, and avoiding over-automation.

-

Comfortable operating in ambiguity and inventing new patterns for genuinely novel problems.

-

Active daily user of AI tools (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) as part of your research, ideation, and prototyping workflow.

**Nice to Have:**

-

Experience designing for non-technical users in complex B2B domains (e.g. procurement, supply chain, manufacturing, finance).

-

Prior work on agentic or autonomous AI features in a shipped product.

-

Familiarity with industrial or procurement software contexts.
