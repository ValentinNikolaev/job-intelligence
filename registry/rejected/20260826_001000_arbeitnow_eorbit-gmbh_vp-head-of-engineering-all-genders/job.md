**Your mission**

Full technical ownership of Rocket — reporting directly to the founders, with no layer in between

In this role you own engineering at MICE DESK — fully. There is no CTO layer above you: architecture, technology, delivery, and team are yours, and you report directly to the founders. This is a hands-on leadership role. You lead the engineering team including our AI/Data function, own the interface to Product, and are accountable for everything that ships.

- **Engineering, end-to-end.** Architecture, system design, delivery against the roadmap, reliability, technical debt. Backend, frontend, DevOps, QA, and the AI/Data team all roll up to you. You set lightweight processes that let a small team ship fast without breaking things.
- **An AI-enabled engineering pipeline.** We don't just build AI products — we build with AI. You make the engineering organization itself AI-native: agentic coding workflows, AI-assisted review and testing, automation of everything repetitive. A small team should ship like one three times its size — making that true is your job.
- **AI as a core system capability.** You architect the integration of LLMs and agentic workflows into production systems: deterministic logic combined with AI-generated outputs, with real validation, fallbacks, and reliability strategies. Hotel operations run on our platform — non-determinism is no excuse for unreliability.
- **The team.** Hire, mentor, scale. You inherit a strong small team and grow it into a high-performance engineering organization with a culture of ownership and speed. Our distributed hiring strategy across multiple locations is yours as well.
- **Product and business alignment.** You work directly with the founders and Product. You challenge requirements, simplify complexity, and connect engineering work to outcomes: revenue, automation, customer impact. As we enter the US market, engineering has to keep pace with enterprise-scale customers on two continents.

**What success looks like:**

- **6 months:** Delivery is predictable, the AI-enabled dev pipeline is running, and the team has grown with A-players you hired.
- **12 months:** The platform scales with US enterprise customers, core systems are reliable and observable, and engineering is a competitive advantage — not a bottleneck.

**Honestly: **This is full accountability with no net above it. No CTO layer to make or catch decisions — the architecture, the team, and everything that ships are yours. If you want a role where someone above you sets direction, this isn't it. If you want exactly that ownership, it is.

**Your profile**

- 10+ years of software engineering, 5+ in an engineering leadership role at high-growth product companies.
- Strong hands-on backend or full-stack background — you can still go deep.
- Proven experience scaling engineering teams at growing startups.
- Strong architecture expertise: APIs, distributed systems, event-driven design, cloud.
- LLM / agentic AI systems in production — and genuine conviction that engineering itself should be AI-assisted.
- Product mindset: you connect technical decisions to business outcomes.
- Comfortable with ambiguity, speed, and full accountability.

**Nice to have, not required: **Background in hospitality tech, CRM, PMS, or revenue management. Enterprise integration experience (Salesforce, Oracle OPERA Cloud, Microsoft Graph API). Experience leading distributed, international teams.

**Why us?**

We are the technology company rethinking group and event sales in hospitality. What runs today through inboxes, spreadsheets and phone calls, our platform Rocket turns into one continuous, AI-supported process — from the first enquiry to the signed contract. European hotel groups already run their group sales on it.
What that means for you: you work with AI, not despite it. People who join us give feedback that shapes product decisions — and sooner or later come to understand why one prompt works better than another.
And because we are growing fast, a lot here is still taking shape. Sometimes the honest answer is “we’re still building that.” That is exactly where the opportunity lies: you will find open space rather than finished structures, and what you build here will carry your signature.
