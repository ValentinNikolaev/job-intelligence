**ABOUT POSITION**

The **Portfolio Commercial Director** is responsible for the overall commercial performance of a blended portfolio of full service hotels, strongly MICE driven hotels in key markets in Germany.

This role holds end-to-end accountability for commercial strategy and execution, covering Sales, Revenue Management, Distribution, and Marketing, with a strong focus on sustainable revenue growth, market share expansion, and profitability, while ensuring alignment with owner expectations and long-term asset value creation.

As a senior commercial leader, the role acts as a trusted advisor to hotel owners and asset managers, delivering transparent, data-driven insights and ensuring that all commercial strategies support both short-term performance and long-term financial returns.

The position operates within a matrix organisation, partnering closely with Operations, Finance, and commercial discipline leaders to drive integrated commercial strategies across the portfolio.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:**

• Develop and execute hotel-level commercial strategies aligned with overall business goals.

• Lead forecasting and budgeting processes to ensure accurate financial planning and performance tracking.

• Drive commercial initiatives at both the hotel and country levels to maximize total hotel revenue opportunities.

• Executive report on results and present business analytics to internal and external stakeholders.

• Act as primary commercial liaison with Hotel Operations, ensuring alignment between commercial and operational priorities.

• Oversee account management and achieving sales targets, maintaining strong customer and partner relationships.

• Manage pricing and inventory strategy, ensuring optimal yield and market competitiveness.

• Develop, manage, and recruit commercial team members, in close cooperation with the relevant commercial disciplinary leader, to build strong regional capabilities and performance.

• Lead structured owner communication, including monthly and quarterly reviews, clearly articulating performance drivers, risks, and opportunities

**HOW YOU WILL DO IT**

• Building strong and trusted relationships with hotel owners, asset managers, Hotel Managers, and internal stakeholders.

• Applying sound commercial judgement and data-driven decision-making to drive revenue growth, market share, and profitability.

• Translating complex commercial performance data into clear business insights and actionable strategies.

• Aligning Sales, Revenue Management, Marketing, and Operations around common business objectives.

• Proactively identifying risks, opportunities, and market trends and adapting commercial strategies accordingly.

• Providing clear direction, coaching, and support to commercial teams, fostering a culture of accountability and high performance.

• Influencing stakeholders at all levels through effective communication, collaboration, and executive presence.

• Maintaining a strong focus on owner satisfaction, long-term asset value creation, and sustainable business growth.

• Ensuring commercial excellence through structured planning, forecasting, performance reviews, and continuous improvement.

• Balancing strategic thinking with hands-on execution to deliver measurable business results.
