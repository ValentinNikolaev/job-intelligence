As the Paid Search Marketing Manager for the DE/AT market, you will play a key role in driving growth and profitability through expertly managed paid search campaigns. You will have the opportunity to work on high-impact initiatives, shaping how we connect with both sides of our marketplace - homeowners and tradespeople. We are looking for someone who thrives in a dynamic environment and is committed to consistently improving performance while exploring new opportunities to innovate and expand. This role reports to a Paid Search Director.

**What you’ll do:**

-

Own the day-to-day management and optimization of Google Ads and Microsoft Ads campaigns, ensuring they deliver maximum profitability.

-

Conduct in-depth keyword research, running ad copy tests, and analyzing performance to continually improve profitability and ROAS.

-

Partner with our Product and Analytics teams to implement advanced reporting and actionable insights.

-

Stay ahead of the curve by monitoring industry trends, testing new strategies, and exploring untapped opportunities for paid search growth.

-

Leverage AI tools and agents (e.g. Claude-based workflows, Google Ads Scripts automation) for campaign monitoring, reporting, and anomaly detection to work more efficiently and surface insights faster.

This role gives you the chance to make a real impact while working in an environment that encourages innovation and data-driven experimentation. Your contributions will directly shape the success of our paid marketing efforts in a key European market.
