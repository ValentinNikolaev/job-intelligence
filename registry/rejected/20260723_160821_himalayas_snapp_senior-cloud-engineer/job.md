### Our Journey So Far

Our ride-hailing and mobility platform connects millions of riders and drivers every day, delivering safe, reliable, and efficient transport solutions. Powered by real-time data and robust infrastructure, we make urban travel faster, simpler, and more sustainable.

We operate with the mindset of a global tech leader and the agility of a startup, building services that scale across markets while staying responsive to local needs.

**Your Impact**
As a **Senior****Cloud Engineer**, you will be at the forefront of developing and maintaining the infrastructure that powers [Snapp](https://himalayas.app/companies/snapp)'s cutting-edge cloud technologies. You will drive the automation and optimization of deployment, scaling, and maintenance processes, ensuring that [Snapp](https://himalayas.app/companies/snapp)’s cloud environment remains scalable, resilient, and up-to-date.

### What You’ll Drive Forward

-

**Cloud Infrastructure Automation:** Develop and manage infrastructure automation using tools like Ansible, Terraform, Helm charts, and Golang to scale and maintain the cloud environment efficiently.

-

**Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC):** Apply best practices in Infrastructure-as-Code, automating cloud operations to improve deployment speed and reduce errors.

-

**Innovating with Open-Source Technologies:** Investigate and integrate new open-source tools and technologies to enhance cloud capabilities and introduce new features.

-

**Issue Resolution & Maintenance:** Troubleshoot and resolve issues in the cloud, ensuring the continued smooth operation of critical components and keeping them up-to-date.

-

**Working with Cutting-Edge Technologies:** Work with technologies such as OpenShift Kubernetes Distribution (OKD), OpenStack, Ceph, and Kubernetes.

-

**Collaboration & Support:** Collaborate with other teams, contribute to documentation, and assist developers by providing best practices for deploying and maintaining services on the cloud platform.

-

**Runbook Contributions:** Contribute to runbooks and participate in the on-call rotation to ensure system reliability.

-

**Monitoring & Alerting:** Work with advanced monitoring and alerting systems to ensure the health and performance of cloud services.

### What Powers Your Drive

-

**Cloud Technologies Expertise:** Strong experience with **Linux**, **networking**, and **OpenStack**, **Ceph**, including deploying, configuring, and managing clusters.

-

**Experience with Cloud Platforms:** Knowledge of **Kubernetes**, **OKD**, and familiarity with **GitOps** methodologies.

-

**Programming Skills:**Familiarity with Programming (preferably Golang): experience with writing k8s controllers/operators and Prometheus exporters.

-

**Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC)** tools like **Terraform** and **Ansible** is a plus.

-

**Problem Solving:** A sharp analytical mindset with the ability to troubleshoot, identify root causes, and resolve complex issues within the cloud infrastructure.

-

**Team Collaboration:** Ability to work effectively within a team, support users, and help build new cloud features.

-

**Adaptability & Learning:** Quick to learn new technologies and tools, staying ahead of the curve to drive improvements in cloud services.
