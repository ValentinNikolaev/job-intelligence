**What You Will Do**

KoRo is scaling its fulfillment setup across multiple warehouses and markets. As our Senior Product Manager - Outbound Logistics & Returns (m/f/d), you will own and shape the systems behind the journey from order placement to delivery and returns, making them more automated, scalable and efficient.

- Own the full order-to-delivery and returns journey, from Shop through ERP/OMS and WMS to carrier integrations, including tracking updates back to the customer
- Shape our distributed fulfillment setup by designing the product logic for automated warehouse allocation and order routing across multiple warehouses
- Identify and prioritize automation opportunities to reduce manual intervention, improve time-to-ship and optimize cost per parcel
- Focus on measurable outcomes, using data and relevant metrics to identify problems, set priorities and measure impact
- Validate significant product opportunities through hypothesis-driven discovery and experimentation before committing engineering capacity
- Work closely with Engineering, Logistics & Operations, as well as external 3PL and carrier partners, to build scalable and reliable outbound flows
- Ensure new solutions support our evolving architecture and maintain platform stability and scalability throughout ongoing replatforming

**What You Bring**

- 5+ years of product management experience with backend, integration-heavy or operational products
- Hands-on experience with commerce operations systems such as OMS, ERP, WMS, carrier integrations and API-based partner flows
- A proven track record of automating operational processes and delivering measurable improvements
- Strong analytical skills and confidence working with KPIs, dashboards and operational data
- The ability to navigate complex dependencies and collaborate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Experience with multi-warehouse fulfillment, international shipping, EDI, or both D2C and B2B/retail order flows is a plus
- Fluent English; German is a plus
