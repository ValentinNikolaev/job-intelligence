**RESPONSIBILITIES**:

-

Drive the use of AI across the Production team, responsible for finding agent-driven solutions to optimise all aspects of the production workload.

-

Consult and work closely with a range of Enterprise customers to understand their business needs and align LILT’s SLAs and KPIs to measure, achieve and exceed these.

-

Lead implementation of processes and systems to meet customer requirements — including customised workflow, project management, invoicing and service delivery — with automation and agentic tooling at the core of solution design.

-

Leads the onboarding of new customers, taking an AI-first approach to accelerate and standardise onboarding.

-

Scopes new services, including pricing proposals and SLAs, using intelligent tooling to support scoping, estimation and proposal development.

-

Provides local support in the required time zone for direct customer engagement on strategic topics and initiatives.

-

Lead complex remediations from beginning to end; participate in LILT’s continuous improvement program, using automation to identify root causes and prevent recurrence.

-

Work cross functionally to develop AI agents to manage multi step processes, allowing Production Staff to focus on high level goals and edge cases.

-

Eliminate waste and quality issues from Production processes by agentising workflows and processes as much as possible.

-

Maintain and exceed performance against KPIs, with automated monitoring and reporting, to include, but not limited to:

-

Account Health

-

Onboarding (Time to first dollar)

-

Customer SLAs

-

QRM resolution time

-

Healthy account portfolio financials

**DUTIES**:

-

Onboards new customers and service lines with the appropriate people, processes and tools.

-

Produces competitive and well reasoned quotations, with necessary caveats and assumptions.

-

In collaboration with Solutions and Product, will drive the implementation, testing and iteration of complex technical production workflows to ensure optimal efficiency.

-

Demonstrates strong analytical capabilities to monitor and control production team performance against KPIs

-

Directs tasks for production resources to meet and exceed KPIs and customer SLAs

-

Regularly reports internally and externally on production metrics, performance against objectives (including KPIs and customer SLAs), explaining trends, addressing shortfalls proactively with cross-functional action plans.

-

Communicates seamlessly with client contacts, local colleagues, freelancers and internal project team members.

-

Manages production escalations, drives RCA and CAPA to completion.

-

Conducts post-mortems on key account initiatives, sharing results and planning follow-up actions with key stakeholders to ensure continuous improvements and learnings.

-

Implements account onboarding, process and workflows, escalating requirements for customizations/deviations for appropriate consideration.

-

Demonstrates a deep understanding of all production workflows and processes related to their accounts.

-

Maintains up to date plans and documentation for account

-

Monitors and enforces adherence to correct production procedures

-

Leads the account portfolio team, identifies opportunities for training and development

-

Works in close collaboration with Revenue and Customer Success to ensure comprehensive coverage and implementation of customer requirements while focusing on Production-specific SLAs
