### About the Role

Join a small, high-conviction founding team building an **AI-powered project management platform for the construction industry**. As one of the first engineers, you will own the product technically from day one — partnering directly with the founders to build a category-defining platform that automates documentation, workflow management, decision tracking, and risk analysis for construction projects.

This is a high-ownership, high-impact role where you will work across the full stack, from data models and backend systems to polished, responsive user interfaces, with your code shipping to real customers in days.

**Note: German language fluency is a hard requirement for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Build customer-facing features end-to-end, from database design through beautiful, responsive frontends.

-

Design robust, scalable systems capable of handling complex workflows and large, real-world datasets.

-

Operate in a high-velocity environment where ideas move to production in days, not months.

-

Work with complex construction-industry data and build sophisticated, automated workflows.

-

Contribute directly to product direction and technical architecture alongside the founding team.
