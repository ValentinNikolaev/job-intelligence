### About the Role

We're an early-stage, Munich-based startup building an AI-powered digital assistant for B2B sales teams. Our product automates the administrative burden of sales work — from meeting preparation and real-time note-taking (without audio recording, fully GDPR-compliant) to CRM updates and follow-up management — so sales professionals can focus on what they do best: selling.

Our engineering team is small and moving fast. We're building a true multi-platform product: a **React Web App**, an **Electron Desktop Client**, a **Swift Mobile App**, and two backend services. As an **Associate Founding Engineer (Full-Stack)**, you'll work side-by-side with founding engineers from the very beginning — learning deeply, contributing meaningfully, and growing quickly as you take on increasing responsibility.

**Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Learn from and support founding engineers on real, high-impact product work.

-

Contribute to core features across Web, Desktop, Mobile, and backend services.

-

Receive direct mentorship from senior technical leaders.

-

Participate in product and architecture discussions from day one.

-

Take on greater ownership and responsibility as you demonstrate your strengths.
