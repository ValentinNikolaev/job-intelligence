# Senior Edge Infrastructure Engineer

Posted: 2026-08-04T09:01:27Z

## Rejection

- Category: location_requirement

We are looking for a Senior Edge Infrastructure Engineer to own provisioning and operations for our distributed fleet of Linux-based edge devices, cameras, and sensors. You'll replace manual processes with automated PXE-based deployment and configuration management, and support our onsite Installation Engineers by keeping systems deployment-ready and fleet health strong. Senior individual-contributor role.

**Why join us**

-

**Mission with real impact. **You'll help the world's most essential companies stay competitive, sustainable, and safe in a world of labor shortages and rising costs.

-

**Category-defining moment. **With global manufacturers already deploying Almetra and fresh funding, we're scaling fast across Europe and the US.

-

**Fleet at scale, real ownership. **You'll build the automated backbone behind hundreds of edge devices running in live factories.

-

**High bar, high trust. **You'll work directly with founders, product, and engineering to define how we deploy and operate hardware at scale.

**What you’ll do**

-

Own and develop PXE-based provisioning pipelines; automate configuration, testing, and deployment for Linux edge devices

-

Manage the operational lifecycle of the deployed fleet, including monitoring, patching, and remote diagnostics

-

Support Installation Engineers with fully prepared equipment, clear documentation, and remote troubleshooting

-

Manage secure network configurations (routing, VPN, firewalls) for distributed customer environments

-

Define standards for fleet maintenance, reliability, and automated tooling

**You are**

-

Significant experience administering and troubleshooting Linux systems

-

Hands-on with PXE/network boot and unattended provisioning

-

Proficient in Bash/Python and configuration management/IaC tools

-

Solid networking knowledge (IP, VLANs, DHCP, DNS, Firewalls, VPNs)

-

Experience with WireGuard or similar VPN tools like Tailscale

-

Experience managing distributed fleets at scale

-

Ability to work from Berlin with up to 50% travel to customer sites

-

Professional English proficiency

**Nice to have**

-

Experience building agentic or LLM-based workflows to generate, adapt, or validate infrastructure and device configurations

-

Familiarity with industrial environments (IoT, cameras, fieldbus protocols)

-

Professional German proficiency

-

a valid driver's license to be able to transport equipment to client site and conduct installations.
