# **Your Role**

As a **Senior MRI Application Specialist**, you play a key role in driving our innovation initiatives by assisting in building new applications of our AI-powered MRI technology.

You contribute to laying the groundwork for our advancements by identifying and documenting biomarkers that provide our customers with the answers they seek. In addition, you will be managing and performing data acquisition for our cutting-edge MRI imaging systems. You will be working with an interdisciplinary innovation team, primarily collaborating with MRI scientists, data scientists, and data platform specialists to drive cutting-edge solutions. You will help define new applications working together with the customer, organize logistics, support test runs in-house and on customer sites, and ensure the smooth operation of our unique MRI technologies, as well as actively participate in development of new applications.

With strong attention to detail and a commitment to continuous improvement, you will be integral to our mission of unleashing AI-powered imaging for everyone and everything, helping us push the boundaries of what's possible in imaging.

# **Your Day-to-Day**

-

Build biomarker expertise to further application development within a multidisciplinary team, working closely with members from diverse fields to achieve shared goals.

-

Manage data acquisition for applications, strongly optimizing and automating trial processes to help scale data acquisition and improve operational efficiency.

-

Ensure accurate and consistent capture of unique data points, optimizing data integrity and workflow.

-

Monitor and track system performance by collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data from multiple resources using scientific methods.

-

Review and annotate MRI scans, following established protocols to ensure the accuracy, consistency, and quality of images and annotation results.

-

Travel frequently (up to 40%) to customer sites for extended periods of time to provide on-site support, gather customer needs, work with the customer and their samples. All to translate this into hypotheses and biomarker definitions.

-

Ensure accurate traceability throughout the project lifecycle by documenting all processes, data, and materials from inception to completion.
