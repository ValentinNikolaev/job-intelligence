-

Collaborate closely with UX/UI, Product Management, and Backend to turn product requirements into robust, user-friendly features.

-

Define and continuously improve frontend standards, including coding guidelines, design systems, testing, and development practices.

-

Shape the long-term frontend strategy and evaluate new technologies, frameworks, and tools where they create real value.

-

Support the team through code reviews, technical guidance, knowledge sharing, and AI-supported development workflows.

-

You bring at least 4 years of professional frontend development experience with strong hands-on expertise in Angular and TypeScript.

-

Previous experience in technical leadership, team coordination, or mentoring enables you to guide developers while staying close to the code.

-

You have experience building and maintaining larger web applications or SPAs and are confident with modern frontend architecture and design patterns.

-

Strong knowledge of JavaScript, HTML, CSS/SCSS, responsive design, testing, and software quality practices is part of your toolkit.

-

You are comfortable defining technical standards, conducting code reviews, and making pragmatic architectural decisions with long-term maintainability in mind.

-

Experience with modern Angular concepts and technologies such as NgRx, Signals, SignalStore, NX, TanStack Query, or data visualization libraries is an advantage.

-

You communicate confidently in English and work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
