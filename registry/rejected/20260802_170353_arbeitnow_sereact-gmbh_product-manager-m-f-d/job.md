## **Who We Are:**

We are a rapidly growing embodied AI company revolutionizing physical labor. Leveraging cutting-edge robotics and advanced artificial intelligence, we develop transformative technologies that redefine how work is done across warehouses and fulfillment operations worldwide. Our products represent the frontier of what intelligent, dexterous automation can do — and we are only getting started.

# **Overview:**

We are seeking a Product Manager to own the full product lifecycles — from concept and roadmap to field deployment across 50+ production sites. This is a high ownership, cross functional role at the intersection of AI, robotics hardware, and industrial operations. You will be the connective tissue between our AI/R&D team, software & hardware engineering, solutions architects, and customers — translating real-world operational complexity into a product that runs autonomously at scale with minimal human supervision.

# **Your Responsibilities:**

**Product Strategy & Roadmap**

-

Own the product roadmap across hardware, software, AI, and field reliability

-

Set and defend priorities — monthly to annual — against commercial goals and technical constraints

**AI & Technical Ownership**

-

Shape training data strategy, model evaluation criteria, and deployment thresholds with the R&D team

-

Close the loop between field failure data and Cortex training

**Customer & Field Collaboration**

-

Spend time at production sites; turn operator observations and edge cases into structured requirements

-

Own capability specs, performance SLAs, and integration requirements for customer-facing delivery

**Cross-functional Execution**

-

Run a customer-driven backlog; prioritize by impact, frequency, & strategic fit

-

Sequence work with engineering leads; unblock dependencies and maintain momentum

-

Track KPIs: uptime, pick success rate, intervention rate, time-to-value

-

Be the product voice in pre-sales technical conversations, build product collateral for the commercial team
