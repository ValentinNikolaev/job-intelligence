### About the Role

Join a fast-moving, early-stage **marketing AI company** that is automating the marketing services industry — starting with SEO and AI search optimization. Our platform uses autonomous agents to drive organic growth, content strategy, backlink building, and ad campaign management for B2B SaaS startups. As a **Technical Account Manager**, you'll be the primary technical partner for a portfolio of fast-growing startup clients, helping them unlock measurable growth outcomes from the platform.

### What You'll Do

-

Serve as the primary technical point of contact for clients, driving platform adoption and delivering measurable growth results.

-

Translate client needs into clear technical requirements and coordinate with product and engineering teams to deliver solutions.

-

Onboard new customers and coach them on best practices to optimize their marketing ROI.

-

Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and customer success to resolve issues and ensure clients realize value quickly.

-

Monitor client success metrics and provide strategic, data-driven recommendations to continuously improve growth outcomes.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Experience at a fast-growth startup with a demonstrated track record of delivering measurable wins.

-

Hands-on familiarity with marketing automation tools, data workflows, and integrations.

-

Proven ability to onboard and coach customers to drive platform adoption and ROI.

-

Ability to translate ambiguous client needs into concrete technical requirements and work closely with engineering and product.

-

Experience tracking success metrics and turning data into strategic recommendations.

-

Exceptional written and verbal communication skills — clear, persuasive, and client-ready.

-

High technical aptitude; strong CS fundamentals or equivalent problem-solving capability is a plus.

-

High EQ and comfort operating in a fast-paced, ambiguous, early-stage environment.

-

US work authorization required — **visa sponsorship is not available.**

**Nice to Have:**

-

Industry experience in marketing services, SEO, or AI search optimization (AEO).
