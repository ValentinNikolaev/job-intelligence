- Administration of Linux and MS Windows based servers & services

- Setting up customer applications

- Monitoring, analysis and reporting of performance and volume of the server infrastructure

- Strong understanding of OS-level security (Linux/Windows)

- Setup and administration of Oracle, MySQL, PostgreSQL and MSSQL databases

- Build, Manage, and optimize containerized environment using Docker

- Automate configuration management, deployment, and orchestration using Ansible

- Execute backup operations, including monitoring, failure resolution, and periodic restore testing and validation

- Maintain technical documentation and operational procedures

- Perform OS patching, firmware updates, and system upgrades as per schedules

- Ensure timely service restoration and effective communication during incidents

## Requirements

- Successfully completed studies in computer science or a comparable qualification

- Several years of professional experience as a system administrator (7-10 years)

- Ability to work in an international, multicultural company

- Good experience working with cloud computing platforms such as AWS, MS Azure or Google Cloud or OCI -

- Very good knowledge of at least one of the following operating systems: RHEL, MS Windows Server, Docker and Ansible

- Solid administration knowledge in at least one of the following: MSSQL, MariaDB/MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle DB

- Good knowledge of VMware vSphere and shared storage

- Knowledge of DNS, LDAP, AD and Remote Services and SSH

- Experience in automation and scripting

- Efficient troubleshooting skills

- Good understanding of network fundamentals (e.g. TCP/IP, routing, subnetting, DNS, firewall)

- Good language skills in English (spoken and written), German desirable.
