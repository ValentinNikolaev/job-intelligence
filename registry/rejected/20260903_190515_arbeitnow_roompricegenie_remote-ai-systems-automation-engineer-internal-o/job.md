## **Your Role**

As an **AI Systems & Automation Engineer**, you will drive internal AI adoption across Customer Success, Sales, and Operations. Your job isn't to build raw ML models or write throwaway demos. You will work directly with business leads to unblock legacy processes, connect internal data to core operational tools, and deploy dependable, supervised AI agents that automate complex workflows.

You will join our AI & Security team and bridge the gap between our core data platforms (Snowflake, Postgres, DocumentDB, S3) and our GTM stack (HubSpot, Chargebee, Modjo, Notion), establishing practical patterns for safe, agentic automation across the business.

## **What You’ll Do**

-

**Translate Business Pain into Systems:** Partner directly with Customer Success, Sales, and Ops teams to map out manual, multi-step workflows and design high-ROI AI automation targets.

-

**Build Production Agents & Integrations:** Write clean, maintainable code (Python/TypeScript) connecting LLM APIs to internal data stores and third-party SaaS platforms (HubSpot, Chargebee, Modjo, Notion).

-

**Implement Modern Agent Standards:** Build modular agent capabilities using standardized tools and schemas (such as Agent Skills standards, Claude Plugins, or custom function-calling pipelines) to give models safe, structured action boundaries.

-

**Design Human-in-the-Loop Workflows:** Implement strict guardrails, schema validation, human approval gates, and escalation paths so non-technical staff can safely supervise AI outputs.

-

**Ensure Integration Reliability:** Manage API rate limits, Webhooks, asynchronous jobs, and retry logic to keep integrations rock-solid as internal data volume grows.

-

**Measure & Iterate:** Build lightweight evaluation harnesses to track agent accuracy, hallucination rates, latency, and cost, optimizing prompt architectures and model choices based on real production usage.
