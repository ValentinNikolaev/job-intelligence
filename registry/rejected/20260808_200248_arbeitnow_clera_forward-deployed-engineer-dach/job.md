### About the Role

A fast-growing, Y Combinator–backed enterprise AI automation company is hiring a **Forward Deployed Engineer** based in Munich to embed directly with major DACH enterprise customers and help them become AI-native. You will work alongside an Enterprise AI Transformation Lead, scoping, building, and shipping production-grade AI agents and integrations that deliver measurable business value — and feed those learnings back into the core product.

This is a high-ownership, high-impact role at the intersection of engineering and customer success. You will be on the ground with customers, acting as a technical peer to CTOs and platform teams, and leaving behind systems they can own and operate independently.

Significant travel across the DACH region is expected. Native-level German proficiency is required. **Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.**

### What You'll Do

-

Serve as the engineer on the ground at major DACH enterprise accounts, partnering closely with the Enterprise AI Transformation Lead.

-

Embed on-site with customers, running workshops, hackathons, and implementation sprints.

-

Scope, build, integrate, debug, and teach customer teams to adopt production AI agents and workflows.

-

Ship end-to-end, high-leverage use cases: complex agents, custom integrations, MCP servers, and multi-step workflows.

-

Act as a technical peer and sparring partner with customer CTOs and platform teams.

-

Deliver solutions customers can own and operate independently after you leave.

-

Identify platform limitations and work directly with internal engineering — often writing fixes yourself.

-

Push the product forward by surfacing and implementing customer-driven improvements.

-

Automate your own work by building agents that eliminate manual tasks.
