Mobileye’s Automotive Software development team is looking for SW developers to work on the cutting-edge of technologies bringing to market Mobileye Autosar self-driving platforms.

We are looking for experienced SW developers with strong debugging skills and Autosar diagnostic experience who are passionately motivated by challenges and excel in troubleshooting complex problems.

**What will your day look like:**

- Developing and configuring AUTOSAR modules, below the RTE

- Creating and implementing software components on top of the RTE

- Create design documents, SW requirements, SW unit tests and test cases

- Establish bi-directional traceability between SW requirements and SW design

- Optimization of the design or code in regards to consumption of resources

- Debugging on bench and in vehicle, using Lauterbach debuggers, DLT and XCP

- Participate in design and code and requirements reviews

- Perform AUTOSAR embedded software integration

- Troubleshooting and root cause analysis of issues

- Participation in internal, customer, supplier and OEM meetings

- Knowledge transfer from and into the development teams

**All you need is:**

- Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering or Software Engineering or equivalent technical field from an accredited institution, Masters is a strong advantage

- 4 years of experience on Embedded Software in automotive electrical & electronics industry

- AUTOSAR expertise, Adaptive AUTOSAR is an advantage

- Strong customer orientation, willingness to meet deadlines, and self-management skills

- Deep passion for solving problems to resolution, including root cause analysis skills

- Ability to work and collaborate in a dynamic and global team environment and adapt to changing priorities and customer needs

**Advantages: **

- Knowledge of scripting tools like Python, CAPL - An advantage

- Experience with software engineering tools (JIRA, GIT) - An advantage

- Experience with UDS protocol / Bootloader / OEM Specific - An advantage

- Advanced experience with NXP processors and QNX OS

- Advanced experience with AUTOSAR abstraction (Mcu, Wdg, Gpt, Dio, Port, Icu, …)

- Advanced experience with AUTOSAR BSW and RTE, architecture, specifications, and configuration tools (eg. ElektroBitTresOs, Vector DaVinci)

- Strong fundamentals programming in C, embedded system concepts, and software build process

- Experience with software engineering process models (Automotive Spice (ISO 15504), Functional Safety (ISO 26262), Agile, MISRA C)

- Experience with Vector products (CANoe, CANape, vFlash, vT Family)

- Experience with debugging methods and tools (e.g., Lauterbach Trace32)
