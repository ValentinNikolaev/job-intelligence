### About the Role

Join a small, engineering-heavy team building the leading open-source LLM engineering platform — think "Datadog for AI applications." The platform helps teams trace, evaluate, and manage prompts across the full LLM development lifecycle, and is trusted by a large and rapidly growing base of developers at startups and Fortune 50 enterprises alike. The company recently joined forces with a major data infrastructure player, enabling it to move even faster on product while staying true to open-source and self-hosting.

As a **Product Engineer on the Growth team**, you'll own the full funnel — from top-of-funnel awareness through activation, adoption, and expansion — and have real latitude to run bold experiments that directly move the needle on growth.

### What You'll Do

-

**Analytics & data stack:** Build and maintain the product analytics infrastructure (e.g. event tracking, BI tooling, columnar databases) to generate deep insights into user behavior and funnel performance.

-

**Funnel mapping:** Define and instrument core funnels for each product feature; make it easy for the team to monitor activation, adoption, and retention.

-

**Experimentation:** Design and run A/B tests and growth experiments — from onboarding flows to in-product nudges — to validate hypotheses and improve key metrics.

-

**SDK & integration development:** Develop and maintain client SDKs and integrations (multi-language, versioned, packaged) that reduce time-to-value for new users.

-

**Self-serve & activation:** Build in-product experiences, guided flows, and documentation hooks that help customers reach their "aha moment" faster with less hand-holding.

-

**Feature adoption & expansion:** Identify and act on opportunities to help customers expand from a single product area to the full platform offering.

-

**Cross-functional collaboration:** Work closely with product, design, marketing, and sales to align on priorities, communicate tradeoffs, and ship impactful work quickly.

-

**Open-source community:** Contribute to open-source workflows and engage with a vibrant developer community building cutting-edge AI applications.
