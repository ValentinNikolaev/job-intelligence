### About the Role

This is a founding engineering hire at an early-stage, AI-powered platform built for industrial energy procurement and cost optimisation. You'll join a small, high-conviction team and take genuine ownership — from shaping the architecture to shipping features directly with customers. The platform handles real-time energy market data, forecasting, portfolio management, and optimisation at enterprise scale.

If you're a generalist engineer with a strong product mindset, comfort with ambiguity, and a curiosity for data-intensive domains, this role is a rare opportunity to have outsized impact from day one.

*Note: Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.*

### What You'll Do

-

Own the full product lifecycle — from design and requirements through to deployment and iteration.

-

Translate product objectives into clear, actionable requirements in close collaboration with customers.

-

Design simple, iterative solutions to complex energy procurement and market problems.

-

Build performant backend services for a highly data-intensive application.

-

Develop intuitive, elegant user interfaces for a sophisticated decision-support system.

-

Train time-series forecasting models for power consumption and renewables production.

-

Develop and refine linear optimisation algorithms to reduce customer energy costs.

-

Help define and evolve the core tech stack — including **PostgreSQL, TypeScript, React, and Python**.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

2+ years of software engineering experience delivering scalable web applications in an early-stage startup environment.

-

Proven track record of delivering full product features independently, working directly with customers.

-

Strong software engineering fundamentals — you write robust, maintainable, scalable code.

-

Hands-on experience building backend services for data-intensive applications.

-

Experience building frontend UIs for complex, data-rich decision-support tools.

-

A genuine product mindset: you think about outcomes, not just outputs.

-

Comfort thriving in a fast-paced, early-stage environment with high ambiguity and rapid change.

**Strong advantages:**

-

Experience with time-series forecasting and/or linear optimisation.

-

Familiarity with **PostgreSQL, React, Python**; exposure to **FastAPI, TypeScript, or Supabase** is a plus.

-

Background as a CTO, founding engineer, or startup founder.

-

Corporate background (e.g. industrials, energy, consulting) paired with a demonstrated startup mindset and grit.

-

Curiosity for energy markets, industrial technology, or data-intensive B2B applications.

### Location
