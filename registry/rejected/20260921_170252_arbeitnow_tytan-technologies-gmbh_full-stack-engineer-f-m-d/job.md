#### TYTAN Technologies

The **PACE** is fast. The expectations are high. The contribution matters. Working at TYTAN is more than a job, this is a mission. We thrive, think big, and hold ourselves to the highest standard. Our mission and values guide everything we do, and we're looking for people who thrive on ownership, embrace challenges, and are energized by meaningful work. If you're motivated by impact and thrive in dynamic environments, you'll fit right in.

At **TYTAN**, we aim to set a global standard in autonomous air defense with AI-driven, cost-efficient systems that counter unmanned aerial threats at scale. We protect people. We protect critical infrastructure. We protect Europe’s sovereignty.

We are seeking an experienced and hands-on **Full Stack Engineer** to join our Platform Team. In this role you build and own the internal software our engineers and production teams rely on every day: the applications that track what runs on every system we build, support our manufacturing and test processes, and turn operational data into better products. This is not a customer-facing web role: your users sit in the same building, and the tools you build directly determine how fast we can build, test and deliver.

#### **Your roadmap to make an impact**

-

Design, build and own internal web applications end to end, covering frontend, backend, data model and deployment

-

Develop tooling that supports our manufacturing, where clarity and correctness in the user interface directly affect hardware outcomes

-

Build and maintain the configuration and traceability applications that connect our engineering, production and business systems into one coherent chain

-

Extend our ML and data tooling so that operational data flows reliably from the field into datasets, models and back into our systems

-

Integrate heterogeneous systems (PLM, ERP, version control, device fleet, data platform) through robust APIs and clean interfaces

-

Work directly with the engineers, operators and test teams who use your software, and translate their day-to-day problems into simple, durable solutions

-

Take ownership of the reliability of what you build, as these applications are part of our production and development critical path

-

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field

-

4+ years of professional experience building and operating production web applications, with clear depth in at least one half of the stack and solid competence in the other

-

Strong backend engineering in a modern language such as Python, Java, Kotlin, C#, Go, TypeScript/Node or comparable. We care more about engineering strength than about prior exposure to a specific language, and expect strong engineers to pick up an unfamiliar stack quickly

-

Practical frontend experience with a component-based framework (React, Angular, Vue or similar) and the judgement to design an interface that people can use under time pressure

-

Sound relational data modelling and practical experience with relational databases

-

Confident with containerisation, version control workflows and CI/CD; able to deploy, monitor and debug your own services

-

Proven ownership of an internal tool or platform: gathering requirements from colleagues, scoping them realistically, and maintaining the result over time

-

A pragmatic engineer who builds robustly where correctness matters and deliberately simply everywhere else

Structured problem solver with a hands-on mindset and a bias toward shipping

Eager to work closely with a multidisciplinary team of software, hardware, production and flight test engineers
