### The role

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to build the event-processing pipelines and services that turn Nebius product usage into revenue.
You will work on systems that process a continuous stream of usage events, where correctness and failure recovery matter as much as throughput. The output is money: a silently dropped or double-counted event is a real financial error.
Usage of every Nebius product arrives as a stream of events. Our pipelines and services ingest, validate, enrich and price it in near real time, and deliver the results to invoicing, reporting and customer-facing account management.

#### In this role, you will:

- Build stateful streaming pipelines that compute billed consumption in near real time.

- Develop and operate Go services that ingest usage events, move data between our storage systems and deliver consumption data to users through usage APIs and billing data exports.

- Guarantee correctness under failures: idempotency, exactly-once processing and reconciliation.

- Operate transactional storage, persistent queues and change data capture under high throughput.

- Build reusable streaming infrastructure rather than solving each use case independently.

- Improve the reliability, scalability, latency and observability of the billing platform.

- Take end-to-end ownership of your systems: design, implementation, reliability and deployment.

#### We expect you to have:

- Strong software engineering fundamentals.

- Professional experience building and operating backend, streaming or data-intensive systems.

- The ability to reason about consistency and data correctness under failures.

- Experience with databases, messaging systems or event-processing platforms.

- The ability to investigate unfamiliar systems and solve problems without an obvious predefined solution.

- The ability and willingness to learn the team's technology stack.

#### It will be an added bonus if you have:

- Experience with a stateful stream-processing framework such as Apache Flink or Kafka Streams.

- Experience designing streaming pipelines.

- Go development experience.

- Experience operating services in Kubernetes.
