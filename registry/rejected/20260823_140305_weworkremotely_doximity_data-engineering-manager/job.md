***About you***

- ***Foremost an engineer****.* You exemplify high code quality and guide others, and have at least **5 years** of industry experience as a data engineer or similar role.

- You are experienced in creating automated testing, applying design patterns, and other engineering best practices.

- You are an expert in Python and possess fluency in SQL and have developed maintainable data pipelines with each.

- ***Experienced People Manager.*** You have experience managing 3 or more people whereby you were accountable for their growth and success as individuals and team members.

- ***Communication Extraordinaire.*** You excel at communicating to both technical and non-technical people, with an ability to explain complex concepts simply.

- ***Player Coach****.* You are able to contribute individually, outside of your reports or team, balancing priorities between being a player (i.e. individual contributor) and a coach (i.e. manager or mentor).

- ***See a project from end-to-end****.* You have led several projects, your own ideas and other's. This means from idea generation to planning, execution, through delivering.

- ***ML/AI–aware. ***You have working knowledge of how ML/AI applications are deployed and monitored, and you’re motivated to use this awareness to advance data-driven initiatives.

***Here's How You Will Make an Impact***

- *Collaborate with product managers, data analysts, and machine learning engineers to develop pipelines and ETL tasks in order to facilitate the extraction of insights.*

- *Establish data architecture processes and practices that can be scheduled, automated, replicated and serve as standards for other teams to leverage.**Inform data team strategy by working with the product leaders and managers. *

- *Actively participate in execution and some planning of organizational data team strategy. *

- *Collaborate with Doximity’s Data Leadership team to ensure alignment and success across the entire Data Organization.*

- *Manage individual Data Engineers to foster learning, growth and success at Doximity.*
