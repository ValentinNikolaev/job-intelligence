### About the Role

A fast-growing, pre-seed AI-powered energy management SaaS startup — backed by leading European venture investors — is hiring a **Founders' Associate / Chief of Staff** to work directly alongside the founding team. This role sits at the intersection of outbound enterprise sales, strategic project ownership, and operational support, and is central to building the company's go-to-market engine across the DACH region.

You will be an extension of the founders: owning projects end-to-end, driving pipeline, shaping positioning, and helping steer the company's direction. This is an ideal role for a high-ownership generalist who thrives in ambiguity and wants to make a measurable impact at an early-stage company in the energy sector.

**Work arrangement:** On-site in Munich, approximately 4 days per week. Visa sponsorship is not available.

### What You'll Do

-

Build and maintain a strong enterprise sales pipeline across DACH in close partnership with the founding team.

-

Run outbound execution — cold calls, email campaigns, and LinkedIn outreach — with tailored, insight-driven messaging.

-

Refine ICP, targeting strategy, and value propositions based on market and prospect feedback.

-

Produce high-quality sales enablement materials: pitch decks, one-pagers, follow-ups, and business cases to improve conversion rates.

-

Join discovery calls to surface customer insights and translate them into sharper positioning and messaging.

-

Support investor relations — prepare investor updates, key reporting materials, and strategic briefings.

-

Lead high-priority special projects end-to-end, including market research, partnership development, and internal initiatives.

-

Contribute to marketing content and event preparation (newsletters, social content, messaging).

-

Rapidly develop deep knowledge of energy markets and the product to continuously improve sales and product direction.

-

Operate autonomously in ambiguous environments and iterate on team processes as the company scales.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

3+ years of experience in enterprise sales or outbound demand generation, with a track record of building and delivering top-of-funnel pipeline in the DACH region.

-

Proven ability to lead cross-functional initiatives and act as an extension of a founding team in a startup environment, owning projects end-to-end.

-

Experience preparing investor updates and collaborating on investor relations.

-

Skilled at creating and delivering sales enablement materials (decks, one-pagers, business cases) and refining messaging using market feedback.

-

Proficiency with tools such as HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Notion, and Google Workspace.

-

Knowledge of energy markets — or a demonstrated ability to rapidly learn them — and translate insights into sales and product improvements.

-

Strong German language skills (essential for DACH outreach and customer engagement).

-

Startup mindset: comfortable with ambiguity, ownership-driven, and able to operate with limited structure.

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Experience in energy sector partnerships or procurement contexts.

-

Familiarity with MEDDICC or similar enterprise sales methodologies.

-

Background at industrial, energy, or technology companies (e.g. in sectors such as manufacturing, utilities, or industrial technology).

### Location

This role is based in **Munich, Bavaria, Germany**. You'll work on-site with the founding team approximately 4 days per week. Visa sponsorship is not available for this position.

-

Work closely with a high-calibre founding team and experienced investors shaping the future of industrial energy management.
