## About the role:

As a Business Analyst Consultant at YLD, you'll be part of a client team driving an Energy Transformation program. We're looking for someone curious, passionate, driven, and enthusiastic, skilled in mapping complex business processes and translating energy market requirements into clear technical specifications. You're also an effective communicator and a strong team player, thriving in a fast-paced environment as you manage data migration and prepare systems.

You'll coordinate with Product Managers, Engineering, third-party suppliers, and domain experts to define clear specifications, bridging legacy processes and the new platform so complex energy setups are properly supported, and keeping the team informed with the customer and business insight they need to contribute effectively.

## Preferred Skills:

- Understanding of the UK Energy market;

- Strong track record writing clear specifications and user acceptance criteria;

- Experience documenting technical requirements: data mapping, system integrations, logic flows;

- Delivery experience on large-scale system transformation or data migration programs under tight deadlines;

- Proactive risk management skills;

- Confidence facilitating input from Engineering, Design, and Commercial teams;

- Strong presentation and communication skills.
