### About the Role

We're hiring a **Developer Relations Engineer** to join a small, engineering-heavy team building one of the most widely adopted open-source platforms in the LLM engineering space. This is a rare opportunity to sit at the intersection of engineering, content creation, and community — helping developers worldwide build better AI applications through tracing, evaluation, and prompt management.

This role is based in **Berlin, Germany** (hybrid), with one week per month expected in the Berlin office, plus occasional travel to conferences and events.

### What You'll Do

-

Own and continuously improve the developer documentation — make it the best in the category

-

Write blog posts, social content, and thought leadership pieces that define how engineers should think about LLM observability and AI application development

-

Create cookbooks, tutorials, and code examples that deliver real, practical value to users

-

Produce videos, demos, and engaging technical content (yes, memes welcome)

-

Build small applications and tools that support community growth in creative, unconventional ways

-

Engage deeply with the open-source community — onboard new users, gather product feedback, and be a trusted voice in the ecosystem

-

Represent the platform at developer-focused events, webinars, and user cohorts through public speaking and live demonstrations

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

Engineering or Data Science background — you can read, write, and reason about code

-

Proficiency in **Python**; comfortable becoming a deep expert user of the platform

-

Proven experience creating developer-facing content: blog posts, tutorials, cookbooks, or videos

-

Experience in developer relations, community building, or a closely related function (user onboarding, support, feedback collection)

-

Active involvement or contributions in open-source communities; genuine OSS mindset

-

Strong public speaking and presentation skills — comfortable with both in-person and virtual developer audiences

-

Willingness to be on-site in Berlin on a regular basis with occasional conference travel

**Nice to Have:**

-

Impressive side projects or standout achievements from previous roles — we love hearing about these

-

Enthusiasm for talking directly to users to understand their needs and pain points

-

Experience working in small, high-accountability teams where ownership is the norm

-

Familiarity with LLM application development, AI agents, or related ML engineering workflows
