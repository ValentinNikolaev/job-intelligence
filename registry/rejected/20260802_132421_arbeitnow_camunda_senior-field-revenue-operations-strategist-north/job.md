**About the Role:**

You'll be the person a Theater Sales VP can't run the business without.

This role merges Sales Operations and Deal Desk into one seat — no handoffs, no "let me check with the other team." You own the full commercial engine for your theater: pipeline integrity, forecast accuracy, deal structuring, pricing approvals, and quote-to-cash, start to finish.

You report solid-line to RevOps, so you're always tied to global standards and systems. Day to day, though, you're embedded with the Theater VP — in the room for the calls that matter, not reading about them in a recap email. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role for someone who thrives at the intersection of strategy and execution, is equally comfortable in a forecast call and a complex deal pricing strategy and takes genuine ownership of the outcomes they influence.

**What you'll be doing:**

-

Serve as commercial deal partner on complex/non-standard deals, from early engagement through close

-

Structure and review pricing, discounting, and contract terms for policy compliance

-

Advise AEs on deal architecture and margin trade-offs

-

Own CPQ and order form process, quote accuracy, and SLAs

-

Manage escalations, looping in Finance/Legal with clear recommendations

-

Own the regional forecast process, pipeline coverage modeling, and SFDC hygiene/dashboards

-

Support quota-setting, territory planning, and QBR/pipeline reviews

-

Act as primary interface between the field and RevOps, Finance, Legal, and People teams

**What You Will Bring to the Role:**

5–8+ years in Sales Ops/RevOps (B2B SaaS preferred), including 3+ years in Deals Desk/quoting, deep Salesforce skills, deal-structuring experience, strong analytical and executive presence, and SaaS revenue model fluency (ARR, TCV, NRR).
