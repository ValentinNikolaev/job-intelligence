### About the Role

We are a bootstrapped AI platform and product studio based in Munich, Germany, serving a fast-growing base of SME customers. Our platform enables teams to connect data sources, build custom AI workflows, and deploy tailored AI assistants — without needing to be technical experts. We're now looking for a **Senior Full Stack Engineer** to help us scale our platform from its current user base toward 1 million users.

You'll join a small, product-focused engineering team and take real ownership of complex architectural challenges — from multi-terabyte data synchronization to secure sandbox infrastructure and high-volume LLM processing pipelines.

### What You'll Do

-

Own the most complex architectural problems across the platform, including multi-terabyte data synchronization and secure sandbox infrastructure.

-

Architect, build, and ship high-quality full-stack features using **React** and **TypeScript**, with a focus on long-term scalability and UX.

-

Design and implement systems to synchronize and index terabytes of client data while enforcing fine-grained permissions.

-

Build infrastructure to process millions of LLM calls per month with high reliability and security.

-

Implement scalable, secure code sandbox solutions capable of spawning thousands of sandboxes per day.

-

Create base infrastructure and tooling for fast custom integrations, enabling new systems to be connected within hours.

-

Collaborate on technical roadmaps and align on architectural decisions with the broader engineering team.

-

Debug production incidents, identify root causes, and implement durable fixes to keep the platform stable.

-

Mentor junior engineers and raise the bar through constructive, thorough code reviews.

-

Continuously improve development processes, code quality standards, and overall team performance.

### What We're Looking For

**Must-haves:**

-

7+ years of professional software engineering experience.

-

Strong hands-on experience with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **PostgreSQL** (including relational data modeling and query performance optimization).

-

Experience with **tRPC** or an equivalent type-safe API communication framework.

-

Proven experience designing and implementing **secure, multi-tenant sandbox environments**.

-

Experience building systems to process **millions of LLM/API calls per month** with high reliability and security.

-

Familiarity with the broader stack: **Docker**, **Kubernetes**, and modern CI/CD tooling.

-

Willingness to work on-site in **Munich, Germany** (hybrid arrangement).

**Nice-to-haves:**

-

Experience scaling complex software architectures for high-growth B2B products.

-

A strong product ownership mindset with genuine attention to end-user experience.

-

A passion for mentoring engineers and shaping engineering standards and processes.

-

Comfort with fast-paced, high-growth environments and rapid context switching.
