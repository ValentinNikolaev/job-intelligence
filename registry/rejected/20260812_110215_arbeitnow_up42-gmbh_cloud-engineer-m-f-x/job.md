UP42 was founded in 2019 with a clear purpose: to transform how organizations order, access, and analyze Earth observation data. Our platform brings together the world’s leading geospatial providers, and offers a single touchpoint to access, manage, and process data at scale. From vegetation management and infrastructure monitoring to disaster response, UP42 helps organizations across industries find solutions to complex problems through Earth observation. Our expert support team and detailed documentation guide our users through every step. Since July 2025, UP42 has been a part of Neo Space Group.

We are looking for a **Cloud Engineer (m/f/x)** to join our motivated SRE team in Berlin to help scale and maintain our cloud infrastructure while ensuring the reliability and performance of our geospatial processing platform.

**What you will do:**

-

Build, maintain and scale reliable infrastructure solutions using cloud-native technologies.

-

Contribute to our IaC practices by managing and deploying cloud resources using Terraform and other modern tools.

-

Manage, troubleshoot, and optimize Kubernetes clusters and workloads in Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

-

Maintain and enhance monitoring and alerting solutions across our platform using tools like Prometheus.

-

Drive continuous improvements in system resilience by automating repetitive tasks and streamlining operations.

-

Collaborate with development teams to establish, track, and maintain effective SLOs/SLIs.

-

Participate in our office-hours-only on-call rotation to troubleshoot issues and ensure platform stability.

**Here is a good summary of what we are looking for:**

-

Several years of proven experience in Site Reliability Engineering or DevOps roles

-

Practical experience managing infrastructure in Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or comparable experience with AWS/Azure

-

Solid working knowledge of Terraform (you can confidently read, modify, and deploy existing IaC modules in production)

-

Hands-on experience deploying, operating, and troubleshooting containerized workloads in production environments

-

Proven experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and automation scripts

-

Experience with observability solutions and monitoring systems like Prometheus

-

Driven to make a hands-on impact as an Individual Contributor (IC), bringing a growth mindset, technical empathy, and a collaborative approach to the team

-

Full business fluency in English, with the ability to clearly communicate technical concepts to diverse teams

**Bonus points if you bring:**

-

Experience with building Terraform modules

-

Cloud networking knowledge (VPC, subnets, routing…)

-

Familiarity with DevSecOps principles and cloud security best practices
