## **About The Opportunity**

We are looking for professional or semi-professional voice talents with a passion for language and technology for an exciting project. The recordings will be used for model training.

## **What You’ll Deliver **

-

Voice Samples

-

Voice qualities of interest:

-

Age-appropriate vocal quality. The voice should sound in the age range between 28–40 - dynamic, and not too serious or too mature.

-

Tone and timbre. We are looking for a voice that sounds full-bodied, warm, energetic and dynamic - rich in tone, with natural depth and resonance. Voices that sound too dry or serious will be excluded. Voices that are high-pitched or shrill will be excluded as well

-

No regional accent. The Voice Talent must speak standard German with absolutely no regional dialect influence.

-

Voice Characteristics:

-

Female-sounding

-

Flawless diction and articulation.

## **Qualifications **

-

Must be native speaker of the target locale: **GERMAN**

-

Experience with voice acting, narration, podcasting, or streaming

-

Access to a professional or high-quality home recording setup

-

Recording software

-

Microphone

-

Headset/speakers

-

Audio Interface

-

Professional Digital Audio Workstation, software, plugins

-

Treated space, quiet environment, free from background noise, music, clicks, muffling, etc.

-

Able to follow specific technical requirements when recording (volume level, file naming, format, gaps between segments, audio quality, etc.)

-

Able to provide demo, sample recordings, voiceover samples

-

Comfortable with fast-turnaround projects and able to meet tight delivery deadlines when engaged

-

Open to licensing your voice for AI voice cloning / TTS training

-

Depending on the project, you may undergo a test in our module to be subsequently vetted against all requirements.

-

Have not been and are not currently involved in AI voice training for any other company.
