# Senior Product Manager - Human AI Collaboration (all genders)

Posted: 2026-09-04T12:30:33Z

## Rejection

- Category: tech_stack
- Reason: role does not mention Go/Golang or PHP

We're looking for someone to own Human Understanding & Adaptive Collaboration as one of wingmaite's four foundational product disciplines. This isn't a traditional product role, you won't simply manage features or execute roadmaps. You'll define how wingmaite understands humans and collaborates with them, building the discipline through which experts externalize knowledge that only they currently hold.

## Tasks

**What you'll do**

- **Enterprise Knowledge Elicitation:** Define how wingmaite interviews experts, asks follow-up questions, reconstructs decisions and surfaces tacit knowledge

- **Adaptive Collaboration:** Decide when the system should ask, explain, interrupt, remind, wait, or deliberately not infer

- **Collaboration Conditions:** Model the situation, not the person: authority, expertise, timing, necessary friction

- **Human Understanding Integrity:** Establish what the product may legitimately learn about a person in context, and what it must deliberately leave unmodeled

- **Challenge** the CPO, CTO and Engineers on questions inside your pillar as one of four intellectual owners at the company

- Turn **deep thinking** into product behavior that's measurable and useful

- Build toward a small team, starting as a **strong individual contributor**

**What this role is explicitly not**

HR tech. Employee analytics. Performance management. Personality profiling. Engagement optimization. Prompt engineering. Traditional UX research. wingmaite models conditions, not characteristics. No scoring people, no manipulating behavior, no inferring personality, no replacing human judgment.

## Requirements

**Must-haves**

- Background in AI product, human-computer interaction, cognitive science, knowledge engineering, learning sciences, human factors, behavioral design, or enterprise software

- Exceptional product judgment and experimental thinking

- Think in situations, decisions, authority, expertise, and conditions, not fixed user traits

- Real ethical restraint: You know when a system should not infer something, even if it could

- Ability to turn deep thinking into measurable product behavior

- Fluent English (C1–C2)

**Nice-to-haves**

- Experience building early-stage products or working in startup environments

- Prior work with technical or expert users

- Familiarity with enterprise software contexts

- Hands-on mindset and technical skills that help you prepare prototypes and realize smaller features on your own

**Who you are**

- You start with the decision someone's facing, their authority and expertise, not their personality traits

- You can hold complex thinking and translate it into clear product decisions

- You have real restraint about what systems should and shouldn't do

- You take full responsibility for your discipline and its outcomes

- You work closely with leadership, engineering, and customers to shape the product

- You thrive on creating new disciplines, not executing existing ones

- The opportunity to define one of four foundational product disciplines at wingmaite
