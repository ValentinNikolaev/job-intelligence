**The AI Teams at Agile Robots** are looking for a **Technical Project Manager (m/f/d) AI Projects**, who will coordinate the planning, delivery, and resourcing of AI and robotics projects across engineering, research, and operations teams.

### Your Responsibilities

- **Project Delivery:** Define objectives, plan, and schedule projects, owning task allocation, resourcing, and execution through to deployment across distributed stakeholder locations.

- **Stakeholder Communication:** Act as the interface between technical teams and non-technical stakeholders, maintaining alignment on goals, milestones, and progress.

- **Team Coordination:** Coordinate cross-functional project teams and facilitate Agile ceremonies such as Scrum and Kanban.

- **Risk Management:** Identify project risks, develop mitigation plans, and resolve issues that threaten milestones or delivery commitments.

- **Hardware Procurement:** Manage procurement of hardware and related equipment, coordinating with vendors and internal stakeholders on acquisition, delivery, and allocation within project budgets.

- **Process Improvement:** Evaluate and refine project workflows and run post-project reviews to capture lessons learned.

### Essential Skills

- **Background:** Degree in robotics, computer science, mechatronics, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience managing technical projects.

- **Project Management:** Experience managing complex technical projects with Agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban), preferably in automation or robotics environments.

- **Communication:** Verbal and written communication that translates technical concepts for varied audiences.

- **Technical Grounding:** Working understanding of robotic system components, including sensors, actuators, and communication interfaces.

- **Language:** Fluency in English, written and spoken.

### Beneficial Skills

- **Technical Tools:** Experience with ROS, Python, and Linux-based environments.

- **Chinese Language:** Proficiency in Chinese.

- **Travel Readiness:** Willingness to travel to stakeholder and customer locations (25–40%), primarily in East Asia.
