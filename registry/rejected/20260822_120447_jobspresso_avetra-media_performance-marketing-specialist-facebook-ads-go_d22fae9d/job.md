**Performance Marketing Specialist (Meta Ads & Google Ads) – Remote**

Aventra Media is a Digital Media & Performance Marketing company helping global brands grow through data-driven advertising and scalable marketing systems. We specialize in Meta Ads, Google Ads, conversion optimization, and growth marketing for e-commerce and international businesses.

We are looking for **Performance Marketing Specialists** to join our remote team and manage high-performing advertising campaigns for international projects.

### Key Responsibilities

* Build, manage, and optimize Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns

* Monitor KPIs including CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, ROAS, and Conversion Rate

* Set up tracking with Meta Pixel, Conversion API, Google Tag Manager, and related tools

* Analyze campaign data and optimize targeting, bidding, and performance

* Conduct A/B testing for ads, audiences, and landing pages

* Collaborate with Creative and Content teams to improve campaign results

* Manage advertising budgets and prepare performance reports

### Requirements

* Hands-on experience with Meta Ads and/or Google Ads

* Strong understanding of Performance Marketing

* Ability to analyze data and make optimization decisions

* Familiar with tracking and analytics tools

* Self-motivated with excellent time management and communication skills

* Comfortable working independently in a remote environment

* Experience managing large budgets or international campaigns is a plus

This is a **fully remote position** open to candidates worldwide. If you’re passionate about performance marketing and want to work on international projects with a growth-focused team, we’d love to hear from you.
