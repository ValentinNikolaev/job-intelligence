## **Join Easygenerator and help customers unlock the full value of e-learning**

Step into an exciting role with an innovative e-learning software company in the EdTech space as a Customer Success Manager.

Easygenerator is a fast-growing scale-up revolutionizing e-learning with our award-winning authoring tool. Join us to build trustful relationships with customers across a broad range of industries and geographies, and drive the retention, expansion, and advocacy that fuel our growth.

**What you will be doing**

-

Own and manage a portfolio of accounts, ensuring high levels of customer satisfaction, retention, and growth.

-

Build and sustain strong relationships with key stakeholders, acting as a trusted advisor to help customers achieve their business outcomes.

-

Objectively assess account health scores and proactively take action to improve them.

-

Identify potential risks within accounts early and lead mitigation plans in collaboration with customer stakeholders.

-

Lead regular check-ins, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), and strategic discussions with customers.

-

Develop and execute strategic success plans tailored to each customer's objectives, supporting retention and unlocking expansion and upsell opportunities.

-

Act as the voice of the customer internally, providing feedback to Product and influencing roadmap decisions.

**What you will bring**

-

A bachelor's degree or higher.

-

2–3 years of Customer Success or key account management experience; prior experience is a plus but not required.

-

Excellent written and spoken English, with the ability to communicate clearly and lead conversations while knowing when to listen.

-

A commercial mindset and passion for negotiating strong outcomes for both customers and internal stakeholders.

-

A customer-oriented approach, with the ability to identify the real need behind a request.

**Nice to have**

-

A passion for HR tech and familiarity with the latest trends in the space.
