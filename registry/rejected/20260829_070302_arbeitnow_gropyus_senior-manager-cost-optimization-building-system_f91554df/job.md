**About The Company**

GROPYUS is a technology-based construction company focused on building multi-story residential buildings. Thanks to its prefabricated building system with various design options, industrial offsite construction, and fully digitalized processes, the company manufactures aspirational, sustainable, and affordable homes using timber construction methods. GROPYUS is using scalable construction and manufacturing solutions to tap into a future market, boost Europe's strength in innovation, while also playing a substantial role in improving sustainability.

## About the Role

Join GROPYUS and help drive cost competitiveness across our innovative building systems. Working at the intersection of product development, engineering, manufacturing, procurement, and construction, you will identify and realize cost optimization opportunities throughout the building lifecycle.

As Senior Cost Optimization Manager - Building Systems, you will play a key role in developing transparent cost structures, challenging technical solutions, and driving value engineering initiatives across our product portfolio.

This role is particularly suited to **candidates from automotive****/aviation/ ship building, industrial manufacturing, or other lean-driven indust****ries **who bring a strong track record in cost optimization, value engineering, and operational excellence to support the industrialization of building systems.

Your area of responsibilities include:

- Support the development and implementation of the Building Systems Cost Optimization strategy, ensuring cost competitiveness and value creation across the product portfolio

- Apply design-to-cost, should-cost, value engineering, and target-costing methodologies across product development, manufacturing, procurement, and construction functions

- Identify, analyze, and prioritize cost optimization opportunities for building systems, components, materials, and construction interfaces

- Develop and maintain transparent cost models and cost benchmarks, ensuring continuous learning from estimated versus actual cost performance

- Drive cross-functional cost optimization initiatives and support implementation of improvement measures

- Partner closely with Product, Engineering, Manufacturing, Procurement, Construction, and R&D teams to embed cost awareness into decision-making processes
