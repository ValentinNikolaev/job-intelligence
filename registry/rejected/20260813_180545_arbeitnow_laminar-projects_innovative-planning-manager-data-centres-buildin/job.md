It’s common for people to be exposed to several projects in their first year with us. You’ll work on some of the most complex and interesting environments in the world. You might find yourself working in sectors such as **data centres**, **infrastructure **(including defense, rail, and highways), **high tech** (such as advanced manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, and semiconductors), or **complex building projects**. You’re tasked with understanding and solving common problems. We don’t want to reinvent the wheel every time, so you’ll be responsible for documenting new solutions and evolving ones we’ve developed. This goes into our knowledge repository, which we’re going to open source to the industry.
