### About the Role

This is a rare opportunity to be the **first dedicated GTM and B2B Sales hire** at a fast-moving preventive healthtech company. You won't be running plays from an existing playbook — you'll be writing it. If you thrive in zero-to-one environments, love building pipeline with modern tooling, and want real commercial ownership, this role was designed for you.

The company offers a membership-based personalized health platform built around comprehensive biomarker testing (100+ markers), clinician-reviewed insights, and year-round concierge support. On the B2B side, the platform powers a **Diagnostics-as-a-Service** offering — including an API and white-label capabilities — targeting employers, insurers, clinics, corporate wellness programs, and location partners.

You'll work directly with the founding team to build and own the entire revenue motion from scratch.

### What You'll Do

-

Build and own the B2B pipeline end-to-end — outbound prospecting, partnership development, and inbound conversion.

-

Run the full sales cycle: discovery → demo → negotiation → close, with deal sizes ranging from **€10k to €500k+**.

-

Define and execute the go-to-market strategy for B2B channels, including employers, insurers, clinics, and corporate wellness buyers.

-

Set up the sales stack from zero — CRM, data enrichment, automation workflows, and intent signals.

-

Partner directly with founders on ICP definition, pricing strategy, and packaging decisions.

-

Feed real market signal back into product and business strategy.

### What We're Looking For

**Required:**

-

2–6 years of B2B sales or GTM experience, ideally in healthtech, corporate benefits, diagnostics, or a related sector.

-

**Native or fully fluent German speaker** — this is a hard requirement for this role.

-

Proven ability to self-source pipeline; you don't need an SDR feeding you leads.

-

Hands-on experience with modern sales tooling (e.g. CRM, enrichment tools, outbound automation).

-

Comfortable operating in an early-stage, high-ambiguity environment with minimal structure.

-

Strong commercial instincts — you can navigate complex stakeholders and multi-step deal cycles.

**Nice to have:**

-

Experience selling into HR, employee benefits, occupational health, or insurance buyers.

-

Background at a startup or scale-up in healthtech, biotech, or wellness.

-

Familiarity with tools like HubSpot, Clay, Apollo, n8n, or similar GTM stack components.

**This role is not a fit if you:**

-

Have only an SDR or lead-qualification background.

-

Are accustomed to enterprise environments where leads are served on a plate.

-

Require a remote or hybrid arrangement.
