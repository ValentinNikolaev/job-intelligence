# Support automation results and projection

This note accompanies `achievements.yaml`. The recorded results come from the
candidate-supplied `Q1_vs_Q2_2026_Automation_Comparison.docx`, transcribed in
`registry/candidate/user-supplied-support-automation-comparison-2026-10-02.md`.
The original eight exports were not supplied. The document reports aggregate
support-operation results and does not measure Valentin's individual causal
contribution.

## Recorded results

| Period | All closed | Closed automatically | Automation rate | Closed by a person | AI Agent closed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| January–March 2026 | 419,127 | 310,195 | 74.0% | 108,932 | 190,521 |
| April–June 2026 | 377,251 | 303,840 | 80.5% | 73,411 | 242,823 |

The automation rate increased by 6.5 percentage points, while the count of
automatically closed conversations fell by 2.0% as total closed volume fell by
10.0%. AI Agent closed 52,302 more conversations (+27.5%). April–June averaged
125,750 *closed conversations across all handlers* per month. This is not, by
itself, the number of tickets processed by Valentin's Go service: the earlier
candidate-confirmed lower bound of 20,000 tickets per ordinary month has a
different stated scope. Keep both figures distinct until their populations are
confirmed to match.

## July–September extrapolation

The third period has ended, but no measured July–September export was supplied.
The following is a mechanical continuation of the two recorded periods:

- Projected all closed: `377251 × (377251 / 419127) = 339559` (rounded).
- Projected automation rate: `(303840 / 377251) +
  [(303840 / 377251) − (310195 / 419127)] = 87.1%` (rounded).
- Projected automatically closed: `339559 × 87.0713% ≈ 295658`.
- Projected human-handled: `339559 − 295658 ≈ 43901`.

If the automation rate instead stays at the April–June level, the same
projected total implies about 273,483 automated and 66,076 human-handled
closures. Independent extrapolation of the individual handler channels is
inconsistent: extending AI Agent's +27.5% growth alone would exceed the
projected automated total, while extending Fin AI's absolute decline would
produce a negative count. Do not treat either scenario as a measured outcome
or as evidence that the candidate delivered that outcome.

## Achievement wording

Use these as wording guides only after reconciling the Simple.life employment
dates and checking the target vacancy's evidence rules. The wording uses
elapsed months and does not give the candidate sole credit for aggregate
changes:

- Built Go-based support automation workflows; across the wider support
  operation, about 125,750 conversations closed per month, and the share
  closed without a person rose from 74.0% to 80.5%.
- Built LLM support-agent scenarios on a platform where AI Agent closed
  242,823 conversations over three months, 27.5% more than in the preceding
  three months.

The 87.1% and 295,658 projection can support a forward-looking planning
statement that names its assumptions. It must not support a historical
achievement claim until actual July–September measurements confirm it.
