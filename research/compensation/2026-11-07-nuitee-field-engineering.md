## Calculation and selection

Use a provisional EUR-denominated continental hiring scenario because Spain is an
employer-attested working hub and both Spain and Ireland are explicit hybrid options
for this vacancy. Dublin is retained as a hiring-market comparator, not a verified
engineering centre. London remains a separately priced alternative; choosing EUR
does not prove Nuitée uses continental bands for an Italy-based remote hire.

With pay policy unknown, the **EUR 80–110k scenario envelope** spans the two EUR
comparators. Its midpoint is `(80,000 + 110,000) / 2 = 95,000`. Rounded to EUR 5,000,
the form proposal remains **EUR 95,000**. This is an explicit envelope heuristic,
not an average of employees, not equal headcount weighting, and not a statistical
confidence interval. EUR 90k and EUR 100k are the individual band midpoints.

A London-linked or globally benchmarked offer could be materially higher. A local
Italy band, different internal level or B2B agreement could also change the answer.
Until the employer clarifies those points, EUR 95k is a negotiable proposal with low
budget confidence. Do not claim that EUR 117k is impossible or that the employer
would reject it. Do not add an automatic discount or premium.
